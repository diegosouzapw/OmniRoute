- **fix(sse):** the structural request-admission gate no longer keeps rejecting traffic with a stale `critical` resource-pressure reading — when the cached state is critical and past its refresh deadline, admission now waits (bounded by `admissionRefreshTimeoutMs`, default 10s) for the already-scheduled refresh cycle to settle before deciding, instead of rejecting on the stale value until the next request happens to trigger a background refresh ([#13618](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/pull/13618))
