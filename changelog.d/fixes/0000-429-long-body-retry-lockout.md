- **fix(sse):** a 429 whose body carries a typed Google `RetryInfo.retryDelay` longer than the request-queue budget (Gemini answers an exhausted daily quota with `"86399s"`) now locks that model on the connection (on antigravity the exact model, never its quota family), and exactly the name the client asked for when an alias resolved it, which account selection (and the combo pre-dispatch gate, for targets pinned to the connection) already skips, instead of pausing the key's per-model limiter for the whole wait — that pause made every request on the key sit out `maxWaitMs` and fail as a local 503 while the combo kept routing back to it. Prose and untyped retry hints and connection-wide limiters keep the pause, but a pause that outlasts a caller's remaining queue time now turns that call away at once instead of letting it wait the budget out, and the 429 header log no longer claims a pause it does not take.
