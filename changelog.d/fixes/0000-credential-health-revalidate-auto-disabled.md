- **fix(credential-health):** a connection the gateway switched off by itself (OAuth refresh retry budget, stream expiry, missing Antigravity project, ...) is now marked `autoDisabledAt` and retested by the credential-health sweep, at most once per 6 h each and 5 per sweep; a passing test switches it back on. Operator-disabled connections, banned or deactivated accounts and inactive rows with no marker are never retested. `OMNIROUTE_DISABLE_AUTO_DISABLED_REVALIDATION=true` turns it off ([#16190](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/issues/16190))
