- **fix(resilience):** a provider circuit breaker no longer stays `DEGRADED` forever when the provider stops receiving traffic. `DEGRADED` now returns to `CLOSED` after the longest `OPEN` hold for its last failure kind (`cooldownByKind` value, else `resetTimeout`, × `maxBackoffMultiplier`, at least one cooldown) with no new failure (lazily, on read; a restored row with no failure time decays at once), and a valid connection test closes a `DEGRADED` provider or connection breaker. A `CLOSED` partial count still recovers one success at a time, and `OPEN` and `HALF_OPEN` still recover only through a request probe.
