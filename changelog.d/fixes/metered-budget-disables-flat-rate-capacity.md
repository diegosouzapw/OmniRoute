- **fix(budget):** a spent dollar budget no longer disables flat-rate capacity. The budget is scoped by `apiKeyId` and was enforced at the api-key policy phase, before any provider was resolved — so once the allowance was gone, a subscription/coding-plan provider that costs nothing per call was rejected alongside the metered APIs, and a mixed combo could not fall back to it. The chat path now defers the metered budget to the candidate boundary (`enforceApiKeyPolicy(..., { meteredBudget: "defer-to-candidate" })`) and re-applies it per resolved provider, before a credential is selected: a candidate that draws on the allowance is refused, one that does not stays eligible and is chosen by the normal ranking. Nothing else moves — the budget can only remove a candidate, never nominate one. Unauthorized metered spend is still prevented (an unknown provider counts as metered, and a request with no eligible non-metered candidate fails closed without dispatching), the refusal is tagged `BUDGET_EXCEEDED` so the combo loop never cools a healthy account for it, and flat-rate traffic no longer draws the allowance down while remaining fully visible in cost analytics. Every other endpoint keeps enforcing the budget at the policy phase, unchanged.
