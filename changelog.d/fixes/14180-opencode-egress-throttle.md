- **fix(sse):** the multi-account rotation can now pace itself per network egress and back off fleet-wide under sustained throttling. When several concurrent requests share the same egress, at most two dispatch at once and the rest wait 5-15 s (jittered) before re-picking an account, so a burst spreads instead of hitting the provider all at once; after ten burst 429s in a minute the fleet is treated as suspect for 60-120 s, during which each request tries at most two accounts before returning the last upstream answer, and the first success clears the state. Real rate limits (parseable Retry-After or rate-limit wording) never feed the suspect counter — they keep the existing early-stop path. Everything fails open (a full queue or timeout proceeds without a slot, a false suspect still tries two accounts) and the behavior is opt-in via `OPENCODE_EGRESS_THROTTLE_ENABLED=1` (default off: rotation unchanged).
