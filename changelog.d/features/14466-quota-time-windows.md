- **feat(dashboard):** quota pools can now be scheduled. Each pool takes a list of time windows (days of the week + hours, in an IANA zone of its own) saying when OmniRoute may use its accounts: an `allow` window turns the pool into a whitelist, a `block` window is a blackout that always wins over an overlapping `allow`. Inside an open window two independent limits apply, either of which may be left off — **keep free**, one floor per metered quota window (`5h`, `weekly`, …, or `any` for whichever is most consumed), measured on the whole-account saturation signal so a human burning the same credentials pushes OmniRoute out first; and **OmniRoute budget**, a requests/tokens/usd allowance measured on OmniRoute's OWN recorded consumption, so somebody else's traffic on the account neither eats nor inflates it. Edited from Costs → Quota Share on each pool card, served by `GET`/`PUT /api/quota/pools/{id}/schedules`. ([#14466](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/pull/14466))
