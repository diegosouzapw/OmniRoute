## `claude-web`

`claude-web` na-ezipụ arịrịọ nkata n'ụdị OpenAI site na nnọkọ ihe nchọgharị `claude.ai`
nke e mere nkwenye njirimara ya. Onye mmezu ahụ na-ahazi kuki enyere, na-achọpụta otu nzukọ
e mere nkwenye njirimara ya, na-akwadebe ọnọdụ mkparịta ụka, na-ahọrọ ụzọ mbufe ozugbo ma ọ bụ nke ihe nchọgharị, ma
na-atụgharị nzaghachi SSE sitere n'elu nke ọma. Nhazi usoro a dị na
`open-sse/executors/claude-web.ts:320`.

> **Ị bụ onye ọhụrụ n'iji ndị na-eweta Web Cookie?**
>
> Gụọ **`docs/getting-started/WEB-COOKIE-GUIDE.md`** maka usoro nhazi izugbe, nduzi nkwenye njirimara, oke, na idozi nsogbu tupu ị soro ntuziaka a akọwapụtara maka onye na-eweta a.

### Katalọgụ ụdị

Ndebanye aha ndị na-eweta na-ekpughe ugbu a kpọmkwem ID ụdị asaa ndị a na-agbanweghi agbanwe
(`open-sse/config/providers/registry/claude/web/index.ts:11`):

| ID ụdị                      | Aha ngosi               |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| `claude-fable-5`            | Claude Fable 5 (web)    |
| `claude-opus-4-8`           | Claude Opus 4.8 (web)   |
| `claude-sonnet-5`           | Claude Sonnet 5 (web)   |
| `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001` | Claude Haiku 4.5 (web)  |
| `claude-opus-4-7`           | Claude Opus 4.7 (web)   |
| `claude-opus-4-6`           | Claude Opus 4.6 (web)   |
| `claude-sonnet-4-6`         | Claude Sonnet 4.6 (web) |

Emejuputabeghị nchọpụta ụdị na-agbanwe agbanwe maka onye na-eweta a. Ndepụta dị n'elu bụ
katalọgụ oge-ngagharị.

### Ozi nzere na nchọpụta nzukọ

Nye ma nkụnye isi Cookie `claude.ai` zuru ezu ma ọ bụ uru nnọkọ naanị ya. A na-ahazi uru ndị naanị ha ka ha bụrụ `sessionKey`; a na-echekwa kuki ndị ọzọ ma ọ bụrụ na e nyere ha. Onye mmezu ahụ na-anabata
kuki ahụ site na `cookie` ma ọ bụ `apiKey` ma
na-agụ uru `deviceId` na `orgId` nke bụ nhọrọ site na data njikọ
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web.ts:72`).

Ọ bụrụ na `orgId` adịghị, onye mmezu ahụ na-akpọ `GET https://claude.ai/api/organizations` ma jiri nzukọ mbụ
nnọkọ Claude Web e mere nkwenye njirimara ya weghachiri
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web.ts:141`). Ọ na-akwụsị n'ụzọ echekwara mgbe a na-eweghachighị nzukọ ziri ezi,
na-akọ ikike nnọkọ a jụrụ dịka 401, ma na-amata ọdịiche dị n'etiti
nnwale Cloudflare na ọdịda nkwenye njirimara.

### Ọrụ mkparịta ụka

Ihe `claude_web` dị n'ọkwa kachasị elu, nke bụ nhọrọ, siri ike. A na-ajụ oghere ndị a na-amaghị. A kọwara
oghere ndị ọ na-anabata na `open-sse/executors/claude-web/session.ts:50`:

| Oghere                | Ihe ọ pụtara                                                     |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `operation`           | `completion` na ndabara; jiri `retry` maka ntụgharị nnwale ọzọ   |
| `conversation_id`     | UUID akọwapụtara maka mkparịta ụka dị adị                        |
| `parent_message_uuid` | UUID akọwapụtara maka ozi onye enyemaka bụ nne na nna            |
| `timezone`            | Aha mpaghara oge IANA ziri ezi                                   |
| `locale`              | Locale ziri ezi n'usoro nhazi                                    |
| `tool_states`         | N'usoro ọnọdụ-ngwaọrụ akaụntụ nke bụ nhọrọ, oke ya bụ ndenye 128 |

Arịrịọ ndị akwadoro na-eji otu n'ime ebe njedebe abụọ dị n'elu
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web.ts:203`):

- Ntugharị ọhụrụ ma ọ bụ nke na-esote na-ezipụ na
  `POST https://claude.ai/api/organizations/{orgId}/chat_conversations/{conversationId}/completion`.
- Nnwale ọzọ na-ezipụ na
  `POST https://claude.ai/api/organizations/{orgId}/chat_conversations/{conversationId}/retry_completion`.

Ntugharị ọhụrụ gụnyere `create_conversation_params`. Ntugharị na-esote echekwara na cache ma ọ bụ nke ejikọtara kpọmkwem
gụnyere `parent_message_uuid` ma hapụ `create_conversation_params`. Nnwale ọzọ chọrọ ma
ọnọdụ mkparịta ụka ma nke ozi nne na nna, ọ naghị ezipụkwa mkpali
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web/session.ts:254`). Mkparịta ụka ọhụrụ na-emepe UI e mere nkwenye njirimara ya
na `/new`; ntugharị ndị na-esote echekwara na cache ma ọ bụ nke ejikọtara kpọmkwem na-emepe ibe mkparịta ụka ahụ kpọmkwem
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web/session.ts:324`).

Ọnọdụ mkparịta ụka bụ cache dị na ebe nchekwa nke SHA-256 nke mpaghara akaụntụ na ndegharị okwu
ọkpụkpọ a haziri n'ụdị ọkọlọtọ ji dị ka igodo. Ndenye na-ekubi ume mgbe nkeji 30 gachara, a na-amachikwa cache ahụ na ndenye 5,000
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web/session.ts:12`). A na-akwado ọnọdụ naanị mgbe onye nyocha iyi siri ike hụrụ `message_stop`; ịmalitegharị usoro na-ehichapụ ya. Mgbe cache enweghị ihe achọrọ,
a na-ahazi arịrịọ nwere ọtụtụ ozi ka ọ bụrụ otu mkpali mgbake kama ịhapụ
ozi ndị mbụ na nzuzo.

Locale na mpaghara oge na-eji usoro mkpa a: uru `claude_web` nke arịrịọ, uru njikọ,
uru oge-ngagharị, emesịa `en-US` maka locale ma ọ bụ `UTC` maka mpaghara oge
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web/session.ts:218`).

### Ngwaọrụ na ibu arịrịọ

Arịrịọ ozugbo na-agbanwe naanị ngwa ọrụ fọnkụshọn OpenAI ndị ziri ezi n'usoro nhazi nke
ọkpụkpọ nyere. Enweghị ndepụta ngwaọrụ ndabara kwụ ọtọ e chepụtara echepụta
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web/payload.ts:102`).

Kama nke ahụ, arịrịọ ihe nchọgharị na-ejide arịrịọ UI e mere nkwenye njirimara ya ma na-edowe ngwaọrụ akaụntụ ya,
ọnọdụ ngwaọrụ, na ụdị ahaziri maka onye ọrụ. Mkparịta ụka, ụdị, ntụgharị uche, mkpali, na
oghere UUID ozi ndị akwadoro ka na-aka arịrịọ e jidere
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web/browserTransport.ts:175`). A na-ekewa ndebiri ihe nchọgharị site na
hash nke akaụntụ, nzukọ, kuki, locale, na mpaghara oge, ha na-ekubikwa ume mgbe nkeji 30 gachara
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web/browserTransport.ts:11`,
`open-sse/executors/claude-web/browserTransport.ts:158`). Mgbe arịrịọ ozugbo enweghị
ngwaọrụ onye ọkpụkpọ, ọ nwere ike iji ndebiri ahụ e kewara ọzọ; ngwaọrụ onye ọkpụkpọ akọwapụtara na-ebute ụzọ
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web/browserTransport.ts:214`).

### Nhọrọ ụzọ mbufe

Ụzọ ndabara bụ `sendClaudeWebDirect()`, nke na-akpọ `tlsFetchClaude()` site na iji
profaịlụ Chrome 146 ahaziri na kuki enyere (`open-sse/services/claudeTlsClient.ts:23`). Ọ naghị
amalite onye na-edozi nsogbu ma ọ bụ mepụta kuki nnọchi.

Tọọ `WEB_COOKIE_USE_BROWSER` ka ọ bụrụ `1`, `true`, ma ọ bụ `on` iji mee ka ihe nkwụnye ihe nchọgharị nke metụtara akaụntụ bụrụ ụzọ mbufe bụ isi. Tọọ `OMNIROUTE_BROWSER_POOL` ka ọ bụrụ otu n'ime ụkpụrụ ndị ahụ iji
kwe ka ihe ịma aka Cloudflare 403 a matara si na mbufe ozugbo laghachi n'ihe nkwụnye
ihe nchọgharị (`open-sse/executors/claude-web.ts:195`). Ọdịda HTTP ndị ọzọ anaghị akpalite
nlaghachi ahụ.

Ihe nkwụnye ihe nchọgharị na-edobe cookies n'ime otu context Playwright e jikọtara ọnụ, na-eji key
hashed nwere oke akọwara n'elu, ma na-eziga mmecha ahụ site na context ahụ
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web/browserTransport.ts:444`). Ọ naghị ebupụ cookie nke ihe nchọgharị
doziri gaa na client TLS ozugbo. Nnwalegharị ihe nchọgharị chọrọ template UI na-agwụbeghị oge
nke ejikọtara na otu context Playwright ahụ kpọmkwem (`open-sse/executors/claude-web/browserTransport.ts:467`).
Ọgụgụ nzaghachi ihe nchọgharị na-aga n'ihu nke nta nke nta n'ime page e nyere ikike, na-asọpụrụ
nkagbu arịrịọ, ma na-akagbu body upstream ozugbo ọ gafere 16 MiB
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web/browserTransport.ts:259`).

Executor ahụ na-eweghachiri logger arịrịọ a na-ekekọrịta projection audit e zoro ozi nzuzo: organization,
conversation na message UUIDs, ederede prompt, nkọwa tools, cookies, na device identifiers
anaghị esonye (`open-sse/executors/claude-web.ts:237`,
`open-sse/executors/claude-web.ts:252`). Mwepu transport na-eweghachikwa njehie njikọ izugbe
kama ozi e tụpụrụ.

### Omume SSE

`createClaudeWebResponse()` na-ahazi framing LF ma ọ bụ CRLF yana fields `data:` nwere ọtụtụ ahịrị. Ọ na-atụgharị
text deltas gaa na `content`, thinking deltas gaa na `reasoning_content`, na metadata events ndị a maara
gaa na mgbatị nzaghachi `claude_web`. A na-ebufe metadata event ọ bụla site na allowlist nke field nke ya
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web/stream.ts:37`). A na-eweghachikwa
metadata conversation, parent-message, assistant-message, na operation n'ime
headers `X-OmniRoute-Claude-Web-*` (`open-sse/executors/claude-web/stream.ts:364`).

Parser ahụ na-akwụsị n'ụzọ nchekwa ma ọ bụrụ JSON ezighi ezi, events `error` sitere na upstream, ụdị event
a na-amaghị, usoro ezighi ezi, content-block na-adabaghị, ma ọ bụ EOF tupu `message_stop`. Mmepụta streaming
na-ewepụta otu finish chunk na otu `[DONE]`; mmepụta buffered na-eji otu parser ahụ. Parser ahụ na-ewere
`message_stop` dị ka njedebe ozugbo, na-akagbu data upstream na-esochi, ma na-ebufe
nkagbu downstream gaa na reader upstream (`open-sse/executors/claude-web/stream.ts:461`,
`open-sse/executors/claude-web/stream.ts:563`). Ahịrị SSE ndị a na-emechaghị na events ndị a chịkọtara
nwere oke 1 MiB (`open-sse/executors/claude-web/stream.ts:17`,
`open-sse/executors/claude-web/stream.ts:62`).

### Ntọala

1. Malite OmniRoute site na `npm run dev` ma ọ bụ installation e wuru.
2. Mepee Dashboard → Providers → Add Provider.
3. Họrọ category Web Cookie na Claude Web.
4. Mado header Cookie zuru ezu e depụtaghachiri site na arịrịọ `claude.ai` e nyere ikike.
