> n’ịrịọ ha: ọ dịghị ihe na-agafe na ha ma ọ bụrụ na onye na-akpọ arịọghị id ahụ n’aha ya, ọ dịghịkwa

## Ihe kpatara nke a ji dị

OmniRoute azaworị ajụjụ abụọ gbasara ọnụ ahịa, ma ọ dịghị nke ọ bụla bụ nke ọtụtụ ndị na-ahụ maka sistemụ na-ajụ.

| Usoro dị ugbu a                                          | Ihe ọ na-aza                                       |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| `hidePaidModels` (`autoCombo/paidModelFilter.ts`)        | "e depụtara model a dị ka nke efu?"                |
| `freeAccessPolicy: "strict"` (`strictZeroCostFilter.ts`) | "connection a nwere ike ịkwụ m ụgwọ mgbe ọ bụla?"  |
| `quotaPreflight` (`combo/quotaExhaustionCutoff.ts`)      | "connection a ọ dị nso na oke ya?"                 |
| `budgetCap` / `budgetFallback` (`autoCombo/engine.ts`)   | "kwụsị mmefu n’ókè, laghachi na nke kacha ọnụ ala" |

Usoro niile na-eji naanị ihe efu **na-emechi mgbe e nwere ọdịda** — free pool agwụla bụ pool tọgbọ chakoo, ọ bụghị
ịgafe gaa na nhọrọ a na-akwụ ụgwọ — usoro niile nke akụkụ a na-akwụ ụgwọ anaghịkwa ele ọkwa anya. Ọ dịghị nke na-aza:

> "Jiri quota m na-akwụla ụgwọ ya. Mgbe ọ gwụrụ, kwụsị, ma ọ bụ rịgoro otu ọkwa n’otu oge
> site na nhọrọ ndị a na-akwụ ụgwọ ma dị ọnụ ala karịa — ma laghachi ozugbo ọ tọgharịrị."

## Billing bụ eziokwu gbasara connection, ọ bụghị gbasara model

`classifyTier()` (`open-sse/services/tierResolver.ts`) na-eji `(provider, model)` dịka key ma weghachite
`free | cheap | premium` site na ọnụahịa catalog. Ma ma request ọ na-ebute ego mgbakwunye
dabere na **connection nke na-ejere ya ozi**: otu model ahụ so na plan site na connection OAuth nke Claude Code,
ma a na-akwụ ụgwọ ya kwa token site na connection API-key.

`provider_connections.auth_type` abụghị proxy a pụrụ ịtụkwasị obi n’akụkụ ọ bụla — connection OAuth
ndị a na-atụ mita dị, connection API-key ndị so na plan dịkwa (seat token nke Copilot abụghị
API key a na-atụ mita). Ya mere, billing class na-abịa site na **catalog ahọpụtara nke ọma**,
`open-sse/config/connectionBillingCatalog.ts`, nke e ji aka hazie dịka usoro na ọnọdụ onye provider ọ bụla bipụtara —
otu ụkpụrụ ahụ `FreeModelBudget.hardStopGuaranteed` guzobere maka model efu.

```ts
type ConnectionBillingClass = "subscription" | "metered" | "keyless" | "unknown";
type ConnectionOverageBehavior = "hard-stop" | "meters-to-paid" | "unknown";
```

Usoro mkpebi (`autoCombo/connectionBilling.ts`): sentinel no-auth e mepụtara →
`keyless`; ntinye catalog nke dabara provider **na** `authType`; ntinye metụtara provider niile;
ma ọ bụghị ya, `unknown`. **Ihe a na-ahọrọbeghị abụghị nke efu** — a na-eji `unknown` dịka `metered`
ebe niile, ya mere provider agbakwunyere echi na-amalite n’èzí ọkwa subscription, a ga-ejikwa ụma
họrọ ya tinye.

## Model nke ọkwa

Ọkwa ise n’usoro nrịgo. Ihe dị iche na ha abụghị naanị ọnụahịa — nke ọ bụla nwere **akara
nke ike ọgwụgwụ nke ya**, nke mere na nke a abụghị naanị nhazi n’usoro.

| #   | Ọkwa           | Ndị so na ya                                             | Mgbe a na-ewere ya na ọ gwụrụ                  |
| --- | -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| 0   | `subscription` | `billing: "subscription"` ahọpụtara nke ọma              | windo quota ruru cutoff ma ọ bụ dị n’okpuru ya |
| 1   | `keyless`      | ụzọ no-auth e mepụtara                                   | cooldown / breaker nke connection              |
| 2   | `free`         | connection a na-atụ mita, `classifyTier() === "free"`    | allowance efu agwụla                           |
| 3   | `cheap`        | connection a na-atụ mita, `classifyTier() === "cheap"`   | e jirila budget nke ọkwa ahụ                   |
| 4   | `premium`      | connection a na-atụ mita, `classifyTier() === "premium"` | e jirila budget nke ọkwa ahụ                   |

Ọkwa 0-2 na-agwụ n’ihi **quota**, nke a pụrụ ịhụ ma a na-esokwa ya ugbu a. Ọkwa 3-4 enweghị
quota — connection a na-akwụ ụgwọ na-aga n’ihu ijere ozi ruo mgbe ebighị ebi — ya mere naanị akara ezi uche dị na ya nke ike ọgwụgwụ bụ
**budget** maka ọkwa ọ bụla. E wezụga nke ahụ, "rịgoro mgbe cheap gwụrụ" enweghị ihe ga-akpalite ya.

## `auto/subscription` — jụ ma ọ bụrụ na enweghị nkwenye

Pool = naanị ọkwa 0, ma kpachie ya na njikọ ndị overage ha bụ `hard-stop` e dere n’akwụkwọ nkọwa, nke a nyochara nke ọ bụla ozugbo iji gosi na quota ha ka nwere ohere. A na-ewepụ ihe niile na-edoghị anya: provider a na-ahazighị, ọgụgụ quota a na-apụghị ịkwado, ọgụgụ nke kara aka, ma ọ bụ overage a na-atụgharị gaa n’ụgwọ.

Pool tọgbọ chakoo bụ azịza **e bu n’obi**, ọ bụghị ntụpọ — ụzọ empty-pool nke onye na-akpọ ya nwere ugbu a na-atụgharị ya ka ọ bụrụ njehie doro anya kama ịbụ fallback dị jụụ nke a ga-akwụ ụgwọ. Nke ahụ bụ nkwa niile nke id ahụ.

A kpachapụrụ anya mee ka `keyless` **ghara** iru eru: nchịkọta a pụtara “plan m na-akwụ ụgwọ maka ya”, ya mere backend na-achọghị auth esoghị na ya. Jiri `auto/thrifty` (ma ọ bụ `auto/best-free`) maka nke ahụ.

### Nchekwa njikọ

Ọ bụghị mgbe niile ka a na-ejikọta otu candidate na naanị otu njikọ — logical candidate nwere allowlist `allowedConnectionIds`, ebe a na-ahọrọ account a ga-eji n’ezie mgbe e mesịrị, n’oge dispatch, site na `open-sse/services/combo/autoStrategy.ts`. Ya mere, grouping abụọ ahụ na-enyocha **njikọ ọ bụla n’otu n’otu** ma degharịa `allowedConnectionIds` ka ọ bụrụ kpọmkwem subset ndị lanarịrị — ọ bụghị list mbụ niile, ọ bụghịkwa otu member a họọrọ n’enweghị usoro. N’ihi na `autoStrategy.ts` na-amanye array ahụ ugbua dịka hard allowlist, idegharị ya ebe a na-eme ka “nke a nyochara” na “nke e ji mee ihe n’ezie” bụrụ otu set site na nhazi ya. Nke a bụ otu invariant ahụ, na otu echiche ahụ, dịka
[STRICT_ZERO_COST](./STRICT_ZERO_COST.md).

## `auto/thrifty` — rịgoro otu ọkwa n’otu oge

Pool = ọkwa niile, ahaziri dịka rung index si dị, ebe a na-egbochi candidates ndị quota ha gwụla. Engine `auto` ka na-enye akara **n’ime** pool ndị lanarịrị: ladder na-ekpebi ọkwa ndị ga-esonye, scoring na-ekpebi candidate ga-emeri n’ime ha. Nhazi na-adịgide n’ime otu ọkwa, ya mere overlay a anaghị agbanwegharị ranking nke scorer n’onwe ya.

Nke a bụ overlay maka nhazi + gating, **ọ bụghị** dispatcher ọhụrụ: speculative loop dị na `combo.ts` na-agafe targets n’usoro ma daba na nke na-esote ma nke mbụ daa, ya mere exhaustion n’oge runtime nke preflight ahụghị ka ga-eme ka ọ rịgoro n’ọkwa na-esote n’ime otu request ahụ.

Ebe `auto/subscription` na-ajụ ma ọ bụrụ na enweghị nkwenye, `auto/thrifty` na-aga n’ihu **n’agbanyeghị enweghị nkwenye**: a ka na-anwale njikọ dị n’ime plan nke na-enweghị ọgụgụ quota a pụrụ iji eme ihe. Ịnwale ya anaghị efu ihe ọ bụla, ma ọ bụrụ na e mesịa chọpụta na quota ya agwụla, fall-through ga-eru ọkwa na-esote — ebe ịjụ ịnwale ya ga-eziga request ahụ n’ọkwa a na-akwụ ụgwọ n’ihi telemetry na-efu, nke bụ kpọmkwem nsonaazụ grouping a ji dị iji zere.

## Ịlaghachi na plan mgbe reset gasịrị

Ihe atọ nọọrọ onwe ha ga-expire tupu routing alaghachi n’ọkwa 0. Idozi naanị otu ga-eme ka ladder ahụ nọgide rapara n’ọkwa ndị a na-akwụ ụgwọ ogologo oge mgbe plan ahụ jupụtachara ọzọ.

1. **Cache nke quota-state** — `freeAccessQuota.ts` na-echekwa cache maka `(provider, connection)` ọ bụla nwere
   TTL nke 180s. Cached entry nke `resetAt` nke ya gafereelarị na-akọwa window na-adịghịzi adị, ya mere a na-ewere ya ugbu a dịka nke kara aka **n’agbanyeghị afọ ya** ma mee ka e mee refresh.
   Ewezuga nke a, plan jupụtachara ọzọ n’etiti abalị ga-anọgide na-egosi na quota agwụla ruo mgbe TTL ga-emesịa lapse.
2. **State nke ladder n’onwe ya** — ọ dịghị nke ọ bụla, dịka e siri hazie ya. A na-agbakọghachi eligibility nke ọkwa site na live quota state oge ọ bụla a na-ewu pool; enweghị record “nọ ugbu a n’ọkwa 3” echekwara nke nwere ike ịdịgide karịa reset ma mee ka routing rapara.
3. **Cooldown nke njikọ** — 429 nke mere mgbe quota gwụrụ na-edobe `rateLimitedUntil` site na exponential backoff, nke nwere ike ịgafe ezigbo reset maka njikọ plan. `clampCooldownToReset()`
   (`subscriptionLadder.ts`) na-ebelata cooldown ruo n’oge reset nke upstream n’onwe ya ma ọ gaghị enwe ike ịgbatị ya. **E mepụtala ma nwalee ya, mana ejikọbeghị ya**: a na-eme invalidate quota cache na `src/sse/services/auth.ts` _tupu_ edee cooldown ọ bụla, ya mere a ga-enweta `resetAt` tupu mgbe ahụ n’ime function ahụ — mgbanwe na resilience hot path nke kwesịrị ịnọ na PR nke ya a ga-enyocha. Ruo mgbe ahụ, ịbanye ọzọ ga-echere ruo mgbe cooldown njikọ gwụchara
   (nke na-ebu ụzọ họrọ hints `Retry-After` sitere na upstream mgbe provider zipụrụ ha).

### Mgbochi ịma jijiji

A na-anabata ọkwa ka reset mere ọhụrụ naanị mgbe ọ gafere `reentryMinRemainingPercent` (default bụ 5), ebe njikọ nọworị n’ọrụ chọrọ naanị ịnọgide karịa `exitCutoffPercent` (default bụ 2, nke kwekọrọ na `quotaPreflight.defaultThresholdPercent`). Oghere dị n’etiti ha bụ hysteresis band — ewezuga ya, njikọ na-anọgharị n’akụkụ cutoff ga na-awụgharị n’etiti ọkwa n’ime requests na-esochi ibe ha.

## Nhazi

Maka ịhazigharị naanị. E kpachaara anya mee ka ọkọlọtọ `enabled` **ghara ịdị**: ihe mgba ọkụ nwere ike ịgbanyụ ha
ga-eme ka `auto/subscription` jiri nwayọ na-enye ọdọ niile — gụnyere ụdị ndị a na-akwụ ụgwọ — n'okpuru
aha na-ekwe nkwa ihe megidere nke ahụ.

```jsonc
{
  "subscriptionLadder": {
    "exitCutoffPercent": 2,
    "reentryMinRemainingPercent": 5,
    "rungBudgetUsd": { "cheap": 5.0, "premium": 0 }, // 0 na-agbanyụ rung kpamkpam
  },
}
```

Mgbochi dabere na mmefu anaghị arụ ọrụ ruo mgbe ejikọrọ spend resolver: mgbe enweghị usoro ndekọ ego, a na-ahazi
rung a na-akwụ ụgwọ mana anaghị egbochi ya. Site na v3.8.51, schema na-anabata ntọala `rungBudgetUsd`
mana etinyebeghị ya n'ọrụ — were ya dị ka nhazi echekwara maka ọdịnihu, ọ bụghị oke mmefu na-arụ ọrụ ugbu a. Nhazi rung, mmecha dabere na oke ojiji, na nlọghachi mgbe reset gasịrị
na-arụ ọrụ na-enweghị ya.

## Ngwakọta

`subscription` na `thrifty` bụ ụkpụrụ `AutoTier`, ya mere ha na ụdị niile nwere ike ịgwakọta:
`auto/coding:thrifty`, `auto/reasoning:subscription`, na ndị ọzọ. A na-egosipụta ID abụọ ahụ kwụ ọtọ
(`auto/subscription`, `auto/thrifty`) na `/v1/models` nakwa na dashboard.

Ọ dịghị nke ọ bụla n'ime ID ndị ahụ bụ ọkwa a na-akwụ ụgwọ, ya mere `isPaidTierAutoId()` na-eweghachi `false` maka ha abụọ,
`auto/subscription` na-agafekwa `hidePaidModels`.
