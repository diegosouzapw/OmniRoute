## Nchọpụta na Ndozi Nsogbu Zuru Ezu

---

### Mmachi Ọnụego n'Ebe Ndị Na-enye Ọrụ Efù Dị (429 / 400 / 401)

**Mgbaàmà**: Mgbe ị na-eji `model: "auto"` tinyere ndị na-enye ọrụ efu/na-achọghị nyocha njirimara (opencode, auggie, wdg.), ị na-enweta `HTTP 429`, `400`, ma ọ bụ `401` mgbe ụfọdụ kama azịza. Arịrịọ ndị ahụ na-aga nke ọma mgbe a nwara otu ntụziaka ahụ ọzọ obere oge gachara, mana akpaaka (ọrụ cron, ndị nnọchi anya, skript) na-akwụsị na ọdịda mbụ.

**Isi ihe kpatara ya**: Ụdị ọdịda atọ nọọrọ onwe ha na-agbakọta:

1. **Mmachi ọnụego nke onye na-enye ọrụ (`429`)**: Ọkwa efu nwere ike ịmanye oke ojiji n'ime oge a kapịrị ọnụ. Ọtụtụ oku yiri ibe ha a na-eme n'otu oge na-emecha oke ahụ, ya mere a na-ajụ arịrịọ na-esote ruo mgbe oge ahụ malitere ọzọ.
2. **Ụdịdị mebiri emebi na passthrough (`400`/`401`)**: Ọdọ `auto/*` nwere ike ịgụnye ụdịdị passthrough sitere na `opencode` ndị e debanyere n'akwụkwọ ndekọ mana na-enweghị ozi njirimara na-arụ ọrụ (dịka ọmụmaatụ, `oc/north-mini-code-free` → `401`). Auto-router na-anwale otu, ọ daa, njehie ahụ agbasaa tupu ịgbanwe gaa na nhọrọ ọzọ amalite.
3. **Mmụba n'ihi ime ihe n'otu oge (`429` mgbe ibu dị ukwuu)**: Mgbe ọtụtụ nnọkọ agent/cron rutere `auto` n'otu oge, mkpokọta ọnụego arịrịọ na-agafe ihe ndị na-enye ọrụ efu nwere ike ịnagide, ya mere a na-akanye oku ziri ezi dịka nke na-emegbu ọrụ ahụ.

**Ndozi a nwapụtara (ndị obodo kọrọ ya, 2026-08-10)**: hazie environment variables atọ ka ntụgharị, ime ihe n'otu oge, na ịgbanwe gaa na nhọrọ ọzọ nwee ike ịnagide mgbanwe nke ọkwa efu kama ịkwụsị n'ihi ya:

```bash
export OMNIROUTE_ROTATE_ON_400=true           # gaa na ụdịdị/onye na-enye ọrụ ọzọ mgbe 400/401 mere (na-amafe ụdịdị passthrough ndị mebiri emebi)
export OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT=4   # oke nnabata doro anya maka ọrụ ndị dị arọ (anaghị edobe ya na ndabara: enweghị oke ọnụọgụ arịrịọ, lee ndetu n'okpuru)
export OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS=5000 # chere ogologo oge mana n'ime oke maka ikike ibu dị arọ kama 503 a pụrụ ịnwale ọzọ ozugbo
```

Tọọ ihe ndị a n'ebe mgbanwe gburugburu ebe obibi nke usoro OmniRoute dị (daemon ahụ, dịka ọmụmaatụ site na LaunchAgent plist ma ọ bụ `systemctl edit`), wee malitegharịa OmniRoute. Ọkọlọtọ ntụgharị ahụ bụ ihe otu kachasị arụ ọrụ: ọ na-agbanwe ọdịda siri ike ka ọ bụrụ nnwale ọzọ na-enweghị onye ọrụ ịhụ ya megide onye na-enye ọrụ dị mma n'ime ọdọ ahụ.

**Ndetu**: `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT` na-amachi ọnụọgụ arịrịọ dị arọ — ndị nwere ogologo context — nwere ike ịrụ ọrụ n'otu oge; oke ahụ bụ ọnụ ụzọ nnabata, ọ bụghị ihe mmachi ọnụego nke onye na-enye ọrụ. **Mmelite #503-fanout:** anaghịzi edobe var a na ndabara (ọ na-arụ ọrụ naanị mgbe a haziri ya kpọmkwem, dịka e mere n'elu) — kama nke ahụ, a na-achịkwa nnabata nke ọrụ dị arọ site na mmefu byte a na-ewepụta na-akpaghị aka (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES`) nke na-agbanwe onwe ya dabere na oke ebe nchekwa n'ezie nke host, ya mere nrụnye ọhụrụ kwesịrị ịhụ `503 chat_admission_busy` ndị a jụrụ pere mpe nke ukwuu n'etinyeghị var a ma ọlị; idobe ya kpọmkwem ebe a ka na-arụ ọrụ dịka akọwara. Mgbanwe mmefu byte e mere kpọmkwem na-anọ n'etiti 8 MiB–2 GiB. `413 body_exceeds_budget` abụghị ihe ga-agafe n'onwe ya: bulie mmefu byte ahụ, wedata `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_HARD_MAX_BODY_BYTES`, ma ọ bụ bulie oke ebe nchekwa nke usoro ahụ. Mwepụ `inflight_bytes_budget` bụ esemokwu ikike nwa oge, a ka nwekwara ike ịnwale ya ọzọ. A na-achịkwa mmachi ọnụego nke onye na-enye ọrụ ọ bụla (`open-sse/services/rateLimitManager.ts`) iche site na `RATE_LIMIT_MAX_WAIT_MS`, `RATE_LIMIT_MAX_QUEUE_DEPTH`, na `RATE_LIMIT_AUTO_ENABLE` — lee `.env.example`.

**Otu esi achọpụta na ọ rụrụ ọrụ**: gbaa agent/cron gị ugboro abụọ n'usoro ngwa ngwa ma gosi na ha abụọ gara nke ọma. Tupu ndozi ahụ, ịgba ya nke abụọ na-ewepụtakarị `429`/`401`. Mgbe ndozi ahụ gasịrị, a na-anwale ọdịda (ọ bụrụ na ọ dị) ọzọ na-enweghị onye ọrụ ịhụ ya, oku ahụ wee gwụchaa. Ị nwekwara ike iji `curl /monitoring/health` ma lelee oghere `rateLimitedUntil` na njikọ ndị na-enye ọrụ yana `circuitBreakers.providerBreakers[].state` maka ndị na-enye ọrụ metụtara — ọnọdụ ahụ bụ otu n'ime `CLOSED`, `DEGRADED`, `OPEN`, ma ọ bụ `HALF_OPEN` (lee `src/shared/utils/circuitBreaker.ts`), onye na-enye ọrụ nke na-aga n'ihu ịda ga-agbanwe `CLOSED → DEGRADED → OPEN` tupu oge nrụgharị ekwe ka nyocha gafere (`HALF_OPEN`).

**Ọ bụrụ na ị ka na-ahụ 429**: akaụntụ nọ n’ọrụ maka onye na-eweta ọrụ ahụ agwụla _oke ojiji_ ya n’ezie (ọ bụghị naanị oke ọsọ arịrịọ). Tinye akaụntụ nke abụọ maka otu onye na-eweta ọrụ ahụ na dashboard OmniRoute → Providers → Accounts, ma ọ bụ tinye onye ọzọ na-eweta ọrụ n’efu (dịka `routeway`, `auggie`). Ntugharị akaụntụ na-enyere naanị maka nsogbu oke ọsọ/400/401 ndị na-adịru nwa oge; mgbe oke ojiji gwụsịrị kpamkpam, a chọrọ nzere nke abụọ ma ọ bụ onye ọzọ na-eweta ọrụ.

**Ọ bụrụ na ị hụ 403 na ụdị ọhụụ (`auto/vision`, `bazaarlink/*`)**: akaụntụ ejikọrọ enweghị atụmatụ akwụ ụgwọ nke gụnyere ọhụụ, ma ọ bụ igodo API ahụ enweghị ikike zuru oke. Nyochaa na dashboard onye na-eweta ọrụ na oke ikike igodo ahụ gụnyere ọhụụ/multimodal, ma ọ bụ jikọọ akaụntụ ọkwa akwụ ụgwọ ma debe ya dịka ebe e lekwasịrị anya maka ọhụụ.

## Nchọpụta Nje Na-abụghị Eziokwu nke Antivirus

<a name="antivirus-false-positives"></a>

### Avast/AVG na-etinye `README.md` na quarantine dịka `MD:HttpRequest-inf[Susp]`

**Nke a bụ nchọpụta na-abụghị eziokwu. Ọ dịghị ihe nje metụtara, ọ dịghịkwa ihe a chọrọ ka e mee.**

Avast na AVG na-eji usoro heuristic nke na-akara faịlụ ederede nkịtị/Markdown nwere ọtụtụ
njikọ ndị yiri arịrịọ HTTP. `README.md` nke OmniRoute na-abịa n'ime ngwugwu npm (e depụtara
ya na `package.json` → `files`), ya mere ọ na-abata na `node_modules/omniroute/README.md`
mgbe e mere nrụnye global — ọ nwekwara ihe atụ `http://localhost:20128/...` ruru ~15
(endpoints HTTP/SSE nke MCP, URL `.well-known` nke A2A, na snippets `curl`). Ọtụtụ njikọ
ndị a ezuola ịkpalite heuristic ahụ.

Ọ bụrụ na nke a malitere naanị n'oge na-adịbeghị anya: ụdị faịlụ ahụ agbanwebeghị. README
gbasawanyere tebụl endpoints ya (agbakwunyere MCP HTTP + SSE + A2A) yana ihe atụ `curl`
ndị ọzọ, nke mere ka ọ gafee oke ahụ.

Faịlụ ahụ bụ naanị akwụkwọ nkọwa na-adịghị arụ ọrụ ma o nweghị ọdịnaya a pụrụ ịrụ. Ị nwere
ike iweghachi ya na quarantine n'enweghị nsogbu.

**Ihe ị ga-eme:**

1. **Kwụsị ọkwa ndị ahụ** — wepụ directory nrụnye ahụ na nyocha antivirus gị
   (Avast: Settings → Exceptions), site n'ịgbakwunye ụzọ global `node_modules` gị na/ma ọ bụ
   directory data OmniRoute (`~/.omniroute/`).
2. **Kọwaa nchọpụta na-abụghị eziokwu ahụ** — <https://www.avast.com/false-positive-file-form.php>,
   ma tinye `README.md` ahụ dị na quarantine. Nke a bụ ndozi na-enyere onye ọ bụla aka, ebe ọ bụ
   heuristic nke onye nrụpụta ahụ na-emeghachi omume gabiga ókè megide faịlụ ederede.

**Ihe mere anyị anaghị “edozi” nke a n'akụkụ anyị:** ihe atụ niile bụ `http://localhost`,
localhost enweghịkwa ike ịbụ `https` n'enweghị nsogbu asambodo e ji aka onye ahụ bịanye aka.
Ịgbanwe akwụkwọ nkọwa ahụ n'ụzọ na-adịghị mma iji zere heuristic nke otu onye nrụpụta ga-emebi
ahụmịhe onye ọ bụla na-agụ ya naanị iji meeju ntụpọ dị na scanner ahụ.

### Kaspersky na-akara ngwa Desktop dịka `PDM:Trojan.Win32.Generic`

**Nke a bụ nchọpụta na-abụghị eziokwu sitere na heuristic omume. Ọ dịghị ihe nje metụtara.**
Prefix `PDM:` nke Kaspersky pụtara na mkpebi ahụ sitere na Proactive Defense Module
(System Watcher) ya, nke na-ekpe ikpe dabere n'ihe installer ahụ _na-eme_ kama iji ya tụnyere
malware ndị amaara. Mgbe ọ kpalitere, Kaspersky na-eme “rollback” nke nrụnye ahụ dum — na-ehichapụ
faịlụ ndị o derelarị — ya mere ngwa ahụ na-emesị mebie ma ọ bụ fuo.

Faịlụ ndị ọ na-akara bụ akụkụ ọkọlọtọ nke dependencies open-source ndị e kwupụtara, nke
e jikọtara na ngwa desktop ahụ, dịka ọmụmaatụ:

- `resources/app/.build/next/node_modules/playwright-<hash>/lib/…/agentParser.js` na
  `workerProcessEntry.js` — [Playwright](https://playwright.dev), library browser-automation
  a na-eji maka nbanye provider n'ime ngwa na nkata nke browser na-akwado.
- `resources/app/.build/next/node_modules/@wreq-js/binding-win32-<arch>-msvc-<hash>/wreq-js.win32-<arch>-msvc.node`
  — native binding `wreq-js` a kpọchiri na ụdị a kapịrị ọnụ, nke a na-eji maka HTTP nwere
  fingerprint browser n'elu providers web-cookie (`<arch>` bụ `x64` ma ọ bụ `arm64`).

**Ihe mere ọ ji akpalite:** installer Windows ahụ **enwebeghị code-signing**, ya mere installer
NSIS na-enweghị mbinye aka enweghị aha ọma ọ bụla, heuristics omume na-arụkwa ọrụ n'ike kachasị.
Mgbe e jikọtara nke a na native DLL e tinyere n'ime ya na ọtụtụ narị faịlụ `.js` e dere n'okpuru
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\OmniRoute` (gụnyere directories ngwugwu nwere suffix hash sitere na
standalone build Next.js), nke ahụ ezuola ịkpalite heuristic ahụ. A na-eme atụmatụ code signing;
ruo mgbe e mere ya, releases ọhụrụ nwere ike imeghachi nke a.

**Ihe ị ga-eme:**

1. **Buru ụzọ nyochaa ihe ị budatara** (nke a ga-ewepụ ohere na e metụgharịrị faịlụ ahụ).
   Release ọ bụla na-ebipụta `latest.yml`, nke field `sha512` ya (base64) na-ekpuchi installer
   `OmniRoute.Setup.<version>.exe`. Na PowerShell, site na folder nwere installer ahụ:
   ```powershell
   $b = [System.Security.Cryptography.SHA512]::Create().ComputeHash(
     [System.IO.File]::ReadAllBytes("$PWD\OmniRoute.Setup.<version>.exe"))
   [Convert]::ToBase64String($b)
   ```
   Ihe ọ na-ewepụta ga-adịrịrị ka `latest.yml` → `sha512`. Ọ bụrụ na ha adịghị otu, hichapụ faịlụ
   ahụ ma budata ya ọzọ naanị site na [ibe GitHub releases](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/releases).
2. **Weghachi + wepụ na nyocha** — weghachi ihe ndị rollback wepụrụ site na quarantine ma gbakwunye
   exclusion maka `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\OmniRoute` (Kaspersky → Settings → Threats and Exclusions),
   wee wụnye ya ọzọ.
3. **Kọwaa nchọpụta na-abụghị eziokwu ahụ** — <https://opentip.kaspersky.com/>. Akụkọ FP ndị
   ndị ọrụ zitere na-eme ka allowlisting dị ngwa n'ezie.

## Ndakọrịta Node.js

<a name="nodejs-compatibility"></a>

### Ihu nbanye na-akụda ma ọ bụ na-egosi njehie "Module self-registration"

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Ị na-eji ụdị Node.js dị n'èzí oke kacha ala nke gburugburu oge-ọsọ nchekwa OmniRoute kwadoro. Ihe a na-ahụkarị bụ iji ọkwa patch ochie nke Node 22 ma ọ bụ 24 nke dị n'okpuru oke nchekwa emezigharịrị nke OmniRoute chọrọ.

**Mgbaàmà:**

- Ihu nbanye na-egosi ihuenyo tọgbọ chakoo ma ọ bụ njehie sava
- Console na-egosi `Error: Module did not self-register` ma ọ bụ njehie native binding yiri ya
- Ihu nbanye na-egosi **ọkwa ịdọ aka ná ntị oroma** nke nwere ụdị Node gị ma ọ bụrụ na gburugburu oge-ọsọ ahụ adịghị n'ime iwu nchekwa akwadoro

**Ndozi:**

1. Wụnye ụdị Node.js LTS akwadoro (nke a tụrụ aro: Node.js 24.x):
   ```bash
   nvm install 24
   nvm use 24
   ```
2. Nyochaa ụdị gị: `node --version` kwesịrị igosi `v24.0.0` ma ọ bụ nke ka ọhụrụ n'ahịrị 24.x LTS
3. Wụnyegharị OmniRoute: `npm install -g omniroute`
4. Malitegharịa: `omniroute`

> **Ụdị nchekwa akwadoro:** `>=22.22.2 <23` ma ọ bụ `>=24.0.0 <27`. A na-akwado Node.js 24.x LTS (Krypton) na Node.js 26 n'ụzọ zuru oke.

### npm v11+: Awụnyeghị `better-sqlite3` (Cannot find module)

<a name="npm-v11-better-sqlite3-not-installed-cannot-find-module"></a>

**Ihe kpatara ya:** npm v11 (nke so Node.js 24+ abịa) na-egbochi scripts nrụnye maka dependencies nhọrọ
na ndabara. Ebe ọ bụ na edepụtara `better-sqlite3` na `optionalDependencies`
ma chọọ native compilation (`node-gyp rebuild`), npm na-amafere ya n'enweghị ọkwa.

**Mgbaàmà:**

- Sava na-akụda mgbe ọ na-amalite ma gosipụta `Cannot find module 'better-sqlite3'`
- `ls node_modules/better-sqlite3` na-egosi "No such file or directory"
- `npm ls better-sqlite3` na-egosi `(empty)`

**Ndozi:**

1. Kwado scripts nrụnye ahụ ma wụnyegharị:
   ```bash
   npm approve-scripts better-sqlite3
   npm install
   ```
2. Ma ọ bụ jiri aka wụnye prebuilt ahụ:
   ```bash
   npm pack better-sqlite3@13.0.1
   tar -xzf better-sqlite3-*.tgz -C node_modules
   mv node_modules/package node_modules/better-sqlite3
   rm better-sqlite3-*.tgz
   ```
3. Nyochaa na ọ na-arụ ọrụ: `node -e "require('better-sqlite3')(':memory:').close(); console.log('OK')"`

### macOS: `dlopen` / "slice is not valid mach-o file"

<a name="macos-native-module-rebuild"></a>

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Mgbe emechara `npm install -g omniroute` zuru ụwa ọnụ, o nwere ike ịbụ na a chịkọtara native binary nke `better-sqlite3` dị n'ime package ahụ maka architecture ma ọ bụ Node.js ABI dị iche na nke na-agba ọsọ na mpaghara gị. Nke a na-emekarị na macOS (ma Apple Silicon ma Intel) mgbe binary e wuru tupu oge erughị gburugburu gị.

**Mgbaàmà:**

- Sava na-akwụsị ozugbo mgbe ọ na-amalite ma gosipụta njehie `dlopen`
- Njehie ahụ nwere `slice is not valid mach-o file`
- Ọmụmaatụ zuru ezu:

```
dlopen(/Users/<user>/.nvm/versions/node/v24.14.1/lib/node_modules/omniroute/app/node_modules/better-sqlite3/build/Release/better_sqlite3.node, 0x0001): tried: '...' (slice is not valid mach-o file)
```

**Ndozi — wughachi ya maka gburugburu mpaghara gị (ọ dịghị mkpa iweda ụdị Node.js):**

```bash
cd $(npm root -g)/omniroute/app
npm rebuild better-sqlite3
omniroute
```

> **Rịba ama:** Nke a na-achịkọtagharị native binding ahụ megide ụdị Node.js na CPU architecture dị na mpaghara gị, nke na-edozi enweghị ndakọrịta binary ahụ. Oke runtime akwadoro n'ihu ọha bụ **`>=22.22.2 <23` ma ọ bụ `>=24.0.0 <27`** (`SUPPORTED_NODE_RANGE` na `src/shared/utils/nodeRuntimeSupport.ts`, nke kwekọrọ na mpaghara `engines` nke `package.json`). A na-akwado Node.js 24.x LTS (Krypton) na Node.js 26 n'ụzọ zuru oke na `better-sqlite3` v12.x.

## Nsogbu Proxy

<a name="proxy-issues"></a>

### Nnyocha onye na-eweta ọrụ na-egosi "fetch failed"

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Ebe njedebe maka inyocha igodo API (`POST /api/providers/validate`) na-agafeghị nhazi proxy na mbụ, nke na-akpata ọdịda na gburugburu ebe a chọrọ ka arịrịọ gafee proxy.

**Ndozi (v3.5.5+):** Edozila nke a ugbu a. Nnyocha onye na-eweta ọrụ na-agafe na `runWithProxyContext`, ma na-agbaso ntọala proxy nke onye na-eweta ọrụ na nke zuru ụwa ọnụ na-akpaghị aka.

### Nnyocha ahụike token na-ada na "fetch failed"

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Mmelite token OAuth na-eme n'azụ anaghị achọpụta nhazi proxy maka njikọ ọ bụla.

**Ndozi (v3.5.5+):** Onye nhazi oge maka nnyocha ahụike token na-achọpụta nhazi proxy maka njikọ ọ bụla ugbu a tupu ọ nwaa ime mmelite. Melite gaa na v3.5.5+.

### Proxy SOCKS5 na-eweghachi "invalid onRequestStart method"

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Na Node.js 22, dispatcher nke undici@8 adabaghị na mmejuputa `fetch()` arụnyere n'ime Node.

**Ndozi (v3.5.5+):** OmniRoute na-eji ọrụ `fetch()` nke undici n'onwe ya ugbu a mgbe dispatcher proxy na-arụ ọrụ, iji hụ na omume ya na-adị otu. Melite gaa na v3.5.5+.

### Proxy MITM n'okpuru WSL: anaghị ejide ngwa desktọpụ dị na kọmputa Windows nnabata

**Ihe kpatara ya:** A na-etinye proxy MITM na asambodo CA ya n'ime gburugburu ebe OmniRoute na-arụ ọrụ. N'okpuru WSL, gburugburu ahụ bụ Linux ọbịa, ebe ngwa AI desktọpụ (Kiro, Trae, Copilot, Zed, …) na-arụ ọrụ na kọmputa Windows nnabata. Ngwa ndị dị na kọmputa nnabata anaghị atụkwasị ebe nchekwa asambodo nke sistemụ ọbịa obi, ha anaghịkwa agafe na proxy sistemụ nke ọbịa, ya mere njide okporoụzọ desktọpụ anaghị arụ ọrụ n'ebe ahụ.

**Nkwado:** Gbaa OmniRoute ozugbo na otu OS nke ngwa desktọpụ ndị ịchọrọ ijide na ya (Windows maka ngwa Windows; otu a kwa maka macOS/Linux). Idowe OmniRoute n'ime WSL mgbe ị na-elekwasị anya na ngwa ndị dị na kọmputa nnabata chọrọ ka i jiri aka mee ka asambodo CA emepụtara bụrụ nke a tụkwasịrị obi na kọmputa Windows nnabata, ma tụnye ntọala netwọkụ/proxy nke ngwa ọ bụla dị na kọmputa nnabata gaa na ebe njedebe proxy WSL — nhazi na-enweghị nkwado ma dịkwa mfe imebi.

### Docker IPv6 / Ntọgharị Njikọ

<a name="docker-ipv6"></a>

**Mgbaàmà:** `curl http://localhost:20128/v1/models` na-eweghachi `curl: (56) Recv failure: Connection reset by peer`. Dashboard na endpoint ndị na-achọghị nyocha njirimara na-arụ ọrụ, mana endpoint ndị chọrọ nyocha njirimara anaghị arụ ọrụ — ọ na-adị ka nsogbu auth mana ọ bụghị.

   ```bash
     -p 127.0.0.1:20128:20128 -v omniroute-data:/app/data diegosouzapw/omniroute:latest
   ```
   Nke a na-amanye bind IPv4 ma na-egbochikwa ikpughe proxy na interface host niile.

## Ntọala Nkwụsi Ike

### Mmachi ọnụego akpaka anaghị amalite

- Mmachi ọnụego akpaka na-emetụta naanị ndị na-eweta API key (ọ bụghị OAuth/ndebanye aha)
- Nyochaa na agbanyere mmachi ọnụego akpaka na **Ntọala → Nkwụsi Ike → Profaịlụ Ndị Na-eweta Ọrụ**
- Lelee ma onye na-eweta ọrụ na-eweghachi koodu ọnọdụ `429` ma ọ bụ nkụnye isi `Retry-After`

### Ịhazigharị exponential backoff

Profaịlụ ndị na-eweta ọrụ na-akwado ntọala ndị a:

- **Oge nchere mbido** — Oge nchere mbụ mgbe ọdịda mbụ gasịrị (ndabara: 1s)
- **Oge nchere kachasị** — Oke kachasị nke oge nchere (ndabara: 30s)
- **Ihe mmụba** — Oke a ga-eji mụbaa oge nchere maka ọdịda ọ bụla na-esochi ibe ya (ndabara: 2x)

### Mgbochi ìgwè na-abata n'otu oge

Mgbe ọtụtụ arịrịọ na-eme n'otu oge rutere onye na-eweta ọrụ nwere mmachi ọnụego, OmniRoute na-eji mutex + mmachi ọnụego akpaka hazie arịrịọ ka ha na-aga n'usoro ma gbochie ọdịda na-esochi ibe ya. Nke a na-eme na-akpaghị aka maka ndị na-eweta API key.

### Arịrịọ nkata na-ada na 503 / chat_admission_busy

**Ihe mgbaàmà:**

- Ebe njedebe mmecha nkata na-eweghachi nzaghachi `503` enwere ike ịnwalegharị, nke koodu njehie ya bụ
  `chat_admission_busy`.
- Nzaghachi ahụ gụnyere `Retry-After`. Kemgbe #12135, a na-enweta uru ahụ site na njupụta a hụrụ
  — nke ka ukwuu n'etiti windo `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS` nke arịrịọ ahụ cherelarị
  na ogologo oge e jiderela heavyweight leases ndị dị ugbu a — a na-agbakọta ya elu ruo na
  sekọnd zuru ezu ma debe oke ya na 60. N'ọnụ ụzọ na-enweghị ibu, ọ na-edobe oke ala ndị e ji eme ihe mbụ: sekọnd 2 n'ụzọ
  dabere na byte, sekọnd 1 n'ụzọ dabere na nhazi (nke gụnyekwara
  `reason: "structure_limit"`).
- Nke a nwere ike ime mgbe nkata heavyweight ọzọ ma ọ bụ nzaghachi streaming na-agba ogologo oge ka
  na-aga n'ihu.

Ahụ nzaghachi dabere na byte bụ:

```json
{
  "error": {
    "message": "Chat admission capacity is temporarily unavailable. Retry shortly.",
    "type": "server_error",
    "code": "chat_admission_busy"
  }
}
```

Nzaghachi dabere na nhazi na-eji otu ụdị na koodu ahụ, tinyere ozi
`Local chat admission capacity is busy for this structurally heavy request; upstream provider routing was not attempted. Retry shortly.`
na `reason: "structure_limit"`.
N'ókè ndabara, arịrịọ dị arọ n'ihe gbasara nhazi mgbe o nwere opekata mpe ozi `200`,
opekata mpe ngwaọrụ `64`, ma ọ bụ opekata mpe token e mere atụmatụ `32,000`, ma ọ bụ mgbe atụmatụ nhazi nwere oke
mechara oke ya nke node `10,000` a gara ma ọ bụ omimi `12`.

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Nke a bụ mbelata ibu e mere ụma n'ime OmniRoute, ọ bụghị ọdịda sitere n'aka onye na-eweta ọrụ upstream.
Process ọ bụla na-eji nche mpaghara process dobe ikike heavyweight pere mpe tupu o debe
ma nyochaa ahụ nnukwu arịrịọ. A na-ejide heavyweight lease ruo ogologo ndụ nzaghachi SSE.

**#503-fanout:** tupu ndozi a, nche ahụ na-edobe concurrency n'COUNT arịrịọ a kapịrị ọnụ
(`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT`, ndabara `1`) n'agbanyeghị ebe nchekwa host, ya mere fan-out nke coding-agent
(ọtụtụ subagents/CLIs, ahụ arịrịọ na-abụkarị > 256 KB) dara ruo concurrency dị irè
nke ihe dịka 1 ma weghachite 503 n'okpuru ibu nkịtị kpamkpam. Ugbu a, nche ahụ na-ahazi onwe ya: budget BYTE ingest
a na-enweta na-akpaghị aka (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES`) nke e mere nha ya site na
oke ebe nchekwa process ahụ n'ezie na-achịkwa ya, ọ na-enyochakwa mgbaàmà nrụgide akụrụngwa dị ndụ — ya mere ọ
na-ebelata ibu naanị mgbe host nọ n'okpuru ezigbo nrụgide ebe nchekwa, ọ bụghị naanị n'ihi na ihe karịrị otu
arịrịọ dị arọ bịara n'otu oge. A ka na-asọpụrụ oke COUNT ochie (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT`),
mana naanị ma ọ bụrụ na ị tọọ ya n'ụzọ doro anya.

Mgbe ikike jupụtara, arịrịọ heavyweight na-ebu ụzọ chere ruo
`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS` (ndabara `2000`, `0` na-ewepụ nchere ahụ) ka ohere tọhapụ
tupu ọ zaa `503` enwere ike ịnwalegharị. Nchere nwere oke dị ka nke a ka clients ndị na-arụ ọrụ dịka agent
(OpenCode, Claude Code, Cursor) nke na-ezipụ sub-request dị arọ ọtụtụ n'otu oge nwee ike ịhazi oke mbata ahụ
n'usoro kama imefusị budget nnwalegharị ha niile na ajụjụ ozugbo ma kwụsị n'etiti ọrụ.
A na-egosi njupụta heavyweight lease dị ugbu a, budget byte e kpebiri, na ogo nrụgide dị ndụ
na `GET /api/monitoring/health` → `chatAdmission` (`inflightBytes`, `maxInflightBytes`,
`budgetSource`, `pressureSeverity`, `countCapEnabled`) — lelee ihe ndị a tupu ịgbanwe env var ọ bụla.
Ntọala → Nkwụsi Ike → Ahịrị Arịrịọ → Arịrịọ Na-aga N'otu Oge anaghị achịkwa nke a; ntọala ahụ
na-achịkwa usoro ahịrị arịrịọ onye na-eweta ọrụ dị iche.

**Ndozi:**

1. Buru ụzọ nwaleegharị. Clients kwesịrị ịsọpụrụ `Retry-After` ma jiri backoff kama ịmegharị
   arịrịọ ahụ ozugbo.
2. Lelee `/api/monitoring/health` → `chatAdmission` tupu ịhazigharị ihe ọ bụla. `countCapEnabled:
false` na `maxInflightBytes` buru ibu pụtara na budget a na-enweta na-akpaghị aka amalitela ịrụ
   ọrụ ya; `pressureSeverity` nke `high`/`critical` pụtara na host ahụ enweghị ebe nchekwa zuru ezu n'ezie —
   enweghị ike idozi nke ahụ site na admission env var; ọ chọrọ RAM karịa ma ọ bụ ibu ọrụ pere mpe.
3. Naanị ma ọ bụrụ na `/api/monitoring/health` gosiri na budget a na-enweta na-akpaghị aka pere ezigbo mpe maka
   host gị (ọ na-adịkarịghị — ọ na-agbasaworị site na container ruo bare-metal), jiri
   `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES` dochie ya ozugbo kama ịlaghachi n'oke COUNT arịrịọ ochie.

Lee [ntụaka environment-variable](../reference/ENVIRONMENT.md#4-security--authentication)
maka ntọala admission bụ isi e ji eme ihe.

## Nhazi ọdịda RAG / LLM nke bụ nhọrọ (nsogbu 16)

Ụfọdụ ndị ọrụ OmniRoute na-etinye gateway ahụ n'ihu RAG ma ọ bụ usoro agent. N'ime nhazi ndị ahụ, a na-ahụkarị usoro dị ịtụnanya: OmniRoute na-adị ka ọ dị mma (providers na-arụ ọrụ, routing profiles dị mma, enweghị ọkwa ịdọ aka ná ntị gbasara rate limit), mana azịza ikpeazụ ka na-ezighi ezi.

N'omume, ihe omume ndị a na-esikarị na pipeline RAG nke dị n'okpuru pụta, ọ bụghị na gateway n'onwe ya.

Ọ bụrụ na ịchọrọ usoro okwu a na-ekekọrịta iji kọwaa ọdịda ndị ahụ, ị nwere ike iji WFGY ProblemMap, ihe enyemaka ederede dị n'èzí nwere ikikere MIT nke na-akọwa usoro ọdịda RAG / LLM iri na isii na-eme ugboro ugboro. N'ozuzu, ọ gụnyere:

- ntụgharị retrieval na ókè context mebiri emebi
- indexes na vector stores efu ma ọ bụ nke ochie
- enweghị ndakọrịta n'etiti embedding na semantic
- nsogbu prompt assembly na context window
- ndakpọ logic na azịza nwere ntụkwasị obi karịrị akarị
- ọdịda long chain na nhazi agent
- ntụgharị memory na role n'etiti ọtụtụ agent
- nsogbu deployment na usoro bootstrap

Echiche ahụ dị mfe:

1. Mgbe ị na-enyocha azịza na-adịghị mma, dekọọ:
   - ọrụ na arịrịọ onye ọrụ
   - ngwakọta route ma ọ bụ provider dị na OmniRoute
   - context RAG ọ bụla ejiri n'okpuru (documents eweghachitere, tool calls, wdg)
2. Jikọọ ihe omume ahụ na otu ma ọ bụ abụọ n'ime nọmba WFGY ProblemMap (`No.1` … `No.16`).
3. Chekwaa nọmba ahụ na dashboard, runbook, ma ọ bụ incident tracker nke gị n'akụkụ logs OmniRoute.
4. Jiri ibe WFGY kwekọrọ na ya kpebie ma ị ga-agbanwe RAG stack, retriever, ma ọ bụ routing strategy gị.

Ederede zuru ezu na usoro doro anya dị ebe a (ikikere MIT, naanị ederede):

[WFGY ProblemMap README](https://github.com/onestardao/WFGY/blob/main/ProblemMap/README.md)

3. Malitegharịa OmniRoute ma nwalee ọzọ site na **Dashboard → CLI Tools**

### Model cooldown rapaara (ntọgharị aka)

**Ihe mgbaàmà:**

- A ka na-edepụta model n'ime cooldown ọbụna mgbe oge ngwụcha gafere
- Arịrịọ ka na-awụpụ model ahụ na combo routing n'agbanyeghị na timestamp ahụ agafeela

**Ntọgharị aka:**

- **Dashboard:** **Settings → Model Cooldowns** → pịa **Re-enable** na card emetụtara
- **API:** `DELETE /api/resilience/model-cooldowns` tinyere management auth headers

### Njikọ Command Code provider dara na 403

**Ihe mgbaàmà:**

- 403 mgbe a na-anwale njikọ Command Code provider
- Card provider ahụ na-egosi "enweghị ikike" mgbe agbakwunyere ya ọhụrụ

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Usoro OAuth ahụ emechaghị (anataghị callback ma ọ bụ echekwaghị token).

**Ndozi:**

- Gbaa `omniroute providers` site na CLI iji kpalitegharịa usoro OAuth ahụ, ma ọ bụ
- Gbaa OAuth ọzọ site na **Dashboard → Providers → Command Code → Reconnect**

### ModelScope na-eweghachi cooldowns 429 siri ike

**Ihe mgbaàmà:**

- Cooldowns dị mkpụmkpụ nke ukwuu ma ọ bụ nke na-amalite ozugbo na ModelScope mgbe e zigachara obere ìgwè arịrịọ
- Combo routing na-awụpụ ModelScope tupu oge a tụrụ anya ya

**Ihe kpatara ya:** ModelScope na-ewepụta headers `Retry-After` ndị akọwapụtara maka provider. v3.8.0 nwere njikwa pụrụ iche maka headers ndị ahụ, ya mere ụdị ndị ochie na-agụ ha n'ụzọ na-ezighi ezi dịka ntụziaka rate-limit nkịtị.

**Ndozi:**

- Gbaa mbọ hụ na ị nọ na v3.8.0 ma ọ bụ nke ka ọhụrụ
- Nyochaa na agbanyere toggle `useUpstream429BreakerHints` n'okpuru **Settings → Resilience**

### OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET adịghị na production

**Ihe mgbaàmà:**

- 401 na arịrịọ Codex/Responses WebSocket bridge ọ bụla mgbe a na-agba ya na remote production host
- WebSocket bridge handshake na-emechi ozugbo e jikọtara ya

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Env var `OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET` adịghị na production environment.

**Ndozi:**

1. Mepụta secret enweghị usoro: `openssl rand -hex 32`
2. Tọọ `OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET=<random-secret>` n'ime env nke production server (yana client ọ bụla na-ekwurịta okwu na bridge ahụ)
3. Malitegharịa OmniRoute

### Responses API: background mode belatara gaa na synchronous

**Ihe mgbaàmà:**

- Edere ịdọ aka ná ntị: `background mode degraded to synchronous`
- Arịrịọ `background: true` na-eweghachi azịza synchronous nkịtị kama background job handle

**Ihe kpatara ya:** v3.8.0 kpachaara anya mee ka `background: true` na Responses API rụọ ọrụ dịka synchronous execution ma na-ewepụta ịdọ aka ná ntị. Async background execution zuru ezu bụ ihe a ga-eweta n'ọdịnihu.

**Ndozi:**

- Hazie client ka ọ kpọọ na-enweghị `background`, ma ọ bụ
- Chere mwepụta ọzọ nke ga-eweta async background mode zuru ezu (soro changelog)

## Mmalite Nwayọ / Oge Ichere Njikere Agwụla

Ọ bụrụ na CLI ebipụta `⚠ Server did not respond within 60s` mana sava ahụ na-arụ ọrụ
n’ezie, oge e nyere nyocha njikere dị mkpụmkpụ karịa ihe gburugburu gị chọrọ.

Nke a na-emekarị na Windows (antivirus, ndị na-enyocha faịlụ sistemụ) ma ọ bụ n'ime containers
nwere nnukwu ibu ọrụ n'oge mmalite.

**Ndozi — bulie oge e nyere:**

```bash
# Site na env var (na-adịgide n’ofe mmalite):
export OMNIROUTE_READY_TIMEOUT_MS=180000   # nkeji 3
omniroute serve

# Site na ọkọlọtọ CLI (naanị otu oge):
omniroute serve --ready-timeout 180000
```

Ndabara bụ 60 000 ms (60 s). Ịdọ aka ná ntị ahụ bụ naanị maka inye ozi; sava ahụ
ga-aga n’ihu ịmalite n’azụ ma a ga-enwe ike iru ya ozugbo mmalite zuru ezu.

Lee [`docs/reference/ENVIRONMENT.md`](../reference/ENVIRONMENT.md) maka nkọwa zuru ezu
banyere `OMNIROUTE_READY_TIMEOUT_MS`.
