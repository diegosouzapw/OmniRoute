## Nchọpụta Nsogbu Zuru Ezu

---

### Ịmachi Ọnụego na Ndị Na-eweta Ọrụ Efu (429 / 400 / 401)

**Mgbaàmà**: Mgbe ị na-eji `model: "auto"` na ndị na-eweta ọrụ efu/ndị na-achọghị nkwenye njirimara (opencode, auggie, wdg.), ị na-enweta `HTTP 429`, `400`, ma ọ bụ `401` mgbe ụfọdụ kama nzaghachi. Arịrịọ ndị ahụ na-aga nke ọma mgbe a nwara otu prompt ahụ ọzọ obere oge ka e mesịrị, mana akpaaka (ọrụ cron, agents, scripts) na-akwụsị na ọdịda mbụ.

**Isi ihe kpatara ya**: Ụdị ọdịda atọ dị iche iche na-agbakọta:

1. **Oke ọnụego onye na-eweta ọrụ (`429`)**: Ọkwa efu nwere ike ịmanye okeọrụ n'ime oge a kara aka. Ọtụtụ oku emere n'otu oge na-emeju oke ahụ, ya mere a na-ajụ arịrịọ na-esote ruo mgbe windo oge ahụ malitegharịrị.
2. **Model mebiri emebi na passthrough (`400`/`401`)**: Pool `auto/*` nwere ike ịgụnye model passthrough sitere na `opencode` ndị edebanyere na katalọgụ mana enweghị ozi njirimara na-arụ ọrụ (dịka `oc/north-mini-code-free` → `401`). Auto-router na-anwale otu, ọ daa, a na-ebufe njehie ahụ tupu fallback amalite.
3. **Mmụba sitere na concurrency (`429` n'okpuru ibu ọrụ)**: Mgbe ọtụtụ session agent/cron na-akpọ `auto` n'otu oge, mkpokọta ọnụego arịrịọ na-agafe ihe ndị na-eweta ọrụ efu nwere ike ịnagide, ya mere a na-akara oku ziri ezi dịka nke na-emegbu usoro ahụ.

**Ndozi akwadoro (obodo ndị ọrụ kọrọ ya, 2026-08-10)**: hazie environment variables atọ ka rotation, concurrency, na fallback nwee ike ịnagide mgbanwe na ọgba aghara nke ọkwa efu kama ịkwụsị n'ihi ya:

```bash
export OMNIROUTE_ROTATE_ON_400=true           # gafee na model/onye na-eweta ọrụ ọzọ mgbe 400/401 mere (na-amafe model passthrough ndị mebiri emebi)
export OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT=4   # oke nnabata heavyweight akọwapụtara (anaghị edobe ya na ndabara: enweghị oke ọnụọgụ arịrịọ, lee ndetu n'okpuru)
export OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS=20000 # bulie oge nchere amachiri ka ọ gafee ndabara RATE_LIMIT_MAX_WAIT_MS maka upstream ndị na-adịghị ngwa
```

Tọọ ihe ndị a na environment nke process OmniRoute (daemon ahụ, dịka site na LaunchAgent plist ma ọ bụ `systemctl edit`), wee malitegharịa OmniRoute. Flag rotation bụ otu ihe kacha nwee mmetụta: ọ na-agbanwe ọdịda siri ike ka ọ bụrụ nnwale ọzọ a na-eme n'ụzọ doro anya megide onye na-eweta ọrụ dị mma nọ na pool ahụ.

**Ndetu**: `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT` na-amachi ọnụọgụ arịrịọ heavyweight — ndị nwere ogologo context — nwere ike ịrụ ọrụ n'otu oge; oke ahụ bụ ọnụ ụzọ nnabata, ọ bụghị ihe na-amachi ọnụego onye na-eweta ọrụ. **Mmelite #503-fanout:** anaghịzi edobe var a na ndabara (ugbu a, ọ na-arụ ọrụ naanị mgbe ahaziri ya kpọmkwem, dịka e gosiri n'elu) — kama nke ahụ, a na-achịkwa nnabata heavyweight site na byte budget ewepụtara na-akpaghị aka (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES`) nke na-agbanwe nha ya dabere na ezigbo oke memory nke host, ya mere deployment ọhụrụ kwesịrị ịhụ ajụjụ `503 chat_admission_busy` ole na ole karịa n'etinyeghị var a ma ọlị; idebe ya kpọmkwem ebe a ka na-arụ ọrụ kpọmkwem dịka e dere na documentation. Override byte-budget akọwapụtara na-amachi onwe ha n'etiti 8 MiB–2 GiB. `413 body_exceeds_budget` abụghị ihe na-adịru nwa oge: bulie byte budget ahụ, belata `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_HARD_MAX_BODY_BYTES`, ma ọ bụ bulie oke memory nke process ahụ. Mwepụ `inflight_bytes_budget` bụ asọmpi akụnụba na-adịru nwa oge, a ka nwekwara ike ịnwa ya ọzọ. A na-achịkwa mmachi ọnụego nke onye na-eweta ọrụ ọ bụla (`open-sse/services/rateLimitManager.ts`) iche site na `RATE_LIMIT_MAX_WAIT_MS`, `RATE_LIMIT_MAX_QUEUE_DEPTH`, na `RATE_LIMIT_AUTO_ENABLE` — lee `.env.example`.

**Otu esi achọpụta na ọ rụrụ ọrụ**: mee ka agent/cron gị gbaa ọsọ ugboro abụọ n’usoro ngwa ngwa ma chọpụta na ha abụọ gara nke ọma. Tupu ndozi ahụ, ọsọ nke abụọ na-ewepụtakarị `429`/`401`. Mgbe ndozi ahụ gasịrị, a na-anwale ọdịda (ọ bụrụ na e nwere nke ọ bụla) ọzọ n’enweghị ihe onye ọrụ ga-ahụ, oku ahụ wee gwụchaa. Ị nwekwara ike iji `curl /monitoring/health` wee leba anya n’oghere `rateLimitedUntil` dị na njikọ ndị na-eweta ọrụ yana `circuitBreakers.providerBreakers[].state` maka ndị na-eweta ọrụ metụtara — ọnọdụ ahụ bụ otu n’ime `CLOSED`, `DEGRADED`, `OPEN`, ma ọ bụ `HALF_OPEN` (lee `src/shared/utils/circuitBreaker.ts`), onye na-eweta ọrụ nke na-anọgide na-ada ga-agbanwe site na `CLOSED → DEGRADED → OPEN` tupu oge nrụgharị ahụ ekwe ka nnwale gafee (`HALF_OPEN`).

**Ọ bụrụ na ị ka na-ahụ 429**: akaụntụ na-arụ ọrụ maka onye na-eweta ọrụ ahụ agwụla _quota_ ya n’ezie (ọ bụghị naanị oke ọsọ arịrịọ). Tinye akaụntụ nke abụọ maka otu onye na-eweta ọrụ ahụ na dashboard OmniRoute → Ndị na-eweta ọrụ → Akaụntụ, ma ọ bụ tinye onye ọzọ na-eweta ọrụ n’efu (dịka `routeway`, `auggie`). Ịtụgharị akaụntụ na-enyere naanị aka maka nsogbu nwa oge nke oke ọsọ/400/401; mgbe _quota_ gwụchara kpamkpam, a chọrọ credential nke abụọ ma ọ bụ onye na-eweta ọrụ ọzọ.

**Ọ bụrụ na ị hụ 403 na ụdị vision (`auto/vision`, `bazaarlink/*`)**: akaụntụ ejikọrọ enweghị atụmatụ akwụ ụgwọ nke gụnyere vision, ma ọ bụ API key ahụ enweghị ikikere zuru ezu. Nyochaa na dashboard onye na-eweta ọrụ na scope nke key ahụ gụnyere vision/multimodal, ma ọ bụ jikọọ akaụntụ nke atụmatụ akwụ ụgwọ ma debe ya dịka ebe a na-eziga arịrịọ vision.

## Ịdọ aka ná ntị npm install (ERESOLVE / peer / deprecated)

Mgbe ị na-agba `npm install -g omniroute`, ị nwere ike ịhụ ọtụtụ ịdọ aka ná ntị dịka `npm warn ERESOLVE`, ọkwa gbasara peer-dependency, na ozi `deprecated`. **A na-atụ anya ha, ha adịghịkwa emerụ ihe ọ bụla.** Nrụnye gị gara nke ọma ma ọ bụrụ na ị hụ `added <N> packages` na mmepụta ahụ.

Iji gbochie ịdọ aka ná ntị gbasara idozi peer-dependency, jiri usoro nrụnye OmniRoute na-akwado:

```bash
npm install -g omniroute --legacy-peer-deps
```

`--legacy-peer-deps` na-egbochi naanị `ERESOLVE` na ọkwa peer-dependency. Ọkwa deprecation ka ga-apụta n'ihi na ha sitere na ngwugwu ndị ọzọ nke nrụnye ahụ dabere na ha; ha anaghị egosi na nrụnye ahụ dara.

Ịdọ aka ná ntị ndị ahụ sitere na oke peer-dependency merela ochie dị na ngwugwu ndị ọzọ OmniRoute anaghị achịkwa:

1. **`marked-terminal` chọrọ `marked >=1 <16`, ma achọtara `marked@18`** — ọ na-arụ ọrụ nke ọma n'eziokwu; naanị ihe bụ na oke peer nke upstream emela ochie.
2. **`deprecated prebuild-install@7.1.3`** — ngwa enyemaka nke dependency ọzọ na-eji ebudata faịlụ binary native. A naghị
   eji ya etinye binding transport `wreq-js` e kpebiri ụdị ya, ọ naghịkwa egosi na nhazi transport nke onye na-enye web-cookie
   dara.

**Ọ dịghị ihe ị ga-eme** — enweghị ike imechi ịdọ aka ná ntị ndị ahụ kpamkpam na-enweghị ime fork nke ngwugwu upstream.

## Ịdọ aka ná ntị na-egosi na egbochiri npm install-scripts (nrụnye global)

<a name="npm-install-scripts-blocked-warning-global-install"></a>

Mgbe ị na-agba `npm install -g omniroute`, ụdị npm ndị ọhụrụ (v11+ na v12+) na-egbochi package lifecycle scripts na ndabara ma gosipụta ịdọ aka ná ntị install-scripts:

```
npm warn install-scripts Egbochiri install scripts nke ngwugwu 7 n'ihi na allowScripts anaghị ekpuchi ha:
  omniroute@<version> (postinstall: node scripts/build/postinstall.mjs)
  keytar, @parcel/watcher, @swc/core, protobufjs, esbuild, onnxruntime-node
```

### Enwere ike ileghara ịdọ aka ná ntị a anya?

**N'akụkụ ụfọdụ, mana a naghị atụ aro ka e jiri ya rụọ ọrụ na-enweghị scripts.** OmniRoute nwere fallback ndị na-arụ ọrụ nke ọma: ọ bụrụ na enweghị ike ibunye binding native `better-sqlite3` site na standalone bundle, ngwa ahụ ga-eji driver ndị ọzọ dịka `node:sqlite` ma ọ bụ `sql.js` (lee [Mkpebi SQLite Runtime](../ops/SQLITE_RUNTIME.md)). Server ahụ ka ga-amalite, mana iji WASM fallback dịka `sql.js` arụ ọrụ na-eme ka ọnọdụ SQLite WAL (Write-Ahead Logging) kwụsị ma na-abawanye ibu ebe nchekwa na disk-sync.

### Kedu scripts ndị dị mkpa n'ezie?

Ọ bụghị scripts niile e gbochiri dị mkpa iji mee ka OmniRoute rụọ ọrụ:

- **Ndị dị mkpa (`omniroute`, `better-sqlite3`):** OmniRoute na-arụ ọrụ site na Next.js standalone application bundle (`dist/`). N'oge nrụnye, npm na-ebudata ma ọ bụ na-achịkọta native SQLite driver nke dabara na host n'ime `node_modules/better-sqlite3/` nke ọkwa kachasị elu. Script `postinstall` nke OmniRoute (`scripts/build/postinstall.mjs`) na-enyochazị ma na-edepụta binary ndị a dabara na host n'ime `dist/node_modules/` ka standalone server nwee ike ịnweta ha. Ọ bụrụ na egbochie postinstall script nke `omniroute`, a ga-awụpụ nzọụkwụ ndegharị a.
- **Ndị nhọrọ/transitive (`keytar`, `@parcel/watcher`, `@swc/core`, `protobufjs`, `esbuild`):** Ndị a bụ ngwa enyemaka maka build, ngwa watcher, ma ọ bụ ngwa system keychain. Ikwe ka ha rụọ ọrụ na-egbochi ịdọ aka ná ntị build, mana ha adịghị mkpa maka ọrụ routing ndị bụ isi.
- **Nke ewepụrụ (`onnxruntime-node`):** Ọ bụ dependency nhọrọ maka local AI embeddings, ọ **dịghịkwa** mkpa ka OmniRoute rụọ ọrụ. Postinstall script ya na-ebudata akụ CUDA ruru ihe dịka 300MB site na NuGet; etinyela ya na allow-list ma ọ bụrụ na i bughị n'obi iji local ONNX acceleration na hardware a na-akwado.

### Ndozi a tụrụ aro

Iji kwe ka install scripts ndị dị mkpa rụọ ọrụ n'oge nrụnye global ma mechie ịdọ aka ná ntị, nyefee `--allow-scripts` maka OmniRoute na native dependencies ya:

```bash
npm install -g omniroute --allow-scripts=omniroute,better-sqlite3,keytar,@parcel/watcher,@swc/core,protobufjs,esbuild
```

N'aka nke ọzọ, iji hazie nke a ka ọ dịgide maka nrụnye global niile na akaụntụ onye ọrụ gị:

```bash
npm config set allow-scripts=omniroute,better-sqlite3,keytar,@parcel/watcher,@swc/core,protobufjs,esbuild --location=user
npm install -g omniroute
```

### Nyochaa nrụnye gị

Mgbe nrụnye gasịrị, gbaa ngwa diagnostic arụnyere n'ime ya iji hụ na native bindings na gburugburu runtime dị mma:

```bash
omniroute doctor
```

## Nchọpụta Ụgha nke Antivirus

<a name="antivirus-false-positives"></a>

### Avast/AVG na-etinye `README.md` na kwarantaini dịka `MD:HttpRequest-inf[Susp]`

**Nke a bụ nchọpụta ụgha. Ọ dịghị ihe butere nje, ọ dịghịkwa ihe a chọrọ ka eme.**

Avast na AVG na-eji usoro heuristic nke na-akanye faịlụ ederede nkịtị/Markdown nwere ọtụtụ
njikọ ndị yiri arịrịọ HTTP. `README.md` nke OmniRoute so n'ime ngwugwu npm (e depụtara ya
na `package.json` → `files`), ya mere, mgbe echichi zuru ụwa ọnụ gasịrị, ọ na-adị na
`node_modules/omniroute/README.md` — ọ nwekwara ihe atụ `http://localhost:20128/...` ruru ~15
(ebe njedebe MCP HTTP/SSE, URL A2A `.well-known`, na iberibe `curl`). Ọnụọgụ njikọ ndị ahụ
ezuola ime ka heuristic ahụ nye mkpu.

Ọ bụrụ na nke a malitere naanị n'oge na-adịbeghị anya: ụdị faịlụ ahụ agbanwebeghị. Tebụl ebe
njedebe dị na README bawanyere (agbakwunyere MCP HTTP + SSE + A2A), tinyekwa ọtụtụ ihe atụ
`curl`, nke mere ka ọ gafee oke ahụ.

Faịlụ ahụ bụ naanị akwụkwọ nkọwa na-adịghị arụ ọrụ, na-enweghị ọdịnaya ọ bụla a pụrụ imepụta.
Ị nwere ike iweghachi ya na kwarantaini n'enweghị nsogbu.

**Ihe ị ga-eme:**

1. **Kwụsị ọkwa ndị ahụ** — wepụ ndekọ echichi ahụ n'ihe antivirus gị na-enyocha
   (Avast: Settings → Exceptions), site n'ịgbakwunye ụzọ `node_modules` zuru ụwa ọnụ gị
   na/ma ọ bụ ndekọ data OmniRoute (`~/.omniroute/`).
2. **Kọọrọ ha nchọpụta ụgha ahụ** — <https://www.avast.com/false-positive-file-form.php>,
   ma tinye `README.md` e debere na kwarantaini. Nke a bụ ndozi ga-enyere mmadụ niile aka,
   ebe ọ bụ heuristic nke onye nrụpụta ahụ na-emeghachi omume gabiga ókè nye faịlụ ederede.

**Ihe mere anyị anaghị “edozi” nke a n'akụkụ anyị:** ihe atụ niile bụ `http://localhost`,
localhost enweghịkwa ike ịbụ `https` n'enweghị nsogbu asambodo nke onwe. Ịgbanwe akwụkwọ
nkowapụta ahụ iji gbanahụ heuristic nke otu onye nrụpụta ga-emerụ onye ọ bụla na-agụ ya
naanị iji meeju ahụhụ dị na nyocha ahụ afọ.

### Kaspersky na-akanye ngwa Desktop dịka `PDM:Trojan.Win32.Generic`

**Nke a bụ nchọpụta ụgha sitere na heuristic omume. Ọ dịghị ihe butere nje.** Prefix `PDM:`
nke Kaspersky pụtara na mkpebi ahụ sitere na Proactive Defense Module (System Watcher) ya,
nke na-ekpebi ihe installer ahụ _na-eme_ kama iji ya tụnyere malware ndị a maara. Mgbe ọ
nyere mkpu, Kaspersky na-“eweghachi azụ” echichi ahụ dum — na-ehichapụ faịlụ ndị o deela —
ya mere ngwa ahụ na-emesị mebie ma ọ bụ funahụ.

Faịlụ ndị ọ na-akanye bụ akụkụ nkịtị nke ndabere open-source ndị ekwuputara, nke esonyere na
ngwa desktop ahụ, dịka ọmụmaatụ:

- `resources/app/.build/next/node_modules/playwright-<hash>/lib/…/agentParser.js` na
  `workerProcessEntry.js` — [Playwright](https://playwright.dev), ọbaakwụkwọ otomeshọn
  ihe nchọgharị eji maka nbanye provider n'ime ngwa na nkata na-adabere n'ihe nchọgharị.
- `resources/app/.build/next/node_modules/@wreq-js/binding-win32-<arch>-msvc-<hash>/wreq-js.win32-<arch>-msvc.node`
  — njikọ native `wreq-js` akpọchiri na ụdị a kapịrị ọnụ, nke eji maka HTTP nwere akara
  mkpịsịaka ihe nchọgharị na provider ndị na-eji kuki webụ (`<arch>` bụ `x64` ma ọ bụ `arm64`).

**Ihe mere o ji enye mkpu:** installer Windows ahụ **enwebeghị mbinye aka koodu**, ya mere
installer NSIS na-enweghị mbinye aka enweghị aha ọma ọ bụla, heuristics omume na-arụkwa ọrụ
n'ụzọ kacha sie ike. Mgbe nke a jikọtara na DLL native esonyere na ya na ọtụtụ narị faịlụ
`.js` e dere n'okpuru `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\OmniRoute` (gụnyere ndekọ ngwugwu nwere hash
na njedebe sitere na build standalone nke Next.js), nke ahụ ezuola ime ka heuristic ahụ nye
mkpu. A na-eme atụmatụ itinye mbinye aka koodu; ruo mgbe e mere ya, mwepụta ọhụrụ nwere ike
ime ka nke a mee ọzọ.

**Ihe ị ga-eme:**

1. **Buru ụzọ nyochaa nbudata gị** (nke a ga-ewepụ ohere na e metụgharịrị faịlụ ahụ). Mwepụta
   ọ bụla na-ebipụta `latest.yml`, nke mpaghara `sha512` ya (base64) na-ekpuchi installer
   `OmniRoute.Setup.<version>.exe`. Na PowerShell, site na folda nwere installer ahụ:
   ```powershell
   $b = [System.Security.Cryptography.SHA512]::Create().ComputeHash(
     [System.IO.File]::ReadAllBytes("$PWD\OmniRoute.Setup.<version>.exe"))
   [Convert]::ToBase64String($b)
   ```
   Nsonaazụ ahụ ga-adabarịrị na `latest.yml` → `sha512`. Ọ bụrụ na ọ dabaraghị, hichapụ faịlụ
   ahụ ma budata ya ọzọ naanị site na [ibe mwepụta GitHub](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/releases).
2. **Weghachi + wepụ n'ihe a na-enyocha** — weghachi ihe ndị a weghachiri azụ site na
   kwarantaini ma gbakwunye mwepu maka `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\OmniRoute` (Kaspersky →
   Settings → Threats and Exclusions), wee wụnye ya ọzọ.
3. **Kọọrọ ha nchọpụta ụgha ahụ** — <https://opentip.kaspersky.com/>. Akụkọ FP ndị
   ndị ọrụ zitere na-eme ka itinye ya na ndepụta ihe a na-anabata mee ngwa ngwa n'ezie.

## Ndakọrịta Node.js

<a name="nodejs-compatibility"></a>

### Ihu nbanye na-akwụsị ịrụ ọrụ ma ọ bụ na-egosi njehie "Module self-registration"

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Ị na-eji ụdị Node.js nke na-anọghị n’ogo runtime echedoro OmniRoute kwadoro. Ihe na-emekarị bụ iji ụdị patch Node 22 ma ọ bụ 24 ochie nke dị n’okpuru ogo nchekwa emezigharịrị OmniRoute chọrọ.

**Ihe mgbaàmà:**

- Ihu nbanye na-egosi ihuenyo efu ma ọ bụ njehie sava
- Console na-egosi `Error: Module did not self-register` ma ọ bụ njehie native binding yiri ya
- Ihu nbanye na-egosi **ọkwa ịdọ aka ná ntị oroma** nke nwere ụdị Node gị ma ọ bụrụ na runtime ahụ anọghị n’usoro nchekwa akwadoro

**Ndozi:**

1. Wụnye ụdị Node.js LTS akwadoro (nke a tụrụ aro: Node.js 24.x):
   ```bash
   nvm install 24
   nvm use 24
   ```
2. Nyochaa ụdị gị: `node --version` kwesịrị igosi `v24.0.0` ma ọ bụ nke ka ọhụrụ n’ahịrị 24.x LTS
3. Wụnye OmniRoute ọzọ: `npm install -g omniroute`
4. Malitegharịa: `omniroute`

> **Ụdị echedoro akwadoro:** `>=22.22.2 <23` ma ọ bụ `>=24.0.0 <27`. A na-akwado Node.js 24.x LTS (Krypton) na Node.js 26 n’uju.

### npm v11+: awụnyeghị `better-sqlite3` (Cannot find module)

<a name="npm-v11-better-sqlite3-not-installed-cannot-find-module"></a>

**Ihe kpatara ya:** npm v11 (nke na-eso Node.js 24+) na-egbochi scripts nrụnye maka dependencies nhọrọ
na ndabara. Ebe ọ bụ na edepụtara `better-sqlite3` na `optionalDependencies`
ma ọ chọrọ native compilation (`node-gyp rebuild`), npm na-awụpụ ya n’enweghị ọkwa.

**Ihe mgbaàmà:**

- Sava na-akwụsị ịrụ ọrụ mgbe ọ na-amalite, na-egosi `Cannot find module 'better-sqlite3'`
- `ls node_modules/better-sqlite3` na-egosi "No such file or directory"
- `npm ls better-sqlite3` na-egosi `(empty)`

**Ndozi:**

1. Kwado scripts nrụnye ma wụnye ya ọzọ:
   ```bash
   npm approve-scripts better-sqlite3
   npm install
   ```
2. Ma ọ bụ jiri aka wụnye prebuilt ahụ:
   ```bash
   npm pack better-sqlite3@13.0.1
   tar -xzf better-sqlite3-*.tgz -C node_modules
   mv node_modules/package node_modules/better-sqlite3
   rm better-sqlite3-*.tgz
   ```
3. Nyochaa na ọ na-arụ ọrụ: `node -e "require('better-sqlite3')(':memory:').close(); console.log('OK')"`

### macOS: `dlopen` / "slice is not valid mach-o file"

<a name="macos-native-module-rebuild"></a>

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Mgbe emechara `npm install -g omniroute` zuru ụwa ọnụ, enwere ike na a chịkọtara native binary `better-sqlite3` dị n’ime package ahụ maka architecture ma ọ bụ Node.js ABI dị iche na nke na-arụ ọrụ na ngwaọrụ mpaghara ahụ. **Ihe mgbaàmà:**

**Ndozi — wughachi ya maka gburugburu mpaghara gị (ọ dịghị mkpa iweda ụdị Node.js):**

## Nsogbu Proxy

<a name="proxy-issues"></a>

### Nnyocha nkwado onye na-eweta ọrụ na-egosi "fetch failed"

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Ebe njedebe maka inyocha igodo API (`POST /api/providers/validate`) na-agafeghị nhazi proxy na mbụ, nke mere ka ọdịda mee na gburugburu ebe achọrọ ka e si na proxy ziga arịrịọ.

**Ndozi (v3.5.5+):** Edozila nke a ugbu a. Ụzọ nnyocha nkwado onye na-eweta ọrụ na-agafe na `runWithProxyContext`, ma na-asọpụrụ ntọala proxy nke onye na-eweta ọrụ na nke zuru ụwa ọnụ na-akpaghị aka.

### Nnyocha ọnọdụ token dara site na "fetch failed"

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Mmelite token OAuth nke na-eme n'azụ anaghị achọpụta nhazi proxy maka njikọ ọ bụla.

**Ndozi (v3.5.5+):** Onye nhazi oge maka nnyocha ọnọdụ token na-achọpụtazi nhazi proxy maka njikọ ọ bụla tupu ọ nwaa imelite ya. Melite gaa na v3.5.5+.

### Proxy SOCKS5 na-eweghachi "invalid onRequestStart method"

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Na Node.js 22, dispatcher nke undici@8 adabaghị na mmejuputa `fetch()` arụnyere n'ime Node.

**Ndozi (v3.5.5+):** OmniRoute na-ejizi ọrụ `fetch()` nke undici n'onwe ya mgbe dispatcher proxy na-arụ ọrụ, iji hụ na omume ya na-adị otu. Melite gaa na v3.5.5+.

### Proxy MITM n'okpuru WSL: a naghị ejide ngwa desktọpụ dị na Windows host

**Ihe kpatara ya:** A na-etinye proxy MITM na asambodo CA ya n'ime gburugburu ebe OmniRoute na-arụ ọrụ. N'okpuru WSL, gburugburu ahụ bụ Linux guest, ebe ngwa desktọpụ AI (Kiro, Trae, Copilot, Zed, …) na-arụ ọrụ na Windows host. Ngwa ndị dị na host anaghị atụkwasị ebe nchekwa asambodo nke guest obi, ha anaghịkwa agafe na proxy sistemụ nke guest, ya mere njide okporo ụzọ desktọpụ anaghị arụ ọrụ ebe ahụ.

**Nkwado:** Gbaa OmniRoute ọsọ ozugbo n'otu OS ahụ ngwa desktọpụ ịchọrọ ijide na-arụ ọrụ na ya (Windows maka ngwa Windows; otu a kwa maka macOS/Linux). Idobe OmniRoute n'ime WSL mgbe ị na-elekwasị anya na ngwa host chọrọ ka i jiri aka mee ka Windows host tụkwasị asambodo CA emepụtara obi, ma tụọ ntọala netwọk/proxy nke ngwa host ọ bụla gaa na ebe njedebe proxy WSL — nhazi a anaghị enweta nkwado ma dịkwa mfe imebi.

## Nsogbu Docker

### Docker IPv6 / Ntọgharị Njikọ

<a name="docker-ipv6"></a>

**Mgbaàmà:** `curl http://localhost:20128/v1/models` na-eweghachi `curl: (56) Recv failure: Connection reset by peer`. Dashboard na endpoint ndị na-achọghị authentication na-arụ ọrụ, mana endpoint ndị chọrọ authentication anaghị arụ ọrụ — ọ na-adị ka nsogbu auth, mana ọ bụghị.

**Ihe kpatara ya:** `docker run -p 20128:20128` na-ebipụta na `0.0.0.0` (IPv4) na `::` (IPv6), mana process dị n'ime container na-ege ntị naanị na IPv4. N'elu host ebe `localhost` na-ebu ụzọ resolve gaa na `::1`, njikọ ahụ na-erute port IPv6 e bipụtara nke na-enweghị listener n'azụ ya → a na-emegharị njikọ ahụ.

**Ndozi:**

2. **Ndozi na-adịgide adịgide:** Kee ya na IPv4 kpọmkwem site n'iji `-p 127.0.0.1:20128:20128` n'iwu `docker run` gị:
   ```bash
   docker run -d --name omniroute --restart unless-stopped --stop-timeout 40 \
     -p 127.0.0.1:20128:20128 -v omniroute-data:/app/data diegosouzapw/omniroute:latest
   ```
   Nke a na-amanye IPv4 bind ma na-egbochikwa ikpughe proxy ahụ na interface niile nke host.

## Ntọala Nkwụsi Ike

### Mmachi ọsọ akpaaka anaghị amalite ọrụ

- Mmachi ọsọ akpaaka na-emetụta naanị ndị na-eweta ọrụ ji igodo API (ọ bụghị OAuth/ndebanye aha)
- Chọpụta na agbanyere mmachi ọsọ akpaaka na **Settings → Resilience → Provider Profiles**
- Lelee ma onye na-eweta ọrụ ọ na-eweghachi koodu ọnọdụ `429` ma ọ bụ nkụnyeisi `Retry-After`

### Ịhazigharị exponential backoff

Profaịlụ ndị na-eweta ọrụ na-akwado ntọala ndị a:

- **Nkwụsị ntọala** — Oge nchere mbụ mgbe ọdịda mbụ gasịrị (ndabara: 1s)
- **Nkwụsị kachasị** — Oke kachasị nke oge nchere (ndabara: 30s)
- **Ihe mmụba** — Ogo a ga-eji mụbaa nkwụsị maka ọdịda ọ bụla na-esochi ibe ya (ndabara: 2x)

### Mgbochi ìgwè na-ebili n'otu oge

Mgbe ọtụtụ arịrịọ ndị na-aga n'otu oge rutere onye na-eweta ọrụ nwere mmachi ọsọ, OmniRoute na-eji mutex + mmachi ọsọ akpaaka ahazi arịrịọ n'usoro ma gbochie ọdịda na-agbasawanye. Nke a na-eme na-akpaghị aka maka ndị na-eweta ọrụ ji igodo API.

### Arịrịọ nkata na-ada na 503 / chat_admission_busy

**Mgbaàmà:**

- Ebe njedebe mmecha nkata na-eweghachi nzaghachi `503` enwere ike ịnwale ọzọ nke koodu njehie ya bụ
  `chat_admission_busy`.
- Nzaghachi ahụ gụnyere `Retry-After`. Kemgbe #12135, a na-enweta uru ahụ site na njupụta a hụrụ
  — nke ka ukwuu n'etiti windo `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS` nke arịrịọ ahụ cherelarị
  na oge ejigidere mgbazinye dị arọ ugbu a — a na-ebuli ya ruo sekọnd zuru ezu
  ma machie ya na 60. N'ọnụ ụzọ na-enweghị ọrụ, ọ na-edobe oke ala akụkọ ihe mere eme: sekọnd 2 n'ụzọ
  dabere na byte, sekọnd 1 n'ụzọ dabere na nhazi (nke na-agụnyekwa
  `reason: "structure_limit"`).
- Nke a nwere ike ime mgbe nkata ọzọ dị arọ ma ọ bụ nzaghachi mgbasa na-ewe ogologo oge ka
  na-aga n'ihu.

Ahụ nzaghachi dabere na byte bụ:

```json
{
  "error": {
    "message": "Chat admission capacity is temporarily unavailable. Retry shortly.",
    "type": "server_error",
    "code": "chat_admission_busy"
  }
}
```

Nzaghachi dabere na nhazi na-eji otu ụdị na koodu ahụ, yana ozi
`Local chat admission capacity is busy for this structurally heavy request; upstream provider routing was not attempted. Retry shortly.`
na `reason: "structure_limit"`.
Na oke ndabara, arịrịọ na-adị arọ n'ụzọ nhazi mgbe ọ nwere opekata mpe ozi `200`,
opekata mpe ngwaọrụ `64`, ma ọ bụ opekata mpe token `32,000` e mere atụmatụ, ma ọ bụ mgbe atụmatụ nhazi nwere oke
gwụsịrị oke ya nke ọnụ e letara `10,000` ma ọ bụ omimi `12`.

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Nke a bụ mbelata ibu e mere ụma n'ime OmniRoute, ọ bụghị ọdịda sitere n'aka onye na-eweta ọrụ dị n'elu.
Usoro ọ bụla na-eji ihe nche mpaghara-usoro idowe ikike dị arọ nwere oke tupu ijigide
ma tụgharịa ahụ arịrịọ buru ibu. A na-ejide mgbazinye dị arọ n'oge ndụ niile nke nzaghachi SSE.

**#503-fanout:** tupu ndozi a, ihe nche ahụ na-amachi ọnụ ọgụgụ arịrịọ na-aga n'otu oge n'ọnụọgụ arịrịọ edoziri
(`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT`, ndabara `1`) n'agbanyeghị ebe nchekwa nke igwe nnabata, ya mere mgbasa
coding-agent (ọtụtụ subagents/CLIs, ahụ arịrịọ na-adịkarị > 256 KB) dara ruo ikike
ịrụkọ ọrụ n'otu oge nke ihe dịka 1 ma nye 503 n'okpuru ibu nkịtị kpamkpam. Ugbu a ihe nche ahụ na-edozi onwe ya: a na-achịkwa ya
site na mmefu BYTE mbubata a na-enweta na-akpaghị aka (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES`) nke ahaziri site na
oke ebe nchekwa n'ezie nke usoro ahụ, ọ na-enyochakwa mgbaàmà nrụgide akụrụngwa dị ndụ — ya mere ọ
na-ebelata ibu naanị mgbe igwe nnabata nọ n'ezie n'okpuru nrụgide ebe nchekwa, ọ bụghị naanị n'ihi na ihe karịrị otu
arịrịọ dị arọ bịara n'otu oge. A ka na-asọpụrụ oke ọnụọgụ ochie (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT`),
mana naanị ma ọ bụrụ na i setịpụrụ ya n'ụzọ doro anya.

Mgbe ikike jupụtara, arịrịọ dị arọ na-ebu ụzọ chere ruo
`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS` (ndabara ya bụ `RATE_LIMIT_MAX_WAIT_MS`; `0` na-ewepụ nchere ahụ) ka oghere tọhapụ
tupu ọ zaa `503` enwere ike ịnwale ọzọ. Nchere nwere oke ahụ dị ka ndị ahịa ụdị agent
(OpenCode, Claude Code, Cursor) ndị na-agbasa arịrịọ nta dị arọ n'otu oge nwee ike ịhazi mgbawa ahụ n'usoro
kama imefu mmefu nnwale ọzọ ha niile n'ọjụjụ ozugbo wee kwụsị n'etiti ọrụ.
A na-egosi njupụta mgbazinye dị arọ ugbu a, mmefu byte e kpebiri, na ogo nrụgide dị ndụ
na `GET /api/monitoring/health` → `chatAdmission` (`inflightBytes`, `maxInflightBytes`,
`budgetSource`, `pressureSeverity`, `countCapEnabled`) — lelee ndị a tupu imetụ env var ọ bụla aka.
Settings → Resilience → Request Queue → Concurrent Requests anaghị achịkwa nke a; ntọala ahụ
na-achị usoro kwụ dị iche maka arịrịọ onye na-eweta ọrụ.

**Ndozi:**

1. Buru ụzọ nwaa ọzọ. Ndị ahịa kwesịrị ịkwanyere `Retry-After` ùgwù ma jiri backoff kama ịmegharị
   arịrịọ ahụ ozugbo.
2. Lelee `/api/monitoring/health` → `chatAdmission` tupu ịhazigharị ihe ọ bụla. `countCapEnabled:
false` na `maxInflightBytes` sara mbara pụtara na mmefu e nwetara na-akpaghị aka na-arụlarị
   ọrụ ya; `pressureSeverity` nke `high`/`critical` pụtara na igwe nnabata nwere ebe nchekwa dị ala n'ezie —
   enweghị ike idozi nke ahụ site na env var nnabata; ọ chọrọ RAM karịa ma ọ bụ ibu ọrụ pere mpe.
3. Naanị ma ọ bụrụ na `/api/monitoring/health` gosiri na mmefu e nwetara na-akpaghị aka pere mpe n'ezie maka
   igwe nnabata gị (ọ na-adịkarị ụkọ — ọ na-abaworị site na container ruo bare-metal), jiri
   `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES` gbanwee ya ozugbo kama ịlaghachi n'oke ọnụọgụ arịrịọ ochie.

Hụ [ntụaka mgbanwe gburugburu](../reference/ENVIRONMENT.md#4-security--authentication)
maka ntọala nnabata ndị bụ isi.

## Nhazi nsogbu ọdịda RAG / LLM a na-ahọrọ iji (nsogbu 16)

Ụfọdụ ndị ọrụ OmniRoute na-etinye gateway ahụ n'ihu ngwugwu RAG ma ọ bụ agent. N'ime nhazi ndị ahụ, a na-ahụkarị usoro dị ịtụnanya: OmniRoute yiri ka ọ dị mma (ndị provider na-arụ ọrụ, profaịlụ routing dị mma, enweghị ọkwa ịdọ aka ná ntị maka oke arịrịọ), mana azịza ikpeazụ ka na-ezighi ezi.

N'omume, nsogbu ndị a na-esikarị na pipeline RAG dị n'okpuru pụta, ọ bụghị na gateway n'onwe ya.

Ọ bụrụ na ịchọrọ okwu ndị otu niile ga-eji kọwaa ọdịda ndị ahụ, ị nwere ike iji WFGY ProblemMap, akụ ederede mpụga nwere ikike MIT nke na-akọwa usoro ọdịda RAG / LLM iri na isii na-eme ugboro ugboro. N'ozuzu, ọ na-ekpuchi:

- ndahie retrieval na ókè context mebiri emebi
- index na vector store ndị tọgbọ chakoo ma ọ bụ nke ochie
- enweghị ndakọrịta n'etiti embedding na semantics
- nsogbu nchịkọta prompt na context window
- ndakpọ logic na azịza nwere ntụkwasị obi gabigara ókè
- ọdịda n'agbụ ogologo na nhazi agent
- ndahie ebe nchekwa na ọrụ n'etiti ọtụtụ agent
- nsogbu deployment na usoro bootstrap

Echiche ya dị mfe:

1. Mgbe ị na-enyocha azịza ọjọọ, dekọọ:
   - ọrụ na arịrịọ onye ọrụ
   - njikọta route ma ọ bụ provider dị na OmniRoute
   - context RAG ọ bụla ejiri n'okpuru (akwụkwọ ewepụtara, oku tool, wdg.)
2. Tinye nsogbu ahụ n'okpuru otu ma ọ bụ nọmba WFGY ProblemMap abụọ (`No.1` … `No.16`).
3. Chekwaa nọmba ahụ na dashboard, runbook, ma ọ bụ incident tracker nke gị n'akụkụ ndekọ OmniRoute.
4. Jiri ibe WFGY kwekọrọ ekwekọ kpebie ma ị kwesịrị ịgbanwe ngwugwu RAG, retriever, ma ọ bụ atụmatụ routing gị.

Ederede zuru ezu na usoro omume doro anya dị ebe a (ikike MIT, naanị ederede):

[README WFGY ProblemMap](https://github.com/onestardao/WFGY/blob/main/ProblemMap/README.md)

Ị nwere ike ileghara ngalaba a anya ma ọ bụrụ na ị naghị arụ pipeline RAG ma ọ bụ agent n'azụ OmniRoute.

## Nsogbu Ndị A Maara na v3.8.0

Nsogbu ndị metụtara kpọmkwem mwepụta v3.8.0 na ụzọ ngafe ha dị ugbu a. Ọ bụrụ na e tinye ndozi na patch e mesịrị, a ga-emelite ma ọ bụ wepụ ndenye ahụ.

### Ọdịda auth nke Devin CLI

**Mgbaàmà:**

- "Ahụghị Devin CLI" ma ọ bụ "auth dara" mgbe a na-akpọ tool ndị Devin na-akwado
- Nnyocha runtime nke CLI na-akọ `installed=false`

**Ihe kpatara ya:**

- `CLI_DEVIN_BIN` na-atụ aka na path na-adịghị adị
- Awụnyebeghị Devin CLI na host ahụ

**Ndozi:**

1. Wụnye Devin CLI maka platform gị
2. Tọọ `CLI_DEVIN_BIN=/usr/local/bin/devin` (ma ọ bụ path ziri ezi) na `.env`
3. Malitegharịa OmniRoute ma nwalee ọzọ site na **Dashboard → Ngwaọrụ CLI**

### Cooldown model rapaara (nrụpụta ọhụrụ aka)

**Mgbaàmà:**

- A ka na-edepụta model dị ka ọ nọ na cooldown ọbụna mgbe oge ngwụcha gafere
- Arịrịọ ka na-amafe model ahụ na combo routing n'agbanyeghị na timestamp ahụ bụ nke gara aga

**Nrụpụta ọhụrụ aka:**

- **Dashboard:** **Ntọala → Cooldown Model** → pịa **Mee ka ọ rụọ ọrụ ọzọ** na kaadị emetụtara
- **API:** `DELETE /api/resilience/model-cooldowns` tinyere header auth nchịkwa

### Njikọ provider Command Code dara na 403

**Mgbaàmà:**

- 403 mgbe a na-anwale njikọ provider Command Code
- Kaadị provider na-egosi "enweghị ikike" mgbe agbakwunyere ya ọhụrụ

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Usoro OAuth emechaghị (anabataghị callback ma ọ bụ echekwaghị token).

**Ndozi:**

- Gbaa `omniroute providers` site na CLI iji kpalite usoro OAuth ọzọ, ma ọ bụ
- Gbaa OAuth ọzọ site na **Dashboard → Ndị Provider → Command Code → Jikọọ ọzọ**

### ModelScope na-ewepụta cooldown 429 siri ike

**Mgbaàmà:**

- Cooldown dị nnọọ mkpụmkpụ ma ọ bụ ozugbo na ModelScope mgbe obere arịrịọ bịara n'ìgwè
- Combo routing na-amafe ModelScope tupu oge a tụrụ anya ya

**Ihe kpatara ya:** ModelScope na-ewepụta header `Retry-After` ndị metụtara provider kpọmkwem. v3.8.0 nwere nhazi pụrụ iche maka header ndị ahụ, ya mere ụdị ndị ochie na-agụ ha n'ụzọ na-ezighi ezi dị ka akara oke arịrịọ nkịtị.

**Ndozi:**

- Gbaa mbọ hụ na ị nọ na v3.8.0 ma ọ bụ nke ka ọhụrụ
- Nyochaa na agbanyere toggle `useUpstream429BreakerHints` n'okpuru **Ntọala → Nkwụsi ike**

### OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET adịghị na production

**Mgbaàmà:**

- 401 na arịrịọ bridge WebSocket Codex/Responses ọ bụla mgbe ọ na-arụ na host production dị anya
- Handshake bridge WebSocket na-emechi ozugbo ejikọchara ya

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Env var `OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET` adịghị na gburugburu production.

**Ndozi:**

1. Mepụta secret enweghị usoro: `openssl rand -hex 32`
2. Tọọ `OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET=<random-secret>` na env sava production (nakwa na client ọ bụla na-ekwurịta okwu na bridge ahụ)
3. Malitegharịa OmniRoute

### Responses API: e wedatara background mode ka ọ bụrụ synchronous

**Mgbaàmà:**

- Edekọrọ ịdọ aka ná ntị: `background mode degraded to synchronous`
- Arịrịọ `background: true` na-ewepụta azịza synchronous nkịtị kama handle nke ọrụ background

**Ihe kpatara ya:** v3.8.0 na-ama ụma wedata `background: true` na Responses API ka ọ bụrụ mmezu synchronous ma na-ewepụta ịdọ aka ná ntị. Mmezu background async zuru ezu bụ ihe a ga-eweta n'ọdịnihu.

**Ndozi:**

- Hazie client ka ọ kpọọ na-enweghị `background`, ma ọ bụ
- Chere mwepụta e mesịrị nke nwere background mode async zuru ezu (soro changelog)

## Mmalite Nwayọ / Oge Nchere Njikere Agwụla

Ọ bụrụ na CLI ebipụta `⚠ Sava azaghị n'ime 60s` mana sava ahụ na-arụ ọrụ
n'ezie, oge e nyere nyocha njikere dị mkpụmkpụ karịa ka gburugburu gị chọrọ.

Nke a na-emekarị na Windows (antivirus, ndị na-enyocha sistemụ faịlụ) ma ọ bụ n'ime containers
nwere nnukwu ibu ọrụ n'oge mmalite.

**Ndozi — bulie oge e nyere:**

```bash
# Site na env var (na-adịgide n'ofe mmalite):
export OMNIROUTE_READY_TIMEOUT_MS=180000   # nkeji 3
omniroute serve

# Site na akara CLI (naanị otu oge):
omniroute serve --ready-timeout 180000
```

Ntọala ndabara bụ 60 000 ms (60 s). Ịdọ aka ná ntị ahụ bụ naanị ozi; sava ahụ
na-aga n'ihu ịmalite n'azụ ma a ga-enwe ike iru ya ozugbo mmalite zuru ezu.

Lee [`docs/reference/ENVIRONMENT.md`](../reference/ENVIRONMENT.md) maka nkọwa
zuru ezu banyere `OMNIROUTE_READY_TIMEOUT_MS`.
