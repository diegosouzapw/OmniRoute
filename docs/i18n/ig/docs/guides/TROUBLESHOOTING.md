## Ndozi Nsogbu Zuru Ezu

---

### Ịmachi Ọnụego na Ndị Na-eweta Ọrụ Efu (429 / 400 / 401)

**Mgbaàmà**: Mgbe ị na-eji `model: "auto"` na ndị na-eweta ọrụ efu/na-achọghị nkwado njirimara (opencode, auggie, wdg.), mgbe ụfọdụ ị na-enweta `HTTP 429`, `400`, ma ọ bụ `401` kama nzaghachi. Arịrịọ ndị ahụ na-aga nke ọma mgbe a nwara otu mkpali ahụ ọzọ obere oge gachara, mana ọrụ akpaaka (ọrụ cron, ndị nnọchi anya, skripti) na-akwụsị ozugbo ọdịda mbụ mere.

**Isi ihe kpatara ya**: Ụdị ọdịda atọ nọọrọ onwe ha na-agbakọta:

1. **Oke ọnụego onye na-eweta ọrụ (`429`)**: Ọkwa efu nwere ike ịmanye oke-nri maka oge ọ bụla. Ọtụtụ oku e mere n'otu oge na-emecha ya, ya mere a na-ajụ arịrịọ na-esote ruo mgbe oge ahụ maliteghachiri.
2. **Ụdị arụghị ọrụ na passthrough (`400`/`401`)**: Otu ọdọ `auto/*` nwere ike ịgụnye ụdị passthrough sitere na `opencode` ndị edebanyere n'ime katalọgụ mana enweghị ozi njirimara dị ndụ (dịka `oc/north-mini-code-free` → `401`). Auto-router na-anwa otu, ọ daa, njehie ahụ wee gbasaa tupu ịlaghachi azụ amalite.
3. **Mmụba n'ihi ọtụtụ ọrụ n'otu oge (`429` mgbe ibu dị arọ)**: Mgbe ọtụtụ nnọkọ onye nnọchi anya/cron rutere `auto` n'otu oge, mkpokọta ọnụego arịrịọ na-agafe ihe ndị na-eweta ọrụ efu nwere ike ịnabata, ya mere a na-akanye oku ziri ezi dịka mmegbu.

**Ndozi a kwadoro (ndị obodo kọrọ ya, 2026-08-10)**: hazie mgbanwe gburugburu atọ ka ntụgharị, ọtụtụ ọrụ n'otu oge, na ịlaghachi azụ wee nagide mgbanwe nke ọkwa efu kama ịkwụsị n'ihi ya:

```bash
export OMNIROUTE_ROTATE_ON_400=true           # gafee na ụdị/onye na-eweta ọrụ ọzọ mgbe 400/401 mere (na-amafe ụdị passthrough ndị mebiri emebi)
export OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT=4   # oke nnabata akọwapụtara maka ọrụ dị arọ (edoghị ya na ndabara: enweghị oke ọnụọgụ arịrịọ, lee ndetu n'okpuru)
export OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS=5000 # ichere nwere oke ogologo oge maka ikike ọrụ dị arọ kama 503 a pụrụ ịnwale ọzọ ozugbo
```

Tọọ ndị a na gburugburu usoro OmniRoute (daemon ahụ, dịka ọmụmaatụ site na LaunchAgent plist ma ọ bụ `systemctl edit`), wee malitegharịa OmniRoute. Ọkọlọtọ ntụgharị ahụ bụ ihe otu kacha nwee mmetụta: ọ na-agbanwe ọdịda siri ike ka ọ bụrụ nnwale ọzọ a na-adịghị ahụ anya megide onye na-eweta ọrụ dị mma n'ime ọdọ ahụ.

**Ndetu**: `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT` na-amachi ọnụ ọgụgụ arịrịọ dị arọ — ndị nwere ọnọdụ ogologo — nwere ike ịrụ ọrụ n'otu oge; oke ahụ bụ ọnụ ụzọ nnabata, ọ bụghị ihe na-amachi ọnụego onye na-eweta ọrụ. **Mmelite #503-fanout:** anaghịzi edobe var a na ndabara (ugbu a ọ na-amachi naanị mgbe ahaziri ya n'ụzọ doro anya, dịka n'elu) — kama nke ahụ, mmefu byte a na-enweta na-akpaghị aka (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES`) na-achịkwa nnabata ọrụ dị arọ, nke na-ahazi onwe ya dịka oke ebe nchekwa n'ezie nke host si dị, ya mere nrụnye ọhụrụ kwesịrị ịhụ ọdịda `503 chat_admission_busy` ole na ole nke ukwuu n'enweghị ịtọ var a ma ọlị; ịtọ ya n'ụzọ doro anya ebe a ka na-arụ ọrụ kpọmkwem dịka e dere na akwụkwọ. Mgbanwe mmefu byte akọwapụtara na-amachi n'etiti 8 MiB–2 GiB. `413 body_exceeds_budget` abụghị ihe na-adịru nwa oge: bulie mmefu byte ahụ, belata `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_HARD_MAX_BODY_BYTES`, ma ọ bụ bulie oke ebe nchekwa usoro ahụ. Mwepu `inflight_bytes_budget` bụ esemokwu nwa oge, a ka nwekwara ike ịnwale ya ọzọ. A na-achịkwa mmachi ọnụego maka onye na-eweta ọrụ ọ bụla (`open-sse/services/rateLimitManager.ts`) iche site na `RATE_LIMIT_MAX_WAIT_MS`, `RATE_LIMIT_MAX_QUEUE_DEPTH`, na `RATE_LIMIT_AUTO_ENABLE` — lee `.env.example`.

**Otu esi enyocha na ọ rụrụ ọrụ**: mee ka agent/cron gị gbaa ọsọ ugboro abụọ n’usoro ngwa ngwa ma gosi na ha abụọ gara nke ọma. Tupu ndozi ahụ, ọsọ nke abụọ na-ewepụtakarị `429`/`401`. Mgbe ndozi ahụ gasịrị, a na-anwale ọdịda ọ bụla ọzọ (ọ bụrụ na ọ dị) na nzuzo ma oku ahụ emechaa nke ọma. Ị nwekwara ike iji `curl /monitoring/health` wee lelee mpaghara `rateLimitedUntil` na njikọ ndị na-eweta ọrụ, yana `circuitBreakers.providerBreakers[].state` maka ndị na-eweta ọrụ emetụtara — ọnọdụ ahụ ga-abụ otu n’ime `CLOSED`, `DEGRADED`, `OPEN`, ma ọ bụ `HALF_OPEN` (lee `src/shared/utils/circuitBreaker.ts`), onye na-eweta ọrụ nke na-aga n’ihu na-ada ga-agbanwe `CLOSED → DEGRADED → OPEN` tupu oge nrụgharị ahụ ekwe ka arịrịọ nnwale gafee (`HALF_OPEN`).

**Ọ bụrụ na ị ka na-ahụ 429**: akaụntụ na-arụ ọrụ maka onye na-eweta ọrụ ahụ agwụla _quota_ ya n’ezie (ọ bụghị naanị oke ọsọ arịrịọ). Tinye akaụntụ nke abụọ maka otu onye na-eweta ọrụ ahụ na dashboard OmniRoute → Providers → Accounts, ma ọ bụ tinye onye na-eweta ọrụ efu ọzọ (dịka `routeway`, `auggie`). Ntugharị akaụntụ na-enyere naanị na nsogbu nwa oge nke oke ọsọ/400/401; ịgwụcha quota kpamkpam chọrọ nzere nke abụọ ma ọ bụ onye na-eweta ọrụ dị iche.

**Ọ bụrụ na ị hụ 403 na ụdị vision (`auto/vision`, `bazaarlink/*`)**: akaụntụ ejikọrọ enweghị atụmatụ akwụ ụgwọ nke gụnyere vision, ma ọ bụ API key ahụ enweghị ikike zuru ezu. Nyochaa na dashboard nke onye na-eweta ọrụ na scope nke key ahụ gụnyere vision/multimodal, ma ọ bụ jikọọ akaụntụ ọkwa akwụ ụgwọ ma debe ya dịka ebumnuche vision.

## Ịdọ aka ná ntị npm install (ERESOLVE / peer / deprecated)

Mgbe ị na-agba `npm install -g omniroute`, ị nwere ike ịhụ ọtụtụ ịdọ aka ná ntị dịka `npm warn ERESOLVE`, ọkwa gbasara peer-dependency, na ozi `deprecated`. **A na-atụ anya ihe ndị a, ha anaghịkwa emerụ ihe ọ bụla.** Nrụnye gị gara nke ọma ma ọ bụrụ na ị hụ `added <N> packages` na nsonaazụ ahụ.

Iji gbochie ịdọ aka ná ntị gbasara idozi peer-dependency, jiri usoro nrụnye OmniRoute na-akwado:

```bash
npm install -g omniroute --legacy-peer-deps
```

`--legacy-peer-deps` na-egbochi naanị `ERESOLVE` na ọkwa peer-dependency. Ọkwa deprecation ka ga-apụta n'ihi na ha sitere na ngwugwu ndị ọzọ nọ n'okpuru; ha apụtaghị na nrụnye ahụ dara.

Ịdọ aka ná ntị ndị ahụ sitere na oke peer-dependency ochie dị na ngwugwu ndị ọzọ OmniRoute anaghị achịkwa:

1. **`marked-terminal` chọrọ `marked >=1 <16`, ma a hụrụ `marked@18`** — ọ na-arụ ọrụ nke ọma n'ezie; naanị ihe bụ na oke peer nke upstream ahụ emeela ochie.
2. **`deprecated prebuild-install@7.1.3`** — ihe enyemaka nọ n'okpuru maka iweta native-binary. A naghị
   eji ya arụnye njikọ transport `wreq-js` akpọchiri na ụdị akọwapụtara, ọ pụtaghịkwa na nhazi transport
   nke onye na-eweta web-cookie dara.

**Ọ dịghị ihe ị ga-eme** — enweghị ike imechi ịdọ aka ná ntị ndị ahụ kpamkpam ma e wezụga ịmepụta fork nke ngwugwu upstream.

## Nchọpụta Ụgha nke Antivirus

<a name="antivirus-false-positives"></a>

### Avast/AVG na-etinye `README.md` na kwarantaini dịka `MD:HttpRequest-inf[Susp]`

**Nke a bụ nchọpụta ụgha. Ọ dịghị ihe butere nje, ọ dịghịkwa ihe achọrọ ka eme.**

Avast na AVG na-eji usoro nyocha heuristic nke na-akanye faịlụ ederede nkịtị/Markdown nwere ọtụtụ
njikọ ndị yiri arịrịọ HTTP. `README.md` nke OmniRoute na-esonye n'ime ngwugwu npm (e depụtara ya
na `package.json` → `files`), ya mere ọ na-abata na `node_modules/omniroute/README.md` mgbe
echichi zuru ụwa ọnụ gasịrị — ma o nwere ihe dịka njikọ `http://localhost:20128/...` iri na ise (ebe njedebe MCP
HTTP/SSE, URL A2A `.well-known`, na ihe atụ `curl`). Ịba ụba nke njikọ ndị ahụ
ezuola ịkpalite usoro heuristic ahụ.

Ọ bụrụ na nke a malitere naanị n'oge na-adịbeghị anya: ụdị faịlụ ahụ agbanwebeghị. README ahụ gbasawanyere
tebụl ebe njedebe ya (agbakwunyere MCP HTTP + SSE + A2A) yana ọtụtụ ihe atụ `curl`, nke mere ka
ọ gafee oke ahụ.

Faịlụ ahụ bụ naanị akwụkwọ nkọwa na-adịghị arụ ọrụ, na-enweghị ọdịnaya ọ bụla a pụrụ imepụta. Ị nwere ike iweghachi ya
na kwarantaini n'enweghị nsogbu.

**Ihe ị ga-eme:**

1. **Kwụsị ọkwa ndị ahụ** — wepụ ndekọ echichi ahụ na nyocha antivirus gị
   (Avast: Settings → Exceptions), site n'ịgbakwunye ụzọ `node_modules` zuru ụwa ọnụ gị na/ma ọ bụ
   ndekọ data OmniRoute (`~/.omniroute/`).
2. **Kọọrọ ha nchọpụta ụgha ahụ** — <https://www.avast.com/false-positive-file-form.php>,
   ma tinye `README.md` e debere na kwarantaini. Nke a bụ ndozi ga-enyere onye ọ bụla aka, ebe ọ bụ
   usoro heuristic nke onye na-eweta antivirus na-emebiga ihe ókè n'ihi faịlụ ederede.

**Ihe mere anyị anaghị “edozi” nke a n'akụkụ anyị:** ihe atụ niile bụ `http://localhost`, ma
localhost enweghị ike ịbụ `https` na-enweghị nsogbu asambodo mmadụ bịanyere aka n'onwe ya. Ịgbanwe akwụkwọ nkọwa ahụ
iji gbanahụ usoro heuristic nke otu onye na-eweta antivirus ga-emebi ahụmịhe onye ọ bụla na-agụ ya naanị iji meeju njehie nyocha afọ.

### Kaspersky na-akanye ngwa Desktọpụ dịka `PDM:Trojan.Win32.Generic`

**Nke a bụ nchọpụta ụgha sitere na usoro heuristic omume. Ọ dịghị ihe butere nje.** Prefix `PDM:`
nke Kaspersky pụtara na mkpebi ahụ sitere na Proactive Defense Module (System Watcher) ya,
nke na-ekpebi dabere n'ihe installer ahụ _na-eme_ kama iji ya tụnyere malware ndị amaara. Mgbe
ọ malitere ọrụ, Kaspersky “na-eweghachi azụ” echichi ahụ dum — na-ehichapụ faịlụ ndị ọ
degoro — ya mere ngwa ahụ na-emecha mebie ma ọ bụ pụọ.

Faịlụ ndị ọ na-akanye bụ akụkụ ọkọlọtọ nke dependency ndị ekwuputara, ndị bụ open-source ma sonye
n'ime ngwa desktọpụ ahụ, dịka ọmụmaatụ:

- `resources/app/.build/next/node_modules/playwright-<hash>/lib/…/agentParser.js` na
  `workerProcessEntry.js` — [Playwright](https://playwright.dev), library akpaaka ihe nchọgharị
  a na-eji maka nbanye provider n'ime ngwa na nkata na-eji ihe nchọgharị arụ ọrụ.
- `resources/app/.build/next/node_modules/@wreq-js/binding-win32-<arch>-msvc-<hash>/wreq-js.win32-<arch>-msvc.node`
  — native binding `wreq-js` nke a kpọchiri na ụdị a kapịrị ọnụ, nke a na-eji maka HTTP nwere akara njirimara ihe nchọgharị n'aka provider
  ndị na-eji web-cookie (`<arch>` bụ `x64` ma ọ bụ `arm64`).

**Ihe mere ọ ji amalite ọrụ:** **akabeghị Windows installer ahụ code-sign**, ya mere NSIS
installer na-enweghị mbinye aka enweghị ntụkwasị obi ọ bụla, usoro heuristic omume na-arụkwa ọrụ n'ike kachasị elu. Mgbe
nke a jikọtara ya na native DLL e tinyere n'ime ya yana ọtụtụ narị faịlụ `.js` e dere n'okpuru
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\OmniRoute` (gụnyere ndekọ ngwugwu nwere hash na njedebe sitere na
Next.js standalone build), nke ahụ ezuola ịkpalite usoro heuristic ahụ. E mere atụmatụ maka code signing;
ruo mgbe emechara ya, ụdị ọhụrụ nwere ike imeghachi nke a.

**Ihe ị ga-eme:**

1. **Buru ụzọ nyochaa ihe ị budatara** (nke a na-egosi na ọ bụghị faịlụ e megharịrị n'ụzọ ọjọọ). Mbipụta ọ bụla na-ebipụta
   `latest.yml`, nke field `sha512` ya (base64) na-ekpuchi installer `OmniRoute.Setup.<version>.exe`.
   Na PowerShell, site na folda nwere installer ahụ:
   ```powershell
   $b = [System.Security.Cryptography.SHA512]::Create().ComputeHash(
     [System.IO.File]::ReadAllBytes("$PWD\OmniRoute.Setup.<version>.exe"))
   [Convert]::ToBase64String($b)
   ```
   Nsonaazụ ahụ ga-adabarịrị na `latest.yml` → `sha512`. Ọ bụrụ na ọ dabaraghị, hichapụ faịlụ ahụ ma
   budata ya ọzọ naanị site na [ibe mbipụta GitHub](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/releases).
2. **Weghachite + wepụ na nyocha** — weghachite ihe ndị e weghachiri azụ site na kwarantaini ma gbakwunye mwepụ na nyocha
   maka `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\OmniRoute` (Kaspersky → Settings → Threats and Exclusions),
   wee wụnye ya ọzọ.
3. **Kọọrọ ha nchọpụta ụgha ahụ** — <https://opentip.kaspersky.com/>. Akụkọ FP
   ndị ọrụ zigara na-eme ka ntinye na allowlist gaa ngwa ngwa n'ezie.

## Ndakọrịta Node.js

<a name="nodejs-compatibility"></a>

### Ihu nbanye na-akụda ma ọ bụ na-egosi njehie "Module self-registration"

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Ị na-eji ụdị Node.js nke na-anọghị n'oke runtime echekwara nke OmniRoute kwadoro. Ihe a na-ahụkarị bụ iji ọkwa patch ochie nke Node 22 ma ọ bụ 24 nke dị n'okpuru oke nchekwa emeziri nke OmniRoute chọrọ.

**Mgbaàmà:**

- Ihu nbanye na-egosi ihuenyo efu ma ọ bụ njehie sava
- Console na-egosi `Error: Module did not self-register` ma ọ bụ njehie native binding yiri ya
- Ihu nbanye na-egosi **ọkwa ịdọ aka ná ntị oroma** nwere ụdị Node gị ma ọ bụrụ na runtime ahụ anọghị n'ime iwu nchekwa akwadoro

**Ndozi:**

1. Wụnye mbipụta Node.js LTS a na-akwado (nkwado: Node.js 24.x):
   ```bash
   nvm install 24
   nvm use 24
   ```
2. Nyochaa ụdị gị: `node --version` kwesịrị igosi `v24.0.0` ma ọ bụ nke ka ọhụrụ n'ahịrị 24.x LTS
3. Wụnyegharịa OmniRoute: `npm install -g omniroute`
4. Malitegharịa: `omniroute`

> **Ụdị echekwara a na-akwado:** `>=22.22.2 <23` ma ọ bụ `>=24.0.0 <27`. A na-akwado Node.js 24.x LTS (Krypton) na Node.js 26 n'ụzọ zuru ezu.

### npm v11+: Awụnyeghị `better-sqlite3` (Cannot find module)

<a name="npm-v11-better-sqlite3-not-installed-cannot-find-module"></a>

**Ihe kpatara ya:** npm v11 (nke na-abịa na Node.js 24+) na-egbochi script nrụnye maka dependency
nhọrọ na ndabara. Ebe ọ bụ na e depụtara `better-sqlite3` na `optionalDependencies`
ma ọ na-achọ native compilation (`node-gyp rebuild`), npm na-awụpụ ya n'enweghị ọkwa.

**Mgbaàmà:**

- Sava na-akụda mgbe ọ na-amalite site na `Cannot find module 'better-sqlite3'`
- `ls node_modules/better-sqlite3` na-egosi "No such file or directory"
- `npm ls better-sqlite3` na-egosi `(empty)`

**Ndozi:**

1. Kwado script nrụnye ndị ahụ ma wụnyegharịa:
   ```bash
   npm approve-scripts better-sqlite3
   npm install
   ```
2. Ma ọ bụ jiri aka wụnye prebuilt ahụ:
   ```bash
   npm pack better-sqlite3@13.0.1
   tar -xzf better-sqlite3-*.tgz -C node_modules
   mv node_modules/package node_modules/better-sqlite3
   rm better-sqlite3-*.tgz
   ```
3. Nyochaa na ọ na-arụ ọrụ: `node -e "require('better-sqlite3')(':memory:').close(); console.log('OK')"`

### macOS: `dlopen` / "slice is not valid mach-o file"

<a name="macos-native-module-rebuild"></a>

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Mgbe emechara `npm install -g omniroute` zuru ụwa ọnụ, binary native nke `better-sqlite3` dị n'ime package ahụ nwere ike ịbụ nke a compile-rụrụ maka architecture ma ọ bụ Node.js ABI dị iche na nke na-arụ ọrụ na kọmputa ahụ. Nke a na-emekarị na macOS (ma Apple Silicon ma Intel) mgbe binary pre-built ahụ adabaghị na gburugburu gị.

**Mgbaàmà:**

- Sava anaghị amalite ozugbo ma na-egosi njehie `dlopen`
- Njehie ahụ nwere `slice is not valid mach-o file`
- Ọmụmaatụ zuru ezu:

```
dlopen(/Users/<user>/.nvm/versions/node/v24.14.1/lib/node_modules/omniroute/app/node_modules/better-sqlite3/build/Release/better_sqlite3.node, 0x0001): tried: '...' (slice is not valid mach-o file)
```

**Ndozi — rụgharịa ya maka gburugburu kọmputa gị (ọ dịghị mkpa iweda ụdị Node.js):**

```bash
cd $(npm root -g)/omniroute/app
npm rebuild better-sqlite3
omniroute
```

> **Rịba ama:** Nke a na-emegharị compile nke native binding ahụ megide ụdị Node.js na CPU architecture dị na kọmputa gị, nke na-edozi enweghị ndakọrịta binary ahụ. Oke runtime a na-akwado n'ụzọ ọchịchị bụ **`>=22.22.2 <23` ma ọ bụ `>=24.0.0 <27`** (`SUPPORTED_NODE_RANGE` na `src/shared/utils/nodeRuntimeSupport.ts`, nke dabara na field `engines` nke `package.json`). A na-akwado Node.js 24.x LTS (Krypton) na Node.js 26 n'ụzọ zuru ezu site na `better-sqlite3` v12.x.

## Nsogbu Proxy

<a name="proxy-issues"></a>

### Nkwado onye na-eweta ọrụ na-egosi "fetch failed"

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Ebe njedebe nkwado igodo API (`POST /api/providers/validate`) na-agafe nhazi proxy na mbụ, nke na-ebute ọdịda n'ebe ndị chọrọ ka ebufe njikọ site na proxy.

**Ndozi (v3.5.5+):** Edozila nke a ugbu a. Ụzọ nkwado onye na-eweta ọrụ na-agafe na `runWithProxyContext`, ma na-agbaso ntọala proxy nke onye na-eweta ọrụ na nke zuru ụwa ọnụ na-akpaghị aka.

### Nnyocha ọnọdụ token na-ada na "fetch failed"

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Mmelite token OAuth n'azụ anaghị achọpụta nhazi proxy maka njikọ ọ bụla.

**Ndozi (v3.5.5+):** Onye nhazi oge nnyocha ọnọdụ token na-achọpụtazi nhazi proxy maka njikọ ọ bụla tupu ọ nwaa imelite ya. Melite gaa na v3.5.5+.

### Proxy SOCKS5 na-eweghachi "invalid onRequestStart method"

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Na Node.js 22, onye nkesa undici@8 adabaghị na mmejuputa `fetch()` arụnyere n'ime Node.

**Ndozi (v3.5.5+):** OmniRoute na-ejizi ọrụ `fetch()` nke undici n'onwe ya mgbe onye nkesa proxy na-arụ ọrụ, iji hụ na omume na-adị otu. Melite gaa na v3.5.5+.

### Proxy MITM n'okpuru WSL: anaghị ejide ngwa desktọpụ dị na Windows host

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Proxy MITM na asambodo CA ya na-etinye n'ime gburugburu ebe OmniRoute na-arụ ọrụ. N'okpuru WSL, gburugburu ahụ bụ Linux guest, ebe ngwa desktọpụ AI (Kiro, Trae, Copilot, Zed, …) na-arụ ọrụ na Windows host. Ngwa ndị dị na host anaghị atụkwasị ebe nchekwa asambodo nke guest obi, ha anaghịkwa ebufe njikọ site na proxy sistemụ nke guest, ya mere njide okporo ụzọ desktọpụ anaghị arụ ọrụ ebe ahụ.

**Ndụmọdụ:** Gbaa OmniRoute ozugbo n'otu OS ahụ ngwa desktọpụ ndị ịchọrọ ijide na-arụ ọrụ na ya (Windows maka ngwa Windows; otu ahụkwa maka macOS/Linux). Idobe OmniRoute n'ime WSL mgbe a na-elekwasị anya na ngwa host chọrọ ka i jiri aka gị mee ka Windows host tụkwasị asambodo CA emepụtara obi, ma tụọ ntọala netwọkụ/proxy nke ngwa host ọ bụla gaa na ebe njedebe proxy WSL — nke a bụ nhazi na-enweghị nkwado ma dịkwa mfe imebi.

## Nsogbu Docker

### Docker IPv6 / Mweghachi Njikọ

<a name="docker-ipv6"></a>

**Mgbaàmà:** `curl http://localhost:20128/v1/models` na-eweghachi `curl: (56) Recv failure: Connection reset by peer`. Dashboard na endpoint ndị anaghị achọ nkwenye njirimara na-arụ ọrụ, mana endpoint ndị chọrọ nkwenye njirimara anaghị arụ ọrụ — ọ na-adị ka nsogbu auth, mana ọ bụghị ya.

   ```bash
     -p 127.0.0.1:20128:20128 -v omniroute-data:/app/data diegosouzapw/omniroute:latest
   ```
   Nke a na-amanye bind IPv4 ma na-egbochikwa ikpughe proxy na interface host niile.

## Ntọala Nkwụsi Ike

### Mmachi ọnụego akpaaka anaghị amalite ịrụ ọrụ

- Mmachi ọnụego akpaaka na-emetụta naanị ndị na-eweta ọrụ ji API key (ọ bụghị OAuth/ndebanye aha)
- Nyochaa na agbanyere mmachi ọnụego akpaaka na **Settings → Resilience → Provider Profiles**
- Lelee ma onye na-eweta ọrụ na-eweghachi koodu ọnọdụ `429` ma ọ bụ nkụnye isi `Retry-After`

### Ịhazigharị exponential backoff

Profaịlụ ndị na-eweta ọrụ na-akwado ntọala ndị a:

- **Nkwụsị ntọala** — Oge nchere mbụ mgbe ọdịda mbụ gasịrị (ndabara: 1s)
- **Nkwụsị kacha elu** — Oke oge nchere kachasị elu (ndabara: 30s)
- **Ọnụọgụ mmụba** — Ole a ga-eji mụbaa oge nchere maka ọdịda ọ bụla na-esochi ibe ya (ndabara: 2x)

### Mgbochi thundering herd

Mgbe ọtụtụ arịrịọ na-aga n'otu oge rutere onye na-eweta ọrụ e tinyere mmachi ọnụego, OmniRoute na-eji mutex + mmachi ọnụego akpaaka hazie arịrịọ ka ha na-aga n'usoro ma gbochie ọdịda na-agbasawanye. Nke a na-eme na-akpaghị aka maka ndị na-eweta ọrụ ji API key.

### Arịrịọ nkata na-ada na 503 / chat_admission_busy

**Ihe mgbaàmà:**

- Endpoint mmecha nkata na-eweghachi nzaghachi `503` enwere ike ịnwale ọzọ nke koodu njehie ya bụ
  `chat_admission_busy`.
- Nzaghachi ahụ gụnyere `Retry-After`. Kemgbe #12135, a na-enweta uru ahụ site na njupụta a hụrụ
  — nke ka ukwuu n'etiti windo `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS` nke arịrịọ ahụ cherelarị
  na oge ejiderela heavyweight leases ndị dị ugbu a — a na-ebuli ya ruo sekọnd zuru ezu
  ma debe oke ya na 60. N'ọnụ ụzọ na-enweghị ọrụ, ọ na-edobe opekempe ndị e ji kemgbe: sekọnd 2 n'ụzọ
  dabere na byte, sekọnd 1 n'ụzọ dabere na nhazi (nke gụnyekwara
  `reason: "structure_limit"`).
- Nke a nwere ike ime mgbe heavyweight chat ọzọ ma ọ bụ nzaghachi streaming na-ewe ogologo oge ka
  na-aga n'ihu.

Ahụ nzaghachi dabere na byte bụ:

```json
{
  "error": {
    "message": "Chat admission capacity is temporarily unavailable. Retry shortly.",
    "type": "server_error",
    "code": "chat_admission_busy"
  }
}
```

Nzaghachi dabere na nhazi na-eji otu type na code ahụ, yana ozi
`Local chat admission capacity is busy for this structurally heavy request; upstream provider routing was not attempted. Retry shortly.`
na `reason: "structure_limit"`.
N'oke ndabara, arịrịọ dị arọ n'ụzọ nhazi ma ọ bụrụ na o nwere opekata mpe ozi `200`,
ngwaọrụ `64` opekata mpe, ma ọ bụ token e mere atụmatụ ya ruru `32,000` opekata mpe, ma ọ bụ mgbe atụmatụ nhazi nwere oke
gwụsịrị oke ya nke node `10,000` e letara ma ọ bụ omimi `12`.

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Nke a bụ ịbelata ibu a kpachapụrụ anya n'ime OmniRoute, ọ bụghị ọdịda nke onye na-eweta ọrụ upstream.
Process ọ bụla na-eji ihe nchebe dị naanị na process ahụ iji dobe ikike heavyweight nwere oke tupu ijide
ma nyochaa nnukwu ahụ arịrịọ. A na-ejide heavyweight lease ruo ogologo ndụ nzaghachi SSE.

**#503-fanout:** tupu ndozi a, ihe nchebe ahụ na-amachi concurrency na COUNT arịrịọ a kapịrị ọnụ
(`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT`, ndabara `1`) n'agbanyeghị ebe nchekwa host, ya mere fan-out nke coding-agent
(ọtụtụ subagent/CLI, ahụ ndị na-adịkarị > 256 KB) dara ruo concurrency dị irè
nke ~1 ma wepụta 503 n'okpuru ibu nkịtị kpamkpam. Ugbu a ihe nchebe ahụ na-ahazi onwe ya: a na-achịkwa ya
site na mmefu ego ingest BYTE a na-enweta na-akpaghị aka (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES`) nke a tụrụ dabere na
oke ebe nchekwa n'ezie nke process ahụ, ọ na-enyochakwa akara nrụgide akụrụngwa dị ndụ — ya mere ọ
na-ebelata ibu naanị mgbe host nọ n'ezie n'okpuru nrụgide ebe nchekwa, ọ bụghị naanị n'ihi na ihe karịrị otu
arịrịọ dị arọ bịara n'otu oge. A ka na-asọpụrụ oke count ochie (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT`),
mana naanị ma ọ bụrụ na ị tọọ ya n'ụzọ doro anya.

Mgbe ikike juru, arịrịọ heavyweight ga-ebu ụzọ chere ruo
`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS` (ndabara `2000`, `0` na-agbanyụ nchere ahụ) ka oghere tọhapụ
tupu ọ zaa `503` enwere ike ịnwale ọzọ. Nchere nwere oke dị ka ndị ahịa ụdị agent
(OpenCode, Claude Code, Cursor) ndị na-ezipụ sub-request dị arọ n'otu oge wee hazie mwụpụ ahụ n'usoro
kama imefusị mmefu ego nnwale ọzọ ha niile na ọjụjụ ozugbo ma kwụsị n'etiti ọrụ.
A na-egosi njupụta heavyweight lease dị ugbu a, mmefu ego byte e kpebiri, na ogo nrụgide dị ndụ
na `GET /api/monitoring/health` → `chatAdmission` (`inflightBytes`, `maxInflightBytes`,
`budgetSource`, `pressureSeverity`, `countCapEnabled`) — lelee ihe ndị a tupu imetụ env var ọ bụla aka.
Settings → Resilience → Request Queue → Concurrent Requests anaghị achịkwa nke a; ntọala ahụ
na-achịkwa usoro request-queue nke onye na-eweta ọrụ nke dị iche.

**Ndozi:**

1. Buru ụzọ nwaa ọzọ. Ndị ahịa kwesịrị ịgbaso `Retry-After` ma jiri backoff kama ikwugharị
   arịrịọ ahụ ozugbo.
2. Lelee `/api/monitoring/health` → `chatAdmission` tupu ịhazigharị ihe ọ bụla. `countCapEnabled:
false` na `maxInflightBytes` sara mbara pụtara na mmefu ego e nwetara na-akpaghị aka arụworị
   ọrụ ya; `pressureSeverity` nke `high`/`critical` pụtara na host ahụ nwere obere ebe nchekwa n'ezie —
   enweghị ike iji admission env var dozie nke ahụ; ọ chọrọ RAM karịa ma ọ bụ ibu ọrụ pere mpe.
3. Naanị ma ọ bụrụ na `/api/monitoring/health` gosiri na mmefu ego e nwetara na-akpaghị aka pere ezigbo mpe maka
   host gị (ọ naghị adịkarị — ọ na-agbatịworị site na container ruo bare-metal), jiri
   `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES` dochie ya ozugbo kama ịlaghachi na oke count arịrịọ ochie.

Hụ [ntụaka environment-variable](../reference/ENVIRONMENT.md#4-security--authentication)
maka ntọala admission ndị bụ isi e ji eme ihe.

## Nhazi nhọrọ maka ọdịda RAG / LLM (nsogbu 16)

Ụfọdụ ndị ọrụ OmniRoute na-etinye gateway n'ihu usoro RAG ma ọ bụ usoro agent. N'ime nhazi ndị ahụ, ọ na-abụkarị ihe a na-ahụkarị ịhụ usoro dị ịtụnanya: OmniRoute na-adị ka ọ dị mma (providers na-arụ ọrụ, routing profiles dị mma, enweghị ọkwa rate limit) mana azịza ikpeazụ ka na-ezighị ezi.

N'eziokwu, nsogbu ndị a na-esikarị na pipeline RAG dị n'okpuru pụta, ọ bụghị na gateway n'onwe ya.

Ọ bụrụ na ịchọrọ okwu ndị mmadụ niile ga-eji kọwaa ọdịda ndị ahụ, ị nwere ike iji WFGY ProblemMap, ihe ederede mpụga nwere ikikere MIT nke na-akọwa usoro ọdịda RAG / LLM iri na isii na-eme ugboro ugboro. N'ogo dị elu, ọ na-ekpuchi:

- ntụgharị retrieval na oke context mebiri emebi
- indexes na vector stores tọgbọ chakoo ma ọ bụ ochie
- enweghị ndakọrịta n'etiti embedding na semantics
- nsogbu prompt assembly na context window
- ndakpọ logic na azịza nwere ntụkwasị obi gabigara ókè
- ọdịda ogologo chain na nhazi agent
- ntụgharị memory na role n'etiti ọtụtụ agent
- nsogbu deployment na usoro bootstrap

Echiche ahụ dị mfe:

1. Mgbe ị na-enyocha azịza na-adịghị mma, dekọọ:
   - ọrụ na arịrịọ onye ọrụ
   - route ma ọ bụ ngwakọta provider dị na OmniRoute
   - context RAG ọ bụla e ji mee ihe n'okpuru (documents ewepụtara, tool calls, wdg)
2. Jikọta nsogbu ahụ na otu ma ọ bụ abụọ n'ime nọmba WFGY ProblemMap (`No.1` … `No.16`).
3. Chekwaa nọmba ahụ na dashboard, runbook, ma ọ bụ incident tracker nke gị n'akụkụ logs OmniRoute.
4. Jiri ibe WFGY kwekọrọ na ya kpebie ma ị ga-agbanwe RAG stack, retriever, ma ọ bụ routing strategy gị.

Ederede zuru ezu na usoro ọrụ doro anya dị ebe a (ikikere MIT, naanị ederede):

[WFGY ProblemMap README](https://github.com/onestardao/WFGY/blob/main/ProblemMap/README.md)

Ị nwere ike ileghara ngalaba a anya ma ọ bụrụ na ị naghị arụ pipeline RAG ma ọ bụ agent n'azụ OmniRoute.

## Nsogbu Ndị A Maara na v3.8.0

Nsogbu ndị metụtara kpọmkwem mbipụta v3.8.0 na ụzọ mgbagharị ha dị ugbu a. Ọ bụrụ na etinyere mmezi na patch e mesịrị, a ga-emelite ma ọ bụ wepụ ndenye ahụ.

### Ọdịda auth Devin CLI

**Mgbaàmà:**

- "Ahụghị Devin CLI" ma ọ bụ "auth dara" mgbe a na-akpọ tools ndị Devin na-akwado
- Nnyocha runtime CLI na-egosi `installed=false`

**Ihe kpatara ya:**

- `CLI_DEVIN_BIN` na-atụ aka na path na-adịghị adị
- Awụnyebeghị Devin CLI na host ahụ

**Mmezi:**

1. Wụnye Devin CLI maka platform gị
2. Tọọ `CLI_DEVIN_BIN=/usr/local/bin/devin` (ma ọ bụ path ziri ezi) n'ime `.env`
3. Malitegharịa OmniRoute ma nwalee ọzọ site na **Dashboard → CLI Tools**

### Cooldown model rapaara (ntọgharị aka)

**Mgbaàmà:**

- A ka na-edepụta model n'ime cooldown ọbụlagodi mgbe oge njedebe gafere
- Arịrịọ ka na-amafe model ahụ na combo routing n'agbanyeghị na timestamp ahụ dị n'oge gara aga

**Ntọgharị aka:**

- **Dashboard:** **Settings → Model Cooldowns** → pịa **Re-enable** na card emetụtara
- **API:** `DELETE /api/resilience/model-cooldowns` tinyere management auth headers

### Njikọ provider Command Code dara na 403

**Mgbaàmà:**

- 403 mgbe a na-anwale njikọ provider Command Code
- Card provider na-egosi "enweghị ikike" mgbe agbakwunyere ya ọhụrụ

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Usoro OAuth agwụchaghị (anabataghị callback ma ọ bụ echekwaghị token).

**Mmezi:**

- Gbaa `omniroute providers` site na CLI iji kpalite usoro OAuth ọzọ, ma ọ bụ
- Gbaa OAuth ọzọ site na **Dashboard → Providers → Command Code → Reconnect**

### ModelScope na-eweghachi cooldowns 429 siri ike

**Mgbaàmà:**

- Cooldowns dị mkpụmkpụ nke ukwuu ma ọ bụ nke na-amalite ozugbo na ModelScope mgbe obere ọtụtụ arịrịọ gasịrị
- Combo routing na-amafe ModelScope tupu oge a tụrụ anya ya

**Ihe kpatara ya:** ModelScope na-ewepụta headers `Retry-After` pụrụ iche maka provider. v3.8.0 nwere nhazi pụrụ iche maka headers ndị ahụ, ya mere ụdị ochie na-agụ ha n'ụzọ na-ezighi ezi dịka ntụnye rate-limit nkịtị.

**Mmezi:**

- Gbaa mbọ hụ na ị nọ na v3.8.0 ma ọ bụ nke ọhụrụ karịa
- Nyochaa na agbanyere toggle `useUpstream429BreakerHints` n'okpuru **Settings → Resilience**

### OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET adịghị na production

**Mgbaàmà:**

- 401 na arịrịọ WebSocket bridge Codex/Responses ọ bụla mgbe ọ na-arụ ọrụ na remote production host
- Handshake WebSocket bridge na-emechi ozugbo njikọ gasịrị

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Env var `OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET` adịghị na production environment.

**Mmezi:**

1. Mepụta secret enweghị usoro: `openssl rand -hex 32`
2. Tọọ `OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET=<random-secret>` n'ime env nke production server (yana client ọ bụla na-ekwurịta okwu na bridge)
3. Malitegharịa OmniRoute

### Responses API: e wedatara background mode ka ọ bụrụ synchronous

**Mgbaàmà:**

- Warning e dekọrọ: `background mode degraded to synchronous`
- Arịrịọ `background: true` na-eweghachi azịza synchronous nkịtị kama background job handle

**Ihe kpatara ya:** v3.8.0 na-ama ụma wedata `background: true` na Responses API ka ọ bụrụ synchronous execution ma na-ewepụta warning. Full async background execution bụ ihe a ga-ewepụta n'ọdịnihu.

**Mmezi:**

- Hazie client ka ọ kpọọ na-enweghị `background`, ma ọ bụ
- Chere mbipụta e mesịrị nke ga-eweta full async background mode (soro changelog)

## Nmalite Dị Nwayọ / Oge Nchere Njikere Agwụla

Ọ bụrụ na CLI egosi `⚠ Server did not respond within 60s` mana sava ahụ na-arụ ọrụ n'ezie, oge e nyere nyocha njikere dị mkpụmkpụ maka gburugburu gị.

Nke a na-emekarị na Windows (antivirus, ndị na-enyocha sistemụ faịlụ) ma ọ bụ n'ime containers nwere nnukwu ibu ọrụ mgbe ha na-amalite.

**Ndozi — bulie oge e nyere:**

```bash
# Site na env var (ọ na-adịgide n'ofe mmalite dị iche iche):
export OMNIROUTE_READY_TIMEOUT_MS=180000   # nkeji 3
omniroute serve

# Site na ọkọlọtọ CLI (naanị otu oge):
omniroute serve --ready-timeout 180000
```

Ntọala ndabara bụ 60 000 ms (60 s). Ịdọ aka ná ntị ahụ bụ naanị maka inye ozi; sava ahụ na-aga n'ihu ịmalite n'azụ, a ga-enwekwa ike iru ya ozugbo mmalite zuru ezu.

Lee [`docs/reference/ENVIRONMENT.md`](../reference/ENVIRONMENT.md) maka nkọwa zuru ezu gbasara `OMNIROUTE_READY_TIMEOUT_MS`.
