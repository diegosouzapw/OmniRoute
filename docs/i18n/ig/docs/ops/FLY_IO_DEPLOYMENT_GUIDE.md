Akwụkwọ a na-akọwa usoro mbugharị OmniRoute n'ezie na Fly.io, ma na-ekpuchi ọnọdụ ndị a:

- Ibugharị ọrụ dị ugbu a na Fly.io na nke mbụ
- Ibipụta mmelite koodu ndị na-esote
- Ọrụ ọhụrụ ndị na-agbaso otu usoro mbugharị ahụ

Ntuziaka a dabere na nhazi a nwalere ma chọpụta na ọ na-arụ ọrụ maka ọrụ dị ugbu a. Aha ngwa ahụ bụ `omniroute`.

## 1. Ebumnuche Mbugote

- Ikpo okwu: Fly.io
- Usoro mbugote: Mbipụta ozugbo site na `flyctl` mpaghara
- Ebe ọrụ: Iji `Dockerfile` na `fly.toml` ndị dị ugbu a n'ebe nchekwa koodu
- Nchekwa data na-adịgide adịgide: Fly Volume etinyere na `/data`
- URL nnweta: `https://omniroute.fly.dev/`

---

## 2. Nhazi Ndị Dị Mkpa nke Ọrụ Dị Ugbu A

Ekwenyela na `fly.toml` dị n'ebe nchekwa koodu ugbu a nwere ihe ndị a dị mkpa:

```toml
app = 'omniroute'
primary_region = 'sin'

[[mounts]]
  source = 'data'
  destination = '/data'

[processes]
  app = 'node run-standalone.mjs'

[http_service]
  internal_port = 20128

[env]
  TZ = "Asia/Shanghai"
  HOST = "0.0.0.0"
  HOSTNAME = "0.0.0.0"
  BIND = "0.0.0.0"
```

Nkọwa:

- `app = 'omniroute'` na-ekpebi ngwa Fly mbugote ahụ ga-aga na ya
- `destination = '/data'` na-ekpebi ndekọ ebe a ga-etinye volume na-adịgide adịgide
- Ọrụ a ga-edoberịrị `DATA_DIR=/data`, ma ọ bụghị ya, a ga-ede nchekwa data na igodo n'ime ndekọ nwa oge nke container ahụ

## 4. Ibugote Ọrụ Dị Ugbu A na Nke Mbụ

```powershell
git clone https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute.git
cd OmniRoute
```

### 4.2 Kwadoo Aha Ngwa ahụ

Mepee `fly.toml` ma nyochaa ahịrị na-esonụ:

```toml

```

Ọ bụrụ na ị na-ebuga ya na ngwa ọhụrụ nke gị, ị nwere ike ịgbanwe ya gaa n'aha pụrụ iche n'ụwa niile, dịka ọmụmaatụ:

app = 'omniroute-yourname'

Rịba ama:

- Gbaa mbọ hụ na ngwa ị na-ahụ na console dabara na uru `app` dị na `fly.toml`

### 4.3 Mepụta Ngwa ahụ

Ọ bụrụ na ngwa ahụ adịghị ugbu a:

```powershell
flyctl apps create omniroute
```

Ọ bụrụ na ị gbanwere aha ngwa ahụ, jiri aha ị họọrọ dochie `omniroute`.

### 4.4 Mbugote Mbụ

```powershell
flyctl deploy
```

## 5. Paramita Ndị A Chọrọ

Ọrụ a na-atụ aro ka ị hazie ma ọ dịkarịa ala paramita ndị na-esonụ na Fly.io.

### 5.1 Paramita Ndị E Kwadoburu

E jirila paramita ndị a mee mbugote n'ezie na ngwa `omniroute` dị ugbu a:

- `API_KEY_SECRET`
- `DATA_DIR`
- `JWT_SECRET`
- `MACHINE_ID_SALT`
- `NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_URL`
- `OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET` (a chọrọ ya na production — a na-eji ya maka nkwenye njirimara WebSocket bridge)
- `STORAGE_ENCRYPTION_KEY`

### 5.2 Banyere `INITIAL_PASSWORD`

Ọrụ dị ugbu a anaghị edobe `INITIAL_PASSWORD` n'ihi na mbugote a achọghị ya.

Ọ bụrụ na edoghị ya:

- Startup log ga-egosi na okwuntughe ndabara bụ `CHANGEME`
- Ị kwesịrị ịgbanwe okwuntughe nbanye na ntọala sistemụ ozugbo enwere ike mgbe mbugote gasịrị

Ọ bụrụ na ịchọrọ ịmalite okwuntughe backend n'enweghị enyemaka mmadụ, ị nwere ike ịgbakwunye ya ma emechaa:

- `INITIAL_PASSWORD`

## 6. Paramita Ndị A Tụrụ Aro

### 6.1 Nhazi Nzuzo

A na-atụ aro mgbanwe ndị a maka Fly Secrets:

| Mgbanwe                      | Ntụnye Aro                | Nkọwa                                               |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| `API_KEY_SECRET`             | Achọrọ                    | A na-eji ya emepụta ma nyochaa API Key              |
| `JWT_SECRET`                 | Achọrọ                    | A na-eji ya maka nnọkọ nbanye na ịbịanye aka na JWT |
| `OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET` | Achọrọ na mmepụta         | Nzuzo nkwenye njirimara maka njikọ WebSocket        |
| `STORAGE_ENCRYPTION_KEY`     | A na-atụ aro ya nke ukwuu | Na-ezochi ozi njikọ dị mkpa mgbe echekwara ya       |
| `MACHINE_ID_SALT`            | A tụrụ aro                | Na-emepụta njirimara igwe na-adịgide adịgide        |
| `INITIAL_PASSWORD`           | Nhọrọ                     | Na-edobe okwuntughe mbụ nke backend na mbunye mbụ   |
| Nzere nzuzo OAuth/API        | Dị ka mkpa si dị          | Nhazi nkwenye njirimara maka ikpo okwu mpụga        |

### 6.2 Uru Ndị A Tụrụ Aro Maka Ihe Ndị A Na-arụ Ugbu A

| Mgbanwe                | Uru A Tụrụ Aro              |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------- |
| `DATA_DIR`             | `/data`                     |
| `NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_URL` | `https://omniroute.fly.dev` |

Ihe ndetu:

- `DATA_DIR=/data` dị oke mkpa ma ga-adabarịrị ebe e jikọtara Fly Volume
- Onye nhazi oge, callback nke frontend, na ọnọdụ ndị yiri ya na-eji `NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_URL`

### 6.3 Nhazi URL Callback OAuth

Ọ bụrụ na ịchọrọ ime ka ndị na-enye ọrụ dabere na OAuth (dịka Antigravity, Gemini, Cursor) rụọ ọrụ na mbunye Fly.io, gbaa mbọ hụ isi ihe abụọ ndị a:

1. **Debe `NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_URL` ka ọ bụrụ ngalaba HTTPS ọha gị**

   ```powershell
   flyctl secrets set NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_URL=https://omniroute.fly.dev -a omniroute
   ```

   Ọ bụrụ na ị na-eji ngalaba ahaziri iche, jiri ngalaba kwekọrọ dochie ya (dịka `https://omniroute.yourdomain.com`).

2. **Hazie URL callback na console nke onye na-enye ọrụ**

   Ndị niile na-enye OAuth na-eji otu ụzọ callback `/callback` — ENWEGHỊ ụzọ callback dị iche maka onye na-enye ọrụ ọ bụla:

   ```text
   <NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_URL>/callback
   ```

   Dịka ọmụmaatụ, ma ọ bụ Gemini, Antigravity, Cursor, ma ọ bụ GitLab Duo:
   - `https://omniroute.fly.dev/callback`

   Ọ bụrụ na `NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_URL` adabaghị na URL callback e debanyere aha n'aka onye na-enye ọrụ, usoro OAuth ga-ada na nzọụkwụ ntụgharị ihe nchọgharị.

## 7. Iji Otu Iwu Hazie Nzuzo

Iwu ndị a na-emepụta uru enweghị usoro e chekwara nke ọma ma dee paramita niile achọrọ maka ihe ndị a na-arụ ugbu a na Fly Secrets n'otu nzọụkwụ.

Ihe ndetu:

- Ọ naghị agụnye `INITIAL_PASSWORD`
- Ezubere ya maka ihe ndị a na-arụ ugbu a `omniroute`

```powershell
$apiKeySecret = [Convert]::ToHexString((1..32 | ForEach-Object { Get-Random -Minimum 0 -Maximum 256 })).ToLower()
$jwtSecret = [Convert]::ToHexString((1..64 | ForEach-Object { Get-Random -Minimum 0 -Maximum 256 })).ToLower()
$machineIdSalt = [Convert]::ToHexString((1..32 | ForEach-Object { Get-Random -Minimum 0 -Maximum 256 })).ToLower()
$storageKey = [Convert]::ToHexString((1..32 | ForEach-Object { Get-Random -Minimum 0 -Maximum 256 })).ToLower()
$wsBridgeSecret = [Convert]::ToHexString((1..32 | ForEach-Object { Get-Random -Minimum 0 -Maximum 256 })).ToLower()

flyctl secrets set `
  API_KEY_SECRET=$apiKeySecret `
  JWT_SECRET=$jwtSecret `
  MACHINE_ID_SALT=$machineIdSalt `
  STORAGE_ENCRYPTION_KEY=$storageKey `
  OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET=$wsBridgeSecret `
  DATA_DIR=/data `
  NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_URL=https://omniroute.fly.dev `
  -a omniroute
```

Na Linux / macOS, ị nwekwara ike iji `openssl rand -hex 32`:

```bash
flyctl secrets set OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET=$(openssl rand -hex 32) -a omniroute
```

Ihe ndetu:

- A chọrọ `OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET` na mmepụta; ọ bụrụ na ọ dịghị, nkwanye aka nke njikọ WebSocket ga-emebi

Ọ bụrụ na ịchọrọ ịtọkwa okwuntughe mbụ:

```powershell
flyctl secrets set INITIAL_PASSWORD=your-strong-password -a omniroute
```

## 9. Mmelite na Mwepụta Ndị Na-esote

Mgbe emelitechara koodu, usoro mwepụta ahụ dị mfe:

```powershell
git pull
flyctl deploy
```

Ọ bụrụ naanị na ịchọrọ imelite paramita n’agbanweghị koodu:

```powershell
flyctl secrets set KEY=value -a omniroute
```

Fly ga-eme mmelite igwe n’usoro na-akpaghị aka.

### 9.1 Ịso Mmelite Ebe Nchekwa Upstream Ka Ị Na-echekwa `fly.toml` nke Fork Gị

Ọ bụrụ na ebe nchekwa dị ugbu a bụ fork ma ịchọrọ imekọrịta mmelite sitere na upstream `https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute`, soro usoro ọrụ dị n'okpuru.

Nke mbụ, nyochaa remotes gị:

```powershell
git remote -v
```

Ị kwesịrị ịhụ ma ọ dịkarịa ala:

- `origin` na-atụ aka na fork nke gị
- `upstream` na-atụ aka na ebe nchekwa mbụ

Ọ bụrụ na ahazibeghị `upstream`, tinye ya:

```powershell
git remote add upstream https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute.git
```

Tupu imekọrịta na upstream, weta commits na tags kachasị ọhụrụ:

```powershell
git fetch upstream --tags
```

Lelee ụdị dị ugbu a na tags nke upstream:

```powershell
git describe --tags --always
git show --no-patch --oneline v3.4.7
```

> Rịba ama: Ụdị ọrụ a dị ugbu a bụ `v3.8.0`. E debere ntụaka `v3.4.7` ndị dị n'okpuru naanị dịka ihe atụ akụkọ ihe mere eme. Maka mwepụta ndị a na-eji eme ihe n'ezie, jiri `:latest` ma ọ bụ tag nke ụdị dị ugbu a (dịka `:v3.8.0`).

Ọ bụrụ na ịchọrọ ijikọta `main` kachasị ọhụrụ nke upstream ma jigidesie `fly.toml` nke fork gị ike, soro usoro ọrụ a:

```powershell
git merge upstream/main
git checkout HEAD~1 -- fly.toml
git add -- fly.toml
git commit -m "chore(deploy): keep fork fly.toml"
git push origin main
```

Ihe ndị ị ga-eburu n'uche:

- `git merge upstream/main` na-emekọrịta koodu kachasị ọhụrụ sitere na ebe nchekwa mbụ
- `git checkout HEAD~1 -- fly.toml` na-eweghachi `fly.toml` nke fork gị dịka ọ dị tupu njikọta ahụ
- Ọ bụrụ na upstream agbanwebeghị `fly.toml`, nzọụkwụ a agaghị eweta ọdịiche ọ bụla
- Ọ bụrụ na upstream gbanwere `fly.toml`, nzọụkwụ a na-ahụ na edegharịghị aha ngwa Fly gị, njikọta volume, region, na nhazi mbugharị ndị ọzọ pụrụ iche nye fork gị

Ọ bụrụ na ịchọrọ ịhazi ya ka ọ daba na otu tag mwepụta akọwapụtara (dịka `v3.4.7`), buru ụzọ chọpụta na tag ahụ adịlarị na `upstream/main`:

```powershell
git merge-base --is-ancestor v3.4.7 upstream/main
```

Nlaghachi gara nke ọma pụtara na `upstream/main` enweelarị ụdị ahụ; ị nwere ike ijikọta `upstream/main` ozugbo.

### 9.2 Usoro Mwepụta Ọkọlọtọ Mgbe Emekọrịtachara Upstream

Mgbe emekọrịtachara ya na ebe nchekwa mbụ, soro usoro mwepụta a akwadoro:

1. `git fetch upstream --tags`
2. `git merge upstream/main`
3. Weghachite `fly.toml` nke fork ahụ
4. `git push origin main`
5. `flyctl deploy`
6. `flyctl status -a omniroute`
7. `flyctl logs --no-tail -a omniroute`

Nke a bụ usoro ọrụ e ji mee ihe n'ezie mgbe a na-ebulite ọrụ dị ugbu a gaa na `v3.4.7` (ihe atụ a na-ezo aka na ụdị akụkọ ihe mere eme; ụdị dị ugbu a n'ezie bụ `v3.8.0`).

## 11. Ihe Na-egosi Ihe Ịga nke Ọma

Mgbe mbugharị gara nke ọma, ndekọ kwesịrị igosi ọdịnaya yiri nke a:

```text
[bootstrap] Edebere ihe nzuzo na: /data/server.env
[DB] Ebe nchekwa data SQLite adịla njikere: /data/storage.sqlite
```

Isi ihe abụọ ndị a dị oke mkpa:

- `/data/server.env` na-akwado na edere ihe nzuzo oge ojiji na voluum na-adịgide adịgide
- `/data/storage.sqlite` na-akwado na edere ebe nchekwa data na voluum na-adịgide adịgide

Ọ bụrụ na ị hụ `/app/data/...` kama nke ahụ, ahazighị `DATA_DIR` nke ọma, a ga-edozikwa ya ozugbo.

## 12. Nsogbu Ndị A Na-ahụkarị

### 12.1 Ibe `Secrets` Ghe Ọtọ

Ọ na-abụkarị n'ihi otu n'ime ihe abụọ ndị a:

- Ị kabeghị agba `flyctl secrets set`
- Ị na-ele ngwa dị iche (dịka ọmụmaatụ, `oroute` kama `omniroute`)

### 12.2 `flyctl deploy` Na-akọ `app not found`

Buru ụzọ mepụta ngwa ahụ:

```powershell
flyctl apps create omniroute
```

### 12.3 Ntụgharị `fly.toml` Dara

Lelee ihe ndị a:

- Ma enwere mkpụrụedemede ghasara aghasa na nkọwa
- Ma nhota na nhazi oghere TOML ziri ezi

### 12.4 Data Anaghị Adịgide

Nyochaa ihe abụọ ndị a:

- `fly.toml` nwere `destination = '/data'`
- Atọrọ `DATA_DIR` ka ọ bụrụ `/data`

### 12.5 Ọ Nwere Ike Ịrụ Ọrụ Na-enweghị `INITIAL_PASSWORD`?

Ee, ọ nwere ike ịrụ ọrụ. Ọ ga-alaghachi n'iji okwuntughe ndabara `CHANGEME`. A na-atụ aro ka ị gbanwee okwuntughe backend ozugbo enwere ike n'ebe nrụpụta.

## 13. Iji Ya Ọzọ Maka Ọrụ Ngo Ọhụrụ

Ọ bụrụ na ị na-ebuga ọrụ ngo ọhụrụ site n'ịgbaso akwụkwọ a, naanị ihe ndị a ka ị ga-agbanwe:

1. Gbanwee uru `app` dị na `fly.toml`
2. Gbanwee `NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_URL`
3. Debe `DATA_DIR=/data`
4. Mepụtaghachi `API_KEY_SECRET`, `JWT_SECRET`, `MACHINE_ID_SALT`, na `STORAGE_ENCRYPTION_KEY`
5. Mgbe mbugharị mbụ gasịrị, nyochaa na edere ndekọ na `/data`

Ejikwala igodo sitere n'ọrụ ngo gara aga ọzọ.

## 14. Ndepụta Nyocha Mwepụta Kachasị Nta Maka Ọrụ Ngo Dị Ugbu A

Iwu ndị a bụ ndị a na-ejikarị eme ihe maka mwepụta ndị na-esote:

```powershell
flyctl auth whoami
flyctl status -a omniroute
flyctl secrets list -a omniroute
flyctl deploy
flyctl logs --no-tail -a omniroute
```

Maka mwepụta nkịtị, iwu bụ isi bụ naanị:

```powershell
flyctl deploy
```

Maka mbugharị mbụ na gburugburu ọhụrụ, usoro ndị bụ isi bụ:

1. `flyctl auth login`
2. `flyctl apps create omniroute`
3. `flyctl secrets set ... -a omniroute`
4. `flyctl deploy`
5. `flyctl logs --no-tail -a omniroute`
