## Akụkụ 2 — Ntụle Dị Mkpa (ike + adịghị ike ndị e kwuru n’eziokwu)

### Ike (ihe ndị karịrị nkezi)

1. **Igwe ratchet nwere ọtụtụ metric.** Nke a bụ obi nke sistemụ ahụ. Metric 24 dị na `quality-baseline.json`
   - Baseline 4 pụrụ iche, nke ọ bụla nwere ntụziaka (`up`/`down`), nnabata (`eps`), ohere
     (`tightenSlack`) na akara `dedicatedGate`. Ihe ndị e doziri **na-anọgide** na-adịzi mma — ọ bụ
     ihe mgbochi megide mmebi nwayọọ nwayọọ nke codebase.
2. **Nchebe nwere ọtụtụ oyi akwa maka supply-chain.** SAST (CodeQL/Sonar) + secrets (gitleaks nwere
   `useDefault`) + SCA (osv/npm-audit/Trivy/Dependabot) + licenses + lockfile + SBOM + SLSA
   provenance + Scorecard + ime ka workflow sie ike (zizmor). Ọ bụ naanị codebase ole na ole nwere stack a zuru oke.
3. **Ihe mgbochi megide Iwu Goodhart.** Iji coverage dị ka ebumnuche bụ anti-pattern a ma ama
   ("mgbe ihe e ji atụ ihe ghọrọ ebumnuche, ọ kwụsị ịbụ ezigbo ihe atụ"). Anyị nwere ihe ndị
   na-eme ka ọ guzozie: **mutation testing** (na-atụ ma test ahụ ọ na-achọpụta bug ahụ, ọ bụghị naanị
   ma ọ na-eme execute line ahụ), **`check-test-masking`** (na-egbochi ime ka assert ghara isi ike ka ọ gafee),
   **oke coverage n’otu module n’otu module** (na-amanye ịnwale code nwere nnukwu ihe ize ndụ, ọ bụghị naanị akụkụ ndị dị mfe) na
   **`check-pr-evidence`** (Iwu Siri Ike #18).
4. **Gate megide hallucination / enweghị nkwekọrịta.** Ụdị a anaghị adịkarị ma baa uru: `check-known-symbols`,
   `check-fetch-targets`, `check-openapi-routes`, `check-docs-symbols` na-ahụ na docs, specs na
   string dispatches na-atụ aka na symbol ndị ka dị ndụ. Ọ na-achọpụta “ire ure” nke lint/test anaghị achọpụta.
5. **Usoro ndụ advisory→blocking.** Gate ọhụrụ na-amalite dị ka advisory (anaghị egbochi merge mgbe
   ha ka na-eto eto), ma mesịa bụrụ blocking na njedebe cycle. Ọ na-ebelata esemokwu n’ebughị ụzọ tufuo oke kachasị elu.
6. **Skip n’enweghị nsogbu mgbe infra adịghị.** Scanner (`--ratchet`) na-eji `exit 0` pụọ ma ọ bụrụ na binary/network
   adaala — infra na-adịghị adị anaghị egbochi PR ziri ezi. Nke a bụ engineering tozuru etozu.
7. **Omenala edere dịka iwu.** Iwu Siri Ike + `trust-but-verify` + stale-allowlist + evidence-gate
   na-agbanwe ịdọ aka ná ntị ka ọ bụrụ nkwenye akpaaka.

### Adịghị ike ndị e kwuru n’eziokwu (oghere ndị dị adị)

1. **🔴 Nkewa fast-gates ka na-ahapụ oghere n’usoro.** `quality.yml` (PR→`release/**`)
   na-agba typecheck ugbu a, test deterministic ndị dị ngwa, yana production build advisory maka code PR,
   mana ọ ka anaghị agba surface release-PR zuru ezu sitere na `ci.yml` (coverage ratchets,
   package artifact, integration, E2E, SonarQube). Ihe kpatara ya (ọsọ) ziri ezi, mana gate ahụ
   kwesịrị ịdị ebe merge na-eme (shift-left). **Nke a bụ ndozi usoro kachasị ukwuu ka na-echere.**
2. **🟠 Ihe ize ndụ nke gate ịba ụba/ịgwụ ndị mmadụ ike.** Ihe dịka gate 46 + job 25 dị UKWU nke ukwuu. Sonar n’onwe ya dọrọ aka ná ntị:
   condition karịrị akarị na-ebute “ike ọgwụgwụ gate” na arụmụka gbasara ihe kacha mkpa, tinyere ihe ize ndụ nke
   ileghara gate anya. DORA dọrọ aka ná ntị na gate ndị dị arọ na-abawanye lead-time. Anyị na-ebelata nke a site na advisory tier na
   ratchet ndị na-abụghị absolute, mana **ntụle ROI kwa oge maka gate ọ bụla** adịghị (enwere ike ijikọta ụfọdụ micro-gate maka
   doc-sync).
3. **🟠 Mutation score abụbeghị ratchet.** Ihe mgbochi kachasị ike megide iji coverage egwuri egwu bụ
   **advisory**. Ọ bụ ihe ka na-echere nke bara uru kachasị elu (ma arụchaalarị 90% ya).
4. **🟡 Advisory ndị kwesịrị ịbụ blocking (n’ime scope kwesịrị ekwesị).** `osv` (vulnCount) na `oasdiff` bụ
   advisory n’agbanyeghị baseline ndị akpọchiri. osv-advisory nwere ezi uche (CVE ọhụrụ na dep ochie nwere ike igbochi
   PR na-enweghị njikọ) — mana enwere etiti kwesịrị ekwesị (gbochie naanị CRITICAL+fixable, dịka anyị mere na
   Trivy). Ịhapụ oasdiff dịka advisory pụtara na mgbanwe na-emebi contract nwere ike ịgafe.
5. **🟡 Runtime security na-agba naanị n’abalị.** schemathesis/garak/promptfoo/chaos/k6 na-agba n’abalị.
   Mkpebi ziri ezi (ha na-egbu oge, ma chọọ live server), mana PR nwere ike iweta regression na injection-guard
   nke a ga-achọpụta naanị n’abalị na-esote.
6. **🟡 Branch-protection na `main` dị OFF.** `BRANCH_LOCK_TOKEN` na-akpọchi branch _release_, mana
   `main` n’onwe ya enweghị nchebe. Scorecard/DSOMM na-ebelata akara n’ihi ya. A chọrọ ka owner mee ihe.
7. **🟡 CodeQL default-setup; semgrep abụghị ihe e dere dịka iwu.** default-setup na-arụ ọrụ (alert 0), mana
   `codeql.yml` e commitere na-enye njikwa ka ukwuu; semgrep na-agba site na external cloud platform, ọ bụghị
   ihe e tinyere version ya na repo.

## Akụkụ 3 — Katalọgụ Zuru Ezu nke Ebe Nnyocha Ogo (nwere ike ibugharị)

Ụdị 12 dị n'okpuru bụ "usoro ogo" n'ụdị enwere ike iji ọzọ. Nke ọ bụla depụtara
**ebumnuche** (ihe a ga-echekwa), **ngwaọrụ anyị na-eji** na **ihe dakọtara na ya nke na-adabereghị na ngwaọrụ**
iji megharịa ya na stack ọ bụla.

### 1. Ụdị & nhazi (a kapịrị ọnụ, ngwa ngwa)

- **OmniRoute:** Prettier + ESLint site na lint-staged (pre-commit), oghere 2/akara nhota abụọ/100col.
- **Izugbe:** otu formatter nwere ike idozi ihe na-akpaghị aka + otu linter, na-arụ ọrụ na pre-commit n'elu faịlụ ndị e tinyere na staged.

### 2. Ụdị data

- **OmniRoute:** `typecheck:core` (na-egbochi) + `typecheck:noimplicit:core` (ndụmọdụ) + ratchet `type-coverage` 92.17% + oke any nke faịlụ ọ bụla.
- **Izugbe:** typecheck siri ike na CI + metrik type-coverage nwere ratchet + oke `any`/ụzọ mgbapụ nke faịlụ ọ bụla.

### 3. Nnwale (ike)

- **OmniRoute:** runners 2 ndị na-adịghị ekpuchi ibe ha (Node native + vitest), shards 8, coverage zuru ụwa ọnụ 60/60/60/60 + ratchet ~76% + **oke ala 8 n'otu modul maka modul ndị dị oke mkpa** + nnwale property kwa abalị + **nnwale mutation** kwa abalị.
- **Izugbe:** test runner(s) + oke ala coverage **zuru oke** (mgbochi efu) + **ratchet** coverage (mgbochi nlọghachi azụ) + **oke ala n'otu modul maka koodu nwere nnukwu ihe ize ndụ** (mgbochi Goodhart) + nnwale dabere na property maka mgbagha dị ọcha + **nnwale mutation** kwa abalị dịka ezigbo ihe atụ ogo nnwale.

### 4. Iwu nnwale (mgbochi aghụghọ)

- **OmniRoute:** `pr-test-policy` (koodu prod chọrọ nnwale), `check-test-masking` (na-egbochi asserts ndị e mere ka ha ghara isi ike), `pr-evidence` (nkwupụta ihe ịga nke ọma chọrọ blọk ihe akaebe), `test-discovery` (runner na-anakọta nnwale ọ bụla).
- **Izugbe:** ọnụ ụzọ "koodu ọhụrụ ⇒ nnwale ọhụrụ" + onye nchọpụta assert e wepụrụ/tautology + ihe achọrọ maka ihe akaebe (TDD ma ọ bụ nnwale dị ndụ) + nkwa na ọ dịghị nnwale a hapụrụ n'èzí globs.

### 5. Mgbagwoju anya & ahụike koodu (ratchets)

- **OmniRoute:** ịdọ aka ná ntị ESLint (3769↓), mmegharị jscpd (5.72%↓), mgbagwoju anya cyclomatic+max-lines (1800↓), mgbagwoju anya cognitive sonarjs (753↓), koodu nwụrụ anwụ/exports anaghị eji knip (339↓), nha faịlụ n'otu faịlụ (akpọchiri, naanị ibelata), circular-deps (Tarjan omenala, na-egbochi).
- **Izugbe:** tinye ratchet na metrik ahụike ọ bụla (ịdọ aka ná ntị, mmegharị, mgbagwoju anya cyclomatic **na** cognitive, koodu nwụrụ anwụ, nha faịlụ, okirikiri import). Ntuziaka bụ mgbe niile "ekwela ka ọ laghachi azụ".

### 6. Nche static (SAST + ihe nzuzo)

- **OmniRoute:** CodeQL (ọkwa ọkwa ratchet = 0), gitleaks (`[extend] useDefault=true` — dị oke mkpa!), SonarQube, iwu nche omenala (public-creds, error-helper, route-guard-membership, route-validation).
- **Izugbe:** SAST (CodeQL/Sonar/semgrep) nwere ratchet ọkwa ọkwa + ihe nyocha ihe nzuzo nwere **ruleset ndabara e ketara** (nhazi omenala nke na-anọchi ndabara = ikpu ìsì) + ọnụ ụzọ nche Hard Rule nke metụtara kpọmkwem ọrụ ahụ.

### 7. Agbụ ọkọnọ (dependencies)

- **OmniRoute:** osv-scanner + npm-audit + Trivy + Dependabot (SCA), license-checker (SPDX allowlist), lockfile-lint (HTTPS+sha512+registry), `check-deps` mgbochi slopsquatting (allowlist + afọ ≥72h).
- **Izugbe:** SCA sitere n'ọtụtụ ebe + allowlist ikike + nyocha iguzosi ike n'ezi ihe lockfile + allowlist dependency nwere nyocha afọ/typosquatting + bot mmelite e kekọtara n'ìgwè.

### 8. Agbụ ọkọnọ (build & release)

- **OmniRoute:** SBOM (CycloneDX + syft), provenance SLSA (`--provenance`), OpenSSF Scorecard (kwa izu), ime ka workflow sie ike (zizmor: artipacked→`persist-credentials:false`, cache-poisoning, token-permissions).
- **Izugbe:** mepụta SBOM mgbe a na-ebipụta + provenance e binyere aka (SLSA L2+) + Scorecard a haziri oge ya + mee ka workflow niile sie ike (token nwere ikike kacha nta, enweghị credentials echekwara na checkout nke onye na-abụghị pusher, actions e ji SHA kpọchie).

### 9. Nkwekọrịta & API

- **OmniRoute:** oasdiff (mgbanwe OpenAPI na-emebi ihe), schemathesis (contract fuzz kwa abalị), openapi-coverage (% nke routes e dere akwụkwọ maka ha, ratchet 38.3%), openapi-security-tiers (spec megide route-guard).
- **Izugbe:** contract diff nke mgbanwe na-emebi ihe (oasdiff/buf) + fuzz dabere na property megide spec (schemathesis) + coverage akwụkwọ nwere ratchet + nkwekọrịta spec↔code.

### 10. Akwụkwọ & i18n (mgbochi ire ere)

- **OmniRoute:** docs-sync (ụdị e mere ka ha yiri ibe ha), docs-counts-sync (ọnụọgụ dị na docs megide koodu), env-doc-sync, doc-links, fabricated-docs, cli-i18n, i18n-ui-coverage (`--threshold=65` + ratchet 80.1%).
- **Izugbe:** mekọrịta ụdị/ọnụọgụ/env-vars n'etiti docs na koodu (ọnụ ụzọ, ọ bụghị ntụkwasị obi) + nyochaa njikọ ime + coverage i18n nwere ratchet.

### 11. Mgbochi hallucination / nkwekọrịta (ụdị a na-adịghị ahụkarị)

- **OmniRoute:** known-symbols (string dispatch ⇒ symbol dị ndụ), provider-consistency, fetch-targets (client fetch ⇒ route dị adị), docs-symbols, db-rules (Hard Rules #2/#5), migration-numbering.
- **Izugbe:** maka "isi mmalite eziokwu e megharịrị" ọ bụla (registry, string dispatch, ntụaka gafere layer), ọnụ ụzọ na-egosi na akụkụ abụọ ahụ kwekọrọ. Ọ na-ejide ire ere nke typecheck/nnwale anaghị ejide.

### 12. Nkwụsi ike & ngalaba (kpọmkwem maka ngwaahịa)

- **OmniRoute:** chaos (ịtinye mmejọ), heap-growth (leak), k6 (soak), promptfoo+garak (LLM red-team OWASP LLM Top 10), iwu nkwụsi ike 3 (circuit-breaker/cooldown/lockout).
- **Izugbe:** chọpụta ụzọ ọdịda nke ngalaba **gị** ma nwee ọnụ ụzọ (ọbụlagodi ma ọ bụrụ kwa abalị) maka nke ọ bụla. Maka ngwa AI: red-team injection. Maka distributed systems: chaos + leak + soak.

## Nkebi 4 — Atụmatụ Mmegharị Maka Ọrụ Ọ Bụla

Wuo ya na **nkebi**, nke ọ bụla na-eweta uru n'onwe ya. Anwala itinye ngalaba 12 niile n'otu oge —
nke ahụ na-akpata kpọmkwem ike ọgwụgwụ nke ọnụ ụzọ nyocha nke Nkebi 2 dọrọ aka ná ntị banyere ya. Ọnụ ụzọ nyocha ọhụrụ ọ bụla na-amalite dị ka **ndụmọdụ** ma
ghọọ **ihe mgbochi** mgbe ọ kwụsiri ike.

### Isi ihe a pụrụ iji ọzọ: "usoro ime nke ọnụ ụzọ ratchet"

Sistemụ niile gbadoro ụkwụ n'ụkpụrụ faịlụ 3 a. Buru ụzọ detuo ya:

1. **`baseline.json`** — uru metric e mere ka ọ ghara ịgbanwe + `direction` (`up`/`down`) + `eps` (mgbochi flake) + `tightenSlack` + `dedicatedGate`.
2. **`collect-metrics.<ext>`** — na-eme ngwa ahụ, na-ewepụta ọnụọgụ ahụ, ma na-ede `metrics.json`.
3. **`check-ratchet.<ext>`** — na-atụnyere `metrics.json` na `baseline.json`; `exit 1` **naanị** ma ọ bụrụ na ọ laghachiri azụ karịa `eps`; `exit 0` (ịwụli ya n'enweghị nsogbu) ma ọ bụrụ na ngwa/akụrụngwa adịghị; ya na `--require-tighten`, `exit 1` ma ọ bụrụ na ọ **ka mma** n'emeliteghị baseline ahụ (na-eme ka uru ahụ ghara ịla n'iyi).

Mgbe nke a dị, metric ọhụrụ **ọ bụla** (coverage, complexity, warnings, SAST alerts, bundle size, mutation score…) bụ naanị otu ahịrị n'ime baseline.

### Nkebi 0 — Ntọala (izu 1)

CI dị; formatter + linter + typecheck + 1 test runner + oke ala coverage nke bụ **zuru oke**
(dịka ọmụmaatụ, 60%). Pre-commit na-eme nyocha ngwa ngwa ndị a pụrụ idozi na-akpaghị aka. _Nsonaazụ: ọ dịghị PR na-emebi ihe ndị bụ isi._

### Nkebi 1 — Injin ratchet (izu 2) — **ntọala nke ihe niile**

Mepụta faịlụ 3 ndị dị n'elu. Mee ka baselines ghara ịgbanwe maka: warnings, coverage, complexity, duplication,
dead code, file size. _Nsonaazụ: codebase ahụ nwere ike ịka mma naanị site ugbu a gaa n'ihu._

### Nkebi 2 — Omimi nyocha static (izu 3)

SAST (CodeQL/Sonar/semgrep) nwere ratchet maka alert; secrets scanner (**keta ruleset ndabara**);
SCA (osv/Dependabot) + allowlist ikike + lockfile-lint. _Nsonaazụ: adịghị ike nchekwa ndị a maara na
ihe nzuzo ndị gbapụtara agaghị agafe._

### Nkebi 3 — Usoro ọkọnọ build (izu 4)

SBOM mgbe a na-ebipụta + provenance e binyere aka (SLSA L2) + Scorecard ahaziri oge ya + ime ka workflow sie ike
(zizmor: token kacha nta, enweghị credentials echekwara, actions a kpọgidere na ụdị a kapịrị ọnụ). _Nsonaazụ: releases ndị a pụrụ ịchọpụta ebe ha si bịa na ndị a na-apụghị ịgbanwe n'ụzọ aghụghọ._

### Nkebi 4 — Ike nke nnwale (izu 5–6)

Runner nke abụọ ma ọ bụrụ na ọ bara uru; **oke ala coverage maka module ọ bụla dị oke mkpa** (mgbochi-Goodhart);
property-based testing maka pure logic; **mutation testing kwa abalị** → mgbe score nke mbụ bịara, mee
`mutationScore` ka ọ bụrụ ratchet. _Nsonaazụ: coverage akwụsị ịbụ metric eji anya isi; nnwale na-egosi n'ụzọ doro anya na ha na-achọpụta bugs._

### Nkebi 5 — Contract & dynamic (izu 7)

Ọ bụrụ na e nwere API ọha: oasdiff (breaking-change, **ihe mgbochi**) + schemathesis (fuzz kwa abalị).
DAST/red-team kwa abalị dịka o kwesịrị maka ngalaba ahụ. _Nsonaazụ: contracts anaghị emebi n'enweghị onye maara._

### Nkebi 6 — Mgbochi-hallucination & ngalaba (izu 8)

Otu ọnụ ụzọ consistency maka "eziokwu e megharịrị" ọ bụla n'ime ọrụ ahụ. Ọnụ ụzọ failure-mode
ndị akọwapụtara maka ngalaba ahụ (maka AI: injection red-team). _Nsonaazụ: ire ure n'usoro na ọdịda ndị metụtara ngalaba ahụ nwere ụgbụ nchekwa._

### Nkebi 7 — Ọchịchị (na-aga n'ihu)

- Usoro ndụmọdụ→ihe mgbochi maka ọnụ ụzọ ọhụrụ ọ bụla.
- `stale-allowlist`: suppression ọ bụla nwere ihe mere e ji mee ya + issue; a na-achọpụta suppression nke gharala ịdị mkpa.
- `evidence-gate`: nkwupụta ihe ịga nke ọma n'ime PR chọrọ ihe akaebe (test ma ọ bụ living test).
- **Ntụle ROI kwa nkeji afọ maka ọnụ ụzọ ọ bụla** (kwụsị/wepụ ego n'aka ndị na-adịghị eweta uru — nke a na-alụso ike ọgwụgwụ ọgụ).
- Gbanwee Hard Rules nke ọrụ gị ka ha bụrụ ọnụ ụzọ a pụrụ ime ka ha rụọ ọrụ.

### Ụkpụrụ ndị na-emetụta akụkụ niile (a naghị ekwe ka e kparịta ha ụka)

- **Ratchet, ọ bụghị absolute.** Mee ka _enweghị ịlaghachi azụ_ bụrụ ihe ọnụ ụzọ na-enyocha, ọ bụghị ọnụọgụ a kapịrị ọnụ (ewezuga oke ala mgbochi-efu).
- **Absolute floor + ratchet ọnụ.** Floor na-egbochi ọdịda; ratchet na-egbochi mbelata nwayọọ nwayọọ.
- **Mgbochi-Goodhart site na nhazi.** Metric ọ bụla e mere ebumnuche chọrọ ihe na-eme ka ọ guzozie (coverage ⇒ mutation + mgbochi-masking; oke ala maka module ọ bụla iji mee ka a nwalee code siri ike).
- **Ịwụli ya n'enweghị nsogbu.** Akụrụngwa na-adịghị adị anaghị egbochi; naanị ezigbo ịlaghachi azụ na-egbochi.
- **`dedicatedGate` maka metrics dị oke ọnụ.** Metrics chọrọ external binary na-enweta script nke ha (nwere skip), n'èzí ratchet etiti nke na-arụ ọrụ synchronously.
- **Debe ọnụ ụzọ ebe merge na-eme.** Ahapụla oghere n'etiti ọnụ ụzọ ngwa ngwa na merge n'onwe ya (ihe mmụta sitere na nkewa fast-gates).
- **Ọnụ ụzọ mgbochi ole na ole, ndị a họọrọ nke ọma.** Sonar/DORA: conditions karịrị akarị = ike ọgwụgwụ. Họrọ ndụmọdụ + ratchet kama mgbidi jupụtara n'ọnụ ụzọ mgbochi.

## Akụkụ 5 — Nkwalite ndị akwadoro (ahaziri dịka mkpa ha si dị, dakọtara)

**P0 — ROI kachasị elu, fọrọ nke nta ka ọ dị njikere**

1. **Mutation score ratchet** (mgbe Stryker abalị mbụ wepụtara ụkpụrụ). Ọ bụ ihe mgbochi dị mkpa megide coverage-Goodhart; ~90% emechaala.
2. **Mechie oghere fast-gates fọdụrụ** — bulie production build dị na `quality.yml` mgbe izu ndụmọdụ ya gachara ma nọgide na-ebuga nyocha deterministic ndị bụ naanị maka release-PR n'ime ụzọ PR→release.
3. **Branch-protection na `main`** (ntọala onye nwe ya) — na-ebuli Scorecard, na-emechi oghere DSOMM.

**P1 — bara uru** 4. **osv/oasdiff → ka ha bụrụ ihe na-egbochi, n'ime scope kwesịrị ekwesị** — osv naanị CRITICAL+nke enwere ike idozi (usoro nzọụkwụ abụọ dịka Trivy); oasdiff na-egbochi breaking-changes. 5. **`require-tighten` → ka ọ bụrụ ihe na-egbochi** (na ngwụcha cycle) — na-eme ka uru metric ndị enwetara ghara ịla azụ. 6. **Nnyocha ROI/oge maka gate ọ bụla** n'ime `ci-summary` — chọta ma wepụ gates ndị dị nwayọ/uru ha dị ala.

**P2 — uru na-ebelata ka a na-agbakwunye ha** 7. **SLSA L3** — builder hermetic/reproducible (GitHub SLSA generator) ma ọ bụrụ na ịchọrọ isi na L2 gbagoo. 8. **CodeQL config e debere na repo + semgrep nwere version** — njikwa/reproducibility ka mma. 9. **DAST smoke maka PR ọ bụla** — subset ngwa ngwa nke schemathesis/promptfoo na endpoints ndị nwere ihe ize ndụ kachasị elu (ọ bụghị naanị kwa abalị). 10. **Dashboard flakiness + metrics DORA** — hụ na gates anaghị ebelata ọsọ.

## Akụkụ 6 — Ihe ndị a mụtara kpọmkwem site na release (gates a ga-agbakwunye na Phase 9)

> Akụkụ a na-edekọ ezigbo ihe mere mgbe a na-emechi releases ebe gate **na-efu**,
> tinyere ihe akaebe doro anya na gate a tụrụ aro. Ihe ọ bụla bụ candidate maka Akụkụ 5.

### Ihe mmụta v3.8.27 (2026-06-17) — "oghere fast-gates" na-eme ka regressions deterministic ruo ụbọchị release

**Ihe mere.** N'oge v3.8.27 `/generate-release`, release PR (`release/v3.8.27` → `main`)
bụ **oge mbụ** a gbara matrix `ci.yml` zuru ezu n'ime cycle ejikọtara ọnụ. Nsonaazụ: failures 12
n'otu oge — **tests deterministic 3** + ~9 flakes/env. Ọ dịghị nke ọ bụla bụ regression nke ngwaahịa dị ndụ, mana
a hụghị ha niile n'ihi na cycle PRs na-abanye `release/**` site na **Fast QG
(`quality.yml`)**, nke NA-ADỊGHỊ agba unit suite zuru ezu, ma ọ bụ `pr-test-policy` (test-masking), ma ọ bụ
integration suite zuru ezu, ma ọ bụ nyocha schema parity. Ndị deterministic 3 ahụ bụ:

1. **Test UI change mere ka ọ bụrụ nke ochie** — `permissions modal switch buttons declare button type`:
   #4034 gbakwụnyere switch nke anọ (a nọgidere na-edobe a11y `type="button"`); ọnụ ọgụgụ `=== 3` nke test ahụ ghọrọ
   nke ochie. Static analysis kwesịrị ịchọpụta nke a n'ime #4034 PR.
2. **Test packaging change mere ka ọ bụrụ nke ochie** — `findMissingArtifactPaths ... root runtime files`:
   `dist/http-method-guard.cjs` ghọrọ required-path ziri ezi; ndepụta a tụrụ anya ya n'ime test ahụ ghọrọ
   nke ochie.
3. **Ọdịiche sitere na modularization na-efunahụ data (nke kachasị njọ)** — `settings schemas accept ... unprefixed
toggle`: `updateSettingsSchema` nke e mere ka ọ bụrụ **modularized** (`schemas/settings.ts`, nke #3988 mepụtara) kewapụrụ
   na nke canonical (`settingsSchemas.ts`): **fields 45 megide 85 — 40 furu efu + 6 dị iche (qdrant\*)**. Ọ bụ
   **dead-code** (runtime na-eji nke canonical), ya mere o nweghị mmetụta na sistemụ dị ndụ, mana naanị parity
   test e ji aka dee chọpụtara ya. #4030 weghachiri drops 16 yiri nke a sitere na #3988/#3993, mana nke a gafere n'amaghị ama.

**Gates a tụrụ aro (Phase 9):**

- **G1 — Mechie oghere fast-gates n'ezie (na-agbatị P0 #2).** N'ime `quality.yml` (PR→`release/**`),
  karịa typecheck + impacted tests, gbaa **`pr-test-policy` (test-masking) + deterministic
  unit suite zuru ezu** (ma ọ bụ opekata mpe files static/parity, ndị dị ngwa ma na-abụghị flaky).
  N'ụzọ dị otu a, a ga-achọpụta tests ndị ochie na mwepụ assert n'ime PR webatara ha — ọ bụghị n'ụbọchị
  release. Debe integration/e2e n'èzí (dị nwayọ/flaky), mana o ZIGHỊ EZI ka layer deterministic nọgide bụrụ naanị
  na PR→main.
- **G2 — Gate parity maka modularization (ỌHỤRỤ, anaghị ekpuchi ya taa).** Nnyocha nke, maka symbol ọ bụla
  modularized barrel (`src/shared/validation/schemas/*`, modules `providerRegistry`,
  wdg.) na-eme re-export, na-atụnyere **shape** (`z.object` keys, registry entries) ya na source
  canonical ma **daa ma ọ bụrụ na ha dị iche** (field furu efu/agbakọrọ). Ọ gaara achọpụta mfu fields 40 sitere na
  #3988 n'ime PR ahụ kpọmkwem. Ọ na-eme ka parity tests e ji aka dee gbasaa n'ozuzu (ndị dị naanị ebe mmadụ
  chetara ide ha). Ọ dị ọnụ ala: ọ na-import ha abụọ ma na-eme diff nke `Object.keys(shape)`.
- **G3 — Triage deterministic maka flake (nkwado).** LiveWS-startup na integration-combo/breaker
  tests na-ada n'ihi server timeout/cascade n'ime CI (env), ọ bụghị logic. Kaa ndị a dịka
  `known-flaky` (e debere iche tinyere issue) ka uhie release-PR bụrụ **naanị ezigbo signals**, ọ bụghị noise
  na-ekpuchi regressions deterministic ndị dị n'etiti.

**Ụkpụrụ:** _gate ga-agbarịrị n'ebe merge na-eme_ (ọ dịlarị na "Cross-cutting principles"). Ihe mere na
v3.8.27 na-egosi na nke a metụtakwara **deterministic test layer**, ọ bụghị naanị lint/typecheck —
ma ọ bụghị ya, ụgwọ sitere na tests ndị ochie + modularization na-efunahụ data ga-apụta naanị na PR→main, n'otu batch, n'oge
kachasị njọ.
