## Ọgụgụ ọkwa ọhaneze

Ọgụgụ ọkwa izugbe dị iche na akara njirimara Radar. Dashboard Home na
ihe nlele Changelog na-eweta `news.json` ọhaneze nke repository site na `GET` nkịtị gaa na
`NEWS_JSON_URL` (`src/shared/utils/releaseNotes.ts`). Ha anaghị eziga ntọala Radar, prompt, nhazi provider,
ndekọ ojiji, ma ọ bụ ọnọdụ mkpochapụ mpaghara.

`news.json` na-eji schema v2 mechiri emechi nke `parseNewsPayload()` mepụtara:

- `schemaVersion: 2` na nchịkọta `items[]` nwere oke;
- ụkpụrụ `id` ọkwa ndị kwụsiri ike ma bụrụ ndị pụrụ iche;
- field `active` na ISO `publishedAt` ndị akọwapụtara nke ọma;
- ederede Bekee dị mkpa yana ederede asụsụ mpaghara nke bụ nhọrọ;
- njikọ HTTPS na-achọghị credential na icon sitere na allowlist, ha abụọ bụ nhọrọ;
- nhọpụta nke kachasị ọhụrụ ma na-arụ ọrụ mbụ, ịlaghachi na Bekee ma ọ bụrụ na locale adịghị, na mkpochapụ mpaghara maka ID ọ bụla.

Parser ahụ na-anabata nwa oge ụdị otu ihe ochie `{ active, title, message, ... }` ka
fork ochie nwee ike ịkwaga na-enweghị imebi nlele Changelog. Feed na-ezighi ezi anaghị eme ihe ọ bụla. Ntinye mwepụta Radar
na-abịa na `active: false`; ịgbanwe ya ka ọ bụrụ `true` bụ ihe omume mwepụta dị iche a na-eme mgbe merge na deploy gachara,
ọ naghị agbanwe `RADAR_ENABLED` ma ọ bụ opt-in feed-sync nke nọọrọ onwe ya.

## Akara: `RADAR_ENABLED` (agbanyụrụ na ndabara)

A na-egbochi Radar site na mmalite ruo na njedebe site na akara njirimara `RADAR_ENABLED`
(`src/shared/constants/featureFlagDefinitions.ts`, category `policies`,
`defaultValue: "false"`).

**Mgbe agbanyụrụ akara ahụ, akụkụ a adịghị:**

- Endpoint `/api/radar/*` niile, gụnyere ịgụ na ide ọnọdụ model mpaghara,
  na-eweghachi `404` tupu ha emetụ module Radar ọ bụla aka.
- Ihuenyo dashboard (`/dashboard/radar`, `/dashboard/radar/setup`,
  `/dashboard/radar/combos`, `/dashboard/radar/offers`, `/dashboard/radar/intel`) na-render
  `notFound()`.
- `getRadarCatalog()` (`src/lib/radar/index.ts`) na-eweghachi baseline ahụ dịka ọ dị —
  otu ọnụọgụ ntinye ahụ, otu ụkpụrụ ndị ahụ, ntinye ọ bụla nwere akara `origin: "baseline"` — ọ dịghịkwa mgbe ọ
  na-agụ cache feed.
- A naghị akpọ netwọkụ Radar ma ọlị; module sync ọ bụla na-eweghachi `{ status: "disabled" }`
  tupu o metụ `fetch` aka.

Nke a bụ ọnụ ụzọ superset siri ike: ịgbanye akara ahụ na-emeghe naanị _ihuenyo_,
ọ dịghị ihe ọzọ. Ọ naghị ebugo data, ọ naghị amalite sync n'azụ, ọ naghịkwa agbanwe
routing ma ọ bụ nhọpụta model — lee opt-in dị iche n'okpuru.

## Sync data bụ opt-in DỊ ICHE — nkwa nzuzo

Ịgbanye `RADAR_ENABLED` na-emeghe naanị UI. Ime sync feed chọrọ opt-in nke abụọ,
nke nọọrọ onwe ya ma echekwara na `radar_settings.opt_in` (`src/lib/db/radar.ts`,
migration `136_radar_cache_settings.sql`). `syncRadar()` na-enyocha akara ahụ _yana_ opt-in
tupu o mee oku netwọkụ ọ bụla:

```
Agbanyụrụ akara → { status: "disabled" }   — enweghị oku netwọkụ
Opt-in bụ false → { status: "opt_out" }    — enweghị oku netwọkụ
```

Mgbe ha abụọ dị na on, ụzọ sync bụ:

1. `GET <feed base URL>/v1/catalog/latest` nwere `x-omniroute-radar-schema: 2` na header
   `Authorization: Bearer <supporter key>` nke bụ nhọrọ (lee n'okpuru). Server na-eji artifact mgbanwe v1
   a bịanyere aka na ya iche dịka ndabara mgbe schema header adịghị, ka client ochie arụnyere wee nọgide
   na-anata mmelite.
2. Nke a bụ usoro ngwa nke na-ebudata naanị, mana ọ ka bụ request HTTPS. Akụrụngwa
   e hostere na-anata metadata njikọ nkịtị dịka IP isi mmalite. Mgbe ahaziri supporter
   key, sync na-ezigakwa key ahụ na header Bearer ka service ahụ nwee ike ịchọpụta
   entitlement. N'otu revision private-server ahụ kpọmkwem nke akọwapụtara n'ókè ihe akaebe dị n'elu,
   ndekọ request feed na-eji hash key, ojiji agbakọtara, na HMAC e bepụrụ mkpụmkpụ nke IP
   nke na-agbanwe kwa ụbọchị maka nyocha mmegbu a na-eji aka eme; table ndị ahụ anaghị echekwa key ma ọ bụ IP n'ụdị raw.
   Log nnweta akụrụngwa na outbox nnyefe ezoro ezo bụ ókè ọrụ
   dị iche iche.
3. OmniRoute anaghị eziga prompt, response, mkparịta ụka, credential provider, traffic model,
   uptime, latency, ma ọ bụ nhazi provider mpaghara gaa na service Radar.
4. A na-enyocha, validate, ma cache response ahụ na mpaghara (lee
   [Ụdị nchekwa](#security-model)). Radar nwere kpọmkwem ụzọ netwọkụ server-side anọ:
   `syncRadar()` maka catalog, `syncRadarReferrals()` maka referral, na
   `syncRadarOffers()` / `syncRadarIntel()` maka offer na Intel ndị naanị supporter nwere ike inweta.

**Supporter key** bụ token Bearer nke bụ nhọrọ (`radar_settings.supporter_key`)
nke na-enye service feed ohere ikpebi tier ọ ga-enye (lee
[Tier](#tiers-community-and-live)). A na-eme ihe ndị a na ya:

- A na-echekwa ya **n'ụdị ezoro ezo mgbe echekwara ya** site n'otu helper AES-256-GCM `encrypt()`/`decrypt()`
  (`src/lib/db/encryption.ts`) ndị a na-eji maka credential provider.
- A na-edobe ya site na `POST /api/radar/settings` (`{ supporterKey: "omr_" + 40 hex chars }`) ma
  **anaghị eweghachi ya ma ọlị** — response na-eweghachi ụdị kpuchiri ekpuchi (`omr_****abcd`).
- Ịgbanwe ma ọ bụ ihichapụ ya na-eme ka cache catalog, referral, offer, na Intel ghara ịdị irè n'otu oge. Sync/ịgụ
  na-esote ga-achọpụta entitlement ọhụrụ na server-side; ichekwa key anaghị eme
  request netwọkụ n'onwe ya ma ọ bụ jiri activation key otu-ojiji.
- A na-eziga ya na service feed dịka token Bearer na sync GET — ọ dịghị ihe ọzọ gbasara
  key ahụ na-apụ na client.

Akara ahụ anaghị egbochi ma ọ bụ kagbuo igodo na-akpaghị aka. Mweghachi na-akagbu ma dochie igodo
furu efu ma na-echekwa ụbọchị ngwụcha dị ugbu a; ọ naghị ebido oge a zụtara ma ọ bụ e nyere ọzọ.

A na-eji aka ahọpụta onyinye ndị dị ozugbo, ha nwekwara ike ịgbanwe ma ọ bụ gwụchaa. Ihuenyo
nkwenye ahụ na-akọwakwa oke nzuzo kpọmkwem: a na-ebudata metadata katalọgụ/ntụnye aka e binyere
aka; igodo ziri ezi na-emepekwa onyinye e binyere aka na Intel; igodo Bearer na metadata njikọ
nkịtị na-erute ọrụ a na-akwado; prompts, responses, conversations, provider credentials, model
traffic, uptime, latency, na local provider configuration anaghị eru ya.

## Otu esi enweta igodo onye nkwado

Ihu ihuenyo mmalite (`/dashboard/radar`) nwere njikọ gaa n'usoro abụọ maka **inweta**
igodo onye nkwado. Ebe nchekwa OSS n'onwe ya anaghị enye igodo, anaghị eme koodu ịkwụ ụgwọ,
ma **ọ naghị ekwupụta ọnụahịa ma ọlị** — a na-ekpebi ma gosipụta ọnụahịa kpamkpam na
ibe njedebe, ọ bụghị n'ebe nchekwa a (mkpebi nkọwa D14).

- **"Abụ m onye nyere aka"** — na-emepe `RADAR_CONTRIBUTOR_CLAIM_URL` (ndabara
  `https://radar.omniroute.online/auth/github`), usoro nkwupụta GitHub OAuth nke a na-akwado na
  sava Radar nkeonwe. Ọ na-enyocha ọkwa izu zuru ezu kachasị ọhụrụ: Ndị Top 10 na-enweta ụbọchị 365
  ebe ọnọdụ 11–100 na-enweta ụbọchị 90. N'èzí Top 100, ọnụọgụ PR anaghị enye ohere; kama nke ahụ,
  usoro ahụ na-enyocha ọkwa dị iche nke star + follow a na-eji naanị otu ugboro.
- **"Kwado ọrụ a"** — na-emepe `RADAR_SUPPORTER_PLANS_URL` (ndabara
  `https://radar.omniroute.online/planos`), ibe a na-akwado maka nhọrọ otu ugboro nke ọnwa 6, afọ 1,
  na nke ndụ niile. Ibe OSS ahụ ka anaghị egosi uru ego ọ bụla.

A na-ekpebi URL abụọ ahụ n'akụkụ sava (`src/lib/radar/links.ts`, otu usoro env-override
dị ka `RADAR_FEED_URL`) ma ziga ha na dashboard site na nzaghachi
`GET /api/radar/settings` dị ugbu a (`contributorClaimUrl`, `supporterPlansUrl`) — kọmpọnentị
ahịa anaghị agụ `process.env` n'onwe ya.

| Var                           | Ebumnuche                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `RADAR_CONTRIBUTOR_CLAIM_URL` | Na-edochi URL nkwupụta onye nyere aka (ndabara `https://radar.omniroute.online/auth/github`). |
| `RADAR_SUPPORTER_PLANS_URL`   | Na-edochi URL atụmatụ onye nkwado (ndabara `https://radar.omniroute.online/planos`).          |

### Iweghachite igodo onye nkwado furu efu

Ebe mbata mgbake nke ọrụ a na-akwado bụ `https://radar.omniroute.online/recover`; e nwekwara
njikọ ya na ibe atụmatụ. Mgbake ka na-adị kpamkpam n'èzí ahịa OSS n'ihi na nrụnye mpaghara
anaghị enweta e-mail onye zụrụ ihe/onye nyere aka, ọ pụghịkwa iji ntọala ezoro ezo wughachi igodo
raw.

1. Nyefee e-mail ejikọtara na igodo ahụ. Ọrụ ahụ na-eweghachi otu ibe nnabata ahụ ma ikike a pụrụ
   iweghachite ọ̀ dị ma ọ bụ na ọ dịghị, ka fọm ahụ ghara ịdepụta akaụntụ.
2. Ọ bụrụ na o tozuru, worker nnyefe ahụ na-eziga njikọ dị mkpụmkpụ ndụ nke a na-eji otu ugboro. Imepe ya
   na-ebuga token ahụ ozugbo n'ime kuki `HttpOnly`/`Secure` ezoro ezo nwa oge ma bugharịa gaa na URL
   `/recover` dị ọcha; ibe ahụ enweghị token, e-mail, igodo ochie, ma ọ bụ igodo nnọchi.
3. Kwenye na nkagbu ahụ. Ọrụ nkeonwe ahụ na-akagbu igodo gara aga, mepụta nnọchi nwere
   otu atụmatụ/oge ngwụcha, ma tinye ya n'ahịrị maka e-mail n'ime otu transaction. A naghị
   eweghachite nnọchi ahụ na ihe nchọgharị.
4. Tapawa nnọchi ahụ n'ime `/dashboard/radar`. Igodo ochie ahụ ga-agbadarịrị bụrụ `community`; nnọchi
   ahụ ga-emepụta mmekọrịta `live` ekwenyesiri ike. Imepe otu njikọ mgbake ahụ ọzọ ga-adarịrị
   site na nzaghachi izugbe nke na-ekwu na ọ baghị uru/agwụla oge.

Ụzọ mgbake a na-akwado na worker ozi nwere ike ịdị na koodu ma ka ghara ịdị na otu
deployment. Akpọla usoro ahụ nke dị njikere maka production ruo mgbe etinyere sava ahụ, hazie
onye na-enye nnyefe ya na onye nnata a na-achịkwa, ma nwalee njikọ otu-ojiji ahụ n'uju.

Ozugbo onye ọbịa nwere igodo (`omr_` + mkpụrụedemede hex 40), ihuenyo mmalite
(`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/radar/page.tsx`) nwere oghere ntinye tapawa-igodo dị ka ụzọ
bụ isi: ịtapawa igodo na izipu ya na-eziga `POST /api/radar/settings`
(`{ optIn: true, supporterKey }`) n'otu oku — ịtapawa igodo na-edobe ya ma na-ahọrọ isonye,
na-emepe ihuenyo ahụ. A na-ebu ụzọ enyocha usoro ahụ (`omr_` + mkpụrụedemede hex 40) n'akụkụ ahịa
site na enyemaka `isValidSupporterKeyFormat()` a na-ekekọrịta (`src/lib/radar/supporterKey.ts`)
dị ka ihe na-eme ka UX dị mma; schema Zod nke sava ahụ bụ nyocha nwere ikike n'ụzọ ọ bụla. Ozugbo
edobere igodo, ihuenyo mmalite na-egosi ụdị ezoro akụkụ ya (`supporterKeyMasked` sitere na
`GET /api/radar/settings`) kama oghere ntinye efu, yana njikwa "gbanwee igodo" iji
tapawa nke ọhụrụ — a naghị egosipụta igodo raw ahụ ọzọ. Bọtịnụ nkwupụta/atụmatụ abụọ dị n'elu
ka bụ ụzọ isi _nweta_ igodo na mbụ; oghere ntinye a bụ ebe onye njikwa
nwere otu ugbua na-eme ka ọ rụọ ọrụ.

### Mmalite site na nsọtụ ruo na nsọtụ na nhazi a na-eduzi

Ọrụ feed nkeonwe na ahịa OSS a nwere ókè e mere ka ọ dị warara: ọrụ ahụ
na-enye ma na-akwado igodo onye nkwado, ebe nrụnye OmniRoute mpaghara na-ezobe igodo ahụ,
na-emekọrịta artifacts ndị e binyere aka n'akụkụ sava, ma na-eduzi nhazi provider. Usoro nkwado enyere aka bụ:

1. Nweta igodo e nyere ọhụrụ ma ọ bụ nke eweghachiri site na nkwupụta onye nyere aka, plans/checkout, usoro
   mgbake, ma ọ bụ n'aka onye ọrụ sava nkeonwe nwere ikike. Etinyela igodo ahụ n'onwe ya n'ime ndekọ,
   nseta ihuenyo, nkwupụta nsogbu, ma ọ bụ arụmụka ahịrị-iwu.
2. Gbanyụọ ọkọlọtọ njirimara `RADAR_ENABLED` na nrụnye OmniRoute dị n'ime mpaghara. Nke a na-eme ka UI
   pụta, mana ọ gaghị arụ ọrụ na netwọkụ ruo mgbe echekwara nkwenye ịbanye dị iche.
3. Mepee `/dashboard/radar`, mado igodo ahụ, wee mee ka ọ rụọ ọrụ. Ihe nchọgharị ahụ na-eziga otu
   `POST /api/radar/settings` mpaghara nwere `{ optIn: true, supporterKey }`; a na-ezochi igodo ahụ na mpaghara,
   nzaghachi ahụ nwekwara naanị `omr_****<last4>`.
4. Hapụ ihuenyo mbido ọrụ ka ọ mee mmekọrịta katalọgụ ya, ma ọ bụ họrọ **Mekọrịta ugbu a**. Kwenye na ibe ahụ
   na-egosi `live`, ụdị ndepụta ozi, na oge nnweta. Maka nyocha mpaghara nwere nkwenye njirimara,
   `GET /api/radar/status` na-akọ nkwenye ịbanye/ọnụnọ igodo na ọnọdụ nchekwa nwa oge anọ ahụ n'eweghachighị
   igodo ahụ. `POST /api/radar/sync-all` nwere ike imelite katalọgụ, ntụnye, onyinye, na Intel n'ụzọ doro anya.
5. Mepee `/dashboard/radar/setup?provider=<provider>`. Soro URL nzere nke onye na-enye ọrụ nwe,
   họrọ **Tinye igodo API**, chekwaa site na fọm onye na-enye ọrụ n'ezie, laghachi na ntuziaka ahụ, wee mee
   **Nwalee njikọ**. Ntuziaka ahụ na-eji ụzọ `/api/providers` na
   `/api/providers/<connection-id>/test` ndị nkịtị; ọ naghị emepụta nzere Radar ọzọ yiri ya.
6. Mepee `/dashboard/radar/combos` mgbe opekata mpe njikọ ndị na-enye ọrụ abụọ dakọtara nọ n'ọrụ.
   Nyochaa ezinụlọ a tụrụ aro ma mepụta ngwakọta ahụ site na API ngwakọta dị adị. Onyinye na
   Intel ka bụ nchekwa nwa oge bịanyere aka, nke `live` naanị, a pụkwara ịlele ha n'ibe Radar pụrụ iche nke ha.
7. Bugharịa `/dashboard/radar` na ibe ntọlite ọzọ. Nkwenye ịbanye, ọnọdụ igodo ezoro akụkụ ya, nchekwa nwa oge a kwadoro, njikọ
   onye na-enye ọrụ echekwara, na omume nnwale ga-anọgide mgbe e bugharịchara ibe ahụ. Dekọọ ihe akaebe naanị mgbe
   igodo ahụ n'onwe ya na nzere onye na-enye ọrụ anaghịzi apụta.

Ichekwa igodo abụghị n'onwe ya ihe akaebe nke ikike `live`. Ihe akaebe ahụ bụ njikọta nke nsonaazụ
`GET /v1/license/check` nke ọrụ nkeonwe, ọkwa `live` nke katalọgụ OSS na-enye, nchekwa nwa oge bịanyere aka
nke a kwadoro, na usoro njikọ/nnwale onye na-enye ọrụ n'ezie. Igodo na-adịghị irè, kubie ume, ma ọ bụ nke a kagburu
na-eme ka katalọgụ laghachi na `community` n'enweghị nsogbu; a gaghị akọ ya dịka nkwado igodo `live` gara nke ọma.

### Njikọ panel nchịkwa nkeonwe

`RADAR_ADMIN_URL` nwere ike ịgbakwunye **Nchịkwa Radar ↗** ozugbo n'azụ ihe Radar nke onye ọrụ na-ahụ
n'akụkụ Costs nke ogwe akụkụ. E kpachaara anya ghara inye ya uru ndabara: mgbe edoghị mgbanwe ahụ
ma ọ bụ mgbe ọ na-adịghị irè, ogwe akụkụ kwụ otu ebe, palet iwu, na ihuenyo nhazi ogwe akụkụ enweghị
ihe nchịkwa ma ọ bụ URL nkeonwe ọ bụla.

A na-ekpebi uru ahụ n'akụkụ sava ma zipụ ya site na nzaghachi `GET /api/settings`
nke njikwa kwadoro njirimara, naanị gaa na nnọkọ dashboard akwadoro njirimara, ma ọ bụ nye onye nwe
loopback a tụkwasịrị obi n'oge mbido mpaghara na-enweghị nbanye. Nkwado njirimara CLI, ọrụ-ime, na igodo API
nwere oke manage anaghị enweta ya. Ihe nchọgharị ahụ na-enyocha nzaghachi ahụ ọzọ tupu o mepụta
njikọ mpụga ahụ, nke na-eji `noopener noreferrer` emeghe.

Jiri URL ọwara HTTPS/tailnet na-enweghị nzere. A na-anabata HTTP nkịtị naanị maka ntụgharị SSH loopback
dịka `http://127.0.0.1:9351`; usoro ndị ọzọ, nzere agbakwunyere, URL ndị ezighi ezi, na
ebe HTTP dị anya na-emechi n'enweghị nsogbu ma mee ka ngagharị ghara ịrụ ọrụ.

## Ụdị nchekwa

### Mbinye aka Ed25519 n'elu baitị ndị ahụ kpọmkwem

E ji Ed25519 binye aka na payload nke feed ahụ. `verifyFeedBytes()`
(`src/lib/radar/verify.ts`) na-enyocha mbinye aka ahụ n'elu **baitị nzaghachi ndị ahụ kpọmkwem**
a natara site na netwọkụ — a naghị emegharị payload ahụ ka ọ bụrụ usoro ọzọ tupu nyocha, ya mere
ịtụgharị koodu ya n'otu baitị n'otu baitị agaghị enwe ike ime ka nyocha mbinye aka ghara ịdị ire ma ọ bụ gafee ya
na nzuzo. Ọdịda nyocha (`invalid_signature`) na-akwụsị sync tupu e nwee ike
ịtụgharị payload ahụ ma ọ bụ chekwaa ya na cache.

### Igodo ọha akwadoro + ntụgharị igodo

A kwadoro igodo ọha e ji enyocha ihe na `src/lib/radar/pinnedKeys.ts`
(`PINNED_FEED_PUBLIC_KEYS`), nke bụ array ka e nwee ike ịgbakwunye igodo ọhụrụ n'ihu tupu
ntụgharị igodo, ebe feed ochie dị na cache nke e ji igodo gara aga binye aka ka na-adị ire ruo mgbe
emegharịrị sync.

### Ndochi env ndị dị mma maka fork

Env vars abụọ na-enye fork na ndị na-akwado sistemụ n'onwe ha ohere iduzi client gaa na feed nke ha kama
ọrụ OmniRoute ndabara — lee
[Otu esi akwado feed n'onwe gị](#how-to-self-host-a-feed) n'okpuru:

| Var                 | Ebumnuche                                                                                                       |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `RADAR_FEED_URL`    | Na-edochi URL ntọala nke feed (ndabara `https://radar.omniroute.online`).                                       |
| `RADAR_FEED_PUBKEY` | Na-edochi igodo ọha akwadoro (base64-DER SPKI ma ọ bụ PEM), ma jiri otu igodo a dochie array arụnyere n'ime ya. |

### Oke ụdị kacha ala

`syncRadar()` na-ajụ feed ebudatara ma ọ bụrụ na `version` ya abụghị nke ọhụrụ karịa
ụdị dị ugbu a na cache (`compareVersions()`, ntụnyere `YYYY.MM.DD.n` nwere ntụpọ) —
`{ status: "stale" }`. Nke a na-egbochi endpoint feed e mebiri ma ọ bụ ahaziri n'ụzọ na-ezighi ezi
ịweghachi client na payload ochie nke e ji mbinye aka dị iche binye.

### Ụbọchị abụọ, na ihe mere e ji echekwa ha abụọ

Feed dị na cache nwere ụbọchị abụọ dị iche iche, na igbochi mgbagwoju anya n'etiti ha bụ isi ihe mere e ji
echekwa ha abụọ:

| Field         | Ebe o si abịa               | Ihe ọ na-aza                  |
| ------------- | --------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| `generatedAt` | body nke feed e binyere aka | afọ ole **data** ahụ dị       |
| `fetchedAt`   | elekere nke nrụnye a        | mgbe nrụnye a **budatara** ya |

Feed e budatara nkeji ole na ole gara aga nwere ike ịnwe ọnụ ọgụgụ ndị dị izu ole na ole, ya mere naanị `fetchedAt` enweghị ike
ịgwa onye na-ahụ maka ya ma overlay ahụ ọ dị ọhụrụ karịa baseline ọ dị n'elu ya. A na-
echekwa ha abụọ na `radar_feed_cache`, `getRadarCatalog().meta` na-eweghachi ha, ma
`GET /api/radar/status` na-akọ ha iche iche. Row e tinyere na cache tupu column `generated_at`
adị (migration 163) na-alọghachi dị ka `null` — ihe a na-amaghị na-anọgide bụrụ ihe a na-amaghị kama
iji oge e budatara ya dochie ya. `radar_referrals_cache` echekwala `generated_at` nke ya kemgbe
migration 142.

Oke ụdị kacha ala dị n'elu na-atụnyere `version`, ọ bụghị ụbọchị nke ọ bụla n'ime ha.

Oghere abụọ ka dị, ma ha abụọ bụ nke e kpachapụrụ anya hapụ: dashboard ahụ ka na-egosi naanị `Last fetched`, ya mere ịgụ
ụbọchị build n'ebe ahụ chọrọ label ọhụrụ (yana ndenye locale 41 ya); cache nke offers na intel
anaghị echekwa ụbọchị build ma ọlị, n'agbanyeghị na schema feed ha nwere otu — ya mere `GET
/api/radar/status` anaghị etinye field ahụ maka ha abụọ kama ịkọ `null`
nke a ga-agụ dị ka "amaghị".

### Nnyocha schema

A na-atụgharị ma na-enyocha baitị ndị ebudatara megide `RadarFeedSchema`
(`src/lib/radar/feedSchema.ts`, schema Zod) **mgbe** enyochachara mbinye aka. Ekwekọghị
schema na-eweghachi `{ status: "invalid_schema" }`, a hapụkwa cache ahụ
n'emetụghị ya aka. A na-akpachapụ anya enyocha payload dị na cache ọzọ kwa oge a na-agụ ya
(`getRadarCatalog()`) — row cache mebiri emebi ma ọ bụ nke e ji aka dezie na-alaghachi na
baseline kama inye ya.

### Oke nha nzaghachi (10 MB)

`syncRadar()` na-etinye **oke siri ike nke 10 MB** n'ahụ nzaghachi feed — feed e binyere
aka bụ akwụkwọ JSON nke nha ya dị na KB, ya mere ihe ọ bụla gafere nke a na-egosi `RADAR_FEED_URL`
a haziri n'ụzọ na-ezighi ezi ma ọ bụ nke nwere ebumnuche ọjọọ (ma ọ bụ upstream na-enye ihe mkpofu), ọ bụghị catalog ziri ezi.
A na-eme mmanye a n'ọkwa abụọ:

1. Nnyocha preflight nke `Content-Length` na-awụpụ ịgụ body ahụ kpamkpam mgbe
   header ekwuola uru karịrị oke ahụ.
2. Nnyocha ngụkọta na-aga n'ihu mgbe a na-agụ body ahụ na-amanye oke ahụ ọbụna mgbe
   `Content-Length` adịghị ma ọ bụ na-ekwu nha dị ala karịa nke bụ eziokwu — anaghị atụkwasị
   naanị header obi. Ijikọta chunk ndị a chịkọbara na-echekwa baitị ndị ahụ kpọmkwem
   achọrọ maka nyocha mbinye aka Ed25519 e mesịrị.

Ịgafe oke ahụ na-eweghachi `{ status: "too_large" }` ma hapụ cache ahụ n'emetụghị ya aka,
na-agbaso otu usoro anaghị emebi ihe dịka ọdịda sync ndị ọzọ niile
(`invalid_signature`, `invalid_schema`, `stale`).

## Ọkwa: `community` na `live`

Schema feed ahụ nwere field `tier: "community" | "live"`, nke ọrụ feed na-ekpebi **n'akụkụ sava**
dabere na arịrịọ ahụ (ma supporter key dị nakwa ma ọ bụ nke ziri ezi)
— client anaghị ekpebi ọkwa nke ya mgbe ọ bụla.

- **`community`** — katalọgụ efu nke na-anọ ihe dịka ụbọchị 30 n'azụ data
  kachasị ọhụrụ. Nke a bụ ihe arịrịọ na-enweghị nyocha njirimara ma ọ bụ nke nwere key na-ezighi ezi na-enweta.
- **`live`** — katalọgụ kachasị ọhụrụ, nke a na-enye arịrịọ ndị nwere supporter
  key ziri ezi.

**Supporter key na-ezighi ezi ma ọ bụ nke kubiela na-adaghachi na `community` — ọ naghị abụ
njehie mgbe ọ bụla.** Ụzọ sync ahụ na-ekewa naanị ọdịda signature/schema/version (ha niile
nwere ike idozi, ha niile anaghị ebute mmebi na state echekwara) na `{ status:
"updated", version, tier }` gara nke ọma. Enweghị ụzọ njehie pụrụ iche maka ọkwa nke client kwesịrị
ijikwa.

### Ọkwa e nyere na-abịa site na response header, ọ bụghị signed body

Field `tier` nke **body** feed e tinyere mbinye aka na ya na-abụ `"live"` mgbe niile — ọrụ feed na-ezipụ
**artifact abụọ e tinyere mbinye aka na ha maka version ọ bụla**: live gụnyere campaign ndị dị ugbu a ebe community
na-ewepụ ha. A na-etinye mbinye aka na artifact ọ bụla dabere kpọmkwem na bytes nke ya. Body ahụ ka
anaghị arụ ọrụ dịka mkpebi ikike; ọkwa ahọpụtara n'ezie maka arịrịọ dị
na **response header `x-omniroute-feed-tier`**, nke a na-ekpebi n'akụkụ sava site na
key `Authorization` nke arịrịọ ahụ.

`syncRadar()` (`src/lib/radar/sync.ts::parseServedTierHeader()`) bụ naanị ebe
a na-ekpebi ọkwa client kwesịrị ịtụkwasị obi:

1. Jiri `RadarTierSchema` (Zod) mee parse nke `x-omniroute-feed-tier` — header na-adịghị, ma ọ bụ
   uru na-abụghị kpọmkwem `"community"` ma ọ bụ `"live"`, ka a na-ewere dịka ihe **na-adịghị
   adị** (a naghị atụkwasị ya obi ma tinye ya na cache/UI dịka ọ dị; nke a gụnyekwara sava feed
   ochie ndị dị tupu e nwee header ahụ).
2. Laghachi na field `tier` nke signed body (nke na-abụ `"live"` mgbe niile) naanị mgbe nzọụkwụ 1
   enyeghị ihe ọ bụla.
3. Ọkwa e kpebiri bụ nke a na-echekwa na cache ma weghachite dịka `{ status: "updated",
version, tier }` — nke a bụ uru dashboard na-egosi, ọ bụghị field raw nke body
   ahụ.

## Iwu njikọta overlay n'oge ọgụgụ

`applyFeed()` (`src/lib/radar/applyFeed.ts`) na-ejikọta feed dị na cache **n'elu**
baseline static n'**oge ọgụgụ**, n'ime `getRadarCatalog()`. A naghị agbanwe array baseline
(`FREE_MODEL_BUDGETS`) mgbe ọ bụla — a na-agbakọ `MergedEntry[]` ọhụrụ na call ọ bụla.

Iwu anọ, dịka usoro mkpa ha si dị:

1. **Feed anaghị edochi local override mgbe ọ bụla.** Maka field ọ bụla: ọ bụrụ na operator
   ahaziela field dị na entry (`localOverrides` map, nke key ya bụ `provider:modelId`),
   a na-amafere uru feed maka field ahụ kpọmkwem — uru operator na-emeri.
2. **`enabled: false` na-eme ka entry ghara ịrụ ọrụ, ma na-edekọ ebe o si pụta.** Feed entry nke na-agbanyụ
   entry na-etinye `enabled: false` na `disabledBy: "radar"` na nsonaazụ e jikọtara,
   ka UI nwee ike ịkọwa _ihe kpatara_ entry ji si na nke dị adị ghọọ nke agbanyụrụ.
3. **Entry onye ọrụ gbakwunyere nke na-adịghị na feed na-adịgide n'enweghị mgbanwe.** Entry ndị
   dị naanị na baseline (ma ọ bụ ndị agbakwunyere n'ime local) ma na-enweghị feed
   entry kwekọrọ na ha na-agafe n'enweghị mgbanwe.
4. **A naghị eweghachi entry e tinyere tombstone mgbe ọ bụla.** Ọ bụrụ na operator hichapụla
   entry n'ụzọ doro anya (`tombstones` set), feed ịgbakwunye `provider:modelId` ahụ ọzọ na
   version e mesịrị anaghị eweghachi ya.

A na-edobe field ndị a pụrụ idezi na tombstone n'ime
`radar_local_model_state` (migration `153_radar_local_model_state.sql`). Adapter DB ọha
(`src/lib/db/radar.ts`) na-atụgharị row ndị ahụ ka ha bụrụ `localOverrides` map na
`tombstones` set nke `applyFeed()` na-eji; `getRadarCatalog()` nke production na-eburu state ahụ
mgbe gate nke flag, cache, na schema gafesịrị. Naanị `displayName` na `enabled` ka
operator nwere ike idezi. A pụghị ide provider/model identity, ebe feed si pụta, quota, capabilities, ToS,
na data setup site na surface a.

Dashboard na-enye action local anọ:

- **Dezie** na-agbanwe display name local na enabled state.
- **Tọgharịa mgbanwe local** na-ekpochapụ field abụọ ahụ a pụrụ idezi n'agbanweghị tombstone.
- **Zoo** na-emepụta tombstone, ka mmelite feed ndị ga-abịa ghara ịmepụta row ahụ ọzọ.
- **Weghachite** na-ewepụ tombstone; override ọ bụla e debere iche na-anọgide na-arụ ọrụ.

Feed `enabled: false` ka bụ safety exception: ọ na-emeri local
`enabled: true` ochie, na-eme ka entry e jikọtara nọgide bụrụ nke agbanyụrụ, ma dekọọ `disabledBy: "radar"`.

Mbipụta katalọgụ na-eji `schemaVersion: 2`. `contextWindow` na nke ọ bụla n'ime `tools`, `vision`, na
`thinking` bụ `number | null` / `boolean | null` n'onwe ha: `null` pụtara na amaghị, ebe
`false` pụtara na isi mmalite provider gọọmentị nke D16 kwadoro kwuru n'ụzọ doro anya na capability ahụ adịghị.
A naghị ebuli flag registry/model-spec dị n'ime OmniRoute ozugbo ka ha bụrụ eziokwu feed. Client
ka na-anabata snapshot v1; n'ihi na builder ochie ji `false` dịka placeholder maka enweghị ihe, a na-eme ka v1 `false`
bụrụ nke amaghị ebe v1 `true` na-anọgide bụrụ eziokwu. Schema version ndị a na-amaghị na-ajụ n'ụzọ nchekwa, ebe
cache ikpeazụ ziri ezi ka na-adị. Model v2 ọ bụla nwere context/capability na-abụghị null ga-enwerịrị
`metadataEvidenceUrls[]` HTTPS na-achọghị credential; ma ọ bụghị ya, validation schema ga-ada, a gaghịkwa
edochi cache. Tebụl katalọgụ na-egosi state atọ ahụ dịka `✓`, `✕`, na `?`.

### Combo a na-eduzi na ohere MCP

Uru `familyId` ndị akwadoro na-adịgide mgbe overlay oge ọgụgụ gasịrị ma na-achịkwa module pure

na alias/prefix match na-edoghị anya na-ajụ n'ụzọ nchekwa. Atụmatụ na-eji strategy `priority` dị ugbu a, na-ahazi
budget kwa ọnwa na-emegharị emegharị kachasị ukwuu ka ọ buru ụzọ; UI na-emepụta ha naanị site na `POST /api/combos`.

`GET /api/radar/catalog` na `GET /api/combos/builder/options`. Ọ dịghị mgbe ọ na-ebute mmekọrịta Radar,
agụ ozi nzere nke ndị na-eweta ọrụ, ma ọ bụ dee ozugbo na nchekwa data combo.

Ndị ahịa MCP nwere ike iji `omniroute_radar_catalog` (`read:radar`) gụọ otu ntule mpaghara ahụ. A na-enyocha
nzacha `provider`, `familyId`, na `enabledOnly` ndị a na-ahọrọ iji mee ihe mgbe otu
ọgụgụ `GET /api/radar/catalog` mpaghara gasịrị. Mmepụta ya e mechiri emechi gụnyere metadata katalọgụ yana onye na-eweta ọrụ/model,
aha ngosipụta, `familyId`, oke ojiji, ikike, ọnọdụ agbanyere, mmalite, na `disabledBy`; URL nhazi,
usoro, njikọ, adreesị ozi-e, igodo, na data ntụnye anaghị alaghachi ma ọlị. Ngwaọrụ a bụ
naanị maka ịgụ ihe, ọ dịghịkwa mgbe ọ na-akpọ `/api/radar/sync`.

## Onyinye ndị nkwado

Onyinye na-eji artifact nke aka ha e binyere aka, `GET /v1/offers/latest`, ma ha anaghị ekekọrịta cache katalọgụ ma ọ bụ nke referral.
Endpoint sava ahụ chọrọ igodo Bearer nke onye nkwado dị irè ma na-arụ ọrụ; enweghị fallback nke obodo.
N’ihi ya, `syncRadarOffers()` na-akwụsị tupu ọ banye na netwọk mgbe feature flag ahụ
gbanyụrụ, onye na-ahụ maka sistemụ ekwenyeghị isonye, ma ọ bụ mgbe ahazighị igodo onye nkwado.

Mgbe GET gara nke ọma, client ahụ na-enyocha mbinye aka Ed25519 n’elu bytes nzaghachi ndị ahụ kpọmkwem,
na-eme nkwado `RadarOffersFeedSchema`, na-achọ ka ma body e binyere aka ma
header `x-omniroute-feed-tier` kwuo `live`, na-amanye dotted version nke ọhụrụ karịa nke dịbu, ma ọ bụ naanị mgbe ahụ ka ọ
na-eji usoro atomic dochie `radar_offers_cache` (migration `144_radar_offers_cache.sql`). Oke 10 MB
nke gụnyere header na stream, nke feeds ndị ọzọ na-eji, metụtakwara nke a. Ọdịda mbinye aka, schema, tier, replay, nha, HTTP,
na netwọk niile na-echekwa cache ikpeazụ e nyochara.

Ụdị onyinye mechiri emechi ahụ na-akwado ụdị uru atọ a pụrụ iji tụnyere: pasent n’ime basis points, kredit
n’ime nkeji ego kacha nta, ma ọ bụ ụbọchị trial. Onyinye onye mmekọ ga-agụnye public baseline nke otu ụdị ahụ, uru ya
ga-abụrịrị nke karịrị ya; onyinye gọọmentị enweghị partner baseline. URL ga-abụ HTTPS na-enweghị
credential. `getRadarOffers()` na-eji nchedo nyochagharịa payload dị na cache ma na-ewepụ
ndekọ ndị kubiela na ọgụgụ local ọ bụla; `/dashboard/radar/offers` na-enyochakwa expiry ọzọ tupu rendering,
na-eji ederede Portuguese mgbe ọ dị, jiri English dị ka fallback, ma na-akpọ onyinye ndị mmekọ aha n'ụzọ doro anya.

Browser na-akpọ naanị routes local: ọ na-agụ snapshot settings ezoro akụkụ ya, na-arịọ
`POST /api/radar/offers/sync` ka o mee refresh n’akụkụ sava, wee gụọ `GET /api/radar/offers`. Mgbe igodo adịghị,
ọ na-egosi links enyemaka/onye nyere aka ndị dịbu kama ịnwa iziga arịrịọ feed. Links onyinye ndị dị na mpụga
na-emepe na taabụ ọhụrụ nwere `noopener noreferrer`. E kpugheghị tool MCP `radar_offers` na
release a.

## Radar Intel, baajị onye nkwado, na CLI

Intel bụ artifact e binyere aka dị na `GET /v1/intel/latest`. `RadarIntelFeedSchema` mechiri emechi na-anabata
naanị ọkwa ELO nke Radar nwepụtara, nke private curator wepụtara site na ntụnyere ndị akwadoro na mgbanwe eziokwu
nke afọ/ọnụọgụ katalọgụ sitere na snapshots katalọgụ e binyere aka. E debere methodology ahụ ka ọ bụrụ rating mbụ
1000 na K=32. Ọkwa efu dị irè mgbe a na-akwadobeghị ntụnyere ọ bụla; client anaghị
emepụta nke adịgboroja.

`syncRadarIntel()` na-etinye otu Bearer dị n’akụkụ sava, timeout nke sekọnd 30, oke stream nke 10 MiB,
nyocha Ed25519 nke bytes kpọmkwem, schema siri ike, ihe achọrọ ka body/header bụrụ `live`, version floor, na
nchekwa cache ikpeazụ dị mma dịka onyinye. Mgbe echekwara snapshot live e nyochara, client ahụ
na-ewepụta `radar:<sha256(supporter key)>`, na-echekwa naanị identity otu ụzọ ahụ, ma na-ewepụta event nnabata pụrụ iche
`radar_supporter`. Baajị `radar-supporter` ya bụ idempotent ma na-enye XP efu;
ọ naghị emelite leaderboards ma ọ bụ jiri `token_share` ọzọ. `/dashboard/radar/intel` na-egosipụta baajị ahụ
naanị site na metadata cache local e nyochara.

CLI na-enye `omniroute radar status` na `omniroute radar sync`. Ha abụọ na-ekwurịta naanị na
API OmniRoute local. `status` na-eme `GET /api/radar/status` nke bụ naanị ọgụgụ; `sync` na-eziga otu
`POST /api/radar/sync-all` ma bipụta nsonaazụ maka feed ọ bụla. Iwu abụọ ahụ anaghị agụ, anabata, ma ọ bụ bipụta
igodo onye nkwado, ọ dịghịkwa nke na-akpọtụrụ ọrụ Radar ozugbo.

## Njikọ ntụnye aka (kredit efu)

A na-enye njikọ ntụnye aka site na iyi data **nọọrọ onwe ya, nke na-adị ọhụrụ mgbe niile** —
`GET /v1/referrals/latest` — nke dị iche na iyi data katalọgụ. E mere nke a ụma: iyi data
katalọgụ nke ọkwa community bụ snapshot nke nwere ike ịbụ nke ruru ụbọchị 30 gara aga, ya mere
njikọ ntụnye aka e wepụtara na ya na-anọbu n'azụ ndepụta njikọ dị adị nke sava ahụ ruo otu
ogologo oge ahụ (ntụnye aka ọhụrụ e tinyere agaghị erute onye ọrụ free/community ruo ihe ruru otu ọnwa).
Iyi data ntụnye aka na-ewepụ igbu oge ahụ site n'ime mmekọrịta data n'onwe ya, n'usoro oge dị nnọọ mkpụmkpụ.

```ts
// Ahụ nzaghachi GET /v1/referrals/latest (Ed25519 bịanyere aka na ya, otu pinned key ahụ dị ka
// iyi data katalọgụ):
{
  feed: "omniroute-radar-referrals",
  schemaVersion: 1,
  generatedAt: string,           // ISO — deterministic: max(updatedAt) gafee ntụnye aka
                                  // niile, ka arịrịọ abụọ yiri onwe ha wee mepụta kpọmkwem
                                  // otu bytes/mbinye aka ahụ
  referrals: {
    fixed: RadarReferral[],      // dị na ọkwa NIILE, gụnyere no-auth/community
    campaigns: RadarReferral[],  // a na-ejupụta naanị maka Bearer key dị irè ma ka na-arụ ọrụ (supporter);
                                  // arịrịọ no-auth/expired-key na-enweta []
  },
}
// RadarReferral = { provider, url, kind: "fixo" | "campanha", validUntil,
//                    requiredAction, isDefault }
```

N'adịghị ka iyi data katalọgụ, ahụ a enweghị field `tier` ma ọlị — sava ahụ na-ekpebi
ihe a ga-etinye n'arịrịọ ọ bụla dabere na key `Authorization`, ya mere header nzaghachi
`x-omniroute-feed-tier` bụ NANỊ isi mmalite maka ọkwa e nyere
(`referralsSync.ts::syncRadarReferrals`); header na-adịghị ma ọ bụ nke a na-amataghị na-adaba na
`"community"`, nke bụ echiche nwere ikike kacha nta. `RadarReferralsFeedSchema`
(`src/lib/radar/referralsFeedSchema.ts`) na-enyocha ahụ dum, na-eji otu
`RadarReferralSchema` ahụ e zipụtara site na `feedSchema.ts` maka ntụnye aka nke ọ bụla, ka iyi data abụọ ahụ wee
na-enyocha ntụnye aka n'otu ụzọ. `RadarReferral.url` ọ bụla ga-abụrịrị `https://` —
url `http://` agaghị agafe nyocha schema.

A ka na-edobe field `referrals` OCHIE nke etinyere n'ime katalọgụ na `RadarFeedSchema` (`feedSchema.ts`)
maka ndakọrịta azụ na iyi data katalọgụ ndị echekwara na cache, mana `getRadarReferrals()`
anaghịzi agụ ya — lee [Accessor](#accessor) n'okpuru.

### Mmekọrịta data

`syncRadarReferrals()` (`src/lib/radar/referralsSync.ts`) bụ NANỊ modulu
na-eji netwọkụ maka ntụnye aka, na-eṅomi nkwekọrịta `syncRadar()` kpọmkwem: flag gbanyụrụ
→ `disabled`; opt-in bụ false → `opt_out`; ọ na-ebudata `${RADAR_FEED_URL}/v1/referrals/latest`
(otu fork overrides `RADAR_FEED_URL`/`RADAR_FEED_PUBKEY` ahụ dị ka katalọgụ), na-enyocha
mbinye aka Ed25519 n'elu bytes nzaghachi ahụ kpọmkwem (`verifyFeedBytes`), na-enyocha ya site na
`RadarReferralsFeedSchema`, ma na-echekwa ya na cache n'ime tebụl `radar_referrals_cache`
(migration `142_radar_referrals_cache.sql`) — tebụl dị nnọọ iche na
`radar_feed_cache` nke katalọgụ. Oke nzaghachi 10 MB na ala `generatedAt` na-ajụ
iyi data na-abata ma ọ bụrụ na ọ tọrọ nke dị na cache, na-echebe megide imegharị artifact ochie
e binyere aka na ya. A na-anabata timestamp hà nhata: sava ahụ na-ama ụma enye ụdị ntụnye aka
community na live otu `generatedAt` deterministic ahụ, ka payload e binyere aka na ya
na ọkwa e nyere nwee ike ịgbanwe mgbe supporter-key gbanwere n'agbanyeghị na setị njikọ dị n'okpuru
agbanwebeghị. Ọ naghị atụba exception mgbe ọ bụla — ọ na-eweghachi status object mgbe niile; errors anaghị ebu stack trace
na `reason`.

Triggers abụọ na-eme ka cache ntụnye aka nọgide na-ekpo ọkụ, ha abụọ anaghị adabere na usoro
24h nke katalọgụ:

- **Mmekọrịta data mgbe a na-agụ** — `GET /api/radar/referrals` n'onwe ya na-akpọ `syncRadarReferrals()`
  inline mgbe ọ bụla cache na-adịghị ma ọ bụ mgbe ọ karịrị `REFERRALS_STALE_MS` (1h,
  `shouldSyncReferralsOnRead()`), tupu o nye nzaghachi. Nke a bụ ihe na-eme ka njikọ fixed
  bụrụ "nke na-adị ọhụrụ mgbe niile" maka mbido dashboard na-esote ozugbo, na-echeghị
  background timer ọ bụla.
- **Mmekọrịta data n'akụkụ scheduler** — `radarSchedulerTick()` (`scheduler.ts`) na-enyocha
  n'onwe ya ma ntụnye aka ọ bụrụ stale n'otu tick kwa elekere ahụ a na-eji maka katalọgụ, na-akpọ
  `syncRadarReferrals()` mgbe oge ruru. Nke a na-arụ ọrụ n'agbanyeghị ma oge katalọgụ n'onwe ya
  ruru na tick ahụ, ọ dịghịkwa agbanwe shape nke `RadarTickResult` (naanị side
  effect nke best-effort, a na-eloda error).

### Accessor

`src/lib/radar/index.ts` na-ebupụta accessors read-only abụọ, ha abụọ anaghị atụba exception
(otu nkwekọrịta nchedo ahụ dị ka `getRadarCatalog()` — flag gbanyụrụ, enweghị cache, ma ọ bụ payload
dị na cache mebiri emebi, ha niile na-ebute shape efu kama error):

- `getRadarReferrals()` → `{ fixed: RadarReferral[], campaigns: RadarReferral[] }`,
  na-agụ site na `radar_referrals_cache` (site na `getRadarReferralsCache()`) ma na-enyocha
  site na `RadarReferralsFeedSchema` — **ọ bụghị** cache katalọgụ.
- `getDefaultReferralFor(provider)` → ntụnye aka `fixed` nwere `isDefault: true` maka
  provider ahụ, ma ọ bụ `null`. Ọ na-ele naanị `fixed` — anaghị eji campaign eme njikọ
  "default" nke provider.

Iwu kpọmkwem nke "ntụnye aka nke bụ default maka provider" dị na
`findDefaultReferral()` (`src/lib/radar/referrals.ts`), pure function pere mpe nke **enweghị
DB import** — ọ dị nchebe ibubata ya n'ime component `"use client"`. `getRadarReferrals`/
`getDefaultReferralFor` (n'ime `index.ts`) na-adọta `@/lib/db/radar`, ya mere ha na-anọgide
naanị na sava; dashboard providers na-ebubata `referrals.ts` ozugbo kama
`index.ts` (lee n'okpuru) iji zere itinye `better-sqlite3` n'ime bundle browser.

### `GET /api/radar/referrals`

Na-agbaso kpọmkwem otu usoro gate ahụ ụzọ Radar ndị ọzọ niile na-agbaso: `RADAR_ENABLED` gbanyụrụ →
`404` (a na-enyocha nke a mbụ, na-enweghị mgbanwe ọbụla na byte); onye na-enweghị authentication → `401`; ma ọ bụghị ya,
ọ na-akpalite sync-on-read (lee n’elu) mgbe data kara nka, wee zaghachi `200` yana
`{ fixed, campaigns, tier }` — `tier` na-esite ozugbo na row cache ahụ (nke enwere ike ime ka ọ dị ọhụrụ ugbu a)
ma bụrụ naanị maka inye ozi (ọ na-achịkwa ederede soft upsell nke UI dị n’okpuru). Ọ dịghị mgbe ọ
na-eme proxy nke feed server ozugbo — source nke route ahụ enweghị oku `fetch(` ọbụla;
netwọkụ na-eme naanị n’ime `syncRadarReferrals()`, dịka ụkpụrụ local-cache-only
nke `/api/radar/catalog`.

### UI Dashboard — taabụ "Free credits" na `/dashboard/radar`

Ọ na-eji peeji Radar dị ugbu a (`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/radar/page.tsx`) dịka
taabụ nke abụọ kama route ọhụrụ — nke a na-ebelata routing/i18n surface maka atụmatụ bụ
mgbanwe nke data peeji ahụ na-eweburị. Ozugbo onye ọrụ sonyeere, ogwe taabụ na-enye
**Catalog** (tebụl dị ugbu a) na **Free credits**:

- A na-achịkọta fixed links dịka provider si dị, nke ọ bụla na-egosi `requiredAction` (mgbe ọ dị)
  yana bọtịnụ `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"` nke na-eduga na referral URL ahụ.
- Campaigns na-egosi otu ihe ahụ, tinyere `validUntil` mgbe ọ dị.
- Mgbe `campaigns` tọgbọ chakoo **ma** tier e nyere bụ `community`, UI na-egosi
  obere ozi upsell ("limited-time campaigns are a supporter extra") — nke a **adịghị mgbe**
  ezobe ma ọ bụ gbochie ndepụta fixed links, nke na-anọgide juputara maka tier niile. Ozi
  upsell ahụ bụ naanị ozi dị nro, ọ bụghị ihe mgbochi ma ọlị.

### Referral link n’aha provider (dashboard ndị provider)

`ProviderPageHeader` (`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/providers/[id]/components/`)
ejikọtalarị aha provider na `providerInfo.website` mgbe ọ dị, ma nwee otu
ihe atụ gara aga nke link a na-enweta ego na ya: ndetu partner-link nke Kimi (Moonshot AI)
(i18n key `providers.kimiPartnerLinkNote`). D28 na-eji otu discreet-note pattern ahụ
kpọmkwem maka default referrals nke Radar kama iweta key ọhụrụ.

Loose coupling, dịka e siri hazie ya:

- `resolveProviderHeaderLink()` (`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/providers/providerPageUtils.ts`)
  bụ function **pure** — `(staticWebsite, referralUrl) => { website, isReferralLink }`
  — na-enweghị dependency na `@/lib/radar` ma ọ bụ `@/lib/db/*`. `providerPageUtils.ts` n’ozuzu ya
  ka na-enweghị imports ndị ahụ (dịka
  `tests/unit/provider-header-referral-link.test.ts` na-akwado).
- `ProviderDetailPageClient.tsx` (component `"use client"`) bụ naanị ebe enyere ikike
  iwepụta data Radar — site na `fetch("/api/radar/referrals")`, otu local-route
  pattern ahụ peeji dashboard Radar n’onwe ya na-eji — ma ọ na-eji
  `findDefaultReferral()` sitere na `src/lib/radar/referrals.ts` nke na-enweghị DB gbakọọ default referral
  n’akụkụ client.
- Mgbe `RADAR_ENABLED` gbanyụrụ, fetch ahụ na-ewepụta 404, `referralUrl` na-anọ `null`, ma
  `resolveProviderHeaderLink()` na-eweghachi static catalog `website` n’enweghị mgbanwe — peeji
  provider ahụ na-adị otu byte dịka ọ dị tupu atụmatụ a adị. Otu ihe ahụ na-eme mgbe
  cache adịghị ma ọ bụ mgbe default referral adịghị maka provider ahụ kpọmkwem.
- Mgbe default referral dabara, `ProviderPageHeader` na-enweta `isReferralLink`
  ma na-egosi otu ndetu/tooltip ahụ dị nro dịka Kimi partner link (na-ejigharị
  key `providers.kimiPartnerLinkNote`) — ọ dịghị mgbe ọ na-eweta ọdịdị ọhụrụ dị iche.

## Otu esi akwado feed n'onwe gị

Fork ma ọ bụ onye na-akwado sistemụ n'onwe ya nke chọrọ ịchịkwa katalọgụ ahụ n'ụzọ zuru ezu nwere ike ịgba ọrụ feed nke ya
na-emetụghị koodu client aka:

1. Nye endpoint `GET /v1/catalog/latest` nke na-eweghachi body JSON nke na-emezu
   `RadarFeedSchema` (`src/lib/radar/feedSchema.ts`) — `feed:
"omniroute-radar"` nke dị n'ọkwa kachasị elu, `schemaVersion: 2`, `version`, `tier`, `providers`, `models`,
   `quirks`, na `totals`. Soro `x-omniroute-radar-schema: 2`; server kwekọrọ na mgbanwe
   kwesịrị ime ka arịrịọ ndị na-enweghị ya jiri artifact v1 e binyere aka iche.
2. Jiri key pair Ed25519 binye bytes nzaghachi ahụ kpọmkwem aka ma weghachite signature base64
   n'ime response header `x-omniroute-feed-signature`.
3. Tọọ `RADAR_FEED_URL` ka ọ bụrụ base URL ọhụrụ ahụ na `RADAR_FEED_PUBKEY` ka ọ bụrụ
   public key kwekọrọ na ya (base64-DER SPKI ma ọ bụ PEM) — lee
   [ntụaka env var](../reference/ENVIRONMENT.md#27-radar-feed-self-hosting).
4. Kwado `RADAR_ENABLED` ma họrọ isonye site na `POST /api/radar/settings`
   (`{ optIn: true }`).

Achọghị mgbanwe koodu ọ bụla ọzọ — `verifyFeedBytes()` na-eji override ahụ
na-akpaghị aka (`getFeedPublicKeys()` dị na `src/lib/radar/pinnedKeys.ts`), ma ntụnyere
version, nkwado schema, na iwu merge na-emetụta feed a kwadoro n'onwe ya n'otu ụzọ ahụ.

Njikọ referral (lee [Njikọ referral (kredit efu)](#referral-links-free-credits)
n'elu) bụ artifact ọzọ nke nhọrọ: fork nke na-enye naanị `/v1/catalog/latest`
ka na-arụ ọrụ n'ụzọ zuru ezu — `syncRadarReferrals()` na-alaghachi na `{ status: "error" }` mgbe ọ natara `404`
site na `/v1/referrals/latest`, cache ahụ na-anọgidekwa efu, ya mere
`GET /api/radar/referrals` na-aga n'ihu na-eweghachi `{ fixed: [], campaigns: [], tier: null }`
kama ime ka akụkụ ndị ọzọ nke peeji ahụ daa. Iji nyekwa njikọ referral, nye
`GET /v1/referrals/latest` nke na-emezu `RadarReferralsFeedSchema`
(`src/lib/radar/referralsFeedSchema.ts`) ma jiri otu key pair Ed25519 ahụ e ji binye
feed katalọgụ ahụ binye ya aka.

Onyinye ndị nkwado bụkwa artifact ọzọ nke nhọrọ. Iji nye ha, mejuputa
`GET /v1/offers/latest` site na `RadarOffersFeedSchema` mechiri emechi
(`src/lib/radar/offersFeedSchema.ts`), chọọ ikike live entitlement, weghachite
`x-omniroute-feed-tier: live`, ma jiri otu key ahụ binye bytes ahụ kpọmkwem aka. Fork nke na-enweghị
endpoint a na-ahapụ omume katalọgụ/referral ka ọ dị; imegharị onyinye na-ada n'emebighị ihe ọ bụla,
cache onyinye mpaghara ikpeazụ e nyochara na-anọgidekwa dị.

Intel bụkwa nke nhọrọ n'otu ụzọ ahụ. Onye na-akwado sistemụ n'onwe ya nwere ike inye `GET /v1/intel/latest` site na
`RadarIntelFeedSchema` (`src/lib/radar/intelFeedSchema.ts`), chọọ ikike live entitlement, weghachite
`x-omniroute-feed-tier: live`, ma jiri key Ed25519 a na-ekekọrịta binye bytes ahụ kpọmkwem aka. Ịhapụ
endpoint ahụ anaghị agbanwe katalọgụ, referral, na onyinye; imegharị Intel na-echekwa snapshot mpaghara
ikpeazụ e nyochara, ma ọ bụrụ na ọ dị.
