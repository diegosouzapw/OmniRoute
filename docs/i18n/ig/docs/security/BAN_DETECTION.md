OmniRoute na-enyocha nzaghachi njehie sitere n’aka ndị na-enye ọrụ maka ihe mgbaàmà na-egosi na
**akaụntụ anwụọla kpamkpam** (a kwụsịtụrụ ya / gbanyụọ ya / machibido ya n’ihi ToS), ma, mgbe
ọ dabara, ọ na-ebuga njikọ ahụ n’ime **ọnọdụ njedebe `banned`** ka a ghara
ịhọrọzi ya maka arịrịọ. Nke a bụ ihe kaadị ntọala **Nchekwa → Okwu Ndị A Machibidoro**
na-ahazi ("Okwu ndị ọzọ na-akpalite nchọpụta mmachibido akaụntụ
na-adịgide adịgide. Okwu ndị arụnyere n’ime sistemụ na-emetụta mgbe niile.").

Ibe a na-akọwa ndepụta arụnyere n’ime sistemụ, usoro nchọpụta, ókè ọ na-emetụta, otu esi etinye
okwu ahaziri iche n’enweghị nsogbu, na otu esi weghachite njikọ e tinyere akara. Ọnọdụ
njedebe ahụ n’onwe ya bụ akụkụ nke usoro nkwụsi ike — lee
[RESILIENCE_GUIDE](../architecture/RESILIENCE_GUIDE.md) ("Ọnọdụ njedebe").

**Isi mmalite eziokwu:** `open-sse/services/accountFallback.ts`
(`ACCOUNT_DEACTIVATED_SIGNALS`, `getMergedBannedSignals()`, `isAccountDeactivated()`),
tinyere `open-sse/services/errorClassifier.ts` maka klaasị nkwenye na-abụghị nke njedebe
(`ACCOUNT_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED_SIGNALS` / `isAccountVerificationRequired()`) nakwa maka
ngalaba 403 nke na-eji ya.

## Okwu igodo arụnyere n'ime ya

Ụdọ nta 7 ndị a na-emetụta mgbe niile (n'agbanyeghị mkpụrụedemede ukwu ma ọ bụ nta), n'agbanyeghị ndepụta ahaziri iche ọ bụla:

```
account_deactivated
account has been deactivated
account has been disabled
your account has been suspended
this account is deactivated
this service has been disabled in this account for violation    (Antigravity)
this service has been disabled in this account                  (Antigravity)
```

> Ndepụta a na-agbanwe ka ndị na-eweta ọrụ na-agbanwe mkpụrụokwu ha ji egosi mmachibido. Ụdị
> a tụkwasịrị obi bụ `ACCOUNT_DEACTIVATED_SIGNALS` dị na `open-sse/services/accountFallback.ts`;
> were ngọngọ dị n'elu dịka foto ọnọdụ ya n'otu oge.

### Ọ bụghị mmachibido: arịrịọ nkwenye onye njikwa nwere ike ime ihe banyere ha

`verify your account to continue` **dịbu** na ndepụta dị n'elu. Ọ bụghị akara
mmachibido, ma ọ dị ugbu a na `ACCOUNT_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED_SIGNALS`, nke na-ekewa ya dịka
`PROJECT_ROUTE_ERROR` enwere ike idozi kama imechi njikọ ahụ kpamkpam.

Google Cloud Code / Antigravity na-eweghachi ya dịka `403 VALIDATION_REQUIRED`. Ọ bụ
**nke na-adịru nwa oge ma na-eme na akaụntụ dị mma, ndị nwere oke ojiji ha zuru ezu** — dịka e tụrụ na
ntinye ọrụ dị ndụ (2026-09-25, `proxy_logs`): otu njikọ Antigravity weghachiri 33 n'ime
403 ndị a n'ime nkeji 10 ma nọgide bụrụ `active`, ebe njikọ ibe ya nke nwere 100 % nke
oke ojiji ya na windo 17 niile ka otu nke **naanị otu** machibidoro kpamkpam. Naanị
ihe dị iche bụ mbọ nke e mere ka sava zaa n'oge ahụ.

Ọdịiche a dị mkpa n'ihi na ndakọrịta ikpeazụ bụ `permanent: true` (oge nchere otu afọ,
ọ naghị agbake n'onwe ya ma ọlị), ebe onye njikwa na-edozi arịrịọ nkwenye n'ime ihe nchọgharị.
Ịhapụ nkebiokwu ahụ na ndepụta mmachibido mekwara ka ngalaba cloud-code 403 enwere ike idozi dị na
`classifyProviderError` bụrụ nke a na-apụghị iru maka mkpụrụokwu a, n'ihi na a na-enyocha `accountDeactivated`
mbụ — ya mere mgbake ụzọ ọrụ ahụ agbakwunyere maka Gemini Code Assist na
[#868](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/pull/868) na
[#6452](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/pull/6452) enweghị ike ịrụ ọrụ ma ọlị.

Tebụl akara atọ ndị dị ya nso, ma **dị iche**, abụghị akụkụ nke nchọpụta okwu igodo mmachibido:

- `CREDITS_EXHAUSTED_SIGNALS` — ego ịkwụ ụgwọ/oke ojiji agwụla (`insufficient_quota`,
  `credit_balance_too_low`, `payment required`, …) → `credits_exhausted` ikpeazụ.
- `OAUTH_INVALID_TOKEN_SIGNALS` — **ọ bụghị nke ikpeazụ**; ime ka token dị ọhụrụ nwere ike idozi ya.
- `ACCOUNT_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED_SIGNALS` — **ọ bụghị nke ikpeazụ**; onye njikwa ga-
  ekwenyeghachi akaụntụ ahụ n'akụkụ onye na-eweta ọrụ. Ọ dị na `open-sse/services/errorClassifier.ts`
  (abụọ ndị ọzọ dị na `accountFallback.ts`). Lee ngalaba dị n'elu.

Rịba ama: a na-ejikwa nkebiokwu ndị na-adịru nwa oge dịka **`rate limit`** / `429` site na
ụzọ rate-limit / oge-nchere njikọ, ha **abụghịkwa** akara mmachibido.

## Usoro nchọpụta

```
nzaghachi njehie sitere n'aka onye na-eweta ọrụ dị n'elu
  → atụgharị body ka ọ bụrụ string + gbanwee ya niile ka ọ bụrụ obere mkpụrụedemede
  → isAccountDeactivated(body): getMergedBannedSignals().some(sig => body.includes(sig))   [ntụnyere substring]
  → ọ dabara?
      → connection testStatus = "banned"      (na-adịgide adịgide — oge nchere otu afọ, anaghị eweghachi onwe ya)
      → ọ bụrụ na agbanyere ntọala `autoDisableBannedAccounts` ma `autoDisableBannedScope`
        gụnyere njikọ a (`all`, ma ọ bụ `subscription` maka OAuth/cookie/session)
        → isActive = false kwa. Igodo API akwụgoro ụgwọ tupu oge eruo ka na-arụ ọrụ mgbe scope bụ
        `subscription`.
      → a na-amafere njikọ ahụ mgbe a na-ahọrọ akaụntụ (ọnọdụ combo QUOTA_BLOCKING)
```

- Ntụnyere ahụ bụ ọchụchọ **substring na-adịghị ele nnukwu mkpụrụedemede ma ọ bụ obere mkpụrụedemede anya** n'ime **body**
  nke nzaghachi (`isAccountDeactivated`, `accountFallback.ts`).
- Mmechi na-adịgide adịgide `banned` na-amalite mgbe body nwere akara mmachibido na **ọnọdụ
  HTTP ọ bụla** (site na `markAccountUnavailable` → `checkFallbackError`). Akara
  **`deactivated`** nke nwere oke karịa (`isActive=false` mgbe njikọ ahụ enweghị
  igodo API ọzọ) ka ụzọ inline `chatCore.ts` na-ede naanị na **HTTP 401 / 403**
  (a na-ekewa ya site na `classifyProviderError` → `ACCOUNT_DEACTIVATED`). Rịba ama na
  ụzọ `markAccountUnavailable()` na-ede ọnọdụ njedebe _dị iche_ —
  **`expired`** — maka otu akara `ACCOUNT_DEACTIVATED` ahụ (site na
  `resolveTerminalConnectionStatus`), ya mere otu mmachibido ahụ nwere ike ịpụta dịka
  `deactivated` ma ọ bụ `expired` dabere n'ụzọ doziri nzaghachi ahụ. (Nkọwa koodu
  ochie na-ekwu "mgbe body nke 401 nwere string ndị a" — nke ahụ anaghị akọwa omume
  dị ugbu a n'uju.)
- A na-ewepụ njikọ `banned` na nhọrọ n'ebe niile a na-enyocha ọnọdụ njedebe
  (`isTerminalConnectionStatus`, combo `QUOTA_BLOCKING_CONNECTION_STATUSES`).

## Oke ọrụ — ndị provider a na-enyocha

**Ndị provider niile.** Nnyocha ahụ na-arụ ọrụ n'ime usoro izugbe maka ijikwa njehie nke arịrịọ upstream ọ bụla dara ada na-agafe — **ejedebeghị** ya na ndị scraper OAuth/subscription. Ọnọdụ terminal nke na-esi na ya pụta bụ nke **connection** ọ bụla, ọ bụghị nke provider ọ bụla.

Otú ọ dị, e mere _strings_ ndị e wuru n'ime ya maka ndị provider subscription/OAuth nwere ezigbo ihe ize ndụ nke mmachibido (ChatGPT Web Codex, Claude Web, Codex, Muse Spark, Antigravity). Provider na-eji API-key ga-akpalite detector ahụ naanị ma ọ bụrụ na body njehie ya nwere otu n'ime substrings ndị ahụ kpọmkwem.

`autoDisableBannedScope` (`all` | `subscription`, ndabara bụ `all`) na-achịkwa ma match ọ ga-agbanwekwa `isActive=false`. `subscription` pụtara seats ndị a na-abanye dịka login (ndebanye aha akwụ ụgwọ na akaụntụ efu, gụnyere sessions web-cookie). Ọ ka na-edekọ `testStatus=banned` maka API keys akwụgoro ụgwọ mana ọ na-ahapụ ha n'ime routing pool. Nhazi ga-adịgide adịgide bụ override maka provider ọ bụla na akaụntụ ọ bụla; enum zuru ụwa ọnụ bụ mbido mbụ.

## Keywords mmachibido ahaziri iche

Tinye ma ọ bụ wepụ keywords na **Security → Banned Keywords** (a na-echekwa ya dịka setting zuru ụwa ọnụ `customBannedSignals` site na `PATCH /api/settings`). A na-**agbakwunye ha na** ndepụta e wuru n'ime ya — ha anaghị edochi ya — ha na-eme hot-reload mgbe echekwara ha (nakwa mgbe mmalite) site na `setCustomBannedSignals()`. A na-amachi keyword ọ bụla na mkpụrụedemede 200; enweghị oke ogologo array.

**⚠ Ihe ize ndụ false-positive — họrọ ahịrịokwu ndị akọwapụtara nke ọma.** Detection ahụ bụ raw substring match n'elu response body dum, match ọ bụla bụkwa **nke na-adịgide adịgide** (cooldown otu afọ, mgbake aka). Keyword sara mbara nwere ike machibido connection zuru oke ma dị mma:

- **Ọjọọ:** `quota`, `limit`, `error`, `denied` — ha na-apụta n'ọtụtụ njehie nwa oge.
- **Ọma:** ahịrịokwu mmachibido zuru ezu, dịka `your account has been suspended for`,
  `account permanently banned`, `violation of our terms`.

Họrọ ahịrịokwu kachasị ogologo nke na-enweghị mgbagwoju anya provider na-eweghachi mgbe enwere ezigbo mmachibido. Mgbe ị na-ejighị n'aka, buru ụzọ lelee `lastError` nke connection ahụ, wee tinye okwu ahụ kpọmkwem.

## Iweghachi connection e tinyere akara

Ọnọdụ terminal `banned` / `deactivated` **anaghị agbake n'onwe ha ma ọlị** (a na-ewepụ ha na proactive-recovery tick — naanị cooldowns `unavailable` na-agbake n'onwe ha). Onye operator ga-ehichapụ ha n'ụzọ doro anya:

1. **Nyochaa connection ahụ ọzọ** — dashboard action **Test**
   (`POST /api/providers/{id}/test`); probe gara nke ọma na-emegharị `testStatus` ka ọ bụrụ
   `active` ma hichapụ fields njehie.
2. **Mee authentication ọzọ / dezie credentials** — maka ndị provider OAuth, mee usoro login
   / refresh ọzọ; routes create/import nke provider na-edobe `isActive = true`.
3. **Gbanyụọ ma gbanye connection ahụ ọzọ** — ọ bụrụ na auto-disable debere `isActive = false`
   (scope `all`, ma ọ bụ `subscription` maka connection OAuth/cookie/session),
   gbanye ya ọzọ mgbe ị dozichara akaụntụ ahụ.

Enweghị bọtịnụ “clear ban flag” dị iche — mgbake bụ re-test, re-auth, ma ọ bụ
re-enable, dịka iwu izugbe terminal-state dị na
[RESILIENCE_GUIDE](../architecture/RESILIENCE_GUIDE.md).

## Ikewapụ probe (model test-all)

**Probe-origin failure** (model test-all / health-check dispatches emere
n'ime `runAsProbe`) anaghị ewepụ connection na pool (#9817): a
**na-edekọ ya ka a hụ ya** (`last_error`, `last_error_type`, `error_code`,
`last_error_at`) mana ọ na-awụpụ mgbanwe routing **niile** — cooldowns, ọnọdụ
terminal (`banned` / `deactivated` / `credits_exhausted`), lockouts nke model
ọ bụla, circuit breaker nke provider, quota cache nke nkeji 5, refresh token
OAuth na auto-disable. Naanị failure sitere n'ụzọ ezigbo request na-eme
deactivation. Njehie e dekọrọ bụ ihe na-eme ka akaụntụ e tinyere akara pụta ìhè
na dashboard ka ọ ka na-ejere traffic ozi.

Ebe mkpebi naanị ya bụ `shouldIsolateProbeFailures()`
(`src/shared/utils/probeOrigin.ts`), nke saịtị **ọ bụla** nwere ike ịgbanwe
routing state site na probe-origin failure na-enyocha:

- `markAccountUnavailable` (`auth.ts`) — naanị ndekọ (`lastError` raw text,
  `lastErrorType`, `errorCode`, `lastErrorAt`; kpachapụrụ anya **enweghị**
  `backoffLevel`, nke ga-akpalite selection-time auto-decay ma hichapụ
  ndekọ ahụ)
- `maybeAutoDisableBannedAccount` — enweghị auto-disable
- `chatCore` — FORBIDDEN, ACCOUNT_DEACTIVATED, QUOTA_EXHAUSTED (naanị ndekọ,
  enweghị terminal `credits_exhausted`), GEO_BLOCKED (enweghị mwepụ awa 24),
  MODEL_NOT_FOUND (enweghị `lockModel`), codex 429 account-rotation failover
  (enweghị `markCodexScopeRateLimited`, enweghị `rate_limited_until` echekwara,
  enweghị ihichapụ session-affinity), `persistCodexQuotaState` (enweghị ide
  quota-state, enweghị cache invalidation), `recordKeyHealthStatus` (emetụghị
  key-health rotator aka)
- Refresh OAuth — ma proactive refresh n'ime executor base
  (`base.ts` `execute()`, anaghị eri refresh-token rotation) ma reactive
  401/403 path n'ime `chatCore` (enweghị deactivation `expired`)
- `chat.ts` — circuit breaker nke provider na quota cache nke nkeji 5
  (`markAccountExhaustedFrom429`) anaghị emebi emebi

Njehie e dekọrọ bụ ihe na-eme ka akaụntụ e tinyere akara pụta ìhè na dashboard
ka ọ ka na-ejere traffic ozi. Rịba ama: ndekọ probe na-echekwa ederede njehie
**raw** (anaghị ebipụ ya), n'adịghị ka mbelata `slice(0,100)` nke ezigbo path.

Ndị operator na-eji test-all dịka ngwá ọrụ maintenance nwere ike iweghachi
omume gara aga (probe na-agụ dịka ezigbo generation) site n'otu n'ime ndị a:

- setting `probeCanDisable` (`POST /api/settings` nwere
  `{"probeCanDisable": true}`, ma ọ bụ ndezi DB `key_value` kpọmkwem), ma ọ bụ
- feature flag **`PROBE_CAN_DISABLE=true`** (env ma ọ bụ DB override; ọ na-emeri
  setting ahụ).

Fail-safe: ọ bụrụ na nchọgharị flag ma ọ bụ settings ebute exception, isolation ga-anọgide na-arụ ọrụ.
