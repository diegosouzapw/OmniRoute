## Nchịkọta

A na-ekewa ụzọ API njikwa OmniRoute niile n’otu n’ime ọkwa nchekwa atọ.
Nhazi a anaghị agbanwe agbanwe, akọwapụtara ya na `src/server/authz/routeGuard.ts`,
a na-enyochakwa ya tupu ngalaba nyocha njirimara ọ bụla ọzọ amalite.

## Ọkwa

### Ọkwa 1 — LOCAL_ONLY

**Ihe na-amanye ya:** `isLocalOnlyPath(path)` → nyocha host loopback
**Ụzọ mgbapụ:** Ọ dịghị na ndabara. E nwere obere mwepu maka ụzọ ndị dị na
`LOCAL_ONLY_MANAGE_SCOPE_BYPASS_PREFIXES` mgbe arịrịọ ahụ nwere igodo API ziri ezi
nke nwere ikike `manage` (lee [Mwepu ikike manage](#manage-scope-carve-out)).

Ụzọ ndị a na-amalite usoro ụmụ ma ọ bụ na-eme koodu runtime. Ikwe ka okporo ụzọ
na-abụghị loopback nweta ha ga-eme ka onye mwakpo nwetara JWT ziri ezi (dịka ọmụmaatụ,
site na ọwara Cloudflared/Ngrok) nwee ike ịkpalite mmalite usoro — otu ụdị CVE
a ma ama ([GHSA-fhh6-4qxv-rpqj](https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-fhh6-4qxv-rpqj)).

**Ihe GHSA-fhh6-4qxv-rpqj bụ (ụdị mwakpo ahụ):** sava njikwa/agent
na-ekpughe endpoint nke na-amalite usoro nta (`npm install`, `node`, ihe nchọgharị,
proxy, `git`, `tar`, …). Ọ bụrụ na enwere ike iru endpoint ahụ site n’èzí host — n’ihi na
onye na-ahụ maka ya tinyere OmniRoute n’azụ ọwara nginx/Cloudflare/Tailscale ma JWT
agbapụ, ma ọ bụ na ahazighị nyocha njirimara nke ọma — onye mwakpo ahụ na-agbanwe “ịkpọ API” ka ọ bụrụ “ime
iwu na host” (ime koodu site n’ebe dị anya). OmniRoute na-egbochi nke a site n’ịmanye
**nyocha host loopback mgbe niile, tupu nyocha njirimara ọ bụla**, n’ụzọ ọ bụla
nwere ike ịmalite usoro: token gbapụrụ agbapụ site n’ọwara agaghị enwekwa ike iru ebe a na-amalite usoro.

**Nchịkọta LOCAL_ONLY zuru ezu.** Isi mmalite a pụrụ ịdabere na ya bụ
`LOCAL_ONLY_API_PREFIXES` / `LOCAL_ONLY_API_PATTERNS` dị na
`src/server/authz/routeGuard.ts`; tebụl dị n’okpuru na-egosipụta ọnọdụ dị ugbu a. Ọnụ ụzọ
`check-route-guard-membership` na-edepụta `route.ts` ọ bụla dị n’okpuru
prefix ndị nwere ike ịmalite usoro ma mee ka CI daa ma ọ bụrụ na edoghị nke ọ bụla dịka local-only.

#### Mwepu maka scope njikwa

Enwere ike ịnweta otu obere akụkụ nke ụzọ LOCAL_ONLY site na adreesị na-abụghị loopback ma ọ bụrụ, naanị ma ọ bụrụ,
na arịrịọ ahụ nwere `Authorization: Bearer <api-key>` nke
metadata ya gụnyere scope `manage` (ma ọ bụ `admin`). A na-achịkwa mwepu ahụ
n'ụzọ doro anya n'otu ụzọ n'otu ụzọ site na `LOCAL_ONLY_MANAGE_SCOPE_BYPASS_PREFIXES` ka
ndabara maka ụzọ LOCAL_ONLY ọhụrụ ọ bụla nọgide bụrụ strict-loopback. A ka na-ajụ arịrịọ
ndị a na-akwadoghị njirimara na arịrịọ nwere key na-abụghị nke njikwa site na
`403 LOCAL_ONLY`.

Taa, naanị prefix enwere ike ịgafe bụ `/api/mcp/`. E wepụrụ `/api/cli-tools/runtime/` na
`/api/services/` ụma n'ihi na ha nwere ike ịmalite subprocess ọ bụla
(`npm install`, `node`), nke bụ kpọmkwem ụdị CVE e mepụtara ọkwa
LOCAL_ONLY iji gbochie.

**#7895 — scope `mcp:connect` dị warara:** mwepu `/api/mcp/` NA-ANABATAKWA
key Bearer nwere scope `mcp:connect` dị warara
(`src/shared/constants/managementScopes.ts::MCP_CONNECT_SCOPE`), nke a na-enyocha site na
`hasMcpConnectOrManageScope()` n'ime `src/server/authz/policies/management.ts`.
Nke a metụtara `/api/mcp/` NANI — `mcp:connect` anaghị enye ikike ọ bụla n'ụzọ
njikwa ọzọ ọ bụla (gụnyere prefix mwepu LOCAL_ONLY ọ bụla ọzọ, ma ọ bụrụ na a gbakwunye otu
mgbe ọ bụla), ma e wepụrụ ya ụma na
`MANAGEMENT_API_KEY_SCOPES`. Key nwere `manage`/`admin` ka na-agafe
mwepu ahụ kpọmkwem dịka ọ dị na mbụ; `mcp:connect` bụ nhọrọ nwere obere ikike
maka ndị na-akpọ MCP naanị site n'ebe dị anya, ndị na-ekwesịghị ịchọ ohere njikwa sara mbara.

| Arịrịọ                                                       | Ụzọ                        | Nsonaazụ                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Na-abụghị loopback, enweghị Bearer                           | `/api/mcp/*`               | 403 LOCAL_ONLY                  |
| Na-abụghị loopback, Bearer nwere scope `manage`              | `/api/mcp/*`               | Kwe ka ọ gafee                  |
| Na-abụghị loopback, Bearer nwere scope `mcp:connect`         | `/api/mcp/*`               | Kwe ka ọ gafee                  |
| Na-abụghị loopback, Bearer na-enweghị `manage`/`mcp:connect` | `/api/mcp/*`               | 403 LOCAL_ONLY                  |
| Na-abụghị loopback, Bearer nwere scope `mcp:connect`         | `/api/cli-tools/runtime/*` | 403 LOCAL_ONLY                  |
| Na-abụghị loopback, Bearer nwere scope `manage`              | `/api/cli-tools/runtime/*` | 403 LOCAL_ONLY                  |
| Loopback, Bearer ọ bụla/enweghị Bearer                       | LOCAL_ONLY ọ bụla          | Kwe ka ọ gafee (ọnụ ụzọ gafere) |

#### Nduzi onye na-arụ ọrụ & nyocha ndekọ

Ọ bụrụ na ị na-agba OmniRoute n'azụ reverse proxy ma ọ bụ tunnel (nginx, Caddy, Cloudflare
Tunnel, Tailscale, Ngrok), nlele loopback ka na-echebe ụzọ ndị nwere ike
ịmalite usoro dị n'elu — a na-ajụ arịrịọ nke adreesị klayent ya na-abụghị loopback site na
`403 LOCAL_ONLY` **tupu auth arụ ọrụ**, ya mere JWT gbapụtara apụghị iru ebe mmalite usoro. Ọrụ
abụọ ka dịịrị onye na-arụ ọrụ:

- **Anwala “idozi” 403 site n'ime ka IP klayent yie loopback n'ụzọ aghụghọ.** Ịtọ
  `X-Forwarded-For: 127.0.0.1`, ma ọ bụ proxy nke na-edegharị adreesị isi mmalite ka ọ bụrụ
  loopback, na-emepegharị kpọmkwem ụdị RCE ọkwa a mechiri. Mee ka
  dashboard/API dị site na proxy — emela ka ụzọ ndị nwere ike ịmalite usoro pụta ìhè.
- **Debe mwepu manage-scope ka ọ pere mpe.** Naanị `/api/mcp/` ka enwere ike ịgafe, ma
  naanị site na API key nwere scope `manage`. A pụghị itinye `SPAWN_CAPABLE_PREFIXES` mgbe ọ bụla
  na ndepụta mwepu — schema zod na-ajụ ha, ma
  `isLocalOnlyBypassableByManageScope` na-egbochi ha n'oge runtime (nchedo nwere ọtụtụ ọkwa),
  nke bụ ihe dashboard pụtara site na “enweghị ike ime ka enwere ike ịgafe ya”. A na-ekpuchi ụzọ
  nwere segment na-agbanwe agbanwe na ụzọ static ndị nwere ike ịmalite usoro n'okpuru `/api/providers/` (dịka `/login`,
  `/refresh-cursor`) site na ndị mmekọ regex `SPAWN_CAPABLE_PATTERNS` /
  `SPAWN_CAPABLE_PATTERN_ANCESTORS` dị na
  `src/shared/constants/spawnCapablePrefixes.ts`, ọ bụghị site na array larịị
  `SPAWN_CAPABLE_PREFIXES` — array larịị ahụ ga-ekpuchirịrị
  prefix `/api/providers/` niile iji jide ha, nke ga-eme ka osisi ụzọ nke
  dashboard ndị dị anya na-eji n'ụzọ ziri ezi maka CRUD nke provider gbasaa ókè.

**Nnyocha ohere** — iji chọpụta na ọ dịghị ihe si na mpụga host na-erute ụzọ ndị a:

- Mepee **Ndepụta Ikikere** na `/dashboard/settings/security`: ọ na-egosipụta
  ndepụta prefix LOCAL_ONLY dị ugbu a, prefix ndị a pụrụ ịgafe, yana setị
  spawn-capable nke oge compile ("enweghị ike ime ka a na-agafe ya").
- Jiri Grep chọọ na ndekọ reverse-proxy / access gị maka prefix ndị dị n'elu nke ejikọtara na
  adreesị client na-abụghị loopback. Hit ọ bụla dị otu a nke weghachiri `200` kama
  `403 LOCAL_ONLY` pụtara na proxy ahụ na-ezo ezigbo IP client — dozie proxy ahụ.
- `403 LOCAL_ONLY` dị na ndekọ OmniRoute maka otu n'ime path ndị a bụ guard ahụ
  na-arụ ọrụ dịka e zubere, ọ bụghị njehie e kwesịrị igbochi.

### Ọkwa 2 — ALWAYS_PROTECTED

**Ihe na-amanye ya:** `isAlwaysProtectedPath(path)` → gafee bypass `requireLogin=false`
**Ịgafe ya:** Enweghị mgbe `requireLogin=false`; a na-achọ JWT mgbe niile

Route ndị a na-emebi ihe ma ọ bụ enweghị ike ịlaghachi azụ. Ikwe ka ha rụọ ọrụ na nrụnye
"enweghị-password" ga-apụta na onye ọ bụla nọ n'otu LAN ahụ nwere ike ihichapụ database ma ọ bụ kwụsị
usoro server ahụ.

| Path                                      | Ihe kpatara ya                                                |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/api/shutdown`                           | Na-akwụsị usoro server                                        |
| `/api/settings/database`                  | Mbupụ, mbubata, na ihichapụ database                          |
| `/api/db-backups`                         | Ịnweta archive backup database zuru ezu                       |
| `/api/settings/export-json`               | Na-ebupụ blob settings zuru ezu (gụnyere secrets)             |

**Nzaghachi mgbe e mebiri iwu:** `401 Authentication required`

`/api/settings/obsidian` na-ekpuchi child `/webdav` ya: `POST` na-atụgharị ọrụ faịlụ WebDAV —
nke custom Node layer na-enye tupu Next.js, n'èzí pipeline a — gaa na root onye na-akpọ họọrọ
ma na-eweghachi Basic credentials emepụtara ọhụrụ, `DELETE` na-agbanwe ha, ebe parent `POST` na-echekwa
token Obsidian REST API. GHSA-62vw kpuchiri naanị mkpughe password nke `GET`; mmepụta ya
ka nọkwa na ọkwa fail-open (GHSA-7pq4-8pvv-rx7r). `enableObsidianVaultSync()` na-ajụkwa
vault nke bụ data directory, nke nọ n'ime ya, ma ọ bụ nke nwere ya.

### Bootstrap nke nrụnye ọhụrụ bụ naanị maka loopback — site na ezigbo peer, ọ bụghị `Host`

Mgbe ahazighị management password ọ bụla (ma enweghị `INITIAL_PASSWORD`), `isAuthRequired()` dị na
`src/shared/utils/apiAuth.ts` na-ahapụ anonymous bootstrap ka ọ ghe oghe **naanị maka loopback peers**.
A na-ekpebi loopback site na trusted peer signals, n'usoro a: ezigbo TCP peer e tinyere akara token
(`PEER_IP_HEADER` + `VIA_PROXY_HEADER`, ihe policy ahụ na-ahụ), verdict nke pipeline ahụ n'onwe ya
`AUTHZ_HEADER_PEER_LOCALITY` (ihe route handlers na-ahụ, nke a tụkwasịrị obi naanị mgbe
edobere `OMNIROUTE_PEER_STAMP_TOKEN`), ma ọ bụ ezigbo socket peer maka ndị na-akpọ ozugbo. A naghị
eji `Host` / `nextUrl.hostname` eme mkpebi ma ọlị, a na-edobekwa ide password mbụ
(`POST /api/settings/require-login`) n'okpuru otu mmachibido a kama imeghe ya nye peer niile
nọ na network (GHSA-7pq4-8pvv-rx7r). `managementPolicy` na-ebufe verdict `peerContext` nke ya
n'ụzọ doro anya, ya mere headers nke arịrịọ ORIGINAL (tupu strip) anaghị ekpebi ya.

### Ọkwa 3 — MANAGEMENT (ndabara)

Route management ndị ọzọ niile. A na-achọ auth ọ gwụla ma ahaziri
`requireLogin=false`. CLI tokens nwere ike ime authentication maka route ndị a (loopback + HMAC ziri ezi).

## Usoro ntule

```
managementPolicy.evaluate(ctx)
  1. isLocalOnlyPath(path)?
     → loopback                                  → fall through
     → non-loopback, manage-scope Bearer
        AND isLocalOnlyBypassableByManageScope   → allow (management_key)
     → otherwise                                  → reject 403 LOCAL_ONLY
  2. isInternalModelSyncRequest(ctx)?
     → allow (system)
  3. hasValidCliToken(headers)?
     → allow (cli) [loopback + timingSafeEqual HMAC check]
  4. isAlwaysProtectedPath(path) or requireLogin=true?
     → isDashboardSessionAuthenticated?
        → allow (dashboard_session)
     → manage-scope Bearer on a non-bypassable path?
        → allow (management_key)
     → reject 401/403
  5. requireLogin=false?
     → allow (anonymous)
```

Alaka manage-scope dị na nzọụkwụ 1 bụ naanị ụzọ e nyochara njirimara nke nwere ike imeju
ụzọ LOCAL_ONLY; ọnọdụ ọdịda auth-backend na-eweghachi 503 (ọ bụghị 403) ka DB
kubie ume ghara iji nwayọọ gbadata gaa na "jụ".

## Idekọ Tier Nchekwa na OpenAPI

Mgbe ị na-agbakwunye ụzọ ọhụrụ na `docs/openapi.yaml`, tinye vendor extension
dabara adaba ma ọ bụrụ na `routeGuard.ts` ekewala ụzọ ahụ n'ụdị:

| Nkewa routeGuard.ts          | Nkọwa YAML                 | Mmanye                                          |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| `LOCAL_ONLY_API_PREFIXES`    | `x-loopback-only: true`    | A na-egbochi non-loopback n'enweghị ihe ọzọ     |
| `ALWAYS_PROTECTED_API_PATHS` | `x-always-protected: true` | A chọrọ auth ọbụna mgbe `requireLogin=false`    |
| Ụzọ admin/debug dị n'ime     | `x-internal: true`         | Ezoro ya na /dashboard/api-endpoints na ndabara |
| Enweghị (auth ọha / ọkọlọtọ) | (ọ dịghị nkọwa achọrọ)     | Nnweta ọkọlọtọ nke `requireLogin` na-achịkwa    |

### Nkwado izi ezi

Skrip abụọ na-amanye nkwekọrịta n'etiti nkọwa YAML na `routeGuard.ts`:

- `scripts/check/check-openapi-coverage.mjs` — na-ada ma ọ bụrụ na mkpuchi < 99%
- `scripts/check/check-openapi-security-tiers.mjs` — na-ada ma ọ bụrụ na nkọwa `x-loopback-only` ma ọ bụ
  `x-always-protected` dị iche na konstants oge compile

Skrip abụọ ahụ na-agba na hook pre-commit nakwa na CI.

### Iwu False Positive

Ọ bụrụ na e tinyere nkọwa `x-always-protected` ma ọ bụ `x-loopback-only` na ụzọ nke NA-ADỊGHỊ na
konstant `routeGuard.ts`, skrip mkpuchi ahụ ga-ada. Ndozi ya bụ mgbe niile ime ka
YAML kwekọọ n'ihe `routeGuard.ts` na-amanye n'ezie — ọ bụghị ịgbakwunye ụzọ na `routeGuard.ts`
n'etinyeghịkwa lojiik mmanye ahụ.
