## 💰 ~1.62B Tokan / Wata Daga Matakan Kyauta na Wasu Kamfanoni

</div>

> **Kawo asusun masu samar da sabis naka.** Wannan ƙiyasin jimilla ne daga matakan kyauta na wasu kamfanoni waɗanda ake samun cancantarsu daban-daban, ba tallafin tokan daga OmniRoute ba ne. Yi rajista, sami bayanan shiga inda ake buƙata, sannan ka haɗa masu samar da sabis da za ka iya amfani da su; kowane mai samar da sabis ne ke kula da iyakokinsa, samuwarsa, da sharuɗɗansa.
>
> Haɗa matakan kyauta da hannu yana da wahala — SDK da dama, iyakokin yawan buƙata da dama, kuma ba ka san ainihin adadin da kake da shi ba. OmniRoute yana lissafa **shigarwar matakin kyauta guda 471 a cikin maɓallan rukuni masu maimaituwa guda 34** kuma yana ƙididdige adadin tokan da aka nuna daga **rukuni 17 masu tabbataccen kasafin wata da aka wallafa tare da iyakokin Groq guda biyar na kowane samfuri**, bayan cire maimaituwa bisa rukuni ɗaya da ake rabawa. Ana nuna keɓantattun ƙididdiga da ake buɗewa kawai bayan tantance shaidar yanki (a yau: ModelScope) a ware, +~6M bayan tantance shaidar yanki, kuma ba a taɓa haɗa su cikin babban adadin da aka nuna ba. Sakamakon yana ci gaba da bayyana a dashboard (`/dashboard/free-tiers`).

<img src="./docs/diagrams/free-tier-budget.svg" width="100%" alt="Katin kasafin matakin kyauta na OmniRoute: kusan tokan kyauta biliyan 1.62 a kowane wata akai-akai, har zuwa kusan biliyan 2.22 a watan farko tare da kuɗin rajista, daga maɓallan rukuni masu maimaituwa guda 34 da aka rubuta, waɗanda suka ƙunshi shigarwar matakin kyauta guda 471 da aka lissafa a bayan endpoint guda ɗaya. Ƙididdiga ta gaskiya bayan cire maimaituwar rukuni — ana ƙirga kowane rukuni da ake rabawa sau ɗaya, ciki har da rukuni masu maimaituwa guda 17 waɗanda ke da tabbataccen kasafin tokan na wata da aka wallafa tare da iyakokin Groq guda biyar na kowane samfuri; an yi wa masu samar da sabis guda 12 alamar a guje su a kundin haɗarin sharuɗɗa domin kai ne ka yanke shawara. Sandar kasafi ta ƙunshi Mistral 1B, Nara 210M, LLM7 150M, xKiro 150M, Groq 30M (iyakoki guda biyar na kowane samfuri) da ƙananan rukuni, tare da kuɗin rajista na watan farko da masu samar da sabis na kyauta na dindindin waɗanda ba su da iyakar tokan, waɗanda ake nuna su a ware domin kada su taɓa ƙara girman babban adadin. Adadin da aka yi amfani da shi/ya rage kai tsaye a /dashboard/free-tiers."/>

> Takaitaccen bayani mai motsi na shafin `/dashboard/free-tiers` kai tsaye. Cikakkiyar hanya (cire maimaituwar rukuni, matakan kuɗi, sharuɗɗan masu samar da sabis): **[docs/reference/FREE_TIERS.md](docs/reference/FREE_TIERS.md)**.
>
> <sub>Ana sake tantance waɗannan alkaluma kowane mako biyu ta hanyar kwatanta su da kundin bayanai kai tsaye, kuma **suna iya motsawa ta kowace fuska** — idan mai samar da sabis ya dakatar da matakin kyauta, adadin zai ragu; idan sabon mataki ya bayyana, zai ƙaru. Muna wallafa abin da kundin bayanai yake ƙididdigewa a zahiri, ba mafi kyawun yanayi da aka ƙara kusantar da adadinsa ba.</sub>

## 📡 Radar na OmniRoute

Babban adadin shirin kyauta har yanzu shi ne **~1.62B tokens/wata** daga kundin da aka bayyana a sama, wanda aka cire maimaituwar bayanai tsakanin rumbuna. Ƙimar rajista ta wucin gadi daga masu samarwa na iya ƙara adadin watan farko daban zuwa **~2.22B**. Radar wani zaɓin ƙarin kundi ne mai sa hannu, ga mutanen da ke son samun sabbin bayanai kan samuwar samfuran kyauta tsakanin fitowar sabbin sigogin OmniRoute; kundin al'umma da duk fasalolin kyauta da ake da su za su ci gaba da kasancewa kyauta.

Masu tallafawa za su iya samun kundin kai-tsaye da ƙarin damammaki daga masu samarwa. Matsakaicinsa na daban, wanda zai iya sauyawa, shi ne **kimanin 3B tokens/wata a mafi yawa**, gwargwadon samuwar masu samarwa. Wannan matsakaici ba garanti ba ne: masu samarwa za su iya sauya ƙa'idojin amfani, cancanta, samfura, ko yankuna a kowane lokaci.

Radar na zaɓi ne kuma GET-only. Abokin hulɗar OmniRoute ba ya loda prompts, zirga-zirga, saitunan masu samarwa, bayanan telemetry na amfani, ko matsayin kin nuna sanarwa na cikin gida. Ƙara sani game da cancanta da kundin na yanzu a **[radar.omniroute.online/planos](https://radar.omniroute.online/planos)**.

## 🔌 Cikakken CLI + A2A & MCP

</div>

> Bayan uwar garken, OmniRoute **cikakkiyar cibiyar sarrafa layin umarni ce** mai **umarni sama da 80**, tare da buɗaɗɗun ƙa'idojin wakilai domin wakilin AI ya iya sarrafa ta **da kansa**.

### ⌨️ CLI na gaske (ba `start` kawai ba)

```bash
omniroute               # ƙaddamar da gateway + dashboard (tashar 20128)
omniroute chat          # abokin hulɗar taɗi na TUI (slash: /model /combo /skill /memory)
omniroute setup         # jagorar saitin fara amfani
omniroute doctor        # gano matsalolin masu samarwa, tashoshi, native deps
```

### 🛰️ Yanayin nesa — gudanar da CLI a nan, OmniRoute a kan VPS

OmniRoute na kan uwar garke? Sarrafa shi daga kwamfutar tafi-da-gidanka da **CLI iri ɗaya**. Shiga sau ɗaya
da scoped access token; daga nan kowane umarni zai nufi na'urar nesa.

```bash
omniroute connect 192.168.0.15            # kalmar sirri → scoped token, an adana shi a matsayin context
omniroute models                         # ← yana gudana a kan uwar garken NESA
omniroute configure codex                 # ← yana zaɓar model na nesa, yana rubuta profile na Codex na gida
omniroute tokens create --name ci --scope read   # ƙirƙiri tokens masu ƙuntataccen izini don wasu na'urori
omniroute contexts use default            # ← koma zuwa uwar garken gida
```

Tokens suna da scopes na `read` / `write` / `admin`; routes masu ƙaddamar da processes suna nan a loopback kawai.

Combo na stacked na tsoho yana gudanar da `RTK → Caveman`. Idan dukkansu suna aiki a kan kayan aikin tool/context ɗaya, tanadi yana haɗuwa:

combined = 1 − (1 − RTK) × (1 − Caveman_input)
average  = 1 − (1 − 0.80) × (1 − 0.46) = 89.2%
range    = 78.4 – 94.6%

Injin kiyayewa yana **kullum kare** code blocks, URLs, JSON da bayanan da aka tsara.

### 🎚️ Bayan injunan — salon fitarwa, ma'aunin dacewa & sarrafa kowace buƙata

Injunan 12 da ke sama suna rage abin da ke shiga **ciki**. Sauran matakai uku suna tsara **yadda**, **yaushe**, da abin da ke **fita**:

- **🪄 Salon Fitarwa** _(jagorar axis-fitarwa)_ — shigar da umarnin tsara-amsa masu tabbas, amintattu ga cache; ana iya haɗa su, kowanne a matakin `lite` / `full` / `ultra`. Ƙara salon shine shigarwa ta layi ɗaya a rajista:
  - **Prose gajere** — cire cika-kalma / articles / neman kaucewa; ci gaba da riƙe abun fasaha daidai.
  - **Ƙarancin code** — "lazy senior dev" YAGNI: mafi ƙaramin canji da ke aiki, babu scaffolding da ba a nema ba.
  - **Ponytail (lazy senior dev)** — hau tsirar YAGNI, gyara tushen matsala, mafi ƙaramin diff da ke aiki.
  - **Ina da ADHD (aiki-da-farko)** — matakin gaba yana jagora, matakai suna da lambobi, mataki na gaba ɗaya mai tabbas, babu gabatarwa.
  - **Terse CJK (文言)** — salon ultra-gajere na Sinanci na gargajiya (an ƙuntata shi zuwa `zh`).
- **🎯 Kasafin context mai dacewa** _(ma'auni)_ — maimakon ƙofar token ɗaya ta kunna/bice, ɗaga mafi araha, injunan da ba su da asara kawai har zuwa inda ake buƙata don **samar da dacewa da tagar context na samfurin**. Manufofi: `reserve-output` (tsoho, ya san samfurin) · `percentage` · `absolute`. Yanayi: `floor` (tabbatar da dacewa) · `replace-autotrigger` (zaɓin ku na sarif yana nasara) · `off` (ƙofar gado).
- **🎛️ Inda ake yanke shawarar matstsawa** _(fifiko, sama → ƙasa)_ — header na kowace buƙata `x-omniroute-compression` › override na routing-combo › profile mai suna da ke aiki › dacewa / auto-trigger › tsohon panel › kashe. Tsarin da aka yi amfani da shi yana dawowa a cikin header na amsa `X-OmniRoute-Compression: <mode>; source=<source>`.

Kunna ta atomatik ta ƙofar token, kunna ma'aunin dacewa, kafa profile mai suna, saita na lokaci ɗaya ga kowace buƙata, ko sanya pipeline ga kowace routing combo — duk wanda ya dace da aikin. **Eval harness** na zaɓi mara layi (`npm run eval:compression`) yana ƙididdige aminci vs. tanadi a kan corpus da aka kafa kafin ku inganta canji.

## 📦 Ƙarin hanyoyin girkawa — Docker, tushe, pnpm, Arch

**🐳 Docker**

```bash
docker run -d --name omniroute --restart unless-stopped --stop-timeout 40 \
  -p 127.0.0.1:20128:20128 -v omniroute-data:/app/data diegosouzapw/omniroute:latest
```

`:latest` yana bin mafi girman tsayayyen SemVer da aka **wallafa**. Ba ya bin git `main`. Saka takamaiman `:X.Y.Z` don GitOps. Duba [Tashoshin Fitarwar Docker](docs/guides/DOCKER_GUIDE.md#release-channels).An saita hoton zuwa **`OMNIROUTE_MEMORY_MB=1024`**. Wannan ya isa ga dashboard da hira marar nauyi. **Wakilan coding** (`POST /v1/responses` daga Claude Code, Codex, Grok, …) suna buƙatar V8 heap mafi girma sosai, in ba haka ba tsarin zai yi `FATAL ERROR` a kusan 12 GiB yayin da dogayen contexts guda biyu suka zo lokaci guda. Saita girman container sama da heap (native buffers suna wajen V8):

| Nauyin aiki                                | Heap (`-e OMNIROUTE_MEMORY_MB`) | Container (`--memory`) |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Dashboard / hira marar nauyi               | `1024` (tsohon saitin hoton)    | ≥2 g                   |
| Wakilin coding guda ɗaya                   | `8192`                          | ≥10 g                  |
| Dogayen `/v1/responses` biyu a lokaci guda | `10240`–`12288`                 | ≥12–16 g               |

```bash
docker run -d --name omniroute --restart unless-stopped --stop-timeout 40 \
  -e OMNIROUTE_MEMORY_MB=8192 --memory=10g \
  -p 127.0.0.1:20128:20128 -v omniroute-data:/app/data diegosouzapw/omniroute:latest
```

Cikakken jadawali: [Jagorar Docker — RAM na lokacin aiki](docs/guides/DOCKER_GUIDE.md#runtime-ram-for-coding-agents).

> **Tashar Docker ta kafin fitarwa:** `diegosouzapw/omniroute:next` da
> `diegosouzapw/omniroute:next-web` suna bin reshen `release/v*` na yanzu wanda ake amfani da shi ta tsoho.
> Waɗannan tags masu sauyawa an tanade su ne kawai don gwada gyare-gyaren da ba a fitar ba kuma

**🥟 Bun**

Ana goyon bayan daidaitaccen `bun install` da girkawa ta global (`bun install -g omniroute`) ta hanyar gano Bun runtime:

- **Ginannen `bun:sqlite`**: OmniRoute yana amfani da ginannen driver na Bun mai suna `bun:sqlite` lokacin da yake gudana ƙarƙashin Bun, sannan ya koma amfani da `better-sqlite3` a Node.js ko `sql.js`.
- **Zaɓin Webpack bundler ta atomatik a dev**: Development (`bun run dev`) yana gano Bun ta atomatik kuma ya kashe Turbopack don amfani da Webpack domin hana rashin jituwa da native V8 bindings. Production builds (`bun run build`) suna bin `OMNIROUTE_USE_TURBOPACK` daidai kamar a Node: Turbopack ta tsoho, `OMNIROUTE_USE_TURBOPACK=0` don yin build da Webpack (`Dockerfile.bun` yana samar da shi a matsayin `--build-arg`).
- **Dockerfile na musamman don Bun**: `Dockerfile.bun` mai matakai da yawa don native Bun production deployments (`docker build -f Dockerfile.bun -t omniroute:bun .`).

```bash
# Girka kuma gudanar da Bun
bun install
bun run dev
```

**⚡ Girkawa mafi sauri / marar nauyi (tsallake native build)**

Native SQLite engine (`better-sqlite3`) dependency ne na **zaɓi**, don haka global
install ba ya taɓa tsayawa yana jiran compilation daga tushe: yana amfani da prebuilt binary idan akwai wanda ya dace da
platform/Node ɗinka, idan kuma babu sai ya koma kai tsaye zuwa pure-JS engine
(`node:sqlite` a Node 22+, in ba haka ba bundled `sql.js` WASM) — ba a buƙatar build tools.

Don tsallake native warm-up na bayan girkawa gaba ɗaya (CI, headless, ko na'urori masu jinkiri):

```bash
OMNIROUTE_SKIP_POSTINSTALL=1 npm install -g omniroute   # CI=1 shi ma yana tsallake shi
```

Don girkawa mafi sauri, fi son **pnpm** (content-addressed store + hard links — duba sama).
Don runtime marar dashboard kuma headless, yi amfani da Docker `base` profile (a sama) ko
[jagorar Termux](docs/guides/TERMUX_GUIDE.md). CLI da web dashboard ana samar da su ne ta
tsari guda a port guda, don haka babu wani package na CLI-kawai a halin yanzu.
