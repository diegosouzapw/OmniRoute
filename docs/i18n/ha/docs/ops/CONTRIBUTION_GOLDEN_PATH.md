Yi amfani da wannan jagorar don zaɓar mafi ƙanƙantar amintaccen zagayen haɓakawa don pull request. Ba ya
maye gurbin takardun gine-gine da tsaro na takamaiman ɓangare da aka haɗa a ƙasa; yana haɗa kowane
nau'in canji da aka saba yi da ƙa'idojinsa, takamaiman bincike, da ɗaukar nauyin CI.

## Hanyar da kowane canji ke bi

1. **Zaɓi tushe kafin gyarawa.** Nemo reshen `release/v*` mafi girma da ke aiki, sannan ka ƙirƙiri reshe daga
   ƙarshensa. Ka nufi wannan reshen, ba `main` ba. Idan an dakatar da canje-canjen fitarwa, kada ka nufi reshen da aka
   dakatar; yi amfani da zagayen aiki na gaba da aka bayyana a
   [Tsarin Reshe da Fitarwa](BRANCHING_MODEL.md).
2. **Ambaci ƙa'idojin.** Gano kowane kundin bayanai, schema, abin da aka samar ta atomatik, API na jama'a, ko
   mahaɗin mai amfani da canjin ya shafa. Teburin da ke ƙasa yana ba da mafi ƙarancin saitin farawa.
3. **Rubuta ko sabunta takamaiman gwaje-gwaje.** Canje-canjen production a `src/`, `open-sse/`, `electron/`, ko
   `bin/` suna buƙatar gwaji na atomatik a cikin PR ɗin guda. Gudanar da mafi ƙanƙantar fayilolin gwaji da ke tabbatar da
   halayen, sannan ka gudanar da takamaiman matakan tantancewa da aka jera.
4. **Bar CI ya gudanar da babban matrix.** Cikakkun unit shards, Vitest, coverage ratchet, da
   production build suna gudana a kan PR. Ka gudanar da babban suite a gida ne kawai idan gazawar takamaiman gwaji ta nuna
   tasiri mai faɗi ko kuma idan canjin ya shafi ƙananan tsare-tsare da dama.
5. **Daidaita kafin bita.** Fetch tushen da ke aiki, bincika sabbin commits ɗinsa da diff ɗinka idan aka kwatanta
   da shi, sannan ka yi rebase ko merge na tushen bisa ga tsarin aikin masu ba da gudummawa. Warware rikice-rikicen fayilolin
   da aka samar da kundin bayanai daga tushensu, sake samar da su, sake gudanar da takamaiman zagayen, sannan ka tabbatar cewa
   PR har yanzu yana nufin reshen fitarwa da ke aiki.
6. **Yi rikodin hujjoji.** A cikin samfurin PR, jera commands da aka gudanar, kowane fayil ɗin gwaji da aka ƙara ko aka canja,
   migrations ko feature flags, da duk wani tabbatarwa na CI kaɗai da har yanzu ake jira.

## Hanyoyi na asali bisa nau'in canji

Commands da ke ƙasa su ne mafi ƙarancin takamaiman bincike, ba izini ba ne na tsallake gwajin da ke gwada
halayen da ka canja kai tsaye.

### Provider

**Ƙa'idoji**

- Bayanin provider a `src/shared/constants/providers/` da haɗa shi a cikin
  `src/shared/constants/providers.ts`.
- Models da capabilities a `open-sse/config/providerRegistry.ts` ko fayilolin registry da aka ware daga gare shi.
- Zaɓin executor/translator, saitin OAuth ko API-key, assets na dashboard, da bayanin
  provider da aka samar idan ya dace.
- Credentials na jama'a dole ne su yi amfani da `resolvePublicCred()`; error responses dole ne su yi amfani da shared sanitized
  error helpers. Duba `docs/security/PUBLIC_CREDS.md` (git; ba a haɗa shi cikin `/docs` ba) da
  [Tsabtace Kuskure](../security/ERROR_SANITIZATION.md).

**Takamaiman zagaye**

```bash
npm run check:provider-consistency
npm run check:provider-assets
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/provider-translate-path-golden.test.ts
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/<provider-or-executor>.test.ts
npm run gen:provider-reference   # idan kundin ya canja; yi commit na diff da aka samar
npm run lint
```

Haka kuma, gwada kowane dangin request da abin ya shafa: chat, Responses, images, embeddings, audio, ko video.
Yi bitar kundin da aka samar da golden diffs a matsayin canje-canjen ƙa'ida; kada ka amince da su kawai ba tare da dubawa ba.

### Routing

**Ƙa'idoji**

- Ƙimar strategy na jama'a da metadata na UI a `src/shared/constants/routingStrategies.ts`.
- Dispatch da ordering a ƙarƙashin `open-sse/services/combo.ts` da `open-sse/services/combo/`.
- Combo schemas, persistence, resilience state, model capabilities, da API/UI controls.
- [Injin Auto-Combo](../routing/AUTO-COMBO.md) da takardun resilience idan halaye sun canja.

**Takamaiman zagaye**

```bash
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/combo-<behavior>.test.ts
npm run test:combo:matrix        # canje-canjen strategy ko dispatch
npm run check:known-symbols      # canje-canjen rajistar strategy
npm run lint
```

Yi amfani da deterministic mocked-upstream tests a gida. Live combo smokes suna buƙatar credentials kuma
na hannu ne, ba maye gurbin CI ba.

### UI / UX

**Ƙa'idoji**

- Iyakar Next.js route/page da shared component a ƙarƙashin `src/app/` da
  `src/shared/components/`.
- Siffofin API response, yanayin loading/empty/error, halayen keyboard da screen-reader,
  responsive layout, theming, da faɗaɗa locale.
- Turancin UI source strings a `src/i18n/messages/en.json`; kada ka hard-code sabon rubutun da masu amfani za su gani.

**Takamaiman zagaye**

```bash
node --import tsx --test tests/unit/dashboard/<feature>.test.ts
npx vitest run --config vitest.config.ts tests/unit/ui/<component>.test.tsx
npm run check:dashboard-typecheck
npm run lint
```

Gudanar da app don canje-canjen hulɗa ko na gani, sannan ka bincika duka narrow da wide viewports. CI yana gudanar da
production build da suites masu faɗi; har yanzu halayen gani suna buƙatar takamaiman component, Playwright,
ko binciken hannu da aka rubuta wanda ya dace da canjin.

### i18n

**Ƙa'idoji**

- `src/i18n/messages/en.json` shi ne tushen UI; `config/i18n.json` shi ne tushen locale.
- Kundin CLI suna dabam a ƙarƙashin `bin/cli/locales/`.
- Kiyaye ICU placeholders da tags daidai yadda suke. Kada ka fassara sunayen product/provider/model,
  sunayen protocol da header, commands, code/JSON identifiers, URLs, environment variables, ko
  protected terms kamar `OmniRoute`, `OAuth`, `MCP`, da `A2A`. Jerin tushen yanzu yana
  `scripts/i18n/glossary/protected-terms.json`.

**Takamaiman zagaye**

```bash
npm run i18n:sync-ui:dry
npm run i18n:check-ui-coverage
npm run i18n:check-value-drift
npm run i18n:check-glossary
npm run check:cli-i18n          # idan strings/catalogs na CLI sun canja
npm run lint
```

Wannan jagora ne ga tsarin da ake da shi, ba gayyata ba ce ta faɗaɗa kayan aikinsa ko tsarin key.
Ka kiyaye patches na i18n su zama takamaimai yayin da ake ƙirƙira tsarin maye gurbinsa. Kada ka gudanar da translation
commands da ke kiran external services sai dai idan aikin ya buƙaci generated translations a sarari kuma
ka yi bitar diff da aka samu.

### CLI

**Ƙa'idoji**

- Umarnin jama'a da tutoci a cikin `bin/cli/`, umarnin API da aka samar, lambobin fita, stdout/stderr da
  siffofin fitarwar JSON, halayen saiti/muhalli, da fayilolin da aka kunsa.
- Rubutun da masu amfani ke gani a CLI dole ne su yi amfani da matakin i18n na CLI kuma a kiyaye kasidun `en`/`pt-BR` cikin daidaito.
- A kiyaye Node a matsayin runtime da ake tallafawa da kuma ƙa'idar binary da aka wallafa.

**Zagayen da aka mayar da hankali a kai**

```bash
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/cli/<command>.test.ts
npm run check:cli-i18n
npm run build:cli             # sauye-sauyen CLI da aka samar/haɗa
npm run check:pack-policy     # sauye-sauyen ɓangaren package
npm run lint
```

Yi amfani da ainihin umarnin a cikin kundin bayanai na wucin gadi idan halayya ta dogara da parsing, fayiloli, ko matsayin
fita. CI yana gudanar da ƙarin binciken artifact na package da na tsarin software.

### Bayanai

**Ƙa'idoji**

- Domain modules da ke ƙarƙashin `src/lib/db/`; shigo da takamaiman modules kai tsaye (an cire tsohon matakin sake fitarwa na `localDb.ts`).
- SQL migrations masu lambobi kuma idempotent a ƙarƙashin `src/lib/db/migrations/`, amincin transaction, halayen upgrade,
  indexes, da duk wani mai kira da schema ya shafa.
- Routes da handlers ba sa aiwatar da raw SQL kai tsaye.

**Zagayen da aka mayar da hankali a kai**

```bash
npm run check:migration-numbering
npm run check:db-rules
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/db/<domain>.test.ts
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/db/migration-<number>.test.ts
npm run lint
```

Gwada sabon database da kuma upgrade daga schema na baya lokacin ƙara migration. Gwaje-gwajen database
dole ne su rufe handles kuma su kira `resetDbInstance()` yayin tsaftacewa. Gudanar da `npm run test:bun:db` kawai idan
hanyar Bun adapter ta best-effort ta canza; Node ne har yanzu yake da ikon ƙarshe.

### Ginawa / turawa

**Ƙa'idoji**

- Root da workspace manifests/lockfile, `scripts/build/`, haɗa standalone na Next.js, abubuwan da ke cikin package na `dist/`,
  metadata na dandamalin Electron, ayyukan CI, da deployment sentinels.
- Dole ne a kiyaye jerin Node ranges da ake tallafawa da kuma amfani da Bun da aka amince da shi a `CLAUDE.md`.
- Build artifacts su ci gaba da kasancewa ba a sa ido a kansu ba; manufofin dependency, lasisi, workflow, da package suna aiki.

**Zagayen da aka mayar da hankali a kai**

```bash
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/build/<behavior>.test.ts
npm run check:build-scope
npm run check:lockfile         # sauye-sauyen dependency ko lockfile
npm run check:pack-policy      # sauye-sauyen ɓangaren package da aka wallafa
npm run lint
```

Yi amfani da `npm run build` a cikin na'urar gida kawai idan sauyin ya shafi compilation, standalone assembly, assets,
ko runtime bundling. Yi amfani da `npm run build:release` kawai don tabbatar da release/deploy. Build na CI shi ne
alama ta ƙarshe ta cross-platform; sauye-sauyen Electron na takamaiman dandamali suna buƙatar focused build da ya dace
ko shaidar smoke test.

## Zagayen ɗan takara na gida

Gwaje-gwajen lambar tushe ba za su iya tabbatar da cewa kunshin da aka samar yana farawa ba: jerin abubuwan da ake sakawa cikin kunshi, dependencies da aka cire,
da native binaries suna nuna matsala ne kawai bayan an shigar da tarball kuma an fara shi. `npm run dev:candidate`
(`scripts/dev/candidate.mjs`) shi ne ɓangaren gida na tsarin gina sau ɗaya / tabbatarwa / ɗaukakawa daga
RFC #8084: yana gina ɗan takara guda ɗaya, yana tabbatar da ainihin artifact ɗin, yana ɗaukaka shi ta hanyar
sake sanya wa kundin adireshi suna, sannan yana mayar da shi baya idan slot ɗin da aka ɗaukaka ya gaza health check.

```bash
npm run dev:candidate -- run --dry-run --json  # buga tsarin, kada a canza komai
npm run dev:candidate -- run                   # gina + tabbatar + ɗaukaka, mayar da baya ta atomatik
npm run dev:candidate -- build                 # npm pack + shigar cikin _artifacts/candidate/<id>/
npm run dev:candidate -- validate --id <id>    # fara a kan port da babu mai amfani da shi, /api/health + /v1/models
npm run dev:candidate -- promote --id <id>     # <id> → current, current → previous
npm run dev:candidate -- rollback              # musanya current da previous
npm run dev:candidate -- run --from-tarball <file.tgz>  # sake amfani da tarball da aka gina a wani wuri (CI)
```

- **Gina sau ɗaya.** `build` yana shirya current tree a matsayin kunshi (yana buƙatar `dist/server.js`, don haka fara gudanar da
  `npm run build:release`) ko kuma ya kwafi `--from-tarball`, sannan ya shigar da tarball cikin
  npm prefix mai keɓancewa, saboda tarball ba ya ɗauke da `node_modules`. Id ɗin shi ne gajeren `HEAD`
  sha (`-dirty` idan tree yana da canje-canje na gida) ko `tgz-<sha256>` don tarball. Id mai tsabta wanda
  aka riga aka gina za a sake amfani da shi maimakon a sake gina shi; aika `--force` don sake gina shi.
- **Tabbatar da kunshin, ba lambar tushe ba.** `validate` yana fara CLI da aka shigar
  (`serve --port <free port>`) tare da sabon `DATA_DIR=<candidate>/data`, sirrika na bogi, kuma an cire
  `OMNIROUTE_API_KEY`, `STORAGE_ENCRYPTION_KEY` da `INITIAL_PASSWORD` na mai gudanarwa daga
  environment, ta yadda zai gwada keyless loopback posture na sabon shigarwa. Yana jira
  `GET /api/health` ya mayar da 200, yana buƙatar `GET /v1/models` ya mayar da 200, yana dakatar da process group,
  sannan ya rubuta hukuncin cikin `validation.json` tare da hash na tarball.
- **Ɗaukaka artifact ɗin guda ɗaya.** `promote` yana ƙin ɗan takara marar validation mai nasara don
  hash na tarball ɗinsa na yanzu. Default active slot shi ne `_artifacts/candidate/current`; `--target <dir>`
  yana zaɓar wani kundin adireshi a filesystem ɗaya, kuma previous slot ɗinsa shi ne `<dir>.previous`.
  Kowane sake sanya suna atomic ne, kuma gazawa a tsakiyar aiki tana juya duk sake sanya sunayen da aka riga aka yi.
- **Mayar da baya.** `run` yana sake tabbatar da slot ɗin da aka ɗaukaka kuma yana sake musanya `current` da `previous` idan
  wannan gwajin ya gaza. Ba a taɓa ɗaukaka ɗan takarar da ya gaza validation na farko.

Ana rubuta komai ƙarƙashin `_artifacts/candidate/` da git ke yin watsi da shi; ba a taɓa wani shigarwar OmniRoute
da ke akwai ko kundin bayanansa. CLI da aka ɗaukaka shi ne
`_artifacts/candidate/current/prefix/bin/omniroute`; fara shi da `DATA_DIR` naka lokacin da kake
amfani da shi. Exit codes: `0` nasara, `1` validation ko ɗaukakawa ta gaza, `2` kuskuren amfani ko build da babu.

## Zagayen gida idan aka kwatanta da CI

| Gudanar a gida don kowane gyara                                                                 | CI yana samar da sigina mai faɗi                                          |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Gwaje-gwajen ɗabi'a kai tsaye da matakan tantance rukuni da ke sama                             | Cikakken jerin gwaje-gwajen unit da aka rarraba, da gwaje-gwajen serial   |
| `npm run lint`                                                                                  | Jerin gwaje-gwajen Vitest da matakan tsaurara ɗaukar gwaji/inganci        |
| Binciken nau'i ko ginawa kawai idan yarjejeniyar da abin ya shafa ta buƙaci hakan               | Gina na samarwa, tsaro, takardu, dogaro, da matakan tantance manufofin PR |
| Mu'amala da hannu/binciken kai tsaye kawai idan sarrafa kansa ba zai iya tabbatar da ɗabi'ar ba | Haɗin kai tsakanin jobs da binciken dandamali da workflow ya saita        |

Nasarar zagayen gwaji mai mayar da hankali hujja ce game da yarjejeniyar da aka canza, ba tabbaci ba ne cewa binciken CI marasa alaƙa
za su yi nasara. Haka kuma, kada a sa kowane gyaran gida ya jira cikakken tsarin gwajin repository.

## Jerin binciken daidaitawa

Kafin neman bita:

- Tabbatar cewa tushen PR har yanzu shi ne reshen `release/v*` mafi girma da ke aiki.
- Fetch wannan tushe sannan ka duba commits da suka shiga tun bayan da ka ƙirƙiri reshenka.
- Duba `git diff <active-base>...HEAD` don gano canje-canje na bazata ko waɗanda aka samar ta atomatik.
- Warware rikice-rikicen catalog da takardun da aka samar ta hanyar sabunta tushen sannan a sake samar da fitarwa.
- Sake gudanar da kowane gwaji/matakin tantancewa mai mayar da hankali da aka jera a bayanin PR bayan daidaitawa.
- Kada a taɓa sassauta assertions ko cire gwaje-gwajen da ake buƙata kawai don dacewa da tushen da ya sauya.

Don ƙa'idodin dakatar da fitarwa da sake saita manufa, yi amfani da
[Tsarin Reshe & Fitarwa](BRANCHING_MODEL.md). Don cikakken jerin CI, yi amfani da
[Manazartar Matakan Inganci](../architecture/QUALITY_GATES.md).
