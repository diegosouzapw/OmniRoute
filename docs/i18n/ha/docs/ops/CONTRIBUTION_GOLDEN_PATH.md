Yi amfani da wannan jagorar don zaɓar mafi ƙarancin zagayen haɓakawa mai inganci don pull request. Ba ya
maye gurbin takardun gine-gine da tsaro na kowane sashe da aka haɗa a ƙasa; yana haɗa kowane nau'in
canji da aka saba yi da ƙa'idojinsa, takamaiman bincike, da ɗaukar nauyin CI.

## Hanyar da kowane canji ke bi

1. **Zaɓi tushen reshe kafin gyarawa.** Nemo reshen `release/v*` mafi girma da ke aiki, sannan ka ƙirƙiri reshe daga
   ƙarshensa. Ka nufi wannan reshen, ba `main` ba. Idan an dakatar da canje-canjen fitarwa, kada ka nufi reshen da aka
   dakatar; yi amfani da zagaye mai aiki na gaba da aka bayyana a
   [Tsarin Reshe da Fitarwa](BRANCHING_MODEL.md).
2. **Ambaci ƙa'idojin.** Gano kowane katalog, schema, abin da aka samar, API na jama'a, ko mahaɗin
   mai amfani da canjin zai shafa. Teburin da ke ƙasa yana ba da mafi ƙarancin saitin farawa.
3. **Rubuta ko sabunta takamaiman gwaje-gwaje.** Canje-canjen samarwa a `src/`, `open-sse/`, `electron/`, ko
   `bin/` suna buƙatar gwaji mai sarrafa kansa a PR ɗin guda. Gudanar da mafi ƙanƙantar fayilolin gwaji da ke tabbatar da
   halayen, sannan ka gudanar da takamaiman matakan tantancewa da aka lissafa.
4. **Bar CI ya gudanar da faffadan matrix.** Cikakkun sassan unit, Vitest, matakin coverage, da
   ginin samarwa suna gudana a PR. Gudanar da faffadan jerin gwaje-gwaje a kwamfutarka kawai idan gazawar takamaiman gwaji ta nuna
   tasiri mai faɗi ko kuma idan canjin ya ratsa ƙananan tsaruka da dama.
5. **Daidaita kafin bita.** Ɗauko tushen reshe mai aiki, binciki sabbin commits ɗinsa da bambancin canjinka da
   shi, sannan ka yi rebase ko merge na tushen bisa tsarin aikin masu ba da gudummawa. Warware rikice-rikicen fayilolin da aka samar
   da na katalog daga tushensu, sake samar da su, sake gudanar da takamaiman zagayen, sannan ka tabbatar cewa
   har yanzu PR yana nufin reshen fitarwa mai aiki.
6. **Rubuta hujjoji.** A cikin samfurin PR, jera umarnin da aka gudanar, duk fayilolin gwaji da aka ƙara ko aka canza,
   migrations ko feature flags, da duk wani tantancewar CI kaɗai da har yanzu ake jira.

## Ingantattun hanyoyi bisa nau'in canji

Umarnin da ke ƙasa su ne mafi ƙarancin takamaiman bincike, ba izinin tsallake gwajin da ke gwada
halayen da ka canza kai tsaye ba.

### Mai bayarwa

**Ƙa'idoji**

- Ma'anar mai bayarwa a `src/shared/constants/providers/` da haɗuwarta a
  `src/shared/constants/providers.ts`.
- Models da capabilities a `open-sse/config/providerRegistry.ts` ko fayilolin registry da aka ware daga gare shi.
- Zaɓin executor/translator, saitin OAuth ko API-key, kadarorin dashboard, da bayanin
  mai bayarwa da aka samar idan ya dace.
- Dole ne takardun shaidar jama'a su yi amfani da `resolvePublicCred()`; dole ne martanin kurakurai su yi amfani da shared sanitized
  error helpers. Duba `docs/security/PUBLIC_CREDS.md` (git; ba a haɗa shi cikin `/docs` ba) da
  [Tsabtace Kurakurai](../security/ERROR_SANITIZATION.md).

**Takamaiman zagaye**

```bash
npm run check:provider-consistency
npm run check:provider-assets
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/provider-translate-path-golden.test.ts
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/<provider-or-executor>.test.ts
npm run gen:provider-reference   # idan katalog ya canza; yi commit na bambancin da aka samar
npm run lint
```

Haka kuma gwada kowane rukunin request da abin ya shafa: chat, Responses, images, embeddings, audio, ko video.
Duba bambance-bambancen katalog da golden da aka samar a matsayin canje-canjen ƙa'ida; kada ka amince da su ba tare da dubawa ba.

### Routing

**Ƙa'idoji**

- Ƙimomin strategy na jama'a da metadata na UI a `src/shared/constants/routingStrategies.ts`.
- Dispatch da ordering a ƙarƙashin `open-sse/services/combo.ts` da `open-sse/services/combo/`.
- Combo schemas, persistence, resilience state, model capabilities, da sarrafawar API/UI.
- [Injin Auto-Combo](../routing/AUTO-COMBO.md) da takardun resilience idan halaye sun canza.

**Takamaiman zagaye**

```bash
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/combo-<behavior>.test.ts
npm run test:combo:matrix        # canje-canjen strategy ko dispatch
npm run check:known-symbols      # canje-canjen rajistar strategy
npm run lint
```

Yi amfani da gwaje-gwajen mocked-upstream masu sakamako tabbatacce a kwamfutarka. Live combo smokes suna buƙatar takardun shaida kuma
na hannu ne, ba maye gurbin CI ba.

### UI / UX

**Ƙa'idoji**

- Iyakokin route/page na Next.js da shared component a ƙarƙashin `src/app/` da
  `src/shared/components/`.
- Siffofin martanin API, loading/empty/error states, halayen keyboard da screen-reader,
  responsive layout, theming, da faɗaɗa locale.
- Rubutun tushen UI na Turanci a `src/i18n/messages/en.json`; kada a saka sabon rubutun da mai amfani zai gani kai tsaye cikin code.

**Takamaiman zagaye**

```bash
node --import tsx --test tests/unit/dashboard/<feature>.test.ts
npx vitest run --config vitest.config.ts tests/unit/ui/<component>.test.tsx
npm run check:dashboard-typecheck
npm run lint
```

Gudanar da app don canje-canjen hulɗa ko gani, sannan ka duba viewports masu faɗin ƙunci da masu faɗi. CI yana gudanar da
ginin samarwa da faffadan jerin gwaje-gwaje; har yanzu halayen gani suna buƙatar takamaiman component, Playwright,
ko rubutaccen binciken hannu da ya dace da canjin.

### i18n

**Ƙa'idoji**

- `src/i18n/messages/en.json` shi ne tushen UI; `config/i18n.json` shi ne tushen locale.
- Katalog na CLI suna daban a ƙarƙashin `bin/cli/locales/`.
- Kiyaye ICU placeholders da tags daidai. Kada a fassara sunayen product/provider/model,
  sunayen protocol da header, commands, code/JSON identifiers, URLs, environment variables, ko
  kalmomin da aka kare kamar `OmniRoute`, `OAuth`, `MCP`, da `A2A`. Jerin tushen yanzu yana
  `scripts/i18n/glossary/protected-terms.json`.

**Takamaiman zagaye**

```bash
npm run i18n:sync-ui:dry
npm run i18n:check-ui-coverage
npm run i18n:check-value-drift
npm run i18n:check-glossary
npm run check:cli-i18n          # idan strings/catalogs na CLI sun canza
npm run lint
```

Wannan jagora ne don tsarin da ake da shi, ba gayyata don faɗaɗa kayan aikinsa ko tsarin key ba.
Ka kiyaye patches na i18n su kasance takamaimai yayin da ake tsara tsarin maye gurbinsa. Kada ka gudanar da umarnin fassara
da ke kiran external services sai idan aikin ya buƙaci fassarorin da aka samar kai tsaye kuma
ka duba bambancin da ya haifar.

### CLI

**Ƙa'idoji**

- Umarnin jama'a da tutoci a cikin `bin/cli/`, umarnin API da aka samar, lambobin fita, stdout/stderr da
  siffofin fitarwar JSON, halayen saituna/muhalli, da fayilolin da aka haɗa cikin kunshin.
- Rubutun CLI da masu amfani ke gani dole ne su yi amfani da matakin i18n na CLI kuma su tabbatar kundin `en`/`pt-BR` sun yi daidai.
- A kiyaye Node a matsayin runtime da ake tallafawa da kuma ƙa'idar binary da aka wallafa.

**Zagaye mai mayar da hankali**

```bash
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/cli/<command>.test.ts
npm run check:cli-i18n
npm run build:cli             # canje-canjen CLI da aka samar/haɗa
npm run check:pack-policy     # canje-canjen ɓangaren kunshi
npm run lint
```

Yi amfani da ainihin umarnin a cikin kundin bayanai na wucin gadi idan halayen sun dogara da parsing, fayiloli, ko matsayin
fita. CI yana aiwatar da ƙarin gwaje-gwajen kayan kunshi da tsarin muhalli.

### Ma'ajiyar bayanai

**Ƙa'idoji**

- Modulolin domain da ke ƙarƙashin `src/lib/db/`; shigo da takamaiman moduli kai tsaye (an cire tsohon matakin sake-fitarwa na `localDb.ts`).
- Migrations na SQL masu lambobi kuma idempotent a ƙarƙashin `src/lib/db/migrations/`, amincin transaction, halayen upgrade,
  indexes, da duk wani mai kira da schema ya shafa.
- Routes da handlers ba sa aiwatar da raw SQL kai tsaye.

**Zagaye mai mayar da hankali**

```bash
npm run check:migration-numbering
npm run check:db-rules
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/db/<domain>.test.ts
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/db/migration-<number>.test.ts
npm run lint
```

Gwada sabuwar ma'ajiyar bayanai da kuma upgrade daga schema na baya yayin ƙara migration. Gwaje-gwajen ma'ajiyar bayanai
dole ne su rufe handles kuma su kira `resetDbInstance()` yayin tsaftacewa. Gudanar da `npm run test:bun:db` kawai idan
hanyar adaftan Bun ta best-effort ta canza; Node ne har yanzu tushen hukunci.

### Ginawa / turawa

**Ƙa'idoji**

- Manifests/lockfile na root da workspace, `scripts/build/`, haɗa standalone na Next.js, abubuwan cikin kunshin `dist/`,
  metadata na dandamalin Electron, ayyukan CI, da deployment sentinels.
- Dole ne a kiyaye ranges na Node da ake tallafawa da amfani da Bun da aka amince da shi a cikin `CLAUDE.md`.
- Ba a sa ido kan build artifacts; manufofin dependencies, lasisi, workflow, da kunshi suna aiki.

**Zagaye mai mayar da hankali**

```bash
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/build/<behavior>.test.ts
npm run check:build-scope
npm run check:lockfile         # canje-canjen dependency ko lockfile
npm run check:pack-policy      # canje-canjen ɓangaren kunshin da aka wallafa
npm run lint
```

Yi amfani da `npm run build` a cikin na'urarka kawai idan canjin ya shafi compilation, haɗa standalone, assets,
ko runtime bundling. Yi amfani da `npm run build:release` kawai don tabbatar da release/deploy. Build na CI shi ne
sigina na ƙarshe na dukkan dandamali; canje-canjen Electron na takamaiman dandamali suna buƙatar focused build da ya dace
ko shaidar smoke test.

## Zagaye na gida da CI

| Gudanar a gida ga kowane gyara                                                      | CI yana samar da cikakken bayani                                       |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Gwaje-gwajen halayya kai tsaye da matakan tantance rukuni da ke sama                | Cikakken jerin gwaje-gwajen unit da aka rarraba da gwaje-gwajen serial |
| `npm run lint`                                                                      | Jerin gwaje-gwajen Vitest da matakan ɗaukaka coverage/quality          |
| Typecheck ko build kawai idan kwangilar da abin ya shafa ta buƙaci hakan            | Build na samarwa, tsaro, docs, dependency, da matakan manufofin PR     |
| Gwajin hulɗa da hannu/live kawai idan automation ba zai iya tabbatar da halayyar ba | Gwaje-gwajen haɗin kai tsakanin jobs da dandamali da workflow ya tsara |

Nasarar zagaye da aka mayar da hankali a kai hujja ce game da kwangilar da aka canza, ba tabbaci ba ne cewa gwaje-gwajen CI marasa alaƙa
za su yi nasara. Haka kuma, kada a sa kowane gyaran gida ya jira cikakken matrix na ma’ajiyar lambar.

## Jerin dubawa don daidaitawa

Kafin neman review:

- Tabbatar cewa tushen PR har yanzu shi ne reshen `release/v*` mafi girma da ke aiki.
- Yi fetch na wannan tushe sannan ka duba commits da aka haɗa tun lokacin da ka ƙirƙiri reshenka.
- Duba `git diff <active-base>...HEAD` don gano canje-canje na bazata ko waɗanda aka samar ta atomatik.
- Warware rikice-rikicen catalog da takardun da aka samar ta hanyar sabunta tushen da sake samar da output.
- Sake gudanar da kowane gwaji/matakin tantancewa da aka mayar da hankali a kai wanda aka jera a bayanin PR bayan daidaitawa.
- Kada ka taɓa raunana assertions ko cire gwaje-gwajen da ake buƙata kawai don dacewa da tushe da ya motsa.

Don ƙa’idojin dakatar da fitarwa da sake saita manufa, yi amfani da
[Tsarin Rassa da Fitarwa](BRANCHING_MODEL.md). Don cikakken jerin CI, yi amfani da
[Manunin Matakan Inganci](../architecture/QUALITY_GATES.md).
