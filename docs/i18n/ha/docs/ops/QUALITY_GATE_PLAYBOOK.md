## Sashe na 2 — Tantancewa Mai Muhimmanci (ƙarfi + raunin da aka faɗa da gaskiya)

### Ƙarfi (abubuwan da suka fi matsakaici)

1. **Injin ratchet mai ma’aunai da yawa.** Shi ne jigon tsarin. Ma’aunai 24 a cikin `quality-baseline.json`
   - Ma’aunan tushe guda 4 na musamman, kowannensu yana da alkibla (`up`/`down`), haƙuri (`eps`), sassauci
     (`tightenSlack`) da tutar `dedicatedGate`. Abubuwan da aka gyara suna **ci gaba da kasancewa**
     a gyare — wannan ne maganin lalacewar codebase a hankali.
2. **Kariya mai matakai da yawa ga supply-chain.** SAST (CodeQL/Sonar) + sirrika (gitleaks tare da
   `useDefault`) + SCA (osv/npm-audit/Trivy/Dependabot) + lasisoshi + lockfile + SBOM + asalin SLSA
   - Scorecard + ƙarfafa workflow (zizmor). Ƙalilan ne daga cikin codebase suke da wannan cikakken tsarin.
3. **Magungunan Dokar Goodhart.** Ɗaukar coverage a matsayin manufa sanannen anti-pattern ne
   ("idan ma’auni ya zama manufa, ya daina zama ma’auni mai kyau"). Muna da abubuwan da ke daidaita shi:
   **mutation testing** (yana auna ko test ya gano bug, ba wai kawai ko ya aiwatar da layin ba),
   **`check-test-masking`** (yana hana raunana asserts domin a samu nasara), **mafi ƙarancin coverage
   na kowane module** (yana tilasta gwada code mai haɗari SOSAI, ba sassa masu sauƙi kaɗai ba) da
   **`check-pr-evidence`** (Doka Mai Tsauri #18).
4. **Ƙofofin hana hallucination / tabbatar da daidaito.** Wani rukuni ne mai wuya a samu kuma mai
   matuƙar amfani: `check-known-symbols`, `check-fetch-targets`, `check-openapi-routes`,
   `check-docs-symbols` suna tabbatar da cewa docs, specs da string dispatches suna nuni zuwa symbols
   masu aiki. Suna gano “ruɓewa” da lint/test ba sa ganowa.
5. **Tsarin rayuwa daga advisory→blocking.** Sabbin ƙofofi suna farawa a matsayin advisory (ba sa hana
   merges yayin da suke balaga), sannan su zama blocking a ƙarshen zagaye. Wannan yana rage cikas ba
   tare da rasa iyakar inganci ba.
6. **Tsallakewa cikin natsuwa idan infra ba ya nan.** Scanners (`--ratchet`) suna fita da `exit 0` idan
   binary/network ya gaza — rashin infra ba zai taɓa hana halattaccen PR ba. Wannan cikakkiyar
   ƙwarewar injiniyanci ce.
7. **Al’ada da aka mayar da ita ƙa’ida.** Hard Rules + `trust-but-verify` + stale-allowlist +
   evidence-gate suna sauya ɗa’a zuwa tabbatarwa ta atomatik.

### Rauni na gaskiya (ainihin giɓi)

1. **🔴 Rabon fast-gates har yanzu yana barin giɓin tsarin gine-gine.** `quality.yml` (PR→`release/**`)
   yanzu yana gudanar da typecheck, gwaje-gwaje masu sauri kuma deterministic, da production build na
   advisory don PRs na code, amma har yanzu ba ya gudanar da cikakken fannin release-PR daga `ci.yml`
   (coverage ratchets, package artifact, integration, E2E, SonarQube). Dalilin (sauri) yana da inganci,
   amma ya kamata ƙofar ta kasance inda merge yake faruwa (shift-left). **Shi ne mafi girman gyaran
   tsarin gine-gine da ya rage.**
2. **🟠 Haɗarin yawaitar ƙofofi/gajiya.** Kimanin ƙofofi 46 + jobs 25 suna da YAWA. Sonar da kansa
   yana gargaɗi cewa: sharuɗɗa masu yawa suna haifar da “gajiyar ƙofofi” da muhawara kan fifiko,
   tare da haɗarin yin watsi da wata ƙofa. DORA yana gargaɗin cewa ƙofofi masu nauyi suna ƙara
   lead-time. Muna rage wannan da matakan advisory da ratchets waɗanda ba cikakkun iyakoki ba ne,
   amma babu **bita na ROI na lokaci-lokaci ga kowace ƙofa** (ana iya haɗa wasu ƙananan ƙofofin
   doc-sync).
3. **🟠 Mutation score bai zama ratchet ba tukuna.** Magani mafi ƙarfi ga yin maguɗi da coverage
   har yanzu **advisory** ne. Shi ne abu mafi daraja da ke jiran kammalawa (kuma an riga an gina
   kashi 90% nasa).
4. **🟡 Advisories da ya kamata su zama blocking (tare da madaidaicin scope).** `osv` (vulnCount) da
   `oasdiff` advisory ne duk da daskararrun baselines. Kasancewar osv-advisory yana da ma’ana (sabon
   CVE a kan tsohon dep zai iya hana PR marar alaƙa) — amma akwai matsakaicin matsayi (a hana kawai
   CRITICAL+fixable, kamar yadda muka yi da Trivy). Kasancewar oasdiff advisory yana nufin canjin da
   ke karya contract zai iya wucewa.
5. **🟡 Tsaron runtime yana gudana da dare kawai.** schemathesis/garak/promptfoo/chaos/k6 suna gudana
   da dare. Shawara ce da ta dace (suna da jinkiri, kuma suna buƙatar live server), amma PR zai iya
   shigar da koma-baya a injection-guard wanda sai dare na gaba ne za a gano shi.
6. **🟡 Branch-protection a kan `main` yana KASHE.** `BRANCH_LOCK_TOKEN` yana kulle rassan _release_,
   amma `main` kansa ba shi da kariya. Scorecard/DSOMM suna rage maki saboda haka. Ana buƙatar matakin
   mai shi.
7. **🟡 CodeQL default-setup; semgrep ba a mayar da shi ƙa’ida ba.** default-setup yana aiki
   (faɗakarwa 0), amma `codeql.yml` da aka commit yana ba da ƙarin iko; semgrep yana gudana ta wata
   external cloud platform, ba a sarrafa sigarsa a cikin repo ba.

## Sashe na 3 — Cikakken Kundin Wuraren Duba Inganci (mai sauƙin ɗauka)

Rukunnai 12 da ke ƙasa su ne "tsarin inganci" a sigar da za a iya sake amfani da ita. Kowanne yana lissafa
**manufa** (abin da za a kare), **kayan aikin da muke amfani da su** da kuma **madaidaicin da bai dogara da takamaiman kayan aiki ba**
domin aiwatar da irinsa a kowane stack.

### 1. Salo da tsara rubutu (mai sakamako tabbatacce, mai sauri)

- **OmniRoute:** Prettier + ESLint ta hanyar lint-staged (pre-commit), sarari-2/alamomin zance biyu/100col.
- **Gaba ɗaya:** formatter guda ɗaya mai iya gyara ta atomatik + linter guda ɗaya, suna gudana a pre-commit kan fayilolin da aka yi staged.

### 2. Nau'uka

- **OmniRoute:** `typecheck:core` (mai toshewa) + `typecheck:noimplicit:core` (na shawarwari) + ratchet na `type-coverage` 92.17% + kasafin any na kowane fayil.
- **Gaba ɗaya:** tsauraran typecheck a CI + ma'aunin type-coverage mai ratchet + kasafin `any`/hanyar-tserewa na kowane fayil.

### 3. Gwaje-gwaje (tsanani)

- **OmniRoute:** runners 2 marasa cin karo (Node native + vitest), shards 8, ɗaukar gwaji na gaba ɗaya 60/60/60/60 + ratchet ~76% + **ƙananan iyakoki 8 na kowane module ga muhimman modules** + gwaje-gwajen property na dare + **mutation testing** na dare.
- **Gaba ɗaya:** test runner(s) + **cikakkiyar** ƙaramar iyakar ɗaukar gwaji (hana-sifili) + **ratchet** na ɗaukar gwaji (hana-koma-baya) + **ƙananan iyakoki na kowane module ga lambar mai haɗari sosai** (hana-Goodhart) + gwaje-gwaje masu dogaro da property don tsantsar dabaru + **mutation testing** na dare a matsayin ainihin ma'aunin ingancin gwaji.

### 4. Manufar gwaji (hana-yaudara)

- **OmniRoute:** `pr-test-policy` (lambar prod tana buƙatar gwaji), `check-test-masking` (yana toshe asserts da aka raunana), `pr-evidence` (da'awar nasara tana buƙatar evidence block), `test-discovery` (kowane gwaji runner ne yake tattara shi).
- **Gaba ɗaya:** shingen "sabuwar lamba ⇒ sabon gwaji" + mai gano assert da aka cire/tautology + buƙatar shaida (TDD ko gwaji mai rai) + tabbatar da cewa babu gwajin da ya zama maraya a wajen globs.

### 5. Sarƙaƙiya da lafiyar lamba (ratchets)

- **OmniRoute:** ESLint-warnings (3769↓), kwafi na jscpd (5.72%↓), sarƙaƙiyar cyclomatic+max-lines (1800↓), sarƙaƙiyar fahimta ta sonarjs (753↓), dead-code/unused-exports na knip (339↓), girman fayil na kowane fayil (an daskarar, ragewa-kawai), circular-deps (Tarjan na musamman, mai toshewa).
- **Gaba ɗaya:** sanya ratchet ga kowane ma'aunin lafiya (gargaɗi, kwafi, sarƙaƙiyar cyclomatic **da** ta fahimta, dead code, girman fayil, import cycles). Alkiblar ita ce koyaushe "kada a koma baya".

### 6. Tsaron tsaye (SAST + sirrika)

- **OmniRoute:** CodeQL (ratchet na faɗakarwa = 0), gitleaks (`[extend] useDefault=true` — yana da matuƙar muhimmanci!), SonarQube, ƙa'idojin tsaro na musamman (public-creds, error-helper, route-guard-membership, route-validation).
- **Gaba ɗaya:** SAST (CodeQL/Sonar/semgrep) tare da ratchet na faɗakarwa + na'urar binciken sirrika mai **gadon tsoffin ƙa'idoji** (tsarin musamman da ya maye gurbin tsoho = makanta) + shingayen tsaro na Hard Rule na musamman ga aikin.

### 7. Sarkar samarwa (dependencies)

- **OmniRoute:** osv-scanner + npm-audit + Trivy + Dependabot (SCA), license-checker (SPDX allowlist), lockfile-lint (HTTPS+sha512+registry), `check-deps` na hana-slopsquatting (allowlist + shekaru ≥72h).
- **Gaba ɗaya:** SCA daga tushe da yawa + allowlist na lasisi + duba ingancin lockfile + allowlist na dependency tare da duba shekaru/typosquatting + bot na sabuntawa a rukuni.

### 8. Sarkar samarwa (ginawa da fitarwa)

- **OmniRoute:** SBOM (CycloneDX + syft), asalin SLSA (`--provenance`), OpenSSF Scorecard (kowane mako), ƙarfafa workflow (zizmor: artipacked→`persist-credentials:false`, cache-poisoning, token-permissions).
- **Gaba ɗaya:** samar da SBOM yayin publish + provenance mai sa hannu (SLSA L2+) + Scorecard da aka tsara lokaci-lokaci + ƙarfafa duk workflows (tokens masu mafi ƙarancin gata, babu credentials da aka adana a non-pusher checkout, actions da aka ɗaure da SHA).

### 9. Contracts da API

- **OmniRoute:** oasdiff (canjin OpenAPI mai karya daidaito), schemathesis (contract fuzz na dare), openapi-coverage (% na routes da aka rubuta takardunsu, ratchet 38.3%), openapi-security-tiers (spec da route-guard).
- **Gaba ɗaya:** contract diff na canjin da ke karya daidaito (oasdiff/buf) + fuzz mai dogaro da property a kan spec (schemathesis) + ɗaukar takardu mai ratchet + daidaiton spec↔code.

### 10. Takardu da i18n (hana-lalacewa)

- **OmniRoute:** docs-sync (sigogi masu madubi), docs-counts-sync (lambobi a takardu da lamba), env-doc-sync, doc-links, fabricated-docs, cli-i18n, i18n-ui-coverage (`--threshold=65` + ratchet 80.1%).
- **Gaba ɗaya:** daidaita sigogi/ƙididdiga/env-vars tsakanin takardu da lamba (shinge, ba amincewa kawai ba) + tabbatar da ingancin hanyoyin haɗin ciki + ɗaukar i18n mai ratchet.

### 11. Hana-hallucination / daidaito (rukuni da ba kasafai ake samu ba)

- **OmniRoute:** known-symbols (string dispatch ⇒ living symbol), provider-consistency, fetch-targets (client fetch ⇒ real route), docs-symbols, db-rules (Hard Rules #2/#5), migration-numbering.
- **Gaba ɗaya:** ga kowane "tushen gaskiya da aka maimaita" (registry, string dispatch, nassoshi tsakanin layers), a sami shingen da ke tabbatar da ɓangarorin biyu sun dace. Yana gano lalacewar da typecheck/test ba sa ganowa.

### 12. Juriya da domain (na musamman ga samfur)

- **OmniRoute:** chaos (fault-injection), heap-growth (leak), k6 (soak), promptfoo+garak (LLM red-team OWASP LLM Top 10), dokokin juriya 3 (circuit-breaker/cooldown/lockout).
- **Gaba ɗaya:** gano hanyoyin gazawar **naku** domain sannan ku samar da shinge (ko da na dare ne) ga kowanne. Don manhajojin AI: injection red-team. Don distributed systems: chaos + leak + soak.

## Sashe na 4 — Tsarin Maimaitawa don Kowane Aiki

Gina shi a **matakai**, inda kowane mataki ke samar da amfani da kansa. Kada a yi ƙoƙarin aiwatar da dukkan rukunai 12 lokaci guda —
hakan yana haifar da irin gajiyar shingayen da Sashe na 2 ya yi gargaɗi a kai. Kowane sabon shinge yana farawa a matsayin **mai ba da shawara**, sannan ya koma **mai toshewa** idan ya daidaita.

### Babban jigon da za a iya sake amfani da shi: "tsarin shingen ratchet"

Dukkan tsarin yana zagaye ne a kan wannan tsari na fayiloli 3. Da farko, kwafe shi:

1. **`baseline.json`** — daskararren ƙimar ma'auni + `direction` (`up`/`down`) + `eps` (hana canjin sakamako marar tabbas) + `tightenSlack` + `dedicatedGate`.
2. **`collect-metrics.<ext>`** — yana gudanar da kayan aikin, yana fitar da lambar, sannan ya rubuta `metrics.json`.
3. **`check-ratchet.<ext>`** — yana kwatanta `metrics.json` da `baseline.json`; `exit 1` **kawai** idan an samu koma baya fiye da `eps`; `exit 0` (tsallakewa cikin sauƙi) idan kayan aikin/ababen more rayuwa ba su nan; tare da `--require-tighten`, `exit 1` idan ƙimar ta **inganta** ba tare da sabunta baseline ba (yana tabbatar da ci gaban).

Da zarar an kafa wannan, **kowane** sabon ma'auni (coverage, complexity, warnings, SAST alerts, bundle size, mutation score…) layi ɗaya ne kawai a cikin baseline.

### Mataki na 0 — Ginshiƙi (mako na 1)

Akwai CI; formatter + linter + typecheck + mai gudanar da gwaji 1 + **cikakken** mafi ƙarancin coverage
(misali, 60%). Pre-commit yana gudanar da bincike masu sauri waɗanda za a iya gyarawa ta atomatik. _Sakamako: babu PR da zai karya abubuwan asali._

### Mataki na 1 — Injin ratchet (mako na 2) — **ginshiƙin komai**

Aiwatar da fayiloli 3 da ke sama. Daskarar da baselines don: warnings, coverage, complexity, duplication,
dead code, file size. _Sakamako: daga nan, codebase ɗin ba zai iya yin komai ba sai ingantawa._

### Mataki na 2 — Zurfin bincike tsaye (mako na 3)

SAST (CodeQL/Sonar/semgrep) tare da ratchet na alert; na'urar binciken secrets (**gada tsohon ruleset na asali**);
SCA (osv/Dependabot) + jerin lasisin da aka amince da su + lockfile-lint. _Sakamako: sanannun vulnerabilities da
secrets da suka fallasa ba za su wuce ba._

### Mataki na 3 — Sarkar samar da build (mako na 4)

SBOM yayin publish + provenance da aka sanya wa hannu (SLSA L2) + Scorecard da aka tsara lokaci-lokaci + ƙarfafa workflow
(zizmor: mafi ƙarancin tokens, babu credentials da aka adana, actions da aka ɗaure da takamaiman siga). _Sakamako: releases masu sauƙin bin diddigi
kuma waɗanda ba za a iya yi wa katsalandan ba._

### Mataki na 4 — Ƙarfin gwaji (mako na 5–6)

Mai gudanar da gwaji na 2 idan yana da amfani; **mafi ƙarancin coverage na kowane module mai muhimmanci** (anti-Goodhart);
gwajin property-based don pure logic; **mutation testing kowane dare** → idan score na farko ya zo, mai da
`mutationScore` ratchet. _Sakamako: coverage ya daina zama ma'aunin nuna isa kawai; gwaje-gwaje suna kama bugs a zahiri._

### Mataki na 5 — Contract da dynamic (mako na 7)

Idan akwai API na jama'a: oasdiff (breaking-change, **mai toshewa**) + schemathesis (fuzz na kowane dare).
DAST/red-team kowane dare gwargwadon abin da ya dace da fannin. _Sakamako: contracts ba sa lalacewa a ɓoye._

### Mataki na 6 — Hana hallucination da takamaiman fanni (mako na 8)

Shingen consistency guda ɗaya don kowane "duplicated truth" a cikin aikin. Shingayen failure-mode
na takamaiman fanni (don AI: red-team na injection). _Sakamako: lalacewar tsari da gazawar fanni suna da kariyar tsaro._

### Mataki na 7 — Gudanarwa (mai gudana)

- Zagayowar mai ba da shawara→mai toshewa don kowane sabon shinge.
- `stale-allowlist`: kowane suppression yana da dalili + issue; ana gano suppression da ya daina amfani.
- `evidence-gate`: ikirarin nasara a cikin PR yana buƙatar hujja (gwaji ko living test).
- **Bita na ROI na kowane shinge duk bayan wata uku** (a cire ko a dakatar da tallafin waɗanda ba sa samar da amfani — yana yaƙar gajiya).
- Mayar da Hard Rules na aikinku zuwa shingayen da za a iya aiwatarwa.

### Manufofi masu ratsa komai (ba a sassautawa)

- **Ratchet, ba cikakkiyar ƙima ba.** A tsare _rashin koma baya_, ba tsayayyar lamba ba (sai dai mafi ƙarancin anti-zero).
- **Cikakken mafi ƙarancin ƙima + ratchet tare.** Mafi ƙarancin ƙimar yana hana rugujewa; ratchet yana hana zaizayar hankali.
- **Anti-Goodhart tun daga ƙira.** Kowane ma'aunin da aka sa wa buri yana buƙatar ma'aunin da zai daidaita shi (coverage ⇒ mutation + anti-masking; mafi ƙarancin ƙima na kowane module don tilasta gwada code mai wahala).
- **Tsallakewa cikin sauƙi.** Rashin ababen more rayuwa ba ya taɓa toshewa; koma baya na gaske ne kawai yake toshewa.
- **`dedicatedGate` don ma'aunai masu tsada.** Ma'aunan da ke buƙatar binary na waje suna samun script nasu (tare da tsallakewa), a wajen ratchet na tsakiya mai aiki nan take.
- **Sanya shinge inda merge ke faruwa.** Kada a bar tazara tsakanin shinge mai sauri da ainihin merge (darasin daga rarraba fast-gates).
- **Shingayen toshewa kaɗan, amma zaɓaɓɓu da kyau.** Sonar/DORA: yawan conditions = gajiya. Fi son mai ba da shawara + ratchet maimakon katangar shingayen toshewa.

## Sashe na 5 — Ingantawa da ake ba da shawara (bisa fifiko, masu dacewa)

**P0 — ROI mafi girma, kusan a shirye**

1. **Ratchet na makin mutation** (bayan Stryker na dare na farko ya samar da ƙimomi). Muhimmin maganin coverage-Goodhart; an kammala kusan 90%.
2. **Rufe sauran gibin fast-gates** — ɗaukaka production build na `quality.yml` bayan
   makon gwajinsa, sannan a ci gaba da matsar da binciken deterministic na release-PR-only zuwa hanyar PR→release.
3. **Branch-protection a kan `main`** (saitin mai shi) — yana ƙara Scorecard, yana rufe gibin DSOMM.

**P1 — masu amfani sosai** 4. **osv/oasdiff → masu toshewa tare da madaidaicin scope** — osv ya toshe CRITICAL+fixable kawai (matakai biyu kamar Trivy); oasdiff yana toshe breaking-changes. 5. **`require-tighten` → mai toshewa** (ƙarshen zagaye) — yana tabbatar da dorewar ci gaban ma'aunai. 6. **Binciken ROI/lokaci ga kowane gate** a cikin `ci-summary` — gano tare da cire gates masu jinkiri ko ƙarancin amfani.

**P2 — ragin ƙarin amfani** 7. **SLSA L3** — builder mai hermetic/reproducible (GitHub SLSA generator) idan kuna son tashi daga L2. 8. **CodeQL config da aka commit + semgrep mai version** — ƙarin iko/reproducibility. 9. **DAST smoke ga kowane PR** — ƙaramin rukuni mai sauri na schemathesis/promptfoo a kan endpoints mafi haɗari (ba na dare kawai ba). 10. **Dashboard na flakiness + ma'aunan DORA** — tabbatar da cewa gates ba sa rage sauri.

## Sashe na 6 — Darussan release na zahiri (gates da za a ƙara a Phase 9)

> Wannan sashe yana rubuta ainihin incidents daga rufe release inda wani gate **ya ɓace**,
> tare da tabbataccen shaida da gate ɗin da aka ba da shawara. Kowane abu ɗan takara ne na Sashe na 5.

### Darasin v3.8.27 (2026-06-17) — "gibin fast-gates" yana barin deterministic regressions su kai ga ranar release

**Abin da ya faru.** A lokacin `/generate-release` na v3.8.27, release PR (`release/v3.8.27` → `main`)
shi ne aiwatarwar **farko** ta cikakken matrix na `ci.yml` a cikin haɗaɗɗen zagayen. Sakamako: failures 12
a lokaci guda — **deterministic tests 3** + kusan flakes/env 9. Babu ɗayansu da ya kasance live product regression, amma
duk ba a lura da su ba saboda PRs na zagaye suna shiga `release/**` ta hanyar **Fast QG
(`quality.yml`)**, wanda BA ya gudanar da cikakken unit suite, ko `pr-test-policy` (test-masking), ko
cikakken integration suite, ko binciken daidaiton schema. Waɗannan su ne deterministic guda 3:

1. **Test ya tsufa saboda canjin UI** — `permissions modal switch buttons declare button type`:
   #4034 ya ƙara switch na 4 (an kiyaye a11y `type="button"`); ƙididdigar `=== 3` ta test ɗin ta zama
   tsohuwa. Ya kamata static analysis ya gano wannan a cikin PR na #4034.
2. **Test ya tsufa saboda canjin packaging** — `findMissingArtifactPaths ... root runtime files`:
   `dist/http-method-guard.cjs` ya zama halattaccen required-path; jerin expected na test ɗin ya zama
   tsoho.
3. **Bambancin modularization mai rasa bayanai (mafi tsanani)** — `settings schemas accept ... unprefixed
toggle`: **modularized** `updateSettingsSchema` (`schemas/settings.ts`, wanda #3988 ya ƙirƙira) ya bambanta
   da canonical ɗin (`settingsSchemas.ts`): **fields 45 da 85 — an rasa 40 + 6 sun bambanta (qdrant\*)**. Ya kasance
   **dead-code** (runtime yana amfani da canonical ɗin), don haka babu tasiri kai tsaye, amma parity
   test da aka rubuta da hannu ne kawai ya gano shi. #4030 ya maido da makamantan abubuwa 16 da aka rasa daga #3988/#3993, amma wannan ya kuɓuce.

**Gates da aka ba da shawara (Phase 9):**

- **G1 — A zahiri a rufe gibin fast-gates (yana faɗaɗa P0 #2).** A cikin `quality.yml` (PR→`release/**`),
  bayan typecheck + impacted tests, gudanar da **`pr-test-policy` (test-masking) + cikakken deterministic
  unit suite** (ko aƙalla fayilolin static/parity, waɗanda suke da sauri kuma ba su da flaky).
  Ta wannan hanyar, ana gano tsofaffin tests da cire assert a cikin PR ɗin da ya kawo su — ba a
  ranar release ba. A bar integration/e2e a waje (masu jinkiri/flaky), amma deterministic layer BA ZAI IYA ci gaba
  da kasancewa a PR→main kawai ba.
- **G2 — Gate na daidaiton modularization (SABO, ba a rufe shi a yau ba).** Wani check wanda, ga kowane symbol
  da modularized barrel (`src/shared/validation/schemas/*`, modules na `providerRegistry`,
  da sauransu) ya sake export, yake kwatanta **shape** (`z.object` keys, registry entries) da canonical
  source kuma ya **gaza idan akwai bambanci** (field da aka rasa/ƙara). Da ya gano ɓacewar fields 40 daga
  #3988 a cikin wannan PR ɗin. Yana faɗaɗa hand-written parity tests (waɗanda suke samuwa ne kawai inda wani
  ya tuna ya rubuta su). Mara tsada: yana import dukansu sannan ya kwatanta `Object.keys(shape)`.
- **G3 — Deterministic flake triage (tallafi).** LiveWS-startup da integration-combo/breaker
  tests suna gazawa saboda server timeout/cascade a CI (env), ba saboda logic ba. Yi musu alamar
  `known-flaky` (an keɓe su tare da issue) domin jan alamar release-PR ta zama **ainihin signals kawai**, ba noise
  da ke ɓoye deterministic regressions a tsakiya ba.

**Ƙa'ida:** _dole ne gate ɗin ya gudana a inda merge ke faruwa_ (an riga an ambata a cikin "Cross-cutting principles"). Incident na
v3.8.27 ya nuna cewa wannan ma ya shafi **deterministic test layer**, ba lint/typecheck kawai ba —
in ba haka ba, bashin tsofaffin tests + modularization mai rasa bayanai zai bayyana ne kawai a PR→main, a dunƙule, a
mafi munin lokaci.
