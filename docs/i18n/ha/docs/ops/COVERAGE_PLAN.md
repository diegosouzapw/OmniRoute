## Dokoki

- Manufofin ɗaukar nauyi suna aiki ne kan fayilolin tushe, ba kan `tests/**` ba.
- `open-sse/**` wani ɓangare ne na samfurin kuma dole ne ya ci gaba da kasancewa cikin iyaka.
- Sabuwar lamba bai kamata ta rage ɗaukar nauyi a wuraren da aka taɓa ba.
- A fifita gwajin halayya da sakamakon rassa fiye da cikakkun bayanan aiwatarwa.
- A fifita rumbunan bayanan SQLite na wucin gadi da ƙananan bayanan gwaji fiye da kwaikwayo masu faɗi don `src/lib/db/**`.

## Tsarin umarni na yanzu

- `npm run test:coverage`
  - Babban shingen ɗaukar nauyin lambar tushe don tarin gwaje-gwajen raka'a

  - Cikakken rahoto na kowane fayil daga gudanarwa ta baya-bayan nan

## Manufar ɗaga ma'auni a hankali

A sabunta matakan `npm run test:coverage` ne kawai bayan aikin ya zarce mataki na gaba da tazara mai kyau.

**Ma'aunin yanzu:** `npm run test:coverage` yana tilasta **60 statements / 60 lines / 60 functions / 60 branches** (an sake daidaita ma'aunin a Quality-Gates Fase 6A.1 — tsohon ma'aunin asali na 82.58% ya yi ƙaruwa fiye da gaskiya saboda yana ƙirga fayilolin gwaji kuma yana cire `open-sse`). Umarnin `test:coverage:legacy` yana adana tsohon ma'aunin 50/50/50 domin kwatanta bayanan tarihi.

Don duba matakan kai-tsaye bisa sabon rahoto, yi amfani da:

```bash
node scripts/check/test-report-summary.mjs --threshold 75
```

## Gibin da aka sani

Umarnin coverage na yanzu yana auna babban rukunin gwajin unit na Node kuma yana haɗa source da aka isa daga gare shi, ciki har da `open-sse`. Har yanzu bai haɗa coverage na Vitest cikin rahoto guda ɗaya ba. Yin wannan haɗin daga baya zai dace, amma ba shi ne abin da zai hana fara hawa daga 60% -> 80% ba.
