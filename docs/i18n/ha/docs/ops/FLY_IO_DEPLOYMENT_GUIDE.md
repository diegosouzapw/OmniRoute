Wannan takarda tana bayyana ainihin tsarin tura OmniRoute zuwa Fly.io, tare da bayani kan yanayi guda uku:

- Tura aikin na yanzu zuwa Fly.io a karon farko
- Wallafa sabbin sauye-sauyen lamba daga baya
- Sabbin ayyuka masu bin wannan tsarin tura manhaja iri ɗaya

Wannan jagora ya dogara ne kan saitin da aka tabbatar yana aiki ga aikin na yanzu. Sunan manhajar shi ne `omniroute`.

---

## 1. Manufofin Turawa

- Dandali: Fly.io
- Hanyar turawa: Wallafawa kai tsaye ta amfani da `flyctl` na cikin gida
- Muhallin aiki: Amfani da `Dockerfile` da `fly.toml` da suke akwai a ma'ajiyar lambar
- Dauwamar bayanai: Fly Volume da aka ɗora zuwa `/data`
- URL na samun dama: `https://omniroute.fly.dev/`

---

## 2. Muhimmin Saitin Aikin Yanzu

An tabbatar cewa `fly.toml` da ke cikin ma'ajiyar lambar yanzu yana ɗauke da muhimman abubuwa masu zuwa:

```toml
app = 'omniroute'
primary_region = 'sin'

[[mounts]]
  source = 'data'
  destination = '/data'

[processes]
  app = 'node run-standalone.mjs'

[http_service]
  internal_port = 20128

[env]
  TZ = "Asia/Shanghai"
  HOST = "0.0.0.0"
  HOSTNAME = "0.0.0.0"
  BIND = "0.0.0.0"
```

Bayanan kula:

- `app = 'omniroute'` yana ƙayyade manhajar Fly da za a tura aikin zuwa gare ta
- `destination = '/data'` yana ƙayyade kundin ɗora rumbun ajiya mai ɗorewa
- Dole ne wannan aikin ya saita `DATA_DIR=/data`, in ba haka ba za a rubuta ma'ajiyar bayanai da maɓallan cikin kundin wucin gadi na container

```powershell
flyctl auth login
```

### 3.3 Tabbatar da Matsayin Shiga

```powershell
flyctl auth whoami
flyctl version
```

## 4. Tura Aikin Yanzu a Karon Farko

### 4.1 Kwafi Lambar kuma Shiga Kundin

```powershell
git clone https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute.git
cd OmniRoute
```

### 4.2 Tabbatar da Sunan Manhajar

Buɗe `fly.toml` sannan ka tabbatar da layi mai zuwa:

```toml
app = 'omniroute'
```

Idan kana turawa zuwa sabuwar manhajarka, za ka iya canza shi zuwa suna na musamman a duniya, misali:

```toml
app = 'omniroute-yourname'
```

Lura:

- Tabbatar cewa manhajar da kake gani a console ta yi daidai da ƙimar `app` da ke cikin `fly.toml`
- Idan a baya ka yi amfani da wani suna daban, kamar `oroute`, kada ka rikita shi da `omniroute`

### 4.3 Ƙirƙiri Manhajar

Idan har yanzu manhajar ba ta wanzu ba:

```powershell
flyctl apps create omniroute
```

Idan ka canza sunan manhajar, maye gurbin `omniroute` da sunan da ka zaɓa.

### 4.4 Turawa ta Farko

```powershell
flyctl deploy
```

## 5. Sigogin da Ake Buƙata

Wannan aikin yana ba da shawarar saita aƙalla sigogi masu zuwa a Fly.io.

### 5.1 Sigogin da Aka Tabbatar

An yi amfani da waɗannan sigogi a ainihin turawa a kan manhajar `omniroute` ta yanzu:

- `API_KEY_SECRET`
- `DATA_DIR`
- `JWT_SECRET`
- `MACHINE_ID_SALT`
- `NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_URL`
- `OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET` (ana buƙatarsa a yanayin samarwa — ana amfani da shi don tantance sahihancin gadar WebSocket)
- `STORAGE_ENCRYPTION_KEY`

### 5.2 Game da `INITIAL_PASSWORD`

Aikin na yanzu bai saita `INITIAL_PASSWORD` ba saboda wannan turawar ba ta buƙatarsa.

Idan ba a saita shi ba:

- Bayanan log na farawa za su nuna cewa tsohuwar kalmar sirri ita ce `CHANGEME`
- Ya kamata ka canza kalmar sirrin shiga a saitunan tsarin da wuri-wuri bayan turawa

Idan kana son fara saita kalmar sirrin backend ba tare da sa hannun mai amfani ba, za ka iya ƙara ta daga baya:

- `INITIAL_PASSWORD`

## 6. Sigogin da Aka Ba da Shawara

### 6.1 Saitin Sirrika

Ana ba da shawarar amfani da waɗannan canje-canje na Fly Secrets:

| Canji                        | Shawara                 | Bayani                                                      |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| `API_KEY_SECRET`             | Wajibi                  | Ana amfani da shi wajen ƙirƙira da tabbatar da API Key      |
| `JWT_SECRET`                 | Wajibi                  | Ana amfani da shi don zaman shiga da sanya hannu kan JWT    |
| `OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET` | Wajibi a production     | Sirrin tantancewa na gadar WebSocket                        |
| `STORAGE_ENCRYPTION_KEY`     | Ana ba da shawara sosai | Yana ɓoye muhimman bayanan haɗi yayin da suke a ma'ajiya    |
| `MACHINE_ID_SALT`            | Ana ba da shawara       | Yana samar da tsayayyen mai gano na'ura                     |
| `INITIAL_PASSWORD`           | Na zaɓi                 | Yana saita kalmar sirrin backend ta farko a turawa ta farko |
| Bayanan sirri na OAuth/API   | Yadda ake buƙata        | Saitin tantancewa na manhajar waje                          |

### 6.2 Ƙimomin da Aka Ba da Shawara don Aikin Yanzu

| Canji                  | Ƙimar da Aka Ba da Shawara  |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------- |
| `DATA_DIR`             | `/data`                     |
| `NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_URL` | `https://omniroute.fly.dev` |

Bayanan kula:

- `DATA_DIR=/data` yana da matuƙar muhimmanci kuma dole ne ya yi daidai da wurin ɗora Fly Volume
- Mai tsara lokaci, kira-baya na frontend, da makamantan yanayi suna amfani da `NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_URL`

### 6.3 Saitin URL na Kira-baya na OAuth

Idan kana buƙatar kunna masu samarwa masu amfani da OAuth (misali Antigravity, Gemini, Cursor) a turawar Fly.io, ka tabbatar da waɗannan abubuwa biyu:

1. **Saita `NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_URL` zuwa yankin HTTPS na jama'a**

   ```powershell
   flyctl secrets set NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_URL=https://omniroute.fly.dev -a omniroute
   ```

   Idan kana amfani da yankin da aka keɓance, maye gurbinsa da yankin da ya dace (misali `https://omniroute.yourdomain.com`).

2. **Saita URL na kira-baya a na'ura mai sarrafa mai samarwar**

   Duk masu samar da OAuth suna amfani da hanyar kira-baya guda ɗaya `/callback` — BABU hanyar kira-baya ta musamman ga kowane mai samarwa:

   ```text
   <NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_URL>/callback
   ```

   Misali, ko Gemini, Antigravity, Cursor, ko GitLab Duo:
   - `https://omniroute.fly.dev/callback`

   Idan `NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_URL` bai yi daidai da URL na kira-baya da aka yi rajista tare da mai samarwar ba, tsarin OAuth zai gaza a matakin tura burauza.

## 7. Saita Sirrika da Umarni Guda

Waɗannan umarnai suna samar da ƙimomin bazuwar masu tsaro sannan su rubuta duk sigogin da ake buƙata don aikin yanzu zuwa Fly Secrets a mataki guda.

Bayanan kula:

- Bai haɗa da `INITIAL_PASSWORD` ba
- An yi niyya don aikin yanzu `omniroute`

```powershell
$apiKeySecret = [Convert]::ToHexString((1..32 | ForEach-Object { Get-Random -Minimum 0 -Maximum 256 })).ToLower()
$jwtSecret = [Convert]::ToHexString((1..64 | ForEach-Object { Get-Random -Minimum 0 -Maximum 256 })).ToLower()
$machineIdSalt = [Convert]::ToHexString((1..32 | ForEach-Object { Get-Random -Minimum 0 -Maximum 256 })).ToLower()
$storageKey = [Convert]::ToHexString((1..32 | ForEach-Object { Get-Random -Minimum 0 -Maximum 256 })).ToLower()
$wsBridgeSecret = [Convert]::ToHexString((1..32 | ForEach-Object { Get-Random -Minimum 0 -Maximum 256 })).ToLower()

flyctl secrets set `
  API_KEY_SECRET=$apiKeySecret `
  JWT_SECRET=$jwtSecret `
  MACHINE_ID_SALT=$machineIdSalt `
  STORAGE_ENCRYPTION_KEY=$storageKey `
  OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET=$wsBridgeSecret `
  DATA_DIR=/data `
  NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_URL=https://omniroute.fly.dev `
  -a omniroute
```

A kan Linux / macOS, za ka iya kuma amfani da `openssl rand -hex 32`:

```bash
flyctl secrets set OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET=$(openssl rand -hex 32) -a omniroute
```

Bayanan kula:

- `OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET` wajibi ne a production; rashin sa zai hana musayar-gaisuwar gadar WebSocket aiki

Idan kana kuma son saita kalmar sirri ta farko:

```powershell
flyctl secrets set INITIAL_PASSWORD=your-strong-password -a omniroute
```

## 9. Sabuntawa da Fitarwa na Gaba

Bayan sabunta lambar, tsarin fitarwa yana da sauƙi:

```powershell
git pull
flyctl deploy
```

Idan kawai kana buƙatar sabunta saituna ba tare da canza lamba ba:

```powershell
flyctl secrets set KEY=value -a omniroute
```

Fly zai gudanar da sabuntawar injuna a jere ta atomatik.

### 9.1 Bibiyar Sabuntawar Ma'ajiyar Asali Yayin Kiyaye `fly.toml` na Fork ɗinka

Idan ma'ajiyar yanzu fork ce kuma kana son daidaita sabuntawa daga ma'ajiyar asali ta `https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute`, bi tsarin aikin da ke ƙasa.

Da farko, tabbatar da ma'ajiyoyin nesa naka:

```powershell
git remote -v
```

Ya kamata ka ga aƙalla:

- `origin` yana nuna fork naka
- `upstream` yana nuna ma'ajiyar asali

Idan ba a saita `upstream` ba, ƙara shi:

```powershell
git remote add upstream https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute.git
```

Kafin daidaitawa da upstream, ɗauko sabbin commits da tags:

```powershell
git fetch upstream --tags
```

Duba sigar yanzu da tags na upstream:

```powershell
git describe --tags --always
git show --no-patch --oneline v3.4.7
```

> Lura: Sigar aikin yanzu ita ce `v3.8.0`. An bar ambaton `v3.4.7` da ke ƙasa a matsayin misalan tarihi kawai. Don fitowar ainihi, yi amfani da `:latest` ko tag na sigar yanzu (misali `:v3.8.0`).

Idan kana son haɗa sabon `main` na upstream yayin da kake tilasta kiyaye `fly.toml` na fork ɗinka, bi wannan tsarin aikin:

```powershell
git merge upstream/main
git checkout HEAD~1 -- fly.toml
git add -- fly.toml
git commit -m "chore(deploy): keep fork fly.toml"
git push origin main
```

Bayanan kula:

- `git merge upstream/main` yana daidaita sabuwar lamba daga ma'ajiyar asali
- `git checkout HEAD~1 -- fly.toml` yana dawo da `fly.toml` na fork ɗinka daga yanayin da yake kafin haɗawar
- Idan upstream bai gyara `fly.toml` ba, wannan matakin ba zai haifar da wani bambanci ba
- Idan upstream ya gyara `fly.toml`, wannan matakin yana tabbatar da cewa sunan manhajar Fly, haɗin volume, region, da sauran saitunan tura manhaja na musamman ga fork ɗinka ba za a goge su ba

Idan kana son daidaitawa da takamaiman tag na fitarwa (misali `v3.4.7`), da farko tabbatar cewa an riga an haɗa tag ɗin cikin `upstream/main`:

```powershell
git merge-base --is-ancestor v3.4.7 upstream/main
```

Samun sakamako mai nasara yana nufin cewa `upstream/main` ya riga ya ƙunshi wannan sigar; kawai za ka iya haɗa `upstream/main`.

### 9.2 Daidaitaccen Jerin Fitarwa Bayan Daidaitawa da Upstream

Bayan daidaitawa da ma'ajiyar asali, bi wannan jerin fitarwa da aka ba da shawara:

1. `git fetch upstream --tags`
2. `git merge upstream/main`
3. Dawo da `fly.toml` na fork
4. `git push origin main`
5. `flyctl deploy`
6. `flyctl status -a omniroute`
7. `flyctl logs --no-tail -a omniroute`

Wannan shi ne ainihin tsarin aikin da aka yi amfani da shi lokacin haɓaka aikin yanzu zuwa `v3.4.7` (misalin yana nuni da wata sigar tarihi; ainihin sigar yanzu ita ce `v3.8.0`).

## 11. Alamomin Nasara

Bayan an yi nasarar turawa, ya kamata rajistocin su nuna bayanai makamantan waɗannan:

```text
[bootstrap] Secrets persisted to: /data/server.env
[DB] SQLite database ready: /data/storage.sqlite
```

Waɗannan abubuwa biyu suna da matuƙar muhimmanci:

- `/data/server.env` yana tabbatar da cewa an rubuta sirrukan lokacin aiki a kundin ajiya mai ɗorewa
- `/data/storage.sqlite` yana tabbatar da cewa an rubuta ma'ajiyar bayanai a kundin ajiya mai ɗorewa

Idan ka ga `/app/data/...` a maimakon haka, an saita `DATA_DIR` ba daidai ba kuma dole ne a gyara shi nan take.

## 12. Matsalolin da Aka Fi Fuskanta

### 12.1 Shafin `Secrets` Babu Komai

Yawanci akwai dalilai biyu:

- Har yanzu ba ka gudanar da `flyctl secrets set` ba
- Kana duba wata manhaja daban (misali `oroute` maimakon `omniroute`)

### 12.2 `flyctl deploy` Yana Ba da Rahoton `app not found`

Da farko, ƙirƙiri manhajar:

```powershell
flyctl apps create omniroute
```

### 12.3 Tantancewar `fly.toml` Ta Gagara

Bincika waɗannan:

- Ko akwai haruffan da suka lalace a cikin sharhi
- Ko alamomin ambato da tsarin shigar da layi na TOML daidai ne

### 12.4 Ba a Adana Bayanai na Dindindin

Tabbatar da waɗannan biyun:

- `fly.toml` ya ƙunshi `destination = '/data'`
- An saita `DATA_DIR` zuwa `/data`

### 12.5 Zai Iya Aiki Ba Tare da `INITIAL_PASSWORD` Ba?

Eh, zai iya aiki. Zai koma amfani da kalmar sirri ta tsohuwa `CHANGEME`. Ana ba da shawarar canza kalmar sirrin backend da wuri-wuri a yanayin samarwa.

## 13. Sake Amfani da Shi don Sabbin Ayyuka

Idan kana tura sabon aiki ta bin wannan takarda, waɗannan abubuwa kawai kake buƙatar canzawa:

1. Canza ƙimar `app` a cikin `fly.toml`
2. Canza `NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_URL`
3. Bar `DATA_DIR=/data` yadda yake
4. Sake samar da `API_KEY_SECRET`, `JWT_SECRET`, `MACHINE_ID_SALT`, da `STORAGE_ENCRYPTION_KEY`
5. Bayan turawa ta farko, tabbatar cewa ana rubuta rajistoci zuwa `/data`

Kada ka sake amfani da maɓallan wani aikin da ya gabata.

```powershell

flyctl secrets list -a omniroute
```
