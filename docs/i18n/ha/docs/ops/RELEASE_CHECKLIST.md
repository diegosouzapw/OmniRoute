## Amintaccen Bugawa na npm (tsoho tun daga v3.8.51) — ana ajiyewa don amincewa idan an nema, kai tsaye a matsayin madadin

`npm-publish.yml` yana bugawa ta hanyar **npm Trusted Publishing (OIDC)** ta tsohuwa: aikin
`stage-npm` (wanda GitHub ke karɓar bakunci) yana musanya id-token na GitHub da takardar
shaida ta npm mai ɗan gajeren wa'adi don wannan gudu — babu token na npm mai dogon wa'adi a cikin sirrin ma'ajiyar, babu buƙatar 2FA, kuma an haɗa shaidar asali.
Wannan shi ne hanyar kaucewa da npm ya amince da ita yanzu da ake janye token-token da ke tsallake 2FA;
yana dawo da cikakken tsarin atomatik da aikin yake da shi har zuwa v3.8.48 tare da kiyaye
tabbacin WS1.3 (token da ya fallasa ba zai iya bugawa shi kaɗai ba — domin babu token).

**Saitin sau ɗaya (mai shi):** npmjs.com → fakitin `omniroute` → Settings → _Trusted
Publisher_ → GitHub: mai shi `diegosouzapw`, repo `OmniRoute`, workflow `npm-publish.yml`
(environment: babu). Har sai wannan ya kasance, matakin atomatik zai gaza da `ENEEDAUTH`:
sake ƙaddamarwa da `publish_mode=staged` (a ƙasa) ko `direct`.

### Bugawa da aka ajiye don amincewa (idan an nema — `publish_mode=staged`)

Tsarin aikin npm-publish ba ya ƙara bugawa kai tsaye: yana kunna tarball ɗin da aka shirya
(`check:pack-boot`) sannan ya gudanar da `npm stage publish` — ana ajiye ainihin bytes ɗin a
rajista, **ba za a iya girka su ba** har sai mai shi ya amince. An matsar da matakin 2FA na
ɗan Adam zuwa BAYAN tabbatarwa, ba kafin ta ba.

**Tsarin mai shi bayan workflow ya zama lafiya:**

1. `npm stage list omniroute` — nemo stage id (ana kuma buga shi a taƙaitaccen bayanin workflow).
2. Tabbatar da bytes ɗin da aka ajiye (ana ba da shawara): `npm stage download <id>`, sannan girka
   tarball ɗin da aka sauke cikin prefix na wucin gadi kuma kunna shi (`npm run check:pack-boot` yana sarrafa
   irin wannan hukuncin pack→install→boot ta atomatik a CI).
3. `npm stage approve <id>` — buƙatar 2FA ITA CE bugawar. `npm stage reject <id>` yana yarwa.
4. Kariyar bayan bugawa: mai tabbatarwa bayan bugawa (WS1.4 na shirin v3.8.49) yana girka
   sigar da aka buga daga rajistar jama'a a cikin container mai tsabta kuma ya kunna ta.

**Madadin gaggawa:** `workflow_dispatch` tare da `publish_mode=direct` yana dawo da
tsohon `npm publish` na nan take (a yi amfani da shi kawai idan ajiyewar don amincewa kanta ta samu matsala; a rubuta dalili).

**Ƙarfafawa na sau ɗaya (mai shi, npmjs.com):** saita Trusted Publisher na
`omniroute` a yanayin stage-only domin token mai dogon wa'adi da ya fallasa kada ya iya yin `npm publish`
kai tsaye daga ko'ina — CI zai iya ajiyewa don amincewa kawai; 2FA na mai shi kaɗai ne ke fitarwa.

**Tsarin magance gurɓataccen artifact (bai canza ba):** `npm deprecate omniroute@<bad> "<reason> — use <fixed>"`
a matsayin matakin farko na yau da kullum (mintuna, ana iya janyewa); `npm unpublish` kawai a cikin
tagar awa 72/babu masu dogaro, kuma kada ya taɓa zama matakin farko. Docker: kada a sake rubuta tag na siga — komawa baya
shi ne sake nuna `latest` zuwa digest mai kyau na ƙarshe.

**Docker Hub `latest` (wajibi a kowane buga tsayayyen SemVer):** dole ne
workflow na `docker-publish` ya yi wa **duka biyun** `X.Y.Z` tag, sannan idan
`should-promote-latest.sh` ya tabbatar wannan shi ne SemVer tsayayye mafi girma, ya yi wa `:latest`
tag da **digest iri ɗaya**. Bayan aikin: digest na Hub `latest` ya yi daidai da sabon
digest na SemVer kuma `last_updated` ya matsa. Kada a bar `:latest` a kan tsohon
gini yayin da bayanan fitarwa ke magana game da gyare-gyaren da ke kan git kawai. Shirye-shiryen farawa cikin sauri na Compose
suna amfani da `:latest`; ya kamata GitOps ya ci gaba da kafa `X.Y.Z`. Duba
[Tashoshin fitarwa na Docker](../guides/DOCKER_GUIDE.md#release-channels) da #10317.

## Hanyar-Gaggawa ta Hotfix (lakabin `hotfix`)

PR mai lakabin `hotfix` yana tsallake babban matrix na CI (E2E mai shard 9, coverage ratchet,
quality-gate, quality-extended) kuma yana riƙe da ƙofofin bincike masu sauri da ƙarfi: build,
unit shards, integration, vitest, lint/typecheck, docs-sync, `check:pack-artifact`
da gwajin fara aiki na tarball (`check:pack-boot`). Manufa: samun kore cikin ≤15min maimakon ~33min.

**Manufar shiga — dole ne a cika duka huɗu (an tsara bisa hanyoyin gaggawa na Chromium/VS Code/Node):**

1. **Tsanani**: production ya lalace — artifact da aka wallafa yana fāɗuwa yayin farawa / gyaran
   tsaro / duk mai amfani da release ɗin ya shafa. "Mai muhimmanci" ba yana nufin "ya lalace" ba.
2. **Iko**: mai repository ne kaɗai ke sanya lakabin `hotfix`. Lakabin SHI NE
   amincewar — kada a taɓa yi wa kai hidima a kan PR na campaign.
3. **Shaida**: jikin PR yana haɗa mahaɗin cikakken babban run na baya da ya kasance kore (suite ɗin da
   jobs da aka tsallake za su sake tabbatarwa) tare da gwajin gyaran kansa wanda da farko ya gaza sannan ya yi nasara.
4. **Iyaka**: cherry-pick kawai — mafi ƙarancin gyara, babu refactors, babu ƙarin sauye-sauyen da ba su da alaƙa.

Za a sake tabbatar da ɓangaren coverage/ratchet da aka tsallake ta hanyar cikakken run na gaba a kan
release branch (ci gaba da kasancewar release kore) — wannan hanyar tana tsallake JIRA kawai, ba ta taɓa tsallake tabbatarwa ba.
Diffs na tests kawai (duk fayiloli suna ƙarƙashin `tests/`, babu ko ɗaya ƙarƙashin `tests/e2e/`) suna tsallake matrix na E2E
kai tsaye, ba tare da wani lakabi ba.

### Tsarin Build

Ma’ajiyar tana amfani da directories na fitarwa guda uku daban-daban — kada a taɓa gauraya su:

| Directory | Manufa                                                                  | Ana bibiyarsa?   |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| `src/`    | Tushen manhaja (TypeScript / TSX)                                       | E                |
| `.build/` | Matsakaitan build — fitarwar `next build` (`distDir`)                   | A'a (gitignored) |
| `dist/`   | Kunshin npm mai shirye don aikawa — `assembleStandalone` ne ya haɗa shi | A'a (gitignored) |

> **Bayanin mai gudanarwa:** directory na image a VPS na nesa har yanzu yana nan a `/usr/lib/node_modules/omniroute/app/`.
> Fitarwar build ta **cikin ma’ajiya** ce kawai ta koma (`app/` → `dist/`). Deploy skills suna yin rsync na
> abubuwan da ke cikin `dist/` zuwa dir na `app/` na nesa — babu buƙatar sauya path na VPS.

**Tsarin build guda ɗaya:**

```
npm run build:release
  └─ rm -rf .build dist          (tsaftacewa)
  └─ next build → .build/next/   (matsakaitan fayiloli)
  └─ assembleStandalone          (yana kwafe standalone + static + public + natives → dist/)
  └─ writes dist/BUILD_SHA       (alamar HEAD)
```

KADA a gudanar da `npm run build` sannan wani `npm run build:cli` daban don deploy — yi amfani da
`npm run build:release`, wanda yake yin sabon build mai tsabta + sentinel a umarni guda.

## Komawa Baya

Idan sigar da aka fitar tana da matsala mai tsanani:

1. `gh release edit vX.Y.Z --prerelease` (yana nuna cewa ba ita ce sabuwar siga ba)
2. `git tag -d vX.Y.Z && git push --delete origin vX.Y.Z` (kawai idan masu amfani ba su fara amfani da ita ba tukuna)
3. Ko kuma: yi gyaran gaggawa a kan `release/vX.Y.0` → fitar da sigar gyara `vX.Y.(Z+1)`
4. Sanar da mutane nan take a GitHub Discussions da Discord

## Dokoki Masu Tsauri

- Kada a taɓa yin commit kai tsaye zuwa `main`
- Kada a taɓa amfani da `git push --force` zuwa `main` ko rassan `release/*`
- Kada a taɓa tsallake Husky hooks (`--no-verify`)
- Kada a taɓa yin commit na sirrika, bayanan shaidar shiga, ko fayilolin `.env`
- Dole ne coverage ya kasance ≥60/60/60/60 (statements/lines/functions/branches)
- A koyaushe a haɗa ko a sabunta gwaje-gwaje yayin canza lambar samarwa a cikin `src/`, `open-sse/`, `electron/`, ko `bin/`
