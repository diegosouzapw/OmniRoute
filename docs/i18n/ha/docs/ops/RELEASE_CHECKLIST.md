## npm Trusted Publishing (tsoho tun daga v3.8.51) — ana yin staging idan an nema, kai tsaye a matsayin mafita ta madadin

`npm-publish.yml` yana wallafawa ta hanyar **npm Trusted Publishing (OIDC)** a matsayin tsoho: aikin
`stage-npm` (wanda GitHub ke masaukinsa) yana musanya id-token na GitHub da shaidar npm
mai ɗan gajeren wa'adi don wannan gudanarwar — babu token na npm mai dogon wa'adi a cikin sirrukan ma'ajiyar, babu buƙatar 2FA, kuma ana haɗa bayanin asali.
Wannan shi ne hanyar tsallakewa da npm ke amincewa da ita yanzu da ake daina amfani da tokens masu kauce wa 2FA;
yana dawo da cikakken tsarin atomatik da aikin yake da shi har zuwa v3.8.48 tare da kiyaye
garantin WS1.3 (token da ya fallasa ba zai iya wallafawa shi kaɗai ba — babu token).

**Saitin sau ɗaya kawai (mai shi):** npmjs.com → package `omniroute` → Settings → _Trusted
Publisher_ → GitHub: owner `diegosouzapw`, repo `OmniRoute`, workflow `npm-publish.yml`
(environment: babu). Har sai an samar da wannan, matakin atomatik zai gaza da `ENEEDAUTH`:
sake ƙaddamarwa da `publish_mode=staged` (a ƙasa) ko `direct`.

### Wallafawa ta staging (idan an nema — `publish_mode=staged`)

Tsarin aikin npm-publish ba ya ƙara wallafawa kai tsaye: yana kunna tarball ɗin da aka shirya
(`check:pack-boot`) sannan ya gudanar da `npm stage publish` — ana ajiye ainihin bytes ɗin a
registry, kuma **ba za a iya girka su ba** har sai mai shi ya amince. An matsar da ƙofar 2FA
ta ɗan Adam zuwa BAYAN tabbatarwa, ba kafin ta ba.

**Tsarin mai shi bayan workflow ya zama kore:**

1. `npm stage list omniroute` — nemo stage id (ana kuma buga shi a taƙaitaccen bayanin workflow).
2. Tabbatar da bytes da aka yi staging ɗinsu (ana ba da shawara): `npm stage download <id>`, sannan girka
   tarball da aka sauke cikin prefix na wucin gadi kuma kunna shi (`npm run check:pack-boot` yana sarrafa
   irin wannan hukuncin pack→install→boot ta atomatik a CI).
3. `npm stage approve <id>` — buƙatar 2FA ITA CE wallafawar. `npm stage reject <id>` yana watsarwa.
4. Kariyar bayan wallafawa: mai tabbatarwa bayan wallafawa (WS1.4 na shirin v3.8.49) yana girka
   sigar da aka wallafa daga registry na jama'a cikin container mai tsabta sannan ya kunna ta.

**Mafita ta gaggawa:** `workflow_dispatch` tare da `publish_mode=direct` yana dawo da
tsohon `npm publish` na gaggawa (yi amfani da shi kawai idan staging da kansa ya yi matsala; rubuta dalili).

**Ƙarfafawa na sau ɗaya kawai (mai shi, npmjs.com):** saita Trusted Publisher don
`omniroute` a yanayin stage-only domin kada token mai dogon wa'adi da ya fallasa ya iya yin `npm publish`
kai tsaye daga ko'ina — CI zai iya yin staging kawai; 2FA na mai shi kaɗai ne ke fitarwa.

**Jagorar magance artifact da ya lalace (ba a canza ba):** `npm deprecate omniroute@<bad> "<reason> — use <fixed>"`
a matsayin matakin farko na yau da kullum (mintuna, ana iya maidowa); `npm unpublish` kawai a cikin taga na awa 72/babu masu-dogaro
kuma kada ya taɓa zama matakin farko. Docker: kada a sake rubuta alamar siga — rollback yana nufin
sake nuna `latest` zuwa digest mai kyau na ƙarshe.

**Docker Hub `latest` (wajibi ne ga kowane wallafar tsayayyen SemVer):** dole ne
workflow na `docker-publish` ya sanya alama ga **duka** `X.Y.Z` da kuma, idan
`should-promote-latest.sh` ya tabbatar wannan shi ne SemVer mafi girma kuma tsayayye, `:latest`
tare da **digest iri ɗaya**. Bayan aikin: digest na Hub `latest` ya yi daidai da sabon
digest na SemVer kuma `last_updated` ya sabunta. Kada a bar `:latest` a kan tsohon
build alhali bayanan fitowa suna magana game da gyare-gyaren da ke kan git kawai. Hanyoyin farawa cikin sauri na Compose
suna amfani da `:latest`; ya kamata GitOps ya ci gaba da ɗaurewa zuwa `X.Y.Z`. Duba
[Tashoshin fitarwa na Docker](../guides/DOCKER_GUIDE.md#release-channels) da #10317.

## Hanyar Gaggawa ta Hotfix (lakabi `hotfix`)

PR mai lakabin `hotfix` yana tsallake babban tsarin gwaje-gwajen CI (E2E mai kashi 9, coverage ratchet,
quality-gate, quality-extended) kuma yana riƙe da matakan tantancewa masu sauri kuma masu ƙarfi: build,
sassan unit, integration, vitest, lint/typecheck, docs-sync, `check:pack-artifact`
da gwajin fara aiki na tarball (`check:pack-boot`). Manufa: samun koren sakamako cikin ≤15min maimakon ~33min.

**Ka'idar shiga — dole ne duka huɗun su cika (an tsara bisa hanyoyin gaggawa na Chromium/VS Code/Node):**

1. **Muniwar matsala**: production ya lalace — artifact da aka wallafa yana faɗuwa yayin fara aiki / gyaran
   tsaro / matsalar ta shafi kowane mai amfani da release ɗin. "Mai muhimmanci" ba yana nufin "ya lalace" ba.
2. **Iko**: mai repository ne kawai ke sanya lakabin `hotfix`. Lakabin SHI NE
   amincewar — kada a taɓa sanya shi da kai a campaign PR.
3. **Shaida**: jikin PR yana haɗa hanyar zuwa cikakken babban gwajin baya da ya yi kore (suite ɗin da
   jobs ɗin da aka tsallake za su sake tabbatarwa) tare da gwajin gyaran da da farko ya gaza sannan ya yi nasara.
4. **Iyaka**: cherry-pick kawai — mafi ƙarancin gyara, babu refactors, babu ƙarin canje-canje marasa alaƙa.

Za a sake tabbatar da ɓangaren coverage/ratchet da aka tsallake a cikakken gwaji na gaba a
reshe na release (ci gaba da tabbatar da release mai kore) — hanyar tana tsallake JIRA kawai, ba tabbatarwa ba.
Canje-canjen da suka shafi tests kawai (duk fayiloli suna ƙarƙashin `tests/`, babu ko ɗaya ƙarƙashin `tests/e2e/`) suna tsallake tsarin E2E
ta atomatik, ba tare da wani lakabi ba.

> Fitarwar build ta **cikin ma'ajiyar kawai** ce ta koma (`app/` → `dist/`). Ƙwarewar deploy suna rsync
> abubuwan da ke cikin `dist/` zuwa babban fayil ɗin `app/` na nesa — ba a buƙatar canza kowace hanyar VPS.

**Tsarin build guda ɗaya:**

```
npm run build:release
  └─ rm -rf .build dist          (tsaftacewa)
  └─ next build → .build/next/   (abubuwan tsaka-tsaki)
  └─ assembleStandalone          (yana kwafe standalone + static + public + natives → dist/)
  └─ yana rubuta dist/BUILD_SHA  (alamar tsaro ta HEAD)
```

KADA a gudanar da `npm run build` sannan a bi shi da `npm run build:cli` daban don deploy — yi amfani da
`npm run build:release`, wanda ke yin cikakken sake build mai tsafta tare da alamar tsaro a umarni guda.

`scripts/release/dry-run-lts-cut.mjs` ba ya aiwatar da komai: yana karanta git da `gh` sannan ya buga

green — an saita layin jiran Mergify na `release` (G11: `queue_rules`, `checks_timeout`,
