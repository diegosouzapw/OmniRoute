## npm Trusted Publishing (tsoho tun daga v3.8.51) — ana yin staging idan an nema, kai tsaye a matsayin mafita ta madadin

`npm-publish.yml` yana wallafawa ta hanyar **npm Trusted Publishing (OIDC)** a matsayin tsoho: aikin
`stage-npm` (wanda GitHub ke masaukinsa) yana musanya id-token na GitHub da shaidar npm
mai ɗan gajeren wa'adi don wannan gudanarwar — babu token na npm mai dogon wa'adi a cikin sirrukan ma'ajiyar, babu buƙatar 2FA, kuma ana haɗa bayanin asali.
Wannan shi ne hanyar tsallakewa da npm ke amincewa da ita yanzu da ake daina amfani da tokens masu kauce wa 2FA;
yana dawo da cikakken tsarin atomatik da aikin yake da shi har zuwa v3.8.48 tare da kiyaye
garantin WS1.3 (token da ya fallasa ba zai iya wallafawa shi kaɗai ba — babu token).

**Saitin sau ɗaya kawai (mai shi):** npmjs.com → package `omniroute` → Settings → _Trusted
Publisher_ → GitHub: owner `diegosouzapw`, repo `OmniRoute`, workflow `npm-publish.yml`
(environment: babu). Har sai an samar da wannan, matakin atomatik zai gaza da `ENEEDAUTH`:
sake ƙaddamarwa da `publish_mode=staged` (a ƙasa) ko `direct`.

### Wallafawa ta staging (idan an nema — `publish_mode=staged`)

Tsarin aikin npm-publish ba ya ƙara wallafawa kai tsaye: yana kunna tarball ɗin da aka shirya
(`check:pack-boot`) sannan ya gudanar da `npm stage publish` — ana ajiye ainihin bytes ɗin a
registry, kuma **ba za a iya girka su ba** har sai mai shi ya amince. An matsar da ƙofar 2FA
ta ɗan Adam zuwa BAYAN tabbatarwa, ba kafin ta ba.

**Tsarin mai shi bayan workflow ya zama kore:**

1. `npm stage list omniroute` — nemo stage id (ana kuma buga shi a taƙaitaccen bayanin workflow).
2. Tabbatar da bytes da aka yi staging ɗinsu (ana ba da shawara): `npm stage download <id>`, sannan girka
   tarball da aka sauke cikin prefix na wucin gadi kuma kunna shi (`npm run check:pack-boot` yana sarrafa
   irin wannan hukuncin pack→install→boot ta atomatik a CI).
3. `npm stage approve <id>` — buƙatar 2FA ITA CE wallafawar. `npm stage reject <id>` yana watsarwa.
4. Kariyar bayan wallafawa: mai tabbatarwa bayan wallafawa (WS1.4 na shirin v3.8.49) yana girka
   sigar da aka wallafa daga registry na jama'a cikin container mai tsabta sannan ya kunna ta.

**Mafita ta gaggawa:** `workflow_dispatch` tare da `publish_mode=direct` yana dawo da
tsohon `npm publish` na gaggawa (yi amfani da shi kawai idan staging da kansa ya yi matsala; rubuta dalili).

**Ƙarfafawa na sau ɗaya kawai (mai shi, npmjs.com):** saita Trusted Publisher don
`omniroute` a yanayin stage-only domin kada token mai dogon wa'adi da ya fallasa ya iya yin `npm publish`
kai tsaye daga ko'ina — CI zai iya yin staging kawai; 2FA na mai shi kaɗai ne ke fitarwa.

**Jagorar magance artifact da ya lalace (ba a canza ba):** `npm deprecate omniroute@<bad> "<reason> — use <fixed>"`
a matsayin matakin farko na yau da kullum (mintuna, ana iya maidowa); `npm unpublish` kawai a cikin taga na awa 72/babu masu-dogaro
kuma kada ya taɓa zama matakin farko. Docker: kada a sake rubuta alamar siga — rollback yana nufin
sake nuna `latest` zuwa digest mai kyau na ƙarshe.

**Docker Hub `latest` (wajibi ne ga kowane wallafar tsayayyen SemVer):** dole ne
workflow na `docker-publish` ya sanya alama ga **duka** `X.Y.Z` da kuma, idan
`should-promote-latest.sh` ya tabbatar wannan shi ne SemVer mafi girma kuma tsayayye, `:latest`
tare da **digest iri ɗaya**. Bayan aikin: digest na Hub `latest` ya yi daidai da sabon
digest na SemVer kuma `last_updated` ya sabunta. Kada a bar `:latest` a kan tsohon
build alhali bayanan fitowa suna magana game da gyare-gyaren da ke kan git kawai. Hanyoyin farawa cikin sauri na Compose
suna amfani da `:latest`; ya kamata GitOps ya ci gaba da ɗaurewa zuwa `X.Y.Z`. Duba
[Tashoshin fitarwa na Docker](../guides/DOCKER_GUIDE.md#release-channels) da #10317.

## Hanyar Gaggawa ta Hotfix (lakabi `hotfix`)

PR mai lakabin `hotfix` yana tsallake babban tsarin gwaje-gwajen CI (E2E mai kashi 9, coverage ratchet,
quality-gate, quality-extended) kuma yana riƙe da matakan tantancewa masu sauri kuma masu ƙarfi: build,
sassan unit, integration, vitest, lint/typecheck, docs-sync, `check:pack-artifact`
da gwajin fara aiki na tarball (`check:pack-boot`). Manufa: samun koren sakamako cikin ≤15min maimakon ~33min.

**Ka'idar shiga — dole ne duka huɗun su cika (an tsara bisa hanyoyin gaggawa na Chromium/VS Code/Node):**

1. **Muniwar matsala**: production ya lalace — artifact da aka wallafa yana faɗuwa yayin fara aiki / gyaran
   tsaro / matsalar ta shafi kowane mai amfani da release ɗin. "Mai muhimmanci" ba yana nufin "ya lalace" ba.
2. **Iko**: mai repository ne kawai ke sanya lakabin `hotfix`. Lakabin SHI NE
   amincewar — kada a taɓa sanya shi da kai a campaign PR.
3. **Shaida**: jikin PR yana haɗa hanyar zuwa cikakken babban gwajin baya da ya yi kore (suite ɗin da
   jobs ɗin da aka tsallake za su sake tabbatarwa) tare da gwajin gyaran da da farko ya gaza sannan ya yi nasara.
4. **Iyaka**: cherry-pick kawai — mafi ƙarancin gyara, babu refactors, babu ƙarin canje-canje marasa alaƙa.

Za a sake tabbatar da ɓangaren coverage/ratchet da aka tsallake a cikakken gwaji na gaba a
reshe na release (ci gaba da tabbatar da release mai kore) — hanyar tana tsallake JIRA kawai, ba tabbatarwa ba.
Canje-canjen da suka shafi tests kawai (duk fayiloli suna ƙarƙashin `tests/`, babu ko ɗaya ƙarƙashin `tests/e2e/`) suna tsallake tsarin E2E
ta atomatik, ba tare da wani lakabi ba.

### Tsarin Build

Repository ɗin yana amfani da output directories guda uku mabambanta — kada a taɓa gauraya su:

| Directory | Manufa                                                                 | Ana bibiyarsa?   |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| `src/`    | Tushen application (TypeScript / TSX)                                  | E                |
| `.build/` | Matsakaitan build — output na `next build` (`distDir`)                 | A'a (gitignored) |
| `dist/`   | npm bundle mai shirye don aikawa — `assembleStandalone` ne ya haɗa shi | A'a (gitignored) |

> **Bayanin mai gudanarwa:** directory na image na VPS mai nisa har yanzu yana nan a `/usr/lib/node_modules/omniroute/app/`.
> Output na build na **cikin-repo** ne kawai ya matsa (`app/` → `dist/`). Skills na deploy suna yin rsync
> na abubuwan da ke cikin `dist/` zuwa dir na `app/` mai nisa — ba a buƙatar canza path na VPS.

**Gudanarwar build guda ɗaya:**

```
npm run build:release
  └─ rm -rf .build dist          (tsaftacewa)
  └─ next build → .build/next/   (matsakaita)
  └─ assembleStandalone          (yana kwafe standalone + static + public + natives → dist/)
  └─ yana rubuta dist/BUILD_SHA  (alamar tsaro ta HEAD)
```

KADA KA gudanar da `npm run build` sannan wani `npm run build:cli` daban don deploy — yi amfani da
`npm run build:release` wanda ke yin cikakken rebuild mai tsabta + alamar tsaro a umarni guda.

Bayan v3.8.59 sigar da ke biye ita ce 3.9.0, kuma ƙarshenta zai zama rassa biyu masu dogon zango:
`stable/v3` (layin v3 LTS, npm `latest`) da `develop` (v4, an ɗaga zuwa 4.0.0, npm
`nightly`). Tsarin reshe/tasha, forward-port da lakabobi suna cikin
[RELEASE_STRATEGY.md](./RELEASE_STRATEGY.md); shirin yana cikin [ROADMAP](../../ROADMAP.md) (Mataki na 3). Yankan yana gudana sau ɗaya;
3.8.58 yana yin atisayensa daga farko har ƙarshe a kan fork, sannan 3.8.59 ya rufe da
[jerin binciken GO/NO-GO](./LTS_GO_NO_GO.md).

### Gwajin bushe (karantawa kawai, amintacce a kowane lokaci)

```bash
npm run release:dry-run-lts-cut                       # yankan na ainihi: 3.9.0 daga HEAD, tag ɗin da ya gabata v3.8.59
npm run release:dry-run-lts-cut -- --from <3.9.0-tip> # tsayar da commit na tushe
```

`scripts/release/dry-run-lts-cut.mjs` ba ya aiwatar da komai: yana karanta git da `gh`, sannan ya buga
dukkan jerin matakan — sharuɗɗan farko (tushen yana warwarewa, tag ɗin da ya gabata yana nan, `package.json` yana kan
sigar da ake nufi, akwai buɗaɗɗen issue na `release-freeze`, babu buɗaɗɗen issue na `Release branch not green`
a kan reshen fitarwa da yake akwai — reshen da babu shi yana bayar da rahoton `?` wanda ba a sani ba, ba zai taɓa zama
kore ba — an saita jerin gwano na Mergify `release` (G11: `queue_rules`, `checks_timeout`,
lakabin `queue`), har yanzu ruleset na `release/*` yana hana gogewa da force-push, kuma
`stable/v3` da `develop` ba su wanzu ba tukuna), matakan rassan biyu, abubuwan da ke tayar da dormant-workflow
da sharuɗɗan `if:` da suka zama gaskiya (da waɗanda suka ci gaba da kasancewa a kulle da repository variable ko
aka tsayar da su ga canonical repository), dist-tags da ake tsammani (`latest` → 3.9.0, `next` da
`nightly` babu komai) da kuma mayar da baya. Exit `0` = `RESULT: READY`, `1` = wani sharadin farko mai hanawa
ya gaza (`✗`), `2` = kuskuren amfani. `--advisory <id,...>` yana saukar da bincike zuwa gargaɗi (`!`)
ba tare da ɓoye shi ba.

Gudanar da gwajin bushe na yankan na ainihi yayin da daskarar da fitarwar 3.9.0 take a buɗe — ana
ƙirƙirar rassan bayan tag kuma kafin Mataki na 12c ya cire daskararwar.

### Atisayen 3.8.58 (a fork kawai)

```bash
# 1. Yi gwajin bushe a kan ƙarshen yanzu tare da sigogin atisaye
npm run release:dry-run-lts-cut -- --target-version 3.8.58 --previous-tag v3.8.57 \
  --advisory freeze,base-green

# 2. Aiwatar a kan remote na FORK (ana ƙin origin, ko duk wani remote da URL ɗinsa yake na canonical
#    repository; kowane mataki yana neman tabbatarwa a terminal)
git remote add rehearsal https://github.com/<you>/OmniRoute.git
node scripts/release/dry-run-lts-cut.mjs --execute --remote rehearsal \
  --target-version 3.8.58 --previous-tag v3.8.57 --advisory freeze,base-green

# 3. Gwada dormant workflows a cikin fork (workflow_dispatch inda gwajin bushe
#    ya bayar da rahoton tsayarwa ga canonical-repository), sannan a mayar da baya
node scripts/release/dry-run-lts-cut.mjs --execute --rollback --remote rehearsal \
  --target-version 3.8.58 --previous-tag v3.8.57 --advisory freeze,base-green
```

Ana gina commit na ɗaga `develop` da git plumbing (ba a taɓa working tree ba), kuma yana ɗaga
fayiloli biyar iri ɗaya da commit na buɗe-zagaye: `package.json`, `open-sse/package.json`,
`electron/package.json`, `package-lock.json` da `docs/openapi.yaml`. Ana buɗe sashen `[4.0.0]`
na CHANGELOG da kwafinsa na i18n a kan `develop` daga baya, kafin PR na farko. Script ɗin ba ya
taɓa canza npm dist-tags — yi atisayen waɗannan a kan scratch package.

### Artifact na samfotin PR (gina sau ɗaya, ɗaukaka bytes iri ɗaya)

`.github/workflows/preview-artifact.yml` yana gina production tarball guda ɗaya daga kan PR head sannan
ya inganta ainihin wannan build ɗin (#8084 sashe (a)). PRs na repository iri ɗaya kawai; ba a wallafa komai.

```bash
gh workflow run preview-artifact.yml -f pr_number=<N>   # ko ƙara lakabin `preview-artifact`
gh run download <run-id> --name preview-artifact-pr<N>-<sha7> --dir preview
cd preview && sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS
gh attestation verify omniroute-*.tgz --repo diegosouzapw/OmniRoute
npm install -g ./omniroute-*.tgz                          # girka samfoti
```

Gudanarwar tana yin `npm ci`, `npm run build:release`, `npm run check:pack-artifact`, tana shirya
tarball, tana gudanar da `npm run check:pack-boot` (sirrika na bogi, ephemeral data dir), tana sake shiryawa kuma
ta gaza sai dai idan digest ɗin daidai yake, sannan ta rubuta `artifact-identity.json` (head SHA, base
SHA, lockfile hash, platform, arch, node ABI, bundler, build policy —
`scripts/release/artifact-identity.mjs`) kuma ta tabbatar da tarball a wani job na daban. Ɗaukaka
samfoti na nufin girka wannan tarball ɗin: kada a sake ginawa daga tushe.

### Yankan (3.9.0, bayan GO)

1. An rubuta GO a cikin [LTS_GO_NO_GO.md](./LTS_GO_NO_GO.md).
2. `npm run release:dry-run-lts-cut -- --from v3.9.0` yana buga `RESULT: READY`.
3. Ƙirƙiri rassan a kan `origin` da hannu ta amfani da commands da gwajin bushe ya buga —
   script ɗin ya ƙi yin push zuwa `origin`. Don sake amfani da commit na develop da aka duba, gudanar da
   atisayen `--execute` a kan ƙarshen 3.9.0 zuwa fork ɗinka da farko; yana buga SHA guda biyu, kuma


5. Dormant workflows suna kunna bisa wanzuwar reshe: `forward-port.yml` (push zuwa
