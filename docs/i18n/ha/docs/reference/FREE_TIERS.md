## Tsaruka biyu — ƙirgawa da yanke shawara

OmniRoute yana amsa "kyauta ne?" ta hanyar tsaruka biyu waɗanda da gangan suke karanta
mabambantan tushe:

| Tsari                | Tushen gaskiya                                                                                                                                                                                    | Inda ake nunawa                                                                                                                 |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ƙirgawa / nunawa** | Kundin da aka warware — tushen da aka tura wanda bayanan Radar suka lulluɓe (`getRadarCatalog`)                                                                                                   | Jimillar matakin kyauta, katin kasafi, allunan bayanai                                                                          |
| **Yanke shawara**    | Kundin da aka tura kawai (`FREE_MODEL_BUDGETS` a cikin `open-sse/config/freeModelCatalog.data.ts`) tare da dabarun tantancewa na cikin gida (`:free` suffix, farashin sifili, `grantsFreeAccess`) | Duk mai amfani da `src/shared/utils/freeModels.ts`: shigo da samfur, routing na `auto/*`, `GET /v1/models`, da samfotin burauza |

Ƙirgawa na iya inganta duk lokacin da ake samun bayanan feed. Yanke shawara yana nan kan
artifact ɗin release, don haka amsar tana zama iri ɗaya a burauza da kan uwar garke,
ana iya maimaita ta ba tare da intanet ba, kuma ana iya gwada ta ba tare da bayanan ma'ajiyar bayanai ba. Barin samfotin burauza
ya karanta tushe ɗaya yayin da shigo da bayanan uwar garke ke karanta wani zai samar da
samfoti da bai dace da abin da ke faruwa bayan dannawa ba — an kiyaye wannan rabuwar da
gangan.

## Hanya & abubuwan lura

- Lambobin **ƙiyasin iyaka mafi girma ne** daga iyakokin matakin kyauta da kowane mai samarwa ya rubuta zuwa **2026-06-17**, waɗanda aka tattara ta binciken yanar gizo. Matakan kyauta suna sauyawa koyaushe — sake tabbatarwa kafin dogaro da wani adadi.
- **Abin da kowace shigarwa ke tabbatarwa a zahiri.** Babu wata shigarwa da ke ɗauke da ƙimar amincewa ta kowane layi, kuma API ɗin ma ba ya bayar da ita — ɗauki kowane adadin da ke sama a matsayin ƙiyasi mai irin ingancin da ba a bayyana ba. Abubuwa biyu sun bambanta, domin ana tattara su da hannu maimakon samo su ta hanyar hasashe: shigarwa 44 suna da tsayawar dole da aka rubuta ta wata madogara mai zaman kanta (39 daga cikinsu layukan xKiro ne, waɗanda duk suke amfani da izinin yau da kullum guda ɗaya), kuma shigarwa 13 suna ɗauke da bayanin bayyana amfani da umarni wajen horarwa. Ana saita `hardStopGuaranteed` ne kawai idan sharuɗɗan mai samarwar da kansa suka ce wuce adadin kyauta zai sa a ƙi buƙatar maimakon a fara cajin ku a ɓoye, tare da madogarar a cikin tsokaci kusa da shigarwar; ba a taɓa saita shi zuwa `true` ta tsoho ba, kuma shigarwar da babu wanda ya tabbatar da ita tana kasancewa ba a saita ta ba. Saboda haka, rashin tutar tsayawar dole yana nufin "ba a tabbatar ba", ba wai "an san za a caje ku" ba. Yanayin **STRICT** (kariyar zaɓin kai-tsaye ta sarrafa turawa `freeAccessPolicy=strict`) yana amincewa ne kawai da shigarwar da ke ɗauke da tutar; ana cire duk sauran matakan kyauta a matsayin `no-hard-stop` (duba `open-sse/services/autoCombo/strictZeroCostFilter.ts`).
- `estMonthlyFreeTokens` = token na wata-wata masu maimaituwa kawai. **Kiredit ɗin rajista na sau ɗaya ba ya maimaituwa** kuma ana ƙirga shi a matsayin 0. Matakan da aka dakatar su ma 0 ne.
- Iyakacin token na yau da kullum → `monthly = daily × 30`. Idan RPD kaɗai aka rubuta → `RPD × ~800 output tokens × 30`. Idan RPM/TPM kaɗai ne (ba tare da iyakar yau da kullum ba) → **babu iyaka** (duba ƙasa).
- **Kyauta na dindindin, amma babu iyakar token da aka wallafa** (`siliconflow`, `glm-cn`, `tencent`, `baidu`, `kilo-gateway`, `opencode-zen`, `gemini`, `ollama-cloud`): waɗannan dama ce ta kyauta mai maimaituwa ta gaske, wadda aka iyakance ta yawan buƙatu/aiki lokaci guda. Muna rarraba su a matsayin `recurring-uncapped` kuma **ba ma taɓa haɗa jimlarsu** — ninka `RPM × 24/7 × 30d` zai samar da iyakar ƙirƙirarre (wato hauhawar da muke ƙi). An jera su ne domin ku san suna nan.
- **Ƙarin da ajiya ke buɗewa:** ƙaramin ƙarin kuɗi na sau ɗaya wanda ke ɗaga adadin kyauta na dindindin (OpenRouter: $10 → buƙatu 1000/rana ≈ +24M/wata). Ana bayar da shi a matsayin adadi na daban, ba a haɗa shi cikin babban adadin dindindin.
- **Adadin da cancanta ke ƙuntatawa** (`eligibilityGate: "regional-identity"`): ainihin adadin mai maimaituwa wanda yake buɗewa ne kawai bayan tantance shaidar mutum da ke da alaƙa da yanki (tabbatar da suna na ainihi a babban yankin China a yau). Ana ƙirga shi da ƙa'idar cire maimaituwar tafki iri ɗaya a cikin wani adadi na daban (`gatedRecurringTokens`), kuma ba a taɓa haɗa shi cikin babban adadin dindindin ba. Tsarin (`freeType`) bai canza ba, saboda haka turawa ba ta canza ba.
- **Rukunin shaida.** Ƙa'idar ita ce: adadin da ke cikin kundin yana ambaton madogararsa a cikin tsokacin `// evidence:` kusa da shigarwar — `public-page` (shafin mai samarwa da kowa zai iya karantawa), `api-public` (mashigar mai samarwa da ba ta buƙatar tantancewa, misali mashigar tsare-tsare ta jama'a ta NaraRouter a router.bynara.id), ko `console-verified <date> por <who>` (adadin yana bayyane ne kawai a cikin na'urar sarrafa asusu; tsokacin yana rubuta wanda ya gan shi da lokacin da ya gan shi, da kuma shafin jama'a da ke cewa iyakar tana nan). Halin yau: tubalan biyar da aka sake bincika a 2026-09-02 suna ɗauke da shi (`gemini`, `groq`, `mistral`, `ollama-cloud`, `nara`); shigarwar da suka gabaci sake binciken 2026-09-02 suna ci gaba da amfani da binciken baya har sai an sake gyara su; duk wani adadi **sabo ko wanda aka canza** ba tare da tsokacin shaida ba kuskure ne. ## Me ya sa adadinmu ya fi na sauran masu tara bayanai ƙanƙanta

Yawancin alkaluman "token kyauta a kowane wata" a wannan fanni jimillar adadin da aka ware wa kowane samfuri ne. Namu ba haka yake ba, da gangan:

- **Ana ƙirga kowace ma'ajiyar bai ɗaya sau ɗaya kawai.** Tsarin kyauta na Mistral yana da kaso guda na 1B/wata ga kowace ƙungiya; jera shi ƙarƙashin samfura biyar ba ya mai da shi 5B. Idan aka tara bisa kowane samfuri, kundin namu zai nuna **~7.4B** (an sake lissafawa a 2026-09-03; CI ba ya tabbatar da wannan adadi — a sake auna shi duk lokacin da kundin ya canza) — babban taken yana cewa **~1.47B** domin wannan ne ainihin abin da asusu ɗaya na kowane mai samarwa zai iya kashewa.
- **Ana sauya iyakokin yau da kullum, amma ba ƙimar gudu ba.** Iyakacin tokens/rana da aka rubuta ya zama `× 30`; iyakacin buƙatu/rana da aka rubuta ya zama `RPD × ~800 tokens × 30`; mai samarwa da yake wallafa buƙatu-a-kowane-minti kawai ba shi da adadin wata-wata kuma ana jera shi a matsayin _mara iyaka_, ba a taɓa haɗa shi cikin jimilla ba. Ninka iyakar ƙimar gudu da 24/7 shi ne ƙarin ƙarya da muka ƙi yi.
- **Ana nuna ƙason da ke bayan tantance shaidar yanki dabam** (`+~6M behind regional identity verification`), domin yawancin masu karatu ba za su iya amfani da shi ba.
- **Kireditin rajista na watan farko ne kawai** kuma ana bayar da rahotonsa a matsayin adadi na biyu, ba a taɓa haɗa shi da adadin dindindin ba.
- **CI ne ke tilasta daidaiton adadin.** `npm run check:docs-counts` yana sake lissafa jimillar daga kundin kuma yana hana ginin kammalawa idan wannan fayil, README ko katin kasafin kuɗi suka kauce daga gare su.

## Teburin bayanan kula na ToS

> **Alamar ToS shawara ce kawai, ba ƙofar tantance hanyar turawa ba.** Masu samar da sabis da aka yi wa alamar `tos` har yanzu ana haɗa su cikin tsarin zaɓen hanya da haɗaɗɗen tsari/madadin ta tsohuwa; alamar tana bayyana ne kawai a `/dashboard/free-tiers` da `/api/free-tier/summary`. Ma'aunin nema na `excludeTosAvoid` yana shafar kallon taƙaitaccen bayani ne kawai, ba tsarin zaɓen hanya na gaba ɗaya ba. Hukuncin yana cikin `open-sse/config/freeTierCatalog.ts` (don bayani ne, injunan zaɓen hanya ba sa karanta shi).

> Takaitaccen bayani kan sharuɗɗan kowane mai samar da sabis game da wakili na sirri mai amfani guda ɗaya da aka ɗora a sabar kai. `caution` = akwai sharadin amfani na sirri ko na wakili da ya dace a bincika; `ambiguous` = ba a fayyace ba; `ok` = an ba da izini a sarari. Don bayani ne kawai, ba shawarar doka ba — kai ne za ka yanke hukunci.
