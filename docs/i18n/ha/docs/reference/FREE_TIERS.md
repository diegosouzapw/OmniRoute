## Tsarin mulki biyu — ƙidaya vs yanke shawara

OmniRoute yana amsa "shin kyauta ne?" ta hanyar tsarin mulki guda biyu waɗanda da gangan suke karanta
tushe daban-daban:

| Tsarin Mulki        | Tushen gaskiya                                                                                                                                                           | Yana nuna                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ƙidaya / nunawa** | Kundin da aka warware — tushen da aka aika wanda aka rufe ta hanyar ciyarwar Radar (`getRadarCatalog`)                                                                   | Jimillar matakin kyauta, katin kasafin kuɗi, dashboards                                                                             |
| **Yanke shawara**   | Kundin da aka aika kawai (`FREE_MODEL_BUDGETS` a `open-sse/config/freeModelCatalog.data.ts`) tare da ƙa'idodin gida (`:free` suffix, farashi sifili, `grantsFreeAccess`) | Kowane mai amfani da `src/shared/utils/freeModels.ts`: shigo da samfuri, `auto/*` routing, `GET /v1/models`, da previews na browser |

Ƙidaya na iya inganta duk lokacin da akwai ciyarwa. Yanke shawara yana kasancewa akan
kayan aikin saki, don haka amsar iri ɗaya ce a cikin browser da kuma akan uwar garken,
mai iya sake haifuwa a layi, kuma mai iya gwadawa ba tare da database ba. Barin browser
preview ya karanta tushe ɗaya yayin da shigo da uwar garken ya karanta wani zai haifar da
preview wanda bai yarda da abin da ke faruwa akan danna ba — an kiyaye rabuwar
da gangan.

## Hanyar Aiki & Gargadi

- Lambobi sune **ƙididdigar iyaka mafi girma** daga iyakokin kyauta na kowane mai bayarwa kamar yadda aka rubuta a ranar **2026-06-17**, waɗanda aka tattara ta hanyar binciken yanar gizo. Matakan kyauta suna canzawa koyaushe — sake tabbatarwa kafin dogaro da adadi.
- **Abin da shigarwa ke tabbatarwa a zahiri.** Babu wani shigarwa da ke ɗauke da ƙimar amincewa ta kowane jere, kuma API baya bayar da komai — ɗauki kowane adadi a sama a matsayin ƙididdiga na inganci ɗaya, wanda ba a bayyana ba. Gaskiya biyu sun bambanta, saboda an tsara su da hannu maimakon a cire su: shigarwa 44 suna ɗauke da tsayawa mai wuya da aka rubuta da kansa (39 daga cikinsu layukan xKiro, waɗanda duk suna raba izini ɗaya na yau da kullun), kuma shigarwa 13 suna ɗauke da bayanin horar da sauri. `hardStopGuaranteed` an saita shi ne kawai lokacin da sharuɗɗan mai bayarwa suka ce wucewar izinin kyauta yana ƙin buƙatar maimakon fara biyan kuɗi a hankali, tare da tushen a cikin sharhi kusa da shigarwar; ba a taɓa saita shi zuwa `true` ba, kuma shigarwa wanda babu wanda ya tabbatar ya kasance ba a saita shi ba. Don haka alamar tsayawa mai wuya da ta ɓace tana nufin "ba a kafa ba", ba "an san zai biya ku ba". **Yanayin STRICT** (mai gadi na `freeAccessPolicy=strict` mai zaɓi) yana amincewa da shigarwa waɗanda ke ɗauke da alamar kawai; kowane matakin kyauta an cire shi a matsayin `no-hard-stop` (duba `open-sse/services/autoCombo/strictZeroCostFilter.ts`).
- `estMonthlyFreeTokens` = alamomin wata-wata masu maimaitawa kawai. **Kiredit na rajista na lokaci ɗaya baya maimaitawa** kuma yana ƙidaya a matsayin 0. Matakan da aka dakatar suma 0 ne.
- Iyakar alamar yau da kullun → `monthly = daily × 30`. RPD kawai aka rubuta → `RPD × ~800 output tokens × 30`. RPM/TPM kawai (babu iyakar yau da kullun) → **ba a iyakance ba** (duba ƙasa).
- **Kyauta har abada, amma babu iyakar alamar da aka buga** (`siliconflow`, `glm-cn`, `tencent`, `baidu`, `kilo-gateway`, `opencode-zen`, `gemini`, `ollama-cloud`): waɗannan sune ainihin samun kyauta mai maimaitawa, mai iyakancewa da sauri/haɗuwa. Muna rarraba su `recurring-uncapped` kuma **bamu taɓa haɗa su ba** — ninka `RPM × 24/7 × 30d` zai samar da rufin fantasy (haɓakar da muka ƙi). An jera su don ku san cewa suna wanzu.
- **Ƙarfafa buɗe ajiya:** ƙaramin ƙari na lokaci ɗaya wanda ke ɗaga ƙimar kyauta har abada (OpenRouter: $10 → 1000 req/day ≈ +24M/mo). An ruwaito shi a matsayin adadi daban, an ajiye shi daga babban taken.
- **Ƙimar da aka iyakance ta cancanta** (`eligibilityGate: "regional-identity"`): ainihin ƙimar maimaitawa wanda ke buɗewa ne kawai bayan binciken asali mai iyaka da yanki (tabbatar da sunan gaske na babban yankin China a yau). An ƙidaya shi da dokar cirewa ta tafki ɗaya zuwa adadi daban (`gatedRecurringTokens`), ba a taɓa shigar da shi cikin babban taken ba. Tsarin (`freeType`) bai canza ba, don haka hanyar ba ta canza ba.
- **Nau'ikan shaida.** Dokar: lambar a cikin kundin tana ambaton tushen ta a cikin sharhin `// evidence:` kusa da shigarwar — `public-page` (shafin mai bayarwa wanda kowa zai iya karantawa), `api-public` (maɓallin ƙarshe mara izini na mai bayarwa, misali maɓallin ƙarshe na tsare-tsare na jama'a na NaraRouter a router.bynara.id), ko `console-verified <date> por <who>` (adadi yana bayyane ne kawai a cikin na'urar wasan bidiyo ta asusu; sharhin yana rubuta wanda ya gani da kuma lokacin, da kuma shafin jama'a wanda ke cewa iyakar tana wanzu). Halin yau: tubalan biyar da aka sake dubawa a ranar 2026-09-02 suna ɗauke da shi (`gemini`, `groq`, `mistral`, `ollama-cloud`, `nara`); shigarwa waɗanda suka riga 2026-09-02 sake dubawa suna gadon binciken da ya gabata har sai an taɓa su; kowane **sabon ko canza** lambar ba tare da sharhin shaida ba kuskure ne. A yau `mistral` kawai aka tabbatar da na'urar wasan bidiyo.

## Me yasa adadinmu ya fi na sauran masu tattarawa ƙanƙanta

Yawancin adadin "alamomin kyauta a kowane wata" a wannan fannin jimlar alamomin kowane samfuri ne. Nammu ba haka bane, da gangan:

- **Kowane rukunin da aka raba ana kirga shi sau ɗaya.** Tsarin kyauta na Mistral izini ne na 1B/wata ga kowace kungiya; sanya shi a ƙarƙashin samfura biyar baya sanya shi 5B. Idan aka haɗa kowane samfuri, kundinmu zai nuna **~7.4B** (an sake lissafa shi a ranar 2026-09-03; wannan adadin ba CI-gated bane — a sake auna shi duk lokacin da kundin ya canza) — taken yana cewa **~1.47B** saboda shine abin da asusu ɗaya na kowane mai bayarwa zai iya kashewa.
- **An canza iyakar yau da kullun, ba a canza farashi ba.** Iyakar alamomin/rana da aka rubuta ta zama `× 30`; iyakar buƙatun/rana da aka rubuta ta zama `RPD × ~800 tokens × 30`; mai bayarwa wanda kawai ke buga buƙatun-kowane-minti **bashi da** adadin wata-wata kuma an jera shi a matsayin _mara iyaka_, ba a taɓa haɗawa ba. Ninka iyakar farashi da 24/7 shine hauhawar farashin da muka ƙi.
- **An nuna adadin da ke bayan binciken asalin yanki daban** (`+~6M behind regional identity verification`), saboda yawancin masu karatu ba za su iya amfani da su ba.
- **Kiredit na rajista na wata na farko ne kawai** kuma ana bayar da rahoto a matsayin adadi na biyu, ba a taɓa haɗawa cikin adadi mai ɗorewa ba.
- **CI ce ke aiwatar da adadin.** `npm run check:docs-counts` yana sake lissafa jimillar daga kundin kuma yana soke ginawa lokacin da wannan fayil, README ko katin kasafin kuɗi suka kauce daga gare su.

## Teburin kula da Sharuɗɗan Sabis (ToS)

> **Alamar ToS shawara ce, ba hanyar shiga ba.** Masu bayarwa da aka yiwa alama `tos` har yanzu an haɗa su a cikin hanyar sadarwa da haɗawa/komawa ta tsoho; alamar tana bayyana ne kawai a `/dashboard/free-tiers` da `/api/free-tier/summary`. Ma'aunin tambaya na `excludeTosAvoid` yana shafar kallon taƙaitawa kawai, ba hanyar sadarwa ta duniya ba. Hukuncin yana cikin `open-sse/config/freeTierCatalog.ts` (bayani ne, ba injinan hanyar sadarwa ke karanta shi ba).

> Karatu mai sauri game da sharuɗɗan kowane mai bayarwa don wakili na sirri mai mai masaukin kansa, mai amfani ɗaya. `caution` = sharuɗɗan amfani na sirri ko wakili da ya cancanci dubawa; `ambiguous` = ba a bayyana ba; `ok` = an ba da izini a fili. Bayani ne, ba shawarar shari'a ba — kai ne za ka yanke shawara.

## OpenCode Free: takura kwangilar abokin ciniki (#14313)

Mai bada sabis na `opencode` mara maɓalli (na jama'a `https://opencode.ai/zen/v1`) yana ƙin duk wata buƙata wadda bata dace da kwangilar abokin ciniki na OpenCode ba tare da **403 `FreeTierError`** da kuma jumlar _"Ana iya amfani da matakin kyauta na OpenCode ne kawai daga cikin OpenCode"_. Wannan ƙin yarda ne mai iyakance ga buƙata (hukunci ɗaya akan kowane asusu don irin buƙatar), ba hana samfuri bane ko sanyaya haɗi — OmniRoute yana rarraba shi a matsayin `project_route_error`, yana tsallake kulle samfuri / sanyaya, kuma (akan hanyar `noauth` ta roba) yana dakatar da sake zaɓin haɗin kai tsaye na ɗan gajeren lokaci (TTL). Aika buƙatun da ke ɗauke da jerin kayan aiki marasa komai, `stream: true`, da kuma kanun zaman OpenCode/UA (`opencodeFreeTierContract.ts`) ko kuma ku yi tsammanin 403.
