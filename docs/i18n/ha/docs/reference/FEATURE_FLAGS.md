> Maɓallan lokacin aiki ne da ke sauya halayen OmniRoute **ba tare da sake tura manhajar ba**.
> Kowace alama da aka jera a nan an bayyana ta a cikin
> [`src/shared/constants/featureFlagDefinitions.ts`](../../src/shared/constants/featureFlagDefinitions.ts)
> — tushen gaskiya guda ɗaya. Dashboard da REST API dukansu suna karantawa daga
> wannan fayil, don haka an samar da jadawalin da ke ƙasa domin ya yi daidai da shi 1:1.

---

## Mene Ne Feature Flags

Feature flag wani maɓalli ne mai suna (boolean ko enum) wanda za a iya sauya ƙimarsa a
lokacin aiki kuma a adana ta a ma'ajiyar bayanai, ba tare da buƙatar sake tura tsarin ba. Ana
bayyana kowace alama ta hanyar `FeatureFlagDefinition` mai `key`, `label`,
`description`, `category`, `defaultValue`, `type`, da alamar `requiresRestart`.

### Jerin Warware Ƙima

Ana tantance **ƙima mai aiki** ta wata alama ta hanyar
[`resolveFeatureFlag()`](../../src/shared/utils/featureFlags.ts) bisa wannan
jerin fifiko (mafi girman fifiko ne ke rinjaya):

1. **Sauyin DB** — ƙimar da aka adana a teburin `key_value` ƙarƙashin
   namespace na `feature_flags` (wanda aka saita ta dashboard ko REST API).
2. **Environment variable** — `process.env[<KEY>]`, idan an saita shi kuma ba komai ba ne.
3. **Ƙimar tsoho ta bayani** — `defaultValue` daga `featureFlagDefinitions.ts`.

Ana ɗaukar alamar boolean a matsayin **mai aiki** idan ƙimarta mai aiki ita ce `"true"`,
`"1"`, ko `"yes"` (duba `isFeatureFlagEnabled()`).

> [!NOTE]
> Yawancin alamomi kuma suna da environment variable mai dacewa da su mai **suna iri ɗaya**
> wanda aka bayyana a cikin [`ENVIRONMENT.md`](./ENVIRONMENT.md). Sauyin DB na alamar
> yana da fifiko a kan wannan environment variable. Alamar da ke da
> `requiresRestart: true` ana adana ta nan take, amma sai an sake karanta ta yayin fara
> tsarin — sauya ta yana nuna banner na **"Sake Fara Server"** a dashboard.

## Kunna/Kashe Alamomi

### Dashboard

Je zuwa **Dashboard → Settings → Feature Flags**
(`/dashboard/settings/feature-flags`). Grid ɗin
(`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/settings/components/FeatureFlagsGrid.tsx`)
yana goyon bayan:

- **Bincike** ta maɓalli ko bayanin, da **tace** ta rukuni (ƙari da wani hangen nesa na **Requires Restart** da aka ƙirƙira).
- Wani **maɓallin kunnawa/kashewa** don alamomin boolean da wani **zaɓi mai saukowa** don alamomin enum
  (`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/settings/components/FeatureFlagCard.tsx`).
- Alamar **tushe** ga kowace alama — `DB`, `ENV`, ko `DEF` — tana nuna inda ƙimar da take aiki ta fito.
- Maɓallin **Sake Saitawa** (ana nunawa ne kawai don alamomin da aka samo daga `DB`) don cire wuce gona da iri, da maɓallin **Sake Saitawa Duk Wuce Gona da Iri** a ƙasa.
- Wani banner na **Sake Fara Sabar** lokacin da aka canza alamar `requiresRestart`.

### REST API

Duk ayyuka suna wucewa ta hanya ɗaya:
[`src/app/api/settings/feature-flags/route.ts`](../../src/app/api/settings/feature-flags/route.ts).
Kowace hanya tana buƙatar zaman dashboard mai inganci (`401` in ba haka ba).

#### `GET /api/settings/feature-flags`

Yana dawo da kowace alama tare da ƙimar ta mai aiki, tushe, da taƙaitawa.

```jsonc
{
  "flags": [
    {
      "key": "REQUIRE_API_KEY",
      "label": "Require API Key",
      "description": "Require an API key for all incoming requests",
      "category": "security",
      "type": "boolean",
      "enumValues": null,
      "defaultValue": "false",
      "effectiveValue": "false",
      "source": "default", // "db" | "env" | "default"
      "requiresRestart": false,
      "warningLevel": "caution",
    },
    // ... all 77 flags
  ],
  "summary": {
    "total": 56,
    "active": 0,
    "inactive": 0,
    "overriddenByDb": 0,
    "overriddenByEnv": 0,
  },
}
```

#### `PUT /api/settings/feature-flags`

Saita ko cire wani wuce gona da iri guda ɗaya. Jiki: `{ key: string; value?: string }`.
Barin `value` yana cire wuce gona da iri (yana dawo da yanayin muhalli / tsoho).

```bash
# Set a DB override
curl -X PUT http://localhost:20128/api/settings/feature-flags \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"key":"REQUIRE_API_KEY","value":"true"}'

# Remove the override (no "value")
curl -X PUT http://localhost:20128/api/settings/feature-flags \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"key":"REQUIRE_API_KEY"}'
```

Martanin yana nuna sabon `effectiveValue`/`source`, `previousValue`/
`previousSource`, da `requiresRestart`. Maɓallan da ba a sani ba da ƙimar enum da suka wuce iyaka ana ƙi su da `400`.

#### `DELETE /api/settings/feature-flags`

Yana share **dukkanin** wuce gona da iri na DB lokaci ɗaya, yana dawo da kowace alama zuwa ƙimar ta ta muhalli / tsoho. Yana dawo da `{ cleared: <count>, message: "..." }`.

> [!NOTE]
> Alamomin da ke da `requiresRestart: true` suna aiki ne kawai bayan an sake loda tsari.
> Tsarin sake farawa na dashboard yana kiran `POST /api/restart` sannan kuma yana bincika
> `GET /api/health/ping` har sai sabar ta dawo kan layi.

## Madadin Gaggawa na Budget

`OMNIROUTE_EMERGENCY_FALLBACK` (category `runtime`, default `true`) yana sarrafa
hanyar free-fallback ta gaggawa a cikin
[`open-sse/services/emergencyFallback.ts`](../../open-sse/services/emergencyFallback.ts).
Idan an kunna shi, requests da suka ƙare budget ɗinsu za a tura su zuwa free fallback
provider/model maimakon su gaza gaba ɗaya. Saita shi zuwa `false` (ko `0`) — ta
dashboard toggle, DB override, ko environment variable na `OMNIROUTE_EMERGENCY_FALLBACK`
— don kashe wannan halayya da barin requests da budget ɗinsu ya ƙare su
gaza. (An bayyana shi a matsayin dashboard toggle a cikin PRs #3741 / #3752.)
