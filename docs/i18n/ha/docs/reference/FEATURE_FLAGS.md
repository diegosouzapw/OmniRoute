> Maɓallan lokacin aiki da ke sauya halayyar OmniRoute **ba tare da sake turawa ba**.
> Kowace tuta da aka jera a nan an ayyana ta a cikin
> [`src/shared/constants/featureFlagDefinitions.ts`](../../src/shared/constants/featureFlagDefinitions.ts)
> — tushe guda ɗaya na gaskiya. Dashboard da REST API dukansu suna karantawa daga
> wannan fayil, don haka an samar da teburin da ke ƙasa domin ya dace da shi 1:1.

---

## Mene Ne Tutocin Fasali

Tutar fasali maɓalli ne mai suna (boolean ko enum) wanda za a iya sauya ƙimarsa a
lokacin aiki kuma a adana ta a cikin database, ba tare da buƙatar sake tura process ba. Ana
bayyana kowace tuta ta hanyar `FeatureFlagDefinition` mai `key`, `label`,
`description`, `category`, `defaultValue`, `type`, da alamar `requiresRestart`.

### Tsarin Warwarewa

Ana warware **ƙima mai aiki** ta wata tuta ta hanyar
[`resolveFeatureFlag()`](../../src/shared/utils/featureFlags.ts) bisa wannan
jeri na fifiko (mafi girman fifiko ne ke rinjaya):

1. **Sauyin DB** — ƙimar da aka adana a cikin teburin `key_value` ƙarƙashin
   namespace na `feature_flags` (wanda aka saita ta dashboard ko REST API).
2. **Environment variable** — `process.env[<KEY>]`, idan an saita shi kuma ba fanko ba ne.
3. **Ƙimar tsoho ta ayyana** — `defaultValue` daga `featureFlagDefinitions.ts`.

Ana ɗaukar tutar boolean a matsayin **mai aiki** idan ƙimarta mai aiki ita ce `"true"`,
`"1"`, ko `"yes"` (duba `isFeatureFlagEnabled()`).

> [!NOTE]
> Yawancin tutoci kuma suna da environment variable mai **suna iri ɗaya**
> wanda aka rubuta bayaninsa a [`ENVIRONMENT.md`](./ENVIRONMENT.md). Sauyin DB na tutar
> yana da fifiko a kan wannan environment variable. Tutar da ke da
> `requiresRestart: true` ana adana ta nan take amma sai an sake karanta ta yayin fara
> process — sauya ta yana nuna sanarwar **"Sake Fara Server"** a dashboard.

## Kunna da Kashe Tutoci

### Dashboard

Je zuwa **Dashboard → Settings → Feature Flags**
(`/dashboard/settings/feature-flags`). Grid ɗin
(`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/settings/components/FeatureFlagsGrid.tsx`)
yana goyon bayan:

- **Bincike** ta maɓalli ko bayani, da **tacewa** ta rukuni (tare da ƙarin ra'ayin
  **Requires Restart** na wucin gadi).
- **Maɓallin kunna/kashewa** don tutocin boolean da **jerin zaɓi** don tutocin enum
  (`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/settings/components/FeatureFlagCard.tsx`).
- **Alamar tushe** ga kowace tuta — `DB`, `ENV`, ko `DEF` — wadda ke nuna inda
  ƙimar da ake amfani da ita ta fito.
- Maɓallin **Reset** (wanda ake nuna wa tutocin da tushensu yake `DB` kawai) don cire override,
  da maɓallin **Reset All Overrides** a ƙasa.
- Sanarwar **Restart Server** idan aka canza tutar `requiresRestart`.

### REST API

Dukkan ayyuka suna bi ta hanya guda:
[`src/app/api/settings/feature-flags/route.ts`](../../src/app/api/settings/feature-flags/route.ts).
Kowace hanya tana buƙatar ingantacciyar zaman dashboard (`401` idan babu).

#### `GET /api/settings/feature-flags`

Yana dawo da kowace tuta tare da ƙimar da ake amfani da ita, tushenta, da taƙaitaccen bayani.

```jsonc
{
  "flags": [
    {
      "key": "REQUIRE_API_KEY",
      "label": "Require API Key",
      "description": "Require an API key for all incoming requests",
      "category": "security",
      "type": "boolean",
      "enumValues": null,
      "defaultValue": "false",
      "effectiveValue": "false",
      "source": "default", // "db" | "env" | "default"
      "requiresRestart": false,
      "warningLevel": "caution",
    },
    // ... dukkan tutoci 74
  ],
  "summary": {
    "total": 56,
    "active": 0,
    "inactive": 0,
    "overriddenByDb": 0,
    "overriddenByEnv": 0,
  },
}
```

#### `PUT /api/settings/feature-flags`

Saita ko cire override guda ɗaya. Jiki: `{ key: string; value?: string }`.
Rashin saka `value` yana cire override ɗin (yana dawo da env / default).

```bash
# Saita override na DB
curl -X PUT http://localhost:20128/api/settings/feature-flags \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"key":"REQUIRE_API_KEY","value":"true"}'

# Cire override ɗin (babu "value")
curl -X PUT http://localhost:20128/api/settings/feature-flags \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"key":"REQUIRE_API_KEY"}'
```

Amsar tana maimaita sabon `effectiveValue`/`source`, da `previousValue`/
`previousSource`, da kuma `requiresRestart`. Ana ƙin maɓallan da ba a sani ba da ƙimomin enum
da suka fita daga iyakar da `400`.

#### `DELETE /api/settings/feature-flags`

Yana share **dukkan** overrides na DB lokaci guda, yana dawo da kowace tuta zuwa ƙimar
env / default ɗinta. Yana dawo da `{ cleared: <count>, message: "..." }`.

> [!NOTE]
> Tutocin da suke da `requiresRestart: true` suna fara aiki ne kawai bayan sake loda process.
> Tsarin sake kunnawa na dashboard yana kiran `POST /api/restart`, sannan yana ci gaba da bincikar
> `GET /api/health/ping` har sai server ya sake tashi.

## Madadin Kasafin Kuɗi na Gaggawa

`OMNIROUTE_EMERGENCY_FALLBACK` (rukuni `runtime`, tsohuwar ƙima `true`) yana sarrafa
hanyar madadin kyauta ta gaggawa a cikin
[`open-sse/services/emergencyFallback.ts`](../../open-sse/services/emergencyFallback.ts).
Idan an kunna shi, buƙatun da suka ƙare kasafin kuɗinsu ana tura su zuwa
mai bayarwa/samfuri na madadin kyauta maimakon su gaza kai tsaye. Saita shi zuwa `false` (ko `0`) — ta hanyar
maɓallin dashboard, ƙimar maye gurbin DB, ko canjin yanayi na `OMNIROUTE_EMERGENCY_FALLBACK`
— don kashe wannan hali kuma a bar buƙatun da suka ƙare kasafin kuɗinsu
su gaza. (An nuna shi a matsayin maɓallin dashboard a cikin PRs #3741 / #3752.)
