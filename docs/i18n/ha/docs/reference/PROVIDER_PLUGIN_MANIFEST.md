`open-sse/config/providerPluginManifest.ts` yana bayyana ƙa'idar plugin ta mai samarwa
wadda ta dace da JSON. `open-sse/config/providerPluginManifestRegistry.ts` yana haɗa wannan
ƙa'ida da rajistar masu samarwa ta yanzu don sidecars kamar Bifrost,
CLIProxyAPI, ko router na Go/Rust a nan gaba. Rajistar TypeScript ita ce har yanzu
tushen gaskiya, amma sidecars za su iya amfani da manifest ɗin ba tare da shigo da
lambar executor, tsoffin ƙimomin OAuth, headers, ko yanayin environment na process ba.

Ana kuma samun wannan manifest ɗin ta HTTP a
`GET /api/v1/provider-plugin-manifest` don sidecars da ke gudana a wajen process.

OmniRoute yana sanar da Bifrost da CLIProxyAPI wannan URL ɗin ta hanyar
request header na `X-OmniRoute-Provider-Manifest-Url`. Saita
`OMNIROUTE_PROVIDER_MANIFEST_URL` idan sidecar yana buƙatar URL na jama'a ko na hanyar sadarwar
container maimakon asalin request na gida.

## Sabunta Manifest

HTTP endpoint ɗin yana mayar da `Cache-Control: public, max-age=60` da ingantaccen
`ETag`. Ya kamata sidecar ya riƙe manifest na ƙarshe da aka tabbatar kuma ya aika da ETag ɗinsa
a cikin `If-None-Match` yayin sabuntawa. Amsar `304 Not Modified` ba ta da body;
sidecar zai ci gaba da amfani da manifest da ke cikin cache. Idan babu ingantaccen manifest a cache,
dole ne sidecar ya aika request mara sharadi maimakon karɓar `304`.

## Manufa

Matsar da metadata na masu samarwa zuwa ga ƙa'idar plugin domin daga ƙarshe sidecar mai
ƙarancin latency ya iya ɗaukar nauyin hanyar request mai zafi, yayin da OmniRoute ke riƙe
route na TypeScript a matsayin mashigar manufofi da mafita ta madadin. Manifest ɗin ƙari ne:
ba ya sauya routing na request da kansa.

## Ƙa'ida

Manifest ɗin ya ƙunshi:

- id da alias na mai samarwa
- tsarin upstream da sunan executor
- nau'in auth, auth header, da auth prefix na zaɓi
- metadata na endpoint mai tsayayyen ƙima
- cancantar sidecar da bayyanannun dalilan da ya sa mai samarwa zai ci gaba da kasancewa a TS
- metadata na model da ta dace da JSON, kamar tsawon context, alamomin vision/reasoning, da
  params marasa tallafi
- alamomin capability da suka haɗa da `apikey`, `oauth`, `custom-executor`,
  `passthrough-models`, `responses`, `sidecar-candidate`, `usage-fetch`, da `usage-supported`

Da gangan manifest ɗin bai haɗa da:

- OAuth client secrets da tsoffin ƙimomin secret
- warware environment a lokacin runtime
- request headers da kayan taimako na public credential
- masu gina URL masu sauyawa
- functions na executor
- bayanan ciki na session pool

## Alamomin Capability

`capabilities` jerin alamomi ne da aka jera bisa tsari, waɗanda aka samo daga shigarwar rajista. Ya kamata masu haɗawa
su ɗauke shi a matsayin amsar da na'ura za ta iya karantawa ga "me wannan mai samarwa zai iya yi", maimakon
sake karanta tushen TypeScript.

| Alama                | Ma'ana                                                                                                 |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `apikey`             | Yana karɓar API key (`authType` shi ne `apikey` ko `optional`).                                        |
| `oauth`              | Yana amfani da tsarin OAuth ko session.                                                                |
| `responses`          | Yana bayyana base URL na OpenAI Responses-API.                                                         |
| `passthrough-models` | Yana bayar da models kai tsaye daga upstream maimakon static catalog.                                  |
| `custom-executor`    | Yana gudanar da executor wanda ba na tsoho ba, don haka yana ci gaba da kasancewa a hanyar TypeScript. |
| `sidecar-candidate`  | Yana madubi ga `sidecar.eligible` — yana da aminci a yi la'akari da shi don shigar da sidecar.         |
| `usage-fetch`        | Yana da usage ko quota fetcher da aka haɗa (`getUsageForProvider`).                                    |
| `usage-supported`    | Usage API yana karɓar wannan mai samarwa (`isSupportedUsageConnection`).                               |

`usage-fetch` don ganowa kawai ne. Yana nuna cewa OmniRoute ya san yadda ake karanta usage na
mai samarwar; ba ya kunna fetching, sauya ma'anar quota, ko nuna cewa an kunna
widget na quota na Dashboard ga mai samarwar — ana sarrafa wannan widget ɗin dabam ta
`USAGE_SUPPORTED_PROVIDERS`. Tushen gaskiya shi ne `USAGE_FETCHER_PROVIDERS` a cikin
`open-sse/services/usage/fetcherProviders.ts`.

Ana tsara wannan jerin bisa strings da usage dispatcher yake karɓa, don haka yana haɗa canonical ids
da aliases kuma ya ɗan fi yawan masu samarwa da aka yi wa alama: entries waɗanda
ba masu samar da chat ba ne a rajistar manifest (misali mai samar da bincike na `firecrawl`
da mai samar da ACP na `amazon-q`) ba su da manifest entry da za a yi wa alama.

`usage-supported` yana amsa ko routes na usage na server da Dashboard suna karɓar connection
na mai samarwar. Yana madubi ga `isSupportedUsageConnection()` (`src/lib/usage/providerLimits.ts`)
da `supportsProviderQuota()` (`src/shared/utils/providerQuotaVisibility.ts`), dukkansu ana sarrafa su ta
`USAGE_SUPPORTED_PROVIDERS` (`open-sse/services/usage/supportedProviders.ts`). Ba kamar
`usage-fetch` ba, ana fitar da shi ne bisa provider id kaɗai — runtime guard yana yin
`USAGE_SUPPORTED_PROVIDERS.includes(providerId)` ba tare da warware alias ba, don haka manifest
yana kiyaye wannan ƙa'idar. Alamomin biyu suna da iyakoki daban-daban: masu samarwa 3 suna ɗauke da
`usage-fetch` kawai (`opencode`, `opencode-zen`, `xai`) kuma 1 yana ɗauke da
`usage-supported` kawai (`xiaomi-mimo-token-plan`), don haka ɗaya ba ya nufin ɗayan.

## Amfani da Sidecar

Ya kamata sidecars su ɗauki `sidecar.eligible` a matsayin alamar tantance ɗan takara mai taka-tsantsan, ba a matsayin shawarar turawa marar sharaɗi ba. Ya kamata abin da za a fara shigo da shi ya kasance masu samarwa da ke amfani da API-key da static-endpoint ta hanyar default executor. Masu samarwa da ke da custom web executors, hanyoyin OAuth/session, dynamic URL builders, ko pool config za su ci gaba da kasancewa a hanyar TypeScript fallback har sai wani sidecar ya aiwatar da halayya makamanciya kuma telemetry ta tabbatar da daidaito.

Matakan ƙaura da ake ba da shawara:

1. Samar da kuma tabbatar da ingancin provider plugin manifest daga TS registry.
2. Koyar da Bifrost ko CLIProxyAPI yadda za su shigo da manifest ɗin don masu samarwa na API-key/static.
3. Tura masu samarwa da suka cancanta ta cikin sidecar a bayan `OMNIROUTE_RELAY_BACKEND`, tare da barin TS fallback a kunne.
4. Ɗaukaka matsayin masu samarwa ne kawai idan success rate, p99 latency, streaming behavior, da unsupported-param handling sun yi daidai da hanyar TS.
5. Ƙara sidecar-native plugins don custom executors, iyalin mai samarwa ɗaya a lokaci guda.

## Dalilin Rashin Saka Masu Samarwa Kai Tsaye A Cikin Next

Bai kamata Next frontend ya mallaki aiwatar da masu samarwa ba. Ya kamata ya kira API boundary. Daga nan backend zai iya yanke shawarar ko zai yi amfani da TypeScript executor, Bifrost, CLIProxyAPI, ko wani native sidecar na gaba. Wannan yana sanya request signing, allowlist checks, DB policy, da fallback behavior a wuri guda kafin duk wani miƙa aiki ga sidecar.
