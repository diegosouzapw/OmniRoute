Relays (Vercel, Deno, Cloudflare) suna kawo ƙarshen haɗin upstream a kan
backend marar sabar domin OmniRoute ya iya fita daga tabbataccen yanki yayin da ake
ajiye provider API key a ɓangaren sabar. Wannan takarda tana bayanin nau'ikan matsala
biyu da masu gudanarwa suke fuskanta a production da kuma hanyoyin farfaɗowa da
OmniRoute ke samarwa ga kowannensu.

## Yadda ake adana relay auth

Lokacin da kuka tura relay daga **Settings → Proxy Pool → Deploy Relay**,
tsarin turawar yana adana auth token na relay a cikin filin JSON na proxy mai suna
`notes`:

- Idan an saita `STORAGE_ENCRYPTION_KEY`, ana rubuta token ɗin a matsayin `relayAuthEnc`
  (an ɓoye shi da AES yayin ajiya).
- In ba haka ba, ana rubuta shi a matsayin plaintext `relayAuth`.

A lokacin request, `extractRelayAuth(notes)` yana dawo da duk nau'in da yake akwai,
don haka relay ɗin zai ci gaba da aiki bayan sake kunnawa.

## Nau'in matsala na 1 — token da ba za a iya warware ɓoyewarsa ba bayan sauya key

**Alama:** relays da suke aiki a baya yanzu suna dawo da kurakuran upstream
`401`/auth bayan sauya `STORAGE_ENCRYPTION_KEY` a environment ko secret-manager.
Ba za a iya sake warware ɓoyayyen blob na `relayAuthEnc` da aka adana ba, saboda haka
`extractRelayAuth` yana dawo da empty string kuma relay ɗin ba ya aika auth.

**Farfaɗowa — gyara a wurin (ba tare da sake turawa ba):**

1. Buɗe **Settings → Proxy Pool**.
2. Layukan relay da auth ɗinsu ya ɓace suna nuna alamar rawaya ta `auth missing` da
   maɓallin **Repair**.
3. Danna **Repair**. OmniRoute zai kira
   `POST /api/settings/proxies/[id]/repair-relay`, wanda zai:
   - warware ɓoyewar `relayAuthEnc` da **current** key,
   - sake rubuta plaintext `relayAuth` cikin `notes`,
   - dawo da `{ repaired: true, mode: "recovered" }`.

Relay ɗin zai ci gaba da aiki ba tare da sake shigar da bayanan izinin turawa ba.

Wannan yana aiki ne kawai idan `STORAGE_ENCRYPTION_KEY` na yanzu har yanzu zai iya
warware ɓoyewar blob ɗin. Idan kun sauya key ɗin **ba tare da** migration ba, ba za
a iya dawo da blob ɗin ba.

## Nau'in matsala na 2 — token da ba za a iya dawo da shi ba (an sauya key ɗin gaba ɗaya)

**Alama:** kun danna **Repair** kuma kuka samu
`{ repaired: false, mode: "redeploy", status: 409 }`.

**Farfaɗowa — sake turawa:**

Ba za a iya dawo da token ɗin da aka adana ta amfani da key na yanzu ba. Sake tura
relay ɗin daga modal ɗin da kuka yi amfani da shi tun farko (menu na **Deploy Relay**
→ Vercel / Deno / Cloudflare). Tsarin turawar zai rubuta sabon token (wanda aka
ɓoye da key na yanzu), sannan alamar `auth missing` ta layin za ta ɓace.

A cikin UI, maɓallin **Repair** da kansa yana buɗe modal na sake turawa idan ba za
a iya dawo da token ɗin ba, saboda haka ba sai kun neme shi da kanku ba.

## Nau'in matsala na 3 — ana iya isa relay amma ba ya cikin ƙoshin lafiya

**Alama:** gwajin proxy (maɓallin `speed`) yana nuna relay ɗin yana aiki amma requests
har yanzu suna gazawa lokaci-lokaci.

Duba relay awareness headers da auto-test probe na OmniRoute ya dawo da su
(duba **Settings → Proxy Pool** da counters na `relayTested` / `relayAlive`):

- `x-relay-url` — relay backend ɗin da ya amsa.
- `x-relay-mode` — `ts` | `bifrost` | `auto` na wannan request.
- `x-relay-attempts` — adadin relay hops da aka gwada kafin samun nasara.
- `x-relay-fallback` — `true` lokacin da request ya koma daga backend da aka fi so
  zuwa TypeScript relay.

Yawan `x-relay-fallback` mai yawa tare da ƙarancin `relayAlive` yana nufin sidecar
backend ba ya cikin ƙoshin lafiya, kuma ya kamata ku gyara shi ko ku sauya dabarar
relay backend zuwa `ts` (duba `RELAY_BACKEND_STRATEGY.md`).

## Bayanin API

| Hanya  | Path                                      | Body                    | Nasara                                                                                                                                           | Gazawa                                                                                                 |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `POST` | `/api/settings/proxies/[id]/repair-relay` | `{ "id": "<proxyId>" }` | `200 { repaired: true, mode: "recovered" }` idan an sake samar da shi; `200 { repaired: false, mode: "noop" }` idan plaintext ya riga ya kasance | `409 { mode: "redeploy" }` ba za a iya dawo da shi ba; `400` ba nau'in relay ba ne; `404` ba a samu ba |

List route ɗin `GET /api/settings/proxies` yana haɗa
`relayInfo: { isRelay, authMissing, repairMode }` marar secret ga kowane item domin
dashboard ya iya nuna hanyar gyaran ba tare da taɓa fallasa token ɗin ba.
