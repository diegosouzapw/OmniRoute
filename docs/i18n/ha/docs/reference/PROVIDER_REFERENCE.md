## Rukunoni

- **Kyauta** — matakin kyauta tare da maɓallin API (ana saita shi ta dashboard)
- **Ba tare da tantancewa ba** — wuraren haɗin jama'a waɗanda ba sa buƙatar maɓalli ko shiga kwata-kwata
- **OAuth** — OmniRoute ne ke kula da tsarin shiga, ba a buƙatar maɓallin API
- **Kukis na yanar gizo** — yana naɗe manhajar yanar gizon mai samarwa ta amfani da tantancewar kukis
- **Maɓallin API** — mai samarwa na biya wanda ake saitawa ta maɓallin API (ana iya samun kiredit na kyauta)
- **Na gida** — yana aiki a kan na'urar mai amfani (Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM, da sauransu)
- **Bincike** — masu samar da binciken yanar gizo
- **Sauti** — masu samar da sabis na sauti kawai (TTS/STT)
- **Wakilin sama** — masu samarwa waɗanda ke wakilta zuwa wasu masu samarwa
- **Wakilin gajimare** — wakilan shirye-shirye masu aiki na dogon lokaci (Codex Cloud, Devin, Jules)
- **Tsari** — masu samarwa na cikin OmniRoute (loopback, da sauransu)

Ƙarin alamomi: `image`, `video`, `aggregator`, `enterprise`, `embed/rerank`, `self-hosted`.

`Tool calling` (inda aka nuna): `native` — API na ainihin kiran ayyuka; `emulated` — ana kwaikwayon jerin `tools` ta cikin umarni ta amfani da `webTools.ts` (ana fassarawa da regex daga tubalan `<tool>{...}</tool>`); `none` — a halin yanzu ana watsi da `tools` ba tare da sanarwa ba. Duba #7286.

Yi amfani da dashboard a `/dashboard/providers` don kunnawa, saitawa, da gwada kowane mai samarwa.
