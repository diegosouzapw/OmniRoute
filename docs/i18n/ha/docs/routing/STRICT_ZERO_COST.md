> Ana kunna shi ne kawai idan an zaɓa, kuma a kashe yake ta tsohuwa (`settings.freeAccessPolicy !== "strict"` yana barin kowane rukunin ’yan takarar `auto/*`
> ba tare da canza ko byte ɗaya ba). Wannan sigar ta fi `hidePaidModels`
> (`open-sse/services/autoCombo/paidModelFilter.ts`, #6512) tsauri, ga masu gudanarwa da ke buƙatar cikakkiyar
> tabbaci kan KOWANE ƙarin kashe kuɗi, ba kawai waɗanda aka “rubuta cewa kyauta ne” ba.

## Dalilin samuwar wannan, da kuma dalilin da ya sa `hidePaidModels` kaɗai bai isa ba

`hidePaidModels` yana amsa tambayar “shin a halin yanzu an ware wannan samfurin a matsayin kyauta a cikin `FREE_MODEL_BUDGETS`?” —
wato bayanin kundin bayanai na wani takamaiman lokaci, wanda ake dubawa ta `isFreeModel()`/`providerHasFreeModels()`
(`src/shared/utils/freeModels.ts`). Bai ce komai ba game da haɗari biyu na zahiri:

1. Ana iya **ƙare** adadin izinin matakin kyauta na `recurring-*`/`one-time-initial` — kundin bayanai
   zai ci gaba da lissafa samfurin a matsayin kyauta, amma asusun da ke bayansa ba shi da sauran damar amfani.
2. Wuce iyakar matakin kyauta ba koyaushe yake haifar da tsayarwa kai tsaye ba. Wasu masu samarwa suna bayyana a sarari cewa ba
   za a taɓa iya haɗa hanyar biyan kuɗi ba (“ba a buƙatar katin kuɗi”); wasu ba sa faɗin haka, kuma
   kaɗan daga cikinsu suna fara cajin kuɗi ta atomatik bayan an wuce izinin kyauta.

`hidePaidModels` ba zai iya bambance waɗannan ba — ba don haka aka taɓa ƙirƙirarsa ba. STRICT_ZERO_COST yana ƙara
waɗannan bincike guda biyu daidai, ana tantance su ga kowane ɗan takara, **kafin** jeranta rukuni/mataki kuma **kafin**
aika buƙata — ba bayan an riga an aika buƙata ba.

## Rarraba ’yan takara

Ga kowane ɗan takara a cikin rukunin (`open-sse/services/autoCombo/virtualFactory.ts::buildPreparedPool`,
nan da nan bayan `filterPaidOnlyCandidates`):

1. **Babu shi kwata-kwata a cikin `FREE_MODEL_BUDGETS`** → a cire shi. Wannan ya haɗa da samfuran da ake biya da gaske da duk
   wani mai samarwa/samfuri da OmniRoute bai riga ya rarraba ba — sabbin ’yan takara suna farawa a matsayin waɗanda aka cire, ba waɗanda aka haɗa ba.
2. **`freeType: "keyless"`** → zai wuce nan take, **amma sai ga ɗan takarar da ya zo da gaske
   ta hanyar da ba ta buƙatar tantancewa ba** (`connectionId === SYNTHETIC_NOAUTH_CONNECTION_ID`,
   `open-sse/services/autoCombo/resilienceCandidateFilter.ts`). Babu bayanan shiga ga wannan
   ɗan takarar, don haka babu wata buƙata da aka yi ta kansa da za a iya cajin kuɗi — ba a buƙatar binciken lokacin aiki kuma
   ba zai yiwu ba. Mai samarwa/samfuri iri ɗaya da aka yi wa rajistar `keyless` amma aka isa gare shi ta hanyar haɗin DB
   **na gaske** (`connectionId` ainihin id na haɗi ne, ko kuma ɗan takarar yana ɗauke da
   `allowedConnectionIds`) ba ya samun wannan gajeriyar hanya — metadata na `keyless` yana bayyana
   hanyar da ba ta buƙatar tantancewa ne musamman, ba mai samarwar gaba ɗaya ba, kuma ba ya taɓa ba da izini ga asusu na gaske
   mai bayanan shiga. Irin wannan ɗan takara zai ci gaba zuwa bincike na 3 kamar kowane ɗan takara, inda ake
   cire shi sai dai idan shigarwar kundin bayanai tana kuma ɗauke da `hardStopGuaranteed: true` (ainihin shigarwar
   `keyless` ba sa taɓa yin haka — gajeriyar hanyar ce kaɗai hanyar tabbatar da amincinsu).
3. **Duk wani `freeType` dabam** (`recurring-daily`, `recurring-monthly`, `recurring-credit`,
   `recurring-uncapped`, `one-time-initial`, da duk wani nau’i na gaba da wannan module bai
   keɓance don sarrafawa ba) → zai wuce ne kawai idan **duk** waɗannan sharuɗɗa sun cika:
   - An saita `hardStopGuaranteed: true` a shigarwar kundin bayanai (`FreeModelBudget.hardStopGuaranteed`,
     `open-sse/config/freeModelCatalog.ts`) — wannan **tabbataccen bayani ne da aka zaɓa kuma aka saita da hannu** game da
     sharuddan da mai samarwar da kansa ya wallafa (misali, ikirarin “ba a buƙatar katin kuɗi” a sarari), ba a taɓa samo shi daga
     `freeType` ko daga martanin API kai tsaye ba. Wanda ba a saita ba (`undefined`) da `false` duk ana ɗaukar su a matsayin
     “ba a tabbatar ba”.
   - Akwai adaftar amfani ga mai samarwar a cikin `USAGE_FETCHER_PROVIDERS`
     (`open-sse/services/usage.ts`) — wannan rajista ce da ta riga take tallafa wa dashboard na ƙayyadadden amfani da
     `getUsageForProvider()`. Babu adafta → a cire shi har abada, sai an ƙara ɗaya.
   - `FreeAccessState` na kai tsaye da aka adana a cache don **takamaiman haɗin da ake
     tantancewa a zahiri** yana da `status: "SAFE"`, an duba shi a cikin
     `settings.autoRefreshProviderQuotaInterval` (tsoho 180s — saitin da ake da shi, ba sabon
     adadi ba), kuma yana nuna `remainingFreeAllowance` da ya fi ƙaramin tazarar kariya.
4. **`freeType: "discontinued"`** → koyaushe a cire shi.

## Tsaron haɗi (tabbatarwa ga kowane haɗi, ba ga kowane ɗan takara ba)

Ba koyaushe ake ɗaure ɗan takara a cikin tarin auto-combo da haɗi guda ɗaya ba. Ɗan takara na “ma’ana”
(`connectionId: null`) yana ɗauke da jerin izini na `allowedConnectionIds` — haɗe-haɗen
masu samarwa/asusu na ainihi guda ɗaya ko fiye, waɗanda kowannensu zai iya biyan buƙatar — sannan
asusun da za a yi amfani da shi a zahiri ana zaɓarsa daga baya, a lokacin aikawa, ta
`open-sse/services/combo/autoStrategy.ts`
(ta hanyar haɗa `allowedConnectionIds` da tsarin zaɓen haɗinsa, kusan layi na 315-331).

STRICT_ZERO_COST yana tabbatar da yanayin samun dama kyauta na **kowane haɗi a cikin wannan jerin izini
ɗaya bayan ɗaya** (`evaluateCandidateConnections()` a cikin `strictZeroCostFilter.ts`) sannan ya sake
rubuta `allowedConnectionIds` ya rage shi zuwa takamaiman ƙaramin rukunin da ya dawo da `SAFE` — ba
cikakken jerin asali ba, kuma ba memba guda ɗaya da aka zaɓa ba tare da ƙa’ida ba. A zahiri:

- Asusu A `SAFE`, asusu B `UNKNOWN`/ya ƙare/ana cajinsa → A kaɗai ne ya rage ana iya zaɓarsa.
- Duk asusu `UNKNOWN` → ana cire ɗan takarar gaba ɗaya (rukunin amintattu babu komai).
- Ɗan takara mai haɗi guda ɗaya (`connectionId` an saita shi kai tsaye, babu jerin izini) wanda ya gaza
  ana cire shi kai tsaye, ba a taɓa mayar da shi tare da `allowedConnectionIds` marar komai ba.

Saboda `autoStrategy.ts` ya riga ya tilasta `allowedConnectionIds` a matsayin jerin izini mai tsauri kafin
zaɓar haɗin da za a aika zuwa gare shi, sake rubuta shi zuwa ƙaramin rukunin da aka tabbatar yana da `SAFE`
ya isa don tabbatar da cewa haɗin da aka yi amfani da shi a zahiri lokacin aikawa koyaushe ɗaya ne da wannan
matatar da kanta ta tabbatar — ba wani asusu daban da ba a tabbatar da shi ba a kan ɗan takara guda. Duba
`tests/unit/autoCombo/strict-zero-cost-connection-safety.test.ts` don hujjar hana komawar matsalar
(lamuran ketarewa ba tare da maɓalli ba A/B/C, lamuran asusu da yawa 1-5).

`discovered automatically`: mai samarwa/samfuri da aka fitar gobe tare da metadata da ya dace (a cikin
kundin, tare da adaftar amfani, `hardStopGuaranteed: true`) zai kasance mai amfani da zarar OmniRoute ya
san da shi — babu canjin lamba, babu shigarwar jerin izini, babu abin da za a gyara a wannan module. Wanda
aka cire daga kundin shima zai ɓace ta hanya ɗaya. Duba
`tests/unit/autoCombo/strict-zero-cost-autodiscovery.test.ts` don hujjar hana komawar matsalar (ta hanyar
fixtures da za a iya allurawa, ba ta hanyar sauya kundin ainihi ba).

## Adana quota a cache (`open-sse/services/autoCombo/freeAccessQuota.ts`)

Yana sake amfani da `getUsageForProvider()` — babu wani tsarin quota na biyu. Wani cache na ɗan gajeren lokaci,
a cikin memory, mai tsawon rayuwar process yana gabansa (TTL daidai da tsohon ƙimar
`autoRefreshProviderQuotaInterval`) don kada yawan buƙatu irin na Telegram ya taɓa jawo kiran billing-API kai
tsaye ga kowane ɗan takara a kowace buƙata. Karantawa na synchronous ne: idan ba a samu a cache ba, ana mayar
da `undefined` (→ an cire, gazawa-a-rufe) sannan a fara sabuntawa a bango domin karantawa na _gaba_ — babu wani
abu a cikin hanyar gina tarin ’yan takara da ke jiran kiran network.

Ana kiran `invalidateFreeAccessState(provider, connectionId)` daga
`src/sse/services/auth.ts::markAccountUnavailable()` da zarar haɗi ya gaza saboda kowane dalili, don haka
ginin tarin na gaba nan take zai karanta rashin samu mai tsabta a cache maimakon tsohuwar shigarwar `SAFE` —
ba sai an jira TTL ya ƙare bayan amsar 402/403/quota-exhausted ba.

## Kariyar ToS (mai zaman kanta daga amincin tattalin arziki)

`excludeTosAvoid` (tsoho `false`) yana cire duk wani ɗan takara wanda hukuncin `tos` da aka tantance
(`FreeModelBudget.tos`) yake `"avoid"` — yana sake amfani da filin da takardun ɗan'uwan `hidePaidModels`
(`docs/reference/FREE_TIERS.md`) suka riga suka cika. An ware shi da gangan daga
`freeAccessPolicy`: ɗan takara na iya kasancewa `SAFE` ta fuskar tattalin arziki amma har yanzu a cire shi a nan saboda
dalilan yarjejeniya, ko kuma a bar shi idan wannan kariyar a kashe take ko da an kunna `freeAccessPolicy: "strict"`.

## Ganin abin da kariyar ke cirewa

`GET /v1/auto-combo/{channel}/candidates` yana jera kowane ɗan takara, har da waɗanda wannan kariyar
za ta hana turawa, kuma kowannensu yana ɗauke da `freeAccessExclusion` — `null` idan an
cika sharadin kariyar, in ba haka ba kuma dalilin. Jerin yana bayar da rahoto ne kawai; ba ya tilasta ƙa'ida. Kashe manufofin
yana barin filin a matsayin `null` a ko'ina kuma ba ya jawo wani kuɗi.

| `freeAccessExclusion`  | Abin da yake nufi                                                                                                         | Abin da za a yi game da shi                                                                                                                       |
| :--------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `not-in-catalog`       | Babu haɗin mai bayarwa/samfuri a cikin `FREE_MODEL_BUDGETS`.                                                              | Ƙara shigarwar da aka tantance, ko a amince cewa sabbin haɗe-haɗe suna farawa a cire — haka aka tsara tsarin.                                     |
| `regime-not-free`      | Yana cikin kundin, amma `freeType` ɗinsa ba nau'in da ke ba da damar amfani kyauta ba ne (misali, matakin da aka daina).  | Babu abin da za a gyara. Samfurin yana cin kuɗi.                                                                                                  |
| `no-hard-stop`         | Tsari ne na kyauta, amma `hardStopGuaranteed` ba `true` ba ne, don haka wuce adadin da aka ware na iya fara caji a ɓoye.  | Tabbatar da sharuɗɗan mai bayarwa sannan a sa tutar tare da madogara a cikin sharhi — kada a taɓa yin haka don faɗaɗa kundin.                     |
| `contradictory-noauth` | Ɗan takara mara tantancewa wanda shigarwarsa a kundin ba `keyless` ba ce. Ana rufe damar idan metadata ba su da daidaito. | Gyara shigarwar kundin; bayanan biyu ba su yi daidai ba.                                                                                          |
| `exhausted`            | Sabon karatu ya nuna cewa an ƙare adadin da aka ware.                                                                     | Jira sake saitawa. Wannan yana warware kansa.                                                                                                     |
| `state-unknown`        | Babu karatun ƙayyadadden adadi, ko kuma karatun ya tsufa fiye da yadda za a amince da shi.                                | Je a bincika: mai bayarwar na iya rashin adaftar amfani da aka yi wa rajista, ko kuma ɗauko bayanan ƙayyadadden adadin yana gazawa.               |
| `no-connection`        | Ɗan takarar ba ya ɗauke da wani asusu da za a bincika ko kaɗan.                                                           | Wannan ba matsalar ƙayyadadden adadi ba ce: an gina ɗan takarar ba tare da haɗi ba, don haka ba a taɓa neman komai ba. Duba yadda aka haɗa tarin. |

Biyu na ƙarshe su ne waɗanda ya dace a bambanta. Adadin da aka ƙare yana sake saitawa da kansa; karatun
da bai taɓa zuwa ba yana nufin binciken kansa ya lalace, kuma har zuwa yanzu dukansu suna kama iri ɗaya daga
waje — ɗan takarar kawai yana ɓacewa.

**Har yanzu akwai gibi guda, kuma na ganganci ne.** `excludeTosAvoid` har yanzu yana cire 'yan takara kafin a
gina jerin, don haka samfurin da aka tantance da `tos: "avoid"` ba ya bayyana ba tare da bayar da dalili ba — irin
rashin ganuwar da wannan sashe ya warware yanzu ga kariyar rashin kuɗi. Rufe wannan gibin ma yana buƙatar yanke shawarar abin da
wariyar ToS ya kamata ta bayar da rahoto, wanda tambaya ce dabam da amincin tattalin arziki; wannan shafin yana ambaton
gibin maimakon ya yi kamar babu shi.

Don gwajin kafin/bayan ba tare da layi ba, `npx tsx scripts/ad-hoc/dry-run-strict-zero-cost.ts` har yanzu yana aiki
da fitarwar 'yan takara ta wata instance mai aiki; yana karanta ainihin `connectionId` na kowane ɗan takara, don haka yana
kuma gwada hanyar amincin haɗi. Dole ne 'yan takara na `keyless` su zo da `connectionId` na no-auth na roba,
ba ainihin haɗi ba. Hanyar keyless auto da ke cikin tsarin a halin yanzu ita ce OpenCode Free; ainihin adadin 'yan takara
har yanzu ya dogara da gano samfura kai tsaye kuma ya kamata a auna shi a deployment ɗin da ake nufi maimakon
a kwafe shi daga tsohon gwaji. Ɗan takarar `recurring-*` yana wucewa ne kawai idan yana da duka adaftar
amfani da aka yi wa rajista da kuma `hardStopGuaranteed: true`; metadata marasa cikawa suna ci gaba da haifar da rufewa.

Tare da `excludeTosAvoid: true`, ana cire kowane ɗan takara da aka tantance a matsayin `tos: "avoid"`. OpenCode Free
a halin yanzu yana da wannan hukuncin, don haka kunna kariyar na iya zubar da sauran tarin keyless na wani deployment
gaba ɗaya. Wannan sakamakon musaya ne da ake tsammani daga kunna kariyar ToS, ba matsalar software ba ce: kariyar tana `false`
ta tsohuwa saboda wannan dalilin kai tsaye (duba "Kariyar ToS" a sama).

## Kunnawa

```json
PUT /api/settings
{ "freeAccessPolicy": "strict", "excludeTosAvoid": false }
```

Sabbin saitunan biyu suna amfani da tsoffin ƙimominsu na kafin wannan fasalin (`"off"` / `false`) ta tsohuwa — rashin kunna ko ɗayansu
ba ya canza kowane halin da ake da shi na sarrafa hanyar `auto/*`.
