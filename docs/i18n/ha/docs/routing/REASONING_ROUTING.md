Ka’idojin karkatar da reasoning suna faɗaɗa tsarin karkatar da model da combo da ake da shi. Idan babu wata ka’ida mai aiki da ta dace,
halayen thinking, suffix, connection-default, da provider-translation da ake da su za su ci gaba
ba tare da canji ba.

## Gudanarwa

Ana samun gudanar da ka’idoji a ƙarƙashin **Settings → Global Routing**. Editan API-key yana samar da
irin wannan UI na gudanarwa, amma an tace shi zuwa key ɗin da aka zaɓa.

Ana samar da management API ta waɗannan routes:

- `GET` da `POST` a `/api/settings/reasoning-routing-rules`
- `GET`, `PATCH`, da `DELETE` a `/api/settings/reasoning-routing-rules/[id]`
- `POST` a `/api/settings/reasoning-routing-rules/simulate`

Duk routes suna amfani da `requireManagementAuth`. Ana tabbatar da ingancin inputs ta amfani da schemas da ke cikin
`src/shared/validation/schemas/reasoningRouting.ts`. Simulator ba ya taɓa yin upstream call.

## Warware Ka’ida

Binciken farko yana zaɓar ka’ida guda ɗaya tak. Ana duba scopes a wannan tsari:

1. `apiKey`
2. `combo`
3. `model`
4. `global`

A cikin scope, `priority` mafi girma ne ke fara cin nasara, sai cikakken model match ya fi glob pattern,
sannan tsayayyen tsarin `createdAt` da `id`. Ana karanta `requestTags` ne kawai daga `metadata.tags`
kuma suna goyon bayan matching na `any` ko `all`.

Ana tantance ka’idar `connection` ne kawai idan babu wata ka’idar farko da ta yi nasara kuma an riga an
zaɓi takamaiman provider connection. Za ta iya canza effort da budget kawai.

## Effort da Budget

`sourceEffort` yana karɓar `any`, `missing`, `none`, `low`, `medium`, `high`, `xhigh`, `max`, da
`ultra`. `missing` yana nufin cewa request ɗin bai ƙunshi discrete effort ko thinking toggle
ko thinking budget ba. Saboda haka, signal mai budget kawai zai dace da `any` ne kawai.

`effortMode` yana da nau’o’i uku:

- `inherit` yana riƙe client effort tare da ci gaba da ba model ko combo damar canzawa.
- `default` yana saita `targetEffort` ne kawai idan babu explicit reasoning signal.
- `force` yana maye gurbin discrete effort da `targetEffort`.

Ba tare da dogaro da wannan ba, `budgetAction` na iya zama `preserve`, `remove`, ko `set`. `force` tare da `none` yana cire
duk effort da budget fields da aka sani. `none` tare da `set` ba ya da inganci.

Ana ƙin requests da ke nufin models da aka sani ba su dace ba kafin upstream call. Ga combo
targets, ana cire entries da ba su dace ba; idan babu wanda ya rage, request ɗin zai mayar da status `400`.
Bayanan capability da ba a sani ba suna haifar da warning kuma suna barin ka’idar tana aiki.

## Tsaro da Transports

Source da target model, ko source da target combo, suna ci gaba da kasancewa ƙarƙashin API-key
policy da ake da shi. Ka’idar reasoning ba ta taɓa faɗaɗa izinin model, combo, ko quota.

An haɗa engine ɗin cikin Chat Completions, Responses, Anthropic Messages, da internal
Codex WebSocket path. WebSocket path yana karɓar Codex target models kawai; ba za a iya
gudanar da combo targets a wurin ba. Ana adana shawarar ka’idar a cikin route trace da ake da shi ba tare da secrets ba.

## Adanawa

Migration ɗin `src/lib/db/migrations/126_reasoning_routing_rules.sql` yana ƙirƙirar
table ɗin `reasoning_routing_rules`. Ka’idoji suna yin reference zuwa API keys, combos, da provider connections da aka adana.
Deletes suna share ka’idojin da ke da alaƙa. Database access layer da ke cikin
`src/lib/db/reasoningRoutingRules.ts` yana kula da cache da za a iya invalidating don request path.

Ana haɗa ka’idoji a cikin SQLite backups, cikakken database export, da config-sync bundle.
`reconcileReasoningRulesForSync` yana disable imported rules masu missing references kuma yana bayar da rahoton waɗannan
conflicts.
