## Bayani

Injin Raba Ƙayyadadden Amfani yana rarraba ƙayyadadden amfani na mai samarwa wanda ya danganci lokaci (misali, taga ta awa 5 ta Codex, buƙatu 1500/h na Kimi) cikin adalci tsakanin maɓallan API da yawa waɗanda ke amfani da haɗin guda.

**Matsalar da yake warwarewa:** OmniRoute yana wakiltar maɓallan API da yawa zuwa asusun mai samarwa na sama ɗaya. Ba tare da tsarin rabawa ba, tarin buƙatu kwatsam daga maɓalli A zai iya cinye ƙayyadadden amfanin mai samarwa na wannan awa, ya bar maɓallan B da C a toshe har sai tagar ta sake farawa. Injin yana hana hakan ta hanyar:

1. Bibiyar yawan amfani mai motsi na kowane maɓalli bisa kowane ma'auni (%, buƙatu, token, $).
2. Aiwatar da tsarin rabon adalci mai amfani da duk ƙarfin da ake da shi: maɓalli na iya aro daga rabon da ba a amfani da shi muddin jimillar tafkin ba ta kai cikar amfani ba.
3. Tilasta sakamakon a cikin hanyar aiwatarwa mai sauri (`chatCore.ts`) kafin buƙatar ta isa ga mai aiwatarwa na sama.

## Tsari: Rabon Adalci Mai Amfani da Duk Ƙarfi

An aiwatar da shi a `src/lib/quota/fairShare.ts`.

### Yanayi

| Sharaɗi                                    | Yanayi           | Halayya                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `globalUsedPercent < saturationThreshold`  | **Mai sassauci** | Maɓalli na iya aro har zuwa iyakar duniya bayan an cire jimillar da aka cinye |
| `globalUsedPercent >= saturationThreshold` | **Mai tsauri**   | Tilasta rabon adalci na kowane ɗaya sosai                                     |

Tsoho `saturationThreshold = 0.5` (env `QUOTA_SATURATION_THRESHOLD`).

### Hukuncin kowane ma'auni

Ga kowane ma'auni mai aiki a cikin tafkin, injin yana lissafa:

```
fairShareAllowed = poolLimit × (allocationWeight / 100)
consumed        = ƙimar motsi ta yanzu ta wannan maɓalli (daga QuotaStore.peek)
remaining       = fairShareAllowed - consumed
```

Sannan:

- **`policy = hard`**: idan `consumed > fairShareAllowed` kuma yanayin mai tsauri ne → **toshe**.
- **`policy = soft`**: idan `consumed > fairShareAllowed` kuma yanayin mai tsauri ne → **sanya hukunci** (rage fifiko a cikin haɗin; ba a taɓa toshewa gaba ɗaya ba).
- **`policy = burst`**: ba da izini muddin akwai sauran sarari a jimillar amfani, ba tare da la'akari da rabon adalci ba.

### Cikakken iyaka

`capValue` + `capUnit` a kan rabon wani tsayayyen iyaka ne wanda bai dogara da yanayi ko manufa ba. Duk wani ma'auni inda `consumed >= capValue` koyaushe yana **toshe** buƙatar.

### Duba ma'aunai da yawa

Ana toshe buƙata idan **kowane ɗaya** daga cikin ma'aunan da ke cikin tafkin zai toshe ta. Ma'aunan suna zaman kansu — ƙarewar 5h% ba ta shafar ma'aunin weekly%.

### Aro

A yanayin mai sassauci, maɓallin da bai cinye duk rabonsa ba zai iya amfani da rarar rabon sauran maɓallan da ba a ware ba. Tsarin lissafin shi ne:

```
maxAllowed = globalLimit - consumedByOtherKeys
```

inda `consumedByOtherKeys = consumedTotal - consumedByThisKey`. Matsakaicin iyakar duniya (`limit` na tafkin don wannan ma'auni) koyaushe ita ce tsayayyar iyaka.

## Ma'aunin Taga Mai Motsi

An aiwatar da shi a `src/lib/quota/sqliteQuotaStore.ts` da `redisQuotaStore.ts`.

Akwai bokiti biyu ga kowane `(apiKeyId, dimensionKey)`:

- `curr`: bokiti na yanzu (`floor(nowMs / windowMs)`)
- `prev`: bokiti na baya (`curr - 1`)

Ƙimar motsi mai tasiri:

```
effectiveBucketIndex = floor(nowMs / windowMs)
bucketStartMs        = effectiveBucketIndex × windowMs
elapsed              = nowMs - bucketStartMs
weight               = 1 - elapsed / windowMs

effective = prev × weight + curr
```

**Daidaito**: kusan 99% daidai. Kuskuren bai wuce 1% na girman tagar ba a iyakar da ke tsakanin bokiti (wannan yana tattare da tsarin kimantawa na bokiti 2).

### Aiki Lokaci Guda

Direban SQLite: mutex na cikin ƙwaƙwalwa ga kowane maɓallin `(apiKeyId | dimensionKey)` yana hana karo na karanta-gyara-rubuta. Tsarin yana kama da kariyar anti-thundering-herd ta `src/sse/services/auth.ts`.

Direban Redis: rubutun Lua EVAL don ƙari na atomik — yana gudana a matsayin umarnin Redis guda ɗaya.

## Haɗawa da Pipeline

### PRE hook (`open-sse/handlers/chatCore.ts`)

Yana gudana kafin upstream executor, bayan binciken auth da policy:

```
resolveComboTargets / handleSingleModel
  → enforceQuotaShare(apiKeyId, connectionId, provider, estimatedCost)
      → getQuotaStore().peek() ga kowane dimension
      → fairShare.decideFairShare()
      → idan an toshe → mayar da 429 (buildErrorBody, Ƙa'ida Mai Tsauri #12)
      → idan an ba da izini + rage fifiko → saita quotaSoftPenalty=true a kan candidate
  → executor.execute()
```

**Ba da izini idan an samu gazawa**: idan `enforceQuotaShare` ya jefa kuskure, ana barin request ya wuce
tare da log na `pino.warn`. Wannan yana hana bug na quota-engine toshe dukkan
traffic.

### POST hook (rubuta amfani)

Bayan response mai nasara:

```
executor ya mayar da nasara
  → spendRecorder.recordConsumption(apiKeyId, connectionId, provider, actualCost)
      → getQuotaStore().consume() ga kowane dimension
      → ba da izini idan an samu gazawa: ana rubuta kurakurai a matsayin pino.warn, ba a taɓa tura su ga client ba
```

**Bayanin karkacewa**: idan `consume` ya gaza bayan response, rolling counter zai nuna ƙasa da ainihin amfani.
Siginar saturation daga provider (misali `anthropic-ratelimit-unified-5h-utilization`)
tana gyara global estimate a request na gaba.

### Combo soft penalty (`open-sse/services/combo.ts`)

Lokacin da `decision.deprioritize === true`:

```ts
if (candidate.quotaSoftPenalty) {
  score *= QUOTA_SOFT_DEPRIORITIZE_FACTOR; // tsoho 0.7
}
```

Ana amfani da penalty bayan duk sauran abubuwan scoring. Yana rage yiwuwar auto-combo
ta zaɓar key da ya kai saturation ba tare da toshe shi gaba ɗaya ba.

## Magance Matsaloli

### An saita Redis amma baya haɗuwa

Tabbatar cewa an girka `ioredis` (`npm ls ioredis`) kuma ana iya isa ga
`QUOTA_STORE_REDIS_URL`. Idan haɗin ya gaza, factory zai koma amfani da SQLite
(an rubuta shi a matakin `warn`).

### `peek` yana dawo da tsohon bayani / fail-open

Idan `peek` ya jefa kuskure, `enforceQuotaShare` yana ɗaukar sakamakon a matsayin
"ba da izini" (fail-open). Duba logs na `pino` don shigarwar `quota:enforce` da
`quota:factory` domin gano ainihin dalilin.

### Karkacewar ƙididdigar amfani

Idan ainihin amfanin mai samarwa ya bambanta da ƙididdigun, hakan abin tsammani
ne — taga mai zamewa ta rukuni 2 tana da kusan kuskuren 1% a iyakokin taga, kuma
`consume` yana gudana bayan amsa ba tare da jira ba. Alamar cikewa
(`saturationSignals.ts`) tana karanta ainihin matakin amfani na mai samarwa tare
da TTL na daƙiƙa 30 sannan ta daidaita `globalUsedPercent` yadda ya dace.

### Rukuni yana nuna "babu bayanai" don saurin amfani

`computeBurnRate` yana buƙatar aƙalla samfuran tarihi 2. Sababbin rukunoni da ba su
da kiraye-kirayen `consume` na baya za su nuna `tokensPerSecond: 0` da
`timeToExhaustionMs: null`.

## Taƙaitaccen Tsarin DB

An ƙara tebura uku ta migrations `078`, `079` da `085`:

- `quota_pools` + `quota_allocations` — ma'anar pools da allocations na kowane key.
- `quota_consumption` — counters na buckets 2 masu juyawa ga kowane `(apiKeyId, dimensionKey)`.
- `provider_plans` — sauye-sauyen provider plan na hannu (dimensions JSON ga kowane connectionId).

An ƙara dukkan teburan ta hanyar migrations na `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS` masu idempotent.
