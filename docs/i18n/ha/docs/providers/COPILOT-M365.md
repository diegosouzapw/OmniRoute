`copilot-m365-web` (wanda kuma ake kira `m365copilot`) yana aika buƙatun taɗi na tsarin OpenAI ta hanyar zaman burauzar Microsoft 365 Copilot BizChat da aka tabbatar da shi a
`m365.cloud.microsoft/chat`. Wannan haɗin kai ne marar hukuma.

> **Ba ka taɓa amfani da masu samar da Web Cookie ba?**
>
> Karanta **`docs/getting-started/WEB-COOKIE-GUIDE.md`** domin sanin tsarin saiti na gaba ɗaya, jagorar tabbatar da izini, iyakoki, da warware matsaloli kafin bin wannan jagorar da ta keɓanta ga wannan mai samarwa.
>
> **Muhimmi:** Bayanan shaidar wannan mai samarwa **ba cookie ba ne**. Kada ka kwafi
> cookies daga `m365.cloud.microsoft`. Bayanan shaidar su ne ma'aunin tambayar URL na WebSocket
> da wani sashe na hanya, kuma ana karanta dukansu daga shafin Network na DevTools.

## Abubuwan da ake buƙata

- Asusun Microsoft 365 mai damar shiga BizChat a `m365.cloud.microsoft/chat`
- Burauza mai tushen Chromium (Chrome, Edge) domin binciken WebSocket na DevTools
- Lasisin M365 Copilot (ana haɗa shi da tsare-tsaren Microsoft 365 Business/Enterprise da yawa)

## Samun bayanan shaida (hanyar WebSocket)

1. Shiga a `https://m365.cloud.microsoft/chat` sannan ka fara tattaunawa.
2. Buɗe **DevTools → Network**.
3. Tace ta **WS** (WebSocket).
4. Danna haɗin WebSocket na Chathub. Nemo URL da ke farawa da
   `wss://substrate.office.com/m365Copilot/Chathub/`.
5. Daga **Request URL** ɗinsa, kwafi:
   - Ƙimar ma'aunin tambaya na `access_token` (JWT mai ɗan gajeren wa'adin aiki).
   - Sashen hanyar Chathub da ke bayan `/Chathub/` (misali `<oid>@<tenant>`).

Cikakken URL yana da wannan tsari:

```
wss://substrate.office.com/m365Copilot/Chathub/<oid>@<tenant>?...&access_token=<jwt>
```

6. A cikin OmniRoute, je zuwa **Providers → Add Provider**, zaɓi **Microsoft 365 Copilot
   (BizChat)**, sannan ka liƙa bayanan shaidar a wannan tsari:

```
access_token=<jwt>; chathubPath=<oid>@<tenant>
```

7. Danna **Test Connection** sannan **Save**.

## Samun bayanan shaida (shigo da HAR)

Maimakon kwafe ƙimomi da hannu daga URL na WebSocket, za ka iya fitar da fayil ɗin HAR
na DevTools:

1. Buɗe **DevTools → Network**.
2. Fara ko ci gaba da tattaunawar BizChat (wannan yana kunna haɗin WebSocket).
3. Danna-dama a cikin ɓangaren Network sannan ka zaɓi **Save all as HAR with content**.
4. A cikin OmniRoute, yi amfani da maɓallin **Import .har file** a akwatin tattaunawar haɗin mai samarwa.

OmniRoute yana bincika HAR domin nemo URL na WebSocket na Chathub, yana ciro
`access_token` da `chathubPath`, sannan yana cika filayen bayanan shaida ta atomatik. Shigo da
HAR yana fassara lokacin ƙarewar JWT kuma yana nuna ragowar tsawon rayuwar token.

> **Kada ka taɓa commit na ainihin HAR da aka fitar, access token, ko refresh token.** Dole ne ƙimomin gwaji da
> takardu su kasance masu riƙe wuri koyaushe.

## Tsawon rayuwar token da sabuntawa

`access_token` yana da ɗan gajeren wa'adin aiki (yawanci **~minti 75**). Bayan ya ƙare, mai samarwar
zai bayar da rahoton kuskuren haɗi kuma dole ne ka sake samun bayanan shaidar.

### Sabuntawa ta atomatik da `refreshToken`

Domin kauce wa sake samun bayanan da hannu, za ka iya adana Microsoft refresh token a cikin
**Advanced Settings → providerSpecificData** na haɗin:

| Fili           | Bayani                                                                |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `refreshToken` | Microsoft OAuth refresh token na iyakar `substrate.office.com/sydney` |
| `tier`         | `individual` (tsoho), `edu`, ko `enterprise`                          |

Samo refresh token daga tsarin Microsoft device-code ko authorization-code
wanda aka iyakance zuwa `substrate.office.com/sydney`. Idan an saita `refreshToken`, OmniRoute
yana sabunta `access_token` kafin kowace buƙata, don haka ba sai ka
sake samun sa bayan duk lokacin ƙarewar ~minti 75 ba.

Idan ba a saita `refreshToken` ba, dole ne ka sake samun bayanan shaidar WebSocket da hannu
bayan duk lokacin ƙarewa.

### Zaɓin mataki

| Mataki       | Laƙabi     | Fuska                       |
| ------------ | ---------- | --------------------------- |
| `individual` | _(tsoho)_  | M365 Copilot na masu amfani |
| `edu`        | `included` | M365 Copilot na ilimi       |
| `enterprise` | `work`     | M365 Copilot don aiki       |

Saita matakin a **Advanced Settings → providerSpecificData → tier**. Matakin yana tasiri
kan hanyar zirga-zirgar BizChat da damar amfani da model.

## Tsarin tsaro

- **Haɗin da ba na hukuma ba.** Wannan mai samarwa yana amfani da injiniyan-baya don fahimtar ƙa'idar BizChat WebSocket. Yana iya daina aiki ba tare da sanarwa ba idan Microsoft ya sauya endpoint ɗin.
- Alamar `subscriptionRisk: true` tana nuna cewa haɗin yana dogara da endpoint da ba a rubuta takardunsa ba. Ana iya sanya ƙuntatawa ga asusun, amma ba a tabbatar da hakan ba.
- Access token ɗin JWT ne mai ɗan gajeren lokacin aiki. Ɗauke shi kamar kalmar sirri: kada a commit, a log, ko a raba shi.
- Refresh token ɗin yana ba da damar shiga BizChat na dogon lokaci. Ajiye shi da irin kulawar da ake bai wa API key.

## Magance matsaloli

### Tantancewa tana gaza nan take

- Tabbatar cewa ka kwafi daga **WebSocket URL**, ba daga buƙatar XHR/Fetch ba. Bayanan shaidar suna cikin query parameter na `access_token` na haɗin Chathub WebSocket — ba cikin header na `Authorization: Bearer` ba.
- Tabbatar cewa `chathubPath` ya ƙunshi cikakken ɓangaren `<oid>@<tenant>` daga hanyar URL.

### Token yana ƙarewa bayan ~75 minutes

Wannan abin tsammani ne. Idan babu `refreshToken`, access token ɗin yana da ɗan gajeren lokacin aiki. Yi ɗaya daga cikin waɗannan:

- Sake samo bayanan shaidar daga DevTools, ko
- Saita `refreshToken` a Advanced Settings domin sabuntawa ta atomatik.

### Test Connection ya yi nasara amma chat yana mayar da 401

Mai yiwuwa token ɗin ya ƙare tsakanin lokacin tabbatarwa da buƙata ta farko. Sake samo sabbin bayanan shaidar.

### Copilot ba ya bayyana a jerin masu samarwa

Tabbatar cewa an kunna `copilot-m365-web` a **Dashboard → Providers**. Ana iya ɓoye mai samarwar ta tsohuwa a wasu matakai.
