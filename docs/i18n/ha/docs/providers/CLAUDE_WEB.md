## `claude-web`

`claude-web` yana aika buƙatun tattaunawa na tsarin OpenAI ta hanyar zaman burauzan `claude.ai` da aka tabbatar da sahihancinsa. Mai aiwatarwa yana daidaita cookie ɗin da aka bayar, yana tantance ƙungiya guda ɗaya da aka tabbatar da sahihancinta, yana shirya yanayin tattaunawa, yana zaɓar jigilar kai tsaye ko ta burauza, sannan yana fassara martanin SSE na sama da tsauraran ƙa'idoji. Tsarin gudanarwar yana cikin `open-sse/executors/claude-web.ts:320`.

> **Sabon mai amfani da masu samar da Web Cookie ne?**
>
> Karanta **`docs/getting-started/WEB-COOKIE-GUIDE.md`** domin tsarin saitawa na gaba ɗaya, jagorar tabbatar da sahihanci, iyakoki, da magance matsaloli kafin bin wannan jagorar da ta keɓanta ga wannan mai samarwa.

### Kundin samfura

A halin yanzu, rajistar mai samarwa tana bayyana daidai waɗannan ID na samfura guda bakwai da ba sa canzawa
(`open-sse/config/providers/registry/claude/web/index.ts:11`):

| ID na samfuri               | Sunan da ake nunawa            |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| `claude-fable-5`            | Claude Fable 5 (yanar gizo)    |
| `claude-opus-4-8`           | Claude Opus 4.8 (yanar gizo)   |
| `claude-sonnet-5`           | Claude Sonnet 5 (yanar gizo)   |
| `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001` | Claude Haiku 4.5 (yanar gizo)  |
| `claude-opus-4-7`           | Claude Opus 4.7 (yanar gizo)   |
| `claude-opus-4-6`           | Claude Opus 4.6 (yanar gizo)   |
| `claude-sonnet-4-6`         | Claude Sonnet 4.6 (yanar gizo) |

Ba a aiwatar da gano samfura masu canzawa ga wannan mai samarwa ba. Jerin da ke sama shi ne kundin lokacin aiki.

### Bayanan shaida da tantance ƙungiya

Bayar da cikakken taken Cookie na `claude.ai` ko kuma ƙimar zaman kawai. Ana daidaita ƙimomin zaman kawai zuwa `sessionKey`; ana kiyaye sauran cookies idan an bayar da su. Mai aiwatarwa yana karɓar cookie ta hanyar `cookie` ko `apiKey` kuma yana karanta ƙimomin `deviceId` da `orgId` na zaɓi daga bayanan haɗin
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web.ts:72`).

Idan babu `orgId`, mai aiwatarwa yana kiran `GET https://claude.ai/api/organizations` kuma yana amfani da ƙungiya ta farko da zaman Claude Web da aka tabbatar da sahihancinsa ya dawo da ita
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web.ts:141`). Yana dakatar da aiki idan ba a dawo da wata ingantacciyar ƙungiya ba, yana bayar da rahoton ƙin amincewa da izinin zaman a matsayin 401, kuma yana bambance ƙalubalen Cloudflare da gazawar tabbatar da sahihanci.

### Ayyukan tattaunawa

Abun `claude_web` na zaɓi da ke matakin sama yana da tsauraran ƙa'idoji. Ana ƙin filayen da ba a sani ba. An ayyana filayen da yake karɓa a cikin `open-sse/executors/claude-web/session.ts:50`:

| Fili                  | Ma'ana                                                                          |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `operation`           | `completion` ne ta tsohuwa; yi amfani da `retry` domin sake gwada zagaye        |
| `conversation_id`     | UUID bayyananne na tattaunawar da take akwai                                    |
| `parent_message_uuid` | UUID bayyananne na saƙon mataimaki na iyaye                                     |
| `timezone`            | Ingantaccen sunan yankin lokaci na IANA                                         |
| `locale`              | Yankin harshe mai ingantaccen tsari                                             |
| `tool_states`         | Jerin yanayin kayan aiki na asusu na zaɓi, wanda aka iyakance zuwa shigarwa 128 |

Buƙatun da aka shirya suna amfani da ɗaya daga cikin wuraren ƙarshen sama guda biyu
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web.ts:203`):

- Sabon zagaye ko zagayen ci gaba yana aikawa zuwa
  `POST https://claude.ai/api/organizations/{orgId}/chat_conversations/{conversationId}/completion`.
- Sake gwadawa yana aikawa zuwa
  `POST https://claude.ai/api/organizations/{orgId}/chat_conversations/{conversationId}/retry_completion`.

Sabon zagaye yana ƙunshe da `create_conversation_params`. Zagayen ci gaba da aka adana a cache ko aka haɗa bayyananne yana ƙunshe da `parent_message_uuid` kuma baya ƙunsar `create_conversation_params`. Sake gwadawa yana buƙatar duka yanayin tattaunawa da na saƙon iyaye kuma baya aika prompt
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web/session.ts:254`). Sabbin tattaunawa suna buɗe UI da aka tabbatar da sahihancinsa a `/new`; zagayen ci gaba da aka adana a cache ko aka haɗa bayyananne suna buɗe ainihin shafin tattaunawar
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web/session.ts:324`).

Yanayin tattaunawa cache ne na cikin ƙwaƙwalwa wanda aka yi masa mabuɗi da iyakar asusu ta SHA-256 da kuma rubutaccen tarihin mai kira na asali. Shigarwar suna ƙarewa bayan mintuna 30 kuma an iyakance cache zuwa shigarwa 5,000
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web/session.ts:12`). Ana tabbatar da yanayi ne kawai bayan mai fassara kwarara mai tsauraran ƙa'idoji ya lura da `message_stop`; sake fara tsari yana zubar da shi. Idan ba a samu a cache ba, ana jera buƙata mai saƙonni da yawa zuwa prompt guda ɗaya na farfadowa maimakon watsi da saƙonnin baya a ɓoye.

Yankin harshe da yankin lokaci suna amfani da wannan fifiko: ƙimar `claude_web` ta buƙata, ƙimar haɗi, ƙimar lokacin aiki, sannan `en-US` ga yankin harshe ko `UTC` ga yankin lokaci
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web/session.ts:218`).

### Kayan aiki da bayanan buƙata

Buƙatun kai tsaye suna sauya kayan aikin aikin OpenAI masu ingantaccen tsari kawai waɗanda mai kira ya bayar. Babu ƙirƙirar jerin kayan aiki na tsohuwa da ba ya canzawa
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web/payload.ts:102`).

A maimakon haka, buƙatun burauza suna kama buƙatar UI da aka tabbatar da sahihancinta kuma suna riƙe kayan aikin asusunta, yanayin kayan aiki, da salon da aka keɓance. Filayen tattaunawa, samfuri, tunani, prompt, da UUID na saƙo da aka shirya har yanzu suna rinjayar buƙatar da aka kama
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web/browserTransport.ts:175`). Ana iyakance samfuran burauza ta hash na asusu, ƙungiya, cookie, yankin harshe, da yankin lokaci kuma suna ƙarewa bayan mintuna 30
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web/browserTransport.ts:11`,
`open-sse/executors/claude-web/browserTransport.ts:158`). Idan buƙatar kai tsaye ba ta da kayan aikin mai kira, za ta iya sake amfani da wannan samfurin da aka iyakance; kayan aikin da mai kira ya bayar bayyananne suna da fifiko
(`open-sse/executors/claude-web/browserTransport.ts:214`).

### Zaɓin jigilar bayanai

Hanyar tsohuwa ita ce `sendClaudeWebDirect()`, wadda take kiran `tlsFetchClaude()` tare da bayanin martabar Chrome 146 da aka saita da kuma cookie ɗin da aka bayar (`open-sse/services/claudeTlsClient.ts:23`). Ba ta ƙaddamar da mai warwarewa ko ƙirƙirar madadin cookie.

Saita `WEB_COOKIE_USE_BROWSER` zuwa `1`, `true`, ko `on` domin sanya adaftan burauza mai iyaka ga asusun ya zama hanyar sufuri ta farko. Saita `OMNIROUTE_BROWSER_POOL` zuwa ɗaya daga cikin waɗannan ƙimomin domin bai wa ƙalubalen Cloudflare 403 da aka gane damar komawa daga sufuri kai tsaye zuwa adaftan burauza (`open-sse/executors/claude-web.ts:195`). Sauran gazawar HTTP ba sa jawo wannan komawar.

Adaftan burauza yana adana cookies a cikin pooled Playwright context ɗaya, yana amfani da scoped hashed key da aka bayyana a sama, sannan yana aika kammalawar daga wannan context ɗin (`open-sse/executors/claude-web/browserTransport.ts:444`). Ba ya taɓa fitar da cookie da burauza ta warware zuwa direct TLS client. Sake-gwajen burauza suna buƙatar UI template wanda bai ƙare ba kuma aka ɗaura shi da ainihin Playwright context ɗin (`open-sse/executors/claude-web/browserTransport.ts:467`). Ana karanta amsoshin burauza a hankali mataki-mataki a cikin shafin da aka tabbatar da sahihancinsa, suna mutunta soke buƙata, kuma suna soke upstream body da zarar ya wuce 16 MiB (`open-sse/executors/claude-web/browserTransport.ts:259`).

Executor yana mayar da redacted audit projection zuwa shared request logger: ana cire organization, conversation da message UUIDs, prompt text, tool definitions, cookies, da device identifiers (`open-sse/executors/claude-web.ts:237`,
`open-sse/executors/claude-web.ts:252`). Haka kuma, kurakuran sufuri suna mayar da kuskuren haɗi na gama-gari maimakon saƙon da aka jefa.

### Halayen SSE

`createClaudeWebResponse()` yana sarrafa tsarin LF ko CRLF da filayen `data:` masu layuka da yawa. Yana daidaita text deltas zuwa `content`, thinking deltas zuwa `reasoning_content`, da sanannun metadata events zuwa faɗaɗawar amsar `claude_web`. Ana tace kowane metadata event ta jerin filayen da aka amince da su na kansa (`open-sse/executors/claude-web/stream.ts:37`). Ana kuma mayar da metadata na conversation, parent-message, assistant-message, da operation a cikin headers na `X-OmniRoute-Claude-Web-*` (`open-sse/executors/claude-web/stream.ts:364`).

Parser yana dakatar da aiki cikin aminci idan ya gamu da JSON mara inganci, upstream `error` events, nau'ikan event da ba a sani ba, tsari mara inganci, rashin daidaituwar content-block, ko EOF kafin `message_stop`. Streaming output yana fitar da finish chunk guda ɗaya da `[DONE]` guda ɗaya; buffered output yana amfani da parser ɗin iri ɗaya. Parser yana ɗaukar `message_stop` a matsayin ƙarshen aiki nan take, yana soke bayanan upstream da suka biyo baya, sannan yana isar da sokewar downstream zuwa upstream reader (`open-sse/executors/claude-web/stream.ts:461`,
`open-sse/executors/claude-web/stream.ts:563`). An iyakance layukan SSE da ba a rufe ba da events da aka tara zuwa 1 MiB (`open-sse/executors/claude-web/stream.ts:17`,
`open-sse/executors/claude-web/stream.ts:62`).

### Saitawa

1. Fara OmniRoute da `npm run dev` ko built installation.
2. Buɗe Dashboard → Providers → Add Provider.
3. Zaɓi rukunin Web Cookie da Claude Web.
4. Liƙa cikakken Cookie header da aka kwafa daga authenticated `claude.ai` request.
