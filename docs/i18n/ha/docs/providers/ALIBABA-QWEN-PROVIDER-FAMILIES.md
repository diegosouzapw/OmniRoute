Wannan takarda tana rubuta shawarar aiwatarwa don
[Issue #7854](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/issues/7854). Ana wakiltar samfuran da ake samarwa ga jama'a
a matsayin iyalan masu samarwa guda huɗu. Yanki bayanan haɗi ne, ba wani samfuri na daban ba.

## Shawara

| Iyalin mai samarwa                                  | OmniRoute ID            | Yankin duniya | Yankin China |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------- | ------------ |
| Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (biya gwargwadon amfani) | `alibaba`               | Singapore     | Beijing      |
| Alibaba Cloud Token Plan                            | `bailian-coding-plan`   | Singapore     | Beijing      |
| Qwen Cloud (biya gwargwadon amfani)                 | `qwen-cloud`            | Duniya        | Beijing      |
| Qwen Cloud Token Plan                               | `qwen-cloud-token-plan` | Singapore     | Beijing      |

ID ɗin `alibaba-cn` da yake akwai yana ci gaba da kasancewa sunan madadin dacewar lokacin aiki don haɗayen da aka adana, hanyoyin
samfura, haɗaɗɗun tsare-tsare, da tarihin amfani. An haɗa katinsa na dashboard cikin `alibaba`; ba a buƙatar
sake rubuta rumbun bayanai.

Qwen Cloud na biyan gwargwadon amfani da Alibaba Cloud Model Studio a halin yanzu suna amfani da rundunonin lokacin aiki masu dacewa da DashScope
iri ɗaya, amma sun ci gaba da kasancewa keɓaɓɓun shaidun masu samarwa saboda masu amfani suna samun maɓallai kuma suna sarrafa
asusunansu ta hanyoyin samfura daban-daban. Samfuran Alibaba da Qwen Cloud Token Plan suna amfani da
iyalan endpoint daban-daban, don haka dukkan samfuran guda huɗu suna ci gaba da kasancewa ID na masu samarwa daban-daban.

## Jadawalin endpoint

| Iyalin mai samarwa      | `global-sg`                                                              | `china-beijing`                                                      | Tsarin bayanan sadarwa |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| `alibaba`               | `https://dashscope-intl.aliyuncs.com/compatible-mode/v1`                 | `https://dashscope.aliyuncs.com/compatible-mode/v1`                  | OpenAI                 |
| `bailian-coding-plan`   | `https://token-plan.ap-southeast-1.maas.aliyuncs.com/apps/anthropic/v1`  | `https://token-plan.cn-beijing.maas.aliyuncs.com/apps/anthropic/v1`  | Anthropic              |
| `qwen-cloud`            | `https://dashscope-intl.aliyuncs.com/compatible-mode/v1`                 | `https://dashscope.aliyuncs.com/compatible-mode/v1`                  | OpenAI                 |
| `qwen-cloud-token-plan` | `https://token-plan.ap-southeast-1.maas.aliyuncs.com/compatible-mode/v1` | `https://token-plan.cn-beijing.maas.aliyuncs.com/compatible-mode/v1` | OpenAI                 |

Dole ne maɓallin API da endpoint su kasance na samfuri da yanki iri ɗaya. Zaɓin yanki yana canza
endpoint da aka riga aka saita wanda tabbatarwa da buƙatu na yau da kullum ke amfani da shi.

## Yarjejeniyar haɗi

Sabbin haɗi suna adana tsayayyen maɓallin yanki a cikin `providerSpecificData`:

```json
{
  "region": "global-sg"
}
```

Ƙimomin da aka yarda da su su ne `global-sg` da `china-beijing`. Ana samar da endpoint daga wuri guda bisa
ID na mai samarwa da yanki. `providerSpecificData.baseUrl` wanda ba na saiti da aka riga aka tanada ba yana ci gaba da kasancewa takamaiman
sauyin da mai gudanarwa ya yi kuma yana da fifiko, yayin da tsoffin URL na saiti da aka adana ake mayar da su zuwa
jadawalin yanki.

Wannan yana sa tsarin haɗin ya kasance mai sauƙin faɗaɗawa: za a iya ƙara wani yanki zuwa mai warwarewa ba tare da
ƙirƙirar wani katin mai samarwa ko canza ID na masu samarwa da aka adana ba.

## Daidaituwa da ƙaddamarwa

1. Ƙara mai warware endpoint na yanki kuma a riƙe tsoffin saitunan duniya na yanzu.
2. Yi amfani da gano DashScope `/models` na yanki kai tsaye don `alibaba`, ID ɗin daidaituwarsa na `alibaba-cn`, da `qwen-cloud`. Da farko, amsoshinsu suna wucewa ta matatar samar da rubutu guda ɗaya, saboda endpoint na gama-gari yana kuma dawo da samfuran hoto, bidiyo, sauti, embedding, da sauran samfuran da ba na taɗi ba. Sannan `qwen-cloud` yana amfani da allowlist da ake kula da ita wadda aka bayyana a rajistar mai samar da shi. Alibaba Model Studio da Qwen Cloud suna riƙe da kundin samfura daban-daban da aka zaɓa a hankali, duk da cewa a halin yanzu suna amfani da runtime hosts iri ɗaya.
3. Yi rajistar `qwen-cloud-token-plan` tare da allowlist na samfuran rubutu na Token Plan na yanzu.
4. Ƙara mai zaɓen yanki mai nau'i zuwa akwatunan tattaunawar ƙara/gyara haɗi.
5. Ɓoye katin `alibaba-cn` mai maimaituwa kuma haɗa haɗin da yake da su a shafin mai samar da `alibaba`.
6. Ci gaba da samar da damar sauya endpoint na al'ada don turawa na ƙwararru da waɗanda suka keɓanta ga workspace.

Samfuran kafofin watsa labarai na Token Plan suna ci gaba da kasancewa a wajen kundin taɗi saboda suna amfani da keɓaɓɓun endpoints.
An yi rajistar `wan2.7-image` da `wan2.7-image-pro` a cikin `open-sse/config/imageRegistry.ts`;
an yi rajistar `happyhorse-1.1-i2v`, `happyhorse-1.1-t2v`, da `happyhorse-1.1-r2v` a cikin
`open-sse/config/videoRegistry.ts`. Dukkan rajistocin biyu suna sake amfani da shaidar haɗin
`qwen-cloud-token-plan` da bayanan shaidarta na musamman ga yanki.

Qwen Cloud na yau da kullum yana da kundin hotuna da bidiyo na biya gwargwadon amfani daban a ƙarƙashin shaidar `qwen-cloud`.
Samfuran hotunansa su ne `wan2.7-image-pro`, `wan2.7-image`, `qwen-image-3.0-pro`,
`qwen-image-2.0-pro-2026-06-22`, `qwen-image-2.0-2026-03-03`, da `z-image-turbo`. Samfuran
bidiyonsa su ne `happyhorse-1.1-i2v`, `happyhorse-1.1-t2v`, `happyhorse-1.1-r2v`,
`happyhorse-1.0-video-edit`, `wan2.7-t2v`, `wan2.7-i2v`, `wan2.7-r2v-2026-06-12`, da
`wan2.7-videoedit`. Waɗannan samfuran suna sake amfani ne kawai da haɗin `qwen-cloud` na yau da kullum da endpoint na kafofin watsa labarai na DashScope na yanki. Rajistocin Token Plan da na biya gwargwadon amfani ba sa gadon ko haɗa jerin samfuran juna.

Shaidar `bailian-coding-plan` ita ma tana da nata allowlists na kafofin watsa labarai:
`wan2.7-image`, `wan2.7-image-pro`, `qwen-image-2.0`, da `qwen-image-2.0-pro` don samar da
hotuna, tare da
`happyhorse-1.1-i2v`, `happyhorse-1.1-t2v`, da `happyhorse-1.1-r2v` don samar da
bidiyo. Tana amfani ne kawai da haɗin Bailian Coding Plan da endpoint na Coding Plan na yanki.
Rajistocin hotuna da bidiyonta ba sa gado daga ko haɗuwa da kowane mai samar da Qwen Cloud.

Shaidar `alibaba` ta yau da kullum tana da allowlists na hotuna da bidiyo daban. Samfuran hotunanta su ne
`qwen-image-3.0-pro`, `qwen-image-2.0-pro-2026-06-22`, `qwen-image-2.0`,
`z-image-turbo`, da `wan2.6-t2i`. Ƙarin samfuran bidiyonta su ne `happyhorse-1.1-i2v`,
`happyhorse-1.1-t2v`, `happyhorse-1.1-r2v`, `happyhorse-1.0-video-edit`,
`wan2.7-i2v-2026-04-25`, `wan2.6-i2v-flash`, `wan2.7-t2v-2026-06-12`,
`wan2.7-r2v-2026-06-12`, da `wan2.7-videoedit`. Waɗannan samfuran suna amfani ne kawai da haɗin Alibaba Model Studio
da endpoint na kafofin watsa labarai na yankin da aka zaɓa; ba a ƙara su zuwa kowace rajistar Coding Plan ko Qwen
Cloud ba.

Kundin taɗi na Qwen Cloud Token Plan yana bin allowlist na rubutu mai daidaituwar rubutu kai tsaye na tsarin Individual:
`qwen3.8-max-preview`, `qwen3.7-max`, `qwen3.7-plus`, `qwen3.6-flash`, `glm-5.2`, da
`deepseek-v4-pro`. Shigarwar Wan da HappyHorse suna cikin API na samar da hotuna/bidiyo daban.

Alibaba Token Plan yana samar da waɗannan ID guda shida na samfuran samar da rubutu na yanzu ta endpoint ɗinsa mai dacewa da
Anthropic. Zaɓin Singapore ɗinsa yana adana maɓallin yanki `global-sg` da yake akwai;
lakabin da mai amfani ke gani ne kawai ya bambanta da iyalan masu samarwa da ke kiran yankin Global.

Kundin taɗi na Qwen Cloud na yau da kullum da gangan ya fi cikakkiyar amsar rubutu kai tsaye ƙanƙanta. Allowlist ɗinsa
da ake kula da ita tana cikin `open-sse/config/providers/registry/qwen-cloud/index.ts`; gano kai tsaye
yana dawo da ID ne kawai waɗanda suke cikin duka amsar upstream da jerin rajistar.

Alibaba Model Studio yana bin wannan dokar haɗuwar kai tsaye iri ɗaya tare da allowlist ɗinsa mai zaman kanta
da ake kula da ita a `open-sse/config/providers/registry/alibaba/index.ts`. Mai samar da daidaituwa na `alibaba-cn`
yana sake amfani da wannan kundin yayin warware gano ta endpoint na Beijing.
