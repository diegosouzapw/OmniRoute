`chatgpt-web-codex` (wanda kuma ake kira `cgpt-codex`) yana haɗa zagayen Codex Responses ta hanyar zaman burauzar ChatGPT da aka tabbatar da sahihancinsa. Ya kasance mai zaman kansa daga mai bayarwa na gama-gari na `chatgpt-web` da aka daina amfani da shi, kuma yana amfani da aiwatarwa mai sanarwar MIT da ke ƙarƙashin `open-sse/vendor/codex-chatgpt-web/`, wanda aka sabunta zuwa upstream v4.0.7 commit `b59d7dc51b84fb1f465ff1d00f5207f3b2b4a494`.

## Daina amfani da mai bayarwa na gama-gari

Tsoffin ID na mai bayarwa `chatgpt-web` da `cgpt-web` ba sa cikin kayan da ake rarrabawa saboda ba a iya tabbatar da asalin aiwatarwar pre-key/proof-of-work ɗinsu ba. Buƙatun kai tsaye zuwa kowane ɗayan ID ɗin, ciki har da ID na samfuri masu fara da slash da laƙabban da aka adana, suna ƙarewa cikin tsaro da HTTP `410` da lambar **PROVIDER_RETIRED** kafin a aika kowace buƙata zuwa upstream.

Migration `168_retire_chatgpt_web.sql` yana sanya alamar tombstone ga haɗin masu bayarwa da suka dace, sannan yana soke hayar zaman su mai aiki. Yana adana tarihin haɗi da jerin izinin API-key; ba ya ƙara damar madadin zuwa jerin izini. Wannan ritayar ba ta shafi mai bayarwar Codex da haɗinsa ba.

## Abubuwan da ake buƙata tun da farko

- cikakken Cookie header daga zaman ChatGPT da aka shiga;
- Chrome ko Chromium tare da zaman mai zane ko Xvfb display don shigarwar npm, systemd, da PM2;
- idan ana amfani da Docker `web` profile, sabis na Chromium na ciki daga `docker-compose.yml`;
- OpenAI `tunnel-client` v0.0.13 da ChatGPT custom connector don kayan aikin Codex na cikin gida.

Ana buƙatar tunnel ne kawai don zagayen kayan aiki. Kowace hanya da aka jera, ciki har da `pro`, za ta iya amfani da irin wannan damar kayan aiki na cikin gida da aka ɗaure da zagaye idan an saita tunnel da connector.

## Saitin dashboard

1. Buɗe mai bayarwar **ChatGPT Web (Codex)** sannan ka ƙara haɗi.
2. Liƙa cikakken ChatGPT Cookie header, tunnel ID, runtime key, da sunan custom connector. Sabbin saituna masu damar kayan aiki dole ne su yi amfani da sabon connector da aka ƙirƙira mai suna daidai `OmniRoute Codex v2`, tare da saita Authentication zuwa None da Permissions zuwa Allow all actions.
3. Gudanar da binciken haɗi. OmniRoute yana buɗe Temporary Chat mai goyon bayan burauza kuma yana gano ko Sol da Pro suna samuwa ga asusun.
4. Adana haɗin. OmniRoute yana maye gurbin cookie da aka liƙa da ingantaccen Playwright storage state, sannan yana adana shi tare da runtime key ta hanyar rufaffen tsarin sarrafa bayanan shaidar shiga.

Ba a riƙe ainihin cookie bayan an yi nasarar adanawa. Idan zaman ya ƙare, buɗe haɗin, liƙa sabon cikakken Cookie header, sannan ka sake gudanar da binciken. Matsayin doctor a cikin edit dialog yana bayar da rahoto dabam-dabam kan burauza, storage state, shiga, Temporary Chat, tunnel, connector, da zagayen kayan aiki na turawa da dawowa.

> Kada a taɓa commit na ainihin cookie, runtime key, storage state, ko capability token. Dole ne ƙimomin gwaji da
> takardu su kasance placeholders koyaushe.

## Samfura da combos

Tabbatattun hanyoyin samfura su ne:

- `chatgpt-web-codex/luna` — GPT-5.6 Luna, ƙaramin ƙoƙarin tunani
- `chatgpt-web-codex/think` — GPT-5.6 Luna, matsakaicin ƙoƙarin tunani
- `chatgpt-web-codex/instant`
- `chatgpt-web-codex/medium`
- `chatgpt-web-codex/high`
- `chatgpt-web-codex/extra-high`
- `chatgpt-web-codex/pro`

Ƙara ɗaya daga cikinsu zuwa combo kamar kowane samfuri. Manhajar Codex tana aika sunan combo a matsayin `model` zuwa endpoint na Responses na yau da kullum, `/v1/responses`; babu wani endpoint na Codex na daban ko maɓallin sauya yanayi.

Asusun Free/Go suna samar da hanyoyin Luna. Asusun da ke da damar Sol suna samar da Instant har zuwa High, sannan asusun da ke da damar Pro suna kuma samar da Extra High da Pro. Kowace hanya tana da tabbataccen backend model da reasoning effort; explicit Responses effort mai cin karo yana ƙarewa cikin tsaro maimakon canza yanayin burauzar da aka zaɓa a ɓoye.

Kada a sake suna ko a sake amfani da tsohon connector na `Codex Native` ko `OmniRoute Codex`. ChatGPT yana cache na public MCP contract bisa identity na connector, yayin da sabuwar bridge ɗin da aka sabunta ke amfani da sabon direct turn-token contract. Runtime yana ƙin waɗannan tsoffin identities kuma yana buƙatar sabon connector na `OmniRoute Codex v2`.

## Tsarin tsaro

- Hanyar native tana buƙatar buƙatar Responses, abokin cinikin Codex da aka gane, da kuma daidaitattun shaidun thread da turn.
- Workspace, sandbox, manufar amincewa, da kundin kayan aiki suna fitowa daga native Codex shell; rubutun umarni na kai-tsaye ba shi da ikon ƙayyade su.
- ChatGPT yana karɓar izini mai ɗan gajeren lokaci ne kawai ga kowane turn. Dillalin MCP yana karɓar kayan aikin da Codex ya bayar a daidai wannan turn kawai.
- Tabbatar da **Allow once** ta atomatik yana mayar da buƙatar kayan aikin ne kawai zuwa Codex. Codex kaɗai ne ke yanke shawara kan amincewa da aiwatarwa.
- Kafin fitowar farko, combo na iya komawa wata manufa mai jituwa. Bayan fitarwa ta fara, provider, model, connection, da browser turn suna ci gaba da kasancewa a kafe har sai turn ya kammala.
- Cookies, runtime keys, storage state, da capability tokens ba sa bayyana a cikin amsoshin provider ko bayanan buƙata.

## VPS mara allon nuni da Docker

Ga shigarwar npm, systemd, da PM2, OmniRoute yana gano sanannun hanyoyin Chrome da Chromium.
A madadin haka, saita `CHATGPT_WEB_CODEX_CHROME_PATH`. Da gangan, runtime turns suna amfani da
Chrome mai taga saboda ChatGPT yana ƙin tsarin browser na ainihin headless. Saboda haka, na'ura marar
allon nuni dole ne ta gudanar da OmniRoute tare da nuni na Xvfb mai zaman kansa; saita hanyar Chrome
kawai ba ya samar da shi.

Profile na Docker mai suna `web` yana fara `chatgpt-web-codex-browser` a kan cibiyar sadarwar Compose
ta ciki. Sidecar ɗin yana gudanar da Chrome mai taga a cikin Xvfb, don haka ba a buƙatar allon nuni na zahiri. Ba a
bayyana port ɗinsa na CDP a kan host ba. Volume ɗin browser profile da aka kare ya bambanta da
volume ɗin bayanan OmniRoute, kuma browser ɗin yana samun isasshen shared memory. Proxy na CDP
na ciki yana sauraro ne kawai a port `9223` a cikin cibiyar sadarwar Compose; Chrome yana ci gaba da kasancewa
a ɗaure da loopback a cikin sidecar.

Supervisor lease a ƙarƙashin `DATA_DIR` yana hana matakan sarrafa OmniRoute da yawa mallakar
tunnel da broker state iri ɗaya. Doctor yana bayar da rahoton rikici.

## Farfadowa ta hulɗa

Hanyar Docker ta atomatik ba ta da taga da ake iya gani daga host, amma Chrome da kansa yana da taga a cikin
nuni na Xvfb mai zaman kansa. Lokacin da ChatGPT ke buƙatar shiga ko tantancewa ta hulɗa, ana iya amfani da
ababen more rayuwa na VNC browser da suke akwai don farfadowa. Browser UI da CDP dole ne su kasance masu samuwa
kawai ta loopback, authenticated management connection, ko SSH tunnel; noVNC yana kasancewa a kashe
yayin aiki na yau da kullum.

## Madadin WebSocket

Lokacin da combo ya ƙunshi ChatGPT Web (Codex), bridge na Responses WebSocket yana buƙatar
madadin HTTP/SSE kafin haɗawa da upstream. Daga nan, canja wurin yana bi ta
`/v1/responses`.
