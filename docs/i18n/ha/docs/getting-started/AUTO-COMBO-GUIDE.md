> **A taƙaice**: Saita model ɗinka zuwa `auto`, sannan OmniRoute zai zaɓi mafi kyawun mai samar da AI ta atomatik ga kowace buƙata. Ba sai an yi wani saiti ba.

---

## Abin da Yake Yi

Maimakon zaɓar takamaiman model na AI (kamar GPT-4o ko Claude), za ka iya barin OmniRoute ya **zaɓi mafi dacewa ta atomatik** ga kowace buƙata. Yana la'akari da:

- **Lafiya** — Shin mai samarwar yana aiki a yanzu?
- **Sauri** — Yaya saurinsa yake?
- **Kuɗi** — Nawa ne kuɗinsa?
- **Inganci** — Shin ya ƙware a irin wannan aikin?
- **Ƙarfi** — Shin yana da sauran quota?

OmniRoute yana bai wa duk masu samarwar da ka haɗa maki, sannan ya zaɓi mafi kyau. Idan ya gaza, yana gwada na gaba ta atomatik.

---

## Farawa Cikin Sauri

**Mataki na 1**: Saita model ɗinka zuwa `auto` a cikin IDE ko CLI ɗinka:

```
model: "auto"
```

**Mataki na 2**: Shi ke nan! OmniRoute zai kula da sauran.

**Mataki na 3** (na zaɓi): Yi amfani da wani nau'i don takamaiman ayyuka:

```
model: "auto/coding"    # Mafi dacewa da lamba
model: "auto/fast"      # Amsa mafi sauri
model: "auto/cheap"     # Zaɓi mafi arha
```

## Wane "auto" Ya Kamata In Yi Amfani da Shi?

| Idan kana son...             | Yi amfani da wannan | Mafi dacewa da                    | Yadda yake aiki                               |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------- | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Mafi kyau gaba ɗaya**      | `auto`              | Tambayoyi na gama-gari, hira      | Yana daidaita sauri, kuɗi, da inganci         |
| **Mafi kyau ga lamba**       | `auto/coding`       | Rubuta lamba, gyaran kurakurai    | Yana zaɓar models da suka ƙware a coding      |
| **Amsa mafi sauri**          | `auto/fast`         | Amsoshi masu sauri, low latency   | Yana fifita sauri fiye da komai               |
| **Zaɓi mafi arha**           | `auto/cheap`        | Tanadin kuɗi                      | Yana zaɓar mai samarwa mafi arha              |
| **Model mafi kaifin basira** | `auto/smart`        | Ayyuka masu sarƙaƙiya             | Inganci a gaba + gwada sabbin models          |
| **Mafi samuwa**              | `auto/offline`      | Lokacin da masu samarwa suka cika | Yana zaɓar masu samarwa masu mafi yawan ƙarfi |

### Misalai

```bash
# Hira ta gama-gari — daidaitacce
curl http://localhost:20128/v1/chat/completions \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"model":"auto","messages":[{"role":"user","content":"Hello!"}]}'

# Samar da lamba — inganci a gaba
curl http://localhost:20128/v1/chat/completions \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \

# Amsa cikin sauri — sauri a gaba

  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"model":"auto/fast","messages":[{"role":"user","content":"What is 2+2?"}]}'
```

## Yadda Yake Aiki (Sigar Mai Sauƙi)

Lokacin da ka aika buƙata da `model: "auto"`, OmniRoute:

1. **Yana duba duk masu samarwar da ka haɗa** — Duk wani mai samarwa da ka ƙara (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, da sauransu.)
2. **Yana bai wa kowannensu maki**, yana auna abubuwa kamar:
   - Shin yana aiki? (lafiya)
   - Shin yana da isasshen ƙarfi? (quota)
   - Nawa ne kuɗinsa? (farashi)
   - Yaya saurinsa yake? (sauri)
   - Shin ya ƙware a wannan aikin? (inganci)
3. **Yana zaɓar mafi kyau** — Mai samarwa da ya fi samun maki ne zai karɓi buƙatarka
4. **Yana murmurewa ta atomatik** — Idan ya gaza, OmniRoute zai gwada na gaba ta atomatik

### Tsarin Ba da Maki

Kowane mai samarwa yana samun maki daga 0 zuwa 1. Yayin da makin ya fi yawa, hakan yana nuna ya fi dacewa.

| Ma'auni           | Nauyi | Abin da yake nufi                                      |
| ----------------- | ----- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Lafiya            | 20%   | Shin mai samarwar yana aiki? (yanayin circuit breaker) |
| Quota             | 15%   | Shin yana da sauran ƙarfi?                             |
| Kuɗi              | 15%   | Yaya tsadarsa? (mafi arha = maki mafi yawa)            |
| Sauri             | 12%   | Yaya saurinsa? (ƙarancin latency = maki mafi yawa)     |
| Dacewa da Aiki    | 8%    | Shin ya ƙware a irin wannan aikin?                     |
| Kwanciyar Hankali | 5%    | Shin aikinsa daidaitacce ne? (ƙarancin kuskure)        |
| Mataki            | 5%    | Matsayin asusu (Ultra > Pro > Free)                    |
| Sauran            | 20%   | Dacewar context, yawan haɗin kai, da sauransu.         |

### Yadda Nau'o'i Ke Sauya Ba da Maki

## Yadda Yake Magance Gazawa

OmniRoute yana da **matakai uku na kariya**:

### 1. Komawa Ta Atomatik

Idan mafi kyawun mai samar da sabis ya gaza, OmniRoute zai gwada na gaba ta atomatik. Ba kwa buƙatar yin komai.

### 2. Gyaran Kai

Idan mai samar da sabis ya ci gaba da gazawa:

- **Maki < 0.2** → Za a cire shi na mintuna 5
- **Circuit breaker a buɗe** → Za a cire shi ta atomatik
- **Fiye da 50% na masu samar da sabis ba sa aiki** → Yanayin matsala (babu gwaji)

### 3. Komawar Gaggawa

Idan duk masu samar da sabis sun gaza, OmniRoute zai tura buƙatun zuwa tabbatattun masu samar da sabis na kyauta (kamar Kiro ko Qoder) a matsayin mafita ta ƙarshe.

---

## Tallafin Asusun da Yawa

Idan kuna da asusu da yawa na mai samar da sabis ɗaya (misali, maɓallan OpenAI guda biyu), OmniRoute yana ɗaukar kowannensu a matsayin **ɗan takara daban**. Wannan yana nufin:

- Asusun A yana da sauran ƙayyadadden amfani → yi amfani da shi
- An sanya wa Asusun B iyakar yawan buƙatu → tsallake shi
- Asusun C ya fi araha → fifita shi

Ana tantance kowane asusu daban bisa lafiyarsa, ƙayyadadden amfaninsa, da saurinsa.

---

## Gwajin Bandit

Lokaci-lokaci OmniRoute yana **gwada** sabbin masu samar da sabis don gano zaɓuɓɓuka mafi kyau:

- **Na asali**: 5% na buƙatu suna zuwa ga masu samar da sabis da aka zaɓa bazuwar
- **Auto/smart**: Adadin gwaji na 10%
- **An kashe shi** idan fiye da 50% na masu samar da sabis suna da matsala

Wannan yana taimaka wa OmniRoute koyon waɗanne masu samar da sabis ne suka fi dacewa da tsarin amfaninku.

## Tambayoyin da Aka Fi Yi

### "Shin koyaushe zai zaɓi samfurin da ya fi tsada?"

**A'a.** A tsarin asali, farashi yana wakiltar 15% ne kawai na maki. Mai samar da sabis mai araha, sauri, kuma lafiyayye zai iya fin mai tsada. Yi amfani da `auto/cheap` idan kuna son ƙara fifita farashi.

### "Me zai faru idan mai samar da sabis ya daina aiki?"

OmniRoute zai tsallake shi ta atomatik kuma ya gwada na gaba. Idan mai samar da sabis ya ci gaba da gazawa, za a cire shi na ɗan lokaci (mintuna 5-30). Ba kwa buƙatar yin komai.

### "Zan iya ganin wane mai samar da sabis aka yi amfani da shi?"

Duba taken amsa — OmniRoute yana haɗa mai samar da sabis da samfurin da aka yi amfani da su a kowace amsa.

### "Shin yana koyo daga yadda nake amfani da shi?"

Eh! Tsarin bayar da maki yana amfani da bayanan tarihi (jinkiri, adadin kurakurai, adadin nasara) don yanke shawarwari mafi kyau a tsawon lokaci.

### "Mene ne bambanci tsakanin `auto` da `auto/smart`?"

- `auto` — Daidaitacce, gwaji na 5%
- `auto/smart` — Inganci a gaba (ma'auni iri ɗaya da `auto/coding`), gwaji na 10%

Yi amfani da `auto/smart` idan kuna son mafi kyawun inganci kuma ba ku damu da gwaji na lokaci-lokaci ba.

### "Zan iya tilasta amfani da takamaiman mai samar da sabis?"

Eh! Yi amfani da haɗaka mai dabarar `priority` maimakon `auto`, sannan ku aika da **ainihin sunan** haɗakar a matsayin filin `model` (misali `model: "my-combo"` — ba `auto` ba). Duba [Bayanan Fasaha](../routing/AUTO-COMBO.md) don cikakkun bayanai.

### "Ta yaya wannan ya bambanta da round-robin?"

Round-robin yana bi ta kan masu samar da sabis ɗaya bayan ɗaya bisa jeri. Auto-combo yana **ba kowane mai samar da sabis maki** sannan ya zaɓi mafi kyau. Ya fi wayo — yana la'akari da lafiya, sauri, farashi, da inganci.
