> **TOML ne kaɗai tsarin da ke aiki.** Codex na zamani yana karanta `~/.codex/config.toml`
> ne kawai (an tabbatar da wannan da codex-cli 0.147.0: `codex --help` yana bayyana
> cewa `-c/--config` yana maye gurbin saitunan da aka "loaded from `~/.codex/config.toml`"). Tsohon
> `~/.codex/config.yaml` na tsohon npm CLI ne kuma ana yin biris da shi ba tare da sanarwa ba.
> Mai samar da saiti na dashboard (`/api/cli-tools/apply`, kayan aiki `codex`) yana rubuta TOML tare da
> haɗawa cikin taka-tsantsan — ana kiyaye maɓallan da suke akwai da sauran provider blocks, ana ajiye
> API key a cikin `OMNIROUTE_API_KEY` (ba a taɓa saka shi a cikin fayil ba), sannan ana bayar da rahoton ragowar tsohon
> `config.yaml` a matsayin bayanin ƙaura ba tare da taɓa shi ba.

## config.toml mai shirye don liƙawa

Maye gurbin `<YOUR_HOST>` da `<YOUR_KEY>` da ƙimominka:

```toml
# ~/.codex/config.toml
model                          = "cx/gpt-5.5"
model_provider                 = "omniroute"
model_reasoning_effort         = "xhigh"
model_context_window           = 400000
model_auto_compact_token_limit = 350000
tool_output_token_limit        = 32768    # iyakar ajiyar tarihi ga kowane kiran kayan aiki

[model_providers.omniroute]
name                 = "OmniRoute"
base_url             = "http://<YOUR_HOST>:20128/v1"
env_key              = "OMNIROUTE_API_KEY"
requires_openai_auth = false
wire_api             = "responses"
```

```bash
# ~/.bashrc ko ~/.zshrc — ainihin ƙimar maɓalli, kada a taɓa saka ta a config.toml
export OMNIROUTE_API_KEY="<YOUR_KEY>"
```

### macOS: Codex da aka haɗa cikin manhajar ChatGPT

Idan ka girka Codex ta hanyar manhajar ChatGPT ta desktop, binary ɗin `codex` na iya
kasancewa ne kawai a cikin app bundle kuma bai kasance a cikin `PATH` na shell ɗinka ba tukuna. Ƙara
resources directory zuwa fayil ɗin farawa na shell ɗinka:

```bash
export PATH="/Applications/ChatGPT.app/Contents/Resources:$PATH"
```

Buɗe sabon shell, sannan ka tabbatar:

```bash
command -v codex
codex --version
```

### OmniRoute na gida mara buƙatar tantancewa: placeholder key ya isa

Codex yana tabbatar da cewa environment variable da `env_key` ya ambata yana nan
**kafin** buƙata ta farko ta fita daga CLI. Idan instance na OmniRoute na **gida**
ba ya buƙatar tantancewa, duk wani placeholder mara komai zai yi aiki:

```bash
export OMNIROUTE_API_KEY="${OMNIROUTE_API_KEY:-local}"
```

Yi amfani da ainihin maɓalli maimakon haka idan ana kare uwar garken OmniRoute ɗinka ko kuma tana nesa.

> **Zaɓuɓɓukan host da aka fi amfani da su**
>
> | Hanyar shiga | URL                           |
> | ------------ | ----------------------------- |
> | Cibiyar gida | `http://192.168.0.1:20128/v1` |
> | Tailscale    | `http://100.x.x.x:20128/v1`   |
> | Loopback     | `http://localhost:20128/v1`   |

## `wire_api = "responses"` — dalilin da ya sa yake aiki da duk models

Codex CLI ya daina goyon bayan `wire_api = "chat"` (Chat Completions) a Fabrairu 2026 kuma yanzu yana **buƙatar** `wire_api = "responses"` (OpenAI Responses API). Saita `wire_api = "chat"` yana sa manhajar ta rushe nan take yayin farawa tun daga v0.138.

Providers da yawa, ciki har da GLM da Kimi, har yanzu suna samar da endpoint na Chat Completions kawai. DeepSeek V4 yanzu yana samar da Responses API na asali tare da endpoint mai dacewa da Anthropic; OmniRoute yana amfani da Responses a matsayin tsoho kuma yana barin kowace haɗin DeepSeek ta zaɓi dacewa da Anthropic.

**OmniRoute yana warware wannan ba tare da ka lura ba:**

```
Codex CLI
  → wire_api = "responses"
  → POST /v1/responses (OmniRoute)
    → OmniRoute yana zaɓar protocol na asali na provider kuma yana fassara idan ya zama dole
    → POST /responses (DeepSeek V4) ko /chat/completions (Mistral / GLM / Kimi / wasu)
```

Ba ka taɓa buƙatar wani translation proxy na daban yayin amfani da OmniRoute. **Duk models suna amfani da `wire_api = "responses"`** — OmniRoute yana kula da sauran.

> **`wire_api` shi ne tsohon saiti** — field ɗin yana amfani da `"responses"` a matsayin tsoho kuma ana iya cire shi gaba ɗaya daga `config.toml`. Ka saita shi a bayyane ne kawai idan kana son bayyana manufar saitin.

## Tagar mahallin rubutu da taƙaitawa

### Filayen daidaita token

| Fili                             | Bayani                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `model_context_window`           | Jimillar kasafin token na samfurin da ke aiki. Saita shi zuwa iyakar da aka sanar ta samfurin.                                                                                                    |
| `model_auto_compact_token_limit` | Matsayin da ke kunna taƙaita tarihin ta atomatik. **Mafi yawa: 90% na `model_context_window`** — ana yin watsi da ƙimomin da suka haura 90% ba tare da sanarwa ba.                                |
| `tool_output_token_limit`        | Iyakar token da ake adanawa a tarihin kowace fitowar kiran kayan aiki. Yana hana babbar amsa guda ɗaya ta kayan aiki cika tagar. **Wannan ba shi ne iyakar fitarwa ba** — iyakar adana tarihi ce. |
| `compact_prompt`                 | Sauyin gaggawa na cikin layi ga umarnin tsarin da ake amfani da shi yayin taƙaitawa (v0.138+).                                                                                                    |

> **Lura game da `model_max_output_tokens`**: Wannan filin **ba ya cikin tsarin saitunan Codex CLI** (babu shi a rumbun lambar Codex Rust). Ana yin watsi da shi ba tare da sanarwa ba idan an saita shi. Kada ku dogara da shi — yi amfani da `tool_output_token_limit` don sarrafa yawan fitowar kayan aiki da ake adanawa a tarihi.

## Ƙoƙarin Tunani

Yana sarrafa yawan "tunanin" da samfurin zai yi kafin ya ba da amsa.

| Ƙima     | Yi amfani da shi don                                               |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `none`   | Babu tunani — amsa kai tsaye                                       |
| `low`    | Ayyuka masu sauƙi (sake suna, tsara tsari)                         |
| `medium` | **Tsohon saitin sabar** idan ba a ƙayyade ba                       |
| `high`   | Ayyuka masu matsakaicin wahala (sake fasalin lamba, gyara kuskure) |
| `xhigh`  | Tsarin gine-gine, bincike mai zurfi, matsaloli masu sarƙaƙiya      |

```bash
# Sauya saitin ga kowane kira
codex -c model_reasoning_effort=low "sake sunan variable x zuwa count"
codex -c model_reasoning_effort=xhigh "ƙirƙiri tsarin auth module"
```

Haka kuma, saita **taƙaitaccen bayanin** tunani domin Desktop ya iya nuna rubutun tunani (ba rufaffun bayanai kaɗai ba):

```toml
# ~/.codex/config.toml
model_reasoning_effort = "xhigh"   # ko ultra idan ana goyon bayansa
model_reasoning_summary = "detailed"  # auto | concise | detailed | none
```

### Kasafin Tunanin OmniRoute (saitin saba)

A kan uwar-garken OmniRoute, dole ne **Settings → AI → Thinking Budget** ya kasance **`passthrough`** domin ƙoƙarin/taƙaitaccen bayanin Codex ya isa ga sabis na sama. Yanayin **`auto` yana cire** duk filayen `reasoning` / `reasoning_effort` na abokin ciniki kuma zai bar bangarorin tunani babu komai ko da an saita Codex daidai.

Cikakken jagora: [THINKING_BUDGET.md](./THINKING_BUDGET.md).

Matse bayanai da ma'ajiyar wucin-gadi ta prompt ba su dogara da wannan ba kuma suna ci gaba da aiki ƙarƙashin `passthrough`.

## Profiles — saituna masu suna ga kowane samfur/tsarin aiki

Profiles suna ba ka damar sauya samfur + taga mahalli da flag guda ɗaya. Kowane profile fayil ne mai sauƙi
`~/.codex/<name>.config.toml` wanda yake ɗora saitunansa a kan ainihin `config.toml`.

> **Ƙa'idar sanya suna (Codex CLI v0.137+):** dole fayil ɗin ya kasance `~/.codex/<name>.config.toml` — **ba tare da prefix ɗin `profile-` ba**.
> CLI yana warware `-p kimi-k27` → `~/.codex/kimi-k27.config.toml`. Idan ba a sami fayil ɗin ba, za a yi amfani da tsohon saiti a ɓoye.

```bash
codex --profile kimi-k27 "binciki layuka 10k na wannan codebase"
codex -p glm52 "bita kan tsarin gine-gine"
codex --profile deepseek-flash "sake sunan variable"   # mai sauri, mai arha
```

### Profiles na ƙoƙari (samfuri ɗaya, ƙoƙari daban-daban)

```bash
codex -p low      # cx/gpt-5.5, ƙoƙari=low

codex -p high     # cx/gpt-5.5, ƙoƙari=high
```

## Samar da profiles ta atomatik da `omniroute setup-codex`

Idan kana gudanar da OmniRoute a kan VPS, za ka iya samar da fayilolin profile ta atomatik daga kundin models mai aiki:

```bash
# Daga VPS (yana amfani da OmniRoute na cikin gida a port 20128)
omniroute setup-codex

# Daga kowace na'ura — nuna zuwa VPS ɗinka
omniroute setup-codex --remote http://100.x.x.x:20128 --api-key sk-xxx

# Yi samfoti ba tare da rubuta fayiloli ba
omniroute setup-codex --remote http://100.x.x.x:20128 --dry-run

# Samar da profiles na GLM da Kimi kawai
omniroute setup-codex --only glm,kimi

# Rubuta zuwa directory na musamman
omniroute setup-codex --codex-home /path/to/.codex
```

Umarnin yana ɗauko `/v1/models`, yana amfani da profiles da aka daidaita don sanannun models, yana komawa ga metadata na kundin don sauran text models masu jituwa, sannan yana rubuta `~/.codex/<name>.config.toml` ga kowannensu. Ba ya sauya sakamako idan an maimaita shi — yana da aminci a sake gudanar da shi.

OmniRoute kuma zai iya **daidaita waɗannan fayilolin profile ta atomatik** bayan nasarar gano/shigo da models na provider ta sauya kundin mai aiki. Wannan **na zaɓi ne kuma a kashe yake ta tsohuwa**: kunna shi daga **dashboard na CLI Code** ("Daidaitawar profile na CLI ta atomatik" → Codex), ko saita `OMNIROUTE_AUTO_SYNC_CODEX_PROFILES=true` (yana kuma mutunta `CLI_ALLOW_CONFIG_WRITES`, wanda yake kunne ta tsohuwa). Idan an kunna shi, yana rubuta keɓantattun fayilolin profile na `~/.codex/*.config.toml` kawai; ba ya taɓa sauya `~/.codex/config.toml` mai aiki/na tsohuwa, saitunan Codex-lb, auth, ko zaɓin provider.

## Ayyuka masu ɗaukar dogon lokaci

Tsoffin saituna biyu na OmniRoute na iya lalata zaman Codex CLI na sa'o'i da yawa ba tare da an lura ba. Babu ɗayansu da yake saitin Codex CLI — dukansu suna ɓangaren OmniRoute. Masu amfani da ke ƙaura da tsari daga upstream proxies waɗanda ke ɗaure asusu kuma suke kashe iyakokin zaman banza sukan gamu da matsalolin biyu, sannan su yanke hukuncin cewa OmniRoute “ba zai iya ci gaba da dogon zama ba.”

| Alama                                                                            | Dalili mai yiwuwa                                                                              | Maɓallin saiti           |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Zama yana ci gaba da sauya asusu / an rasa ci gaba na prompt-cache tsakanin juyi | Session affinity TTL yana `0` (a kashe)                                                        | `sessionAffinityTtlMs`   |
| Haɗi yana yankewa a tsakiyar tunani ba tare da saƙon da abokin ciniki ke gani ba | Mai sa ido kan rashin aikin stream ya kunna bayan mintuna 10 ba tare da chunk daga upstream ba | `STREAM_IDLE_TIMEOUT_MS` |

Tattaunawa masu alaƙa: [#7126](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/discussions/7126) (yankewar dogon aiki), [#5718](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/discussions/5718) (dalilin da ya sa affinity yake kashe a tsohon saiti). Bibiyar batu: [#7287](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/issues/7287).

### 1. Session affinity — ɗaure tattaunawa ɗaya ga asusu ɗaya

**Tsohon saiti:** `sessionAffinityTtlMs = 0` (a kashe).

**Inda za a saita shi**

- Dashboard → **Settings → Routing** → **Session affinity** → **Affinity TTL (seconds)** (`ComboDefaultsTab`)
- Ko a yi PATCH ga saituna da `sessionAffinityTtlMs` cikin **millisecond** (kewayon Zod `0`–`86_400_000`, wato har zuwa awa 24)

> An sake masa suna a #7274 daga `codexSessionAffinityTtlMs` mai aiki ga Codex kawai. Har yanzu ana karɓar tsohon key ɗin a matsayin alias na karantawa kawai; ya kamata sabbin tsare-tsare su yi amfani da `sessionAffinityTtlMs`. Yanzu affinity yana aiki ga **kowane** provider da zarar TTL ya haura `0`, ba ga Codex kawai ba — duba [`docs/architecture/RESILIENCE_GUIDE.md`](../architecture/RESILIENCE_GUIDE.md) → Session affinity.

**Abin da ke lalacewa idan ya ci gaba da kasancewa 0**

Kowane juyi na tattaunawar Codex mai juyi da yawa ana yi masa routing daban bisa ga dabarar combo da ke aiki, kuma zai iya sauka kan **wani asusu daban a kowane juyi**. Wannan yana karya ci gaban session / prompt-cache na upstream. OmniRoute yana duba Codex session headers (`x-codex-session-id` / `x-session-id` / `x-omniroute-session`) da body fields kamar `prompt_cache_key` / `session_id` ne kawai idan TTL ya fi `0` (`extractSessionAffinityKey` a cikin `src/sse/services/auth.ts`).

**Abin da aka ba da shawara don aiki guda da zai ɗauki sa'o'i da yawa**

Saita TTL **sama da tsawon lokacin ainihi da ake sa ran aikin zai ɗauka** (matsakaicin UI shi ne **86400 seconds** = awa 24):

| Tsawon aikin da ake sa ran | Affinity TTL (UI, seconds) | `sessionAffinityTtlMs` |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| 'Yan sa'o'i                | `14400` (4h)               | `14400000`             |
| Tsawon dare / ~12h         | `43200` (12h)              | `43200000`             |
| Cikakken yini              | `86400` (24h, iyaka)       | `86400000`             |

Shiga da zaɓi abu ne da aka yi da gangan: kashe affinity yana fifita daidaita nauyi tsakanin asusu; kunna shi yana fifita ci gaba ga dogon zaman agent guda ɗaya. Wannan jagorar **ba ta** canza tsohon saiti — dole ne masu gudanar da dogayen ayyukan Codex su zaɓi shiga.

### 2. Lokacin ƙarewar stream mara aiki — kada a kashe juyin tunani mai shiru

**Tsohon saiti:** `STREAM_IDLE_TIMEOUT_MS = 600000` (mintuna 10). Yana gado daga `REQUEST_TIMEOUT_MS` idan ba a saita shi ba; ma'aunin tushe na bai ɗaya ma 600000 ne. Duba [`docs/guides/SETUP_GUIDE.md`](SETUP_GUIDE.md) → Lokutan ƙarewa.

**Abin da ke lalacewa a tsohon saitin**

Zagayen tunani / kayan aiki na Codex da ya yi shiru fiye da mintuna 10 ba tare da **wani ainihin ɓangaren bayanai daga upstream ba** ana rufe shi da ƙarfi ta mai sa ido kan zaman banza na SSE (`open-sse/utils/stream.ts`). Sau da yawa abokin ciniki yana ganin haɗin ya yanke kawai — abin da ya yi daidai da “ya tsaya ta atomatik ba tare da wani sanarwa ba.”

Muhimmin bayani: Bugun-zuciyar SSE na wucin gadi na OmniRoute **ba ya sake saita** agogon zaman banza. Ainihin ɓangaren jikin bayanai daga upstream ne kawai yake sabunta `lastChunkTime`. Samfurin da ya yi shiru alhali har yanzu yana “tunani” yana kama da upstream da ya tsaya cik a mahangar mai sa idon.

Rashin aiki na jikin Undici mai alaƙa: `FETCH_BODY_TIMEOUT_MS` (shi ma a tsohuwa yana amfani da ma’aunin mintuna 10 iri ɗaya; `0` yana kashe shi). Don yawo, `FETCH_TIMEOUT_MS` yana rufe kafa haɗi / karɓar headers na farko ne kawai — da zarar yawo ya fara aiki, `STREAM_IDLE_TIMEOUT_MS` da `FETCH_BODY_TIMEOUT_MS` ne ke sarrafa tsayawar bayanai.

**Shawarar da ta dace don aiki guda da zai ɗauki sa’o’i da yawa**

A cikin muhallin aikin OmniRoute (`.env` / compose / systemd):

```bash
# Kashe iyakokin zaman banza na yawo + rashin aikin jiki don zagayen tunani masu tsawo
STREAM_IDLE_TIMEOUT_MS=0
FETCH_BODY_TIMEOUT_MS=0
```

Ko kuma a ɗaga su sama da mafi tsawon lokacin shiru da kake tsammani (ƙimomin suna cikin milliseƙondi):

```bash
# Misali: ba da damar shiru na har zuwa awa 2 tsakanin ɓangarorin bayanai daga upstream
STREAM_IDLE_TIMEOUT_MS=7200000
FETCH_BODY_TIMEOUT_MS=7200000
```

Sake kunna OmniRoute bayan canza waɗannan env vars.

### Takamaiman hanya — aikin Codex na sa’o’i da yawa

1. **Ƙayyade asusun:** Dashboard → Settings → Routing → Session affinity → Affinity TTL = `43200` (12h) ko `86400` (iyakar 24h).
2. **Ɗaga / kashe iyakokin zaman banza** a muhallin OmniRoute:

```bash
STREAM_IDLE_TIMEOUT_MS=0
FETCH_BODY_TIMEOUT_MS=0
```

3. Ci gaba da amfani da `config.toml` na Codex na yau da kullum (`wire_api = "responses"`, `base_url` daidai, `OMNIROUTE_API_KEY`) — babu saitunan affinity/zaman banza a ɓangaren Codex don waɗannan halaye biyu.
4. Sake kunna OmniRoute, sannan ka fara aikin Codex mai tsawo.

### Shawarar tsoffin ƙimomi (#7287)

| Saiti                    | Tsohon ƙimar fitarwa | A canza a wannan jagorar?                                                                                          |
| ------------------------ | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `sessionAffinityTtlMs`   | `0` (a kashe)        | **A’a** — zai ci gaba da zama na zaɓi (daidaita nauyi da ci gaba da zama; duba Discussion #5718)                   |
| `STREAM_IDLE_TIMEOUT_MS` | `600000` (minti 10)  | **A’a** — zai ci gaba da zama mintuna 10 don zirga-zirgar gama-gari; masu gudanar da Codex su ɗaga ko su kashe shi |

Sauya ɗaya daga cikin tsoffin ƙimomin a duniya zai canza hali ga kowane abokin cinikin wata instance, ba Codex kaɗai ba. A rubuta bayanin saitunan; a bar tsoffin ƙimomin yadda suke har sai an yanke wata takamaiman shawara ta mai gudanarwa.

### Gano yankewar zaman banza

Lokacin da mai sa idon zaman banza ya fara aiki, OmniRoute yana rubuta layi mai kama da haka:

```text
[STREAM] Idle timeout: no data from codex for 600000ms (model: cx/gpt-5.5)
```

Yi grep don `Idle timeout: no data from` (ko lambar `stream_idle_timeout` / sunan kuskure `StreamIdleTimeoutError`). Sashen provider shi ne duk abin da OmniRoute ya yi amfani da shi don wannan buƙatar (`codex`, wani provider id, ko `provider` idan ba a sani ba) — ba koyaushe ne zai zama ainihin rubutun `codex` ba.

## Warware Matsaloli

**`Error: wire_api = "chat" is no longer supported`**
Cire `wire_api = "chat"` daga saitunanka. Saita `wire_api = "responses"` ko ka bar filin ba tare da komai ba (tun daga v0.138, tsohon ƙimarsa ita ce `"responses"`).

**`Error: model not found`**
Tabbatar cewa samfurin yana cikin OmniRoute tare da madaidaicin prefix. Yi amfani da `omniroute models list` ko buɗe `/dashboard/providers/<provider>`.

**`Authentication error`**
Tabbatar an fitar da `OMNIROUTE_API_KEY`: `echo $OMNIROUTE_API_KEY`.

**`ERROR: Missing environment variable: OMNIROUTE_API_KEY`**
Codex yana tabbatar da cewa env var ɗin yana nan kafin yin buƙata ta farko. Fitar da
maɓalli na ainihi don sabarori masu kariya, ko wata ƙima marar fanko ta wucin gadi kamar
`OMNIROUTE_API_KEY=local` idan OmniRoute na **local** ɗinka baya
buƙatar tantancewa. Sake kunna shell idan ka ƙara shi zuwa `~/.bashrc` ko `~/.zshrc`.

**`Connection refused`**
Tabbatar cewa OmniRoute yana aiki kuma host/port na `base_url` ya dace da hanyar sadarwarka (local ko Tailscale ko VPS).

**Zaman yana rushewa kusa da iyakar mahallin**
Saita `model_context_window` da `model_auto_compact_token_limit` a sarari. Duba jadawalin taga mahallin da ke sama.

**Taƙaitawa tana farawa da latti sosai**
Rage `model_auto_compact_token_limit` zuwa 80–85% na tagar. Kada ka taɓa saita shi sama da 90%.

**Profile baya lodawa (`-p <name>` ana yin watsi da shi ba tare da sanarwa ba)**
Tabbatar cewa fayil ɗin yana nan a `~/.codex/<name>.config.toml` (ba tare da prefix na `profile-` ba). Gudanar da `ls ~/.codex/*.config.toml`.

**Dogon aikin Codex yana yankewa a tsakiyar aiki / yana sauya asusu tsakanin matakai**
Duba [Ayyuka masu ɗaukar lokaci mai tsawo](#long-running-tasks). Kunna manne wa zaman (TTL ya fi tsawon aikin) sannan ka ƙara ko ka kashe `STREAM_IDLE_TIMEOUT_MS` / `FETCH_BODY_TIMEOUT_MS`. Yi grep a cikin log na OmniRoute don `Idle timeout: no data from`.
