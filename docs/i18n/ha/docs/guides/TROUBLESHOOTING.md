## Cikakken Magance Matsaloli

---

### Ƙayyade Yawan Buƙatu a Masu Samarwa na Kyauta (429 / 400 / 401)

**Alama**: Lokacin amfani da `model: "auto"` tare da masu samarwa na kyauta/marasa buƙatar tabbatar da izini (opencode, auggie, da sauransu), lokaci-lokaci kana samun `HTTP 429`, `400`, ko `401` maimakon amsoshi. Buƙatun suna yin nasara idan aka sake gwada wannan umarni bayan ƴan daƙiƙu, amma sarrafa aiki ta atomatik (cron jobs, agents, scripts) yana lalacewa a gazawar farko.

**Asalin dalili**: Hanyoyin gazawa guda uku masu zaman kansu suna haɗuwa:

1. **Ƙayyade yawan buƙatu na mai samarwa (`429`)**: Matakan kyauta na iya tilasta ƙayyadadden kaso a kowace taga ta lokaci. Tarin kiraye-kiraye masu gudana lokaci guda yana ƙare wannan kaso, saboda haka za a ƙi buƙata ta gaba har sai tagar ta sake farawa.
2. **Model mara aiki a passthrough (`400`/`401`)**: Rukunonin `auto/*` na iya ƙunsar passthrough models daga `opencode` waɗanda aka yi wa rajista a catalog amma ba su da credentials masu aiki (misali `oc/north-mini-code-free` → `401`). Auto-router yana gwada ɗaya, ya gaza, sannan kuskuren ya bazu kafin fallback ya fara aiki.
3. **Ƙaruwa saboda gudana lokaci guda (`429` a ƙarƙashin nauyi)**: Lokacin da sessions na agent/cron da yawa suka kira `auto` lokaci guda, jimillar yawan buƙatu tana wuce abin da masu samarwa na kyauta za su iya ɗauka, saboda haka ana ɗaukar sahihan kiraye-kiraye a matsayin cin zarafi.

**Tabbatacciyar gyara (al'umma ta ruwaito, 2026-08-10)**: daidaita environment variables guda uku domin rotation, concurrency, da fallback su shawo kan sauye-sauyen matakin kyauta maimakon su gaza saboda su:

```bash
export OMNIROUTE_ROTATE_ON_400=true           # matsa zuwa wani model/mai samarwa a kan 400/401 (yana tsallake passthrough models marasa aiki)
export OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT=4   # bayyanannen matsakaicin karɓar manyan ayyuka (ba a saita shi ta asali: babu iyakar adadin buƙatu, duba bayanin da ke ƙasa)
export OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS=5000 # jira mai tsawo amma mai iyaka domin samun damar manyan ayyuka maimakon retryable 503 nan take
```

Saita waɗannan a muhallin process na OmniRoute (daemon ɗin, misali ta LaunchAgent plist ko `systemctl edit`), sannan sake kunna OmniRoute. Rotation flag ita ce hanya guda mafi tasiri: tana sauya gazawa kai tsaye zuwa sake gwadawa ba tare da an gani ba ta amfani da mai samarwa mai lafiya a cikin rukunin.

**Lura**: `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT` yana iyakance adadin manyan buƙatu — masu dogon context — da za su gudana lokaci guda; iyakar ƙofar karɓa ce, ba mai ƙayyade yawan buƙatu na mai samarwa ba. **Sabuntawar #503-fanout:** ba a ƙara saita wannan var ta asali (yanzu yana aiki ne kawai idan an saita shi a bayyane, kamar yadda ke sama) — a maimakon haka, ana sarrafa karɓar manyan ayyuka da byte budget da aka samo ta atomatik (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES`) wanda yake daidaita kansa bisa ainihin iyakar memory ta host, saboda haka sabon deployment ya kamata ya ga ƙarancin ƙin buƙatu na `503 chat_admission_busy` ba tare da saita wannan var kwata-kwata ba; saita shi a bayyane a nan har yanzu yana aiki daidai yadda aka bayyana. Bayyanannun sauye-sauyen byte-budget suna takaituwa zuwa 8 MiB–2 GiB. `413 body_exceeds_budget` ba matsala ce ta wucin gadi ba: ƙara wannan byte budget, rage `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_HARD_MAX_BODY_BYTES`, ko ƙara iyakar memory ta process. Zubar da `inflight_bytes_budget` cunkoso ne na wucin gadi kuma ana iya sake gwadawa. Ana sarrafa ƙayyade yawan buƙatu na kowane mai samarwa (`open-sse/services/rateLimitManager.ts`) daban ta `RATE_LIMIT_MAX_WAIT_MS`, `RATE_LIMIT_MAX_QUEUE_DEPTH`, da `RATE_LIMIT_AUTO_ENABLE` — duba `.env.example`.

**Yadda za a tabbatar cewa ya yi aiki**: gudanar da agent/cron ɗinka sau biyu cikin sauri a jere sannan ka tabbatar duka sun yi nasara. Kafin gyaran, gudu na biyu yawanci yana jefa `429`/`401`. Bayan gyaran, ana sake gwada gazawa (idan akwai) ba tare da an gani ba kuma kiran yana kammala. Haka kuma za ka iya yin `curl /monitoring/health` sannan ka lura da field na `rateLimitedUntil` a kan haɗin masu samarwa da `circuitBreakers.providerBreakers[].state` na masu samarwar da abin ya shafa — state ɗin yana ɗaya daga cikin `CLOSED`, `DEGRADED`, `OPEN`, ko `HALF_OPEN` (duba `src/shared/utils/circuitBreaker.ts`), kuma mai samarwar da ke ci gaba da gazawa zai sauya daga `CLOSED → DEGRADED → OPEN` kafin tagar sake farawa ta bari probe ya wuce (`HALF_OPEN`).

**Idan har yanzu kana ganin 429**: asusun da yake aiki na wannan mai samarwa ya gama ainihin _quota_ ɗinsa (ba iyakar yawan buƙatu kawai ba). Ƙara asusu na biyu na wannan mai samarwa a dashboard na OmniRoute → Providers → Accounts, ko haɗa wani mai samarwa na kyauta (misali, `routeway`, `auggie`). Sauyawa tsakanin asusu yana taimakawa ne kawai ga matsalolin wucin gadi na iyakar yawan buƙatu/400/401; ƙarewar quota gaba ɗaya na buƙatar bayanan shaidar shiga na biyu ko wani mai samarwa daban.

**Idan kana ganin 403 a kan samfuran vision (`auto/vision`, `bazaarlink/*`)**: asusun da aka haɗa ba shi da tsarin biyan kuɗi wanda ya haɗa da vision, ko kuma API key ɗin ba shi da isassun izini. Tabbatar a dashboard na mai samarwar cewa key scope ɗin ya haɗa da vision/multimodal, ko haɗa asusun matakin biyan kuɗi sannan ka bar shi a matsayin makomar vision.

## Gargadin npm install (ERESOLVE / peer / deprecated)

Lokacin da ka gudanar da `npm install -g omniroute`, za ka iya ganin tarin gargadi kamar `npm warn ERESOLVE`, sanarwar peer-dependency, da saƙonnin `deprecated`. **Ana tsammanin waɗannan kuma ba su da illa.** Shigarwar ta yi nasara idan ka ga `added <N> packages` a cikin sakamakon.

Don ɓoye gargadin warware peer-dependency, yi amfani da tsarin shigarwa da OmniRoute ke goyon baya:

```bash
npm install -g omniroute --legacy-peer-deps
```

`--legacy-peer-deps` yana ɓoye `ERESOLVE` da sanarwar peer-dependency kawai. Sanarwar daina tallafawa za ta ci gaba da bayyana saboda tana fitowa ne daga fakitin wasu kamfanoni da ake dogaro da su a kaikaice; ba ta nuna cewa shigarwar ta gaza ba.

Gargadin yana fitowa ne daga tsofaffin kewayon peer-dependency a cikin fakitin wasu kamfanoni waɗanda OmniRoute ba ya sarrafawa:

1. **`marked-terminal` yana buƙatar `marked >=1 <16`, amma an sami `marked@18`** — yana aiki lafiya a aikace; kewayon peer na upstream ne kawai ya tsufa.
2. **`deprecated prebuild-install@7.1.3`** — mataimaki ne na ɗauko native-binary da ake dogaro da shi a kaikaice. Ba a
   amfani da shi wajen shigar da transport binding na `wreq-js` da aka kulle sigarsa, kuma ba ya nuna cewa saitin transport na
   mai samar da web-cookie ya gaza.

**Ba a buƙatar wani mataki** — ba za a iya ɓoye gargadin gaba ɗaya ba sai an yi fork na fakitin upstream.

## Sakamakon Ƙarya na Antivirus

<a name="antivirus-false-positives"></a>

### Avast/AVG yana killace `README.md` da `MD:HttpRequest-inf[Susp]`

**Wannan sakamakon ƙarya ne. Babu abin da ya kamu da cuta, kuma ba a buƙatar wani mataki.**

Avast da AVG suna gudanar da wata dabarar tantancewa wadda ke yi wa fayilolin rubutu na
kawai/Markdown da ke ɗauke da mahaɗa masu yawa masu kama da buƙatun HTTP alama. Ana haɗa
`README.md` na OmniRoute a cikin kunshin npm (an jera shi a `package.json` → `files`), don
haka yana sauka a `node_modules/omniroute/README.md` yayin girkawa ta global — kuma yana
ɗauke da misalai kusan 15 na `http://localhost:20128/...` (endpoints na MCP HTTP/SSE, URL
na A2A `.well-known`, da snippets na `curl`). Yawan waɗannan mahaɗan ya isa ya kunna
wannan dabarar tantancewar.

Idan wannan ya fara ne kwanan nan kawai: nau'in fayil ɗin bai canza ba. README ya faɗaɗa
teburin endpoints ɗinsa (an ƙara MCP HTTP + SSE + A2A) tare da ƙarin misalan `curl`, wanda
ya sa ya wuce iyakar tantancewar.

Fayil ɗin takardu ne marasa aiki kuma ba ya ɗauke da ko kaɗan na abin da za a iya aiwatarwa.
Za ka iya dawo da shi daga wurin killacewa ba tare da wata matsala ba.

**Abin da za a yi:**

1. **Dakatar da sanarwar** — cire kundin girkawar daga binciken antivirus ɗinka
   (Avast: Settings → Exceptions), ta hanyar ƙara hanyar global `node_modules` ɗinka
   da/ko kundin bayanan OmniRoute (`~/.omniroute/`).
2. **Kai rahoton sakamakon ƙaryar** — <https://www.avast.com/false-positive-file-form.php>,
   tare da haɗa `README.md` da aka killace. Wannan ne gyaran da ke taimaka wa kowa, domin
   dabarar tantancewar mai samar da manhajar ce ke wuce gona da iri kan fayil ɗin rubutu.

**Dalilin da ya sa ba ma “gyara” wannan daga ɓangarenmu:** duk misalan
`http://localhost` ne, kuma localhost ba zai iya zama `https` ba tare da wahalar
takardar shaida mai sanya wa kai hannu ba. Gurɓata takardun don kauce wa dabarar tantancewar
mai samar da manhaja guda ɗaya zai cutar da kowane mai karatu don biyan buƙatar kuskuren
na'urar binciken.

### Kaspersky yana yi wa manhajar Desktop alamar `PDM:Trojan.Win32.Generic`

**Wannan sakamakon ƙarya ne daga dabarar tantance ɗabi'a. Babu abin da ya kamu da cuta.**
Prefix na `PDM:` na Kaspersky yana nufin hukuncin ya fito ne daga Proactive Defense Module
(System Watcher), wanda ke tantance abin da mai girkawar _yake yi_ maimakon kwatanta shi da
malware da aka sani. Idan ya kunna, Kaspersky yana “mayar da baya” ga dukan girkawar — yana
share fayilolin da ya riga ya rubuta — don haka manhajar tana lalacewa ko ɓacewa.

Fayilolin da yake yi wa alama sanannun sassa ne na dependencies na buɗaɗɗen tushe da aka
bayyana kuma aka haɗa da manhajar desktop, misali:

- `resources/app/.build/next/node_modules/playwright-<hash>/lib/…/agentParser.js` da
  `workerProcessEntry.js` — [Playwright](https://playwright.dev), library na sarrafa
  burauza kai tsaye da ake amfani da shi don shiga provider daga cikin manhaja da tattaunawa
  mai amfani da burauza.
- `resources/app/.build/next/node_modules/@wreq-js/binding-win32-<arch>-msvc-<hash>/wreq-js.win32-<arch>-msvc.node`
  — native binding na `wreq-js` da aka ƙayyade wanda ake amfani da shi don HTTP mai kwaikwayon
  sawun yatsa na burauza a kan providers na web-cookie (`<arch>` shi ne `x64` ko `arm64`).

**Dalilin da ya sa yake kunnawa:** Mai girkawar Windows **har yanzu ba a sanya masa hannu
da lamba ba**, don haka mai girkawar NSIS marar sa hannu ba shi da wani suna na amincewa,
kuma dabarun tantance ɗabi'a suna aiki da tsananin ƙarfi. Idan aka haɗa wannan da native DLL
da ɗaruruwan fayilolin `.js` da ake rubutawa a ƙarƙashin
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\OmniRoute` (ciki har da kundin packages masu ƙarewa da hash daga
standalone build na Next.js), hakan ya isa ya kunna dabarar tantancewar. An shirya sanya
hannu da lamba; har sai an aiwatar da hakan, sabbin releases na iya maimaita wannan.

**Abin da za a yi:**

1. **Da farko ka tabbatar da ingancin abin da ka sauke** (wannan yana kawar da yiwuwar an
   sauya fayil ɗin). Kowane release yana wallafa `latest.yml`, wanda filin `sha512` ɗinsa
   (base64) ya ƙunshi checksum na mai girkawar `OmniRoute.Setup.<version>.exe`. A PowerShell,
   daga babban fayil ɗin da ke ɗauke da mai girkawar:
   ```powershell
   $b = [System.Security.Cryptography.SHA512]::Create().ComputeHash(
     [System.IO.File]::ReadAllBytes("$PWD\OmniRoute.Setup.<version>.exe"))
   [Convert]::ToBase64String($b)
   ```
   Dole ne sakamakon ya yi daidai da `latest.yml` → `sha512`. Idan bai yi daidai ba, share
   fayil ɗin sannan ka sake sauke shi kawai daga
   [shafin releases na GitHub](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/releases).
2. **Dawo da shi + cire shi daga bincike** — dawo da abubuwan da aka mayar da baya daga
   wurin killacewa, sannan ka ƙara keɓancewa ga `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\OmniRoute`
   (Kaspersky → Settings → Threats and Exclusions), sannan ka sake girkawa.
3. **Kai rahoton sakamakon ƙaryar** — <https://opentip.kaspersky.com/>. Rahotannin FP da
   masu amfani ke aika wa suna hanzarta saka manhajar cikin jerin amintattu da gaske.

## Daidaituwar Node.js

<a name="nodejs-compatibility"></a>

### Shafin shiga yana rushewa ko yana nuna kuskuren "Module self-registration"

**Dalili:** Kana amfani da sigar Node.js da ba ta cikin mafi ƙarancin amintaccen yanayin aiki da OmniRoute ya amince da shi. Lamarin da aka fi samu shi ne amfani da tsohuwar sigar gyaran Node 22 ko 24 wadda ta yi ƙasa da mafi ƙarancin matakin tsaro mai faci da OmniRoute ke buƙata.

**Alamomi:**

- Shafin shiga yana nuna allo mara komai ko kuskuren sabar
- Na’ura mai nuna bayanai tana nuna `Error: Module did not self-register` ko makamantan kurakuran haɗin native
- Shafin shiga yana nuna **tutar gargaɗi mai launin ruwan lemu** tare da sigar Node ɗinka idan yanayin aikin ba ya cikin ƙa’idar tsaro da ake tallafawa

**Gyara:**

1. Sanya sigar Node.js LTS da ake tallafawa (shawara: Node.js 24.x):
   ```bash
   nvm install 24
   nvm use 24
   ```
2. Tabbatar da sigarka: `node --version` ya kamata ya nuna `v24.0.0` ko sabuwa a layin 24.x LTS
3. Sake sanya OmniRoute: `npm install -g omniroute`
4. Sake farawa: `omniroute`

> **Amintattun sigogin da ake tallafawa:** `>=22.22.2 <23` ko `>=24.0.0 <27`. Ana tallafawa Node.js 24.x LTS (Krypton) da Node.js 26 gaba ɗaya.

### npm v11+: Ba a sanya `better-sqlite3` ba (Cannot find module)

<a name="npm-v11-better-sqlite3-not-installed-cannot-find-module"></a>

**Dalili:** npm v11 (wanda ke zuwa tare da Node.js 24+) yana toshe rubutun umarnin sanyawa na abubuwan dogaro na zaɓi
ta tsohuwa. Saboda an jera `better-sqlite3` a cikin `optionalDependencies`
kuma yana buƙatar haɗawar native (`node-gyp rebuild`), npm yana tsallake shi ba tare da sanarwa ba.

**Alamomi:**

- Sabar tana rushewa yayin farawa tare da `Cannot find module 'better-sqlite3'`
- `ls node_modules/better-sqlite3` yana nuna "No such file or directory"
- `npm ls better-sqlite3` yana nuna `(empty)`

**Gyara:**

1. Amince da rubutun umarnin sanyawa sannan ka sake sanyawa:
   ```bash
   npm approve-scripts better-sqlite3
   npm install
   ```
2. Ko ka sanya sigar da aka riga aka gina da hannu:
   ```bash
   npm pack better-sqlite3@13.0.1
   tar -xzf better-sqlite3-*.tgz -C node_modules
   mv node_modules/package node_modules/better-sqlite3
   rm better-sqlite3-*.tgz
   ```
3. Tabbatar yana aiki: `node -e "require('better-sqlite3')(':memory:').close(); console.log('OK')"`

### macOS: `dlopen` / "slice is not valid mach-o file"

<a name="macos-native-module-rebuild"></a>

**Dalili:** Bayan gudanar da `npm install -g omniroute` na gama-gari, mai aiwatarwar native na `better-sqlite3` da ke cikin fakitin na iya kasancewa an haɗa shi ne don wata architecture ko Node.js ABI dabam da wanda ke aiki a na’urarka. Wannan ya zama ruwan dare a macOS (a Apple Silicon da Intel) idan binary da aka riga aka gina bai dace da muhallinka ba.

**Alamomi:**

- Sabar tana gaza nan take yayin farawa tare da kuskuren `dlopen`
- Kuskuren yana ɗauke da `slice is not valid mach-o file`
- Cikakken misali:

```
dlopen(/Users/<user>/.nvm/versions/node/v24.14.1/lib/node_modules/omniroute/app/node_modules/better-sqlite3/build/Release/better_sqlite3.node, 0x0001): tried: '...' (slice is not valid mach-o file)
```

**Gyara — sake gina shi don muhallinka na gida (ba sai an koma tsohuwar sigar Node.js ba):**

```bash
cd $(npm root -g)/omniroute/app
npm rebuild better-sqlite3
omniroute
```

> **Lura:** Wannan yana sake haɗa native binding ɗin da sigar Node.js da CPU architecture na na’urarka, yana warware rashin daidaituwar binary. Kewayon yanayin aikin da ake tallafawa a hukumance shi ne **`>=22.22.2 <23` ko `>=24.0.0 <27`** (`SUPPORTED_NODE_RANGE` a cikin `src/shared/utils/nodeRuntimeSupport.ts`, wanda ya yi daidai da filin `engines` na `package.json`). Ana tallafawa Node.js 24.x LTS (Krypton) da Node.js 26 gaba ɗaya tare da `better-sqlite3` v12.x.

## Matsalolin Proxy

<a name="proxy-issues"></a>

### Tabbatar da mai samarwa yana nuna "fetch failed"

**Dalili:** A baya, endpoint ɗin tabbatar da maɓallin API (`POST /api/providers/validate`) yana ƙetare saitunan proxy, wanda ke haifar da gazawa a muhallan da ke buƙatar bi ta proxy.

**Gyara (v3.5.5+):** Yanzu an gyara wannan. Hanyoyin tabbatar da mai samarwa suna bi ta `runWithProxyContext`, suna mutunta saitunan proxy na matakin mai samarwa da na gaba ɗaya ta atomatik.

### Binciken lafiyar token yana gazawa da "fetch failed"

**Dalili:** Sabunta token na OAuth a bayan fage ba ya gano saitunan proxy ga kowace haɗi.

**Gyara (v3.5.5+):** Yanzu mai tsara binciken lafiyar token yana gano saitin proxy ga kowace haɗi kafin yunƙurin sabuntawa. Sabunta zuwa v3.5.5+.

### Proxy na SOCKS5 yana mayar da "invalid onRequestStart method"

**Dalili:** A Node.js 22, dispatcher na undici@8 bai dace da aiwatarwar `fetch()` da ke cikin Node ba.

**Gyara (v3.5.5+):** Yanzu OmniRoute yana amfani da aikin `fetch()` na undici idan dispatcher na proxy yana aiki, domin tabbatar da daidaitaccen aiki. Sabunta zuwa v3.5.5+.

### Proxy na MITM a ƙarƙashin WSL: ba a katse manhajojin desktop da ke kan Windows host

**Dalili:** Ana shigar da proxy na MITM da takardar shaidar CA ɗinsa cikin muhallin da OmniRoute yake gudana. A ƙarƙashin WSL, wannan muhalli shi ne Linux guest, yayin da manhajojin AI na desktop (Kiro, Trae, Copilot, Zed, …) suke gudana a kan Windows host. Manhajojin host ba sa amincewa da ma'ajiyar takardun shaida ta guest kuma ba sa bi ta system proxy na guest, saboda haka katse zirga-zirgar desktop ba ya aiki a can.

**Shawara:** Gudanar da OmniRoute kai tsaye a kan OS ɗaya da manhajojin desktop da kake son katsewa (Windows don manhajojin Windows; haka ma macOS/Linux). Barin OmniRoute a cikin WSL yayin da ake niyyar manhajojin host yana buƙatar amincewa da takardar shaidar CA da aka samar da hannu a kan Windows host, sannan a nuna saitunan network/proxy na kowace manhajar host zuwa endpoint ɗin proxy na WSL — tsari ne mara tallafi kuma mai sauƙin lalacewa.

## Matsalolin Docker

### Docker IPv6 / Sake Saita Haɗi

<a name="docker-ipv6"></a>

**Alamomi:** `curl http://localhost:20128/v1/models` yana dawo da `curl: (56) Recv failure: Connection reset by peer`. Dashboard da endpoints marasa tantancewa suna aiki, amma endpoints masu tantancewa suna kasa — yana kama da matsalar auth amma ba haka ba ne.

**Dalili:** `docker run -p 20128:20128` yana buɗe port a kan duka `0.0.0.0` (IPv4) da `::` (IPv6), amma process ɗin da ke cikin container yana sauraro a IPv4 kawai. A hosts inda `localhost` yake komawa zuwa `::1` da farko, haɗin yana isa port ɗin IPv6 da aka buɗe ba tare da listener a bayansa ba → ana sake saita haɗin.

**Gyara:**

1. **Bincike mai sauri:** Gudanar da `curl -4 http://localhost:20128/v1/models`. Idan yana aiki da `-4` amma ya kasa ba tare da shi ba, kana da rashin daidaituwar bind na IPv6.
2. **Gyara na dindindin:** Yi bind zuwa IPv4 a sarari ta amfani da `-p 127.0.0.1:20128:20128` a cikin umarnin `docker run` naka:
   ```bash
   docker run -d --name omniroute --restart unless-stopped --stop-timeout 40 \
     -p 127.0.0.1:20128:20128 -v omniroute-data:/app/data diegosouzapw/omniroute:latest
   ```
   Wannan yana tilasta bind na IPv4 kuma yana hana fallasa proxy a kan dukkan interfaces na host.

## Gano da Gyara Kurakurai

### Kunna Fayilolin Log

Saita `APP_LOG_TO_FILE=true` a cikin fayil ɗin `.env` naka. Ana rubuta logs na application a ƙarƙashin `logs/`.
Ana adana artifacts na request a ƙarƙashin `${DATA_DIR}/call_logs/` lokacin da aka
kunna pipeline na call log a cikin settings.
Lokacin da aka kunna pipeline capture, saita `CALL_LOG_PIPELINE_CAPTURE_STREAM_CHUNKS=false` don barin
payloads na stream chunks, ko daidaita `CALL_LOG_PIPELINE_MAX_SIZE_KB` don canza iyakar artifact a KB.

### Duba Lafiyar Provider

```bash
# Dashboard na lafiya
http://localhost:20128/dashboard/health

# Binciken lafiyar API
curl http://localhost:20128/api/monitoring/health
```

### Ma'ajiyar Runtime

- Babban state: `${DATA_DIR}/storage.sqlite` (providers, combos, aliases, keys, settings)
- Amfani: Tables na SQLite a cikin `storage.sqlite` (`usage_history`, `call_logs`, `proxy_logs`) + `${DATA_DIR}/call_logs/` na zaɓi
- Logs na application: `<repo>/logs/...` (lokacin da `APP_LOG_TO_FILE=true`)
- Artifacts na call log: `${DATA_DIR}/call_logs/YYYY-MM-DD/...` lokacin da aka kunna pipeline na call log

Aikin **Clean history** na shafin Request Logs yana share `call_logs`, tsohon
`request_detail_logs`, da directory na artifacts na gida `${DATA_DIR}/call_logs/`.

## Matsalolin Rubuta Sauti Zuwa Rubutu

### Kuskuren "Unsupported model"

- Yi amfani da id na samfuri wanda ɓangarensa na farko yake na mai bayarwar da kake da bayanan shiga dominsa (`openai/whisper-1`, `openrouter/deepgram/nova-3`). Amfani da `deepgram/nova-3` kai tsaye yana buƙatar maɓallin Deepgram na asali.

### Rubutawar sauti tana dawo da sakamako mara komai ko ta gaza

- Duba tsarin fayilolin sauti da ake goyon baya: `mp3`, `wav`, `m4a`, `flac`, `ogg`, `webm`
- Tabbatar cewa girman fayil ɗin yana cikin iyakokin mai bayarwa (yawanci < 25MB)
- Duba ingancin maɓallin API na mai bayarwa a katin mai bayarwar

### Matsalolin tsari da aka fi samu

- **Alamomin tunani ba sa bayyana** — Duba ko mai bayarwar da ake nufi yana goyon bayan tunani da kuma saitin kasafin tunani
- **Kiran kayan aiki suna ɓacewa** — Wasu fassarorin tsari na iya cire filayen da ba a goyon baya; tabbatar da hakan a yanayin Playground
- **Umarnin tsarin ya ɓace** — Claude da Gemini suna sarrafa umarnin tsarin ta hanyoyi daban-daban; duba sakamakon fassarar
- **SDK yana dawo da tsantsar zaren rubutu maimakon abu** — An warware shi a v1.x; mai tsabtace amsa yana cire filayen da ba na daidaitaccen tsari ba (`x_groq`, `usage_breakdown`, da sauransu) waɗanda ke haifar da gazawar tabbatarwar OpenAI SDK Pydantic. Idan har yanzu kana ganin wannan a v3.x+, da fatan za ka buɗe rahoton matsala.
- **GLM/ERNIE yana ƙin rawar `system`** — An warware shi a v1.x; mai daidaita rawa yana haɗa saƙonnin tsarin kai tsaye cikin saƙonnin mai amfani ga samfuran da ba su dace ba. Idan har yanzu kana ganin wannan a v3.x+, da fatan za ka buɗe rahoton matsala.
- **Ba a gane rawar `developer` ba** — An warware shi a v1.x; ana sauya shi kai tsaye zuwa `system` ga masu bayarwa waɗanda ba OpenAI ba. Idan har yanzu kana ganin wannan a v3.x+, da fatan za ka buɗe rahoton matsala.
- **`json_schema` ba ya aiki da Gemini** — An warware shi a v1.x; yanzu ana sauya `response_format` zuwa `responseMimeType` + `responseSchema` na Gemini. Idan har yanzu kana ganin wannan a v3.x+, da fatan za ka buɗe rahoton matsala.

## Saitunan Juriya

### Iyakance ƙima ta atomatik ba ya fara aiki

- Iyakance ƙima ta atomatik yana aiki ne kawai ga masu samarwa masu amfani da maɓallin API (ba OAuth/biyan kuɗi ba)
- Tabbatar cewa an kunna iyakance ƙima ta atomatik a **Settings → Resilience → Provider Profiles**
- Bincika ko mai samarwar yana dawo da lambobin matsayi na `429` ko kanun `Retry-After`

### Daidaita exponential backoff

Bayanan martabar masu samarwa suna goyon bayan waɗannan saituna:

- **Base delay** — Lokacin jira na farko bayan gazawar farko (tsoho: 1s)
- **Max delay** — Matsakaicin iyakar lokacin jira (tsoho: 30s)
- **Multiplier** — Adadin da za a ƙara lokacin jinkiri bayan kowace gazawa mai jere (tsoho: 2x)

### Rigakafin tururuwar buƙatu lokaci guda

Lokacin da buƙatu masu yawa na lokaci guda suka isa ga mai samarwa da aka iyakance ƙimarsa, OmniRoute yana amfani da mutex + iyakance ƙima ta atomatik don jera buƙatun ɗaya bayan ɗaya da hana gazawa mai yaɗuwa. Wannan yana faruwa ta atomatik ga masu samarwa masu amfani da maɓallin API.

### Buƙatun taɗi suna gazawa da 503 / chat_admission_busy

**Alamomi:**

- Mahadar kammala taɗi tana dawo da amsar `503` da za a iya sake gwadawa, wadda lambar kuskurenta ita ce
  `chat_admission_busy`.
- Amsar tana ƙunshe da `Retry-After`. Tun daga #12135 ana samo ƙimar ne daga yawan amfani da aka lura da shi
  — mafi girma tsakanin taga ta `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS` da buƙatar ta riga ta
  jira da kuma tsawon lokacin da ake riƙe da lasisin nauyi na yanzu — ana zagaye shi zuwa cikakkun
  daƙiƙu kuma ana iyakance shi zuwa 60. Idan ƙofar ba ta da aiki, yana riƙe da tsoffin ƙananan iyakoki: daƙiƙu 2 a
  hanyar da ta dogara da byte, daƙiƙa 1 a hanyar da ta dogara da tsari (wadda kuma ta ƙunshi
  `reason: "structure_limit"`).
- Wannan na iya faruwa yayin da wani taɗi mai nauyi ko amsar streaming mai ɗaukar lokaci mai tsawo har yanzu
  ke gudana.

Jikin amsar da ta dogara da byte shi ne:

```json
{
  "error": {
    "message": "Chat admission capacity is temporarily unavailable. Retry shortly.",
    "type": "server_error",
    "code": "chat_admission_busy"
  }
}
```

Amsar da ta dogara da tsari tana amfani da nau'i da lamba iri ɗaya, tare da saƙon
`Local chat admission capacity is busy for this structurally heavy request; upstream provider routing was not attempted. Retry shortly.`
da `reason: "structure_limit"`.
A tsoffin matakan ƙima, ana ɗaukar buƙata a matsayin mai nauyin tsari idan tana da aƙalla saƙonni `200`,
aƙalla kayan aiki `64`, ko aƙalla kiyasin tokens `32,000`, ko kuma idan ƙididdigar tsari mai iyaka
ta kai iyakarta ta nodes `10,000` da aka ziyarta ko zurfin `12`.

**Dalili:** Wannan rage lodin da aka yi da gangan ne a cikin OmniRoute, ba gazawar mai samarwa na sama ba.
Kowane process yana amfani da kariya ta cikin process don keɓe iyakantacciyar damar sarrafa manyan buƙatu kafin riƙewa
da kuma tantance babban jikin buƙata. Ana ci gaba da riƙe lasisin nauyi har tsawon rayuwar amsar SSE.

**#503-fanout:** kafin wannan gyaran, kariyar tana iyakance concurrency zuwa tsayayyen ADADIN buƙatu
(`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT`, tsoho `1`) ba tare da la'akari da ƙwaƙwalwar host ba, don haka
fan-out na coding-agent (subagents/CLIs da yawa, jikin buƙatu akai-akai > 256 KB) yana rugujewa zuwa
concurrency mai tasiri na ~1 kuma yana mayar da 503 a ƙarƙashin lodin da yake daidai. Yanzu kariyar tana
daidaita kanta: tana dogara da kasafin ingest na BYTE da aka samo ta atomatik
(`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES`) wanda aka ƙayyade daga ainihin iyakar ƙwaƙwalwar
process, sannan kuma tana duba siginar matsin albarkatu kai tsaye — don haka tana
rage lodi ne kawai lokacin da host ɗin ke ƙarƙashin matsin ƙwaƙwalwa na gaske, ba wai kawai saboda buƙatu masu nauyi
fiye da ɗaya sun iso lokaci guda ba. Har yanzu ana mutunta tsohuwar iyakar adadi
(`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT`), amma sai idan ka saita ta kai tsaye.

Lokacin da damar ta cika, buƙata mai nauyi za ta fara jira har zuwa
`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS` (tsoho `2000`, `0` yana kashe jiran) domin wani gurbi ya samu
kafin ta mayar da `503` da za a iya sake gwadawa. An samar da wannan jira mai iyaka ne domin abokan ciniki irin na agent
(OpenCode, Claude Code, Cursor) waɗanda ke rarraba manyan ƙananan buƙatu lokaci guda su jera wannan tururuwa
maimakon ƙona duk kasafin sake gwadawarsu ta hanyar ƙin buƙatu nan take sannan su mutu a tsakiyar aiki.
Ana nuna yawan lasisin nauyi da ake amfani da shi yanzu, kasafin byte da aka ƙayyade, da tsananin matsin kai tsaye
a `GET /api/monitoring/health` → `chatAdmission` (`inflightBytes`, `maxInflightBytes`,
`budgetSource`, `pressureSeverity`, `countCapEnabled`) — bincika waɗannan kafin ka taɓa kowane env var.
Settings → Resilience → Request Queue → Concurrent Requests ba ya sarrafa wannan; wannan saitin
yana kula da wata hanyar jerin buƙatun mai samarwa daban.

**Gyara:**

1. Fara da sake gwadawa. Ya kamata abokan ciniki su mutunta `Retry-After` kuma su yi amfani da backoff maimakon
   maimaita buƙatar nan take.
2. Bincika `/api/monitoring/health` → `chatAdmission` kafin daidaita komai. `countCapEnabled:
false` da `maxInflightBytes` mai yalwa suna nufin kasafin da aka samo ta atomatik ya riga yana yin
   aikinsa; `pressureSeverity` na `high`/`critical` yana nufin host ɗin yana da ƙarancin ƙwaƙwalwa na gaske —
   ba za a iya gyara hakan da env var na admission ba, yana buƙatar ƙarin RAM ko ƙaramin aikin lodawa.
3. Sai kawai idan `/api/monitoring/health` ya nuna cewa kasafin da aka samo ta atomatik ya yi ƙanƙanta sosai ga
   host ɗinka (abu ne mai wuya — ya riga yana daidaituwa daga container zuwa bare-metal), ka maye gurbinsa kai tsaye da
   `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES` maimakon komawa ga tsohuwar iyakar adadin buƙatu.

Duba [manunin environment-variable](../reference/ENVIRONMENT.md#4-security--authentication)
don saitunan admission na hukuma.

## Rabe-raben gazawar RAG / LLM na zaɓi (matsaloli 16)

Wasu masu amfani da OmniRoute suna sanya gateway ɗin a gaban tsarin RAG ko na wakilai. A irin waɗannan tsare-tsaren, ana yawan ganin wani abin mamaki: OmniRoute yana bayyana cikin ƙoshin lafiya (masu samarwa suna aiki, bayanan martabar routing suna daidai, babu faɗakarwar iyakar yawan buƙatu) amma duk da haka amsar ƙarshe ba daidai ba ce.

A aikace, waɗannan matsalolin yawanci suna fitowa ne daga tsarin RAG da ke ƙasa, ba daga gateway ɗin kansa ba.

Idan kana son ƙamus na bai ɗaya don bayyana waɗannan gazawar, za ka iya amfani da WFGY ProblemMap, wata madogarar rubutu ta waje mai lasisin MIT wadda ke bayyana nau’ikan gazawar RAG / LLM guda goma sha shida da ke maimaituwa. A taƙaice, tana ƙunshe da:

- karkacewar retrieval da lalacewar iyakokin mahallin bayani
- indexes da vector stores marasa komai ko waɗanda suka tsufa
- rashin daidaito tsakanin embedding da ma’ana
- matsalolin haɗa prompt da taga mahallin bayani
- rugujewar dabaru da amsoshi masu nuna tabbaci fiye da kima
- gazawar dogayen sarƙoƙi da daidaitawar wakilai
- karkacewar ƙwaƙwalwa da rawar wakilai da yawa
- matsalolin jeren deployment da bootstrap

Manufar mai sauƙi ce:

1. Lokacin da kake binciken amsa mara kyau, tattara:
   - aikin mai amfani da buƙatarsa
   - route ko haɗin masu samarwa a OmniRoute
   - duk wani mahallin RAG da aka yi amfani da shi a tsarin da ke ƙasa (takardun da aka dawo da su, kiran kayan aiki, da sauransu)
2. Daidaita matsalar da lamba ɗaya ko biyu na WFGY ProblemMap (`No.1` … `No.16`).
3. Ajiye lambar a dashboard, runbook, ko tsarin bin diddigin matsalolinka kusa da logs na OmniRoute.
4. Yi amfani da shafin WFGY da ya dace don yanke shawarar ko kana buƙatar canza tsarin RAG ɗinka, retriever, ko dabarar routing.

Cikakken rubutu da takamaiman hanyoyin aiwatarwa suna nan (lasisin MIT, rubutu kawai):

[README na WFGY ProblemMap](https://github.com/onestardao/WFGY/blob/main/ProblemMap/README.md)

Za ka iya yin watsi da wannan sashe idan ba ka gudanar da tsarin RAG ko na wakilai a bayan OmniRoute.

## Sanannun Matsalolin v3.8.0

Matsalolin da suka keɓanta da sakin v3.8.0 da hanyoyin warware su na yanzu. Idan an samar da gyara a wani patch na gaba, za a sabunta ko cire bayanin.

### Gazawar tantancewar Devin CLI

**Alamu:**

- "Ba a sami Devin CLI ba" ko "tantancewa ta gaza" lokacin kiran kayan aikin da Devin ke tallafawa
- Binciken lokacin gudanarwar CLI yana nuna `installed=false`

**Dalilai:**

- `CLI_DEVIN_BIN` yana nuna hanyar da babu ita
- Ba a shigar da Devin CLI a kan host ɗin ba

**Gyara:**

1. Shigar da Devin CLI na platform ɗinka
2. Saita `CLI_DEVIN_BIN=/usr/local/bin/devin` (ko ainihin hanyar) a cikin `.env`
3. Sake kunna OmniRoute sannan a sake gwadawa daga **Dashboard → Kayan Aikin CLI**

### Cooldown na model ya makale (sake saiti da hannu)

**Alamu:**

- Model yana ci gaba da bayyana cikin cooldown ko da bayan lokacin ƙarewar ya wuce
- Har yanzu buƙatu suna tsallake model ɗin a combo routing duk da cewa timestamp ɗin yana nuna lokacin da ya wuce

**Sake saiti da hannu:**

- **Dashboard:** **Saituna → Cooldowns na Model** → danna **Sake kunnawa** a kan katin da abin ya shafa
- **API:** `DELETE /api/resilience/model-cooldowns` tare da headers na tantancewar gudanarwa

### Haɗin mai samar da Command Code ya gaza da 403

**Alamu:**

- 403 lokacin gwada haɗin mai samar da Command Code
- Katin mai samarwar yana nuna "ba a ba da izini ba" bayan an ƙara shi sabo

**Dalili:** Ba a kammala tsarin OAuth ba (ba a karɓi callback ba ko ba a adana token ba).

**Gyara:**

- Gudanar da `omniroute providers` daga CLI don sake kunna tsarin OAuth, ko
- Sake gudanar da OAuth daga **Dashboard → Masu Samarwa → Command Code → Sake haɗawa**

### ModelScope yana mayar da cooldowns na 429 masu tsauri

**Alamu:**

- Cooldowns masu gajarta sosai ko na nan take a ModelScope bayan ƙaramin tarin buƙatu
- Combo routing yana tsallake ModelScope da wuri fiye da yadda ake tsammani

**Dalili:** ModelScope yana fitar da headers na `Retry-After` da suka keɓanta da mai samarwa. v3.8.0 ya zo da keɓantaccen sarrafawa ga waɗannan headers, saboda haka tsofaffin sigogi suna fassara su ba daidai ba a matsayin alamomin iyakar yawan buƙatu na gama-gari.

**Gyara:**

- Tabbatar cewa kana kan v3.8.0 ko wanda ya fi shi sabo
- Tabbatar cewa an kunna toggle na `useUpstream429BreakerHints` a ƙarƙashin **Saituna → Juriya**

### Babu OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET a production

**Alamu:**

- 401 a kan kowace buƙatar WebSocket bridge ta Codex/Responses yayin aiki a kan remote production host
- WebSocket bridge handshake yana rufewa nan take bayan haɗawa

**Dalili:** Babu env var na `OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET` a cikin yanayin production.

**Gyara:**

1. Ƙirƙiri sirri na bazuwar: `openssl rand -hex 32`
2. Saita `OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET=<random-secret>` a cikin env na production server (da duk wani client da ke sadarwa da bridge ɗin)
3. Sake kunna OmniRoute

### Responses API: an rage background mode zuwa synchronous

**Alamu:**

- An rubuta gargaɗi a log: `background mode degraded to synchronous`
- Buƙatar `background: true` tana mayar da amsar synchronous ta yau da kullum maimakon abin riƙe aikin background

**Dalili:** Da gangan v3.8.0 yake rage `background: true` a Responses API zuwa aiwatarwa ta synchronous tare da fitar da gargaɗi. Cikakken aiwatarwar background na async wani aiki ne da za a samar nan gaba.

**Gyara:**

- Daidaita client ɗin ya yi kira ba tare da `background` ba, ko
- Jira wani sakin gaba wanda zai zo da cikakken background mode na async (bi diddigin changelog)

## Jinkirin Farawa / Karewar Lokacin Shirye-shirye

Idan CLI ya nuna `⚠ Server did not respond within 60s` amma a zahiri uwar garken tana aiki, lokacin da aka ware wa gwajin shirye-shirye ya yi gajarta ga muhallinka.

Wannan yakan faru a Windows (riga-kafi, masu sa ido kan tsarin fayiloli) ko a cikin kwantena masu nauyin ayyukan farawa.

**Gyara — ƙara lokacin da aka ware:**

```bash
# Ta hanyar env var (yana ci gaba da aiki a duk lokacin farawa):
export OMNIROUTE_READY_TIMEOUT_MS=180000   # mintuna 3
omniroute serve

# Ta hanyar tutar CLI (na lokaci guda):
omniroute serve --ready-timeout 180000
```

Ƙimar asali ita ce 60 000 ms (60 s). Gargadin na bayar da bayani ne kawai; uwar garken tana ci gaba da farawa a bayan fage kuma za a iya isa gare ta da zarar farawar ta kammala.

Duba [`docs/reference/ENVIRONMENT.md`](../reference/ENVIRONMENT.md) domin cikakkun bayanai kan `OMNIROUTE_READY_TIMEOUT_MS`.
