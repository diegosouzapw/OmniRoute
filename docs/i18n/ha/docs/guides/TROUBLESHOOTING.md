| "Mabuɗin API ba daidai ba ne"              | Mabuɗinka ba daidai ba ne ko ya ƙare             | Sake kwafe mabuɗin daga shafin yanar gizon mai samarwa                                                      |

## Cikakken Warware Matsaloli

---

### Ƙayyade Yawan Buƙatu a Masu Samarwa na Kyauta (429 / 400 / 401)

**Alama**: Lokacin amfani da `model: "auto"` tare da masu samarwa na kyauta/marasa buƙatar tantancewa (opencode, auggie, da sauransu), lokaci-lokaci kana samun `HTTP 429`, `400`, ko `401` maimakon amsoshi. Buƙatun suna yin nasara idan aka sake gwada saƙon umarni iri ɗaya bayan ƴan daƙiƙu, amma sarrafa kai (ayyukan cron, agents, scripts) yana lalacewa a gazawar farko.

**Asalin matsalar**: Nau'ikan gazawa guda uku masu zaman kansu suna haɗuwa:

1. **Ƙayyade yawan buƙatu daga mai samarwa (`429`)**: Matakan kyauta na iya tilasta ƙayyadadden kaso a cikin kowane zangon lokaci. Aika tarin kiraye-kiraye a lokaci guda yana ƙare shi, don haka za a ƙi buƙata ta gaba har sai zangon ya sake farawa.
2. **Model mai matsala a passthrough (`400`/`401`)**: Rukunonin `auto/*` na iya ƙunsar passthrough models daga `opencode` waɗanda aka yi wa rajista a kundin amma ba su da ingantattun bayanan shiga (misali `oc/north-mini-code-free` → `401`). Auto-router yana gwada ɗaya, ya gaza, sannan kuskuren ya bazu kafin komawa madadin ya fara aiki.
3. **Ƙaruwa sakamakon aiki lokaci guda (`429` a ƙarƙashin nauyi)**: Lokacin da zaman agent/cron da yawa suka aika buƙatu zuwa `auto` lokaci guda, jimillar saurin buƙatu yana wuce abin da masu samarwa na kyauta za su iya jurewa, don haka ana yi wa sahihan kiraye-kiraye alamar cin zarafi.

**Tabbataccen gyara (al'umma ta bayar da rahoto, 2026-08-10)**: daidaita environment variables guda uku domin juyawa, aiki lokaci guda, da komawa madadin su ɗauki sauye-sauyen matakin kyauta maimakon tsarin ya gaza saboda su:

```bash
export OMNIROUTE_ROTATE_ON_400=true           # tsallaka zuwa wani model/mai samarwa a kan 400/401 (yana tsallake passthrough models masu matsala)
export OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT=4   # bayyanannen iyakar karɓar ayyuka masu nauyi (ba a saita ta ta asali: babu iyakar adadin buƙatu, duba bayanin da ke ƙasa)
export OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS=20000 # ƙara iyakantaccen lokacin jira fiye da tsohuwar ƙimar RATE_LIMIT_MAX_WAIT_MS don upstreams masu jinkiri
```

Saita waɗannan a muhallin process na OmniRoute (daemon ɗin, misali ta LaunchAgent plist ko `systemctl edit`), sannan ka sake kunna OmniRoute. Alamar juyawa ita ce hanya guda mafi tasiri: tana mayar da gazawa kai tsaye zuwa sake gwadawa a ɓoye ta hanyar ingantaccen mai samarwa da ke cikin rukunin.

**Lura**: `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT` yana iyakance adadin buƙatu masu nauyi — masu dogon mahalli — da ke gudana lokaci guda; wannan iyaka ƙofar karɓa ce, ba mai ƙayyade yawan buƙatun mai samarwa ba. **Sabuntawar #503-fanout:** ba a ƙara saita wannan var ta asali ba (yanzu yana aiki ne kawai idan an saita shi kai tsaye, kamar yadda ke sama) — maimakon haka, ana sarrafa karɓar ayyuka masu nauyi ta kasafin bytes da ake samarwa ta atomatik (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES`) wanda yake daidaita kansa bisa ainihin iyakar memory na host, saboda haka sabon deployment ya kamata ya ga raguwar ƙin buƙatun `503 chat_admission_busy` sosai ba tare da saita wannan var ko kaɗan ba; saita shi kai tsaye a nan har yanzu yana aiki daidai kamar yadda aka bayyana. Takamaiman sauye-sauyen kasafin bytes suna takaita zuwa 8 MiB–2 GiB. `413 body_exceeds_budget` ba kuskure na ɗan lokaci ba ne: ƙara wannan kasafin bytes, rage `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_HARD_MAX_BODY_BYTES`, ko ƙara iyakar memory na process. Zubar da `inflight_bytes_budget` cunkoso ne na ɗan lokaci kuma har yanzu ana iya sake gwadawa. Ana sarrafa ƙayyade yawan buƙatu ga kowane mai samarwa (`open-sse/services/rateLimitManager.ts`) dabam ta `RATE_LIMIT_MAX_WAIT_MS`, `RATE_LIMIT_MAX_QUEUE_DEPTH`, da `RATE_LIMIT_AUTO_ENABLE` — duba `.env.example`.

**Yadda za a tabbatar ya yi aiki**: gudanar da agent/cron ɗinka sau biyu a jere cikin gajeren lokaci, sannan ka tabbatar dukansu sun yi nasara. Kafin gyaran, gudanarwa ta biyu galibi tana fitar da kuskuren `429`/`401`. Bayan gyaran, ana sake gwada gazawar (idan akwai) a ɓoye, kuma kiran yana kammala. Haka kuma za ka iya yin `curl /monitoring/health` sannan ka lura da filin `rateLimitedUntil` a haɗin masu samarwa da kuma `circuitBreakers.providerBreakers[].state` na masu samarwar da abin ya shafa — matsayin yana ɗaya daga cikin `CLOSED`, `DEGRADED`, `OPEN`, ko `HALF_OPEN` (duba `src/shared/utils/circuitBreaker.ts`), kuma mai samarwar da ke ci gaba da gazawa zai sauya daga `CLOSED → DEGRADED → OPEN` kafin lokacin sake saiti ya ba da damar gwaji ya wuce (`HALF_OPEN`).

**Idan har yanzu kana ganin 429**: asusun da ke aiki na wannan mai samarwar ya ƙare da gaske da _quota_ ɗinsa (ba iyakar yawan buƙatu kawai ba). Ƙara asusu na biyu na wannan mai samarwar a OmniRoute dashboard → Providers → Accounts, ko haɗa wani mai samarwa na kyauta (misali `routeway`, `auggie`). Juyawa tsakanin asusu yana taimakawa ne kawai ga matsalolin wucin-gadi na iyakar yawan buƙatu/400/401; ƙarewar ƙayyadadden _quota_ gaba ɗaya na buƙatar bayanan shaidar shiga na biyu ko wani mai samarwa daban.

**Idan kana ganin 403 a kan samfuran vision (`auto/vision`, `bazaarlink/*`)**: asusun da aka haɗa ba shi da tsarin biyan kuɗi da ya haɗa da vision, ko kuma maɓallin API ba shi da isassun izini. Tabbatar a dashboard na mai samarwar cewa iyakar izinin maɓallin ta haɗa da vision/multimodal, ko ka haɗa asusun matakin biyan kuɗi sannan ka bar shi a matsayin wurin da ake tura buƙatun vision.

## Gargadin npm install (ERESOLVE / peer / deprecated)

Lokacin da ka gudanar da `npm install -g omniroute`, za ka iya ganin tarin gargadi kamar `npm warn ERESOLVE`, sanarwar peer-dependency, da saƙonnin `deprecated`. **Ana sa ran waɗannan kuma ba su da illa.** Shigarwar ta yi nasara idan ka ga `added <N> packages` a cikin sakamakon.

Don ɓoye gargadin warware peer-dependency, yi amfani da tsarin shigarwa da OmniRoute ke tallafawa:

```bash
npm install -g omniroute --legacy-peer-deps
```

`--legacy-peer-deps` yana ɓoye `ERESOLVE` da sanarwar peer-dependency ne kawai. Sanarwar daina tallafi za ta ci gaba da bayyana saboda tana fitowa daga fakitin wasu ɓangarori da aka dogara da su kai tsaye ba; ba ta nuna cewa shigarwar ta gaza ba.

Gargadin yana fitowa ne daga tsofaffin iyakokin peer-dependency a cikin fakitin wasu ɓangarori waɗanda OmniRoute ba ya sarrafawa:

1. **`marked-terminal` yana buƙatar `marked >=1 <16`, amma an sami `marked@18`** — yana aiki lafiya a aikace; iyakar peer ta upstream ce kawai ta tsufa.
2. **`deprecated prebuild-install@7.1.3`** — mataimakin ɗauko native-binary ne da aka dogara da shi kai tsaye ba. Ba a
   amfani da shi wajen shigar da transport binding na `wreq-js` da aka ƙayyade, kuma ba ya nuna cewa
   saitin jigilar mai samar da web-cookie ya gaza.

**Ba a buƙatar wani mataki** — ba za a iya ɓoye gargadin gaba ɗaya ba sai an yi fork na fakitin upstream.

## Gargadin Toshe npm install-scripts (shigarwa ta global)

<a name="npm-install-scripts-blocked-warning-global-install"></a>

Lokacin gudanar da `npm install -g omniroute`, sababbin nau'ikan npm (v11+ da v12+) suna toshe package lifecycle scripts ta tsohuwa kuma suna nuna gargadin install-scripts:

```
npm warn install-scripts 7 packages had install scripts blocked because they are not covered by allowScripts:
  omniroute@<version> (postinstall: node scripts/build/postinstall.mjs)
  keytar, @parcel/watcher, @swc/core, protobufjs, esbuild, onnxruntime-node
```

### Za a iya yin watsi da wannan gargadin?

**A wani ɓangare, amma ba a ba da shawarar gudanarwa ba tare da scripts ba.** OmniRoute yana ƙunshe da hanyoyin madadin da ke aiki cikin sauƙi: idan ba za a iya loda native bindings na `better-sqlite3` daga standalone bundle ba, manhajar za ta koma ga wasu drivers kamar `node:sqlite` ko `sql.js` (duba [Warware SQLite Runtime](../ops/SQLITE_RUNTIME.md)). Har yanzu uwar garken za ta fara aiki, amma amfani da hanyoyin madadin WASM kamar `sql.js` yana kashe yanayin SQLite WAL (Write-Ahead Logging) kuma yana ƙara nauyin ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya da daidaitawar faifai.

### Waɗanne scripts ne ake buƙata da gaske?

Ba duk scripts da aka toshe ba ne suke da muhimmanci wajen gudanar da OmniRoute:

- **Masu muhimmanci (`omniroute`, `better-sqlite3`):** OmniRoute yana gudana daga Next.js standalone application bundle (`dist/`). Yayin shigarwa, npm yana sauke ko harhaɗa native SQLite driver da ya dace da host a cikin babban `node_modules/better-sqlite3/`. Sannan `postinstall` script na OmniRoute (`scripts/build/postinstall.mjs`) yana tabbatarwa da kwafe waɗannan binaries na musamman ga host zuwa `dist/node_modules/` domin standalone server ya iya samun damar amfani da su. Idan an toshe postinstall script na `omniroute`, za a tsallake wannan matakin kwafewa.
- **Na zaɓi/waɗanda aka dogara da su kai tsaye ba (`keytar`, `@parcel/watcher`, `@swc/core`, `protobufjs`, `esbuild`):** Waɗannan mataimakan build ne, kayan aikin watcher, ko kayan aikin system keychain. Ba su izini yana hana gargadin build, amma ba a buƙatar su don ainihin aikin routing.
- **Wanda aka ware (`onnxruntime-node`):** Dependency ne na zaɓi don local AI embeddings kuma **ba** a buƙatarsa domin OmniRoute ya yi aiki. Postinstall script ɗinsa yana sauke kusan 300MB na CUDA assets daga NuGet; kada ka saka shi cikin allow-list sai idan kana da niyyar amfani da local ONNX acceleration a kan hardware da ake tallafawa.

### Gyaran da aka ba da shawara

Don ba wa install scripts da ake buƙata izini yayin shigarwa ta global da kuma ɓoye gargadi, tura `--allow-scripts` don OmniRoute da native dependencies nasa:

```bash
npm install -g omniroute --allow-scripts=omniroute,better-sqlite3,keytar,@parcel/watcher,@swc/core,protobufjs,esbuild
```

A madadin haka, don saita wannan na dindindin ga duk shigarwar global a asusun mai amfani naka:

```bash
npm config set allow-scripts=omniroute,better-sqlite3,keytar,@parcel/watcher,@swc/core,protobufjs,esbuild --location=user
npm install -g omniroute
```

### Tabbatar da shigarwarka

Bayan shigarwa, gudanar da kayan aikin bincike na ciki don tabbatar da cewa native bindings da runtime environment suna aiki yadda ya kamata:

```bash
omniroute doctor
```

## Sakamakon Ƙarya na Antivirus

<a name="antivirus-false-positives"></a>

### Avast/AVG na keɓe `README.md` da `MD:HttpRequest-inf[Susp]`

**Wannan sakamakon ƙarya ne. Babu abin da ya kamu, kuma ba a buƙatar ɗaukar wani mataki.**

Avast da AVG suna gudanar da wata hanyar hasashe da ke yi wa fayilolin rubutu na yau da kullum/Markdown masu ɗauke da hanyoyin haɗi da yawa masu kama da buƙatun HTTP alama. Ana haɗa `README.md` na OmniRoute a cikin kunshin npm (an jera shi a `package.json` → `files`), don haka yana sauka a `node_modules/omniroute/README.md` yayin shigarwa ta duniya — kuma yana ɗauke da misalai kusan 15 na `http://localhost:20128/...` (wuraren ƙarshen MCP na HTTP/SSE, URL ɗin A2A na `.well-known`, da gutsattsarin `curl`). Yawan waɗannan hanyoyin haɗi ya isa ya kunna wannan hanyar hasashe.

Idan wannan ya fara ne kwanan nan kawai: nau'in fayil ɗin bai canza ba. README ya faɗaɗa teburin wuraren ƙarshensa (an ƙara MCP HTTP + SSE + A2A) da ƙarin misalan `curl`, wanda ya sa ya wuce iyakar da aka saita.

Fayil ɗin takardu ne marasa aiki kuma ba shi da wani abun da za a iya aiwatarwa. Za ka iya dawo da shi daga keɓewa cikin aminci.

**Abin da za a yi:**

1. **Dakatar da sanarwar** — cire kundin shigarwar daga binciken antivirus ɗinka
   (Avast: Settings → Exceptions), ta ƙara hanyar `node_modules` ta duniya da/ko kundin
   bayanan OmniRoute (`~/.omniroute/`).
2. **Kai rahoton sakamakon ƙaryar** — <https://www.avast.com/false-positive-file-form.php>,
   tare da haɗa `README.md` da aka keɓe. Wannan ne gyaran da zai taimaki kowa, domin hanyar
   hasashen mai samarwar ce take wuce gona da iri kan fayil ɗin rubutu.

**Dalilin da ya sa ba ma “gyara” wannan daga ɓangarenmu:** duk misalan `http://localhost`
ne, kuma localhost ba zai iya zama `https` ba sai da wahalar takardar shaida da aka sanya wa
hannu da kai. Gurɓata takardun don kauce wa hanyar hasashen mai samarwa guda ɗaya zai cutar
da kowane mai karatu domin kawai a gamsar da kuskuren na'urar bincike.

### Kaspersky na yi wa manhajar Desktop alama da `PDM:Trojan.Win32.Generic`

**Wannan sakamakon ƙarya ne daga hanyar hasashen ɗabi'a. Babu abin da ya kamu.** Farkon
`PDM:` na Kaspersky yana nufin hukuncin ya fito ne daga Proactive Defense Module (System Watcher),
wanda ke tantance abin da mai shigarwar _yake yi_ maimakon kwatanta shi da sanannun manhajojin
ɓarna. Idan ya kunna, Kaspersky yana “mayar da” dukkan shigarwar baya — yana share fayilolin
da ya riga ya rubuta — don haka manhajar takan lalace ko ta ɓace.

Fayilolin da yake yi wa alama ɓangarori ne na yau da kullum na dogarorin buɗaɗɗen tushe da
aka ayyana kuma aka haɗa da manhajar desktop, misali:

- `resources/app/.build/next/node_modules/playwright-<hash>/lib/…/agentParser.js` da
  `workerProcessEntry.js` — [Playwright](https://playwright.dev), ma'ajiyar sarrafa
  burauza ta atomatik da ake amfani da ita wajen shiga ga mai samarwa daga cikin manhaja da
  tattaunawar da burauza ke tallafawa.
- `resources/app/.build/next/node_modules/@wreq-js/binding-win32-<arch>-msvc-<hash>/wreq-js.win32-<arch>-msvc.node`
  — native binding na `wreq-js` da aka tsayar da nau'insa, wanda ake amfani da shi don HTTP
  mai kwaikwayon sawun burauza a kan masu samarwa masu amfani da cookies na yanar gizo
  (`<arch>` shi ne `x64` ko `arm64`).

**Dalilin da ya sa yake kunna:** mai shigarwar Windows **har yanzu ba a sanya masa sa hannun
lamba ba**, don haka mai shigarwar NSIS mara sa hannu ba shi da wata ƙima ta amincewa kuma
hanyoyin hasashen ɗabi'a suna aiki da tsaurin kai kololuwa. Idan aka haɗa wannan da native DLL
da ɗaruruwan fayilolin `.js` da ake rubutawa a ƙarƙashin
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\OmniRoute` (ciki har da kundin kunshe-kunshen da ke da hash a
ƙarshensu daga ginin standalone na Next.js), wannan ya isa ya kunna hanyar hasashen. Ana
shirin sanya hannu kan lambar; har sai an yi hakan, sabbin fitowa za su iya maimaita wannan.

**Abin da za a yi:**

1. **Da farko ka tabbatar da ingancin abin da ka sauke** (wannan yana kawar da yiwuwar an
   yi wa fayil ɗin kutse). Kowane fitowa yana wallafa `latest.yml`, wanda filin `sha512`
   ɗinsa (base64) ya ƙunshi ma'aunin mai shigarwar `OmniRoute.Setup.<version>.exe`.
   A cikin PowerShell, daga kundin da ke ɗauke da mai shigarwar:
   ```powershell
   $b = [System.Security.Cryptography.SHA512]::Create().ComputeHash(
     [System.IO.File]::ReadAllBytes("$PWD\OmniRoute.Setup.<version>.exe"))
   [Convert]::ToBase64String($b)
   ```
   Dole ne sakamakon ya yi daidai da `latest.yml` → `sha512`. Idan bai yi daidai ba, share
   fayil ɗin sannan ka sake sauke shi kawai daga [shafin fitowar GitHub](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/releases).
2. **Dawo da su + cire su daga bincike** — dawo da abubuwan da aka mayar baya daga keɓewa,
   sannan ka ƙara cirewa daga bincike ga `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\OmniRoute` (Kaspersky →
   Settings → Threats and Exclusions), sannan ka sake shigarwa.
3. **Kai rahoton sakamakon ƙaryar** — <https://opentip.kaspersky.com/>. Rahotannin FP da
   masu amfani suka aika suna hanzarta saka manhajar cikin jerin abubuwan da aka amince da su.

## Daidaituwar Node.js

<a name="nodejs-compatibility"></a>

### Shafin shiga yana rushewa ko yana nuna kuskuren "Module self-registration"

**Dalili:** Kana amfani da sigar Node.js da ba ta cikin mafi ƙarancin amintaccen yanayin aiki da OmniRoute ya amince da shi. Abin da ya fi faruwa shi ne amfani da tsohuwar sigar gyaran Node 22 ko 24 wadda ba ta kai mafi ƙarancin matakin tsaro da OmniRoute ke buƙata ba.

**Alamomi:**

- Shafin shiga yana nuna farin allo ko kuskuren sabar
- Console yana nuna `Error: Module did not self-register` ko makamantan kurakuran haɗin native
- Shafin shiga yana nuna **tutar gargaɗi mai launin lemu** tare da sigar Node ɗinka idan yanayin aikin ba ya cikin ƙa'idar tsaro da ake tallafawa

**Gyara:**

1. Shigar da sigar Node.js LTS da ake tallafawa (shawara: Node.js 24.x):
   ```bash
   nvm install 24
   nvm use 24
   ```
2. Tabbatar da sigarka: `node --version` ya kamata ya nuna `v24.0.0` ko mafi sabuwa a jerin 24.x LTS
3. Sake shigar da OmniRoute: `npm install -g omniroute`
4. Sake farawa: `omniroute`

> **Amintattun sigogin da ake tallafawa:** `>=22.22.2 <23` ko `>=24.0.0 <27`. Ana tallafa wa Node.js 24.x LTS (Krypton) da Node.js 26 gaba ɗaya.

### npm v11+: Ba a shigar da `better-sqlite3` ba (Ba a iya samun module)

<a name="npm-v11-better-sqlite3-not-installed-cannot-find-module"></a>

**Dalili:** npm v11 (wanda ke zuwa tare da Node.js 24+) yana toshe rubutun shigarwa na abubuwan dogaro na zaɓi
ta tsohuwa. Saboda an jera `better-sqlite3` a cikin `optionalDependencies`
kuma yana buƙatar haɗawa ta native (`node-gyp rebuild`), npm yana tsallake shi ba tare da sanarwa ba.

**Alamomi:**

- Sabar tana rushewa yayin farawa tare da `Cannot find module 'better-sqlite3'`
- `ls node_modules/better-sqlite3` yana nuna "Babu irin wannan fayil ko kundin adireshi"
- `npm ls better-sqlite3` yana nuna `(ba komai)`

**Gyara:**

1. Amince da rubutun shigarwa sannan ka sake shigarwa:
   ```bash
   npm approve-scripts better-sqlite3
   npm install
   ```
2. Ko shigar da wanda aka riga aka gina da hannu:
   ```bash
   npm pack better-sqlite3@13.0.1
   tar -xzf better-sqlite3-*.tgz -C node_modules
   mv node_modules/package node_modules/better-sqlite3
   rm better-sqlite3-*.tgz
   ```
3. Tabbatar cewa yana aiki: `node -e "require('better-sqlite3')(':memory:').close(); console.log('OK')"`

### macOS: `dlopen` / "slice is not valid mach-o file"

<a name="macos-native-module-rebuild"></a>

**Dalili:** Bayan `npm install -g omniroute` na gaba ɗaya, mai aiwatarwar native na `better-sqlite3` da ke cikin kunshin na iya kasancewa an haɗa shi ne don wata architecture ko Node.js ABI dabam da wanda ke aiki a na'urar. Wannan ya zama ruwan dare a macOS (duka Apple Silicon da Intel) idan mai aiwatarwar da aka riga aka gina bai dace da yanayin aikinka ba.

**Alamomi:**

- Sabar tana kasa farawa nan take tare da kuskuren `dlopen`
- Kuskuren yana ɗauke da `slice is not valid mach-o file`
- Cikakken misali:

```
dlopen(/Users/<user>/.nvm/versions/node/v24.14.1/lib/node_modules/omniroute/app/node_modules/better-sqlite3/build/Release/better_sqlite3.node, 0x0001): tried: '...' (slice is not valid mach-o file)
```

**Gyara — sake ginawa don yanayin na'urarka (ba a buƙatar komawa tsohuwar sigar Node.js):**

```bash
cd $(npm root -g)/omniroute/app
npm rebuild better-sqlite3
omniroute
```

> **Lura:** Wannan yana sake haɗa native binding da sigar Node.js da CPU architecture na na'urarka, yana warware rashin dacewar binary. Jerin yanayin aikin da ake tallafawa a hukumance shi ne **`>=22.22.2 <23` ko `>=24.0.0 <27`** (`SUPPORTED_NODE_RANGE` a cikin `src/shared/utils/nodeRuntimeSupport.ts`, wanda aka daidaita da filin `engines` na `package.json`). Ana tallafa wa Node.js 24.x LTS (Krypton) da Node.js 26 gaba ɗaya tare da `better-sqlite3` v12.x.

## Matsalolin Proxy

<a name="proxy-issues"></a>

### Tabbatar da mai samarwa yana nuna "fetch failed"

**Dalili:** A baya, wurin ƙarshen tabbatar da maɓallin API (`POST /api/providers/validate`) yana ƙetare saitunan proxy, wanda ke haifar da gazawa a mahallin da ke buƙatar bi ta proxy.

**Gyara (v3.5.5+):** Yanzu an gyara wannan. Tabbatar da mai samarwa yana bi ta `runWithProxyContext`, tare da mutunta saitunan proxy na kowane mai samarwa da na gaba ɗaya ta atomatik.

### Binciken lafiyar token yana gazawa da "fetch failed"

**Dalili:** Sabunta token na OAuth da ke gudana a bayan fage ba ya tantance saitunan proxy ga kowace haɗi.

**Gyara (v3.5.5+):** Yanzu mai tsara binciken lafiyar token yana tantance saitunan proxy ga kowace haɗi kafin ƙoƙarin sabuntawa. Sabunta zuwa v3.5.5+.

### Proxy na SOCKS5 yana mayar da "invalid onRequestStart method"

**Dalili:** A kan Node.js 22, dispatcher na undici@8 bai dace da ginannen aiwatarwar `fetch()` ta Node ba.

**Gyara (v3.5.5+):** Yanzu OmniRoute yana amfani da aikin `fetch()` na undici kansa lokacin da dispatcher na proxy yake aiki, domin tabbatar da daidaitaccen aiki. Sabunta zuwa v3.5.5+.

### Proxy na MITM ƙarƙashin WSL: ba a kama manhajojin tebur da ke kan Windows host

**Dalili:** Ana shigar da proxy na MITM da takardar shaidar CA ɗinsa a cikin mahallin da OmniRoute yake gudana. A ƙarƙashin WSL, wannan mahallin shi ne Linux guest, yayin da manhajojin tebur na AI (Kiro, Trae, Copilot, Zed, …) suke gudana a kan Windows host. Manhajojin host ba sa amincewa da ma'ajiyar takardun shaida ta guest kuma ba sa bi ta proxy na tsarin guest, saboda haka kama zirga-zirgar manhajojin tebur ba ya aiki a wurin.

**Shawara:** Gudanar da OmniRoute kai tsaye a kan OS ɗaya da manhajojin tebur da kake son kama zirga-zirgarsu (Windows don manhajojin Windows; haka nan ga macOS/Linux). Barin OmniRoute a cikin WSL yayin da ake nufin manhajojin host yana buƙatar amincewa da takardar shaidar CA da aka ƙirƙira da hannu a kan Windows host, sannan a saita saitunan hanyar sadarwa/proxy na kowace manhajar host zuwa wurin ƙarshen proxy na WSL — wannan tsari ne marar tallafi kuma mai sauƙin samun matsala.

## Matsalolin Cloud

### Kurakuran Daidaitawar Cloud

1. Tabbatar `BASE_URL` yana nuni zuwa instance ɗinka da ke aiki (misali, `http://localhost:20128`)
2. Tabbatar `CLOUD_URL` yana nuni zuwa endpoint na cloud ɗinka (misali, `https://omniroute.dev`)

### Cloud `stream=false` Yana Mayar da 500

**Alama:** `Unexpected token 'd'...` a endpoint na cloud don kiraye-kirayen da ba na streaming ba.

**Dalili:** Upstream yana mayar da payload na SSE yayin da client ke tsammanin JSON.

## Matsalolin Docker

### Docker IPv6 / Sake Saita Haɗi

<a name="docker-ipv6"></a>

**Alamomi:** `curl http://localhost:20128/v1/models` yana mayar da `curl: (56) Recv failure: Connection reset by peer`. Dashboard da endpoints marasa tantancewa suna aiki, amma endpoints masu tantancewa suna gaza — yana kama da matsalar tantancewa amma ba haka ba ne.

**Dalili:** `docker run -p 20128:20128` yana buɗe port a kan duka `0.0.0.0` (IPv4) da `::` (IPv6), amma process da ke cikin container yana sauraro a kan IPv4 kawai. A hosts inda `localhost` ke warwarewa zuwa `::1` da farko, haɗin yana isa ga port ɗin IPv6 da aka buɗe ba tare da listener a bayansa ba → sai a sake saita haɗin.

**Gyara:**

1. **Bincike mai sauri:** Gudanar da `curl -4 http://localhost:20128/v1/models`. Idan yana aiki da `-4` amma yana gaza ba tare da shi ba, kana da rashin daidaiton bind na IPv6.
2. **Gyara na dindindin:** Yi bind zuwa IPv4 kai tsaye ta amfani da `-p 127.0.0.1:20128:20128` a cikin umarnin `docker run` naka:
   ```bash
   docker run -d --name omniroute --restart unless-stopped --stop-timeout 40 \
     -p 127.0.0.1:20128:20128 -v omniroute-data:/app/data diegosouzapw/omniroute:latest
   ```
   Wannan yana tilasta bind na IPv4 kuma yana hana fallasa proxy a kan dukkan interfaces na host.

## Saitunan Juriya

### Iyakance ƙimar atomatik ba ya kunnawa

- Iyakance ƙimar atomatik yana aiki ne kawai ga masu samarwa masu amfani da maɓallin API (ba OAuth/biyan kuɗi ba)
- Tabbatar cewa an kunna iyakance ƙimar atomatik a **Settings → Resilience → Provider Profiles**
- Duba ko mai samarwar yana dawo da lambobin matsayi na `429` ko taken `Retry-After`

### Daidaita jinkirin exponential backoff

Bayanan martabar masu samarwa suna goyon bayan waɗannan saitunan:

- **Base delay** — Lokacin jira na farko bayan gazawar farko (tsoho: 1s)
- **Max delay** — Matsakaicin iyakar lokacin jira (tsoho: 30s)
- **Multiplier** — Adadin ƙara jinkiri ga kowace gazawa mai biyo baya (tsoho: 2x)

### Kariya daga cunkoson buƙatu lokaci guda

Lokacin da buƙatu masu yawa na lokaci guda suka isa ga mai samarwa da aka iyakance ƙimarsa, OmniRoute yana amfani da mutex + iyakance ƙimar atomatik don jera buƙatun ɗaya bayan ɗaya da hana gazawa mai yaɗuwa. Wannan yana faruwa ta atomatik ga masu samarwa masu amfani da maɓallin API.

### Buƙatun hira suna gazawa da 503 / chat_admission_busy

**Alamu:**

- Endpoint na kammala hira yana dawo da amsar `503` da za a iya sake gwadawa, wadda lambar kuskurenta ita ce
  `chat_admission_busy`.
- Amsar tana haɗe da `Retry-After`. Tun daga #12135 ana samo ƙimar daga cunkoson da aka lura da shi
  — mafi girma tsakanin taga ta `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS` da buƙatar ta riga ta
  jira da kuma tsawon lokacin da aka riƙe hayar buƙatu masu nauyi na yanzu — ana zagaye ta sama zuwa cikakkun
  sakanni kuma ana iyakance ta zuwa 60. A ƙofar da ba ta da aiki, ana riƙe mafi ƙarancin ƙimomin tarihi: sakanni 2 a
  hanyar da ta dogara da byte, da daƙiƙa 1 a hanyar da ta dogara da tsari (wadda kuma ta haɗa da
  `reason: "structure_limit"`).
- Wannan na iya faruwa yayin da wata hira mai nauyi ko amsar streaming mai ɗaukar lokaci mai tsawo har yanzu
  ke gudana.

Jikin amsar da ta dogara da byte shi ne:

```json
{
  "error": {
    "message": "Chat admission capacity is temporarily unavailable. Retry shortly.",
    "type": "server_error",
    "code": "chat_admission_busy"
  }
}
```

Amsar da ta dogara da tsari tana amfani da nau'i da lamba iri ɗaya, tare da saƙon
`Local chat admission capacity is busy for this structurally heavy request; upstream provider routing was not attempted. Retry shortly.`
da `reason: "structure_limit"`.
A matakan iyaka na tsoho, ana ɗaukar buƙata mai nauyin tsari idan tana da aƙalla saƙonni `200`,
aƙalla tools `64`, ko aƙalla kiyasin tokens `32,000`, ko kuma idan ƙididdigar tsari mai iyaka
ta kai iyakokinta na nodes `10,000` da aka ziyarta ko zurfin `12`.

**Dalili:** Wannan rage kaya ne da aka yi da gangan a cikin OmniRoute, ba gazawar mai samarwa na upstream ba.
Kowane process yana amfani da kariya ta cikin process don tanadar iyakantacciyar damar buƙatu masu nauyi kafin ya riƙe
kuma ya tantance babban jikin buƙata. Ana ci gaba da riƙe hayar buƙata mai nauyi har tsawon rayuwar amsar SSE.

**#503-fanout:** kafin wannan gyaran, kariyar tana iyakance concurrency da tsayayyen ADADIN buƙatu
(`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT`, tsoho `1`) ba tare da la'akari da ƙwaƙwalwar host ba, don haka fan-out na coding-agent
(subagents/CLIs da yawa, jikin buƙatu akai-akai > 256 KB) ya ragu zuwa ingantaccen
concurrency na ~1 kuma ya haifar da 503 a ƙarƙashin kaya na yau da kullum. Yanzu kariyar tana daidaita kanta: ana sarrafa ta
ta hanyar kasafin BYTE na ingest da aka samo ta atomatik (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES`) wanda aka ƙayyade daga
ainihin iyakar ƙwaƙwalwar process, kuma tana kuma duba siginar matsin albarkatu kai tsaye — don haka
tana rage kaya ne kawai lokacin da host yake ƙarƙashin matsin ƙwaƙwalwa na gaske, ba wai kawai saboda buƙatu
masu nauyi fiye da ɗaya sun zo lokaci guda ba. Har yanzu ana mutunta tsohuwar iyakar adadi (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT`),
amma sai idan ka saita ta kai tsaye.

Lokacin da damar ta cika, buƙata mai nauyi za ta fara jira har zuwa
`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS` (tsoho shi ne `RATE_LIMIT_MAX_WAIT_MS`; `0` yana kashe jiran) domin gurbi ya samu
kafin a mayar da amsar `503` da za a iya sake gwadawa. Wannan jira mai iyaka yana samuwa ne domin clients irin na agent
(OpenCode, Claude Code, Cursor) waɗanda ke aika ƙananan buƙatu masu nauyi da yawa lokaci guda su jera wannan cunkoso
maimakon ƙare dukkan kasafin sake gwadawarsu a kan ƙin amincewa nan take sannan su mutu a tsakiyar aiki.
Ana nuna yawan hayar buƙatu masu nauyi na yanzu, kasafin byte da aka warware, da tsananin matsin kai tsaye
a `GET /api/monitoring/health` → `chatAdmission` (`inflightBytes`, `maxInflightBytes`,
`budgetSource`, `pressureSeverity`, `countCapEnabled`) — duba waɗannan kafin ka canza kowace env var.
Settings → Resilience → Request Queue → Concurrent Requests ba ya sarrafa wannan; wannan saitin
yana sarrafa wata dabara ta daban ta jerin buƙatun mai samarwa.

**Gyara:**

1. Da farko a sake gwadawa. Ya kamata clients su mutunta `Retry-After` kuma su yi amfani da backoff maimakon sake
   tura buƙatar nan take.
2. Duba `/api/monitoring/health` → `chatAdmission` kafin daidaita komai. `countCapEnabled:
false` da `maxInflightBytes` mai yalwa suna nufin kasafin da aka samo ta atomatik yana riga yana yin
   aikinsa; `pressureSeverity` na `high`/`critical` yana nufin host ɗin yana da ƙarancin ƙwaƙwalwa na gaske —
   ba za a iya gyara wannan da env var na karɓar buƙata ba, yana buƙatar ƙarin RAM ko ƙaramin nauyin aiki.
3. Sai kawai idan `/api/monitoring/health` ya nuna cewa kasafin da aka samo ta atomatik ya yi ƙanƙanta da gaske ga
   host ɗinka (abu mai wuya — yana riga yana daidaituwa daga container zuwa bare-metal), ka maye gurbinsa kai tsaye da
   `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES` maimakon komawa ga tsohuwar iyakar adadin buƙatu.

Duba [jagorar environment-variable](../reference/ENVIRONMENT.md#4-security--authentication)
don saitunan karɓar buƙatu na hukuma.

## Rarrabuwar gazawar RAG / LLM ta zaɓi (matsaloli 16)

Wasu masu amfani da OmniRoute suna sanya gateway a gaban tsarin RAG ko na wakilai. A irin waɗannan saituna, ana yawan ganin wani baƙon tsari: OmniRoute yana nuna cewa komai yana aiki yadda ya kamata (masu samarwa suna aiki, bayanan martabar routing suna daidai, babu faɗakarwar iyakar amfani), amma har yanzu amsar ƙarshe ba daidai ba ce.

A aikace, waɗannan matsalolin galibi suna fitowa ne daga tsarin RAG na ƙasa, ba daga gateway ɗin kansa ba.

Idan kuna son amfani da ƙamus na bai ɗaya don bayyana waɗannan gazawar, za ku iya amfani da WFGY ProblemMap, wata kafa ta rubutu ta waje mai lasisin MIT wadda ke bayyana nau'ikan gazawar RAG / LLM guda goma sha shida da ke maimaituwa. A taƙaice, tana ƙunshe da:

- karkacewar retrieval da lalacewar iyakokin context
- indexes da vector stores marasa komai ko waɗanda suka tsufa
- rashin daidaito tsakanin embedding da ma'ana
- matsalolin haɗa prompt da context window
- rushewar dabaru da amsoshi masu nuna tabbaci fiye da kima
- gazawar dogayen sarƙoƙi da haɗin gwiwar wakilai
- karkacewar ƙwaƙwalwa da rawar wakilai da yawa
- matsalolin jerin deployment da bootstrap

Manufar mai sauƙi ce:

1. Lokacin da kuke binciken amsa mara kyau, tattara:
   - aikin mai amfani da buƙatarsa
   - haɗin route ko provider a OmniRoute
   - duk wani context na RAG da aka yi amfani da shi a ƙasa (takardun da aka samo, kiran kayan aiki, da sauransu)
2. Daidaita matsalar da lamba ɗaya ko biyu na WFGY ProblemMap (`No.1` … `No.16`).
3. Ajiye lambar a dashboard, runbook, ko tsarin bin diddigin matsalolinku kusa da logs na OmniRoute.
4. Yi amfani da shafin WFGY da ya dace don yanke shawarar ko kuna buƙatar canza tsarin RAG, retriever, ko dabarun routing ɗinku.

Cikakken rubutu da takamaiman hanyoyin aiwatarwa suna nan (lasisin MIT, rubutu kaɗai):

[README na WFGY ProblemMap](https://github.com/onestardao/WFGY/blob/main/ProblemMap/README.md)

Za ku iya yin watsi da wannan sashe idan ba ku gudanar da tsarin RAG ko na wakilai a bayan OmniRoute ba.

## Jinkirin Farawa / Ƙarewar Lokacin Jira

Idan CLI ya nuna `⚠ Server did not respond within 60s` amma uwar garken tana
aiki a zahiri, lokacin da aka ware wa gwajin shirye-shiryen ya yi gajarta ga muhallinka.

Wannan yana yawan faruwa a Windows (riga-kafi, masu sa ido kan tsarin fayiloli) ko kwantena
masu manyan ayyuka yayin farawa.

**Gyara — ƙara lokacin da aka ware:**

```bash
# Ta hanyar env var (yana dorewa a duk lokacin farawa):
export OMNIROUTE_READY_TIMEOUT_MS=180000   # mintuna 3
omniroute serve

# Ta hanyar alamar CLI (sau ɗaya kawai):
omniroute serve --ready-timeout 180000
```

Tsohon ƙima shi ne 60 000 ms (60 s). Gargadin na bayani ne kawai; uwar garken
za ta ci gaba da farawa a bango kuma za a iya samunta da zarar farawa ya kammala.

Duba [`docs/reference/ENVIRONMENT.md`](../reference/ENVIRONMENT.md) don cikakkun
bayanai game da `OMNIROUTE_READY_TIMEOUT_MS`.
