## Bayani Cikin Sauri

**Sabon mai amfani da OmniRoute ne?** Fara daga nan — waɗannan suna magance kashi 90% na matsaloli:

| Abin da nake gani                         | Abin da yake nufi                            | Abin da za a yi                                                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| "Ba a iya haɗawa"                         | OmniRoute ba ya aiki                         | Gudanar da `omniroute` ko `docker restart omniroute`                                                               |
| "Maɓallin API ba daidai ba ne"            | Maɓallinka ba daidai ba ne ko ya ƙare        | Sake kwafe maɓallin daga shafin yanar gizon mai samarwar                                                           |
| "An wuce iyakar amfani"                   | Kana aika buƙatu da yawa                     | Jira minti 1, ko yi amfani da `model: "auto"` don sauyawa ta atomatik                                              |
| "An wuce ƙa'idar kaso"                    | Ka ƙare ƙa'idar kaso ta kyauta/biya          | Haɗa ƙarin masu samarwa, ko yi amfani da masu samarwa kyauta (Kiro, Pollinations)                                  |
| "Amsoshi suna jinkiri"                    | Mai samarwar yana aiki sosai ko yana da nisa | Yi amfani da `model: "auto/fast"` ko haɗa mai samarwa mafi sauri (Groq, Cerebras)                                  |
| "An yi amfani da mai samarwa mara dacewa" | `auto` ya zaɓi wani mai samarwa daban        | Hakan al'ada ne! `auto` yana zaɓar mafi kyau. Tilasta amfani da takamaiman mai samarwa da `model: "openai/gpt-4o"` |

### Iyakance Yawan Buƙatu a Masu Samarwa Kyauta (429 / 400 / 401)

**Alama**: Lokacin amfani da `model: "auto"` tare da masu samarwa kyauta/marasa buƙatar tabbatar da izini (opencode, auggie, da sauransu), lokaci-lokaci kana samun `HTTP 429`, `400`, ko `401` maimakon amsoshi. Buƙatun suna yin nasara idan aka sake gwada saƙon nan da 'yan lokuta, amma sarrafa kansa (ayyukan cron, wakilai, rubutun umarni) yana lalacewa a gazawar farko.

**Tushen matsalar**: Yanayin gazawa uku masu zaman kansu suna haɗuwa:

1. **Iyakacin yawan buƙatun mai samarwa (`429`)**: Matakan amfani kyauta na iya tilasta ƙa'idar kaso ta kowane zangon lokaci. Tashin buƙatu masu gudana a lokaci guda yana ƙarar da ita, don haka za a ƙi buƙata ta gaba har sai zangon lokacin ya sake farawa.
2. **Samfuri mai matsala a passthrough (`400`/`401`)**: Tarin `auto/*` na iya haɗawa da samfuran passthrough daga `opencode` waɗanda aka yi wa rajista a kundin amma ba su da ingantattun bayanan shaida masu aiki (misali `oc/north-mini-code-free` → `401`). Auto-router yana gwada ɗaya, ya gaza, sannan kuskuren ya bazu kafin sauyawa ya fara aiki.
3. **Ƙaruwar haɗarin ayyuka masu gudana lokaci guda (`429` ƙarƙashin nauyi)**: Lokacin da zaman wakili/cron da yawa suka kira `auto` lokaci guda, jimillar yawan buƙatun tana wuce abin da masu samarwa kyauta za su iya jurewa, don haka ana yi wa ingantattun kira alama a matsayin cin zarafi.

**Tabbataccen gyara (al'umma ta bayar da rahoto, 2026-08-10)**: daidaita ma'aunin muhalli uku domin juyawa, ayyuka masu gudana lokaci guda, da sauyawa su shawo kan sauye-sauyen matakin kyauta maimakon tsarin ya mutu saboda su:

```bash
export OMNIROUTE_ROTATE_ON_400=true           # tsallaka zuwa wani samfuri/mai samarwa a 400/401 (yana tsallake samfuran passthrough masu matsala)
export OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT=4   # bayyanannen iyakar karɓar manyan buƙatu (ba a saita shi ta tsohuwa: babu iyakar adadin buƙatu, duba bayanin da ke ƙasa)
export OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS=5000 # tsawaita jira mai iyaka don ƙarfin manyan buƙatu maimakon 503 mai ba da damar sake gwadawa nan take
```

Saita waɗannan a cikin muhallin tsarin OmniRoute (daemon ɗin, misali ta LaunchAgent plist ko `systemctl edit`), sannan sake kunna OmniRoute. Tutocin juyawa shi ne zaɓi guda mafi tasiri: yana sauya gazawa kai tsaye zuwa sake gwadawa a ɓoye ta amfani da lafiyayyen mai samarwa a cikin tarin.

**Lura**: `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT` yana iyakance adadin manyan buƙatu — masu dogon mahallin bayanai — da ke gudana lokaci guda; iyakar ƙofar karɓa ce, ba mai iyakance yawan buƙatun mai samarwa ba. **Sabuntawar #503-fanout:** ba a ƙara saita wannan var ta tsohuwa (yanzu yana aiki ne kawai idan an saita shi a sarari, kamar yadda ke sama) — a maimakon haka, ana sarrafa karɓar manyan buƙatu ta kasafin baiti da aka samo ta atomatik (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES`) wanda yake daidaita kansa bisa ainihin iyakar ƙwaƙwalwar na'urar mai karɓar baƙunci, don haka sabon turawa ya kamata ya ga raguwar ƙin amincewa na `503 chat_admission_busy` sosai ba tare da saita wannan var ba; saita shi a sarari a nan har yanzu yana aiki daidai kamar yadda aka rubuta. Sauye-sauyen kasafin baiti da aka saita a sarari suna tsaya tsakanin 8 MiB–2 GiB. `413 body_exceeds_budget` ba matsala ce ta wucin gadi ba: ƙara kasafin baitin, rage `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_HARD_MAX_BODY_BYTES`, ko ƙara iyakar ƙwaƙwalwar tsarin. Sallamar `inflight_bytes_budget` cunkoso ne na wucin gadi kuma har yanzu ana iya sake gwadawa. Ana sarrafa iyakance yawan buƙatu ga kowane mai samarwa (`open-sse/services/rateLimitManager.ts`) daban ta `RATE_LIMIT_MAX_WAIT_MS`, `RATE_LIMIT_MAX_QUEUE_DEPTH`, da `RATE_LIMIT_AUTO_ENABLE` — duba `.env.example`.

**Yadda za a tabbatar cewa ya yi aiki**: gudanar da wakili/cron ɗinka sau biyu cikin sauri a jere sannan ka tabbatar duka sun yi nasara. Kafin gyaran, gudu na biyu yawanci yana jefa `429`/`401`. Bayan gyaran, ana sake gwada gazawa (idan akwai) a ɓoye kuma kiran yana kammala. Haka kuma za ka iya yin `curl /monitoring/health` sannan ka lura da filin `rateLimitedUntil` a haɗin masu samarwa da `circuitBreakers.providerBreakers[].state` na masu samarwar da abin ya shafa — halin yana ɗaya daga cikin `CLOSED`, `DEGRADED`, `OPEN`, ko `HALF_OPEN` (duba `src/shared/utils/circuitBreaker.ts`), kuma mai samarwar da ke ci gaba da gazawa zai sauya `CLOSED → DEGRADED → OPEN` kafin zangon sake farawa ya bar gwajin bincike ya wuce (`HALF_OPEN`).

**Idan har yanzu kana ganin 429**: asusun da yake aiki na wannan mai samarwar ya ƙare da ainihin _quota_ ɗinsa (ba iyakar yawan buƙatu kaɗai ba). Ƙara asusu na biyu na wannan mai samarwar a dashboard na OmniRoute → Providers → Accounts, ko haɗa wani mai samarwa na kyauta (misali, `routeway`, `auggie`). Juyawa tsakanin asusu yana taimakawa ne kawai kan iyakokin yawan buƙatu/400/401 na ɗan lokaci; ƙarewar quota gaba ɗaya tana buƙatar bayanan shaidar shiga na biyu ko wani mai samarwa daban.

**Idan kana ganin 403 a kan samfuran vision (`auto/vision`, `bazaarlink/*`)**: asusun da aka haɗa ba shi da tsarin biyan kuɗi da ya haɗa da vision, ko kuma API key ɗin ba shi da isassun izini. Tabbatar a dashboard na mai samarwar cewa scope na key ɗin ya haɗa da vision/multimodal, ko ka haɗa asusun matakin biyan kuɗi kuma ka bar shi a matsayin abin da aka nufa don vision.

## Gargadin npm install (ERESOLVE / peer / deprecated)

Lokacin da ka gudanar da `npm install -g omniroute`, za ka iya ganin tarin gargadi kamar `npm warn ERESOLVE`, sanarwar peer-dependency, da saƙonnin `deprecated`. **Ana tsammanin waɗannan kuma ba su da illa.** Shigarwar ta yi nasara idan ka ga `added <N> packages` a cikin sakamakon.

Don ɓoye gargadin warware peer-dependency, yi amfani da hanyar shigarwa da OmniRoute ke tallafawa:

```bash
npm install -g omniroute --legacy-peer-deps
```

`--legacy-peer-deps` yana ɓoye `ERESOLVE` da sanarwar peer-dependency ne kawai. Sanarwar daina tallafawa za ta ci gaba da bayyana saboda tana fitowa daga fakitin ɓangare na uku masu dogaro kai tsaye ko a kaikaice; ba ta nuna cewa shigarwar ta gaza ba.

Gargadin yana fitowa ne daga tsofaffin jeren sigar peer-dependency a cikin fakitin ɓangare na uku waɗanda OmniRoute ba ya sarrafawa:

1. **`marked-terminal` yana buƙatar `marked >=1 <16`, amma an samu `marked@18`** — yana aiki da kyau a zahiri; kawai dai jeren sigar peer na asalin fakitin ya tsufa.
2. **`deprecated prebuild-install@7.1.3`** — kayan taimako ne na ɗauko native-binary mai dogaro a kaikaice. Ba a
   amfani da shi wajen shigar da haɗin transport na `wreq-js` da aka ƙayyade sigarsa, kuma ba ya nuna cewa saitin transport
   na mai samar da web-cookie ya gaza.

**Ba a buƙatar wani mataki** — ba za a iya ɓoye gargadin gaba ɗaya ba sai an yi fork na fakitin asali.

## Gargaɗin Ƙarya na Antivirus

<a name="antivirus-false-positives"></a>

### Avast/AVG na keɓe `README.md` da `MD:HttpRequest-inf[Susp]`

**Wannan gargaɗin ƙarya ne. Babu abin da ya kamu da cuta, kuma ba a buƙatar ɗaukar wani mataki.**

Avast da AVG suna amfani da wata dabara ta hasashe wadda ke yi wa fayilolin rubutu na yau da kullum/Markdown masu ɗauke da hanyoyin haɗi da yawa masu kama da buƙatun HTTP alama. Ana haɗa `README.md` na OmniRoute a cikin kunshin npm (an jera shi a `package.json` → `files`), don haka yana sauka a `node_modules/omniroute/README.md` yayin shigarwa ta gama-gari — kuma yana ɗauke da misalai kusan 15 na `http://localhost:20128/...` (wuraren ƙarshen HTTP/SSE na MCP, URL ɗin `.well-known` na A2A, da misalan `curl`). Yawan waɗannan hanyoyin haɗin ya isa ya kunna wannan dabarar hasashe.

Idan wannan ya fara faruwa kwanan nan kawai: nau'in fayil ɗin bai canza ba. README ya faɗaɗa teburin wuraren ƙarshensa (an ƙara MCP HTTP + SSE + A2A) da ƙarin misalan `curl`, wanda ya sa ya wuce iyakar da aka saita.

Fayil ɗin takaddun bayani ne marar aiki, kuma ba shi da wani abun ciki da za a iya aiwatarwa. Za ku iya dawo da shi daga keɓewa cikin aminci.

**Abin da za a yi:**

1. **Dakatar da sanarwar** — cire kundin shigarwar daga binciken antivirus ɗinku
   (Avast: Settings → Exceptions), ta ƙara hanyar `node_modules` ta gama-gari da/ko kundin
   bayanan OmniRoute (`~/.omniroute/`).
2. **Kai rahoton gargaɗin ƙarya** — <https://www.avast.com/false-positive-file-form.php>,
   tare da haɗa `README.md` da aka keɓe. Wannan shi ne gyaran da ke taimaka wa kowa, domin
   dabarar hasashen kamfanin ce ke wuce gona da iri kan fayil ɗin rubutu.

**Dalilin da ya sa ba ma “gyara” wannan a ɓangarenmu:** duk misalan `http://localhost` ne,
kuma localhost ba zai iya zama `https` ba tare da matsalolin takardar shaida da aka sanya wa hannu da kai ba. Gurɓata takardun bayani don kauce wa dabarar hasashen kamfani guda zai cutar da kowane mai karatu domin biyan buƙatar kuskuren na'urar bincike.

### Kaspersky na yi wa manhajar Desktop alama da `PDM:Trojan.Win32.Generic`

**Wannan gargaɗin ƙarya ne daga dabarar hasashen ɗabi'a. Babu abin da ya kamu da cuta.** Farkon kalmar `PDM:` ta Kaspersky na nufin hukuncin ya fito daga Proactive Defense Module (System Watcher), wanda ke tantance abin da mai shigarwar _yake yi_ maimakon kwatanta shi da sanannun manhajojin ɓarna. Idan ya kunna, Kaspersky yana “mayar da baya” ga dukkan shigarwar — yana share fayilolin da ya riga ya rubuta — don haka manhajar za ta lalace ko ta ɓace.

Fayilolin da yake yi wa alama daidaitattun ɓangarori ne na dogaro na buɗaɗɗen tushe da aka bayyana kuma aka haɗa da manhajar desktop, misali:

- `resources/app/.build/next/node_modules/playwright-<hash>/lib/…/agentParser.js` da
  `workerProcessEntry.js` — [Playwright](https://playwright.dev), ɗakin karatu na sarrafa
  burauza ta atomatik da ake amfani da shi don shiga wurin mai samarwa daga cikin manhaja da tattaunawa mai amfani da burauza.
- `resources/app/.build/next/node_modules/@wreq-js/binding-win32-<arch>-msvc-<hash>/wreq-js.win32-<arch>-msvc.node`
  — haɗin ginin asali na `wreq-js` da aka ƙayyade wanda ake amfani da shi don HTTP mai
  kwaikwayon sawun yatsan burauza kan masu samarwa masu amfani da kukin yanar gizo
  (`<arch>` shi ne `x64` ko `arm64`).

**Dalilin da ya sa yake kunna:** **har yanzu ba a sanya wa** mai shigarwar Windows **sa hannun lamba ba**, don haka mai shigarwar NSIS marar sa hannu ba shi da wani suna na amincewa, kuma dabarun hasashen ɗabi'a suna aiki da tsananin ƙarfi. Idan aka haɗa wannan da DLL na asali da aka haɗa cikin kunshin da ɗaruruwan fayilolin `.js` da aka rubuta a ƙarƙashin `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\OmniRoute` (ciki har da kundin kunshe-kunshe masu ƙarewa da hash daga ginin standalone na Next.js), hakan ya isa ya kunna dabarar hasashen. An shirya sanya sa hannun lamba; har sai an aiwatar da shi, sabbin fitarwa za su iya maimaita wannan.

**Abin da za a yi:**

1. **Da farko ku tabbatar da sahihancin abin da kuka sauke** (wannan yana kawar da yiwuwar an yi wa fayil ɗin kutse). Kowane fitarwa yana wallafa
   `latest.yml`, wanda filin `sha512` dinsa (base64) ke rufe mai shigarwar
   `OmniRoute.Setup.<version>.exe`. A PowerShell, daga babban fayil ɗin da ke ɗauke da mai shigarwar:
   ```powershell
   $b = [System.Security.Cryptography.SHA512]::Create().ComputeHash(
     [System.IO.File]::ReadAllBytes("$PWD\OmniRoute.Setup.<version>.exe"))
   [Convert]::ToBase64String($b)
   ```
   Dole ne sakamakon ya yi daidai da `latest.yml` → `sha512`. Idan bai yi daidai ba, ku share fayil ɗin sannan
   ku sake sauke shi kawai daga [shafin fitarwa na GitHub](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/releases).
2. **Dawo da su + cire su daga bincike** — dawo da abubuwan da aka mayar da baya daga keɓewa sannan ku ƙara
   keɓancewa ga `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\OmniRoute` (Kaspersky → Settings → Threats and Exclusions),
   sannan ku sake shigarwa.
3. **Kai rahoton gargaɗin ƙarya** — <https://opentip.kaspersky.com/>. Rahotannin FP da masu amfani
   suka aika suna hanzarta saka fayil ɗin cikin jerin amintattu da gaske.

## Daidaituwar Node.js

<a name="nodejs-compatibility"></a>

### Shafin shiga yana rushewa ko yana nuna kuskuren "Module self-registration"

**Dalili:** Kana amfani da sigar Node.js da ba ta cikin mafi ƙarancin matakin tsaro da OmniRoute ya amince da shi. Abin da ya fi faruwa shi ne amfani da tsohuwar sigar gyaran Node 22 ko 24 wadda take ƙasa da mafi ƙarancin matakin tsaron da OmniRoute ke buƙata.

**Alamomi:**

- Shafin shiga yana nuna farin allo ko kuskuren uwar garke
- Console yana nuna `Error: Module did not self-register` ko makamantan kurakuran native binding
- Shafin shiga yana nuna **tutar gargaɗi mai launin lemu** tare da sigar Node ɗinka idan runtime ɗin ba ya cikin ƙa'idar tsaro da ake tallafawa

**Gyara:**

1. Shigar da sigar Node.js LTS da ake tallafawa (shawara: Node.js 24.x):
   ```bash
   nvm install 24
   nvm use 24
   ```
2. Tabbatar da sigarka: `node --version` ya kamata ya nuna `v24.0.0` ko mafi sabuwa a layin 24.x LTS
3. Sake shigar da OmniRoute: `npm install -g omniroute`
4. Sake kunnawa: `omniroute`

> **Amintattun sigogin da ake tallafawa:** `>=22.22.2 <23` ko `>=24.0.0 <27`. Node.js 24.x LTS (Krypton) da Node.js 26 suna samun cikakken tallafi.

### npm v11+: Ba a shigar da `better-sqlite3` ba (Cannot find module)

<a name="npm-v11-better-sqlite3-not-installed-cannot-find-module"></a>

**Dalili:** npm v11 (wanda ke zuwa tare da Node.js 24+) yana toshe scripts na shigarwa don optional
dependencies ta tsoho. Saboda an jera `better-sqlite3` a cikin `optionalDependencies`
kuma yana buƙatar native compilation (`node-gyp rebuild`), npm yana tsallake shi ba tare da sanarwa ba.

**Alamomi:**

- Uwar garke tana rushewa yayin farawa tare da `Cannot find module 'better-sqlite3'`
- `ls node_modules/better-sqlite3` yana nuna "No such file or directory"
- `npm ls better-sqlite3` yana nuna `(empty)`

**Gyara:**

1. Amince da scripts na shigarwa sannan ka sake shigarwa:
   ```bash
   npm approve-scripts better-sqlite3
   npm install
   ```
2. Ko ka shigar da prebuilt ɗin da hannu:
   ```bash
   npm pack better-sqlite3@13.0.1
   tar -xzf better-sqlite3-*.tgz -C node_modules
   mv node_modules/package node_modules/better-sqlite3
   rm better-sqlite3-*.tgz
   ```
3. Tabbatar cewa yana aiki: `node -e "require('better-sqlite3')(':memory:').close(); console.log('OK')"`

### macOS: `dlopen` / "slice is not valid mach-o file"

<a name="macos-native-module-rebuild"></a>

**Dalili:** Bayan yin `npm install -g omniroute` na global, mai yiwuwa an haɗa native binary na `better-sqlite3` da ke cikin package ɗin don wata architecture ko Node.js ABI dabam da wadda ke aiki a tsarin na gida. Wannan ya zama ruwan dare a macOS (duka Apple Silicon da Intel) idan pre-built binary bai dace da environment ɗinka ba.

**Alamomi:**

- Uwar garke tana kasa farawa nan take tare da kuskuren `dlopen`
- Kuskuren yana ɗauke da `slice is not valid mach-o file`
- Cikakken misali:

```
dlopen(/Users/<user>/.nvm/versions/node/v24.14.1/lib/node_modules/omniroute/app/node_modules/better-sqlite3/build/Release/better_sqlite3.node, 0x0001): tried: '...' (slice is not valid mach-o file)
```

**Gyara — sake haɗawa don environment ɗinka na gida (ba sai an saukar da sigar Node.js ba):**

```bash
cd $(npm root -g)/omniroute/app
npm rebuild better-sqlite3
omniroute
```

> **Lura:** Wannan yana sake haɗa native binding ɗin daidai da sigar Node.js da CPU architecture na tsarinka na gida, yana magance rashin daidaiton binary. Kewayon runtime da ake tallafawa a hukumance shi ne **`>=22.22.2 <23` ko `>=24.0.0 <27`** (`SUPPORTED_NODE_RANGE` a cikin `src/shared/utils/nodeRuntimeSupport.ts`, wanda aka daidaita da filin `engines` na `package.json`). Node.js 24.x LTS (Krypton) da Node.js 26 suna samun cikakken tallafi tare da `better-sqlite3` v12.x.

## Matsalolin Proxy

<a name="proxy-issues"></a>

### Tabbatar da mai samarwa yana nuna "fetch failed"

**Dalili:** A baya, endpoint ɗin tabbatar da maɓallin API (`POST /api/providers/validate`) yana ƙetare saitunan proxy, wanda ke haifar da gazawa a muhallan da ke buƙatar bi ta proxy.

**Gyara (v3.5.5+):** Yanzu an gyara wannan. Tabbatar da mai samarwa yana bi ta `runWithProxyContext`, tare da mutunta saitunan proxy na matakin mai samarwa da na gaba ɗaya kai tsaye.

### Binciken lafiyar token yana gazawa da "fetch failed"

**Dalili:** Sabunta token na OAuth a bango ba ya tantance saitunan proxy ga kowace haɗi.

**Gyara (v3.5.5+):** Yanzu mai tsara binciken lafiyar token yana tantance saitunan proxy ga kowace haɗi kafin ƙoƙarin sabuntawa. Sabunta zuwa v3.5.5+.

### Proxy na SOCKS5 yana dawo da "invalid onRequestStart method"

**Dalili:** A kan Node.js 22, dispatcher na undici@8 bai dace da ginannen aiwatarwar `fetch()` ta Node ba.

**Gyara (v3.5.5+):** Yanzu OmniRoute yana amfani da aikin `fetch()` na undici da kansa idan dispatcher na proxy yana aiki, don tabbatar da daidaitaccen aiki. Sabunta zuwa v3.5.5+.

### Proxy na MITM a ƙarƙashin WSL: ba a kama manhajojin tebur da ke kan Windows host

**Dalili:** Ana shigar da proxy na MITM da takardar shaidarsa ta CA cikin muhallin da OmniRoute yake gudana. A ƙarƙashin WSL, wannan muhallin shi ne Linux guest, yayin da manhajojin AI na tebur (Kiro, Trae, Copilot, Zed, …) suke gudana a kan Windows host. Manhajojin host ba su amince da ma'ajiyar takardun shaida ta guest ba, kuma ba sa bi ta proxy na tsarin guest, don haka kamawar zirga-zirgar manhajojin tebur ba ta aiki a can.

**Shawara:** Gudanar da OmniRoute kai tsaye a kan OS ɗaya da manhajojin tebur da kake son kama zirga-zirgarsu (Windows don manhajojin Windows; haka nan ga macOS/Linux). Barin OmniRoute a cikin WSL yayin da ake niyyar manhajojin host yana buƙatar amincewa da takardar shaidar CA da aka ƙirƙira da hannu a kan Windows host da kuma saita cibiyar sadarwa/proxy ta kowace manhajar host zuwa endpoint ɗin proxy na WSL — tsari ne marar tallafi kuma mai saurin lalacewa.

## Matsalolin Docker

### Docker IPv6 / Sake Saitin Haɗi

<a name="docker-ipv6"></a>

**Alamomi:** `curl http://localhost:20128/v1/models` yana mayar da `curl: (56) Recv failure: Connection reset by peer`. Dashboard da endpoints marasa buƙatar tantancewa suna aiki, amma endpoints masu buƙatar tantancewa suna gaza — yana kama da matsalar auth amma ba haka ba ne.

**Dalili:** `docker run -p 20128:20128` yana buɗe tashar a kan duka `0.0.0.0` (IPv4) da `::` (IPv6), amma process da ke cikin container yana sauraro a IPv4 kawai. A hosts inda `localhost` ke fara komawa zuwa `::1`, haɗin yana isa tashar IPv6 da aka buɗe ba tare da listener a bayanta ba → ana sake saita haɗin.

**Gyara:**

1. **Bincike cikin sauri:** Gudanar da `curl -4 http://localhost:20128/v1/models`. Idan yana aiki da `-4` amma yana gaza ba tare da shi ba, kana da rashin daidaituwar bind na IPv6.
2. **Gyara na dindindin:** Yi bind zuwa IPv4 kai tsaye ta amfani da `-p 127.0.0.1:20128:20128` a cikin umarnin `docker run` naka:
   ```bash
   docker run -d --name omniroute --restart unless-stopped --stop-timeout 40 \
     -p 127.0.0.1:20128:20128 -v omniroute-data:/app/data diegosouzapw/omniroute:latest
   ```
   Wannan yana tilasta bind na IPv4 kuma yana hana fallasa proxy a duk interfaces na host.

## Debugging

### Kunna Log Files

Saita `APP_LOG_TO_FILE=true` a cikin fayil ɗin `.env` naka. Ana rubuta logs na application a ƙarƙashin `logs/`.
Ana adana artifacts na requests a ƙarƙashin `${DATA_DIR}/call_logs/` lokacin da aka kunna call log pipeline
a cikin settings.
Lokacin da aka kunna pipeline capture, saita `CALL_LOG_PIPELINE_CAPTURE_STREAM_CHUNKS=false` don cire
payloads na stream chunks, ko daidaita `CALL_LOG_PIPELINE_MAX_SIZE_KB` don sauya iyakar artifact a KB.

### Duba Lafiyar Provider

```bash
# Dashboard na lafiya
http://localhost:20128/dashboard/health

# Binciken lafiyar API
curl http://localhost:20128/api/monitoring/health
```

### Ma'ajiyar Runtime

- Babban state: `${DATA_DIR}/storage.sqlite` (providers, combos, aliases, keys, settings)
- Amfani: Teburan SQLite a cikin `storage.sqlite` (`usage_history`, `call_logs`, `proxy_logs`) + `${DATA_DIR}/call_logs/` na zaɓi
- Logs na application: `<repo>/logs/...` (lokacin da `APP_LOG_TO_FILE=true`)
- Artifacts na call log: `${DATA_DIR}/call_logs/YYYY-MM-DD/...` lokacin da aka kunna call log pipeline

Aikin **Clean history** na shafin Request Logs yana share `call_logs`, tsohon
`request_detail_logs`, da directory na artifacts na gida `${DATA_DIR}/call_logs/`.

### Matsalolin tsari da aka saba samu

- **Thinking tags ba sa bayyana** — Bincika ko mai samarwar da aka nufa yana tallafa wa thinking da saitin thinking budget
- **Tool calls suna ɓacewa** — Wasu fassarorin tsari na iya cire fields da ba a tallafa musu ba; tabbatar a yanayin Playground
- **System prompt ya ɓace** — Claude da Gemini suna sarrafa system prompts ta hanyoyi daban-daban; duba sakamakon fassarar
- **SDK yana dawo da raw string maimakon object** — An warware a v1.x; response sanitizer yana cire fields marasa daidaito (`x_groq`, `usage_breakdown`, da sauransu) waɗanda suke haifar da gazawar tabbatarwar OpenAI SDK Pydantic. Idan har yanzu kana ganin wannan a v3.x+, da fatan za ka buɗe rahoton matsala.
- **GLM/ERNIE yana ƙin rawar `system`** — An warware a v1.x; role normalizer yana haɗa system messages kai tsaye cikin user messages ga models marasa jituwa. Idan har yanzu kana ganin wannan a v3.x+, da fatan za ka buɗe rahoton matsala.
- **Ba a gane rawar `developer` ba** — An warware a v1.x; ana sauya ta kai tsaye zuwa `system` ga masu samarwa waɗanda ba OpenAI ba. Idan har yanzu kana ganin wannan a v3.x+, da fatan za ka buɗe rahoton matsala.
- **`json_schema` ba ya aiki da Gemini** — An warware a v1.x; yanzu ana sauya `response_format` zuwa `responseMimeType` + `responseSchema` na Gemini. Idan har yanzu kana ganin wannan a v3.x+, da fatan za ka buɗe rahoton matsala.

## Saitunan Jure Matsala

### Ƙayyade yawan buƙatu ta atomatik ba ya aiki

- Ƙayyade yawan buƙatu ta atomatik yana aiki ne kawai ga masu samarwa masu amfani da maɓallin API (ba OAuth/biyan kuɗi ba)
- Tabbatar cewa an kunna ƙayyade yawan buƙatu ta atomatik a **Settings → Resilience → Provider Profiles**
- Bincika ko mai samarwar yana mayar da lambobin matsayi na `429` ko kanun `Retry-After`

### Daidaita jinkirin exponential backoff

Bayanan martabar masu samarwa suna goyon bayan waɗannan saituna:

- **Jinkirin farko** — Lokacin jira na farko bayan gazawar farko (tsoho: 1s)
- **Matsakaicin jinkiri** — Iyakar mafi tsawon lokacin jira (tsoho: 30s)
- **Mai ninkawa** — Adadin ƙara jinkiri ga kowace gazawa mai biyo baya (tsoho: 2x)

### Hana cunkoson buƙatu lokaci guda

Lokacin da buƙatu masu yawa na lokaci guda suka isa ga mai samarwa da aka ƙayyade yawan buƙatunsa, OmniRoute yana amfani da mutex + ƙayyade yawan buƙatu ta atomatik domin jera buƙatun ɗaya bayan ɗaya da hana gazawa mai sarƙaƙiya. Wannan yana faruwa ta atomatik ga masu samarwa masu amfani da maɓallin API.

### Buƙatun taɗi suna gazawa da 503 / chat_admission_busy

**Alamomi:**

- Ƙarshen API na kammala taɗi yana mayar da amsar `503` da za a iya sake gwadawa, wadda lambar kuskurenta ita ce
  `chat_admission_busy`.
- Amsar tana ƙunshe da `Retry-After`. Tun daga #12135, ana samo ƙimar daga cunkoson da aka lura da shi
  — ana ɗaukar mafi girma tsakanin taga ta `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS` da buƙatar ta riga
  ta jira da kuma tsawon lokacin da lasisin buƙatu masu nauyi na yanzu suka kasance a riƙe — sannan a ɗaga zuwa cikakkun
  daƙiƙu kuma a iyakance zuwa 60. A ƙofa marar aiki, tana kiyaye tsoffin mafi ƙarancin ƙimomi: daƙiƙa 2 a
  hanyar da ta dogara da bytes, daƙiƙa 1 a hanyar da ta dogara da tsari (wadda kuma ta haɗa da
  `reason: "structure_limit"`).
- Wannan na iya faruwa yayin da wani taɗi mai nauyi ko amsar streaming mai ɗaukar lokaci mai tsawo har yanzu
  take kan aiki.

Jikin amsar da ya dogara da bytes shi ne:

```json
{
  "error": {
    "message": "Chat admission capacity is temporarily unavailable. Retry shortly.",
    "type": "server_error",
    "code": "chat_admission_busy"
  }
}
```

Amsar da ta dogara da tsari tana amfani da nau'i da lamba iri ɗaya, tare da saƙon
`Local chat admission capacity is busy for this structurally heavy request; upstream provider routing was not attempted. Retry shortly.`
da kuma `reason: "structure_limit"`.
A iyakokin tsoho, ana ɗaukar buƙata a matsayin mai nauyin tsari idan tana da aƙalla saƙonni `200`,
aƙalla kayan aiki `64`, ko aƙalla kimanin tokens `32,000`, ko kuma idan ƙididdigar tsari mai iyaka
ta kai iyakarta ta nodes `10,000` da aka ziyarta ko zurfin `12`.

**Dalili:** Wannan sauke nauyi ne da aka yi da gangan a cikin OmniRoute, ba gazawar mai samarwa na sama ba.
Kowane process yana amfani da kariya ta cikin process domin keɓe iyakantacciyar damar buƙatu masu nauyi kafin riƙewa
da sarrafa babban jikin buƙata. Lasisin buƙata mai nauyi yana ci gaba da kasancewa a riƙe har tsawon rayuwar amsar SSE.

**#Rarrabawar-503:** kafin wannan gyara, kariyar tana iyakance yawan aiki lokaci guda bisa tsayayyen ADADIN buƙatu
(`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT`, tsoho `1`) ba tare da la'akari da ƙwaƙwalwar host ba, saboda haka rarrabawar
wakilan coding (subagents/CLIs masu yawa, tare da jikin buƙatu da akai-akai ya fi 256 KB) ta durƙushe zuwa ingantaccen
yawan aiki lokaci guda na kusan 1 kuma ta haifar da 503 a ƙarƙashin nauyin aiki na yau da kullum. Yanzu kariyar tana
daidaita kanta: ana sarrafa ta ta hanyar kasafin BYTE na ingest da ake samarwa ta atomatik
(`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES`) wanda aka ƙayyade daga ainihin iyakar ƙwaƙwalwar process,
kuma tana kuma duba siginar matsin albarkatu kai tsaye — don haka tana sauke nauyi ne kawai lokacin da host
ke fuskantar matsin ƙwaƙwalwa na gaske, ba kawai saboda buƙatu masu nauyi fiye da ɗaya sun iso lokaci guda ba.
Har yanzu ana mutunta tsohuwar iyakar adadi (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT`), amma sai idan ka saita ta a fili.

Lokacin da damar ta cika, buƙata mai nauyi za ta fara jira har zuwa
`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS` (tsoho `2000`, `0` yana kashe jiran) domin wani gurbi ya samu
kafin ta mayar da `503` da za a iya sake gwadawa. Wannan jira mai iyaka yana wanzuwa ne domin abokan hulɗa irin na wakili
(OpenCode, Claude Code, Cursor) waɗanda ke rarraba ƙananan buƙatu masu nauyi lokaci guda su jera cunkoson
ɗaya bayan ɗaya maimakon ƙare dukkan kasafin sake gwadawarsu kan ƙin karɓa nan take sannan su mutu a tsakiyar aiki.
Ana nuna cunkoson lasisin buƙatu masu nauyi na yanzu, kasafin bytes da aka tantance, da tsananin matsin kai tsaye
a `GET /api/monitoring/health` → `chatAdmission` (`inflightBytes`, `maxInflightBytes`,
`budgetSource`, `pressureSeverity`, `countCapEnabled`) — bincika waɗannan kafin canza kowace env var.
Settings → Resilience → Request Queue → Concurrent Requests ba ya sarrafa wannan; wannan saitin
yana sarrafa wata dabam hanyar jerin gwano na buƙatun mai samarwa.

**Gyara:**

1. Da farko a sake gwadawa. Ya kamata abokan hulɗa su bi `Retry-After` kuma su yi amfani da backoff maimakon
   maimaita buƙatar nan take.
2. Bincika `/api/monitoring/health` → `chatAdmission` kafin daidaita komai. `countCapEnabled:
false` da `maxInflightBytes` mai yalwa suna nufin kasafin da aka samar ta atomatik ya riga yana
   yin aikinsa; `pressureSeverity` na `high`/`critical` yana nufin host ɗin na fama da ƙarancin ƙwaƙwalwa na gaske
   — ba za a iya gyara wannan da admission env var ba, yana buƙatar ƙarin RAM ko ƙaramin nauyin aiki.
3. Sai kawai idan `/api/monitoring/health` ya nuna cewa kasafin da aka samar ta atomatik ya yi ƙanƙanta da gaske ga
   host ɗinka (abu mai wuya — ya riga yana daidaitawa daga container zuwa bare-metal), ka maye gurbinsa kai tsaye da
   `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES` maimakon komawa ga tsohuwar iyakar adadin buƙatu.

Duba [jagorar environment-variable](../reference/ENVIRONMENT.md#4-security--authentication)
don sahihan saitunan admission.

## Zaɓaɓɓen tsarin rarraba gazawar RAG / LLM (matsaloli 16)

Wasu masu amfani da OmniRoute suna sanya gateway a gaban tsarin RAG ko na agents. A irin waɗannan tsare-tsaren, ana yawan ganin wani yanayi mai ban mamaki: OmniRoute yana kama da yana aiki yadda ya kamata (providers suna aiki, routing profiles suna daidai, babu faɗakarwar rate limit), amma amsar ƙarshe har yanzu ba daidai ba ce.

A aikace, waɗannan matsalolin galibi suna fitowa ne daga tsarin RAG na downstream, ba daga gateway ɗin kansa ba.

Idan kana son ƙamus na bai ɗaya don bayyana waɗannan gazawar, za ka iya amfani da WFGY ProblemMap, wata maɓuɓɓugar rubutu ta waje mai lasisin MIT wadda ke bayyana nau'ikan gazawar RAG / LLM guda goma sha shida da ke maimaituwa. A matakin gaba ɗaya, tana ƙunshe da:

- karkacewar retrieval da lalacewar iyakokin context
- indexes da vector stores marasa komai ko tsofaffi
- rashin daidaito tsakanin embedding da ma'ana
- matsalolin haɗa prompt da context window
- rushewar logic da amsoshi masu nuna tabbaci fiye da kima
- gazawar dogon chain da daidaitawar agents
- karkacewar memory da roles na multi agent
- matsalolin jerin deployment da bootstrap

Manufar mai sauƙi ce:

1. Lokacin da kake binciken amsa mara kyau, tattara:
   - aikin mai amfani da request
   - haɗin route ko provider a cikin OmniRoute
   - duk wani RAG context da aka yi amfani da shi a downstream (retrieved documents, tool calls, da sauransu)
2. Daidaita matsalar da lamba ɗaya ko biyu na WFGY ProblemMap (`No.1` … `No.16`).
3. Ajiye lambar a cikin dashboard, runbook, ko incident tracker naka kusa da logs na OmniRoute.
4. Yi amfani da shafin WFGY da ya dace don yanke shawarar ko kana buƙatar sauya RAG stack, retriever, ko routing strategy naka.

Cikakken rubutu da takamaiman hanyoyin aiwatarwa suna nan (lasisin MIT, rubutu kawai):

[WFGY ProblemMap README](https://github.com/onestardao/WFGY/blob/main/ProblemMap/README.md)

Za ka iya yin watsi da wannan sashe idan ba ka gudanar da RAG ko agent pipelines a bayan OmniRoute.

## Sanannun Matsalolin v3.8.0

Matsalolin da suka shafi sakin v3.8.0 musamman da hanyoyin warware su na yanzu. Idan gyara ya zo a wani patch na gaba, za a sabunta ko a cire bayanin.

### Gazawar auth ta Devin CLI

**Alamomi:**

- "Ba a sami Devin CLI ba" ko "auth ya gaza" lokacin kiran tools da Devin ke tallafawa
- Binciken runtime na CLI yana bayar da rahoton `installed=false`

**Dalilai:**

- `CLI_DEVIN_BIN` yana nuna wani path da babu shi
- Ba a shigar da Devin CLI a host ɗin ba

**Gyara:**

1. Shigar da Devin CLI na platform ɗinka
2. Saita `CLI_DEVIN_BIN=/usr/local/bin/devin` (ko ainihin path ɗin) a cikin `.env`
3. Sake kunna OmniRoute sannan ka sake gwadawa daga **Dashboard → CLI Tools**

### Cooldown na model ya makale (sake saiti da hannu)

**Alamomi:**

- Model yana ci gaba da bayyana a matsayin yana cikin cooldown ko da bayan lokacin ƙarewar ya wuce
- Requests har yanzu suna tsallake model ɗin a combo routing duk da cewa timestamp ɗin yana baya

**Sake saiti da hannu:**

- **Dashboard:** **Settings → Model Cooldowns** → danna **Re-enable** a kan card ɗin da abin ya shafa
- **API:** `DELETE /api/resilience/model-cooldowns` tare da management auth headers

### Haɗin Command Code provider yana gazawa da 403

**Alamomi:**

- 403 lokacin gwada haɗin Command Code provider
- Card ɗin provider yana nuna "unauthorized" bayan sabon ƙarawa

**Dalili:** OAuth flow bai kammala ba (ba a karɓi callback ba ko ba a adana token ba).

**Gyara:**

- Gudanar da `omniroute providers` daga CLI don sake kunna OAuth flow, ko
- Sake gudanar da OAuth daga **Dashboard → Providers → Command Code → Reconnect**

### ModelScope yana dawo da cooldowns na 429 masu tsauri

**Alamomi:**

- Cooldowns masu gajarta sosai ko na nan take a ModelScope bayan ƙaramin tarin requests
- Combo routing yana tsallake ModelScope da wuri fiye da yadda ake tsammani

**Dalili:** ModelScope yana fitar da headers na `Retry-After` na musamman ga provider. v3.8.0 ya zo da keɓaɓɓen tsarin sarrafa waɗannan headers, saboda haka tsofaffin versions suna fassara su ba daidai ba a matsayin alamomin rate-limit na gaba ɗaya.

**Gyara:**

- Tabbatar cewa kana amfani da v3.8.0 ko wanda ya fi haka sabo
- Tabbatar cewa an kunna toggle na `useUpstream429BreakerHints` a ƙarƙashin **Settings → Resilience**

### Babu OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET a production

**Alamomi:**

- 401 a kan kowane Codex/Responses WebSocket bridge request lokacin aiki a remote production host
- WebSocket bridge handshake yana rufewa nan take bayan haɗawa

**Dalili:** Babu env var na `OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET` a production environment.

**Gyara:**

1. Ƙirƙiri random secret: `openssl rand -hex 32`
2. Saita `OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET=<random-secret>` a cikin env na production server (da duk wani client da ke sadarwa da bridge)
3. Sake kunna OmniRoute

### Responses API: background mode ya koma synchronous

**Alamomi:**

- An rubuta gargaɗi a log: `background mode degraded to synchronous`
- Request mai `background: true` yana dawo da synchronous response na yau da kullum maimakon background job handle

**Dalili:** A v3.8.0, da gangan ake mayar da `background: true` na Responses API zuwa synchronous execution tare da fitar da gargaɗi. Cikakken async background execution wani abin da za a samar ne a gaba.

**Gyara:**

- Daidaita client don yin kira ba tare da `background` ba, ko
- Jira wani release na gaba da zai zo da cikakken async background mode (bi diddigin changelog)

## Jinkirin Farawa / Ƙarewar Lokacin Jira

Idan CLI ya nuna `⚠ Server did not respond within 60s` amma a zahiri uwar garken
yana aiki, wa'adin jiran binciken shiri ya yi gajarta ga muhallinka.

Wannan yawanci yana faruwa a Windows (riga-kafi, masu sa ido kan tsarin fayiloli) ko kwantena
masu nauyin ayyukan farawa.

**Gyara — ƙara wa'adin jira:**

```bash
# Ta hanyar env var (yana dorewa a duk lokacin farawa):
export OMNIROUTE_READY_TIMEOUT_MS=180000   # mintuna 3
omniroute serve

# Ta hanyar tutar CLI (na lokaci ɗaya):
omniroute serve --ready-timeout 180000
```

Tsohon ƙima shi ne 60 000 ms (60 s). Gargaɗin na bayani ne kawai; uwar garken
zai ci gaba da farawa a bango kuma za a iya isa gare shi da zarar farawa ya kammala.

Duba [`docs/reference/ENVIRONMENT.md`](../reference/ENVIRONMENT.md) don cikakkun
bayanai game da `OMNIROUTE_READY_TIMEOUT_MS`.
