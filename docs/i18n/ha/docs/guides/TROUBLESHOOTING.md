## Cikakken Jagorar Warware Matsala

### Taƙaita Yawan Buƙatu a Masu Bayarwa na Kyauta (429 / 400 / 401)

**Alama**: Lokacin amfani da `model: "auto"` tare da masu bayarwa na kyauta/marasa buƙatar tantancewa (opencode, auggie, da sauransu), lokaci-lokaci kana samun `HTTP 429`, `400`, ko `401` maimakon amsoshi. Buƙatun suna nasara idan aka sake gwada wannan saƙon bayan ɗan lokaci, amma sarrafa aiki ta atomatik (cron jobs, agents, scripts) yana katsewa a gazawar farko.

**Asalin matsalar**: Hanyoyin gazawa guda uku masu zaman kansu suna taruwa:

1. **Iyakar yawan buƙatun mai bayarwa (`429`)**: Matakan kyauta na iya tilasta ƙayyadadden kaso a kowane zangon lokaci. Aika tarin kiraye-kiraye a lokaci guda yana ƙare shi, don haka za a ƙi buƙata ta gaba har sai zangon ya sake farawa.
2. **Samfuri mara aiki a passthrough (`400`/`401`)**: Tarin `auto/*` na iya ƙunsar samfuran passthrough daga `opencode` waɗanda aka yi wa rajista a kundin bayanai amma ba su da ingantattun bayanan shaida masu aiki (misali `oc/north-mini-code-free` → `401`). Auto-router yana gwada ɗaya, ya gaza, sannan kuskuren ya wuce kafin sauyawa ta fara aiki.
3. **Ƙaruwar tasirin aiki a lokaci guda (`429` a ƙarƙashin nauyi)**: Lokacin da zaman agent/cron da yawa suka aika buƙatu zuwa `auto` a lokaci guda, jimillar yawan buƙatun tana wuce abin da masu bayarwa na kyauta za su iya jurewa, don haka ana ɗaukar ingantattun kiraye-kiraye a matsayin amfani mara kyau.

**Tabbataccen gyara (al'umma ta bayar da rahoto, 2026-08-10)**: daidaita environment variables guda uku domin juyawa, aiki a lokaci guda, da sauyawa su shawo kan rashin daidaiton matakin kyauta maimakon tsarin ya tsaya saboda shi:

```bash
export OMNIROUTE_ROTATE_ON_400=true           # matsa zuwa wani samfuri/mai bayarwa idan an sami 400/401 (yana tsallake samfuran passthrough marasa aiki)
export OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT=4   # bayyanannen iyakar karɓar manyan ayyuka (ba a saita shi ta tsohuwa ba: babu iyakar adadin buƙatu, duba bayanin da ke ƙasa)
export OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS=5000 # jira mai iyaka na tsawon lokaci domin samun ƙarfin manyan ayyuka maimakon 503 mai ba da damar sake gwadawa nan take
```

Saita waɗannan a cikin muhallin aikin OmniRoute (daemon ɗin, misali ta LaunchAgent plist ko `systemctl edit`), sannan sake kunna OmniRoute. Alamar juyawa ita ce hanya guda mafi tasiri: tana sauya gazawa kai tsaye zuwa sake gwadawa ba tare da mai amfani ya lura ba, ta amfani da ingantaccen mai bayarwa a cikin tarin.

**Lura**: `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT` yana iyakance adadin manyan buƙatu — masu dogon mahalli — da za su gudana a lokaci guda; wannan iyaka ƙofar karɓa ce, ba mai taƙaita yawan buƙatun mai bayarwa ba. **Sabuntawar #503-fanout:** ba a ƙara saita wannan var ta tsohuwa ba (yanzu yana aiki ne kawai idan an saita shi a bayyane, kamar yadda aka yi a sama) — maimakon haka, ana sarrafa karɓar manyan ayyuka ta kasafin bytes da aka samar ta atomatik (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES`), wanda yake daidaita kansa bisa ainihin iyakar ƙwaƙwalwar na'urar, don haka sabon deployment ya kamata ya ga raguwar ƙin buƙatu na `503 chat_admission_busy` sosai ba tare da saita wannan var kwata-kwata ba; saita shi a bayyane a nan har yanzu yana aiki daidai kamar yadda aka bayyana. Bayyanannun sauye-sauyen kasafin bytes ana taƙaita su zuwa 8 MiB–2 GiB. `413 body_exceeds_budget` ba matsala ce ta ɗan lokaci ba: ƙara wannan kasafin bytes, rage `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_HARD_MAX_BODY_BYTES`, ko ƙara iyakar ƙwaƙwalwar aikin. Watsar da buƙata saboda `inflight_bytes_budget` cunkoso ne na ɗan lokaci kuma har yanzu ana iya sake gwadawa. Ana sarrafa iyakar yawan buƙatu ta kowane mai bayarwa (`open-sse/services/rateLimitManager.ts`) daban ta `RATE_LIMIT_MAX_WAIT_MS`, `RATE_LIMIT_MAX_QUEUE_DEPTH`, da `RATE_LIMIT_AUTO_ENABLE` — duba `.env.example`.

**Yadda za a tabbatar ya yi aiki**: gudanar da agent/cron ɗinka sau biyu a jere cikin sauri, sannan ka tabbatar dukansu sun yi nasara. Kafin gyaran, gudu na biyu yawanci yana jefa kuskuren `429`/`401`. Bayan gyaran, za a sake gwada buƙatun da suka gaza (idan akwai) ta atomatik ba tare da an nuna maka ba, kuma kiran zai kammala. Haka kuma, za ka iya yin `curl /monitoring/health` sannan ka lura da filin `rateLimitedUntil` a haɗin masu samarwa da kuma `circuitBreakers.providerBreakers[].state` na masu samarwar da abin ya shafa — matsayin zai kasance ɗaya daga cikin `CLOSED`, `DEGRADED`, `OPEN`, ko `HALF_OPEN` (duba `src/shared/utils/circuitBreaker.ts`), kuma mai samarwar da ke ci gaba da gazawa zai sauya daga `CLOSED → DEGRADED → OPEN` kafin lokacin sake saiti ya ba da damar aika buƙatar gwaji (`HALF_OPEN`).

**Idan har yanzu kana ganin 429**: asusun da ke aiki na wannan mai samarwar ya ƙare da gaske da _quota_ ɗinsa (ba wai iyakar saurin buƙatu kawai ba). Ƙara asusu na biyu don mai samarwar iri ɗaya a OmniRoute dashboard → Providers → Accounts, ko haɗa wani mai samarwa na kyauta (misali, `routeway`, `auggie`). Juyawa tsakanin asusu yana taimakawa ne kawai ga matsalolin wucin gadi na iyakar saurin buƙatu/400/401; ƙarewar quota gaba ɗaya na buƙatar bayanan shiga na biyu ko wani mai samarwa daban.

**Idan kana ganin 403 a kan samfuran vision (`auto/vision`, `bazaarlink/*`)**: asusun da aka haɗa ba shi da shirin biyan kuɗi wanda ya ƙunshi vision, ko kuma API key ɗin ba shi da isassun izini. Tabbatar a dashboard na mai samarwar cewa key scope ɗin ya ƙunshi vision/multimodal, ko kuma haɗa asusun matakin biyan kuɗi sannan ka bar shi a matsayin maƙasudin vision.

## Gargaɗin npm install (ERESOLVE / peer / deprecated)

Lokacin da ka gudanar da `npm install -g omniroute`, za ka iya ganin tarin gargaɗi kamar `npm warn ERESOLVE`, sanarwar peer-dependency, da saƙonnin `deprecated`. **Ana tsammanin waɗannan kuma ba sa haifar da wata matsala.** Shigarwar ta yi nasara idan ka ga `added <N> packages` a cikin sakamakon.

Don ɓoye gargaɗin warware peer-dependency, yi amfani da tsarin shigarwa da OmniRoute ke goyon baya:

```bash
npm install -g omniroute --legacy-peer-deps
```

`--legacy-peer-deps` yana ɓoye `ERESOLVE` da sanarwar peer-dependency kawai. Sanarwar daina tallafawa za ta ci gaba da bayyana saboda tana fitowa ne daga fakitin wasu ɓangarori da aka gada; ba ta nuna cewa shigarwar ta gaza ba.

Gargaɗin yana fitowa ne daga tsofaffin iyakokin peer-dependency a cikin fakitin wasu ɓangarori waɗanda OmniRoute ba ya sarrafawa:

1. **`marked-terminal` yana buƙatar `marked >=1 <16`, amma an samu `marked@18`** — yana aiki lafiya a aikace; iyakar peer ta upstream ce kawai ta tsufa.
2. **`deprecated prebuild-install@7.1.3`** — mataimaki ne na ɗauko native-binary da aka gada. Ba a
   amfani da shi wajen shigar da transport binding na `wreq-js` da aka ƙayyade, kuma ba ya nuna cewa saitin transport
   na mai samar da web-cookie ya gaza.

**Ba a buƙatar wani mataki** — ba za a iya ɓoye gargaɗin gaba ɗaya ba sai an yi fork na fakitin upstream.

## Gemini Web da Playwright Chromium

Idan buƙatar Gemini Web ta dawo da `503` tare da saƙon cewa ba a shigar da Playwright Chromium
ba, fakitin npm yana nan amma binary na burauzar ne ya ɓace.
Playwright da gangan yana ware sauke burauza daga shigar da fakitin npm,
saboda haka ana tsammanin wannan martani har sai an shigar da burauzar.

Don shigarwar npm ta global, shigar da Chromium daga kundin adireshin
fakitin OmniRoute domin cache na burauzar ya kasance mallakin shigarwar Playwright ɗin nan:

```bash
cd "$(npm root -g)/omniroute"
npx playwright install chromium
```

Sake kunna OmniRoute bayan shigarwar, sannan ka sake gwada buƙatar Gemini Web. Idan kana
gudanar da OmniRoute daga Docker image, yi amfani da image na `-web` (ko build target na `runner-web`),
wanda ya ƙunshi Chromium da dependencies ɗinsa; base image ba ya
ƙunshe da su.

## Gargaɗin Ƙarya na Antivirus

<a name="antivirus-false-positives"></a>

### Avast/AVG yana keɓe `README.md` da `MD:HttpRequest-inf[Susp]`

**Wannan gargaɗin ƙarya ne. Babu wani abu da ya kamu, kuma ba a buƙatar ɗaukar wani mataki.**

Avast da AVG suna amfani da hanyar tantancewa ta heuristic wadda ke yi wa fayilolin rubutu na yau da kullum/Markdown da ke ɗauke da hanyoyin haɗi masu yawa masu kama da buƙatun HTTP alama. Ana haɗa `README.md` na OmniRoute a cikin kunshin npm (an jera shi a `package.json` → `files`), don haka yana sauka a `node_modules/omniroute/README.md` yayin shigarwa ta global — kuma yana ɗauke da misalai kusan 15 na `http://localhost:20128/...` (endpoints na MCP HTTP/SSE, URL na A2A `.well-known`, da gutsattsarin `curl`). Yawan waɗannan hanyoyin haɗin ya isa ya kunna wannan tantancewar.

Idan wannan ya fara faruwa kwanan nan kawai: nau'in fayil ɗin bai canza ba. README ya ƙara faɗaɗa teburin endpoints ɗinsa (an ƙara MCP HTTP + SSE + A2A) da ƙarin misalan `curl`, wanda ya sa ya wuce iyakar tantancewar.

Fayil ɗin takardu ne marar aiki wanda ba ya ɗauke da wani abun da za a iya aiwatarwa. Za ku iya dawo da shi daga keɓewa cikin aminci.

**Abin da za a yi:**

1. **Dakatar da sanarwar** — ware kundin shigarwar daga binciken antivirus ɗinku
   (Avast: Settings → Exceptions), ta ƙara hanyar `node_modules` ta global da/ko kundin
   bayanan OmniRoute (`~/.omniroute/`).
2. **Kai rahoton gargaɗin ƙarya** — <https://www.avast.com/false-positive-file-form.php>,
   tare da haɗa `README.md` da aka keɓe. Wannan shi ne gyaran da zai taimaki kowa, domin
   heuristic na mai samar da antivirus ne ke yin tsauraran hukunci fiye da kima kan fayil ɗin rubutu.

**Dalilin da ya sa ba ma “gyara” wannan daga ɓangarenmu:** duk misalan `http://localhost` ne,
kuma localhost ba zai iya zama `https` ba sai an fuskanci matsalolin takardar shaida da aka sanya wa hannu da kai. Gurɓata takardun domin kauce wa heuristic na kamfani ɗaya zai cutar da kowane mai karatu don kawai a biya bukatar wata matsalar na'urar bincike.

### Kaspersky yana yi wa manhajar Desktop alama da `PDM:Trojan.Win32.Generic`

**Wannan gargaɗin ƙarya ne daga heuristic na halayya. Babu wani abu da ya kamu.** Farkon
`PDM:` na Kaspersky yana nufin hukuncin ya fito ne daga Proactive Defense Module (System Watcher),
wanda ke tantance abin da mai shigarwar _yake yi_ maimakon kwatanta shi da sanannun malware. Idan
ya kunna, Kaspersky yana “mayar da baya” gaba ɗaya shigarwar — yana share fayilolin da ya riga
ya rubuta — don haka manhajar takan lalace ko ta ɓace.

Fayilolin da yake yi wa alama sassa ne na yau da kullum daga sanannun dependencies masu
buɗaɗɗen tushe da aka haɗa da manhajar desktop, misali:

- `resources/app/.build/next/node_modules/playwright-<hash>/lib/…/agentParser.js` da
  `workerProcessEntry.js` — [Playwright](https://playwright.dev), library na sarrafa burauza
  ta atomatik da ake amfani da shi don shiga ga masu samar da sabis daga cikin manhajar da kuma tattaunawa mai dogaro da burauza.
- `resources/app/.build/next/node_modules/@wreq-js/binding-win32-<arch>-msvc-<hash>/wreq-js.win32-<arch>-msvc.node`
  — native binding na `wreq-js` da aka ƙayyade sigarsa, wanda ake amfani da shi don HTTP mai
  kwaikwayon sawun burauza kan masu samar da sabis masu amfani da web-cookie
  (`<arch>` shi ne `x64` ko `arm64`).

**Dalilin da ya sa yake kunna:** mai shigarwa na Windows **ba a riga an sanya masa hannu ta lamba ba**,
don haka mai shigarwar NSIS marar sa hannu ba shi da wani suna na amincewa, kuma heuristics na halayya
suna aiki da tsananin da ya kai kololuwa. Idan aka haɗa wannan da native DLL da kuma ɗaruruwan fayilolin
`.js` da ake rubutawa ƙarƙashin `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\OmniRoute` (ciki har da kundin kunshe-kunshe
masu ƙarin hash daga standalone build na Next.js), hakan ya isa ya kunna heuristic ɗin. An shirya sanya
hannu ta lamba; har sai an aiwatar da shi, sabbin releases na iya maimaita wannan.

**Abin da za a yi:**

1. **Da farko ku tabbatar da ingancin abin da kuka sauke** (wannan yana kawar da yiwuwar an yi wa fayil ɗin kutse). Kowane release yana wallafa
   `latest.yml`, wanda filin `sha512` nasa (base64) ke rufe mai shigarwar
   `OmniRoute.Setup.<version>.exe`. A PowerShell, daga babban fayil ɗin da ke ɗauke da mai shigarwar:
   ```powershell
   $b = [System.Security.Cryptography.SHA512]::Create().ComputeHash(
     [System.IO.File]::ReadAllBytes("$PWD\OmniRoute.Setup.<version>.exe"))
   [Convert]::ToBase64String($b)
   ```
   Dole ne sakamakon ya yi daidai da `latest.yml` → `sha512`. Idan bai yi daidai ba, ku share fayil ɗin
   sannan ku sake sauke shi kawai daga [shafin releases na GitHub](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/releases).
2. **Dawo da su + ware su** — dawo da abubuwan da aka mayar da baya daga keɓewa sannan ku ƙara keɓancewa
   ga `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\OmniRoute` (Kaspersky → Settings → Threats and Exclusions),
   sannan ku sake shigarwa.
3. **Kai rahoton gargaɗin ƙarya** — <https://opentip.kaspersky.com/>. Rahotannin FP da
   masu amfani suka aika suna taimakawa sosai wajen hanzarta saka shi cikin jerin abubuwan da aka amince da su.

## Daidaituwar Node.js

<a name="nodejs-compatibility"></a>

### Shafin shiga yana rushewa ko yana nuna kuskuren "Module self-registration"

**Dalili:** Kana amfani da sigar Node.js da ba ta cikin mafi ƙarancin amintaccen yanayin aiki da OmniRoute ya amince da shi. Mafi yawan abin da ke faruwa shi ne amfani da tsohuwar sigar gyaran Node 22 ko 24 wadda take ƙasa da mafi ƙarancin matakin tsaro da OmniRoute ke buƙata.

**Alamomi:**

- Shafin shiga yana nuna farin allo ko kuskuren uwar garke
- Console yana nuna `Error: Module did not self-register` ko makamantan kurakuran native binding
- Shafin shiga yana nuna **tutar gargaɗi mai launin lemu** tare da sigar Node ɗinka idan yanayin aikin yana wajen ƙa'idar tsaro da ake tallafawa

**Gyara:**

1. Shigar da sigar Node.js LTS da ake tallafawa (shawara: Node.js 24.x):
   ```bash
   nvm install 24
   nvm use 24
   ```
2. Tabbatar da sigarka: `node --version` ya kamata ya nuna `v24.0.0` ko mafi sabo a layin 24.x LTS
3. Sake shigar da OmniRoute: `npm install -g omniroute`
4. Sake farawa: `omniroute`

> **Amintattun sigogin da ake tallafawa:** `>=22.22.2 <23` ko `>=24.0.0 <27`. Node.js 24.x LTS (Krypton) da Node.js 26 suna samun cikakken tallafi.

### npm v11+: Ba a shigar da `better-sqlite3` ba (Cannot find module)

<a name="npm-v11-better-sqlite3-not-installed-cannot-find-module"></a>

**Dalili:** npm v11 (wanda yake zuwa tare da Node.js 24+) yana toshe scripts na shigarwa don optional
dependencies ta tsoho. Saboda an jera `better-sqlite3` a cikin `optionalDependencies`
kuma yana buƙatar native compilation (`node-gyp rebuild`), npm yana tsallake shi ba tare da sanarwa ba.

**Alamomi:**

- Uwar garke tana rushewa yayin farawa tare da `Cannot find module 'better-sqlite3'`
- `ls node_modules/better-sqlite3` yana nuna "No such file or directory"
- `npm ls better-sqlite3` yana nuna `(empty)`

**Gyara:**

1. Amince da scripts na shigarwa sannan ka sake shigarwa:
   ```bash
   npm approve-scripts better-sqlite3
   npm install
   ```
2. Ko shigar da prebuilt ɗin da hannu:
   ```bash
   npm pack better-sqlite3@13.0.1
   tar -xzf better-sqlite3-*.tgz -C node_modules
   mv node_modules/package node_modules/better-sqlite3
   rm better-sqlite3-*.tgz
   ```
3. Tabbatar yana aiki: `node -e "require('better-sqlite3')(':memory:').close(); console.log('OK')"`

### macOS: `dlopen` / "slice is not valid mach-o file"

<a name="macos-native-module-rebuild"></a>

**Dalili:** Bayan yin `npm install -g omniroute` na gama-gari, wataƙila an compile native binary na `better-sqlite3` da ke cikin package ɗin don wani architecture ko Node.js ABI daban da wanda yake aiki a na'urar. Wannan ya zama ruwan dare a macOS (Apple Silicon da Intel) idan pre-built binary bai dace da yanayinka ba.

**Alamomi:**

- Uwar garke tana kasa farawa nan take tare da kuskuren `dlopen`
- Kuskuren yana ɗauke da `slice is not valid mach-o file`
- Cikakken misali:

```
dlopen(/Users/<user>/.nvm/versions/node/v24.14.1/lib/node_modules/omniroute/app/node_modules/better-sqlite3/build/Release/better_sqlite3.node, 0x0001): tried: '...' (slice is not valid mach-o file)
```

**Gyara — sake ginawa don yanayin na'urarka (ba a buƙatar komawa tsohuwar sigar Node.js):**

```bash
cd $(npm root -g)/omniroute/app
npm rebuild better-sqlite3
omniroute
```

> **Lura:** Wannan yana sake compile native binding ɗin bisa sigar Node.js da CPU architecture na na'urarka, yana warware rashin daidaituwar binary. Matsakaicin yanayin aikin da ake tallafawa a hukumance shi ne **`>=22.22.2 <23` ko `>=24.0.0 <27`** (`SUPPORTED_NODE_RANGE` a cikin `src/shared/utils/nodeRuntimeSupport.ts`, wanda ya yi daidai da filin `engines` na `package.json`). Node.js 24.x LTS (Krypton) da Node.js 26 suna samun cikakken tallafi tare da `better-sqlite3` v12.x.

## Matsalolin Proxy

<a name="proxy-issues"></a>

### Tabbatar da mai bayarwa yana nuna "fetch failed"

**Dalili:** A baya, maƙurar tabbatar da maɓallin API (`POST /api/providers/validate`) tana tsallake saitin proxy, wanda ke haifar da gazawa a muhallan da ke buƙatar tura zirga-zirga ta proxy.

**Gyara (v3.5.5+):** Yanzu an gyara wannan. Hanyoyin tabbatar da mai bayarwa suna bi ta `runWithProxyContext`, tare da mutunta saitunan proxy na matakin mai bayarwa da na gama-gari ta atomatik.

### Binciken lafiyar token yana gazawa da "fetch failed"

**Dalili:** Sabunta token na OAuth da ke gudana a bayan fage ba ya tantance saitin proxy na kowace haɗi.

**Gyara (v3.5.5+):** Yanzu mai tsara binciken lafiyar token yana tantance saitin proxy na kowace haɗi kafin yunƙurin sabuntawa. Sabunta zuwa v3.5.5+.

### Proxy na SOCKS5 yana mayar da "invalid onRequestStart method"

**Dalili:** A Node.js 22, dispatcher na undici@8 bai dace da ginannen aiwatarwar `fetch()` na Node ba.

**Gyara (v3.5.5+):** Yanzu OmniRoute yana amfani da aikin `fetch()` na undici kansa lokacin da dispatcher na proxy ke aiki, domin tabbatar da daidaitaccen aiki. Sabunta zuwa v3.5.5+.

### Proxy na MITM a ƙarƙashin WSL: ba a katse manhajojin tebur a kan Windows host

**Dalili:** Ana shigar da proxy na MITM da takardar shaidar CA ɗinsa a cikin muhallin da OmniRoute ke aiki. A ƙarƙashin WSL, wannan muhalli shi ne Linux guest, yayin da manhajojin AI na tebur (Kiro, Trae, Copilot, Zed, …) ke aiki a kan Windows host. Manhajojin host ba sa amincewa da ma'ajiyar takardun shaida ta guest kuma ba sa tura zirga-zirgarsu ta proxy na tsarin guest, saboda haka katse zirga-zirgar manhajojin tebur ba ya aiki a can.

**Shawara:** Gudanar da OmniRoute kai tsaye a kan OS ɗaya da manhajojin tebur da kake son katse zirga-zirgarsu (Windows don manhajojin Windows; haka nan ga macOS/Linux). Barin OmniRoute a cikin WSL yayin niyyar manhajojin host yana buƙatar amincewa da takardar shaidar CA da aka samar da hannu a kan Windows host, sannan a nuna saitunan cibiyar sadarwa/proxy na kowace manhajar host zuwa maƙurar proxy ta WSL — wannan tsari ne marar tallafi kuma mai sauƙin lalacewa.

## Matsalolin Docker

### Docker IPv6 / Sake Saita Haɗi

<a name="docker-ipv6"></a>

**Alamu:** `curl http://localhost:20128/v1/models` yana mayar da `curl: (56) Recv failure: Connection reset by peer`. Dashboard da endpoints marasa tantancewa suna aiki, amma endpoints masu tantancewa suna gaza — yana kama da matsalar tantancewa amma ba haka ba ne.

**Dalili:** `docker run -p 20128:20128` yana wallafawa a kan `0.0.0.0` (IPv4) da `::` (IPv6), amma process ɗin da ke cikin container yana sauraro a kan IPv4 kawai. A hosts inda `localhost` ke fara resolve zuwa `::1`, haɗin yana sauka a kan published port na IPv6 ba tare da listener a bayansa ba → ana sake saita haɗin.

**Gyara:**

1. **Bincike mai sauri:** Gudanar da `curl -4 http://localhost:20128/v1/models`. Idan yana aiki da `-4` amma yana gaza ba tare da shi ba, kana da rashin daidaiton bind na IPv6.
2. **Gyara na dindindin:** Yi bind zuwa IPv4 kai tsaye ta amfani da `-p 127.0.0.1:20128:20128` a cikin umarnin `docker run` ɗinka:
   ```bash
   docker run -d --name omniroute --restart unless-stopped --stop-timeout 40 \
     -p 127.0.0.1:20128:20128 -v omniroute-data:/app/data diegosouzapw/omniroute:latest
   ```
   Wannan yana tilasta bind na IPv4 sannan kuma yana hana fallasa proxy a kan dukkan interfaces na host.

## Gano da Gyara Matsaloli

### Kunna Fayilolin Log

Saita `APP_LOG_TO_FILE=true` a cikin fayil ɗinka na `.env`. Ana rubuta logs na application a ƙarƙashin `logs/`.
Ana adana artifacts na request a ƙarƙashin `${DATA_DIR}/call_logs/` lokacin da aka kunna pipeline na call log
a cikin settings.
Lokacin da aka kunna pipeline capture, saita `CALL_LOG_PIPELINE_CAPTURE_STREAM_CHUNKS=false` don cire
payloads na stream chunk, ko daidaita `CALL_LOG_PIPELINE_MAX_SIZE_KB` don canza iyakar artifact a KB.

### Duba Lafiyar Provider

```bash
# Dashboard na lafiya
http://localhost:20128/dashboard/health

# Binciken lafiyar API
curl http://localhost:20128/api/monitoring/health
```

### Ma'ajiyar Runtime

- Babban state: `${DATA_DIR}/storage.sqlite` (providers, combos, aliases, keys, settings)
- Amfani: Tables na SQLite a cikin `storage.sqlite` (`usage_history`, `call_logs`, `proxy_logs`) + `${DATA_DIR}/call_logs/` na zaɓi
- Logs na application: `<repo>/logs/...` (lokacin da `APP_LOG_TO_FILE=true`)
- Artifacts na call log: `${DATA_DIR}/call_logs/YYYY-MM-DD/...` lokacin da aka kunna pipeline na call log

Aikin **Clean history** na shafin Request Logs yana share `call_logs`, tsohon
`request_detail_logs`, da directory na artifact na gida `${DATA_DIR}/call_logs/`.

2. Je zuwa **Settings → Resilience → Provider Profiles** sannan ka ƙara iyakar yawan gazawa
3. Duba ko mai bayarwar ya sauya iyakokin API ko yana buƙatar sake tabbatar da izini
4. Bincika bayanan ma'aunin jinkiri — jinkiri mai yawa na iya haifar da gazawar da ta danganci ƙarewar lokaci

## Matsalolin Sauya Sauti Zuwa Rubutu

### Kuskuren "Unsupported model"

- Yi amfani da id na samfurin da ɓangarensa na farko ya kasance mai bayarwar da kake da bayanan shaidarsa (`openai/whisper-1`, `openrouter/deepgram/nova-3`). Amfani da `deepgram/nova-3` kai tsaye yana buƙatar maɓallin Deepgram na asali.
- Tabbatar cewa an haɗa mai bayarwar a **Dashboard → Providers**

### Rubutaccen sakamakon sauti ya dawo fanko ko ya gaza

- Duba nau'ikan fayilolin sauti da ake tallafawa: `mp3`, `wav`, `m4a`, `flac`, `ogg`, `webm`
- Tabbatar cewa girman fayil ɗin yana cikin iyakokin mai bayarwar (yawanci < 25MB)
- Duba ingancin maɓallin API na mai bayarwar a katin mai bayarwar

### Matsalolin tsari da aka fi samu

- **Alamun thinking ba sa bayyana** — Duba ko mai bayarwar da ake nufi yana tallafawa thinking da kuma saitin kasafin thinking
- **Kiran kayan aiki yana ɓacewa** — Wasu fassarorin tsari na iya cire filayen da ba a tallafawa; tabbatar da hakan a yanayin Playground
- **Umarnin system ya ɓace** — Claude da Gemini suna sarrafa umarnin system ta hanyoyi daban-daban; duba sakamakon fassarar
- **SDK yana dawo da ɗanyen string maimakon object** — An warware wannan a v1.x; mai tsabtace amsa yana cire filayen da ba na ƙa'ida ba (`x_groq`, `usage_breakdown`, da sauransu) waɗanda ke haifar da gazawar tantancewar Pydantic ta OpenAI SDK. Idan har yanzu kana ganin wannan a v3.x+, da fatan za ka shigar da rahoton matsala.
- **GLM/ERNIE yana ƙin rawar `system`** — An warware wannan a v1.x; mai daidaita rawa yana haɗa saƙonnin system ta atomatik cikin saƙonnin mai amfani ga samfuran da ba su dace ba. Idan har yanzu kana ganin wannan a v3.x+, da fatan za ka shigar da rahoton matsala.
- **Ba a gane rawar `developer` ba** — An warware wannan a v1.x; ana sauya ta atomatik zuwa `system` ga masu bayarwa da ba OpenAI ba. Idan har yanzu kana ganin wannan a v3.x+, da fatan za ka shigar da rahoton matsala.
- **`json_schema` ba ya aiki da Gemini** — An warware wannan a v1.x; yanzu ana sauya `response_format` zuwa `responseMimeType` + `responseSchema` na Gemini. Idan har yanzu kana ganin wannan a v3.x+, da fatan za ka shigar da rahoton matsala.

## Saitunan Juriya

### Iyakance ƙima ta atomatik ba ta kunnawa

- Iyakance ƙima ta atomatik tana aiki ne kawai ga masu samarwa masu amfani da maɓallin API (ba OAuth/biyan kuɗi ba)
- Tabbatar cewa an kunna iyakance ƙima ta atomatik a **Settings → Resilience → Provider Profiles**
- Duba ko mai samarwar yana mayar da lambobin matsayi na `429` ko kanun `Retry-After`

### Daidaita jinkirin exponential backoff

Bayanan martabar masu samarwa suna goyan bayan waɗannan saituna:

- **Base delay** — Lokacin jira na farko bayan gazawar farko (tsoho: 1s)
- **Max delay** — Matsakaicin iyakar lokacin jira (tsoho: 30s)
- **Multiplier** — Adadin ƙara jinkiri bayan kowace gazawa a jere (tsoho: 2x)

### Hana cunkoson buƙatu lokaci guda

Lokacin da buƙatu masu yawa na lokaci guda suka isa ga mai samarwa da aka iyakance ƙimarsa, OmniRoute yana amfani da mutex + iyakance ƙima ta atomatik don jera buƙatun ɗaya bayan ɗaya da hana gazawa mai yaɗuwa. Wannan yana faruwa ta atomatik ga masu samarwa masu amfani da maɓallin API.

### Buƙatun chat suna gazawa da 503 / chat_admission_busy

**Alamomi:**

- Ƙarshen hanyar cika chat yana mayar da martanin `503` da za a iya sake gwadawa, wanda lambar kuskurensa ita ce
  `chat_admission_busy`.
- Martanin yana ɗauke da `Retry-After`. Tun daga #12135 ana samo ƙimar daga yawan amfani da aka lura da shi
  — mafi girma tsakanin taga ta `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS` da buƙatar ta riga ta
  jira da kuma tsawon lokacin da manyan leases na yanzu suka kasance a riƙe — ana zagaye ta zuwa cikakkun
  daƙiƙu kuma ana iyakance ta zuwa 60. A kan gate marar aiki tana riƙe da mafi ƙarancin ƙimomin tarihi: daƙiƙu 2 a
  hanyar da ta dogara da bytes, daƙiƙa 1 a hanyar da ta dogara da tsari (wadda kuma ta haɗa da
  `reason: "structure_limit"`).
- Wannan na iya faruwa yayin da wani babban chat ko martanin streaming mai ɗaukar lokaci mai tsawo yake
  ci gaba da gudana.

Jikin martanin da ya dogara da bytes shi ne:

```json
{
  "error": {
    "message": "Chat admission capacity is temporarily unavailable. Retry shortly.",
    "type": "server_error",
    "code": "chat_admission_busy"
  }
}
```

Martanin da ya dogara da tsari yana amfani da type da code iri ɗaya, tare da saƙon
`Local chat admission capacity is busy for this structurally heavy request; upstream provider routing was not attempted. Retry shortly.`
da `reason: "structure_limit"`.
A tsoffin ma'aunan iyaka, ana ɗaukar buƙata a matsayin mai nauyin tsari idan tana da aƙalla saƙonni `200`,
aƙalla tools `64`, ko aƙalla kimantattun tokens `32,000`, ko kuma idan kimanta tsari mai iyaka
ta cinye iyakokinta na nodes `10,000` da aka ziyarta ko zurfin `12`.

**Dalili:** Wannan rage kaya ne da aka yi da gangan a cikin OmniRoute, ba gazawar mai samarwa na upstream ba.
Kowane process yana amfani da kariya ta cikin process don tanadin iyakantacciyar damar manyan buƙatu kafin riƙewa
da sarrafa babban jikin buƙata. Babban lease yana kasancewa a riƙe tsawon rayuwar martanin SSE.

**#503-fanout:** kafin wannan gyaran, kariyar tana iyakance concurrency da tsayayyen ADADIN buƙatu
(`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT`, tsoho `1`) ba tare da la'akari da ƙwaƙwalwar host ba, don haka fan-out na
coding-agent (subagents/CLIs da yawa, jikuna da a kai a kai suke > 256 KB) yana faɗuwa zuwa ingantaccen
concurrency na kusan 1 kuma yana haifar da 503 a ƙarƙashin kaya na yau da kullum. Yanzu kariyar tana daidaita
kanta: ana sarrafa ta da kasafin BYTES na ingest da ake samo ta atomatik (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES`) wanda aka tsara daga
ainihin iyakar ƙwaƙwalwar process, sannan kuma tana duba alamar matsin albarkatu kai tsaye — don haka tana
rage kaya ne kawai lokacin da host yake fuskantar matsin ƙwaƙwalwa na gaske, ba don kawai buƙatu masu nauyi fiye da ɗaya
sun iso lokaci guda ba. Har yanzu ana mutunta tsohuwar iyakar adadi (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT`), amma
sai idan ka saita ta kai tsaye.

Lokacin da damar take cike, babban buƙata da farko tana jira har zuwa
`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS` (tsoho `2000`, `0` yana kashe jiran) domin wani gurbi ya samu
kafin a mayar da `503` da za a iya sake gwadawa. An tanadi wannan iyakantaccen jira ne domin clients irin na agent
(OpenCode, Claude Code, Cursor) waɗanda ke fan-out na manyan ƙananan buƙatu lokaci guda su jera cunkoson
ɗaya bayan ɗaya maimakon ƙone duk kasafin sake gwadawarsu a kan ƙin karɓa nan take sannan su mutu a tsakiyar aiki.
Ana nuna yawan amfani da manyan leases na yanzu, kasafin bytes da aka ƙayyade, da tsananin matsin kai tsaye
a `GET /api/monitoring/health` → `chatAdmission` (`inflightBytes`, `maxInflightBytes`,
`budgetSource`, `pressureSeverity`, `countCapEnabled`) — duba waɗannan kafin ka taɓa kowace env var.
Settings → Resilience → Request Queue → Concurrent Requests ba ya sarrafa wannan; wannan saitin
yana sarrafa wata dabara ta daban ta jerin buƙatun mai samarwa.

**Gyara:**

1. Da farko sake gwadawa. Ya kamata clients su mutunta `Retry-After` kuma su yi amfani da backoff maimakon
   maimaita buƙatar nan take.
2. Duba `/api/monitoring/health` → `chatAdmission` kafin daidaita komai. `countCapEnabled:
false` da wadataccen `maxInflightBytes` suna nufin kasafin da aka samo ta atomatik yana riga yana yin
   aikinsa; `pressureSeverity` na `high`/`critical` yana nufin host ɗin yana fama da ƙarancin ƙwaƙwalwa na gaske —
   ba za a iya gyara wannan da admission env var ba, yana buƙatar ƙarin RAM ko ƙaramin workload.
3. Sai kawai idan `/api/monitoring/health` ya nuna cewa kasafin da aka samo ta atomatik ya yi ƙanƙanta sosai ga
   host ɗinka (abu ne da ba kasafai yake faruwa ba — yana riga yana daidaitawa daga container zuwa bare-metal), ka sauya shi kai tsaye da
   `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES` maimakon komawa ga tsohuwar iyakar adadin buƙatu.

Duba [manunin environment-variable](../reference/ENVIRONMENT.md#4-security--authentication)
domin saitunan admission masu cikakken iko.

## Zaɓin RAG / LLM taxonomy na gazawa (matsaloli 16)

Wasu masu amfani da OmniRoute suna sanya gateway ɗin a gaban tarin RAG ko na agent. A irin waɗannan saitunan, ana yawan ganin wani baƙon yanayi: OmniRoute yana bayyana yana aiki yadda ya kamata (providers suna aiki, routing profiles suna daidai, babu sanarwar rate limit), amma amsar ƙarshe har yanzu ba daidai ba ce.

A aikace, waɗannan matsalolin yawanci suna fitowa ne daga RAG pipeline da ke ƙasa, ba daga gateway ɗin kansa ba.

Idan kana son ƙamus na bai ɗaya don bayyana waɗannan gazawar, za ka iya amfani da WFGY ProblemMap, wata hanya ta rubutu ta waje mai lasisin MIT wadda ke bayyana nau'ikan gazawar RAG / LLM guda goma sha shida da ke maimaituwa. A taƙaice, ya ƙunshi:

- karkacewar retrieval da lalacewar iyakokin context
- indexes da vector stores marasa komai ko waɗanda suka tsufa
- rashin daidaito tsakanin embedding da ma'ana
- matsalolin haɗa prompt da context window
- rushewar dabaru da amsoshi masu nuna ƙwarin gwiwa fiye da kima
- gazawar dogayen chains da daidaitawar agents
- karkacewar memory da roles tsakanin agents da yawa
- matsalolin tsarin deployment da bootstrap

Manufar mai sauƙi ce:

1. Lokacin da kake binciken amsa mara kyau, adana:
   - aikin mai amfani da request
   - haɗin route ko provider a OmniRoute
   - duk wani RAG context da aka yi amfani da shi a ƙasa (retrieved documents, tool calls, da sauransu)
2. Daidaita matsalar da lamba ɗaya ko biyu na WFGY ProblemMap (`No.1` … `No.16`).
3. Adana lambar a dashboard, runbook, ko incident tracker naka kusa da logs na OmniRoute.
4. Yi amfani da shafin WFGY da ya dace don yanke shawarar ko kana buƙatar canza RAG stack, retriever, ko routing strategy naka.

Cikakken rubutu da takamaiman hanyoyin aiwatarwa suna nan (lasisin MIT, rubutu kawai):

[WFGY ProblemMap README](https://github.com/onestardao/WFGY/blob/main/ProblemMap/README.md)

Za ka iya yin watsi da wannan sashe idan ba ka gudanar da RAG ko agent pipelines a bayan OmniRoute.

## Sanannun Matsalolin v3.8.0

Matsalolin da suka kebanta da sakin v3.8.0 da hanyoyin wucin gadi na magance su a halin yanzu. Idan aka samar da gyara a wani patch na gaba, za a sabunta ko cire bayanin.

### Gazawar auth na Devin CLI

**Alamomi:**

- "Ba a sami Devin CLI ba" ko "auth ya gaza" lokacin kiran tools masu amfani da Devin
- Binciken lokacin gudanar da CLI yana nuna `installed=false`

**Dalilai:**

- `CLI_DEVIN_BIN` yana nuna wani path da babu shi
- Ba a girka Devin CLI a host ɗin ba

**Gyara:**

1. Girka Devin CLI don platform ɗinka
2. Saita `CLI_DEVIN_BIN=/usr/local/bin/devin` (ko ainihin path ɗin) a cikin `.env`
3. Sake kunna OmniRoute sannan ka sake gwadawa daga **Dashboard → Kayan Aikin CLI**

### Model cooldown ya makale (sake saitawa da hannu)

**Alamomi:**

- Wani model yana ci gaba da bayyana a jerin cooldown ko da bayan lokacin ƙarewa ya wuce
- Requests har yanzu suna tsallake model ɗin a combo routing duk da cewa timestamp ɗin yana nuna lokacin da ya wuce

**Sake saitawa da hannu:**

- **Dashboard:** **Settings → Model Cooldowns** → danna **Re-enable** a kan card ɗin da abin ya shafa
- **API:** `DELETE /api/resilience/model-cooldowns` tare da management auth headers

### Haɗin Command Code provider ya gaza da 403

**Alamomi:**

- 403 lokacin gwada haɗin Command Code provider
- Card ɗin provider yana nuna "ba a ba da izini ba" bayan an ƙara shi sabo

**Dalili:** OAuth flow bai kammala ba (ba a karɓi callback ba ko ba a adana token ba).

**Gyara:**

- Gudanar da `omniroute providers` daga CLI don sake kunna OAuth flow, ko
- Sake gudanar da OAuth daga **Dashboard → Providers → Command Code → Reconnect**

### ModelScope yana mayar da cooldowns na 429 masu tsauri

**Alamomi:**

- Cooldowns masu gajarta sosai ko na nan take a ModelScope bayan ƙaramin tarin requests
- Combo routing yana tsallake ModelScope da wuri fiye da yadda ake tsammani

**Dalili:** ModelScope yana fitar da `Retry-After` headers na musamman ga provider. v3.8.0 ya zo da keɓantaccen tsarin sarrafa waɗannan headers, saboda haka tsofaffin versions suna kuskuren karanta su a matsayin shawarwarin rate-limit na gama-gari.

**Gyara:**

- Tabbatar kana amfani da v3.8.0 ko sabo
- Tabbatar an kunna `useUpstream429BreakerHints` toggle a ƙarƙashin **Settings → Resilience**

### Babu OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET a production

**Alamomi:**

- 401 a kowane Codex/Responses WebSocket bridge request lokacin gudanarwa a remote production host
- WebSocket bridge handshake yana rufewa nan take bayan haɗawa

**Dalili:** Babu env var na `OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET` a production environment.

**Gyara:**

1. Ƙirƙiri random secret: `openssl rand -hex 32`
2. Saita `OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET=<random-secret>` a cikin env na production server (da duk wani client da ke sadarwa da bridge)
3. Sake kunna OmniRoute

### Responses API: background mode ya koma synchronous

**Alamomi:**

- An rubuta gargadi a log: `background mode degraded to synchronous`
- Request mai `background: true` yana mayar da amsar synchronous ta al'ada maimakon background job handle

**Dalili:** v3.8.0 da gangan yana sauya `background: true` a Responses API zuwa synchronous execution tare da fitar da gargadi. Cikakken async background execution wani abu ne da za a samar nan gaba.

**Gyara:**

- Daidaita client ɗin ya yi kira ba tare da `background` ba, ko
- Jira wani sakin na gaba wanda zai zo da cikakken async background mode (bi diddigin changelog)

## Jinkirin Farawa / Ƙarewar Lokacin Jira

Idan CLI ya nuna `⚠ Server did not respond within 60s` amma a zahiri uwar garken
tana aiki, lokacin da aka ware wa binciken shiri ya yi gajarta ga muhallinka.

Wannan yawanci yana faruwa ne a Windows (riga-kafi, masu sa ido kan tsarin fayiloli) ko cikin containers
masu nauyin ayyuka yayin farawa.

**Gyara — ƙara lokacin da aka ware:**

```bash
# Ta hanyar env var (yana dorewa a duk lokacin farawa):
export OMNIROUTE_READY_TIMEOUT_MS=180000   # mintuna 3
omniroute serve

# Ta hanyar alamar CLI (sau ɗaya kawai):
omniroute serve --ready-timeout 180000
```

Tsohon ƙima ita ce 60 000 ms (60 s). Gargadin na bayar da bayani ne kawai; uwar garken
za ta ci gaba da farawa a bango kuma za a iya isa gare ta da zarar farawa ya kammala.

Duba [`docs/reference/ENVIRONMENT.md`](../reference/ENVIRONMENT.md) don cikakkun
bayanai game da `OMNIROUTE_READY_TIMEOUT_MS`.
