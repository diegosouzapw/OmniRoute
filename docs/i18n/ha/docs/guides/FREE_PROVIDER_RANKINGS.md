> **A Taƙaice**: OmniRoute yana jera masu samar da sabis na **kyauta** bisa ingancin samfuri ta amfani da **maki na ELO na Arena AI
> (irin na LMArena)**. Buɗe shafin **Jerin Matsayin Masu Samar da Sabis na Kyauta** a cikin
> dashboard domin ganin waɗanne masu samar da sabis na kyauta ne ke bayar da samfura mafi ƙarfi don aikinka —
> gaba ɗaya, ko bayan tacewa bisa rukuni (rubuta lamba, bita, rubutun bayani, gyaran kurakurai).

---

## Mene Ne

OmniRoute yana rajistar masu samar da sabis 329, ciki har da shigarwar katalog 155 da aka yi wa alamar **kyauta/babu-tantancewa**
(babu-tantancewa,
OAuth mai matakin kyauta, ko API key mai matakin kyauta — duba
[Jagorar Matakan Kyauta](../getting-started/FREE-TIERS-GUIDE.md) da cikakken
[kundin Matakan Kyauta](../reference/FREE_TIERS.md)). Matsalar ita ce: masu samar da sabis na kyauta suna
da bambance-bambancen ingancin samfuri sosai. Mai samar da sabis marar buƙatar tantancewa da ke bayar da frontier model ya fi amfani sosai
fiye da wanda ke bayar da ƙaramin legacy model.

**Jerin Matsayin Masu Samar da Sabis na Kyauta** yana amsa "**wane mai samar da sabis na kyauta ne yake ba ni mafi kyawun samfuri?**" ta hanyar
haɗa katalog na kowane mai samar da sabis na kyauta da **makan inganci da taron jama'a ya samar** daga
**teburin shugabannin Arena AI** (ELO na zaɓin ɗan Adam, irin wannan ra'ayin ne ke bayan filin gasar chatbot na LMArena).
Sannan ana jera masu samar da sabis bisa ƙarfin **mafi kyawun samfurinsu na kyauta**.

Ana lissafa jerin matsayin daga tushe na ainihi guda uku:

1. Jerin masu samar da sabis na kyauta — `NOAUTH_PROVIDERS`, tare da shigarwar `OAUTH_PROVIDERS` /
   `APIKEY_PROVIDERS` da aka yi wa alamar `hasFree`
   (`src/shared/constants/providers.ts`).
2. Katalog ɗin samfuran kowane mai samar da sabis daga rajistar masu samar da sabis
   (`open-sse/config/providerRegistry.ts`).
3. Makan dacewa da aiki da aka samo daga ELO waɗanda injin daidaitawar Arena ELO ya adana a teburin DB na `model_intelligence`
   (`src/lib/arenaEloSync.ts`).

Dabarar haɗawa tana cikin `src/lib/freeProviderRankings.ts`.

## Yadda Ake Samun Dama

### Shafin dashboard

Buɗe dashboard sannan ka je **Kuɗaɗe → Jerin Matsayin Masu Samar da Sabis na Kyauta**, ko ka je kai tsaye zuwa:

```
/dashboard/free-provider-rankings
```

Shafin (`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/free-provider-rankings/page.tsx`) yana nuna:

- **Dandalin ukun farko** (🥇 🥈 🥉) na masu samar da sabis na kyauta da suka fi samun matsayi.
- Cikakken **teburin matsayi** mai ginshiƙai: **Matsayi**, **Mai Samar da Sabis**, **Mafi Kyawun Samfuri**,
  **Maki**, **Matsakaicin Maki**, **Samfura**, **Nau'i**.
- **Maɓallan tace rukuni**: _Duk Rukunnai_, _Na Asali_, _Rubuta Lamba_, _Bita_,
  _Rubutun Bayani_, _Gyaran Kurakurai_.

Alamar **Nau'i** ta kowane mai samar da sabis tana nuna yadda yake zama kyauta:

| Alama    | Ma'ana                                              |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| `NOAUTH` | Kullum kyauta ne, ba a buƙatar bayanan tantancewa   |
| `OAUTH`  | Mai samar da OAuth mai matakin kyauta (`hasFree`)   |
| `APIKEY` | Mai samar da API-key mai matakin kyauta (`hasFree`) |

Ana nuna maki a matsayin lakabi masu sauƙin fahimta (misali _Mafi Daraja_, _Madalla_, _Yana da Kyau Sosai_,
_Yana da Kyau_, _Matsakaici_) maimakon ɗanyen lambobi, saboda ƙimar da ke ƙasa ingancin
matsayi ne na kwatanci, ba kashi cikin ɗari ba.

### API endpoint

Shafin yana samun bayanansa daga public read endpoint
(`src/app/api/free-provider-rankings/route.ts`):

```
GET /api/free-provider-rankings
GET /api/free-provider-rankings?category=coding
GET /api/free-provider-rankings?category=coding&limit=20
```

Query parameters (waɗanda aka tantance da Zod):

| Param      | Nau'i  | Na Asali | Bayani                                                                                                               |
| ---------- | ------ | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `category` | string | (babu)   | Ɗaya daga cikin `default`, `coding`, `review`, `documentation`, `debugging`. A bar shi domin haɗaɗɗen jerin matsayi. |
| `limit`    | number | `50`     | Ana taƙaita shi zuwa kewayon `1–100`.                                                                                |

Tsarin amsa:

```json
{
  "rankings": [
    {
      "id": "<provider-id>",
      "name": "<provider name>",
      "icon": "<icon>",
      "color": "<hex color>",
      "textIcon": "<short label>",
      "category": "noauth | oauth | apikey",
      "topModel": {
        "modelId": "<registry model id>",
        "modelName": "<model display name>",
        "score": 0.0,
        "eloRaw": 0,
        "confidence": "high | medium | low",
        "category": "<task category>"
      },
      "averageScore": 0.0,
      "modelCount": 0
    }
  ]
}
```

`eloRaw` ita ce ƙimar Arena ELO ta asali; `score` ita ce ƙimar dacewa da aiki da aka daidaita
(duba ƙasa). Ana cire masu samar da sabis da ba su da samfuran da aka ba maki daga amsar.

## Yadda Maki Ke Aiki

### Tushe: jadawalin shugabannin Arena AI

Injin daidaita Arena ELO (`src/lib/arenaEloSync.ts`) yana ɗauko jadawalin shugabanni guda biyu — `text`
da `code` — daga API na jadawalin shugabannin Arena AI
(`https://api.wulong.dev/arena-ai-leaderboards/v1/leaderboard`). Kowace shigarwa a jadawalin
shugabannin tana ɗauke da sunan samfur, mai samarwa, `score` na ELO, tazarar amincewa, da yawan ƙuri'u.

Rukunonin jadawalin shugabanni suna daidaituwa da rukunonin ayyukan OmniRoute:

| Jadawalin shugabannin Arena | Rukunonin ayyukan OmniRoute                       |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| `text`                      | `default`, `review`, `documentation`, `debugging` |
| `code`                      | `coding`                                          |

### Daidaita ma'auni (maki na dacewa da aiki)

Ana daidaita ɗanyen makin ELO bisa kowane jadawalin shugabanni zuwa **ƙimar dacewa da aiki a cikin `[0.4, 0.98]`**:

```
taskFit = 0.4 + 0.58 * ((elo - minElo) / (maxElo - minElo))
```

Makin ba ya taɓa kaiwa `0` ko `1`, domin a bar sarari ga sauye-sauyen mai amfani. Wannan shi ne
filin `score` da kake gani a martanin API da kuma alamar da ake nunawa a dashboard.

### Amincewa

Kowace shigarwa tana samun matakin amincewa bisa yawan ƙuri'un Arena:

| Amincewa | Ƙuri'u  |
| -------- | ------- |
| `high`   | ≥ 5,000 |
| `medium` | ≥ 1,000 |
| `low`    | < 1,000 |

### Ajiya da sabuntar bayanai

Ana rubuta shigarwar da aka daidaita zuwa teburin DB na `model_intelligence` tare da
`source = "arena_elo"` (`src/lib/db/modelIntelligence.ts`). Shigarwar suna **ƙarewa bayan
kwanaki 7**, saboda haka mai samarwa da ya daina daidaitawa zai fita daga baya maimakon a ci gaba da
gabatar da tsofaffin bayanai.

Daidaitawar tana aiki **ta tsohuwa**:

- Tana gudana sau ɗaya lokacin fara uwar garke, sannan tana ci gaba bisa agogon lokaci-lokaci
  (`src/lib/arenaEloSync.ts`, an haɗa ta daga `src/instrumentation-node.ts`).
- Ba ta **tare aiki kuma ba ta taɓa haddasa gazawar tsarin** — idan ɗauko bayanai daga tushen waje ya gaza, OmniRoute zai ci gaba da
  aiki, kuma jerin matsayi kawai za su nuna ingantattun bayanai na ƙarshe (ko yanayin da babu komai).

Sauye-sauyen muhalli guda biyu ne ke sarrafa ta (an rubuta bayaninsu a
[`docs/reference/ENVIRONMENT.md`](../reference/ENVIRONMENT.md)):

| Sauyi                     | Tsoho         | Manufa                                             |
| ------------------------- | ------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| `ARENA_ELO_SYNC_ENABLED`  | `true`        | Saita zuwa `false` don ƙin shiga daidaitawar waje. |
| `ARENA_ELO_SYNC_INTERVAL` | `86400` (24h) | Tazarar daidaitawa, a cikin daƙiƙu.                |

### Daidaitawa da hannu / matsayi / gogewa

Ga masu gudanarwa, endpoint na gudanarwa mai buƙatar tantancewa yana ba da ikon sarrafawa da hannu
(`src/app/api/intelligence/sync/route.ts` — yana buƙatar tantancewar gudanarwa):

```
GET    /api/intelligence/sync          # matsayin daidaitawa na yanzu (enabled, lastSync, nextSync, intervalMs)
POST   /api/intelligence/sync          # fara daidaitawa da hannu; body: { "dryRun": true } don samfoti ba tare da rubutawa ba
DELETE /api/intelligence/sync          # goge duk shigarwar bayanan arena_elo da aka daidaita
```

Idan shafin jerin matsayi babu komai, yin `POST /api/intelligence/sync` da hannu (ko kuma kawai
sake kunna uwar garken) zai sake cika shi.

### Daidaita samfura da jadawalin shugabanni

ID na samfurorin rajista da sunayen samfuran Arena ba koyaushe suke daidai gaba ɗaya ba. Jerin matsayi yana amfani da
daidaitawa mai sassauci (`findMatchingIntelligence` a cikin `src/lib/freeProviderRankings.ts`):

1. Daidaituwa kai tsaye da ID na samfur da aka daidaita.
2. Daidaituwa bayan cire ƙarin sigar da ke ƙarshen suna (misali `kimi-k2.6` → `kimi-k2`).
3. Daidaituwar farkon suna (sunan samfur a jadawalin shugabanni shi ne farkon ID na rajista).

A ɓangaren daidaitawa, ana cire sanannun farkon sunayen masu samarwa (`anthropic/`, `openai/`, `google/`, …),
kuma ƙaramin taswirar laƙabi yana faɗaɗa sanannun sunaye zuwa nau'ikan da OmniRoute ke amfani da su
a ciki, don haka ana iya samun samfuran da kowane suna.

### Yadda ake jera mai samarwa

Ga kowane mai samarwa na kyauta, injin yana sanya maki ga kowane samfur a kundinsa, sannan:

- **Mafi Kyawun Samfur** = samfurin mai samarwar da ya fi samun maki.
- **Matsakaicin Maki** = matsakaicin maki na duk samfuran mai samarwar da aka sanya wa maki.
- **Samfura** = yawan samfuran mai samarwar da suke da makin Arena.

Ana jera masu samarwa bisa **makin mafi kyawun samfur** da farko, sannan bisa matsakaicin maki. Wannan yana ba
mai samarwar da ke da aƙalla samfurin kyauta mai ƙarfi tagomashi.

## Amfani da Shi don Zaɓar Masu Bayar da Sabis na Kyauta

1. **Zaɓi rukunin da ya dace.** Yi amfani da matatar **Coding** don ayyukan wakili/rubuta lamba, ko
   ka bar ta a **All Categories** / **Default** don tattaunawa ta gaba ɗaya. Mai bayar da sabis ɗaya na iya
   samun matsayi daban-daban a tsakanin rukunoni saboda mafi kyawun samfurinsa yana bambanta bisa kowane jadawalin matsayi.
2. **Fi son waɗanda ke kan mumbari don saitin amfani guda ɗaya.** Idan kana son haɗa mai bayar da sabis na kyauta
   ɗaya ko biyu kawai, fara da waɗanda ke da matsayi mafi girma a rukuninka.
3. **Duba alamar Type.** Masu bayar da sabis na `NOAUTH` su ne mafi saurin haɗawa (ba sa buƙatar
   bayanan tantancewa). Matakan kyauta na `OAUTH` / `APIKEY` suna buƙatar yin rajista cikin sauri amma galibi suna samar da
   samfura masu ƙarfi. Duba [Jagorar Matakan Kyauta](../getting-started/FREE-TIERS-GUIDE.md) don
   matakan haɗawa.
4. **Haɗa da dama sannan ka bar Auto-Combo ya yanke shawara.** Bayanai iri ɗaya na Arena ELO da ke sarrafa
   wannan shafin suna kuma ciyar da **task-fitness factor** na injin ƙididdigar Auto-Combo
   (`open-sse/services/autoCombo/taskFitness.ts`, tsarin warwarewa
   `user_override → arena_elo → models_dev_tier → static table`). Saboda haka, bayan ka haɗa
   manyan masu bayar da sabis na kyauta, tsara hanyar buƙatu da `model: "auto"` (misali `auto/coding`) zai
   fifita samfuran kyauta masu inganci mafi girma kai tsaye ga kowace buƙata. Duba
   [Auto-Combo](../routing/AUTO-COMBO.md) don cikakken tsarin ƙididdiga mai abubuwa 16.
