OmniRoute yana tsara masu samarwa 352 da yake tallafawa zuwa matakan tattalin arziki 3. Kowace
buƙata tana bi ta cikinsu a jere har sai ɗaya ya dawo cikin nasara — kana
samun amsa mafi arha da za ta yi aiki ba tare da rubuta lambar madadin ba.

## Mataki na 1 — Biyan kuɗin shiga

**Masu samarwar da ka riga ka biya.** OmniRoute yana amfani da dukkan kaso kafin
wa'adinsa ya ƙare.

| Mai samarwa                        | Dalilin kasancewa a Mataki na 1                                       |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Claude Code OAuth                  | Anthropic Pro/Team — ƙayyadadden kuɗi, sau da yawa ba a amfani da shi |
| OpenAI Codex (biyan kuɗin ChatGPT) | Plus/Team ya haɗa da kaso na Codex                                    |
| GitHub Copilot                     | Bisa kowane mai amfani — kaso yana sake farawa kowane wata            |
| Cursor IDE                         | Kason tsarin Pro                                                      |
| Antigravity / Devin Desktop        | Kasoshi da aka gina a ciki                                            |

**Dabara**: fara tura kowace buƙata da ta dace da ƙwarewar samfurin zuwa nan.
Mai bibiyar kaso yana lura da lokutan sake farawa da ke gabatowa, kuma dabarar haɗin
`reset-aware` tana ba da fifiko yadda ya dace. Don fara tura buƙatu zuwa Mataki na 1 sannan
a koma matakan da ake biya kawai idan kaso ya ƙare, yi amfani da id ɗin `auto/thrifty` — ko `auto/subscription`
don ci gaba da amfani da ƙarfin da shirin ya ƙunsa kuma a dakatar idan bai samu ba. Duba
[Turawa mai fara da biyan kuɗin shiga](../routing/SUBSCRIPTION_LADDER.md).

## Mataki na 2 — Mai arha

**Masu samarwa da ake biya bisa kowace token waɗanda kuɗinsu bai kai $1/1M tokens ba.** An keɓe su don ayyuka masu yawa
ko bayan kaso na Mataki na 1 ya kai iyaka.

| Mai samarwa                  | Farashi (shigarwa/fitarwa) | Ƙarfi                     |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| DeepSeek V4 Pro              | $0.27 / $1.10 ga 1M        | Lamba, tunani             |
| GLM-4.5                      | $0.60 / $2.20 ga 1M        | Dogon mahalli             |
| MiniMax M1                   | $0.20 / $1.10 ga 1M        | Sauri                     |
| Qwen Coder                   | $0.30 / $1.20 ga 1M        | Lamba                     |
| OpenRouter (inganta-farashi) | yana bambanta              | Samfura 100+, mai sauyawa |

**Dabara**: haɗin `cost-optimized` yana zaɓar samfurin da ke da mafi ƙarancin $/token wanda ya cika
tacewar ƙwarewar aikin (gani, yanayin JSON, kayan aiki, iyakar mahalli).

## Mataki na 3 — Kyauta

**Masu samarwa marasa kuɗi** — matakan kyauta, shirye-shiryen kiredit, kasoshin OAuth na kullum.

| Mai samarwa      | Kason kyauta / kiredit                                    |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Kiro AI          | Matakin Claude na kyauta (amfani mai karimci bisa adalci) |
| OpenCode Free    | Babu tantancewa, iyakokin ƙima masu yalwa                 |
| Qoder            | OAuth na kyauta                                           |
| Google Vertex AI | Kiredit $300 na sabon asusu                               |
| Amazon Q         | Matakin kyauta ga masu amfani da AWS                      |
| Pollinations     | API na jama'a a buɗe                                      |
| Cloudflare AI    | Matakin Workers AI na kyauta                              |

**Dabara**: haɗin `auto` mai iyakar kasafin kuɗi yana tura buƙatu zuwa nan idan Mataki na 1+2 sun gaza
ko idan an saita `useFreeOnly=true`. Masu samarwa na kyauta sau da yawa suna da raunanan
iyakokin ƙima — circuit breaker yana dawo da su bayan lokacin jira.

## Daidaita matakai

Dashboard → **Matakai** → sanya masu samarwarka. Tsoffin saituna (daga `tierDefaults.json`) suna
da ma'ana; gyara su idan kana da takamaiman biyan kuɗin shiga da za ka ba fifiko ko masu samarwar da za ka cire.

Tsarin ƙididdiga mai abubuwa 16 na Auto-Combo shi ma yana la'akari da mataki. Duba
[`docs/routing/AUTO-COMBO.md`](../routing/AUTO-COMBO.md).

## Bayanan amfani

Dashboard → **Amfani** yana nuna tokens da aka kashe a kowane mataki a kowace rana. Yi amfani da wannan don:

- Tabbatar ana amfani da Mataki na 1 gaba ɗaya (in ba haka ba kana ɓata darajar biyan kuɗinka)
- Gano waɗanne samfuran Mataki na 2 aka fi zaɓa (taƙaita zuwa 1-2)
- Tabbatar Mataki na 3 yana rage kuɗi a ayyukan gwaji/bincike

## Hanyoyin amfani na yau da kullum

### Aikin kyauta kawai

```json
{
  "strategy": "auto",
  "config": { "auto": { "weights": { "costInv": 0.5, "tierPriority": 0.3 } } }
}
```

Yana karkata sosai zuwa Mataki na 3; yana amfani da Mataki na 2 ne kawai idan babu Mataki na 3.

### Fara da biyan kuɗin shiga tare da madadin mai arha

```json
{
  "strategy": "priority",
  "targets": [
    { "provider": "claude-code-oauth", "weight": 1 },
    { "provider": "deepseek", "weight": 1 },
    { "provider": "kiro", "weight": 1 }
  ]
}
```

Jerin da aka tsara a sarari wanda ya dace da Mataki na 1 → Mataki na 2 → Mataki na 3.
