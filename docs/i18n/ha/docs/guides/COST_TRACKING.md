Yadda OmniRoute ke ƙiyasta, yin rikodi, da bayar da rahoton kuɗin kowace buƙata — da kuma dalilin da ya sa
lambar da ke dashboard ta zama **mai bin diddigin tanadi**, ba takardar kuɗi ba.

Duba kuma: [Jagorar Mai Amfani](./USER_GUIDE.md) · [Taskar Fasaloli](./FEATURES.md)

## Abin da yake (da abin da ba shi ba)

OmniRoute yana danganta kuɗin USD na kowace buƙata ga kowane kammalawa ta hanyar ninka adadin token
da farashin samfurin. Waɗannan lambobi ne ke samar da bayanan dashboard na **Kuɗaɗe**,
CLI na `omniroute cost` / `omniroute usage`, fitarwar CSV/JSON, da kasafin kuɗin kowane mabuɗin API.

> **“Kuɗin” da ke dashboard mai bin diddigin tanadi ne, ba takardar kuɗi ba.** OmniRoute ba ya taɓa cajin ku
> — yana tura buƙatunku zuwa masu samar da sabis da kuka riga kuka haɗa (rajistocinku,
> matakan kyauta, da maɓallan API). “Jimillar kuɗi ta $290” da ta taru gaba ɗaya daga samfuran
> kyauta tana nufin kusan **$290 da ba ku biya ba** ga API mai kuɗi. Adadin _ƙiyasi_ ne
> na abin da zirga-zirgar iri ɗaya za ta ci a daidaitattun farashin kasuwa, domin ku iya ganin inda
> amfaninku ya fi yawa da kuma yawan kuɗin da turawa zuwa masu samar da sabis masu rahusa/kyauta ke adana muku.

An bayyana wannan ma’anar kai tsaye a cikin [README](../../README.md) na aikin (“‘kuɗin’ da ke dashboard
mai bin diddigin tanadi ne, ba takardar kuɗi ba”).

Saboda lambar ƙiyasi ce:

- Ta dogara da teburin farashin da OmniRoute yake da shi ga kowane samfuri. Samfurin da ba shi da shigarwar
  farashi yana bayar da kuɗin `0` (yana bayyana a matsayin layin “Tsoho / Kyauta” a cikin mai binciken).
- Zirga-zirgar matakin kyauta da ta rajista har yanzu tana tara _ƙiyastaccen_ kuɗi — wannan shi ne
  adadin da kuke adanawa, ba adadin da ake bin ku ba.

## Yadda ake ƙiyasta kuɗaɗe

### Tushen farashi

Kuɗaɗe suna fitowa ne daga teburin farashi da ake tantancewa bisa wannan jerin fifiko
([`src/lib/pricingSync.ts`](../../src/lib/pricingSync.ts)):

1. **Sauye-sauyen mai amfani** — farashin da kuka saita a dashboard / ta hanyar `PATCH /api/pricing`.
2. **Farashin waje da aka daidaita** — wanda aka ɗauko daga
   `model_prices_and_context_window.json` na jama'a na LiteLLM lokacin da aka kunna daidaitawa (ana adana shi a wani
   namespace na `pricing_synced` na daban don kada ya taɓa goge sauye-sauyenku).
3. **Tsoffin ƙimomin da aka dasa a lamba** — waɗanda aka haɗa da OmniRoute.

Daidaita farashin waje na buƙatar **bayyanannen amincewa**, kuma a kashe yake ta tsohuwa. Muhimman env vars
(duba [`.env.example`](../../.env.example)):

| Env var                 | Tsohon ƙima | Manufa                                                               |
| ----------------------- | ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `PRICING_SYNC_ENABLED`  | `false`     | Kunna daidaita farashin LiteLLM a bango lokacin farawa.              |
| `PRICING_SYNC_INTERVAL` | `86400`     | Tazarar daidaitawa cikin **daƙiƙoƙi** (ta tsohuwa kullum).           |
| `PRICING_SYNC_SOURCES`  | `litellm`   | Jerin tushe da waƙafi ya raba (a yau `litellm` kaɗai ake tallafawa). |

### Tsarin lissafin kuɗi

Ana lissafa kuɗi ga kowace buƙata daga adadin token da farashin kowace token miliyan a cikin
[`src/lib/usage/costCalculator.ts`](../../src/lib/usage/costCalculator.ts)
(`computeCostFromPricing` / `calculateCost`):

- **Token na shigarwa** (bayan cire karatun cache da token na ƙirƙirar cache) × farashin `input`.
- **Token na karatun cache** × farashin `cached` (yana komawa ga farashin shigarwa idan babu).
- **Token na ƙirƙirar cache** × farashin `cache_creation` (yana komawa ga farashin shigarwa idan babu).
- **Token na fitarwa** × farashin `output`.
- **Token na tunani** × farashin `reasoning` (yana komawa ga farashin fitarwa idan babu).

Ana fassara dukkan farashin a matsayin USD ga kowane token 1,000,000. Matakin sabis na Codex na “fast”/“priority” ko
“flex” yana amfani da ninkin kuɗi (`getCodexFastCostMultiplier`) — misali, ana cajin flex
da rangwamen token na 50%, wanda ake nuna shi a matsayin **tanadin flex** a dashboard.

Da farko ana daidaita sunayen samfura (ana cire farkon hanyar mai samar da sabis kamar `openai/` ko
`accounts/fireworks/models/`) domin tsoffin layukan tarihi su ci gaba da dacewa da farashi.

### Yadda ake yin rikodin kashe kuɗi

- Ana lissafa kuɗin kowace buƙata bayan amsa sannan a yi rikodinsa ba tare da jiran kammalawa ba, don kada ya
  taɓa ƙara jinkiri ga abokin ciniki. Ana tsara amfani da ƙayyadadden rabon gama-gari a zagayen
  event-loop na gaba ta hanyar [`src/lib/quota/spendRecorder.ts`](../../src/lib/quota/spendRecorder.ts).
- Ana tara kashe kuɗin mabuɗin API a ma'aji sannan a tura shi rukuni-rukuni ta hanyar
  [`SpendBatchWriter`](../../src/lib/spend/batchWriter.ts) (tazarar turawa ta tsohuwa ita ce 60s,
  ma'ajin shigarwa 1,000). Ana iya daidaita su ta hanyar:

  | Env var                             | Tsohon ƙima | Manufa                                           |
  | ----------------------------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
  | `OMNIROUTE_SPEND_FLUSH_INTERVAL_MS` | `60000`     | Tazarar turawa cikin milliseconds.               |
  | `OMNIROUTE_SPEND_MAX_BUFFER_SIZE`   | `1000`      | Matsakaicin shigarwar da za a tara kafin turawa. |

Ba a karanta alkaluman kuɗin dashboard daga adadin dala da aka adana ga kowane layi **ba** — ana
sake lissafa su nan take daga adadin token da teburin farashi na yanzu a duk lokacin da
endpoint na analytics ya gudana. Wannan yana nufin gyara farashi mara daidai (da sake daidaitawa) yana sabunta
ƙiyasin kuɗin tarihi ta baya.

## Dashboard: shafin Kuɗaɗe

Shafin **Kuɗaɗe** yana a `/dashboard/costs`
(`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/costs/`).
Babban kallonsa shi ne shafin **Bayanin Kuɗaɗe**
(`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/costs/CostOverviewTab.tsx`),
wanda ke loda komai daga `GET /api/usage/analytics`.

Abubuwan da yake nunawa:

- **Katunan kashe kuɗi** — ƙiyasin kuɗin da aka kashe na _Yau (1d)_, _7d_, _30d_, da zangon
  da aka zaɓa. Mai zaɓin zango: `7d`, `30d`, `90d`, `all`.
- **Manyan ma'aunai** — buƙatu a cikin zangon, masu samarwa masu aiki, samfura masu aiki, matsakaicin
  kuɗi ga kowace buƙata.
- **Mai Binciken Kuɗi** — tebur mai iya jerantawa/tacewa wanda aka haɗa bisa **mai samarwa**, **samfuri**,
  **maɓallin API**, **asusu**, ko **matakin sabis**, tare da kuɗi, buƙatu, token, matsakaicin
  kuɗi/buƙata, da kaso na jimilla %.
- **Amfani da token** — jimillar / token na shigarwa / token na fitarwa da rabon shigarwa:fitarwa.
- **Ingancin zaɓin hanya** — adadin komawa madadin, ƙimar komawa madadin, da ɗaukar samfurin da aka nema.
- **Hasashen wata-wata** — yana hasashen kuɗin da za a kashe zuwa ƙarshen wata daga matsakaicin kuɗin yau da kullum na kwanan nan.
- **Kwatanta lokuta** — canjin % tsakanin rabin farko da na biyu na zangon.
- **Jadawali** — yanayin kuɗin yau da kullum, rabon mai samarwa (da'ira), manyan masu samarwa, manyan samfura, kuɗi
  bisa maɓallin API, kuɗi bisa asusu, tsarin amfani na mako-mako, da taswirar zafin ayyuka.
- **Fitarwa** — sauke zangon yanzu a matsayin **CSV** ko **JSON** (maɓallan suna bayyana
  da zarar akwai bayanan kuɗi da ba sifili ba).

Lokacin da babu zirga-zirgar da aka sanya wa farashi, layuka suna nuna alamar "Tsoho / Kyauta" maimakon `$0`,
domin nuna tsarin bibiyar tanadi.

### Ƙananan shafukan Kuɗaɗe masu alaƙa

Yankin Kuɗaɗe kuma yana ɗauke da (duk a ƙarƙashin `/dashboard/costs/`):

- **Farashi** (`/dashboard/costs/pricing`) — duba da sauya farashin kowane samfuri (yana nuna
  shafin Farashi na bai ɗaya).
- **Kasafin kuɗi** (`/dashboard/costs/budget`) — saita iyakokin kashe kuɗi ga kowane fage (yana nuna
  shafin Kasafin kuɗi na bai ɗaya).
- **Rabon Ƙa'ida** (`/dashboard/costs/quota-share`) — tafkunan ƙa'ida na bai ɗaya da kallon saurin
  ƙarewa.

## Wuraren ƙarshen API

Duk waɗannan suna buƙatar tantancewar gudanarwa (loopback/JWT, ta hanyar `requireManagementAuth`) sai dai idan
an bayyana akasin haka.

### Nazarin amfani da kuɗi

| Hanya | Wurin ƙarshe             | Manufa                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ----- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `GET` | `/api/usage/analytics`   | Cikakken nazarin kuɗi/amfani: taƙaitawa, yanayin yau da kullum, bisa mai samarwa/samfuri/maɓallin API/asusu/mataki. Tambaya: `range`, `startDate`, `endDate`, `apiKeyIds`, `presets`. |
| `GET` | `/api/usage/utilization` | Amfani da ƙa'idar kowane mai samarwa a tsawon lokaci. Tambaya: `range` (`1h`/`24h`/`7d`/`30d`), `provider`.                                                                           |
| `GET` | `/api/usage/history`     | Layukan ɗanyen tarihin amfani.                                                                                                                                                        |
| `GET` | `/api/usage/call-logs`   | Rajistan kira na kowace buƙata (samfuri, token, kuɗi, jinkiri, matsayi).                                                                                                              |
| `GET` | `/api/usage/quota`       | Matsayin ƙa'idar mai samarwa.                                                                                                                                                         |
| `GET` | `/api/usage/proxy-logs`  | Rajistan buƙatun proxy.                                                                                                                                                               |

### Kasafin kuɗi

| Hanya  | Wurin ƙarshe             | Manufa                                                                                        |
| ------ | ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `GET`  | `/api/usage/budget`      | Taƙaitaccen kuɗi + duba kasafin kuɗi don maɓallin API guda (`apiKeyId` dole ne a tambaya).    |
| `POST` | `/api/usage/budget`      | Saita iyakokin USD na yau da kullum/mako-mako/wata-wata + matakin faɗakarwa don maɓallin API. |
| `GET`  | `/api/usage/budget/bulk` | Taƙaitaccen kasafin kuɗi na jimilla a duk maɓallan API.                                       |

> API na kasafin kuɗi yana iyakantuwa ga kowane **maɓallin API** (`apiKeyId`). Iyakokin da
> `GET /api/usage/budget` ke mayarwa sun haɗa da `dailyLimitUsd`, `weeklyLimitUsd`, `monthlyLimitUsd`,
> `warningThreshold`, da jimillolin da ke gudana (`totalCostToday`, `totalCostMonth`, …).

### Farashi

| Hanya    | Wurin ƙarshe            | Manufa                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `GET`    | `/api/pricing`          | Farashi da aka haɗa a halin yanzu (na mai amfani + daidaitacce + tsoffin ƙimomi). Yi amfani da `?includeSources=1` don ganin tushe ga kowace shigarwa. |
| `PATCH`  | `/api/pricing`          | Sauya farashi don `{ provider: { model: { input, output, cached, … } } }`.                                                                             |
| `DELETE` | `/api/pricing`          | Mayar da farashi zuwa tsoffin ƙimomi (za a iya iyakancewa ta `?provider=&model=`).                                                                     |
| `GET`    | `/api/pricing/defaults` | Nuna tsoffin ƙimar madadin ga kowane miliyan 1.                                                                                                        |
| `GET`    | `/api/pricing/models`   | Farashin da aka tsara bisa samfuri.                                                                                                                    |
| `POST`   | `/api/pricing/sync`     | Fara daidaitawa da hannu daga tushe na waje (LiteLLM).                                                                                                 |
| `GET`    | `/api/pricing/sync`     | Matsayin daidaitawa na yanzu.                                                                                                                          |
| `DELETE` | `/api/pricing/sync`     | Share duk bayanan farashin da aka daidaita.                                                                                                            |

### Sauran wuraren ƙarshe masu alaƙa da kuɗi

| Hanya | Endpoint                      | Manufa                                                                                                   |
| ----- | ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `GET` | `/api/free-tier/summary`      | Jimillar token na samfurin kyauta, waɗanda aka yi amfani da su a wannan watan, da ragowar adadin kyauta. |

## CLI

CLI na OmniRoute yana samar da umarnin kuɗi, amfani, da farashi (waɗanda aka yi wa rajista a cikin
[`bin/cli/commands/registry.mjs`](../../bin/cli/commands/registry.mjs)).

### `omniroute cost`

Rahoton kuɗi da aka tara daga `/api/usage/analytics`.

```bash
omniroute cost                          # kwanaki 30 da suka gabata, an haɗa bisa mai samarwa
omniroute cost --period 7d              # kwanaki 7 da suka gabata
omniroute cost --group-by model         # haɗa bisa provider | model | combo | api-key | day
omniroute cost --since 2026-06-01 --until 2026-06-13
omniroute cost --api-key <key> --limit 50
```

Ginshikai: rukuni, buƙatu, tokens masu shiga/fita, kuɗi (USD), da % na jimilla. Ana buga layin
jimillar gaba ɗaya a ƙarshe (ana ɓoye shi da `--quiet` ko `--output json`).

### `omniroute usage`

```bash
omniroute usage analytics --period 30d [--provider <id>]   # taƙaitaccen kuɗi na kowane mai samarwa
omniroute usage logs [--limit 100] [--follow] [--api-key <k>] [--search <q>]
omniroute usage quota [--provider <id>] [--check]
omniroute usage utilization [--api-key <k>]
omniroute usage history [--limit 100]
omniroute usage proxy-logs [--limit 100]

# Kasafin kuɗi
omniroute usage budget list
omniroute usage budget get [scope]
omniroute usage budget set <amount> [--scope global] [--period monthly]
omniroute usage budget reset [scope]
```

### `omniroute pricing`

```bash
omniroute pricing list [--provider <p>] [--model <m>] [--limit 200]
omniroute pricing get <model>
omniroute pricing sync [--provider <p>] [--force]   # POST /api/pricing/sync
omniroute pricing diff [--model <m>]
omniroute pricing defaults show
omniroute pricing defaults set [--input <p>] [--output <p>] [--cache-read <p>] [--cache-write <p>]
```

> `pricing defaults show` yana karanta `GET /api/pricing/defaults`. Don gyara farashin kowane model
> dabam, yi amfani da shafin dashboard na **Farashi** ko `PATCH /api/pricing`.

## Warware Matsaloli

- **Duk kuɗaɗe suna nuna $0 / "Tsoho / Kyauta".** Model ɗin da ake amfani da su ba su da bayanin farashi.
  Kunna daidaitawar waje (`PRICING_SYNC_ENABLED=true`) sannan gudanar da `omniroute pricing sync`, ko
  saita farashin da hannu ta shafin Farashi / `PATCH /api/pricing`.
- **An sa wa wani model na tarihi farashi ba daidai ba.** Gyara farashin (sake rubuta shi ko sake daidaitawa) — ana
  sake lissafa kuɗi daga adadin token a duk lokacin karanta bayanan nazari, don haka ƙiyasin yana sabuntawa har zuwa bayanan da suka gabata.
- **Kashe kuɗi yana jinkirin bin ainihin lokaci.** Ana tattara kashe kuɗin kowace key a rukuni-rukuni; rage
  `OMNIROUTE_SPEND_FLUSH_INTERVAL_MS` idan kana buƙatar sabbin alkaluma.
