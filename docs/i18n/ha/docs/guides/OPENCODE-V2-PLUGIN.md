`@omniroute/opencode-plugin-v2` yana sanya dukkan kundin OmniRoute ɗinka — models, combos da
auto-combos — cikin mai zaɓar model na OpenCode v2, tare da sunayen nuni, farashi da
kasafin free-tier.

Kunshi ne daban da `@omniroute/opencode-plugin` saboda OpenCode v1 da v2 suna loda
plugins ta hanyar ƙa'idoji daban-daban. Zaɓi wanda ya dace da babban sigar OpenCode ɗinka; babu abin da
suke rabawa a tsakaninsu, don haka haɓaka ɗaya ba zai taɓa tilasta haɓaka ɗayan ba.

## Abubuwan da ake buƙata

- OpenCode v2.
- Ƙofar OmniRoute da za a iya isa (`http://localhost:20128` ta tsohuwa).
- Node.js 22 ko 24.

## Shigarwa

Ƙara plugin ɗin zuwa `opencode.json`:

```json
{
  "plugins": [
    {
      "package": "@omniroute/opencode-plugin-v2",
      "options": {
        "providerId": "omniroute",
        "baseURL": "http://localhost:20128"
      }
    }
  ]
}
```

Daga nan models za su bayyana a matsayin `omniroute/<provider>/<model>`; `providerId` ne ke ƙayyade wannan prefix da
id na haɗin da OpenCode ke adana credential a ƙarƙashinsa.

## Credentials

Plugin ɗin yana neman mabuɗin gateway a wurare uku, bisa wannan jeri:

1. **Credential ɗin da ka haɗa a OpenCode.** Plugin ɗin yana rajistar integration, don haka
   tsarin auth na OpenCode zai iya adana mabuɗin. Babu abin da zai shiga `opencode.json` — wannan ya fi dacewa.
2. `apiKey` a cikin zaɓuɓɓukan plugin, domin yin override na kowane project. Wannan yana sanya mabuɗin cikin fayil da
   wataƙila kake commit.
3. `OMNIROUTE_API_KEY` a cikin environment.

Idan babu ɗaya daga cikin ukun, kundin zai zama fanko kuma plugin ɗin zai faɗi hakan sau ɗaya lokacin farawa maimakon
barinka da mai zaɓe fanko ba tare da wani bayani ba.

### Management token wani mabuɗi ne daban

Combos, lafiyar provider da enrichment (sunayen nuni, farashi, da kasafin free-tier) suna zuwa ne daga
endpoints na `/api/*` na gateway, waɗanda yawancin deployments ke karewa da token na **management**
maimakon mabuɗin inference:

```json
"options": {
  "baseURL": "http://localhost:20128",
  "managementReadToken": "<management read token>"
}
```

Idan ba a saita shi ba, `managementReadToken` zai koma amfani da `apiKey`. Idan gateway ya ƙi wannan fallback,
har yanzu za a wallafa kundin, amma da raw model ids maimakon sunayen nuni, ba tare da canonical
alias dedupe ba, ba tare da farashi ba, kuma ba tare da combos ba. Plugin ɗin zai yi gargaɗi sau ɗaya ga kowane endpoint idan hakan ta faru,
yana bayyana endpoint ɗin da abin da aka rasa — don kada mai zaɓe da ya ragu inganci ya kasance abin mamaki.

## Zaɓuɓɓuka

| Mabuɗi                           | Tsoho                                            | Bayani                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `providerId`                     | `"omniroute"`                                    | Provider id, integration id, da prefix da models ke bayyana a ƙarƙashinsa                                                |
| `baseURL`                        | dole ne                                          | Tushen gateway, `http(s)` kawai; ana ƙara suffix na `/v1` inda ake buƙata                                                |
| `apiKey`                         | connected credential, sannan `OMNIROUTE_API_KEY` | Mabuɗin chat na `/v1/*`                                                                                                  |
| `managementReadToken`            | yana komawa amfani da `apiKey`                   | Mabuɗin `/api/*` — yawanci **ba** ɗaya ba ne                                                                             |
| `displayName`                    | `"OmniRoute"`                                    | Sunan provider a cikin mai zaɓe                                                                                          |
| `timeoutMs`                      | `10000`                                          | Lokacin ƙarewar fetch na kowane endpoint (auto-combos suna amfani da 5s)                                                 |
| `modelCacheTtlMs`                | `300000`                                         | TTL na cache ɗin kundin; snapshot na disk yana sa cold starts su yi sauri                                                |
| `timeouts`                       | yana komawa amfani da `timeoutMs`                | Kasafin lokaci na kowane endpoint a ms: `models`, `combos`, `autoCombos`, `enrichment`                                   |
| `enrichment`                     | `true`                                           | Ɗauko sunaye, farashi da kasafin free-tier                                                                               |
| `providerTag`                    | `true`                                           | Sanya upstream provider da ake bi ta cikinsa a farkon sunan nuni                                                         |
| `usableOnly`                     | `false`                                          | Riƙe providers kaɗai waɗanda gateway ya bayyana a matsayin provisioned                                                   |
| `visibleModels` / `hiddenModels` | `[]`                                             | Allowlists na exact-or-suffix; deny yana rinjaya                                                                         |
| `geminiSanitization`             | `true`                                           | Cire keywords na JSON-Schema waɗanda Gemini ba ya karɓa daga tool schemas (`$ref` tools ana tura su ba tare da canji ba) |
| `apiFormat.allowAnthropic`       | `false`                                          | Tura ids da ke cikin allowlist ta hanyar Anthropic API block                                                             |
| `apiFormat.anthropicModels`      | `[]`                                             | Cikakkun model ids da ake turawa zuwa Anthropic                                                                          |
| `logLevel` / `startupDebug`      | `warn` / `false`                                 | Yawan bayanan logger                                                                                                     |

## Yadda kundin ke ci gaba da kasancewa sabo

Ana ɗauko kundin ne kawai lokacin da ake buƙatarsa sannan a adana shi na tsawon `modelCacheTtlMs`, kuma kwafin faifai yana tabbatar da cewa kundin ƙarshe da aka sani yana nan lokacin da ba a iya isa ga gateway — katsewar sabis ba ta jawo maka komai sai rashin sabuntawa. Ana wallafa models da combos da zarar sun iso; auto-combos, jerin provider da enrichment overlay suna haɗuwa da zarar sun iso, don kada endpoint guda mai jinkiri ya riƙe gabaɗayan picker ɗin.

Sunaye suna ɗauke da abin da gateway ya sani: upstream provider da model ke bi ta cikinsa, alamar `[Free]` da kuma budget da ke tare da ita — ta haka connections biyu da ke sayar da model iri ɗaya za su ci gaba da bambanta. Kashe prefix ɗin da `"providerTag": false`.

Babu umarnin sabuntawa da hannu: umarnin OpenCode v2 samfuran prompt ne, ba callbacks ba, don haka plugin ba zai iya samar da ɗaya ba. Sabuntawa suna bin TTL; ana neman host ya sake lodawa ne kawai idan kundin ko overlay ya canza da gaske, ba sau ɗaya a kowane sabuntawa ba.

## Kiran kayan aiki a Gemini

Gemini yana ƙin gabaɗayan request idan tool declarations ɗinsa suna ɗauke da `$schema` ko `additionalProperties`, yana amsawa da `400 INVALID_ARGUMENT`. Plugin ɗin yana cire waɗannan keywords daga tool schemas da aka nufa ga Gemini model na wannan provider, yana kuma barin duk sauran requests ba tare da taɓa su ba. Tool da ke ɗauke da `$ref` ana tura shi ba tare da an taɓa shi ba maimakon a cire wani abu, domin cire reference ɗin zai faɗaɗa schema ɗin zuwa “karɓi komai”.
Saita `"geminiSanitization": false` don kashe shi.

## Warware matsaloli

| Alama                                    | Dalili                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Picker bai nuna wani OmniRoute model ba  | Ba a samo key ba (duba gargaɗin farawa), ko kuma ba a iya isa ga gateway                                                  |
| Raw model ids, babu combos, babu pricing | Management endpoints sun ƙi token ɗin — saita `managementReadToken`                                                       |
| An manne session ga `opencode-<id>/…`    | Plugin na v1 ya wallafa `opencode-<id>`; v2 yana wallafa `<id>` kai tsaye, don haka sake zaɓar model a ƙarƙashin `<id>/…` |
