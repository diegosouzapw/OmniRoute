> **Dashboard:** Saituna → **AI** → Kasafin Tunani  
> **API:** `GET` / `PUT` `/api/settings/thinking-budget`  
> **Source:** `open-sse/services/thinkingBudget.ts`

Kasafin Tunani yana sarrafa ko OmniRoute zai **sake rubuta sigogin tunani/nazari na abokin ciniki** yayin aika buƙata zuwa masu samarwa. Ba ya kunna ko kashe matse bayanai, zaɓin hanya, ko ma’ajiyar wucin-gadi ta prompt.

## Yanaye

| Yanayi                    | Abin da OmniRoute yake yi                                                                                                                | Lokacin amfani                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **`passthrough`** (tsoho) | Yana barin filayen abokin ciniki yadda suke (`reasoning`, `reasoning_effort`, Claude `thinking`, Gemini `thinking_config`, da sauransu). | **Codex / Desktop / duk wani abokin ciniki da ya kamata ya sarrafa ƙoƙari + taƙaitattun bayanan nazari.** Wajibi ne don ganin sassan tunani lokacin da abokin ciniki ya nemi `reasoning.summary`. |
| **`auto`**                | Yana **cire dukkan** filayen tunani/nazari daga jikin buƙatar kafin aikawa zuwa upstream.                                                | Sai idan da gangan kake son **mai samarwa** ya ƙirƙiri saitunan tsoho kuma ba ka buƙatar tunanin da abokin ciniki ke sarrafawa. **Ba** yana nufin “nuna tunani ta atomatik” ba.                   |
| **`custom`**              | Yana maye gurbin kowace buƙata da tsayayyen kasafin token na tunani.                                                                     | Don sanya iyaka mai tsauri ga token na tunani a kan duk zirga-zirga.                                                                                                                              |
| **`adaptive`**            | Yana daidaita kasafin daga ƙoƙari na asali bisa adadin saƙonni, kayan aiki, da tsawon prompt.                                            | Don sarrafa token cikin sassauci ba tare da cire niyyar abokin ciniki gaba ɗaya ba.                                                                                                               |

### Abin da `auto` yake cirewa

Lokacin da yanayi yake `auto`, `stripThinkingConfig()` yana share abubuwa kamar haka:

- OpenAI / Responses: `reasoning`, `reasoning_effort`
- Claude: `thinking`, da `output_config.effort` idan yana nan
- Gemini: `generationConfig.thinking_config` / `thinkingConfig`

Idan abokin ciniki (misali Codex Desktop) ya aika `reasoning: { effort: "ultra", summary: "detailed" }`, **auto yana cire wannan object ɗin**. Upstream na iya ci gaba da lissafa kuɗin wasu token na nazari, amma sau da yawa yana dawo da abubuwan nazari **marasa komai ko masu ɓoyayyen bayanai kawai** — don haka UI ba zai nuna wani rafin tunani mai amfani ba.

## Abin da wannan **ba ya nufi**

| Fasali                                       | Alaƙa                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Matse bayanai** (Caveman, RTK, stacked, …) | Pipeline ne na daban. Yana aiki a ƙarƙashin kowane yanayin kasafin tunani.                                                                                                              |
| **Ma’ajiyar prompt / semantic**              | Daban ce. Yanayin kasafin tunani ba ya shafarta.                                                                                                                                        |
| **Combo routing / fallbacks**                | Daban ne. Ba ya shafarsu.                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Iyakokin token na API-key / kasafin kuɗi** | Daban ne. Ba ya shafarsu.                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Ma’ajiyar sake kunna nazari**              | Sake shigar da bayanai tsakanin tattaunawa masu zagaye da yawa ga masu samarwa masu tsauraran ƙa’idoji (DeepSeek, Kimi, Qwen-thinking, …). Ba ɗaya yake da “nuna tunani” na Desktop ba. |
| **Warware `encrypted_content`**              | **Ba zai yiwu ba.** Keɓantattun blobs na nazari na OpenAI/Codex ba sa bayyana abin da ke cikinsu. OmniRoute ba ya taɓa warware su (#7095 / #7176 / #7304).                              |

## Bayyanannen tunani (abokan hulɗar Codex / Responses)

Domin abokin hulɗa ya nuna rubutun tunani, kana buƙatar **dukkan** waɗannan:

1. Yanayin Thinking Budget = **`passthrough`** (ko custom/adaptive wanda har yanzu yake barin buƙatun taƙaitawa yadda ya kamata don hanyar da kake amfani da ita).
2. Abokin hulɗa ya nemi taƙaitawa, misali Codex `model_reasoning_summary = "detailed"` / `auto` (ba `none` ba).
3. Upstream ya watsa `response.reasoning_summary_text.*` a zahiri (ko kuma `reasoning.summary` mara komai ba a kan abin).

Idan kana samun “encrypted private reasoning” kawai, to ko dai:

- yanayin ya kasance **`auto`** (an cire buƙatar abokin hulɗa), ko
- upstream ya dawo da `encrypted_content` ba tare da rubutun taƙaitawa ba (ƙuntatawar mai samarwa ce; OmniRoute zai iya nuna alamar madadi kawai, ba bayyanannen rubutu ba).

## Misalan API

```bash
# Karanta
curl -sS https://localhost:20128/api/settings/thinking-budget \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OMNIROUTE_TOKEN"

# Abin da aka ba da shawara don bayyanar tunanin Codex / Desktop
curl -sS -X PUT https://localhost:20128/api/settings/thinking-budget \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OMNIROUTE_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"mode":"passthrough","customBudget":10240,"effortLevel":"medium"}'
```

Tsari (`updateThinkingBudgetSchema`): `mode` ∈ `passthrough|auto|custom|adaptive`; `customBudget`, `effortLevel`, `baseBudget`, `complexityMultiplier` na zaɓi ne.

### Dorewa / sake farawa

Ana adana ƙimar ƙarƙashin maɓallin saituna `thinkingBudget`, sannan a loda ta lokacin fara aikin tsari (`hydrateThinkingBudgetConfig`). Bayan canzawa ta DB ko wasu hanyoyin da ba na API ba, **sake fara tsarin OmniRoute** domin singleton da ke cikin ƙwaƙwalwa ya yi daidai da abin da ke kan faifai.

## Jerin dubawar mai gudanarwa

- [ ] Masu amfani da Codex / Desktop: yanayi = **passthrough**
- [ ] Bar matsewa a kunne idan kana son rage amfani da token a kan **saƙonni**, ba ta hanyar cire tunani ba
- [ ] Kada ka yi tsammanin `auto` zai “nuna ƙarin tunani”
- [ ] Taƙaitawa masu rufaffen bayani kawai halayyar **mai samarwa** ce; passthrough ba zai iya warware rufin bayanin ba
