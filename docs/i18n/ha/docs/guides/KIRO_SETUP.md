Wannan jagorar ta ƙunshi yadda ake ƙara asusun Kiro (mataimakin rubuta lambar AI da AWS ke ɗaukar nauyinsa) zuwa OmniRoute,
tare da mai da hankali kan gudanar da asusu da yawa a lokaci guda ba tare da rikicin zama ba.

---

## Bayani: Dalilin da ya sa Asusun Kiro Za Su Iya Yin Rikici

Tsarin bayan Kiro yana amfani da rajistar abokan cinikin AWS SSO OIDC don bibiyar zaman da ke aiki.
Muhimmin ƙuntatawar ita ce: **kowace rajistar abokin cinikin OIDC tana goyon bayan zama guda ɗaya
kawai a lokaci guda**. Lokacin da wata na'ura ko wani mai amfani ya tabbatar da shiga ta amfani da
abokin ciniki ɗaya da aka yi wa rajista, tsarin bayan yana soke refresh token na asusun farko.

Wannan ita ce hanyar guda ɗaya da ke haifar da matsaloli lokacin gudanar da `kiro-cli login` a kan
na'urar da wani asusun Kiro ya riga ya shiga — sabon shigar yana soke token na
asusun farko.

---

## Yadda OmniRoute Ke Magance Wannan (v3.8.0+)

Tun daga v3.8.0, OmniRoute yana kiran `registerClient()` (AWS SSO OIDC) yayin kowace
shigo da haɗin Kiro. Wannan yana ba kowane haɗin OmniRoute rajistar abokin cinikin OIDC
ta kansa. Saboda kowace rajistar abokin ciniki mai zaman kanta ce, sabuntawa ko
sake tabbatar da asusu ɗaya ba ya shafar refresh token na wani asusu.

Keɓewar tana aiki ga hanyoyin shigo da refresh-token, yayin da tantancewa ta API-key take
kauce wa zaman sabuntawar OIDC gaba ɗaya:

| Hanyar shigo da bayanai                              | Matsayin keɓewa                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Gudanarwar device-code ta AWS Builder ID / IDC       | An keɓe ta tun lokacin da aka gabatar da gudanarwar device-code                                      |
| **Import Token** (liƙa refresh token da hannu)       | An keɓe ta daga v3.8.0                                                                               |
| **Shiga ta Google / GitHub**                         | An keɓe ta daga v3.8.0                                                                               |
| **Auto-Import** (kiro-cli SQLite)                    | An keɓe ta daga v3.8.0 (an riga an keɓe hanyar SQLite; yanzu ma hanyar madadin SSO-cache an keɓe ta) |
| **API Key** (maɓallin CodeWhisperer na dogon lokaci) | Babu zaman sabuntawa; ana inganta maɓallin sannan a adana shi a matsayin shaidar bearer              |

## Bayanin Ƙaura don Haɗe-haɗen da Aka Ƙirƙira Kafin v3.8.0

Haɗe-haɗen da aka shigo da su kafin v3.8.0 ba su da rajistar abokin cinikin OIDC ta musamman
da aka adana a cikin `providerSpecificData`. Waɗannan haɗe-haɗen za su ci gaba da aiki amma suna amfani da
wurin ƙarshen sabunta tantancewar zamantakewa na bai ɗaya, wanda ke nufin irin waɗannan haɗe-haɗe guda biyu
za su iya ci gaba da soke juna.

**Don samun keɓewa:** share tsohon haɗin daga **Dashboard → Providers** sannan
a sake shigo da shi ta amfani da kowace hanyar shigo da bayanai da ake goyon baya. Duk sabbin haɗe-haɗen da aka ƙirƙira
za su karɓi rajistar abokin ciniki ta kansu kai tsaye.

## Ƙara Asusun Kiro Guda Biyu Tare da Juna

### Abubuwan da ake Buƙata Tun Farko

- OmniRoute v3.8.0 ko mafi sabo.
- Asusun Kiro mai aiki (imel + kalmar sirri, ko shiga ta Google, ko GitHub).
- Idan ana so, asusun Kiro na biyu.

### Mataki na 1: Shigo da asusun farko

1. Buɗe **Dashboard → Providers → Add Provider → Kiro**.
2. Zaɓi ɗaya daga cikin:
   - **Import Token** — liƙa refresh token da ke farawa da `aorAAAAAG`.
   - **API Key** — liƙa API key na Kiro / CodeWhisperer mai dogon lokaci.
   - **Shiga ta Google / GitHub** — kammala gudanarwar OAuth a cikin burauza.
   - **Auto-Import** — danna maɓallin; OmniRoute zai karanta bayanan shaidar shiga daga
     bayanan kiro-cli na cikin na'ura ko `~/.aws/sso/cache`.
3. Ana adana haɗin. Gudanarwar refresh-token za ta yi wa abokin cinikin OIDC na musamman
   rajista kai tsaye. Gudanarwar API-key tana inganta maɓallin tare da AWS kuma ba ta adana refresh token.

### Mataki na 2: Shigo da asusun na biyu

Maimaita mataki na 1 don asusun na biyu. Saboda kowace shigo da bayanai tana ƙirƙirar rajistar
abokin cinikin OIDC ta daban, haɗe-haɗen biyu suna da cikakkiyar keɓewa.

### Mataki na 3: Tabbatar cewa duk haɗe-haɗen biyu suna aiki

1. **Dashboard → Providers** — ya kamata duk haɗe-haɗen Kiro biyu su nuna matsayin **Active**.
2. **Dashboard → Health** — ya kamata duk haɗe-haɗen biyu su wuce gwajin lafiyar token nasu.

### Mataki na 4: Yi amfani da combo don karkatarwa tsakanin asusun

Ƙirƙiri combo mai ɗauke da haɗe-haɗen biyu a matsayin wuraren da ake nufi don daidaita nauyi ko komawa daga ɗaya zuwa ɗaya:

```
kiro/kiro-dev → kiro/kiro-pro
```

Duba [FEATURES.md](./FEATURES.md) da takardun karkatarwa don daidaita combo.

## Masu Amfani na Kamfanoni / IDC

Don asusun AWS IAM Identity Center (IDC), yi amfani da tsarin **AWS Builder ID / lambar na’urar IDC**
daga **Dashboard → Providers → Kiro → Device Code**. Tsarin lambar na’urar
ya kasance a ware gaba ɗaya tun farko. Ba a buƙatar sake shigo da waɗannan haɗin.

Masu amfani na kamfanoni waɗanda ke aiki a yankin AWS wanda ba na asali ba za su iya ƙayyade yankin yayin
shigo da shi ta hanyar Import Token API:

```bash
curl -X POST http://localhost:20128/api/oauth/kiro/import \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"refreshToken": "aorAAAAAG...", "region": "eu-west-1"}'
```

Idan ba a saka filin `region` ba, ƙimarsa ta asali ita ce `us-east-1`.

## Tsarin Shigo da API-Key

Tabbatarwa ta API-key ana amfani da ita ne don takardun shaidar bearer na Kiro / AWS CodeWhisperer masu dogon lokaci. Ba ta
amfani da sabuntawar OAuth, don haka tana kauce wa lalacewar zaman OIDC da aka raba.

### Dashboard

1. Buɗe **Dashboard -> Providers -> Kiro**.
2. Zaɓi **API Key**.
3. Liƙa API key da yankin AWS na zaɓi (`us-east-1` ne na asali).
4. OmniRoute yana tabbatar da key ɗin sannan ya adana haɗin.

### API

```bash
curl -X POST http://localhost:20128/api/oauth/kiro/api-key \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"apiKey": "kiro_or_codewhisperer_key", "region": "us-east-1"}'
```

### Yarjejeniyar Ciki

Hanyar API tana tabbatar da key ɗin ta hanyar kiran `KiroService.validateApiKey()`, wanda
ke amfani da `ListAvailableProfiles` a kan endpoint na CodeWhisperer/Amazon Q da ya dace da yankin
kuma yana gano `profileArn`.

Haɗin da aka adana yana amfani da:

```json
{
  "authType": "apikey",
  "providerSpecificData": {
    "authMethod": "api_key",
    "region": "us-east-1",
    "profileArn": "arn:aws:codewhisperer:..."
  }
}
```

A lokacin aiki, `KiroExecutor.buildHeaders()` yana aika key ɗin a matsayin
`Authorization: Bearer <key>` kuma yana ƙara `tokentype: API_KEY`. Kiran ƙididdigar amfani/profile
yana amfani da wannan alamar domin AWS ya ɗauki bearer ɗin a matsayin API key mai dogon lokaci maimakon
OIDC ko social access token.

---

## Ƙarewar Lokacin OIDC Client

AWS SSO OIDC public clients yawanci suna ƙarewa bayan kwanaki 90
(`clientSecretExpiresAt`). OmniRoute yana adana wannan timestamp a cikin `providerSpecificData`
don sa ido. Idan haɗi ya daina sabuntawa bayan kusan kwanaki 90, sake shigo da
haɗin domin samun sabon rajistar OIDC client. Ana bibiyar sake yin rajista ta atomatik bayan
ƙarewar lokaci a matsayin ingantawa ta gaba.

Haɗin API-key ba shi da ƙarewar lokacin OIDC client saboda ba ya sabuntawa
ta hanyar AWS SSO OIDC.

## Warware Matsaloli

### Asusun na biyu yana ci gaba da fita daga tsarin

- Duba duka haɗin biyu a **Dashboard → Providers** kuma tabbatar kowannensu yana nuna `clientId` wanda ba
  `null` ba a cikin ɗanyen JSON nasa (ana iya gani ta alamar bayanai). Idan ɗaya daga cikin haɗin ba shi da
  `clientId`, an shigo da shi kafin v3.8.0 — sake shigo da shi.

### Shigo da bayanai ya gaza da "Tabbatar da token ya gaza"

- Tabbatar refresh token ya fara da `aorAAAAAG`.
- Tabbatar OmniRoute zai iya isa ga `https://oidc.us-east-1.amazonaws.com` (ko yankin da aka saita).
  Idan kana bayan corporate proxy, saita proxy na matakin provider a
  **Dashboard → Settings → Proxies**.

### Shigo da API-key ya gaza

- Tabbatar key ɗin API key ne na Kiro / CodeWhisperer, ba refresh token ba.
- Tabbatar yankin AWS ya dace da key/asusun. `us-east-1` ne na asali.
- Dole ne key ɗin ya iya kiran `ListAvailableProfiles`; in ba haka ba OmniRoute ba zai iya
  gano `profileArn` da ake buƙata ba.

Don wasu matsaloli, duba babban [TROUBLESHOOTING.md](./TROUBLESHOOTING.md).
