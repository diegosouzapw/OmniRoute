## 1. Antigravity da Antigravity CLI (agy)

Duka masu samar da sabis ɗin suna amfani da **tsarin bayan-fage na Google iri ɗaya** — abokin OAuth iri ɗaya, sabunta token iri ɗaya, endpoints iri ɗaya, da asusun Google iri ɗaya. Bambancin shi ne samfuran da kake gani.

> Duba [sanarwar Antigravity CLI](https://antigravity.google/blog/introducing-google-antigravity-cli) don kwatancen hukuma na Google.

| Bangare               | `antigravity` (IDE)                                         | `agy` (CLI)                                                    |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Samfurin Google**   | Antigravity 2.0 / Antigravity IDE                           | Antigravity CLI                                                |
| **Tsarin bayan-fage** | Google Cloud Code API iri ɗaya                              | Google Cloud Code API iri ɗaya                                 |
| **OAuth / Token**     | Abokin iri ɗaya, sabuntawa iri ɗaya                         | Abokin iri ɗaya, sabuntawa iri ɗaya                            |
| **Kundin samfura**    | Tsayayyen jerin da aka zaɓa (an saka kai tsaye a OmniRoute) | Ana gwada shi kai tsaye daga Google ta `:fetchAvailableModels` |
| **Samfuran Claude**   | Sonnet 4.6, Opus 4.6 (nau'uka 4 kowanne)                    | Sonnet 4.6, Opus 4.6 (nau'uka 4 kowanne)                       |
| **Sunayen Gemini**    | Sunaye masu sauƙi (Low/Medium/High)                         | IDs na tushen sabis (extra-low/low/agent)                      |
| **Ƙarin samfura**     | `gpt-oss-120b-medium`                                       | Zai iya ƙunsar ƙarin samfura daga Google                       |
| **Amfani na asali**   | Haɗawa da IDE (VS Code, JetBrains)                          | Samun dama ta CLI / API                                        |
| **Ƙa'idar amfani**    | Ana rabawa da agy (asusun Google iri ɗaya)                  | Ana rabawa da antigravity (asusun Google iri ɗaya)             |

**Samfuran da ake da su (an tabbatar ta gwaji, 2026-07-29)**:

- Gemini: 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash, 3.1 Pro, 3 Flash, 2.5 Flash (matakan tunani iri-iri)
- Claude: Sonnet 4.6, Opus 4.6 (kowanne yana da nau'ukan default/low/medium/high)
- Sauran: GPT-OSS 120B Medium
- **Ba a samun Claude Sonnet 5** — nau'ukan 4.6 kaɗai ake tallafawa

**Dalilin da ya sa kundin samfuran ya bambanta**: CLI na Google an “inganta shi don sauri da rage nauyin aiki” kuma an “inganta shi tare da samfuran Gemini” (bisa ga bulogin hukuma na Google). Samfurin Web/IDE an “inganta shi don cikakken aiki.” CLI yana amfani da `:fetchAvailableModels` don gano samfura ta atomatik, yayin da IDE ke amfani da tsayayyen jerin da aka zaɓa.

**A aikace**: Yi amfani da prefix na `agy/` don samfuran Gemini (misali `agy/gemini-3.7-flash-high`). Yi amfani da `antigravity/` don tsayayyen jerin da aka zaɓa. Dukansu suna amfani da tsarin bayan-fage na Google iri ɗaya, amma suna nuna sunayen samfura daban-daban. Ana raba ƙa'idar amfani — amfani da kowane ɗaya daga cikin masu samar da sabis ɗin yana shiga cikin iyakokin asusun Google iri ɗaya.

Google Antigravity yana amfani da **ƙayyadadden amfani mai matakai biyu** bisa "Aikin da Aka Yi" (nauyin lissafi), ba yawan saƙonni ba.

- **Samfuran da ba Gemini ba** (Claude, GPT-OSS): Suna da iyakokin ƙimar amfani **na daban**. Za su iya ci gaba da kasancewa a shirye ko da an kulle Gemini.

### Kiredit na AI (Ƙarin Amfani)

### Muhimman Bayanai

- Ƙayyadadden amfani **na matakin asusu ne kuma ana rabawa** — asusun Google ɗaya a cikin Antigravity IDE, CLI, da OmniRoute yana amfani da ma'ajiyar ƙayyadadden amfani guda ɗaya

- Masu amfani da AI Pro sun bayar da rahoton **kullewa na kwanaki 7** maimakon sake farawa bayan awa 5 idan an kai ma'aunin mako (Google ya tabbatar cewa an tsara hakan ne saboda yawan buƙata)

**Lokacin da ƙayyadadden asusunka ya ƙare**: OmniRoute yana sake gwadawa kai tsaye da asusu na gaba da ake samu a cikin combo route. Ba a buƙatar sa hannun hannu.

## 3. Yadda Ake Samun projectId

Kowane haɗin antigravity/agy yana buƙatar `projectId` na Google Cloud Code. Idan babu shi, endpoint na `/v1internal:models` yana mayar da 404.

### Hanya A: Ta Atomatik (Ana Ba da Shawara)

OmniRoute yana gudanar da wannan ta atomatik. Lokacin da ka ƙara sabon asusun Google ta Dashboard OAuth:

1. OmniRoute yana sabunta token
2. Yana kiran `loadCodeAssist` don gano projectId
3. Idan babu project, yana kiran `onboardUser` don ƙirƙirar ɗaya
4. Yana sake gwada `loadCodeAssist` don samun sabon projectId da aka ƙirƙira
5. Yana adana shi a database

**Wannan yana aiki ga yawancin asusu** — ba a buƙatar matakan hannu.

### Hanya B: Da Hannu ta agy CLI

Idan ganowa ta atomatik ya gaza (duba Sashe na 5 don lokutan da hakan ke faruwa):

```bash
# Shigar da agy CLI (idan ba a riga an shigar da shi ba)
npm install -g @anthropic-ai/agy

# Shiga da asusun Google ɗinka
agy login

# Zaɓi asusun da ke buƙatar onboarding
# Wannan yana fara rajistar Cloud Code kuma yana sanya projectId
```

Bayan `agy login` ya yi nasara, sabunta token a OmniRoute Dashboard. Za a gano projectId ta atomatik.

### Yadda za a tabbatar

Duba database:

```bash
# A cikin container na OmniRoute
node -e "const db=require('better-sqlite3')('/app/data/storage.sqlite'); \
  console.log(JSON.stringify(db.prepare(\
    'SELECT email,project_id FROM provider_connections WHERE provider=\"agy\"'\
  ).all(), null, 2))"
```

Ko duba logs:

```
podman logs omniroute 2>&1 | grep "projectId discovered"
```

## 4. URI na Mayar da OAuth

### Matsalar

Google OAuth yana buƙatar ingantaccen URI na mayarwa. Tsohon saitin OmniRoute yana amfani da `http://127.0.0.1:20128/callback` (loopback). Wannan yana aiki ga ginin gida amma **yana kasa aiki a turawa na nesa** (misali, sabar da ake shiga ta LAN IP).

Google yana ƙin URI na mayarwa waɗanda:

- Suke amfani da adiresoshin IP (dole ne ya zama yanki mai ƙarewa da `.com`, `.org`, da sauransu)
- Ba su dace da URI na mayarwa da aka yi wa rajista a cikin saitin abokin ciniki na OAuth ba

### Mafita

**Zaɓi A: Yi amfani da tsarin OAuth da aka tanada (tsohon saiti)**

- Yana aiki lokacin da ka shiga OmniRoute daga `localhost` ko `127.0.0.1`
- Ba a buƙatar wani saiti

**Zaɓi B: Keɓaɓɓun bayanan shaidar OAuth**

- Saita `ANTIGRAVITY_OAUTH_CLIENT_TYPE=web` a cikin muhallinka
- Samar da naka bayanan shaidar Google OAuth:
  ```
  GOOGLE_OAUTH_CLIENT_ID=your-client-id
  GOOGLE_OAUTH_CLIENT_SECRET=your-client-secret
  ```
- Yi wa `https://your-domain.com/callback` rajista a matsayin URI na mayarwa da aka ba da izini a Google Cloud Console

**Zaɓi C: Yi amfani da agy CLI don shiga na farko**

- Gudanar da `agy login` a kan na'urar da za ta shiga OmniRoute
- Ana kammala tsarin OAuth a cikin na'urar, sannan a adana tokens
- Shigo da haɗin zuwa OmniRoute ta Dashboard

### Iyakoki

- Keɓaɓɓun bayanan shaidar OAuth suna buƙatar sunan yanki (Google ba ya karɓar adiresoshin IP a matsayin URI na mayarwa)
- Idan ba ka da yanki, yi amfani da Zaɓi A ko C maimakon haka

## 5. Warware Matsaloli: Lokacin da Saitawa ta Atomatik ta Kasa

OmniRoute yana gudanar da gano projectId da shigar da asusu ta atomatik ga yawancin asusu. Idan ya kasa, tushen matsalar yawanci yana ɗaya daga cikin waɗannan:

### An katange yankin asusun

**Alama**: `agy login` yana mayar da "Eligibility check failed: Your current account is not eligible for Antigravity, because it is not currently available in your location."

**Tushen matsala**: Asusun Google suna da filin "Country Association" a bayan fage wanda ake saitawa lokacin rajista. agy CLI da Cloud Code API suna bincika wannan filin sosai — sabanin Gemini na yanar gizo wanda yake bincika IP ɗinka na yanzu kawai.

> Don bincika ko canza yankin da ke da alaƙa da asusunka, ziyarci [Google Country Association Form](https://policies.google.com/country-association-form).

**Dalilin da ya sa Gemini na yanar gizo yake aiki amma agy ba ya aiki**:

- Gemini na yanar gizo / Google One: yana bincika IP na yanzu kawai (proxy yana aiki)
- agy CLI / Cloud Code API: yana karanta filin Country Association na bayan fage (proxy ba ya taimakawa)

**Gyara**:

1. Ziyarci [Google Country Association Form](https://policies.google.com/country-association-form) yayin da kake kan IP na Amurka
2. Miƙa buƙatar canjin yanki (zaɓi "I live in a different country")
3. Jira awanni 1-24 domin Google ya sarrafa buƙatar + sanarwar imel
4. Bayan haka `agy login` ya kamata ya yi nasara

### Asusun ba shi da aikin Cloud Code

**Alama**: Rajistan ayyuka suna nuna `loadCodeAssist returned no project id` da `onboardUser failed (400)`.

**Tushen matsala**: Ba a taɓa yin wa asusun rajista da Google Cloud Code ba, kuma shigar da asusun ta atomatik ta kasa.

**Gyara**: Gudanar da `agy login` da hannu don kunna rajistar Cloud Code, sannan sabunta token a OmniRoute Dashboard.

### Token ya ƙare ko an soke shi

**Alama**: Kurakuran 401 a cikin rajistan ayyuka, ko saƙonnin "Token has expired".

**Gyara**: Sabunta token a Dashboard → Providers → agy → Danna alamar sabuntawa. Idan an soke refresh token ɗin kansa, za ka buƙaci sake tabbatar da izini ta OAuth.
