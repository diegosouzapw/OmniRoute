---

OmniRoute yana samar da hanyoyi **biyu** na amfani da OpenAI Codex:

| Mai samarwa            | Yadda yake sadarwa da OpenAI                                                                                                                                                                                   | Gargadin amfani                                                |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **`codex`**            | Yana sake aika token ɗin ChatGPT/OpenAI OAuth ɗinka kai tsaye zuwa Responses API                                                                                                                               | **Eh** — ba a ba zaman hukuma izinin amfani da proxy/router ba |
| **`codex-app-server`** | Yana sarrafa **`codex app-server` na Codex CLI da kansa** ta JSON-RPC/WebSocket; CLI ne ke mallaka kuma yake sabunta OAuth ɗinsa da kansa (`~/.codex/auth.json`) daidai kamar zaman `codex` na hulɗa kai tsaye | **A'a** — OmniRoute ba ya taɓa sake aika token zuwa API        |

Saboda `codex-app-server` ba ya taɓa sake aika token, bai ƙunshi gargadin
amfani na sake aika zaman ba. Amma yana buƙatar **Codex CLI da za a iya isa gare shi a
URL na app-server da aka saita**, kuma dole ne a **shiga** wannan CLI.

---

## 1. Tsarin gine-gine

```
┌─ Manhajar OmniRoute ─────────────┐        ┌─ Sidecar na codex-app-server ───────┐
│  CodexAppServerExecutor          │  WS    │  codex app-server                   │
│  ws://codex-app-server:1456 ─────┼───────▶│  --listen ws://0.0.0.0:1456         │
│  (+ token na damar aiki)         │  JSON  │  --ws-auth capability-token         │
│                                  │  RPC   │  yana sarrafa OpenAI OAuth da kansa │
└──────────────────────────────────┘        │  (~/.codex/auth.json, sabuntawa ta  │
        │ yana rabawa (compose volumes)      │  atomatik)                          │
        ▼                                   └─────────────────────────────────────┘
  codex-appserver-token   → token na damar WS (dukansu suna mount ɗinsa)
  codex-appserver-home    → ~/.codex (dashboard ne ke rubuta auth.json,
                             codex app-server na sidecar ne ke karanta shi)
```

- Sidecar yana sauraro **kawai** a kan cibiyar compose ta ciki
  (`ws://codex-app-server:1456`) a bayan token na damar aiki. **Ba a taɓa**
  buɗe shi ga host ko intanet.
- An riga an saka Codex CLI cikin `omniroute:base`, don haka ba a buƙatar shigar
  da codex a kan host ko na'urar mai amfani lokacin da kake gudanar da sidecar.

## 2. Kaddamar da shi

```bash
# Kaddamar da stack TARE DA profile na sidecar na codex app-server:
docker compose --profile base --profile codex-app-server up -d
# (podman: podman compose --profile base --profile codex-app-server up -d)
```

Sidecar yana ƙirƙirar token ɗin damar WS a farkon kunnawa (cikin volume ɗin
`codex-appserver-token` da ake rabawa), sannan manhajar tana karanta token ɗin ta
`OMNIROUTE_CODEX_APPSERVER_WS_TOKEN_FILE`. Ba a buƙatar haɗa token da hannu.

## 3. Haɗawa + shiga

1. A cikin dashboard, ƙara haɗi na **OpenAI Codex (App-Server)**. Ba a buƙatar API
   key ko token (mai samarwa ne marar auth — sidecar ne ke kula da auth).
2. Idan **har yanzu ba a shiga** Codex CLI na sidecar ba, binciken lafiyar haɗin
   zai nuna _"yana aiki amma ba a shiga ba"_ (ba kuskuren auth mai ja ba). Yi amfani da
   **Shiga da ChatGPT**: wannan yana gudanar da daidaitaccen Codex device-OAuth a cikin
   burauzarka, sannan ya rubuta `~/.codex/auth.json` cikin volume ɗin da ake rabawa ta
   **Aiwatar da auth** (shiga guda ɗin nan yana amfani ga masu samarwar `codex` da
   `codex-app-server` duka).
3. Da zarar an shiga, binciken lafiyar zai koma kore (yana tabbatar da `/readyz` **da kuma**
   `account/read` — wato yana aiki _kuma_ an tabbatar da izini) sannan ya fara aiki.

Dashboard ba ya taɓa lalata lafiyayyen `~/.codex/auth.json` da yake akwai — yana rubutawa
ne kawai idan fayil ɗin ba ya nan ko token ɗinsa ya tsufa (kullum ana yin kwafin ajiya).

## 4. Yanayin turawa

- **Mai gudanarwa da Codex CLI wanda aka riga aka tabbatar da shi** — mount
  `~/.codex` na host ɗinka cikin sidecar (`codex-appserver-home`) sannan ka tsallake matakin shiga.
- **Mai amfani na jama'a, ba shi da codex da aka shigar a na'urarsa** — wannan ba matsala ba ce: sidecar yana da
  CLI. Mai amfani yana tabbatar da kansa ne kawai ta dashboard.
- **OmniRoute na bare-metal (babu sidecar, codex na host)** — saita
  `OMNIROUTE_CODEX_APPSERVER_WS` zuwa `codex app-server` naka kuma ka tabbatar an shiga
  codex na host; alamar "ba a shigar da codex ba" za ta bayyana idan binary
  ɗin ba ya nan.

## 5. Residential / UDP egress (ƙarin zaɓi na mai gudanarwa, ba a haɗa shi ba)

Generic sidecar da ke sama yana fitar da zirga-zirga ta cibiyar sadarwar container ta yau da kullum. Mai
gudanarwa da ke buƙatar zirga-zirgar Codex ta fita ta **residential exit** (misali TUN
tailscale sidecar mai ɗaukar TCP + UDP/QUIC) zai gudanar da wannan a matsayin compose
override na daban; da gangan **ba** a haɗa shi cikin profile na `codex-app-server`
da aka samar ba. Duba kundin gudanarwar ayyuka na ciki don wannan saitin.
