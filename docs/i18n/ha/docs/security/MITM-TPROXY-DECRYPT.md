TPROXY transparent decrypt shi ne **yanayin kama zirga-zirga na 5** na OmniRoute don tarin MITM na
[Traffic Inspector](../frameworks/TRAFFIC_INSPECTOR.md) / [AgentBridge](../frameworks/AGENTBRIDGE.md).
Yana tarewa tare da **warware ɓoyewar** zirga-zirgar HTTPS mai fita daga na’ura a Linux
ta amfani da TPROXY na kernel + policy routing — **ba tare da** yin kwaikwayon `/etc/hosts` ba kuma
**ba tare da** sauya saitunan system-proxy na dukkan OS ba. Ya dace da tsarin da ba shi da mu’amalar gani
(babu gyaran DNS da za a share), kuma dokokin firewall suna share kansu ta atomatik idan an sake kunna na’ura.

Ba kamar sauran hanyoyin kama zirga-zirga ba, TPROXY baya buƙatar saitin kowane host dabam: yana
tarewa cikin bayyanannen tsari ga **duk wani** destination host a target port, yana ƙare TLS da
leaf certificate da yake bayarwa nan take ga kowane SNI hostname, yana kama musayar bayanan da aka
warware ɓoyewarsu, sannan ya sake ɓoye request ɗin zuwa asalin destination.

> **Linux kawai, root kawai, kuma sai an zaɓa.** Wannan yanayin yana buƙatar Linux, native addon
> da aka gina da C toolchain, da capability na **CAP_NET_ADMIN** (yawanci root). Ana iyakance shi
> a bayan AgentBridge API na loopback kawai, kuma a kashe yake ta tsohuwa. Amintacciyar
> MITM CA da za ta iya sanya hannu ga kowane host iko ne mai ƙarfi — duba [§6 Tsaro](#6-security).

**Tushe:** `src/mitm/tproxy/`
**Hanyar API:** `GET / POST / DELETE /api/tools/agent-bridge/tproxy`
**Maɓallin dashboard:** Traffic Inspector → toolbar na capture-modes → **"TPROXY Decrypt"** ⚠
**Duba kuma:** [`docs/frameworks/TRAFFIC_INSPECTOR.md`](../frameworks/TRAFFIC_INSPECTOR.md),
[`docs/frameworks/AGENTBRIDGE.md`](../frameworks/AGENTBRIDGE.md)

## §1 Mene ne shi kuma yaushe ya kamata a yi amfani da shi

Kowane ɗaya daga cikin sauran hanyoyin kama zirga-zirga guda huɗu yana da wata iyaka:

| Yanayi            | Yadda ake karkatar da zirga-zirga                 | Iyaka                                              |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| AgentBridge       | Kwaikwayon DNS ta `/etc/hosts` ga tsayayyun hosts | hosts na IDE-agent da aka yi wa rajista kawai      |
| Custom Hosts      | Kwaikwayon DNS ta `/etc/hosts` ga kowane host     | entry ɗaya ga kowane host; sudo don gyara hosts    |
| HTTP_PROXY        | env na `HTTP_PROXY`/`HTTPS_PROXY`                 | apps da ke bin env var kawai                       |
| System-wide proxy | Saitunan proxy na OS                              | yana sauya global state; yana buƙatar mayarwa baya |

TPROXY transparent decrypt yana karkatar da zirga-zirga a matakin **kernel** maimakon haka. Yana
yi wa sabbin local outbound TCP connections zuwa target port (tsoho `443`) alama a cikin
chain na `mangle OUTPUT`, sai `ip rule` ya sake karkatar da marked packets zuwa local delivery,
kuma yayin sake shigowa, target na `TPROXY` na `mangle PREROUTING` yana miƙa su ga
listener na **IP_TRANSPARENT** — wanda daga nan yake ƙare TLS kuma ya kama plaintext.

Yi amfani da shi idan kana son kama tare da warware ɓoyewar zirga-zirga daga process wanda:

- yake sadarwa da host da AgentBridge bai yi wa rajista ba, kuma
- baya bin `HTTP_PROXY`, kuma
- ba ka son ka dagula shi ta hanyar sauya system-wide proxy.

Saboda tarewar tana faruwa a cikin kernel, process ɗin da ya fara zirga-zirgar baya buƙatar **wani
canjin configuration** — amma dole ne process ɗin ya amince da dynamic CA da OmniRoute
ke girkawa (duba [§4](#4-the-per-sni-dynamic-ca-and-trust-store-installer)).

## §2 Abubuwan da ake buƙata

| Abin da ake buƙata | Bayani                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **OS**             | Linux kawai — **IP_TRANSPARENT** zaɓin socket ne da ke aiki a Linux kawai. Mai lodawa yana dawo da "babu shi" a kowane sauran dandali.                            |
| **Izini**          | Ana buƙatar damar **CAP_NET_ADMIN** don ƙirƙirar transparent socket da amfani da dokokin `iptables`/`ip` — a aikace, a gudanar a matsayin root.                   |
| **Native addon**   | Dole ne a gina ƙaramin N-API addon (`src/mitm/tproxy/native/transparent.c`) ko a samar da shi a matsayin prebuild. Duba [§3](#3-the-native-ip_transparent-addon). |
| **Kernel modules** | `iptables` mai goyon bayan daidaitawar `TPROXY`, `mangle`, da `mark` (an tabbatar da shi da kernel 6.8.0).                                                        |

**Rage aiki cikin tsari:** idan wani abin da ake buƙata ya ɓace (ba Linux ba, babu toolchain,
ba a gina addon ba), mai loda addon (`src/mitm/tproxy/transparentSocket.ts::loadTransparentAddon`)
zai dawo da `null` maimakon jefa kuskure. Sannan matsayin capture-mode zai nuna
`available: false`, za a **kashe** maɓallin dashboard tare da tooltip
"TPROXY decrypt yana buƙatar Linux + root + native addon", kuma sauran sassan
OmniRoute za su ci gaba da aiki.

## §3 Native addon na IP_TRANSPARENT

Module ɗin `net` na Node ba zai iya yin `setsockopt(IP_TRANSPARENT)` _kafin_ `bind()` ba, alhali
TPROXY yana buƙatar hakan (in ba haka ba kernel zai watsar da packets da aka karkatar). Addon ɗin
(`src/mitm/tproxy/native/transparent.c`, wanda ake ginawa ta `binding.gyp`) ƙaramin module ne na N-API
wanda ke samar da functions guda uku, waɗanda ake amfani da su ta `transparentSocket.ts`:

| Function na addon                     | Aikin socket                                                                                    | Abin da ake amfani da shi                                                     |
| ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `createTransparentListener(ip, port)` | `socket()` + **SO_REUSEADDR** + **IP_TRANSPARENT** + `bind()` + `listen()`, yana dawo da raw fd | transparent capture listener (Node yana karɓar fd ta `server.listen({ fd })`) |
| `setSocketMark(fd, mark)`             | `setsockopt` **SO_MARK** a kan fd da yake akwai                                                 | anti-loop (sanya wa sockets na proxy alama)                                   |
| `connectMarked(ip, port, mark)`       | `socket()` + **SO_MARK** **kafin** `connect()` marar toshewa, yana dawo da fd                   | isar da upstream da aka sake ɓoyewa (SYN yana ɗauke da alamar)                |

Ana karanta asalin destination daga `socket.localAddress`/`localPort` — TPROXY
yana kiyaye shi, don haka babu binciken **SO_ORIGINAL_DST**/NAT.

### Gina addon

```bash
npm run build:native:tproxy      # shiga src/mitm/tproxy/native sannan a gudanar da node-gyp rebuild
                                 # -> native/build/Release/transparent.node
```

- Yayin `npm run build`, `scripts/build/build-tproxy-native.mjs` yana gudanar da `node-gyp
rebuild`. Yana aiki ne **a Linux kawai kuma gazawarsa ba ta dakatar da gini** — rashin toolchain kawai yana sa
  capture mode ya zama babu shi.
- `assembleStandalone.mjs` yana kwafe `build/Release/transparent.node` zuwa cikin
  standalone bundle; `transparentSocket.ts` yana gano shi ta hanyar module-relative da
  cwd-relative (`<cwd>/src/mitm/tproxy/native/...`).
- `build/` da `prebuilds/` suna cikin git-ignore — ana **gina binary, ba a taɓa
  commit ɗinsa ba**.

Mai lodawa yana bincikawa bisa wannan jerin fifiko:
`native/build/Release/transparent.node`, sannan `native/prebuilds/transparent.node`
(dukansu module-relative da kuma ƙarƙashin `<cwd>/src/mitm/tproxy/`).

## §4 CA mai sauyawa na kowane-SNI da mai girka ma'ajiyar amincewa

> **Sabuntawar #6684:** sabar AgentBridge marar sauyawa (`src/mitm/server.cjs`) yanzu
> tana amfani da wannan tsarin gine-ginen CA/leaf maimakon leaf guda ɗaya
> marar sauyawa mai sanya wa kansa hannu. Tana amfani da samfurin CA
> **na daban** (`src/mitm/cert/rootCa.ts`, wanda ake adanawa a `<DATA_DIR>/mitm/ca.key`/`ca.crt`)
> kuma tana girkawa a ƙarƙashin gurbin ma'ajiyar amincewa na `omniroute-mitm.crt`
> da ya riga ya kasance (tana maye gurbin tsohon leaf guda ɗaya a wurin — babu buƙatar
> tsaftace amincewa biyu), kuma an ware ta gaba ɗaya daga gurbin TPROXY na kansa
> na `omniroute-tproxy-ca.crt` da ke ƙasa. Sabbin girke-girken AgentBridge suna samun
> tsarin CA kai tsaye; girke-girken da ya riga ya amince da tsohon leaf marar sauyawa
> zai ci gaba da amfani da shi har sai mai gudanarwa ya zaɓi shiga ta hanyar
> `MITM_ROOT_CA_ENABLED=true` (duba `src/mitm/cert/migration.ts`) — CA na MITM
> da aka amince da shi wanda zai iya sanya hannu kan leaf na **kowane** host yana da
> iko sosai fiye da tsohon leaf mai tsayayyen-SAN, don haka ba a taɓa yin wannan
> sauyin a ɓoye ga girke-girken da aka riga aka amince da shi.

A tarihi, cert na MITM marar sauyawa na AgentBridge ya yi aiki ne kawai saboda
AgentBridge yana yin DNS-spoof ga jerin host **tsayayyu** (wanda yanzu aka haɗa
da tsarin da ke ƙasa). TPROXY yana katse **kowane irin** host, don haka listener
ɗinsa dole ne ya gabatar da leaf ingantacce ga duk SNI da client ya nema — wannan
buƙata ce iri ɗaya da AgentBridge yake da ita yanzu ga cikakken jerin
`MITM_TOOL_HOSTS` (shigarwar tool 9) maimakon host 4 na antigravity kawai.

### CA mai sauyawa (`src/mitm/tproxy/dynamicCert.ts`)

`DynamicCertStore` yana gudanar da CA na gida (wanda aka gina bisa dependency na
`selfsigned`) wanda:

- Yana samar da CA mai dogon wa'adi ta hanyar `generateMitmCa()` (CN `"OmniRoute MITM CA"`,
  inganci na shekaru 10, `basicConstraints CA=true` + `keyUsage keyCertSign,cRLSign`,
  RSA mai bit 2048 / SHA-256).
- Yana fitar da **leaf ga kowane hostname na SNI bisa buƙata** ta hanyar
  `issueLeafCert()` (inganci na shekara 1, `subjectAltName` = host na SNI) kuma
  yana adana `tls.SecureContext` guda ɗaya a cache ga kowane hostname.
- Yana samar da `createSNICallback()` don sabar da ke ƙare TLS (duba [§5](#5-how-decrypt-and-capture-work)).
- Ana iya ƙirƙirarsa tare da `existingCa` domin CA ya ci gaba da kasancewa iri ɗaya
  bayan sake farawa (don kada a sake buƙatar girka ma'ajiyar amincewa).

Private key na CA **ba ya taɓa barin na'urar**.

### Mai girka ma'ajiyar amincewa (`src/mitm/tproxy/caTrust.ts`)

Client ɗin da aka katse dole ne ya amince da CA mai sauyawa, saboda haka fara yanayin
capture yana girka cert na CA cikin ma'ajiyar amincewa ta OS a ƙarƙashin **guri na musamman** —
`omniroute-tproxy-ca.crt` (constant `TPROXY_CA_CERT_NAME`) — wanda aka ware daga
gurbin cert na MITM marar sauyawa (`omniroute-mitm.crt`) don kada su taɓa maye
gurbin juna.

`installTproxyCa(caPem, sudoPassword?)` yana gano directory na anchor na distro
(a wannan jeri: tsarin Debian da farko) sannan ya gudanar da umarnin refresh da ya dace:

| Directory na anchor                         | Umarnin refresh          |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| `/usr/local/share/ca-certificates`          | `update-ca-certificates` |
| `/etc/ca-certificates/trust-source/anchors` | `update-ca-trust`        |
| `/etc/pki/ca-trust/source/anchors`          | `update-ca-trust`        |
| `/etc/pki/trust/anchors`                    | `update-ca-certificates` |

A lokacin girkawa, ana fara ajiye PEM a temp file, sannan (da izini na musamman)
a yi `mkdir -p` ga directory na anchor, a yi `cp` na file ɗin da aka ajiye zuwa
cikinsa, sannan a gudanar da umarnin refresh. `uninstallTproxyCa()` yana cire
gurbin da aka keɓe kawai (yana barin cert na MITM marar sauyawa yadda yake)
sannan ya yi refresh — ba ya yin komai a kan tsarin da ba Linux ba.

Dukkan umarnin da ke buƙatar izini na musamman suna gudana ta
`execFileWithPassword` (`src/mitm/systemCommands.ts`) — `spawn` tare da
**jerin args, babu shell, babu haɗa string ta interpolation** (Ƙa'ida Mai Tsauri #13).
Idan process ɗin yana gudana a matsayin root (misali, VPS), ana gudanar da target
kai tsaye kuma ba a buƙatar password; a desktop da ba root ba, ana aika
`sudoPassword` ta hanyar `sudo -S` a stdin.

> Ana bayar da `sudoPassword` na desktop a cikin body na POST domin ba da izinin
> girka ma'ajiyar amincewa; ana yin watsi da shi gaba ɗaya idan process ɗin yana
> gudana a matsayin root.

## §5 Yadda warware ɓoyewa da kamawa suke aiki

Tsarin sarrafawa (duk suna ƙarƙashin `src/mitm/tproxy/`):

```
manhajar gida  ──TCP/443──▶  mangle OUTPUT yana yi wa haɗin alama (fwmark)
                              ip rule → jadawalin hanyar gida → lo
                              mangle PREROUTING TPROXY → mai sauraron IP_TRANSPARENT (tashar 8443)
                                  │  captureMode.ts: yana karanta asalin makoma daga socket.localAddress
                                  ▼
                              tlsCapture.ts:
                                1. Ƙare TLS na CLIENT da takardar leaf ta kowane SNI (dynamicCert)
                                2. http.Server na ciki yana fassara bayyanannen rubutun da aka warware ɓoyewarsa
                                3. kama → globalTrafficBuffer.push() tare da source: "tproxy"
                                   (an yi amfani da sanitizeHeaders + maskSecret)
                                4. tura zuwa asalin makoma bayan SAKE-ɓoye shi
                                   ta soket mai alamar ƙetarewa (connectMarked, hana-madauwari)
                                  │
                                  ▼
                              asalin uwar garken sama (api.example.com)
```

- **Ƙare TLS** (`createTlsCaptureServer`): yana naɗe ɗanyen soket ɗin da aka katse
  a cikin `tls.TLSSocket` na ɓangaren uwar garke ta amfani da callback na SNI na CA mai sauyawa,
  sannan ya miƙa rafin da aka warware ɓoyewarsa ga `http.Server` na ciki (dabarar ƙarewa ta MITM
  da aka saba amfani da ita). `MITM_IDLE_TIMEOUT_MS` yana iyakance tsawon rayuwar soket domin
  ramin da ya maƙale kada ya ƙare file descriptors.
- **Kama** (`handleDecryptedRequest`): yana tura `InterceptedRequest` tare da
  `source: "tproxy"`, matsayin da ke farawa da `"in-flight"`, inda ake bi da headers ta
  `sanitizeHeaders()` da bodies ta `maskSecret()` kafin su shiga
  buffer. Daga nan ana sabunta shigarwar da amsa, girma, da latency.
- **Turawa bayan sake ɓoyewa** (`createForward` / `realForward`): yana sake ɓoyewa zuwa
  asalin makoma. `rejectUnauthorized` yana ɗaukar **`true`** a matsayin tsoho (amintacce
  ta asali) — ana tantance takardar upstream bisa SNI/Host da client ya
  nema, don haka proxy yana ƙin ainihin abin da asalin client ɗin ma zai ƙi.

### Hana-madauwari (SO_MARK)

Saboda ƙa'idodin suna yi wa sabbin haɗin fita na gida alama, hanyar turawa ta proxy
**kansa** bayan sake ɓoyewa za a saba sake katse ta — madauwari marar iyaka. Hanyar
turawa tana kare kanta daga wannan da alamar soket ta ƙetarewa (**SO_MARK**):

- `realForward` yana buɗe soket ɗinsa na upstream ta `connectMarked(ip, port, DEFAULT_BYPASS_MARK)`
  — `DEFAULT_BYPASS_MARK = 0x539` — wanda ke saita **SO_MARK** **kafin** `connect()`,
  don haka SYN na turawar yana ɗauke da alamar ƙetarewa.
- Ƙa'idar `mangle OUTPUT` tana ware haɗin da tuni yake ɗauke da alamar ƙetarewa
  (`-m mark ! --mark <bypassMark>`), don haka **ba a** sake yi wa turawar proxy alama,
  kuma ba ta sake shiga TPROXY.

> Bayanin aiwatarwa: dole ne a shigar da soket mai alamar ƙetarewa a
> `createConnection` na agent (`https.request({ createConnection })` ana yin watsi da shi
> a ɓoye idan akwai agent), in ba haka ba turawar za ta buɗe soket marar alama kuma
> madauwar za ta dawo. Wannan shi ne gyaran hana-madauwari da aka tabbatar ta e2e.

## §6 Tsaro

| Matakin kariya                        | Bayani                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **API na loopback kawai**             | `/api/tools/agent-bridge/tproxy` yana ƙarƙashin prefix ɗin `/api/tools/agent-bridge/` a cikin `LOCAL_ONLY_API_PREFIXES` (`src/server/authz/routeGuard.ts`). Ana aiwatar da tilasta loopback **kafin** tantancewa (Dokoki Masu Tsauri #15 + #17) — JWT da ya fallasa ta hanyar tunnel ba zai iya fara kama zirga-zirgar TPROXY ba, wanda ke amfani da dokokin `iptables` kuma yake shigar da CA na ma'ajiyar amana ta hanyar child processes. |
| **Keɓaɓɓen gurbin CA**                | Ana shigar da CA mai sauyawa zuwa `omniroute-tproxy-ca.crt`, ba tare da taɓa maye gurbin static MITM cert ba.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Maɓallin CA ba ya taɓa barin host** | `DynamicCertStore` yana riƙe maɓallin CA a cikin ƙwaƙwalwa; ba a fitar da shi.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Rufe sirrika**                      | `maskSecret()` akan request/response bodies da `sanitizeHeaders()` akan headers suna gudana **kafin** `globalTrafficBuffer.push()`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Babu shell interpolation**          | Duk umarnin `iptables`/`ip`/trust-store suna gudana ta hanyar `execFile`/`execFileWithPassword` tare da arg arrays (Doka Mai Tsauri #13).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Tabbatar da upstream cert**         | Re-encrypted forward yana tabbatar da upstream cert ta tsohuwa (`rejectUnauthorized: true`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Tsabtace kurakurai**                | Error responses na route suna wucewa ta `sanitizeErrorMessage()` (Doka Mai Tsauri #12).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

**MITM CA wata iko ce mai ƙarfi.** CA da OS ya amince da ita, wadda za ta iya sa hannu ga kowane
host, tana nufin cewa za a iya warware duk wani abu da OmniRoute ya katse. An killace ta a bayan
yanayin kama zirga-zirgar TPROXY na musamman, na cikin gida kawai, a kashe ta tsohuwa, kuma ana cire
entry ɗin trust-store lokacin da ka dakatar da yanayin.

## §7 Aiwatarwa / mayarwa ta firewall cikin ma'amala

Karyewar shiri ba za ta taɓa barin dokar `mangle` ko tsohuwar hanya a baya ba. Mai gina umarni
(`src/mitm/tproxy/commands.ts`) da mai gudanarwa (`src/mitm/tproxy/setup.ts`) suna tabbatar da cewa
**mayarwa ita ce cikakkiyar kishiyar aiwatarwa, a tsarin baya**.

`applyTproxy(cfg)` yana gudanar da umarnin aiwatarwa bisa tsari; idan aka samu **kowace irin** gazawa, yana gudanar da
cikakken `revertTproxy(cfg)` gwargwadon iko sannan ya sake jefa kuskuren — don haka ko dai an
aiwatar da firewall gaba ɗaya ko kuma an mayar da shi gaba ɗaya, ba zai taɓa kasancewa an aiwatar da rabinsa kawai ba. `revertTproxy(cfg)` yana gudanar da
umarnin kishiya a tsarin baya kuma yana ƙyale gazawa (idempotent — yana da aminci a kira shi
ba tare da sharadi ba, misali daga tsaftacewar AgentBridge `repairMitm()`).

`validateTproxyConfig(cfg)` yana gudana kafin kowane umarni: dole tashoshin su kasance `1–65535`,
dole `mark`/`routeTable`/`bypassMark` su kasance cikakkun lambobi masu kyau, sannan dole `bypassMark` ya
bambanta da `mark` (hana madauwari).

### Umarnin aiwatarwa (bisa tsari)

```bash
ip rule add fwmark <mark> lookup <routeTable>
ip route add local 0.0.0.0/0 dev lo table <routeTable>
iptables -t mangle -A OUTPUT -p tcp --dport <dport> -m mark ! --mark <bypassMark> -j MARK --set-mark <mark>
iptables -t mangle -A PREROUTING -p tcp --dport <dport> -m mark --mark <mark> -j TPROXY --on-port <onPort> --tproxy-mark <mark>
```

Mayarwa tana share su a tsarin baya: `PREROUTING -D`, `OUTPUT -D`, `ip route del`, `ip rule del`.

> Tsarin yana **tushen-OUTPUT** ne saboda yanayin amfani da MITM ya shafi zirga-zirgar bayanai masu fita
> ta _gida_ (manhajoji a kan wannan na'ura), wadda TPROXY a cikin `PREROUTING` kaɗai ba ya
> gani — `PREROUTING` yana ganin zirga-zirgar da ake tura wa gaba ne kawai. Sarkar `OUTPUT` tana yi wa sababbin
> haɗin gida alama, `ip rule` yana sake karkatar da su zuwa isarwa ta gida (`lo`), sannan
> `PREROUTING` ya miƙa su ga mai sauraro na gaskiya.

## §8 Saituna

Buƙatar farawa (`POST /api/tools/agent-bridge/tproxy`) tana karɓar filaye masu zuwa,
waɗanda `StartTproxyBodySchema` (`tproxy/route.ts`) ke tantancewa. Dukansu na zaɓi ne
kuma suna komawa ga tsoffin ƙimominsu:

| Fili             | Nau'i              | Tsoho    | Bayanan kula                                                                                                             |
| ---------------- | ------------------ | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **dport**        | int (1–65535)      | `443`    | Tashar TCP ta manufa da za a kama a ɓoye                                                                                 |
| **mark**         | int (≥1)           | `0x2333` | Alamar firewall da aka sa a `OUTPUT`, wadda `ip rule` + `PREROUTING` ke daidaitawa                                       |
| **onPort**       | int (1–65535)      | `8443`   | Tashar da mai sauraro na gaskiya (**IP_TRANSPARENT**) yake ɗaurewa                                                       |
| **routeTable**   | int (≥1)           | `233`    | Id na teburin karkatarwa bisa manufa wanda ke riƙe da hanyar `local 0.0.0.0/0`                                           |
| **bypassMark**   | int (≥1, ≠ `mark`) | `0x539`  | Alamar socket ta kaucewa (**SO_MARK**) da proxy yake sakawa a haɗin sama nasa; an cire ta daga `OUTPUT` (hana madauwari) |
| **sudoPassword** | string             | —        | Kwamfutocin tebur marasa root kawai: yana ba da izinin shigarwa a ma'ajiyar amincewa; ana watsi da shi idan root ne      |

Babu **environment variables** na TPROXY — dukkan saituna ana bayar da su ne ta cikin
jikin POST ko tsoffin ƙimomin da ke sama.

## §9 Kunna daga Traffic Inspector

1. Buɗe **Traffic Inspector** (`/dashboard/tools/traffic-inspector`).
2. A cikin maɓallan yanayin kamawa, nemo maɓallin **"TPROXY Decrypt"** ⚠
   (`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/tools/traffic-inspector/components/CaptureModesToolbar.tsx`).
   - Idan an **kashe** shi tare da bayanin "TPROXY decrypt yana buƙatar Linux + root +
     native addon", to native addon ɗin ba ya samuwa a wannan na'ura (ba Linux ba,
     babu toolchain, ko kuma ba a gina addon ɗin ba). Duba [§2](#2-requirements) da [§3](#3-the-native-ip_transparent-addon).
3. Danna maɓallin. Yana kiran `POST /api/tools/agent-bridge/tproxy` ta hanyar
   `startTproxyCaptureMode()` (`src/lib/inspector/tproxyCaptureApi.ts`), wanda yake:
   gina CA mai canzawa, buɗe listener na transparent, aiwatar da dokokin firewall,
   sannan ya shigar da CA a ma'ajiyar amincewa ta OS.
4. Lokacin da yake aiki, toggle ɗin yana komawa launin amber kuma yana nuna adadin
   intercept kai tsaye (`· <interceptCount>`). Buƙatun da aka kama suna bayyana a
   jerin buƙatu tare da `source: "tproxy"`.
5. Sake dannawa don tsayawa — `DELETE /api/tools/agent-bridge/tproxy` ta hanyar
   `stopTproxyCaptureMode()` yana rufe listener, yana cire CA, sannan yana mayar da
   dokokin firewall yadda suke.

Matsayin yanayin kamawa (yana aiki / yana samuwa / adadin intercept / port na listener)
yana zuwa daga `GET /api/tools/agent-bridge/tproxy` (`getCaptureStatus()` a cikin
`src/mitm/tproxy/captureManager.ts`). Zaman TPROXY **ɗaya kacal** zai iya gudana a lokaci
guda — yunƙurin fara na biyu zai ƙi tare da "Yanayin kamawa na TPROXY ya riga ya fara aiki".

## §10 Magance matsaloli

### An kashe toggle

Ba za a iya loda native addon ba. Tabbatar cewa: kana kan Linux, ka gina addon ɗin
(`npm run build:native:tproxy`), kuma process ɗin zai iya loda `transparent.node`.
`isTransparentSocketAvailable()` ne ke sarrafa toggle ɗin; `GET /api/tools/agent-bridge/tproxy`
yana dawo da `available: false` idan addon ɗin ya ɓace.

### Ba a kama komai ba

- Tabbatar cewa process ɗin da ake intercept yana haɗuwa da `dport` da aka saita
  (tsoho `443`).
- Tabbatar cewa process ɗin yana amincewa da CA mai canzawa. Ana shigar da CA ƙarƙashin
  `omniroute-tproxy-ca.crt`; manhajojin da ke da nasu ma'ajiyar amincewa (Firefox/Chrome NSS)
  na iya buƙatar a ƙara cert ɗin a can ma.
- Gudanar da gwajin kai na AgentBridge **Diagnose** (duba
  [`AGENTBRIDGE.md`](../frameworks/AGENTBRIDGE.md)) don binciken amincewa da cert /
  lafiyar server.

### Tsofaffin dokokin firewall bayan faɗuwar tsarin

`revertTproxy()` shi ne kishiyar apply kai tsaye kuma yana da idempotent. Tsayar da
yanayin yana mayar da dokokin; idan an kashe OmniRoute a tsakiyar zaman, yi amfani da
aikin AgentBridge **Repair** (`POST /api/tools/agent-bridge/repair`) don warware
yanayin tsarin da aka bari babu mai kula da shi (DNS spoof, root CA, system proxy).
Dokokin TPROXY na `mangle` da route ɗin ma suna gogewa ta atomatik lokacin sake kunna
na'urar.

### Madauwari marar ƙarewa / proxy yana intercept na forward ɗinsa

Wannan shi ne yanayin hana madauwari. Tabbatar cewa `bypassMark` ya bambanta da `mark`
(validation yana tilasta haka) kuma forward ɗin yana amfani da `connectMarked` (haka
yake a cikin `realForward`). Duba [§5 Hana madauwari](#anti-loop-so_mark).
