Ƙofar sama ta `agentrouter` tana gudanar da matatar abun ciki mai dogaro da kalmomin maɓalli a kan
`messages[].content`. Matatar tana da wani ɓangare mai tabbataccen hali (kullum tana toshe
wasu jimloli) da kuma wani ɓangare mai yiwuwar sauyawa (mai tasirin yawan buƙatu a jere — tana zama
mai tsauri bayan buƙatu masu sauri, sannan ta sassauta bayan ɗan lokacin jira).

Lokacin da WAF ya toshe wata buƙata, yana mayar da:

```
HTTP/1.1 400 Bad Request
{"error":{"code":"content-blocked","message":"content-blocked (request id: ...)","param":"","type":"agent_router_api_error"}}
```

## Iyakokin matatar

WAF yana bincikar `messages[].content` kawai. **Ba ya** bincikar:

- Umarnin `system`
- Tsararrun tubalan abun ciki (`tool_result`, `tool_use`, `thinking`, `image`)
- Filayen `description` da `input_schema` na kayan aiki
- Metadata na buƙata, headers, ko model id

## Tsarukan da ake toshewa koyaushe (ba tare da la’akari da manyan ko ƙananan haruffa ba)

| Tsari                        | Bayani                                                     |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Kowace siga ta `Lorem ipsum` | Ana toshe cikakkun kalmomin Latin na lorem                 |
| `language model` (ita kaɗai) | "the language model" da "large language model" suna wucewa |
| `virtual assistant`          | "AI assistant" yana wucewa                                 |
| `I'm here to help`           | "here to help" ita kaɗai ma ana toshe ta                   |
| `Claude, made by Anthropic`  | Cikakkiyar jimlar kawai                                    |

## Tsarukan da kusan koyaushe ake toshewa

| Tsari                                                            | Bayani                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `placeholder`                                                    | Idan ya tsaya shi kaɗai (ba a matsayin sunan parameter ba, da sauransu) |
| `dummy data`                                                     | Jimlar seed da aka saba amfani da ita don fixtures                      |
| `foo bar baz`                                                    | Daidaitacciyar jimlar placeholder                                       |
| Gajerun tokens da aka maimaita (`AAA BBB CCC`, `test test test`) | Mai gano cunkushe abun ciki da kalmomin maɓalli                         |

## Halayya a ƙarƙashin nauyi

Bayan kusan buƙatu 5 masu sauri cikin ɗan gajeren lokaci, WAF yana fara toshe abun ciki
wanda a al’ada zai wuce. Bucket ɗin yana sassautawa bayan kusan daƙiƙu 5–10 ba tare da
wani aiki ba. Wannan shi ne rate limiter ɗin da aka ɗaure da IP da key wanda ke haifar da
kurakuran `400 content-blocked` lokaci-lokaci yayin da Claude Code ko Codex CLI
ke yin kiraye-kirayen amfani da kayan aiki / aika saƙo da yawa cikin sauri a jere.

## Matakan rage matsalar da aka riga aka aiwatar a OmniRoute

1. **`open-sse/services/wafRateLimit.ts`** — burst guard wanda ke tilasta
   mafi ƙarancin tazarar 500 ms tsakanin buƙatun fita zuwa kowace URL ta `agentrouter:*`.
   Tazarar ta yi ƙasa sosai da yadda ɗan adam zai iya lura da jinkiri kuma tana hana
   WAF kunnawa a kan zirga-zirgar yau da kullum.

2. **`BaseExecutor.WAF_RETRY_CONFIG`** — idan upstream ya mayar da
   `400 content-blocked`, executor zai sake gwada URL ɗin nan ɗaya tare da
   exponential backoff (1.5 s, 3.0 s, mafi yawa ƙoƙari 2). Bayan backoff ɗin,
   WAF yawanci yana sassautawa kuma sake gwajin yana yi nasara.

3. **`tests/unit/compression/harness.test.ts`** — an sauya test fixture
   `longInput` daga `"lorem ipsum dolor sit amet ".repeat(40)`
   zuwa `"example content for testing purposes ".repeat(40)` domin idan
   Claude Code ya karanta wannan fayil ta kayan aikin `Read`, abun cikin fayil ɗin
   kada ya sake gudana ta cikin tubalin `tool_result` har ya kunna WAF.

## Jagora don prompts da fitowar kayan aiki

Idan zaman Claude Code ko Codex CLI yana ta fuskantar
`400 content-blocked`, duba saƙon mai amfani na baya-bayan nan da sakamakon kayan aiki na
baya-bayan nan don gano ko akwai ɗaya daga cikin tsarukan da ke sama, sannan a sake tsara jimlar. Hanyoyin
kauce wa matsalar da aka saba amfani da su:

- Sauya `Lorem ipsum …` da `example text …` ko ainihin abun cikin
  da test ko fixture ke ƙoƙarin wakilta.
- Sauya `placeholder` (idan ya tsaya shi kaɗai) da `example value`,
  `sample value`, ko ainihin ƙimar.
- Sauya `language model` da `large language model` ko `the model`.
- Sauya `dummy data` da `sample data` ko sahihan ƙimomin seed.
- Sauya `I'm here to help` / `here to help` da takamaiman
  farkon magana (misali, "I'll review the file you mentioned").

## Bayar da rahoton false positives ga upstream

Matatar ta yanzu tana da tsauri fiye da kima — tana toshe "Lorem ipsum" a cikin
tubalan `tool_result` duk da cewa a bayyane yake operator bai yi niyyar
shigar da prompt ba. Ya kamata operators da suke son a gyara wannan daga tushe su
tuntuɓi `agentrouter.org` domin bayar da rahoton false positives. Blocklist ɗin
da ke sama shi ne sakamakon gwajin upstream a zahiri har zuwa 2026-08-03.
