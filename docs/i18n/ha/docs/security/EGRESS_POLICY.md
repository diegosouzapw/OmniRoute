> **Ƙayyade zirga-zirgar bayan-gida zuwa dangin IP guda ɗaya — `auto`, `ipv4`, ko `ipv6` — ga kowane proxy, domin hanyar fita ta IPv6-kawai kada ta taɓa komawa IPv4 a ɓoye.**

> **Tushen gaskiya:** `open-sse/utils/proxyFamily.ts`, `open-sse/utils/proxyDispatcher.ts`, `open-sse/utils/proxyFetch.ts`, `open-sse/utils/socksConnectorWithFamily.ts`, `open-sse/utils/proxyFamilyResolve.ts`, `src/shared/validation/schemas.ts`, `src/lib/db/proxies.ts`, `src/lib/db/upstreamProxy.ts`, `src/lib/db/migrations/099_proxy_family.sql`

OmniRoute yana ba kowane proxy damar ɗaukar **umarnin dangin adireshin hanyar fita**. Ta tsohuwa, OS ne ke zaɓar IPv4 ko IPv6 (dual-stack, "Happy Eyeballs"). Lokacin da ka saita umarnin zuwa `ipv4` ko `ipv6`, OmniRoute yana ƙayyade kowace haɗi da ke bi ta wannan proxy zuwa dangin da aka zaɓa kuma **yana hana haɗin gaba ɗaya idan ya gaza** maimakon komawa ɗayan dangin.

Wannan shafin yana bayyana mene ne umarnin, dalilin samuwarsa, inda ake saita shi, da kuma yadda runtime ke warware shi.

## Mene Ne Shi

Kowane proxy a cikin rajista yana da filin `family` mai ƙimomi uku masu yiwuwa, waɗanda Zod enum ke tantancewa:

```ts
// src/shared/validation/schemas.ts
family: z.enum(["auto", "ipv4", "ipv6"]).optional().default("auto"),
```

Filin yana amfani da `"auto"` ta tsohuwa, wanda ke ci gaba da kiyaye halayen dual-stack na baya. Saita shi zuwa `ipv4` ko `ipv6` yana ƙayyade dangin haɗi na wannan proxy.

Ana daidaita umarnin a ko'ina ta hanyar helper guda ɗaya, don haka duk wata ƙima da ba a sani ba tana komawa `auto`:

```ts
// open-sse/utils/proxyFamily.ts
export type ProxyFamily = "auto" | "ipv4" | "ipv6";

export function parseProxyFamily(value: unknown): ProxyFamily {
  return value === "ipv4" || value === "ipv6" ? value : "auto";
}
```

## Dalilin Samuwarsa

An gabatar da shi a cikin PR [#3777](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/pull/3777). Matsalolin da suka sa aka ƙirƙire shi:

| Matsala                                                | Abin da umarnin yake gyarawa                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Hanyar fita ta IPv6-kawai tana zubewa zuwa IPv4**    | Lokacin da host na proxy yake da dukansu A da AAAA records (ko kuma OS ya fi son IPv4), Happy Eyeballs na iya yin haɗin fita ta IPv4 ko da kuwa kana nufin hanyar IPv6-kawai. Ƙayyade `ipv6` yana kawar da wannan zubewar.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Soke izini saboda matsalar hanyar fita da aka raba** | Masu samarwa masu juyawa (codex/openai) suna soke tokens lokacin da asusu da yawa suke fita ta **IP iri ɗaya** da babban adadin zirga-zirga. Sarrafa dangin hanyar fita wani ɓangare ne na tabbatar da cewa asusu suna amfani da hanyoyin fita daban-daban kuma masu tabbas (duba [`src/lib/proxyEgress.ts`](../../src/lib/proxyEgress.ts) don binciken IP na hanyar fita da ke aiki tare da wannan). |
| **Tabbatacciyar hanyar fita don bin ƙa'ida/gwaji**     | Lokacin da dole ne ka tabbatar cewa zirga-zirga tana fita ta wani takamaiman dangi, `auto` bai isa ba.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

Da gangan aka tsara umarnin ya kasance **ga kowane proxy**, ba na gaba ɗaya ba — proxies daban-daban a cikin tarinka na iya samun manufofi daban-daban.

## Yadda Ake Saita Shi

### Dashboard

Mai zaɓin yana cikin fom ɗin proxy na shafin **Proxy Pool**:

1. Buɗe **Dashboard → Settings → Proxy → Proxy Pool**
2. Ƙara ko gyara proxy
3. Saita jerin zaɓin **Iyalin IP** zuwa `Na atomatik (dual-stack)`, `IPv4 kawai`, ko `IPv6 kawai`
4. Ajiye

`ProxyRegistryManager.tsx` ne ke nuna wannan maɓallin sarrafawa (wanda aka ɗora a cikin `proxy/ProxyPoolTab.tsx`).

### API

Filin `family` yana cikin bayanan da ake aikawa don ƙirƙira/sabunta rajistar proxy, waɗanda `createProxyRegistrySchema` / `updateProxyRegistrySchema` (`src/shared/validation/schemas.ts`) ke tantancewa, sannan `POST` / `PATCH /api/v1/management/proxies` ke sarrafa su:

```bash
# Ƙirƙiri proxy mai amfani da IPv6 kawai
curl -X POST http://localhost:20128/api/v1/management/proxies \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "name": "IPv6 egress",
    "type": "socks5",
    "host": "proxy.example.com",
    "port": 1080,
    "family": "ipv6"
  }'

# Canja proxy da ke akwai zuwa mai amfani da IPv4 kawai
curl -X PATCH http://localhost:20128/api/v1/management/proxies \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "id": "proxy-uuid-here", "family": "ipv4" }'
```

Haka kuma, ana karɓar wannan filin a cikin abin saitin proxy na kai-tsaye da ake amfani da shi don shigarwar upstream-proxy (`upstream_proxy_config.family`, duba [Tsarin Bayanai](#data-model)).

Don sauran API na CRUD/ƙayyade proxy, duba [PROXY_GUIDE.md](../ops/PROXY_GUIDE.md).

## Yadda `auto` Ke Tantancewa

Lokacin da `family` yake `auto`, OmniRoute **ba ya** ƙara kowace umarni — ana amfani da URL ɗin proxy yadda yake, kuma ana tantance iyalin haɗin ne bisa halayensa na asali.

A lokacin gina URL (`proxyConfigToUrl` / `normalizeProxyUrl` a cikin `open-sse/utils/proxyDispatcher.ts`), proxy mai `auto` yana samar da URL na yau da kullum ba tare da wata alama ba:

```ts
// open-sse/utils/proxyDispatcher.ts
const fam = parseProxyFamily(config.family);
const normalized = normalizeProxyUrl(proxyUrlStr, "context proxy", {
  allowSocks5,
});
return fam === "auto" ? normalized : `${normalized}?family=${fam}`;
```

A lokacin aikawa (`resolveDispatcherFamily`), `auto` yana komawa ga iyalin asali na mai masaukin da aka rubuta kai tsaye a matsayin IP, ko `null` (a bar OS ya yanke shawara) idan sunan mai masauki ne:

```ts
// open-sse/utils/proxyDispatcher.ts
function resolveDispatcherFamily(parsed: URL): 4 | 6 | null {
  const directive = parseProxyFamily(parsed.searchParams.get("family") ?? undefined);
  const literal = detectIpLiteralFamily(parsed.hostname);
  if (directive === "auto") return literal; // null ga sunan mai masauki → OS ne ke zaɓa
  // ...
}
```

Saboda haka:

- `auto` + mai masaukin da aka rubuta kai tsaye a matsayin IP (`192.0.2.1` / `[2001:db8::1]`) → iyalin wannan rubutun.
- `auto` + sunan mai masauki → `null` → daidaitacciyar tantancewar dual-stack ta OS.

## Yadda Ake Tilasta Amfani da `ipv4` / `ipv6`

Umarnin da ba `auto` ba yana tafiya a matsayin alamar tambaya ta wucin gadi guda ɗaya — `?family=ipv4` ko `?family=ipv6` — wadda ake liƙawa sau ɗaya kacal zuwa URL na proxy da aka daidaita. `normalizeProxyUrl` yana cirewa tare da sake liƙa wannan alama sau ɗaya tak domin kada ta taɓa lalata fassarar port.

Lokacin da ake gina dispatcher, ana karanta alamar sannan a mayar da ita zuwa takamaiman family na haɗi. Idan host ɗin IP literal ne na family na **akasin** wanda aka zaɓa, OmniRoute zai jefa kuskure (ana ƙin ci gaba idan akwai saɓani):

```ts
// open-sse/utils/proxyDispatcher.ts
const want = directive === "ipv6" ? 6 : 4;
if (literal !== null && literal !== want) {
  throw new Error(
    `[ProxyDispatcher] Proxy family directive ${directive} contradicts ${literal === 6 ? "IPv6" : "IPv4"} literal host`
  );
}
```

Daga nan sai a ɗaure takamaiman family ɗin a kan connector:

- **HTTP/HTTPS proxies** (`ProxyAgent`): `proxyTls: { family, autoSelectFamily: false }` — yana kashe Happy Eyeballs domin family ɗin da aka zaɓa shi kaɗai ne za a kira.
- **SOCKS5 proxies**: connector na musamman yana tura `socket_options: { family, autoSelectFamily: false }` zuwa abokin cinikin SOCKS (duba [Dacewar SOCKS5](#socks5-compatibility)).

## Dacewar SOCKS5

Ɗaure family yana aiki da SOCKS5 proxies, amma daidaitaccen `fetch-socks` ba ya samar da socket options da ake buƙata don ɗaure family na matakin proxy. OmniRoute yana zuwa da nasa connector domin wannan:

```ts
// open-sse/utils/socksConnectorWithFamily.ts
export function buildSocksFamilySocketOptions(family: 4 | 6 | null): Record<string, unknown> {
  if (family === 6) return { family: 6, autoSelectFamily: false };
  if (family === 4) return { family: 4, autoSelectFamily: false };
  return {};
}
```

Duk turawar SOCKS5 tana bi ta `createSocksDispatcherWithFamily` ba tare da la’akari da `family` ba (har da `null` / `auto` a kan hostname): `buildSocksFamilySocketOptions(null)` yana samar da `{}`, sannan ana amfani da hanyar `SocksClient.createConnection` + TLS `buildConnector` iri ɗaya tare da ɗaure `socket_options`, domin Happy Eyeballs ba zai iya zaɓar IPv4 ga manufar egress ta IPv6 kaɗai ba.

Tallafin SOCKS5 kansa yana kunne ta tsohuwa (ana iya kashe shi ta `ENABLE_SOCKS5_PROXY=false`); duba [PROXY_GUIDE.md → Environment Variables](../ops/PROXY_GUIDE.md#environment-variables).

## Halayyar Ƙin Ci Gaba Idan An Sami Kuskure

Babban manufar umarnin ita ce ya **ƙi** maimakon ya koma amfani da family mara dacewa a ɓoye. Matakan tsaro guda biyu ne ke tabbatar da wannan:

1. **Saɓanin literal** — umarnin da ya saɓa wa host na IP-literal yana jefa kuskure lokacin gina dispatcher (`resolveDispatcherFamily`, wanda aka nuna a sama).

2. **Binciken DNS na hostname kafin haɗi** — ga proxy mai hostname da aka ɗaure da wani family, `proxyFetch.ts` yana tabbatar da cewa hostname ɗin yana da record a family da ake buƙata **kafin** yin egress, ta hanyar `assertHostnameSupportsFamily`:

   ```ts
   // open-sse/utils/proxyFamilyResolve.ts
   const hasFamily = records.some((r) => r.family === family);
   if (!hasFamily) {
     throw new Error(
       `[ProxyFamily] Proxy host ${host} has no ${family === 6 ? "IPv6 (AAAA)" : "IPv4 (A)"} record; ` +
         `refusing ${family === 6 ? "IPv6" : "IPv4"}-only egress (fail-closed)`
     );
   }
   ```

   Idan ya gaza, `proxyFetch.ts` yana yi wa kuskuren alama da `code = "PROXY_FAMILY_UNAVAILABLE"` da `statusCode = 503`. Haka nan, gazawar warware DNS ana ɗaukarta a matsayin yanayin ƙin ci gaba (a ƙi yin egress).

Hosts na IP-literal ba sa buƙatar komai a binciken DNS na farko — family ɗinsu na asali ne kuma ba ya buƙatar nema.

## Samfurin Bayanai

An ƙara ginshiƙin `family` ta hanyar migration `099_proxy_family.sql` zuwa teburori **biyu**:

```sql
-- src/lib/db/migrations/099_proxy_family.sql
ALTER TABLE proxy_registry ADD COLUMN family TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'auto';
ALTER TABLE upstream_proxy_config ADD COLUMN family TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'auto';
```

- `proxy_registry.family` — umarnin kowane proxy ne ga shigarwar rajista (`src/lib/db/proxies.ts`). Tambayoyin warwarewa suna zaɓar `family` tare da sauran ginshiƙan proxy, kuma ana sauya ƙimar da babu ko wadda ba string ba zuwa `"auto"`.
- `upstream_proxy_config.family` — umarnin shigarwar upstream-proxy (`src/lib/db/upstreamProxy.ts`), tare da tsohuwar ƙima iri ɗaya ta `"auto"`.

Lokacin da proxy object da aka warware yake ɗauke da `family` wanda ba `auto` ba, `proxyConfigToUrl` yana haɗa alamar `?family=` domin ƙayyadadden zaɓin ya ci gaba da kasancewa har zuwa dispatcher.
