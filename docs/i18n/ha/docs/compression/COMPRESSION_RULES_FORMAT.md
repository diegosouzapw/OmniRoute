Dokokin matsawa fayilolin JSON ne da ake lodawa yayin aiki. An tsara su da gangan su ƙunshi bayanai kawai domin a iya duba sabbin
fakitin harsuna da matatun umarnin RTK ba tare da canza lambar injin ba.

> **Tsarin ƙa'ida na asali (tushen gaskiya):** [`open-sse/services/compression/rules/_schema.json`](../../open-sse/services/compression/rules/_schema.json) (JSON Schema draft 2020-12).
> Misalan da ke ƙasa na bayani ne kawai — idan akwai shakku, tabbatar da ingancin fakitinka ta amfani da `_schema.json`.

## Fakitin Dokokin Caveman

Fakitin dokokin Caveman suna ƙarƙashin:

```txt
open-sse/services/compression/rules/<language>/<pack>.json
```

Kowane fakiti yana ɗauke da maye gurbi da ake amfani da su kan rubutu na yau da kullum bayan an ware yankunan da aka kare.

```json
{
  "language": "en",
  "category": "filler",
  "rules": [
    {
      "name": "question_to_directive",
      "pattern": "\\b(?:Can you explain why|Could you show me how)\\b\\s*",
      "replacement": "Explain why ",
      "replacementMap": {
        "can you explain why": "Explain why ",
        "could you show me how": "Show how "
      },
      "flags": "gi",
      "context": "all",
      "category": "context",
      "minIntensity": "lite",
      "description": "Convert verbose questions into direct requests."
    }
  ]
}
```

### Filayen Caveman

| Fili                     | Wajibi ne | Bayani                                                               |
| ------------------------ | --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `language`               | eh        | Maɓallin harshe mai kama da BCP-47 kamar `en`, `pt-BR`, `es`         |
| `category`               | eh        | Sunan fayil/rukunin fakiti, misali `filler` ko `dedup`               |
| `rules`                  | eh        | Jerin dokokin maye gurbin regex                                      |
| `rules[].name`           | eh        | Tsayayyen sunan doka                                                 |
| `rules[].pattern`        | eh        | Tushen regex na JavaScript                                           |
| `rules[].flags`          | a'a       | Tutocin regex na JavaScript; tsoho shi ne `gi`                       |
| `rules[].replacement`    | a'a       | Zaren maye gurbi ko madadin idan `replacementMap` bai dace ba        |
| `rules[].replacementMap` | a'a       | Maye gurbi na takamaiman dacewa bisa daidaitaccen rubutun da ya dace |
| `rules[].context`        | a'a       | `all`, `user`, `assistant`, ko `system`; tsoho shi ne `all`          |
| `rules[].category`       | a'a       | `filler`, `context`, `structural`, `dedup`, `terse`, ko `ultra`      |
| `rules[].minIntensity`   | a'a       | `lite`, `full`, ko `ultra`; tsoho shi ne `lite`                      |
| `rules[].description`    | a'a       | Taƙaitaccen bayanin doka da mutum zai iya karantawa                  |

Yi amfani da `flags` idan bambancin manyan da ƙananan haruffa yana da muhimmanci wajen dacewa, misali cire gaɓar suna kafin rubutun
ƙananan haruffa ba tare da cire `the OpenAI API` ba. Yi amfani da `replacementMap` idan regex guda yana da zaɓuɓɓuka da yawa
waɗanda ke buƙatar sakamako daban-daban; wannan yana sa fakitin dokokin JSON su kasance masu bayanai kawai tare da kiyaye halayen
ayyukan maye gurbi na TypeScript da aka gina a ciki masu ƙarin ƙarfi.

## Kunshin Matatun RTK

Matatun RTK suna ƙarƙashin:

```txt
open-sse/services/compression/engines/rtk/filters/<filter>.json
```

Kowane mataci yana bayyana yadda za a gane da kuma matse dangin fitarwar umarni.

```json
{
  "id": "test-vitest",
  "label": "Vitest output",
  "category": "test",
  "priority": 92,
  "match": {
    "outputTypes": ["test-vitest"],
    "commands": ["vitest", "npm test", "npm run test"],
    "patterns": ["\\bFAIL\\b", "\\bPASS\\b", "\\bTest Files\\b"]
  },
  "rules": {
    "stripAnsi": true,
    "replace": [{ "pattern": "\\s+\\[[0-9]+ms\\]", "replacement": "" }],
    "matchOutput": [
      {
        "pattern": "All tests passed",
        "message": "vitest: ok",
        "unless": "FAIL|Error:"
      }
    ],
    "includePatterns": ["FAIL", "Error:", "Test Files", "Tests"],
    "dropPatterns": ["^\\s*$", "Duration\\s+\\d+"],
    "collapsePatterns": ["^\\s+at "],
    "deduplicate": true,
    "truncateLineAt": 240,
    "maxLines": 160,
    "headLines": 24,
    "tailLines": 40,
    "onEmpty": "vitest: ok",
    "filterStderr": false
  },
  "preserve": {
    "errorPatterns": ["FAIL", "Error:", "AssertionError"],
    "summaryPatterns": ["Test Files", "Tests", "Snapshots"]
  },
  "tests": [
    {
      "name": "keeps failing tests",
      "command": "vitest",
      "input": "FAIL test/a.test.ts\\nError: boom\\nTest Files 1 failed",
      "expected": "FAIL test/a.test.ts\\nError: boom\\nTest Files 1 failed"
    }
  ]
}
```

### Filayen RTK

| Fili                       | Wajibi ne | Bayani                                                                         |
| -------------------------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `id`                       | eh        | Tsayayyen id na mataci                                                         |
| `label`                    | eh        | Sunan da za a iya karantawa a dashboard                                        |
| `category`                 | eh        | Dangin mataci: git, test, build, shell, docker, package, infra, cloud, generic |
| `priority`                 | a'a       | Wanda ke da fifiko mafi girma ne ke yin nasara idan matatu da yawa sun dace    |
| `match.outputTypes`        | a'a       | Id-odin fitarwar mai ganowa waɗanda ke zaɓar wannan mataci                     |
| `match.commands`           | a'a       | Alamomin umarni waɗanda ke zaɓar wannan mataci                                 |
| `match.patterns`           | a'a       | Tsarukan regex waɗanda ke zaɓar wannan mataci daga rubutun fitarwa             |
| `rules.stripAnsi`          | a'a       | Cire jerin tserewa na ANSI kafin matakan regex                                 |
| `rules.replace`            | a'a       | Sauye-sauyen regex masu tsari da ake aiwatarwa layi bayan layi                 |
| `rules.matchOutput`        | a'a       | Dokokin fitarwa masu dakatarwa da wuri tare da kariyar `unless` ta zaɓi        |
| `rules.includePatterns`    | a'a       | Layukan da ya kamata a fi son adanawa                                          |
| `rules.dropPatterns`       | a'a       | Layukan da za a cire a matsayin surutu                                         |
| `rules.collapsePatterns`   | a'a       | Layuka masu maimaituwar dacewa waɗanda za a iya haɗewa                         |
| `rules.deduplicate`        | a'a       | Haɗa layukan da aka daidaita masu maimaituwa                                   |
| `rules.truncateLineAt`     | a'a       | Iyakar haruffa ta kowane layi mai aminci ga Unicode                            |
| `rules.maxLines`           | a'a       | Matsakaicin layukan da za a riƙe kafin adana ƙarshen fitarwa                   |
| `rules.headLines`          | a'a       | Layukan farko da za a riƙe yayin taƙaitawa                                     |
| `rules.tailLines`          | a'a       | Layukan ƙarshe da za a riƙe domin mahallin baya-bayan nan                      |
| `rules.onEmpty`            | a'a       | Saƙon madadin idan tacewa ta cire dukkan abun ciki                             |
| `rules.filterStderr`       | a'a       | Daidaita gabatarwar stderr da aka saba da su kafin matakan tacewa na gaba      |
| `preserve.errorPatterns`   | a'a       | Layukan kuskure da ya kamata su tsira daga taƙaitawa                           |
| `preserve.summaryPatterns` | a'a       | Layukan taƙaitawa da ya kamata su tsira daga taƙaitawa                         |
| `tests[]`                  | a'a       | Samfuran tabbatarwa na ciki da ƙofar tantancewar RTK ke amfani da su           |

RTK yana aiwatar da matakan bayyanawa a wannan tsari: `stripAnsi`, `filterStderr`, `replace`,
`matchOutput`, `dropPatterns`/`includePatterns`, `truncateLineAt`, `headLines`/`tailLines`,
`maxLines`, da `onEmpty`.

Ana iya loda matatu na musamman daga:

1. Fayilolin aikin `.rtk/filters.json` ne kawai bayan an samu hash mai dacewa na `.rtk/trust.json` ko kuma
   an kunna `trustProjectFilters`.
2. `DATA_DIR/rtk/filters.json` na bai ɗaya.
3. Matatun da aka gina a ciki.

Fayiloli na musamman na aikin/na bai ɗaya na iya ƙunsar abu guda na mataci ko jerin abubuwan matatu. Ana tsallake
matatu na musamman marasa inganci tare da bayanan gano matsala; matatu marasa inganci da aka gina a ciki suna sa tantancewa ta gaza.

Fayil ɗin amincewar aikin:

```json
{
  "filtersSha256": "0123456789abcdef..."
}
```

Sauyin muhalli na maye gurbi `OMNIROUTE_RTK_TRUST_PROJECT_FILTERS=1` yana amincewa da matatun aikin ba tare da
hash ba, kuma ya kamata a iyakance shi ga ci gaban cikin gida da ake sarrafawa.

## Dokokin Tsaro

- A kiyaye dokoki su kasance idempotent: gudanar da matatar iri ɗaya sau biyu kada ya lalata sakamakon.
- A kiyaye ainihin rubutun kuskure, hanyoyin fayiloli, lambobin layi, da taƙaitattun umarni inda zai yiwu.
- A guji dokokin da ke gyara tubalan lamba, bayanan JSON, URLs, ko sirrika.
- A ƙara gwaje-gwajen raka'a don sabbin rukunin umarni a gwaje-gwajen ganowa/matata.
- A ƙara samfuran `tests[]` ga kowace ginanniyar matata da kuma matatun al'ada da ake rabawa.

## Tabbatarwa

Ana tabbatar da fakitin dokoki kafin amfani. Ginannun fakitin Caveman da ginannun matatun RTK suna dakatar da aiki nan take
yayin tabbatarwa domin a gano gurɓatattun kadarorin fitarwa kafin aikawa. Ana tsallake matatun RTK na al'ada
tare da bayanan bincike idan tantance tsarin rubutu ko tabbatar da amincewa ya gaza.

Tabbatarwa mai mayar da hankali:

```bash
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/compression/rule-loader.test.ts tests/unit/compression/language-packs.test.ts
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/compression/rtk-verify.test.ts tests/unit/compression/rtk-dsl-pipeline.test.ts
```
