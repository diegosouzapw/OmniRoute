Rabon albarkatu ba ke zama ƙayyadadden adadin mai samarwa ba.

Rukunin ƙayyadadden adadi suna ayyana waɗanne maɓallan API ne za su iya amfani da rukunin mai samarwa da kuma yadda manufofin tsauri, sassauƙa, ko ƙarin amfani na ɗan lokaci suke aiki. Ƙayyadadden adadin mai samarwa wata damar waje ce da mai samarwa ya bayar da rahotonta ko kuma aka samo ta daga wata madogara da aka saita a sarari. Kasafin kuɗin cikin gida na Ghostlight iyakokin gudanarwa ne da mai gudanarwa ya ayyana.

Aikin `ensurePool` yana ba da sakamako iri ɗaya idan aka maimaita shi: rukuni mai bayanai iri ɗaya ba ya canzawa, ana sabunta rabon albarkatu da ya canza, kuma ana ƙirƙirar rukunin da babu shi. An tsara wannan ne don sarrafa kansa da masu kiran API masu bin iyakoki.

Wurin samun matsayin da ake karantawa kawai shi ne `GET /api/omniroute/status`. Umarnin tabbatarwa shi ne `npm run omniroute:verify`; ba ya yin wata buƙatar samfurin kai tsaye.
