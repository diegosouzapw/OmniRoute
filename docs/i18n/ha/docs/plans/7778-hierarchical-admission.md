1. Kulle ƙa'idar semaphore mai maɓalli guda da ake da ita da kuma sabuwar ƙa'idar atomic mai maɓallai da yawa da gwaje-gwaje masu mayar da hankali: babu ajiyar wani ɓangare kawai, jerin gwano na FIFO, abort, timeout, cikar jerin gwano, release mai idempotent, ƙididdiga, da tsaftacewa.
2. Faɗaɗa semaphore na account da ake da shi a wurinsa. Riƙe `acquire()` a matsayin wrapper na dacewa kewaye da `acquireMany()`; kada a ƙara scheduler na biyu ko dependency.
3. Sauya acquisition na account kaɗai a cikin `chatCore` da acquisition guda ɗaya na jimillar global/provider/account nan take kafin `withRateLimit`. Sake acquisition na dukan saitin duk lokacin da rotation na account ya sauya connection, kuma a riƙe release har sai streaming ya kammala.
4. Faɗaɗa pipeline na resilience settings da ake da shi (types, defaults, normalization, schema, martanin API, UI, da fassarori) da caps na global da provider. Sake sanya wa tsohon sarrafawar concurrency na Bottleneck suna zuwa concurrency na connection/quota-scope domin a bayyana ainihin scope ɗinsa.
5. Gudanar da gwaje-gwaje masu mayar da hankali, lint, typecheck, static checks, da cikakken test suite; rubuta bayanin sauyin ɗabi'ar a cikin changelog.

Ɗabi'ar da aka kiyaye da gangan: concurrency na sifili/null yana tsallake gate, masu kira masu amfani da account kaɗai suna ci gaba da amfani da `acquire()`, sarrafawar blocked-account tana riƙe tsarin key da API ɗinta, kuma ɗabi'ar jerin gwano na rate-limit na provider ba ta canza ba.
