## 1. Haƙiƙanin bayanan sabis (takardun hukuma, an sake tabbatarwa)

- Tushen URL: `https://api.anysearch.com` (REST) + `POST /mcp` (MCP, JSON-RPC 2.0).
- `POST /v1/search` - sigogi: `query` (wajibi), `max_results` (1-10, tsoho 10), `tag` (`{domain}.{sub_domain}` na tura buƙata zuwa fanni), `zone` (cn/intl), `language`, `params` (filayen fanni masu tsari), `format` (json/markdown).
- `GET /v1/sub-domains?domain=...` - kundin iyawa, BA ya ƙirguwa cikin ƙayyadadden adadin amfani.
- `POST /v1/extract` - ɗakko/cire `{url, title, content}`; jikin JSON mai tsauraran ƙa’idoji, iyakar 16 KiB.
- `POST /v1/auth/email/register` - rajista da kira guda, yana dawo da maɓallin rubutu na fili na amfani sau ɗaya `as_sk_...`.
- Tantancewa: `Authorization: Bearer <as_sk_...>` na zaɓi ne; amfani ba tare da tantancewa ba yana koma wa iyakokin kowane IP waɗanda ke cin ƙayyadadden adadin kyauta na kullum; maɓalli mara inganci yana dawo da 401/403 ba tare da komawa ga amfani marar tantancewa a ɓoye ba.
- Matakin kyauta: buƙatu 1000/rana, 20 QPS ga kowane maɓalli. Matakin biyan kuɗi: ba a sa farashi ba (Coming Soon).
- Kundin amsa: nasara `{code: 0, message: "success", request_id, data}`; gazawa `{code: -1, message}`. Kurakuran tantancewa/ƙayyadadden amfani ba su ɗauke da `error_code` mai tsari (rubutun saƙon ne alamar); kurakuran da suka shafi cirewa suna ɗauke da `error_code` (`invalid_extract_url`, `extract_failed`). Babu taken `Retry-After` a kan 429.

## 3. Shawarwari (duk an cimma su a 2026-08-26)

1. **Matsayin routing: `fallbackOnly`.** Dalili: mai bayar da sabis na kyauta mai farashi 0 bai kamata ya taɓa mamaye routing na farashi ta atomatik ba; zaɓi kai tsaye da komawa bayan failover ba su shafu ba. Misalin da aka riga aka kafa: shigarwar xquik da aka haɗe.
2. **Iyaka: webSearch + webFetch ta `POST /v1/extract` a cikin v1.** Wannan ya yi daidai da tsarin fahimtar damar biyu na tavily/exa. Fannonin musamman (`tag`, `sub-domains`, `batch_search`) ba sa cikin iyaka — a halin yanzu babu IR don sigogin musamman.
3. **Nuna quota: `freeMonthlyQuota: 0`** (nuni mai taka-tsantsan irin na xquik). Ainihin adadin da aka amince da shi (buƙatu 1000/rana, sake saiti kullum) yana cikin rubutun `authHint` na kundin UI maimakon haka. Dalili: nunin quota dole ne ya dace da tsarin sake saiti; sauya iyakar kullum zuwa kimar wata-wata (30000) alkawari ne na ƙarya wanda UI ba zai iya cikawa ba.
4. **searchTypes: `["web"]` kawai.** Babu wata hujja ta jama'a game da fannin labarai/hotuna a cikin AnySearch API; gateways da ke haɗa APIs ba tare da wani fanni da aka rubuta a takardu ba (misali Google Custom Search) suna samar da web kawai maimakon haɗa news -> web a ɓoye.
5. **Matsayin key:** amfani ba tare da key ba yana aiki; key marar inganci yana mayar da 401/403 kuma ba a taɓa saukar da shi a ɓoye zuwa anonymous ba (daidai da kwangilar upstream).
6. **Abubuwan da aka cire da gangan:** `FETCH_BACKEND_TO_PROVIDER` / `FetchInterceptionBackend` (tsoma bakin chat zai ci gaba da kasancewa firecrawl/jina/tavily); `ANONYMOUS_CAPABLE_WEB_FETCH_PROVIDERS` (remote endpoints na anonymous suna amfani da tafkin da aka iyakance da IP guda ɗaya ga kowane egress IP, saboda haka keyless extract zaɓi ne na opt-in, ba ɗan takarar auto-routing ba); `QUOTA_STATUS_PROVIDERS` (AnySearch yana nuna quota ta 429/envelope, ba matsayin 402/403 ba).
