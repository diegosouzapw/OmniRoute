Wannan takarda tana bayyana tushen daidaita zaɓin hanya bisa ra'ayoyin sakamako da aka ƙara wa
OmniRoute. An tsara shi da gangan ya zama ƙarami: yana gabatar da tashar sakamakon zaɓin hanya
mai nau'i, siginar inganci ta kan-layi da ke ciyar da mai ƙididdige auto-combo da ake da shi, mai
fitar da bayanai zuwa OpenTelemetry na zaɓi, da kuma maƙurar bayani. **Ba ya**
maye gurbin tsarin juriya da ake da shi (circuit breaker, sanyaya haɗi,
kulle samfur, health matrix, autopilot) — yana ƙara masa ƙarfi ne.

## 1. Mahallin gine-gine

OmniRoute matakin bayanai ne mai **hanyar buƙata mai sauri** da kuma **matakin sarrafawa/basira**.
Dole ne hanyar mai sauri ta ci gaba da kasancewa mai sauri, mai ingantaccen amfani da ƙwaƙwalwa,
asynchronous, mai juriya kuma mai sauƙin hasashen hali. Kimantawa, ƙididdige inganci, gwaje-gwaje
da nazarin tarihi suna cikin matakin sarrafawa.

```
Wakilin AI / IDE
      │
      ▼
┌─────────────────────┐
│    OmniRoute        │   matakin bayanai (mai sauri, sync, cikin ƙwaƙwalwa)
│  zaɓin hanya / sauyawa│
│  lafiya / kariya    │
│  ma'ajiyar wucin gadi / yaɗawa │
└──────────┬──────────┘
           │ RoutingEvent (aika-kawai-ba-jira, ~0.2µs)
           ▼
┌─────────────────────┐
│  Masu karɓar ra'ayi │   matakin sarrafawa (async, gwargwadon iko)
│  mai bibiyar inganci│
│  mai fitar da OTel  │
│  ma'ajiyar bayani   │
└──────────┬──────────┘
           ▼  makin inganci
      mai ƙididdige auto-combo
```

### Abin da ya riga ya kasance (an bincika, ba a maimaita ba)

| Ma'ana                                        | Aiwatarwar da ake da ita                                                                       |
| --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Samuwa (za mu iya aika zirga-zirga?)          | Circuit breaker (CLOSED/DEGRADED/OPEN/HALF_OPEN, an adana a DB), sanyaya haɗi, kulle samfur    |
| Rahoton lafiya                                | `providerHealthMatrix.ts`, `providerHealthAutopilot.ts`                                        |
| Zirga-zirgar inuwa                            | `open-sse/services/combo/shadowRouting.ts`                                                     |
| Matakan kariya                                | `src/lib/guardrails/` (ƙugiyoyin kafin/bayan aiki)                                             |
| Ma'ajiyar wucin gadi ta daidaitaccen sakamako | `src/lib/semanticCache.ts` (bisa sa hannu)                                                     |
| Masu kimantawa / zaɓin hanya bisa kimantawa   | `src/lib/evals/`, `open-sse/services/evalRouting.ts`                                           |
| Bayanin shawarar combo                        | `open-sse/services/combo/decisionTrace.ts`                                                     |
| Abubuwan dashboard na ainihin lokaci          | `src/lib/events/eventBus.ts` (tashar sanarwar UI, payloads na `unknown`, tarihin shigarwa 100) |

Matakin routing-event **ba** sake aiwatar da `eventBus` ba ne: wannan bus ɗin shi ne
tashar sanarwar dashboard ta ainihin lokaci (sunayen _event_ masu nau'i, payloads
marasa fayyace tsari, masu amfani na UI). `RoutingEvent` tsari ne na _sakamako_ mai nau'i
(latency/tokens/cost/outcome/finish-reason), wanda masu karɓar ra'ayi na matakin sarrafawa
(mai bibiyar inganci, mai fitar da OTel, ma'ajiyar bayani) ke amfani da shi.

### Abin da ya ɓace (wanda aka ƙara a nan)

1. **Abin da ya faru na sakamakon zaɓin hanya mai nau'i + tsarin sink**
   (`RoutingEvent` / `RoutingEventSink`). `decisionTrace` ya taƙaitu ga combo kuma yana
   cikin ƙwaƙwalwa kawai; `comboMetrics` ƙididdigogi ne masu taruwa; `call_logs` kuma
   adanawa ne na danye ta hanyar async. Babu ɗayansu da yake tashar sakamako mai nau'i,
   wadda ta dogara da sink, kuma mai bibiyar inganci, mai fitar da OTel, ko mai kimantawa
   irin na Future-AGI zai iya yin rajista da ita.
2. **Siginar inganci ta kan-layi** (EWMA) don ingancin fitarwa — a baya mai ƙididdigewar
   yana wakiltar "inganci" ne kawai ta hanyar dacewar aiki marar sauyawa da ƙimar cin
   gwajin kimantawa da ake shiga bisa zaɓi.
3. **Mai fitar da OTel na zaɓi, wanda ba ya buƙatar wani dependency**, ta amfani da ƙa'idojin
   ma'anar GenAI.
4. **Maƙurar bayani** da ke dawo da ainihin shawarwarin zaɓin hanya + yanayin inganci.

## 2. Abubuwan da Suka Faru na Routing (tushen feedback)

Fayiloli: `open-sse/services/routing/events.ts`, `.../index.ts`

`RoutingEvent` yana ɗauke da metadata na routing kawai:

```ts
interface RoutingEvent {
  requestId: string;
  provider: string;
  model: string;
  strategy: string; // "auto" | "priority" | "direct" | ...
  latencyMs: number;
  ttftMs: number | null;
  inputTokens: number | null;
  outputTokens: number | null;
  cost: number | null;
  retries: number;
  fallbackUsed: boolean;
  outcome: RoutingOutcome; // union da ke cikin jerin waɗanda aka yarda da su
  status: number | null;
  finishReason: string | null;
  connectionId: string | null;
  ts: number;
}
```

`RoutingEventSink` trait ne irin na `Send+Sync` a cikin TypeScript:

```ts
interface RoutingEventSink {
  readonly name: string;
  record(event: RoutingEvent): void; // dole ne ya kasance O(1), babu I/O na sync
}
```

Hot path yana kiran `emitRoutingEvent(event)` sau ɗaya ga kowace buƙatar da aka kammala
(callback na kammala streaming, hanyar nasarar da ba ta streaming ba, da kuma
hanyar gazawar malformed-200 a cikin `handleChatCore`). Ana yin dispatch ta fan-out
na synchronous zuwa sinks da aka yi rajista, amma kowane sink yana saka abu cikin layi
ko sabunta state na cikin memory kawai. **Babu rubutun database na synchronous,
babu network I/O a kan hot path.**

Sinks na tsoho:

- `MemoryRoutingEventStore` — ring buffer mai iyaka (500), daga sabo zuwa tsoho, don
  explain endpoint.
- Mai amfani da `QualityTracker` — yana sabunta ƙiyasin quality na EWMA.
- `OtlpHttpsEventSink` — na zaɓi ne, ana kunna shi ne kawai idan an saita
  `OMNIROUTE_OTEL_ENDPOINT` (ko `OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT`).

### Overhead da aka auna (kwatanci na gaskiya)

`npm run bench:routing-events` a kan wannan workstation (iterations 100k; ops na
ƙasa da µs an auna su a matsayin jimillar µs/op saboda percentiles na kowane op
suna ƙasa da resolution na timer na `performance.now()`):

| Yanayi                             | µs/op  | ops/s  |
| ---------------------------------- | ------ | ------ |
| baseline (scoring kawai)           | ~0.045 | ~22 M  |
| baseline + RoutingEvent (sinks 2)  | ~0.168 | ~5.9 M |
| baseline + event + OTel enqueue    | ~0.163 | ~6.1 M |
| concurrent (bursts 8 masu gauraya) | ~0.18  | —      |

Bambancin event-dispatch sama da baseline scoring kusan ~0.12 µs/request ne; OTel sink
yana saka abu cikin layi kawai (push na buffer na O(1)), ba tare da ƙara wani abin
da za a iya aunawa ba. Waɗannan alkaluma sun keɓanta ga wannan na’ura kuma na
kwatanci ne — ba garanti ba ne na production. Alkalumar v1 ta "~0.2 µs" ƙiyasi ne
na jimilla; wannan hanya tana ware baseline na scoring daga kuɗin event-dispatch.

## 3. Alamar Quality (state na provider da feedback ke jagoranta)

Fayiloli: `open-sse/services/routing/quality.ts`

v2 yana ware quality na **operational** daga na **semantic**:

- **Operational** — ana samo shi daga hot path na routing (HTTP 4xx/5xx, gazawar
  connection, 429s, responses marasa tsari, katsewar stream, `finish_reason=length`,
  nasarori marasa output, EWMA na latency/TTFT). BA a ɗaukar 200 a matsayin quality
  na semantic.
- **Semantic** — ainihin ƙimar output da aka samar. Mai evaluator ne KAWAI zai iya
  samar da shi ta hanyar `setSemanticQuality()`. Yana zama `null` har sai an samar da
  shi, kuma ba ya taɓa shiga cikin operational score.

State na kowane-(provider, model) (EWMA + counters masu iyaka):

- `successEwma` — EWMA (α=0.2) na nasarar outcome.
- `latencyEwma` / `ttftEwma` — EWMA na latency (α=0.1).
- `samples`, `anomalies`, `rateLimited`, `semantic`, `semanticConfidence`.
- `recencyMs` — tsawon lokacin da ya wuce tun bayan lura da model ɗin na ƙarshe.

### Sanin confidence / samples

`confidence = clamp01(samples / 50)`, kuma score da ake mayarwa ga scorer ana
haɗa shi zuwa tsakiyar neutral:

```
score = 0.5 + confidence * (operational - 0.5)
```

Sakamako (gwaje-gwaje sun tabbatar):

- Cold provider (samples 0) yana samun score na **0.5** — ba a hukunta shi ba bisa
  rashin adalci, amma ba zai iya fin provider mai dubban ingantattun observations ba.
- Provider mai nasarori 7 na sa’a ana ja da shi zuwa 0.5 (ba ya taɓa fin wasu saboda
  optimistic initialization).
- Provider mai samples 50+ yana converging zuwa ainihin operational score ɗinsa.
- Lalacewa da farfaɗowa suna faruwa a hankali (EWMA), kuma gazawa guda ɗaya tak
  ba ta lalata lafiyayyen provider.

`ProviderQuality` yana fitar da `{ operational, semantic, confidence, samples, anomalies,
rateLimited, successEwma, latencyEwmaMs, ttftEwmaMs, recencyMs }`.

Wannan yana ciyar da auto-combo scorer a matsayin `quality` scoring factor:

- `ScoringFactors.quality` / `ScoringWeights.quality` a cikin
  `open-sse/services/autoCombo/scoring.ts`.
- `DEFAULT_WEIGHTS`: `health` 0.1905 → 0.1605, `quality` 0.03. Jimilla tana nan 1.0.
- `buildAutoCandidates` yana cika `candidate.quality` daga tracker; candidates
  marasa data suna amfani da neutral **0.5** a matsayin tsoho (cold candidate ba a
  ƙara masa fifiko kuma ba a hukunta shi).

Closed loop:

```
RoutingEvent → QualityTracker → getQualityScore → auto-combo quality factor
      ↑                                                    │
      └────── request outcome (handleChatCore) ←────────────┘
```

### Hard exclusion da soft penalty

Alamar quality **soft adaptive preference** ce kawai. Hard exclusion yana nan
tare da resilience stack da ake da shi: circuit breaker OPEN, quota ya ƙare,
gazawar auth, model lockout — quality score ba ya shafar ko ɗaya daga cikinsu.
Provider wanda quality score ɗinsa ya sauka na ɗan lokaci za a rage fifita shi ne,
ba za a taɓa hard-disable ɗinsa ba.

## 3b. Daidaitaccen lokacin stream (TTFT / ITL)

Fayiloli: `open-sse/utils/streamTiming.ts`

`createStreamTiming()` ita ce kaɗai mahadar tattara bayanan lura ga hanyar streaming,
kuma an haɗa ta da `createSSEStream` (open-sse/utils/stream.ts):

- `markByte()` — an karɓi chunk na farko daga upstream.
- `markForward()` — an tura chunk na farko zuwa ga abokin ciniki (ana amfani da shi don TTFT).
- `markInterrupted()` — ƙarewar lokaci/katsewa/kuskuren stream kafin ya kammala yadda ya kamata.
- `ttft()` = jinkirin chunk na SSE na farko da aka tura. **Wannan BA TTFT na matakin token ba ne** —
  chunk guda na SSE na iya ɗaukar sifili/token guda/tokens da yawa. An rubuta bayaninsa daidai.
- `avgItlMs()` = matsakaicin tazarar lokaci tsakanin chunks (ma'aunin kusanci na jinkirin chunk don ITL).

TTFT/ITL/katsewa suna shiga cikin `RoutingEvent` (`ttftMs`, `itlMs`) kuma ana
fitar da su a matsayin siffofin span na GenAI/OmniRoute ta hanyar sink na OTel.

## 4. OpenTelemetry / lura da GenAI

Fayiloli: `open-sse/services/routing/otel.ts`

- Mai fitar da OTLP/HTTP JSON wanda ba ya dogaro da wasu dependencies (yana amfani da `fetch` na duniya, ba
  SDK na `@opentelemetry/*` ba).
- Spans suna bin ƙa'idodin ma'ana na GenAI (`gen_ai.provider.name`,
  `gen_ai.request.model`, `gen_ai.usage.input_tokens/output_tokens`,
  `gen_ai.completion.finish_reason`, `gen_ai.system`) tare da siffofin routing
  na OmniRoute (sakamako, matsayi, ttft, sake-gwadawa, fallback).
- `record()` kawai yana saka abubuwa cikin buffer mai iyaka (O(1)); timer da ke aiki a bango
  yana tura su ta `POST {endpoint}/v1/traces` ba tare da jira ba. Idan nauyi ya yi yawa,
  ana zubar da tsofaffin events (`dropped` counter) — ba a taɓa sanya backpressure a kan
  data plane ba.
- **A kashe yake sai an saita shi.** Dole ne a saita `OMNIROUTE_OTEL_ENDPOINT` (ko
  `OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT`); in ba haka ba, ba za a yi rijistar sink ɗin ba
  kuma babu lambar OTel da za ta gudana.

## 5. Bayyananniyar fahimta

- `GET /v1/explain/routing` yana mayar da `RoutingEvent`s na baya-bayan nan (ainihin
  shawarwarin, daga sabo zuwa tsoho) da kuma hoton inganci na kowane provider/model.
- Tantance izini yana kwaikwayon `/v1/combos` (Bearer API key ko zaman dashboard; ba tare da suna ba a
  girke-girken gida na mai amfani guda ɗaya da ke da `REQUIRE_API_KEY=false`).
- Traces na kowane kira a matakin combo suna nan ta hanyar
  `decisionTrace.ts` da ake da shi (header `X-OmniRoute-Combo-Trace`).
- Tsaro: events suna ɗauke da metadata na routing kawai, ba sa taɓa ɗaukar prompts/bodies/credentials.

## 6. Haɗewar evaluation-plane (shirye-shiryen Future AGI)

OmniRoute yana ɗaukar Future AGI (ko duk wani evaluator) a matsayin **mai yiwuwar
backend na hankali/kimantawa, ba dependency ba**. Mahadodin su ne:

- `RoutingEventSink` zai iya tura events zuwa evaluator ba tare da jira ba.
- `MemoryRoutingEventStore` + hoton inganci suna bai wa evaluator ɗin ainihin
  jerin shawarwarin da aka yanke.
- Wani `Evaluator` na gaba (deterministic, local judge, HTTP, WASM) zai karɓi
  events/traces ya kuma mayar da `QualityScore` da zai shiga hanyar
  `getQualityScore`/quality-factor ɗin nan take.
- Routing da kimantawa ke jagoranta da ake da shi (`open-sse/services/evalRouting.ts`) tuni yana
  sake jera combo targets bisa pass-rates na `eval_runs` idan an kunna shi.

Babu wani evaluation da ke gudana synchronously a hanyar request, kuma gateway yana aiki
cikakke ko da babu evaluator.

## 7. Binciken gine-gine na ƙarshe

1. **Me ya rage a synchronous hot path?** Routing/scoring, binciken guardrail
   na farko, neman cache, da fan-out guda ɗaya na `emitRoutingEvent` (~0.12 µs sama da
   baseline scoring) zuwa sinks na cikin memory.
2. **Me aka matsar zuwa sarrafawa ba tare da jira ba?** Fitar da OTel (timer + fetch),
   adana `call_logs`/usage, rubutun semantic-cache; inganci kuwa yana cikin memory
   kuma O(1) ne (ba ya buƙatar async).
3. **Ta yaya sakamakon routing yake zama feedback?** `handleChatCore` yana fitar da
   `RoutingEvent` → `QualityTracker` yana sabunta yanayin EWMA → `getQualityScore`
   yana ciyar da factor na `quality` na auto-combo.
4. **Ta yaya inganci ke tasiri kan routing na gaba?** Ƙaramin maki na inganci yana rage
   weighted score na wannan provider/model a cikin `scoreAutoTargets`, don haka
   a hankali ana rage fifita models da ingancinsu ya lalace, sannan su farfaɗo yayin da EWMA ɗinsu ke inganta.
5. **Ta yaya Future AGI zai haɗu ba tare da zama dependency ba?** Ta hanyar
   interface na `RoutingEventSink` / adapter na `Evaluator` na gaba — babu
   dependency da aka hardcode.
6. **Me zai faru idan evaluator bai samuwa ba?** Routing ba zai shafa ba;
   inganci zai koma neutral (1.0) ga models da ba su da siginar da aka lura da ita.
7. **Me zai faru idan telemetry bai samuwa ba?** Kawai ba za a yi rijistar sink na OTel ba;
   sauran sashen routing zai ci gaba da gudana ba tare da canji ba.
8. **Me zai faru idan nauyi ya yi yawa?** Buffer na OTel zai zubar da tsofaffin events; inganci
   da ring buffer suna da iyaka tun daga ƙirarsu; babu backpressure.
9. **Ta yaya yanayin provider ke farfaɗowa bayan lalacewa?** EWMA yana sake daidaituwa yayin da
   nasarori ke ƙaruwa; warmup yana barin cold models a matsayin neutral; circuit breaker
   kuma yana farfaɗowa daban ta hanyar gwaje-gwajen HALF_OPEN.
10. **Waɗanne fasaloli da aka gabatar aka yi niyyar BA aiwatar da su ba, kuma me ya sa?**
    - Shadow traffic / experiments — an riga an aiwatar da su
      (`combo/shadowRouting.ts`); ba a sake gina su ba.
    - Guardrails — an riga an aiwatar da su (`src/lib/guardrails/`); ba a maimaita su ba.
    - Semantic cache — an riga an aiwatar da shi (`src/lib/semanticCache.ts`); ba a
      maimaita shi ba.
