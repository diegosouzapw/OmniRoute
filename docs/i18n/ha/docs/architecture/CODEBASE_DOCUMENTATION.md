## 1. Tarin Fasahohi

| Abin la'akari     | Zaɓi                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Tsarin yanar gizo | **Next.js 16** (App Router, standalone output, babu global middleware)                                                   |
| Harshe            | **TypeScript 6.0+** — target `ES2022`, `module: esnext`, `moduleResolution: bundler`, `strict: false`                    |
| Runtime           | **Node.js** `>=22.22.2 <23` ko `>=24.0.0 <27` (ana tilasta shi ta hanyar `engines` + `SUPPORTED_NODE_RANGE`)             |
| Ma'ajiyar bayanai | **SQLite** ta hanyar `better-sqlite3` (singleton, WAL journaling)                                                        |
| Desktop           | **Electron 41** + `electron-builder` 26.10 (workspace na daban a `electron/`)                                            |
| Gwaje-gwaje       | **Node native test runner** (unit/integration), **Vitest** (MCP, autoCombo, cache), **Playwright** (e2e + protocols-e2e) |
| Gina              | Next.js standalone ta hanyar `scripts/build/build-next-isolated.mjs`                                                     |
| Lint/tsarawa      | ESLint flat config + Prettier (`lint-staged` ta hanyar Husky pre-commit)                                                 |
| Tsarin module     | ESM a ko'ina (`"type": "module"`)                                                                                        |
| Workspaces        | npm workspace — `open-sse` ne kawai ƙaramin workspace                                                                    |

Laƙabin hanyoyi (`tsconfig.json`):

- `@/*` → `src/*`
- `@omniroute/open-sse` → `open-sse/index.ts`
- `@omniroute/open-sse/*` → `open-sse/*`

Tsohuwar tashar HTTP: **`20128`** (API da dashboard suna amfani da process ɗaya). Kundin
bayanai shi ne env var `DATA_DIR`, wanda tsohuwar ƙimarsa ita ce `~/.omniroute/`.

### 3.1 `src/app/` — App Router

App Router yana samar da duka UI na dashboard da API na HTTP na jama'a/gudanarwa.
**Babu middleware na duniya** — ana yin katsalandan ne a kowace hanya.

Manyan sassa ƙarƙashin `src/app/`:

| Hanya                                                                         | Manufa                                              |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| `api/`                                                                        | Duk hanyoyin API na HTTP (duba rarrabuwar ƙasa)     |
| `a2a/`                                                                        | Endpoint na A2A JSON-RPC 2.0 (`POST /a2a`)          |
| `.well-known/agent.json/`                                                     | Takardar gano A2A Agent Card                        |
| `(dashboard)/`                                                                | UI na dashboard (rukunin hanya, babu prefix na URL) |
| `auth/`, `login/`, `forgot-password/`, `callback/`                            | Gudanawar tantancewa                                |
| `landing/`                                                                    | Shafin talla/zuwa                                   |

| `status/`, `maintenance/`, `offline/`                                         | Shafukan aiki                                       |
| `privacy/`, `terms/`                                                          | Shafukan doka                                       |
| `400/`, `401/`, `403/`, `408/`, `429/`, `500/`, `502/`, `503/`                | Tsayayyun shafukan kuskure                          |

## 10. Yadda Ake Ba da Gudummawa

### Ƙara sabon mai samarwa

1. Yi rajista a `src/shared/constants/providers.ts` (ana tantance shi da Zod lokacin lodawa).
2. Ƙara mai aiwatarwa a `open-sse/executors/` idan ana buƙatar dabaru na musamman
   (faɗaɗa `BaseExecutor`).
3. Ƙara mai fassara a `open-sse/translator/` idan ba ya amfani da tsarin OpenAI.
4. Idan yana amfani da OAuth, ƙara saituna a ƙarƙashin `src/lib/oauth/providers/` da
   `src/lib/oauth/services/`.
5. Yi rajistar samfura a `open-sse/config/providerRegistry.ts` (ko ma'ajiyar rajista ta takamaiman tsari
   a ƙarƙashin `open-sse/config/`).
6. Rubuta gwaje-gwaje a ƙarƙashin `tests/unit/`.

### Ƙara sabuwar hanyar API

1. Ƙirƙiri `src/app/api/your-route/route.ts`.
2. Bi tsarin: CORS → tantance jikin buƙata da Zod → tabbatar da izini → miƙa aikin ga mai sarrafawa.
3. Idan tsarin buƙatar sabo ne: ƙara tsarin Zod a `src/shared/validation/schemas.ts`.
4. Idan na gudanarwa kawai ne: ƙara hanyar zuwa `src/shared/constants/publicApiRoutes.ts`
   (jerin hanawa na ɓangaren API na jama'a).
5. Ƙara gwaje-gwaje a ƙarƙashin `tests/unit/`.
6. Sabunta `docs/reference/API_REFERENCE.md` da `docs/openapi.yaml`.

### Ƙara sabon tsarin DB

1. Ƙirƙiri `src/lib/db/yourModule.ts` sannan ka shigo da `getDbInstance()` daga `./core.ts`.
2. Fitar da ayyukan CRUD na fannin naka.
3. Idan akwai sabbin tebura: ƙara ƙaura a ƙarƙashin `src/lib/db/migrations/`, mai lamba
   bi-da-bi, wanda za a iya maimaitawa ba tare da illa ba, kuma mai amfani da transaction.
4. Masu shigo da abubuwa su yi amfani da shigo da kai tsaye daga `@/lib/db/yourModule` (ba barrel ba — an cire tsohon matakin sake fitarwa na `localDb.ts`).
5. Ƙara gwaje-gwaje a ƙarƙashin `tests/unit/`.

   `open-sse/mcp-server/schemas/tools.ts`).

3. Yi rajistar kayan aikin a `open-sse/mcp-server/server.ts`.

## 11. Ka'idoji

- **Salon lamba**: tazarar sarari 2, alamomin zance biyu, faɗin haruffa 100, semicolons,
  waƙafi na ƙarshe na `es5` — Prettier ne ke tilasta su ta hanyar `lint-staged`.
- **Shigo da abubuwa**: na waje → na ciki (`@/`, `@omniroute/open-sse`) → na dangi.
- **Sanya suna**: fayiloli `camelCase` ko `kebab-case`, abubuwan haɗawa `PascalCase`,
  tsayayyun ƙimomi `UPPER_SNAKE`.
- **ESLint**: `no-eval`, `no-implied-eval`, `no-new-func` = `error` a ko'ina;
  `no-explicit-any` = `warn` a `open-sse/` da `tests/`, kuskure a sauran wurare.
- **TypeScript**: `strict: false` (tsarin gado). Fi son bayyana nau'uka kai tsaye maimakon
  dogaro da hasashe a iyakokin da ke tsakanin modules.
- **Rumbun bayanai**: kada a taɓa rubuta ɗanyen SQL a hanyoyi ko masu sarrafawa — a koyaushe a bi ta
  modules na `src/lib/db/`. Kada a taɓa yin shigo da barrel — yi amfani da takamaiman modules na `src/lib/db/*` kai tsaye.
- **Nau'in DB-entity (#3512)**: aikin da ke rubutawa ko karanta tsarin layin teburin DB
  ya kamata ya karɓa/mayar da interface na TS mai suna wanda ya yi daidai da ginshiƙan teburin
  1:1, ba `any` ko nau'i marar suna da aka rubuta kai tsaye a wurin kiran ba. Sanya
  interface ɗin kusa da aikin (misali `export interface UsageEntry` a
  `src/lib/usage/usageHistory.ts` sama da `saveRequestUsage`), ka bar kowane
  field a matsayin na zaɓi/mai karɓar null idan marubuta daban-daban suna cika layin
  a hankali, kuma ka fi son `unknown` maimakon `any` ga field wanda tsarinsa
  ya bambanta tsakanin masu kira (a rubuta bayaninsa a kan field ɗin, misali `UsageEntry.tokens`
  yana karɓar duka ɗanyen usage mai tsarin mai samarwa da kuma tsarin da aka daidaita). Da zarar
  adadin `any` na fayil ya kai sifili ta wannan hanya, ƙara shi zuwa jerin izinin
  `check:any-budget:t11` (`scripts/check/check-t11-any-budget.mjs`,
  `maxAny: 0`) don kada ya koma baya. Wannan ka'ida ce ta matakin farko — aikin
  tsaftace "babu `any` marar suna" mafi faɗi yana gudana a hankali a sauran
  codebase ɗin.
- **Kurakurai**: yi amfani da try/catch tare da takamaiman nau'ukan kuskure, kuma yi log da mahallin pino. Kada
  a taɓa haɗiye kurakurai shiru a rafukan SSE; yi amfani da siginonin abort don tsaftacewa.
- **Tsaro**: kada a taɓa amfani da `eval()` / `new Function()` / implied eval. Tantance
  dukkan bayanan shigarwa da Zod. Ɓoye bayanan shaidar shiga yayin ajiya (AES-256-GCM). Tabbatar
  jerin hanawa na `src/shared/constants/upstreamHeaders.ts` ya yi daidai da
  matakin sanitize/tantancewa.
- **Commits**: Conventional Commits — `feat(scope): subject`. Scopes da aka yarda:
  `db`, `sse`, `oauth`, `dashboard`, `api`, `cli`, `docker`, `ci`, `mcp`,
  `a2a`, `memory`, `skills`.
- **Branches**: prefixes `feat/`, `fix/`, `refactor/`, `docs/`, `test/`,
  `chore/`. Kada a taɓa yin commit kai tsaye zuwa `main`.
- **Husky**: pre-commit yana gudanar da `lint-staged` + `check:docs-sync` +
  `check:any-budget:t11`; pre-push yana gudanar da `check:any-budget:t11` + `check:tracked-artifacts` (matakan tantancewa masu sauri; ba ya haɗa da `test:unit`).

## 12. Tsauraran Dokoki (daga CLAUDE.md)

1. Kada a taɓa commit na sirrika ko bayanan shaidar shiga.
2. Kada a taɓa yin barrel-import — yi amfani da takamaiman modules na `src/lib/db/*` kai tsaye.
3. Kada a taɓa amfani da `eval()` / `new Function()` / eval na ɓoye.
4. Kada a taɓa yin commit kai tsaye zuwa `main`.
5. Kada a taɓa rubuta ɗanyen SQL a cikin routes — koyaushe a bi ta modules na `src/lib/db/`.
6. Kada a taɓa haɗiye kurakurai ba tare da sanarwa ba a cikin rafukan SSE.
7. Koyaushe a tabbatar da ingancin inputs ta amfani da schemas na Zod.
8. Koyaushe a haɗa tests yayin sauya production code.
9. Dole coverage ya ci gaba da kasancewa ≥ 60% (statements, lines, functions, branches).
