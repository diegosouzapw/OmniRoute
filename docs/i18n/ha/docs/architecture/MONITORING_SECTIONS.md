## Sashen Kuɗaɗe (sabo, mataki na 1)

Farkon hanya: `/dashboard/costs/`

| Abu                | URL                                  | Bayani                                               |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Taƙaitaccen Bayani | `/dashboard/costs`                   | Haɗaɗɗen dashboard na kuɗaɗe (an matsar daga Nazari) |
| Farashi            | `/dashboard/costs/pricing`           | Teburin farashi na kowane samfuri                    |
| Kasafin Kuɗi       | `/dashboard/costs/budget`            | Iyakokin kasafin kuɗi + faɗakarwa                    |
| Raba Ƙa'ida        | `/dashboard/costs/quota-share`       | Rukunin Quota Share + amfani                         |
| Tsarin Shiri       | `/dashboard/costs/quota-share/plans` | Keɓantattun saitunan shiri na kowane mai bayarwa     |

**Dalili**: A baya, Farashi, Kasafin Kuɗi, da Raba Ƙa'ida suna ƙarƙashin
`Sa-ido > Ma'aunin Kuɗaɗe`. Matsar da su zuwa wani keɓantaccen babban sashe
yana sauƙaƙa gano su ba tare da yin kewayawa ta cikin kayan aikin sa-ido ba.

## Sashen Sa-ido (an sake tsarawa)

Yanzu sashen Sa-ido yana da **Ayyuka a sama** sannan **ƙananan rukuni 3**:

```
Sa-ido
├── Ayyuka              ← Jerin lokaci (babban abu)
├── Rukunin Rajistoci
│   ├── Rajistoci (dukansu)
│   ├── Rajistocin Proxy
│   └── Rajistocin Console
├── Rukunin Binciken Ayyuka
│   ├── Rajistar Binciken Ayyuka
│   ├── Binciken MCP
│   └── Binciken A2A
└── Rukunin Tsari
    ├── Lafiya
    └── Lokacin Aiki
```

### Abin da ya canza daga tsohon tsari

| Kafin                                                                                                       | Bayan                                                      |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ayyuka = shafi a cikin Rajistoci wanda yake nuna Rajistar Binciken Ayyuka                                   | Ayyuka = keɓantaccen jerin bayanai (`/dashboard/activity`) |
| Rukunin Ma'aunin Kuɗaɗe a cikin Sa-ido                                                                      | An matsar zuwa sashen Kuɗaɗe                               |
| Jerin bai ɗaya: Rajistoci, Ayyuka (rajistoci), Bincike, Lafiya, Lokacin Aiki, Farashi, Kasafin Kuɗi, Ƙa'ida | Tsararrun rukuni 3 + keɓantaccen sashen Kuɗaɗe             |

## Ayyuka da Rajistar Binciken Ayyuka

Yanzu waɗannan biyun sun bambanta:

| Ma'auni                    | Ayyuka (`/dashboard/activity`)                                               | Rajistar Binciken Ayyuka (`/dashboard/audit`)    |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Manufa**                 | Jerin abubuwan da suka faru ga mai amfani ("me ya faru kwanan nan")          | Rajistar bin ƙa'ida / tsaro                      |
| **Tushen bayanai**         | `GET /api/compliance/audit-log?level=high`                                   | `GET /api/compliance/audit-log?level=all`        |
| **Tsari**                  | Jerin lokaci, an haɗa bisa rana, kalmomin aiki da gumaka masu sauƙin fahimta | Cikakken tebur mai shafuka, 50/shafi             |
| **Matatu**                 | Rukunin nau'in abin da ya faru                                               | Aiki, tsanani, mai aiwatarwa, zangon kwanan wata |
| **Fitarwa**                | Babu                                                                         | Fitarwa ta JSON                                  |
| **Tace mai aiwatarwa**     | Bai shafi wannan ba                                                          | Ana iya tacewa bisa mai aiwatarwa                |
| **Abubuwan da ake nunawa** | Manyan ayyuka kawai (jerin izini)                                            | Duk abubuwan binciken ayyuka                     |

### Jerin izinin Manyan Ayyuka

An ayyana shi a cikin `src/lib/audit/highLevelActions.ts`. Yana sarrafa abubuwan da
za su bayyana a jerin Ayyuka. Jerin izinin ya haɗa da:

- Abubuwan ƙara/cire/gwada mai bayarwa
- Ƙirƙira/sabunta/share haɗe-haɗe
- Zagayowar rayuwar mabuɗin API (ƙirƙira, soke, juyawa)
- An kai iyakar kasafin kuɗi
- Shiga/fita na tantancewa
- Ƙirƙirar zaman wakilin cloud
- Rajistar kayan aikin MCP
- Ƙirƙira/share webhook
- Canje-canjen rukunin ƙa'ida/shiri (`quota.*` actions, Rukuni B)
- Abubuwan dandamali (sabuntawa, turawa)
- Shigarwa/cire ƙwarewa

Abubuwan da ba sa cikin wannan jeri za su bayyana ne kawai a Rajistar Binciken Ayyuka.

### Ƙara sabon babban aiki

Gyara `src/lib/audit/highLevelActions.ts` sannan a ƙara rubutun aikin zuwa
`HIGH_LEVEL_ACTIONS`. Wannan yana buƙatar PR (jerin lamba ne, ba a iya saita shi ta DB).
Za a iya ƙara gunkin da ya dace zuwa `src/lib/audit/activityIcons.ts`.

## Turawa: `/dashboard/logs/activity`

Ana tura tsohuwar hanyar `/dashboard/logs/activity` ta dindindin (HTTP 308) zuwa
`/dashboard/activity` ta amfani da `permanentRedirect()` a cikin
`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/logs/activity/page.tsx`.

An adana tsohon ID na sandar gefe `logs-activity` a cikin `HIDEABLE_SIDEBAR_ITEM_IDS`
(amma an cire shi daga `SIDEBAR_DEFINITIONS`) don kauce wa lalata saitunan masu amfani
da ke nuni da tsohon ID ɗin.

| Mabuɗin namespace       | Abin da yake ƙunshe da shi                                                  |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `sidebar.costsSection`  | Sunan sashen Kuɗaɗe                                                         |
| `sidebar.activity`      | Abun Ayyuka na sandar gefe                                                  |
| `sidebar.logsGroup`     | Sunan ƙaramin rukunin Rajistoci                                             |
