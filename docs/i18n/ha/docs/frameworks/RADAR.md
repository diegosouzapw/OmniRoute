## Mai karanta sanarwar jama'a

Mai karanta sanarwa na gaba ɗaya ya bambanta da tutar fasalin Radar. Shafin Home na dashboard da
mai duba Changelog suna ɗauko `news.json` na jama'a na ma'ajiyar ta hanyar `GET` na kai tsaye zuwa
`NEWS_JSON_URL` (`src/shared/utils/releaseNotes.ts`). Ba sa aika saitin Radar, prompt, tsarin
provider, rikodin amfani, ko matsayin watsi na gida.

`news.json` yana amfani da rufaffen schema na v2 da `parseNewsPayload()` ya aiwatar:

- `schemaVersion: 2` da tarin `items[]` mai iyaka;
- ƙimomin `id` na sanarwa masu dorewa kuma na musamman;
- filayen `active` da ISO `publishedAt` da aka bayyana sarai;
- rubutun Turanci da ake buƙata tare da rubutun wasu harsuna na zaɓi;
- hanyoyin HTTPS na zaɓi waɗanda ba sa buƙatar bayanan shiga da kuma icon da ke cikin jerin waɗanda aka amince da su;
- zaɓen sabo-mai-aiki-da-farko, komawa Turanci idan locale bai samu ba, da watsi na gida bisa kowane ID.

Parser ɗin yana karɓar tsohon tsarin guda ɗaya na `{ active, title, message, ... }` na ɗan lokaci domin
tsofaffin forks su iya ƙaura ba tare da lalata kallon Changelog ba. Feeds marasa inganci ba sa aiki. Shigarwar ƙaddamar da Radar
tana zuwa da `active: false`; sauya shi zuwa `true` wani matakin fitarwa ne na daban bayan merge da bayan deploy
kuma ba ya sauya `RADAR_ENABLED` ko zaɓin shiga na feed-sync mai zaman kansa.

## Tuta: `RADAR_ENABLED` (a kashe ta tsohuwa)

Ana killace Radar daga farko har ƙarshe da tutar fasalin `RADAR_ENABLED`
(`src/shared/constants/featureFlagDefinitions.ts`, rukunin `policies`,
`defaultValue: "false"`).

**Lokacin da tutar ke kashe, wannan ɓangaren ba ya wanzuwa:**

- Duk endpoints na `/api/radar/*`, har da karatu da rubutun matsayin model na gida,
  suna mayar da `404` kafin su taɓa kowane module na Radar.
- Fuskokin dashboard (`/dashboard/radar`, `/dashboard/radar/setup`,
  `/dashboard/radar/combos`, `/dashboard/radar/offers`, `/dashboard/radar/intel`) suna render
  `notFound()`.
- `getRadarCatalog()` (`src/lib/radar/index.ts`) yana mayar da baseline ɗin da ba a taɓa ba —
  adadin entries iri ɗaya, ƙimomi iri ɗaya, kowace entry tana da alamar `origin: "baseline"` — kuma ba ya taɓa
  karanta cache na feed.
- Ba a taɓa yin kiran hanyar sadarwa na Radar ba; kowane module na sync yana mayar da `{ status: "disabled" }`
  kafin ya taɓa `fetch`.

Wannan ƙofa ce mai tsananin faɗi: kunna tutar yana buɗe _fuskokin_ ne kawai,
ba wani abu fiye da haka ba. Ba ya ɗora bayanai, ba ya fara sync a bango, kuma ba ya sauya
routing ko zaɓin model — duba zaɓin shiga na daban a ƙasa.

## Sync na bayanai zaɓin shiga ne NA DABAN — alkawarin sirri

Kunna `RADAR_ENABLED` yana buɗe UI ne kawai. Yin sync na feed yana buƙatar zaɓin shiga na biyu,
mai zaman kansa, wanda aka adana a `radar_settings.opt_in` (`src/lib/db/radar.ts`,
migration `136_radar_cache_settings.sql`). `syncRadar()` yana duba tutar _da kuma_ zaɓin
shiga kafin yin kowane kiran hanyar sadarwa:

```
Tuta a kashe   → { status: "disabled" }   — babu kiran hanyar sadarwa
Opt-in false   → { status: "opt_out" }    — babu kiran hanyar sadarwa
```

Lokacin da dukansu suke kunne, hanyar sync ita ce:

1. `GET <feed base URL>/v1/catalog/latest` tare da `x-omniroute-radar-schema: 2` da header na zaɓi
   `Authorization: Bearer <supporter key>` (duba ƙasa). Servers suna amfani da artifact na sauyin v1
   da aka sanya wa hannu daban idan schema header ba ya nan, don haka tsofaffin clients da aka girka za su ci gaba da
   karɓar sabuntawa.
2. Wannan tsarin aikace-aikacen saukewa-kawai ne, amma har yanzu request ne na HTTPS. Ababen more rayuwa
   da aka host suna karɓar metadata na haɗi na yau da kullum kamar source IP. Lokacin da aka saita supporter
   key, sync yana kuma aika wannan key ɗin a cikin Bearer header domin service ɗin ya tantance
   entitlement. A daidai revision na private-server da aka bayyana a iyakar shaidar da ke sama,
   lissafin feed-request yana amfani da hashes na key, jimillar amfani, da truncated HMAC na IP
   mai sauyawa kullum domin duba cin zarafi da hannu; waɗannan tables ba sa adana key ko IP a ɗanyen tsari.
   Logs na samun damar ababen more rayuwa da encrypted delivery outbox iyakokin aiki ne
   daban-daban.
3. OmniRoute ba ya taɓa aika prompts, responses, conversations, provider credentials, model traffic,
   uptime, latency, ko tsarin provider na gida zuwa service na Radar.
4. Ana tabbatar da response, a duba ingancinsa, sannan a adana shi a cache na gida (duba
   [Tsarin tsaro](#security-model)). Radar yana da hanyoyin hanyar sadarwa guda huɗu kacal a server-side:
   `syncRadar()` don catalog, `syncRadarReferrals()` don referrals, da
   `syncRadarOffers()` / `syncRadarIntel()` don offers da Intel na supporters kawai.

**Supporter key** wani Bearer token ne na zaɓi (`radar_settings.supporter_key`)
wanda ke ba feed service damar yanke shawarar tier ɗin da zai bayar (duba
[Tiers](#tiers-community-and-live)). Ana:

- Adana shi **a rufaffen tsari lokacin da ba ya aiki** tare da helpers na AES-256-GCM `encrypt()`/`decrypt()`
  (`src/lib/db/encryption.ts`) waɗanda ake amfani da su don provider credentials.
- Saita shi ta `POST /api/radar/settings` (`{ supporterKey: "omr_" + 40 hex chars }`) kuma
  **ba a taɓa maido da shi gaba ɗaya ba** — response ɗin yana mayar da masked form (`omr_****abcd`).
- Sauyawa ko share shi yana invalidating caches na catalog, referrals, offers, da Intel a matsayin aiki guda marar yankewa. Sync/karatu
  na gaba yana tantance sabon entitlement a server-side; adana key ba ya yin
  request na hanyar sadarwa da kansa ko cin single-use activation key.
- Aika shi zuwa feed service a matsayin Bearer token a sync GET — babu wani abu daban game da
  key ɗin da ke barin client.

sakin **ba ya** ikirarin kulle kayan aiki: daidaitawar OSS ba ta ɗaukar sawun kayan aiki ko kula da

yana soke maɓallin da ya ɓace ya kuma maye gurbinsa tare da kiyaye ranar ƙarewar da ake da ita; ba ya sake fara
lokacin da aka saya ko aka bayar.

Ana tsara tayin kai tsaye da hannu kuma suna iya canzawa ko ƙarewa. Allon amincewa kuma yana bayyana ainihin
iyakokin sirri: ana sauke metadata na kundin bayanai/referral masu sa hannu; ingantaccen maɓalli kuma yana buɗe

prompts, responses, conversations, bayanan shaidar provider, zirga-zirgar model, uptime, latency, da tsarin
provider na gida ba sa isa gare shi.

## Samun maɓallin mai tallafawa

Allon kunnawa (`/dashboard/radar`) yana haɗawa zuwa hanyoyi biyu don **samun**
maɓallin mai tallafawa. Ma'ajiyar OSS kanta ba ta taɓa bayar da maɓalli, ba ta taɓa gudanar da lambar biyan kuɗi, kuma
**ba ta taɓa bayyana farashi ba** — ana yanke shawarar farashi kuma ana nuna shi gaba ɗaya a
shafukan da ake nufi, ba a wannan ma'ajiyar ba (shawarar ƙayyadewa D14).

- **"Ni mai ba da gudummawa ne"** — yana buɗe `RADAR_CONTRIBUTOR_CLAIM_URL` (tsoho
  `https://radar.omniroute.online/auth/github`), tsarin neman haƙƙi ta GitHub OAuth da ake masaukinsa a
  sabar Radar mai zaman kanta. Yana duba cikakken jadawalin mako-mako na baya-bayan nan: Manyan 10 suna samun kwanaki 365,
  yayin da matsayi na 11–100 suke samun kwanaki 90. A wajen Manyan 100, yawan PR ba ya taɓa ba da damar shiga; maimakon haka tsarin
  yana duba matakin amfani-sau-ɗaya na tauraro + bi dabam.
- **"Tallafa wa aikin"** — yana buɗe `RADAR_SUPPORTER_PLANS_URL` (tsoho
  `https://radar.omniroute.online/planos`), shafin da aka ɗauki nauyinsa don zaɓuɓɓukan biyan sau ɗaya na watanni 6, shekara 1, da
  na har abada. Har yanzu shafin OSS ba ya nuna wata ƙimar kuɗi.

Ana warware duk URL biyun a ɓangaren saba (`src/lib/radar/links.ts`, tsarin maye-gurbin env iri ɗaya
da `RADAR_FEED_URL`) sannan a isar da su zuwa dashboard ta hanyar amsar
`GET /api/radar/settings` da ake da ita (`contributorClaimUrl`, `supporterPlansUrl`) — ɓangaren
abokin ciniki ba ya karanta `process.env` da kansa.

| Var                           | Manufa                                                                                                         |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `RADAR_CONTRIBUTOR_CLAIM_URL` | Yana maye gurbin URL na neman haƙƙin mai ba da gudummawa (tsoho `https://radar.omniroute.online/auth/github`). |
| `RADAR_SUPPORTER_PLANS_URL`   | Yana maye gurbin URL na tsare-tsaren masu tallafawa (tsoho `https://radar.omniroute.online/planos`).           |

### Maido da maɓallin mai tallafawa da ya ɓace

Mafarin dawo da bayanai na sabis ɗin da aka ɗauki nauyinsa shi ne `https://radar.omniroute.online/recover`; ana kuma
haɗa shi daga shafin tsare-tsare. Dawo da bayanai yana ci gaba da kasancewa gaba ɗaya a wajen abokin cinikin OSS saboda
girkin cikin gida ba ya taɓa karɓar imel ɗin mai saye/mai ba da gudummawa kuma ba zai iya sake gina ɗanyen maɓalli daga
saitunansa da aka rufa ba.

1. Miƙa imel ɗin da ke da alaƙa da maɓallin. Sabis ɗin yana mayar da shafin karɓa iri ɗaya ko da akwai
   lasisin da za a iya dawo da shi ko babu, don haka fom ɗin ba ya lissafa asusu.
2. Idan ya cancanta, ma'aikacin isarwa yana aika hanyar haɗi mai gajeren wa'adi da za a yi amfani da ita sau ɗaya. Buɗe ta nan take yana matsar da
   token zuwa kuki na ɗan lokaci da aka rufa na `HttpOnly`/`Secure`, sannan ya tura zuwa tsabtataccen
   URL na `/recover`; shafin ba ya ƙunshe da token, imel, tsohon maɓalli, ko maɓallin maye.
3. Tabbatar da soke maɓallin. Sabis mai zaman kansa yana soke maɓallin da ya gabata, ya ƙirƙiri maɓallin maye mai
   tsari/wa'adin ƙarewa iri ɗaya, sannan ya sanya shi cikin layin aika imel a ma'amala guda. Ba a taɓa
   mayar da maɓallin maye zuwa burauza ba.
4. Liƙa maɓallin maye cikin `/dashboard/radar`. Yanzu dole ne tsohon maɓallin ya koma `community`; dole ne
   maɓallin maye ya samar da daidaitawar `live` da aka tabbatar. Sake buɗe hanyar haɗin dawo da bayanai ɗaya dole ne ya gaza tare da
   amsar gama-gari ta rashin inganci/ƙarewar wa'adi.

Hanyar dawo da bayanai da ma'aikacin imel na sabis ɗin da aka ɗauki nauyinsa na iya kasancewa a cikin lamba alhali har yanzu ba sa samuwa a wani
turawa. Kada a kira tsarin a matsayin wanda ya shirya don amfani na ainihi har sai an tura sabar, an saita mai samar da
isarwa tare da mai karɓa da ake sarrafawa, kuma an gwada cikakkiyar hanyar haɗin amfani-sau-ɗaya.

Da zarar baƙo yana da maɓalli (`omr_` + haruffan hex 40), allon kunnawa
(`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/radar/page.tsx`) yana da filin liƙa-maɓalli a matsayin babbar
hanya: liƙa maɓalli da miƙawa yana aika `POST /api/radar/settings`
(`{ optIn: true, supporterKey }`) a kira guda — liƙa maɓalli yana saita shi tare da amincewa da shiga,
yana buɗe allon. Ana fara duba tsarin (`omr_` + haruffan hex 40) a ɓangaren abokin ciniki
ta amfani da mataimakin `isValidSupporterKeyFormat()` da aka raba (`src/lib/radar/supporterKey.ts`)
don sauƙaƙa UX; tsarin Zod na sabar shi ne ingantaccen bincike a kowane hali. Da zarar an
saita maɓalli, allon kunnawa yana nuna sigar da aka ɓoye (`supporterKeyMasked` daga
`GET /api/radar/settings`) maimakon filin shigarwa mara komai, tare da abin sarrafa "canja maɓalli" don
liƙa sabon maɓalli — ba a taɓa sake nuna ɗanyen maɓallin ba. Maɓallan neman haƙƙi/tsare-tsare biyu na sama
sun ci gaba da kasancewa hanyar _samun_ maɓalli tun farko; wannan filin shigarwa shi ne wurin da ma'aikacin
da ke da maɓalli tuni yake kunna shi.

### Kunnawa daga farko zuwa ƙarshe da saitawa mai jagoranci

Sabis ɗin feed mai zaman kansa da wannan abokin cinikin OSS suna da iyaka ƙunƙuntacciya da aka tsara da gangan: sabis ɗin
yana bayarwa kuma yana tabbatar da maɓallin mai tallafawa, yayin da girkin OmniRoute na cikin gida yake rufe maɓallin,
yana daidaita kayan aikin da aka sanya wa hannu a ɓangaren saba, kuma yana jagorantar saitin mai samarwa. Tsarin tabbatarwa mai taimako shi ne:

1. Sami sabon maɓalli da aka bayar ko aka dawo da shi daga da'awar mai ba da gudummawa, plans/checkout, tsarin
   dawo da maɓalli, ko daga mai gudanar da sabar sirri mai izini. Kada a liƙa maɓallin kai tsaye cikin rajistan ayyuka,
   hotunan allo, sharhin matsala, ko hujjojin layin umarni.
2. Kunna tutar fasalin `RADAR_ENABLED` a kan shigarwar OmniRoute ta gida. Wannan yana bayyana UI
   amma ba ya yin hulɗa da hanyar sadarwa har sai an adana amincewar shiga ta daban.
3. Buɗe `/dashboard/radar`, liƙa maɓallin, sannan a kunna. Burauzar za ta aika `POST /api/radar/settings`
   guda ɗaya na gida mai `{ optIn: true, supporterKey }`; ana ɓoye maɓallin da sirri a gida kuma
   martanin yana ƙunshe da `omr_****<last4>` kawai.
4. Bari allon kunnawa ya gudanar da daidaita kundinsa, ko zaɓi **Daidaita yanzu**. Tabbatar cewa shafin
   yana nuna `live`, sigar feed, da lokacin ɗaukowa. Don binciken matsala na gida da aka tantance,
   `GET /api/radar/status` yana nuna amincewar shiga/kasancewar maɓalli da halayen cache huɗu ba tare da dawo da
   maɓallin ba. `POST /api/radar/sync-all` na iya sabunta kundin, referrals, offers, da Intel kai tsaye.
5. Buɗe `/dashboard/radar/setup?provider=<provider>`. Bi URL na bayanan shiga mallakar provider,
   zaɓi **Ƙara maɓallin API**, adana ta hanyar ainihin fom ɗin provider, koma jagorar, sannan gudanar da
   **Gwada haɗi**. Jagorar tana amfani da hanyoyin `/api/providers` da
   `/api/providers/<connection-id>/test` na yau da kullum; ba ta ƙirƙirar wani bayanin shiga na Radar na daban.
6. Buɗe `/dashboard/radar/combos` bayan an kunna aƙalla haɗukan provider biyu masu dacewa.
   Duba dangin da aka ba da shawara sannan ƙirƙiri combo ta hanyar combo API da ake da shi. Offers da
   Intel suna ci gaba da kasancewa cache masu sa hannu na live-only daban kuma ana iya duba su a shafukansu na Radar.
7. Sake loda `/dashboard/radar` da shafin setup. Amincewar shiga, halin maɓalli da aka rufe, cache da aka
   tabbatar, haɗin provider da aka adana, da aikin gwaji dole ne su ci gaba da kasancewa bayan sake lodawa. Ɗauki shaida ne kawai bayan
   maɓallin kai tsaye da bayanan shaidar provider sun daina bayyana.

Adana maɓalli kaɗai ba hujjar samun izinin live ba ne. Hujjar ita ce haɗuwar sakamakon
`GET /v1/license/check` na sabis ɗin sirri, matakin `live` da kundin OSS yake bayarwa, cache mai sa hannu
da aka tabbatar, da ainihin tsarin haɗawa/gwajin provider. Maɓalli marar inganci, wanda wa'adinsa ya ƙare, ko aka soke yana
mayar da kundin zuwa `community` cikin aminci; kada a bayar da rahotonsa a matsayin ingantaccen tabbatar da maɓallin live.

### Mahaɗin admin-panel na sirri

`RADAR_ADMIN_URL` yana iya ƙara **Radar Admin ↗** nan take bayan abin Radar na masu amfani
a sashen Costs na sidebar. Da gangan ba shi da ƙimar tsoho: idan ba a saita variable ɗin ba
ko ba shi da inganci, sidebar na dindindin, command palette, da allon keɓance-sidebar ba za su ƙunshi
abin admin ko URL na sirri ba.

Ana tantance ƙimar a ɓangaren sabar sannan a isar da ita ta hanyar martanin
`GET /api/settings` mai tantancewar gudanarwa ga zaman dashboard da aka tantance kawai, ko ga
amintaccen mai loopback yayin fara tsarin no-login na gida. Tantancewar CLI, internal-service, da manage-scope API-key
ba sa karɓarta. Burauzar tana sake tabbatar da martanin kafin samar da
mahaɗin waje, wanda yake buɗewa da `noopener noreferrer`.

Yi amfani da URL na HTTPS tunnel/tailnet marar bayanan shiga. Ana karɓar HTTP mara ɓoyewa ne kawai don loopback SSH
forward kamar `http://127.0.0.1:9351`; sauran schemes, bayanan shiga da aka saka ciki, URLs marasa inganci, da
wuraren HTTP na nesa suna ƙin aiki cikin aminci kuma suna barin kewayawa ba ta aiki.

## Samfurin tsaro

### Sa hannun Ed25519 a kan ainihin bytes

Ana sanya wa bayanan feed hannu da Ed25519. `verifyFeedBytes()`
(`src/lib/radar/verify.ts`) yana tabbatar da sa hannun a kan **ainihin bytes na amsa**
da aka karɓa ta hanyar sadarwa — ba a taɓa sake serializing bayanan kafin tabbatarwa ba, don haka
sake encoding byte-da-byte ba zai iya ɓata ko ƙetare binciken sa hannu a ɓoye ba.
Rashin nasarar tabbatarwa (`invalid_signature`) yana dakatar da sync kafin a taɓa
parsing ko caching na bayanan.

### Maɓallin jama'a da aka kafe + juyawa

An kafe maɓallin jama'a na tabbatarwa a `src/lib/radar/pinnedKeys.ts`
(`PINNED_FEED_PUBLIC_KEYS`), wanda yake array ne domin a iya saka sabon maɓalli a gaba kafin
juyawa, yayin da tsofaffin feeds da aka cache waɗanda aka sanya wa hannu da maɓallin da ya gabata za su ci gaba da aiki har sai
an sake sync ɗinsu.

### Sauye-sauyen env masu sauƙaƙa forks

Env vars guda biyu suna bai wa forks da masu self-hosting damar sa client ya yi amfani da nasu feed maimakon
tsohuwar sabis ɗin OmniRoute — duba
[Yadda ake self-hosting na feed](#how-to-self-host-a-feed) a ƙasa:

| Var                 | Manufa                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `RADAR_FEED_URL`    | Yana maye gurbin asalin URL na feed (tsoho `https://radar.omniroute.online`).                                                         |
| `RADAR_FEED_PUBKEY` | Yana maye gurbin maɓallin jama'a da aka kafe (base64-DER SPKI ko PEM), tare da maye gurbin array na ciki da wannan maɓalli guda ɗaya. |

### Ƙaramin sigar da aka yarda da ita

`syncRadar()` yana ƙin feed da aka sauke idan `version` ɗinsa bai fi
sigar da ke cikin cache a halin yanzu sabo sosai ba (`compareVersions()`, kwatancen `YYYY.MM.DD.n` mai ɗigo) —
`{ status: "stale" }`. Wannan yana hana endpoint na feed da aka kutsa ko aka saita ba daidai ba
mayar da client zuwa tsofaffin bayanai masu wani sa hannu daban.

### Kwanaki biyu, da dalilin adana su duka

Feed da aka cache yana ɗauke da kwanaki biyu mabambanta, kuma guje wa rikita su ne ainihin dalilin
adana su duka:

| Filin         | Inda ya fito                     | Abin da yake amsawa                       |
| ------------- | -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| `generatedAt` | jikin feed da aka sanya wa hannu | tsawon zaman **bayanan**                  |
| `fetchedAt`   | agogon wannan install            | lokacin da wannan install ya **sauke** su |

Feed da aka sauke mintuna kaɗan da suka wuce zai iya ɗauke da alƙaluman da suka kai makonni, don haka `fetchedAt` kaɗai ba zai iya
gaya wa operator ko overlay ɗin ya fi baseline ɗin da yake kansa sabo ba. Ana
adana su duka a `radar_feed_cache`, ana dawo da su ta `getRadarCatalog().meta`, kuma ana bayar da
rahotonsu dabam-dabam ta `GET /api/radar/status`. Row da aka cache kafin samuwar column ɗin `generated_at`
(migration 163) ana karanta shi a matsayin `null` — abin da ba a sani ba yana ci gaba da kasancewa ba a sani ba maimakon
aro lokacin saukewa. `radar_referrals_cache` yana riƙe da nasa `generated_at` tun
migration 142.

Ƙaramin sigar da ke sama yana kwatanta `version`, ba ɗaya daga cikin kwanakin ba.

Akwai giɓi biyu da suka rage, kuma duka da gangan ne: dashboard har yanzu yana nuna `Lokacin saukewa na ƙarshe` kawai, don haka karanta
ranar build a can yana buƙatar sabon label (da entries na locales guda 41); sannan caches na offers da intel
ba sa adana ranar build kwata-kwata, ko da yake schemas na feed ɗinsu suna ɗauke da ita — saboda haka `GET
/api/radar/status` yana barin filin ga waɗannan biyun maimakon bayar da rahoton `null`
wanda za a karanta a matsayin "ba a sani ba".

### Tabbatar da schema

Ana yin parsing da validation na bytes da aka sauke da `RadarFeedSchema`
(`src/lib/radar/feedSchema.ts`, Zod schema) **bayan** tabbatar da sa hannu. Rashin dacewar
schema yana dawo da `{ status: "invalid_schema" }` kuma ba a taɓa
cache ba. Ana sake validation na bayanan da ke cache a matsayin kariya duk lokacin da aka karanta su
(`getRadarCatalog()`) — row na cache da ya lalace ko aka gyara da hannu yana komawa ga
baseline maimakon a yi amfani da shi.

### Iyakar girman amsa (10 MB)

`syncRadar()` yana tilasta **ƙayyadadden iyakar 10 MB** a kan jikin amsar feed — feed ɗin da aka sanya wa
hannu takardar JSON ce mai girman KB, don haka duk abin da ya wuce wannan yana nuna `RADAR_FEED_URL` da aka saita ba daidai ba ko
mai cutarwa (ko upstream da ke bayar da shara), ba catalog na gaskiya ba.
Ana aiwatar da wannan a matakai biyu:

1. Binciken farko na `Content-Length` yana tsallake karanta jikin gaba ɗaya idan
   header ya riga ya bayyana ƙimar da ta wuce iyakar.
2. Binciken jimillar da ke ƙaruwa yayin karanta jikin yana tilasta iyakar ko da
   `Content-Length` ba ya nan ko ya rage ainihin girman — ba a taɓa
   amincewa da header shi kaɗai ba. Haɗa chunks da aka tara yana kiyaye ainihin
   bytes da ake buƙata domin binciken sa hannun Ed25519 daga baya.

Wuce iyakar yana dawo da `{ status: "too_large" }` kuma ba ya taɓa cache,
yana bin tsarin rashin lalata iri ɗaya da kowace sauran gazawar sync
(`invalid_signature`, `invalid_schema`, `stale`).

## Matakai: `community` da `live`

Tsarin feed yana ɗauke da filin `tier: "community" | "live"`, wanda sabis ɗin feed ke tantancewa **a ɓangaren sabar**
bisa ga buƙatar (kasancewa da ingancin maɓallin mai tallafawa)
— abokin hulɗa ba ya taɓa tantance matakinsa da kansa.

- **`community`** — kundin kyauta wanda ake jinkirta shi da kusan kwanaki 30 daga sabbin
  bayanai. Wannan shi ne abin da buƙatar da ba ta da tantancewa ko mai maɓalli mara inganci ke karɓa.
- **`live`** — kundin mafi sabunta bayanai, wanda ake bayarwa ga buƙatun da ke ɗauke da ingantaccen
  maɓallin mai tallafawa.

**Maɓallin mai tallafawa da ba shi da inganci ko ya ƙare yana koma wa `community` — ba a taɓa ɗaukarsa a matsayin
kuskure ba.** Hanyar aiki tare tana bambance gazawar sa hannu/tsari/siga kawai (duk
ana iya murmurewa daga gare su, kuma babu wanda ke lalata halin da aka adana) daga nasarar `{ status:
"updated", version, tier }`. Babu wata hanyar kuskure ta musamman ga wani mataki da abokin hulɗa yake buƙatar
sarrafawa.

### Matakin da aka bayar yana fitowa daga kanun amsa, ba daga jikin da aka sanya wa hannu ba

Filin `tier` na **jikin** feed da aka sanya wa hannu koyaushe `"live"` ne — sabis ɗin feed yana aika
**kayan aiki biyu da aka sanya wa hannu ga kowace siga**: live yana haɗa kamfen na yanzu, yayin da community
ba ya haɗa su. Ana sanya wa kowane kayan aiki hannu bisa ainihin bytes nasa. Har yanzu jikin ba ya
aiki a matsayin shawarar haƙƙin samun dama; matakin da aka zaɓa a zahiri don buƙata yana cikin
**kanun amsa na `x-omniroute-feed-tier`**, wanda ake tantancewa a ɓangaren sabar daga maɓallin
`Authorization` na buƙatar.

`syncRadar()` (`src/lib/radar/sync.ts::parseServedTierHeader()`) shi ne wuri guda
da ke warware matakin da ya kamata abokin hulɗa ya amince da shi:

1. Tantance `x-omniroute-feed-tier` da `RadarTierSchema` (Zod) — kanun da babu shi, ko
   ƙimar da ba daidai take da `"community"` ko `"live"` ba, ana ɗaukarta a matsayin **ba ta
   nan** (ba a taɓa amincewa da ita kai tsaye cikin cache/UI ba; wannan kuma ya shafi tsofaffin sabobin feed
   da suka riga kanun).
2. Koma ga filin `tier` na jikin da aka sanya wa hannu (koyaushe `"live"`) ne kawai idan mataki na 1
   bai samar da komai ba.
3. Matakin da aka warware shi ne ake adanawa a cache kuma a mayar a matsayin `{ status: "updated",
version, tier }` — wannan shi ne ƙimar da dashboard ke nunawa, ba ɗanyen filin jikin ba.

## Dokokin haɗewar overlay a lokacin karantawa

`applyFeed()` (`src/lib/radar/applyFeed.ts`) yana haɗa feed da aka adana a cache **a saman**
tushen tsaye a **lokacin karantawa**, a cikin `getRadarCatalog()`. Ba a taɓa canza jerin tushen
(`FREE_MODEL_BUDGETS`) — ana lissafa sabon `MergedEntry[]` a kowane
kira.

Dokoki huɗu, bisa jerin fifiko:

1. **Feed ba ya taɓa maye gurbin canjin gida.** Ga kowane fili: idan mai gudanarwa ya
   keɓance wani fili na shigarwa (`localOverrides` map, mai maɓallin `provider:modelId`),
   ana tsallake ƙimar feed na wannan takamaiman filin — ƙimar mai gudanarwa ce ke rinjaye.
2. **`enabled: false` yana kashe shigarwar, tare da bayanin asalinsa.** Shigarwar feed da ke kashe
   wata shigarwa tana saita `enabled: false` da `disabledBy: "radar"` a sakamakon da aka haɗa,
   domin UI ya iya bayyana _dalilin_ da ya sa shigarwa ta sauya daga samuwa zuwa kashewa.
3. **Shigarwar da mai amfani ya ƙara wadda ba ta cikin feed tana ci gaba da kasancewa ba tare da canji ba.** Shigarwar da
   ke cikin tushen kawai (ko aka ƙara ta a gida) kuma ba ta da shigarwar feed da ta dace da ita
   tana wucewa ba tare da canji ba.
4. **Ba a taɓa dawo da shigarwar da aka sanya wa tombstone ba.** Idan mai gudanarwa ya share wata
   shigarwa a sarari (`tombstones` set), sake ƙara wannan `provider:modelId` ɗin da feed ya yi a siga
   ta gaba ba zai dawo da ita ba.

Ana adana filayen da za a iya gyarawa da tombstones a cikin
`radar_local_model_state` (migration `153_radar_local_model_state.sql`). Adaftan DB na jama'a
(`src/lib/db/radar.ts`) yana sauya waɗannan layukan zuwa `localOverrides` map da
`tombstones` set da `applyFeed()` ke amfani da su; `getRadarCatalog()` na production yana loda wannan yanayin
bayan an wuce shingayen flag, cache, da schema. `displayName` da `enabled` ne kawai
mai gudanarwa zai iya gyarawa. Ba za a iya rubuta shaidar provider/model, asalin feed, quota, capabilities, ToS,
da bayanan setup ta wannan hanyar ba.

Dashboard yana samar da ayyukan gida guda huɗu:

- **Gyara** yana canza sunan nuni na gida da yanayin enabled.
- **Sake saita sauye-sauyen gida** yana share dukkan filayen da za a iya gyarawa ba tare da canza tombstone ba.
- **Ɓoye** yana ƙirƙirar tombstone, don kada sabuntawar feed na gaba su sake ƙirƙirar layin.
- **Dawo da shi** yana cire tombstone; duk wani override da aka adana dabam yana ci gaba da aiki.

`enabled: false` na feed yana kasancewa keɓantacciyar kariya: yana rinjayar tsohon
`enabled: true` na gida, yana barin shigarwar da aka haɗa a kashe, kuma yana rubuta `disabledBy: "radar"`.

Wallafar kundin suna amfani da `schemaVersion: 2`. `contextWindow` da kowane ɗaya daga `tools`, `vision`, da
`thinking` suna zaman kansu a matsayin `number | null` / `boolean | null`: `null` yana nufin ba a sani ba, yayin da
`false` ke nufin wata majiyar provider ta hukuma da D16 ya tabbatar ta bayyana a sarari cewa babu wannan damar.
Ba a taɓa ɗaga flags na registry/model-spec na cikin OmniRoute kai tsaye zuwa matsayin hujjojin feed ba. Har yanzu abokin hulɗa
yana karɓar snapshots na v1; saboda tsohon builder ya yi amfani da `false` a matsayin madadin rashin ƙima, ana daidaita `false` na v1
zuwa ba a sani ba, yayin da `true` na v1 ke ci gaba da zama tabbatacciyar hujja. Sigogin schema da ba a sani ba suna gaza cikin tsaro kuma
cache na ƙarshe mai inganci yana ci gaba da samuwa. Kowane model na v2 mai context/capability da ba null ba dole ne ya ɗauki
`metadataEvidenceUrls[]` na HTTPS da ba ya buƙatar credentials; in ba haka ba tantancewar schema za ta gaza kuma ba za a
maye gurbin cache ba. Teburin kundin yana nuna dukkan yanayi ukun a matsayin `✓`, `✕`, da `?`.

### Haɗe-haɗen da ake jagoranta da samun damar MCP

Ƙimomin `familyId` da aka tabbatar suna ci gaba da kasancewa bayan overlay na lokacin karantawa kuma suna tafiyar da pure
module na `buildRadarComboSuggestions()` (`src/lib/radar/comboSuggestions.ts`). Ana ba da shawarar family
ne kawai idan aƙalla providers biyu daban-daban suna da active connections kuma suna samar da ainihin curated model
ID. Disabled models, inactive providers, missing model IDs, singleton families, da ambiguous
alias/prefix matches duk suna gaza cikin tsaro. Shawarwarin suna amfani da dabarar `priority` da ake da ita, suna jera
mafi girman recurring monthly budget da farko; UI yana ƙirƙirar su ne kawai ta hanyar `POST /api/combos`.

UI mai jagora yana a `/dashboard/radar/combos`. Yana karanta kawai maƙurar
`GET /api/radar/catalog` da `GET /api/combos/builder/options` na cikin gida. Ba ya taɓa tayar da daidaitawar Radar,
karanta bayanan shaidar mai samarwa, ko rubuta kai tsaye zuwa ma'ajiyar bayanan combo.

Abokan cinikin MCP za su iya karanta wannan tsinkayar ta cikin gida da `omniroute_radar_catalog` (`read:radar`). Ana
tantance matatun `provider`, `familyId`, da `enabledOnly` na zaɓi bayan karatun
`GET /api/radar/catalog` guda ɗaya na cikin gida. Fitowarsa mai iyaka ta ƙunshi metadata na kasida tare da mai samarwa/samfuri,
sunan nuni, `familyId`, ƙayyadadden kaso, iyawa, halin kunnawa, asali, da `disabledBy`; ba a taɓa dawo da URL na saiti,
matakai, haɗe-haɗe, adiresoshin imel, maɓallai, da bayanan tura abokan ciniki ba. Wannan kayan aiki
na karantawa kawai ne kuma ba ya taɓa kiran `/api/radar/sync`.

### Alamomin asali

Kowane shigarwar da aka haɗe yana ɗauke da filin `origin` wanda UI ke nunawa a matsayin lamba:

- `"baseline"` — bai canza daga tsayayyen kundin sakin ba.
- `"radar"` — an sabunta fili ɗaya ko fiye ta hanyar ciyarwar bayanai.
- `"local"` — mai gudanarwa yana da aƙalla sauyin cikin gida guda ɗaya a wannan shigarwar (sauye-sauyen
  cikin gida koyaushe suna rinjayar ciyarwar bayanai bisa ƙa'ida ta 1, ko da menene ciyarwar ta ce).

`src/lib/radar/sync.ts` (kundin), `src/lib/radar/referralsSync.ts` (turawa), da
`src/lib/radar/offersSync.ts` (tayi) tare da `src/lib/radar/intelSync.ts` (Intel); dukkansu suna aiki

`buildErrorBody()`/`sanitizeErrorMessage()` bisa ga ƙa'idar tsabtace kurakurai ta dukkan rumbun
(`docs/security/ERROR_SANITIZATION.md`).

### Tantancewa

Dukkan maƙarƙashiyar Radar suna buƙatar tantancewa ta hanyar `isAuthenticated()`
(`src/shared/utils/apiAuth.ts`) — kuki na zaman dashboard ko maɓallin API mai izinin gudanarwa,
wato kariya iri ɗaya da ke kare sauran `/api/settings/*`. Gwajin `404` na tuta a kashe
koyaushe yana gudana **kafin** gwajin tantancewa, don haka shigarwar da aka kashe `RADAR_ENABLED`
za ta kasance daidai har zuwa matakin byte (ba za a nemi tantancewa kawai don gano cewa hanyar ba ta wanzu ba);
da zarar an kunna tutar, buƙatar da ba a tantance ba za ta sami `401` kafin duk wani karatu ko
rubutu a DB. `GET /api/radar/settings` ba ya taɓa dawo da ainihin maɓallin mai tallafawa ba tare da la'akari da
yanayin tantancewa ba — yana dawo da sigar da aka ɓoye kawai da ƙimar boolean ta `hasSupporterKey`.

## Tayoyin masu tallafawa

Tayoyi suna amfani da nasu artifact mai sa hannu, `GET /v1/offers/latest`, kuma ba sa taɓa amfani da cache na kundin bayanai ko na turawa tare. Endpoint na uwar garken yana buƙatar ingantaccen maɓallin Bearer na mai tallafawa mai aiki; babu madadin al'umma. Saboda haka `syncRadarOffers()` yana tsayawa kafin haɗa hanyar sadarwa idan an kashe alamar fasalin, mai gudanarwa bai amince ya shiga ba, ko kuma ba a saita maɓallin mai tallafawa ba.

Bayan GET mai nasara, abokin ciniki yana tantance sa hannun Ed25519 bisa ainihin bytes na amsar, yana inganta `RadarOffersFeedSchema`, yana buƙatar duka jikin da aka sanya wa hannu da header na `x-omniroute-feed-tier` su ce `live`, yana tilasta sabon siga mai ɗigo wanda ya fi na baya, sannan ne kawai yake maye gurbin `radar_offers_cache` gaba ɗaya ta atomatik (migration `144_radar_offers_cache.sql`). Iyakar 10 MB ta header-da-stream da sauran feeds suke amfani da ita ita ma tana aiki. Gazawar sa hannu, schema, tier, replay, girma, HTTP, da hanyar sadarwa duk suna kiyaye cache na ƙarshe da aka tabbatar.

Tsarin tayi na rufaffen yana goyon bayan nau'ikan fa'ida uku da za a iya kwatantawa: kaso a basis points, credit a ƙananan raka'o'in kuɗi, ko kwanakin gwaji. Dole ne tayin abokin hulɗa ya ƙunshi ma'aunin jama'a na nau'i ɗaya, kuma fa'idarsa dole ne ta fi shi sosai; tayoyin hukuma ba su da ma'aunin abokin hulɗa. Dole ne URLs su kasance na HTTPS marasa bayanan shiga. `getRadarOffers()` yana sake inganta payload ɗin da ke cache don kariya kuma yana tace shigarwar da wa'adinsu ya ƙare a kowane karatun cikin gida; `/dashboard/radar/offers` yana sake tace wa'adin ƙarewa kafin nunawa, yana amfani da rubutun Fotigal idan yana samuwa tare da komawa ga Turanci idan babu, kuma yana yi wa tayoyin abokan hulɗa lakabi a sarari.

Burauzar tana kiran routes na cikin gida kawai: tana karanta snapshot na saituna da aka ɓoye wani ɓangare, tana neman `POST /api/radar/offers/sync` don sabuntawa a ɓangaren uwar garken, sannan tana karanta `GET /api/radar/offers`. Idan babu maɓalli, tana nuna hanyoyin haɗin mai ba da gudummawa/tallafi da suke akwai maimakon ƙoƙarin neman feed. Hanyoyin haɗin tayi na waje suna buɗewa a sabon tab tare da `noopener noreferrer`. Ba a samar da wani MCP tool na `radar_offers` a wannan release ba.

## Radar Intel, bajin mai tallafawa, da CLI

Intel artifact ne mai sa hannu a `GET /v1/intel/latest`. Rufaffen `RadarIntelFeedSchema` yana karɓar matsayoyin ELO mallakar Radar kawai, waɗanda mai kula na sirri ya samo daga tabbatattun kwatance, da bambance-bambancen shekaru/adadi na kundin bayanai bisa gaskiya waɗanda aka samo daga snapshots na kundin bayanai masu sa hannu. An ƙayyade hanyar aiki da ƙimar farko ta 1000 da K=32. Matsayi mara komai yana da inganci idan ba a tabbatar da wani kwatance ba; abokin ciniki ba ya taɓa ƙirƙirar ɗaya da kansa.

`syncRadarIntel()` yana amfani da Bearer na ɓangaren uwar garken iri ɗaya, timeout na daƙiƙa 30, iyakar streamed ta 10 MiB, tabbatar da Ed25519 kan ainihin bytes, tsauraran schema, buƙatar `live` a jiki/header, mafi ƙarancin siga, da kiyaye cache na ƙarshe mai kyau kamar tayoyi. Bayan an adana tabbataccen snapshot na live, abokin ciniki yana samar da `radar:<sha256(supporter key)>`, yana adana wannan ainihi na hanya-ɗaya kawai, kuma yana fitar da keɓaɓɓen taron ganewa na `radar_supporter`. Bajinsa na `radar-supporter` ba ya haifar da kwafi kuma yana bayar da sifilin XP; ba ya taɓa sabunta leaderboards ko sake amfani da `token_share`. `/dashboard/radar/intel` yana nuna bajin ne kawai daga tabbatattun metadata na cache na cikin gida.

CLI yana samar da `omniroute radar status` da `omniroute radar sync`. Dukansu suna sadarwa ne kawai da OmniRoute API na cikin gida. `status` yana yin `GET /api/radar/status` na karatu kawai; `sync` yana aika `POST /api/radar/sync-all` guda ɗaya kuma yana buga sakamako ga kowane feed. Babu ɗayan commands ɗin da ke karantawa, karɓa, ko buga maɓallin mai tallafawa, kuma babu wanda ke tuntuɓar sabis na Radar kai tsaye.

## Hanyoyin tura masu amfani (kiredit kyauta)

Ana samar da hanyoyin tura masu amfani daga wani rafi mai **cin gashin kansa, kuma koyaushe na zamani** —
`GET /v1/referrals/latest` — wanda ya bambanta da rafin kundin. An yi haka ne da gangan: rafin
kundin a matakin al'umma hoto ne na wani lokaci da zai iya kai wa kwanaki 30 da tsufa, don haka
hanyar tura masu amfani da aka ciro daga gare shi kan makara da adadin lokaci ɗaya idan aka kwatanta da
ainihin jerin hanyoyin uwar garken (sabuwar hanyar tura masu amfani da aka ƙara ba za ta isa ga
mai amfani na kyauta/al'umma ba har tsawon wata guda). Rafin hanyoyin tura masu amfani yana kawar da
wannan jinkiri ta hanyar aiki tare da kansa a tazara mafi gajarta.

```ts
// Jikin amsar GET /v1/referrals/latest (an sanya masa hannu da Ed25519, maɓallin da aka kafa ɗaya da na
// rafin kundin):
{
  feed: "omniroute-radar-referrals",
  schemaVersion: 1,
  generatedAt: string,           // ISO — mai tabbataccen sakamako: max(updatedAt) a duk hanyoyin
                                  // tura masu amfani, don haka buƙatu biyu iri ɗaya su samar da ainihin
                                  // bytes/sa hannu iri ɗaya
  referrals: {
    fixed: RadarReferral[],      // yana nan a KOWANE mataki, har da mara-tantancewa/al'umma
    campaigns: RadarReferral[],  // ana cika shi ne kawai da ingantaccen maɓallin Bearer na kai-tsaye
                                  // (mai tallafawa); buƙatun mara-tantancewa/maɓalli da ya ƙare suna samun []
  },
}
// RadarReferral = { provider, url, kind: "fixo" | "campanha", validUntil,
//                    requiredAction, isDefault }
```

Ba kamar rafin kundin ba, wannan jikin ba ya ɗauke da filin `tier` kwata-kwata — uwar garken ce ke
yanke shawarar abin da za a haɗa a kowace buƙata bisa ga maɓallin `Authorization`, don haka kan amsar
`x-omniroute-feed-tier` ne KAWAI tushen matakin da aka samar
(`referralsSync.ts::syncRadarReferrals`); idan kan ba ya nan/ba a gane shi ba, ana saukar da shi zuwa
`"community"`, wato zato mafi ƙarancin gata. `RadarReferralsFeedSchema`
(`src/lib/radar/referralsFeedSchema.ts`) yana tantance dukkan jikin, yana sake amfani da
`RadarReferralSchema` ɗaya na kowane hanyar tura masu amfani da aka fitar daga `feedSchema.ts` domin
duka rafukan su tantance kowace hanyar tura masu amfani ta hanya iri ɗaya. Dole ne kowane
`RadarReferral.url` ya kasance `https://` — url na `http://` ba zai wuce tantance tsarin ba.

An riƙe TSOHON filin `referrals` da ke cikin kundin a `RadarFeedSchema` (`feedSchema.ts`) don
dacewa da tsofaffin nau'ikan rafukan kundin da aka riga aka adana a ma'ajiyar wucin gadi, amma
`getRadarReferrals()` ba ya karanta shi yanzu — duba [Mai isa](#accessor) a ƙasa.

### Aiki tare

`syncRadarReferrals()` (`src/lib/radar/referralsSync.ts`) shi ne KAƊAI sashe da ke
amfani da hanyar sadarwa don hanyoyin tura masu amfani, yana kwaikwayon ƙa'idar `syncRadar()` daidai:
tuta a kashe → `disabled`; yarda da shiga ƙarya → `opt_out`; yana sauke
`${RADAR_FEED_URL}/v1/referrals/latest` (tare da sauye-sauyen fork na `RADAR_FEED_URL`/
`RADAR_FEED_PUBKEY` iri ɗaya da na kundin), yana tabbatar da sa hannun Ed25519 a kan ainihin bytes
na amsar (`verifyFeedBytes`), yana tantancewa da `RadarReferralsFeedSchema`, sannan yana adanawa
cikin teburin `radar_referrals_cache` (ƙaura `142_radar_referrals_cache.sql`) — tebur da ya
keɓanta gaba ɗaya daga `radar_feed_cache` na kundin. Iyakar amsa ta 10 MB da mafi ƙarancin
`generatedAt` suna ƙin rafin shigowa da ya girmi wanda ke ma'ajiyar wucin gadi, suna karewa daga
sake kunna tsohon abin da aka sanya wa hannu. Ana karɓar hatimin lokaci daidai: uwar garken da gangan
tana bai wa nau'ikan hanyoyin tura masu amfani na al'umma da na kai-tsaye `generatedAt` iri ɗaya mai
tabbataccen sakamako, don haka payload da aka sanya wa hannu da matakin da aka samar za su iya
canjawa bayan canjin maɓallin mai tallafawa ba tare da saitin hanyoyin asali ya canja ba. Ba ya taɓa
jefa kuskure — koyaushe yana mayar da abin matsayin aiki; kurakurai ba sa ɗauke da stack trace a
cikin `reason`.

Abubuwa biyu ne ke sa ma'ajiyar wucin gadi ta hanyoyin tura masu amfani ta kasance a shirye, kuma
dukansu sun keɓanta daga tazarar awa 24 ta kundin:

- **Aiki tare lokacin karantawa** — `GET /api/radar/referrals` da kansa yana kiran
  `syncRadarReferrals()` kai tsaye duk lokacin da ma'ajiyar wucin gadi ba ta nan ko ta girmi
  `REFERRALS_STALE_MS` (awa 1, `shouldSyncReferralsOnRead()`), kafin samar da amsar. Wannan ne ke
  sa tabbatattun hanyoyin su kasance "koyaushe na zamani" a lodin dashboard na gaba nan take,
  ba tare da jiran wani mai ƙidayar lokaci na baya ba.
- **Aiki tare na gefe daga mai tsara lokaci** — `radarSchedulerTick()` (`scheduler.ts`) da kansa
  yana tantance tsufar hanyoyin tura masu amfani a tick na awa-awa ɗaya da ake amfani da shi don
  kundin, yana kiran `syncRadarReferrals()` idan lokaci ya yi. Wannan yana gudana ko da kuwa
  lokacin sabunta kundin bai yi ba a wannan tick ɗin, kuma ba ya taɓa shafar tsarin
  `RadarTickResult` (tasirin gefe na gwargwadon hali kawai, ana danne kuskure).

### Mai isa

`src/lib/radar/index.ts` yana fitar da masu isa biyu na karantawa kawai, kuma dukansu ba sa taɓa
jefa kuskure (ƙa'idar kariya iri ɗaya da `getRadarCatalog()` — tuta a kashe, babu ma'ajiyar wucin
gadi, ko gurɓataccen payload da aka adana duk suna komawa ga tsari marar komai maimakon kuskure):

- `getRadarReferrals()` → `{ fixed: RadarReferral[], campaigns: RadarReferral[] }`,
  yana karantawa daga `radar_referrals_cache` (ta hanyar `getRadarReferralsCache()`) kuma yana
  tantancewa ta `RadarReferralsFeedSchema` — **ba** ma'ajiyar wucin gadi ta kundin ba.
- `getDefaultReferralFor(provider)` → hanyar tura masu amfani ta `fixed` mai `isDefault: true`
  ga wannan mai samarwa, ko `null`. Yana duba `fixed` kawai — ba a taɓa amfani da kamfen a matsayin
  hanyar "tsohuwa" ta mai samarwa.

Ainihin ƙa'idar "wace hanyar tura masu amfani ce tsohuwa ga mai samarwa" tana cikin
`findDefaultReferral()` (`src/lib/radar/referrals.ts`), ƙaramin aiki tsantsa wanda ba shi da
**shigo da DB** — yana da aminci a shigo da shi cikin wani sashe na `"use client"`.
`getRadarReferrals`/`getDefaultReferralFor` (a cikin `index.ts`) suna shigo da `@/lib/db/radar`
saboda haka suna kasancewa na uwar garke kawai; dashboard na masu samarwa yana shigo da
`referrals.ts` kai tsaye maimakon `index.ts` (duba ƙasa) don kauce wa haɗa `better-sqlite3`
cikin browser.

### `GET /api/radar/referrals`

Yana bin ainihin jerin ƙofofin da kowace hanyar Radar ke bi: idan `RADAR_ENABLED` a kashe yake →
`404` (ana fara dubawa, yanayin rashin sauyi mai daidaiton byte); wanda bai tabbatar da shaidarsa ba → `401`; in ba haka ba
yana tayar da daidaitawa-lokacin-karantawa (duba sama) idan bayanan sun tsufa, sannan `200` tare da
`{ fixed, campaigns, tier }` — `tier` yana zuwa kai tsaye daga layin cache (wanda mai yiwuwa an sabunta shi yanzu)
kuma don bayar da bayani kawai yake (shi ne ke sarrafa saƙon tallatawar UI mai laushi da ke ƙasa). Ba ya taɓa
wakiltar uwar garken feed kai tsaye — tushen hanyar kansa ba ya ƙunshe da kiran `fetch(`;
cibiyar sadarwa tana faruwa ne kawai a cikin `syncRadarReferrals()`, daidai da ƙa'idar
amfani da cache na gida kawai ta `/api/radar/catalog`.

### UI na Dashboard — shafin "Kiredit kyauta" a `/dashboard/radar`

Yana sake amfani da shafin Radar da yake akwai (`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/radar/page.tsx`) a matsayin
shafi na biyu maimakon sabuwar hanya — wannan yana rage faɗin routing/i18n ga wata siffa da take
bambancin bayanan da shafin ya riga ya ɗebo. Da zarar an shiga, sandar shafuka tana bayar da
**Katalog** (teburin da yake akwai) da **Kiredit kyauta**:

- Ana haɗa tsayayyun hanyoyin haɗi bisa mai bayarwa, kowanne yana nuna `requiredAction` (idan yana nan)
  da maɓallin `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"` zuwa URL na referral.
- Kamfen-kamfen suna nuna iri ɗaya, tare da `validUntil` idan yana nan.
- Lokacin da `campaigns` babu komai **kuma** matakin da aka bayar shi ne `community`, UI yana nuna
  ɗan taƙaitaccen bayanin ƙarfafa saye ("kamfen na ɗan lokaci ƙarin fa'ida ne ga masu tallafawa") — wannan **ba ya taɓa**
  ɓoye ko kulle jerin tsayayyun hanyoyin haɗi, wanda yake ci gaba da kasancewa cikakke ga kowane mataki. Wannan
  saƙon ƙarfafa saye mai laushi ne kawai, ba wani shinge ba.

### Hanyar haɗin referral a sunan mai bayarwa (dashboard na masu bayarwa)

`ProviderPageHeader` (`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/providers/[id]/components/`)
ya riga yana haɗa sunan mai bayarwa zuwa `providerInfo.website` idan yana nan, tare da misali guda
na hanyar haɗi mai samar da kuɗi: bayanin hanyar haɗin abokin hulɗa na Kimi (Moonshot AI)
(maɓallin i18n na `providers.kimiPartnerLinkNote`). D28 yana sake amfani da ainihin wannan tsarin
bayani marar jan hankali don tsoffin referral na Radar maimakon ƙirƙirar sabon maɓalli.

Sassauƙan haɗin kai, da gangan:

- `resolveProviderHeaderLink()` (`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/providers/providerPageUtils.ts`)
  aikin **pure** ne — `(staticWebsite, referralUrl) => { website, isReferralLink }`
  — ba tare da dogaro da `@/lib/radar` ko `@/lib/db/*` ba. `providerPageUtils.ts` gaba ɗaya
  yana ci gaba da zama ba tare da waɗannan import ɗin ba (wanda
  `tests/unit/provider-header-referral-link.test.ts` ya tabbatar).
- `ProviderDetailPageClient.tsx` (wani bangaren `"use client"`) shi ne wuri guda da aka yarda
  ya ɗebo bayanan Radar — ta hanyar `fetch("/api/radar/referrals")`, daidai da tsarin hanyar-gida
  da shafin dashboard na Radar kansa yake amfani da shi — kuma yana ƙididdige tsohon referral
  a ɓangaren client ta amfani da `findDefaultReferral()` daga `src/lib/radar/referrals.ts` wanda ba ya dogaro da DB.
- Idan `RADAR_ENABLED` a kashe yake, ɗebowar tana dawo da 404, `referralUrl` yana ci gaba da zama `null`, sannan
  `resolveProviderHeaderLink()` yana mayar da tsayayyen `website` na katalog ba tare da canji ba —
  shafin mai bayarwa yana kasancewa daidai da na kafin samuwar wannan siffa har zuwa matakin byte. Haka sakamakon yake
  idan har yanzu babu cache ko kuma babu tsohon referral ga wannan takamaiman mai bayarwa.
- Idan tsohon referral ya dace, `ProviderPageHeader` yana karɓar `isReferralLink`
  kuma yana nuna irin bayanin/tooltip marar jan hankali na hanyar haɗin abokin hulɗar Kimi (yana sake amfani da
  maɓallin `providers.kimiPartnerLinkNote`) — ba ya taɓa ƙirƙirar sabon tsarin gani na daban.

## Yadda ake ɗaukar nauyin feed da kanka

Wani reshe ko mai ɗaukar nauyin sabis da kansa wanda yake son cikakken iko a kan kundin zai iya gudanar da nasa sabis na feed ba tare da taɓa lambar abokin hulɗa ba:

1. Samar da endpoint na `GET /v1/catalog/latest` wanda yake mayar da jikin JSON da ya cika
   `RadarFeedSchema` (`src/lib/radar/feedSchema.ts`) — a matakin sama akwai `feed:
"omniroute-radar"`, `schemaVersion: 2`, `version`, `tier`, `providers`, `models`,
   `quirks`, da `totals`. Bi `x-omniroute-radar-schema: 2`; sabar da ta dace da lokacin sauyawa
   ya kamata ta mayar da buƙatun da ba su ɗauke da shi zuwa wani kayan v1 da aka sanya wa hannu daban.
2. Sanya wa ainihin bytes na amsar hannu da maɓallan Ed25519 guda biyu, sannan a mayar da
   sa hannun base64 a cikin response header na `x-omniroute-feed-signature`.
3. Saita `RADAR_FEED_URL` zuwa sabon URL na asali, sannan `RADAR_FEED_PUBKEY` zuwa
   public key ɗin da ya dace (base64-DER SPKI ko PEM) — duba
   [manunin env var](../reference/ENVIRONMENT.md#27-radar-feed-self-hosting).
4. Kunna `RADAR_ENABLED`, sannan ka amince ta hanyar `POST /api/radar/settings`
   (`{ optIn: true }`).

Ba a buƙatar wasu sauye-sauyen lamba — `verifyFeedBytes()` yana gano override ɗin
ta atomatik (`getFeedPublicKeys()` a cikin `src/lib/radar/pinnedKeys.ts`), kuma kwatanta
version, tabbatar da schema, da ƙa’idojin haɗawa suna aiki iri ɗaya ga feed da aka ɗauki
nauyinsa da kai.

Hanyoyin referral (duba [Hanyoyin referral (kuɗaɗen amfani na kyauta)](#referral-links-free-credits)
a sama) wani kayan daban ne na zaɓi: reshen da yake samar da `/v1/catalog/latest` kawai
har yanzu yana aiki cikakke — `syncRadarReferrals()` yana sauka zuwa `{ status: "error" }` idan ya sami `404`
daga `/v1/referrals/latest`, kuma cache kawai yana ci gaba da zama babu komai, don haka
`GET /api/radar/referrals` yana ci gaba da mayar da `{ fixed: [], campaigns: [], tier: null }`
maimakon hana sauran shafin aiki. Don kuma samar da hanyoyin referral, samar da
`GET /v1/referrals/latest` wanda ya cika `RadarReferralsFeedSchema`
(`src/lib/radar/referralsFeedSchema.ts`), sannan a sanya masa hannu da maɓallan Ed25519 guda biyu
da aka yi amfani da su wajen feed na kundin.

Tayin masu goyon baya wani kayan zaɓi ne daban. Don samar da su, aiwatar da
`GET /v1/offers/latest` tare da rufaffen `RadarOffersFeedSchema`
(`src/lib/radar/offersFeedSchema.ts`), buƙaci live entitlement mai aiki, mayar da
`x-omniroute-feed-tier: live`, sannan a sanya wa ainihin bytes hannu da wannan maɓalli. Reshen da bai samar da wannan
endpoint ba zai bar halayen kundi/referrals yadda suke; sabunta tayi zai gaza ba tare da lalata komai ba, kuma
cache na tayin gida na ƙarshe da aka tabbatar zai ci gaba da kasancewa.

Intel ma na zaɓi ne ta wannan hanya. Mai ɗaukar nauyin sabis da kansa zai iya samar da `GET /v1/intel/latest` ta amfani da
`RadarIntelFeedSchema` (`src/lib/radar/intelFeedSchema.ts`), ya buƙaci live entitlement mai aiki, ya mayar da
`x-omniroute-feed-tier: live`, sannan ya sanya wa ainihin bytes hannu da shared Ed25519 key. Rashin samar da
endpoint ɗin zai bar kundi, referrals, da tayi yadda suke; sabunta Intel zai adana duk wani local snapshot na ƙarshe
da aka tabbatar.
