AgentBridge shi ne wakilin MITM (Man-in-the-Middle) na OmniRoute wanda ke katse zirga-zirgar HTTPS daga wakilan AI na IDE sannan ya sake tura ta ta injin sarrafa hanyoyi na bai ɗaya na OmniRoute. Yana tallafa wa **wakilan IDE guda 10** — Antigravity, Kiro, GitHub Copilot, GHE Copilot, OpenAI Codex, Cursor, Zed, Claude Code, Open Code, da Trae (ana bincike) — wanda hakan ya sa OmniRoute ya zama wakilin MITM mai mafi faɗin tallafi ga mataimakan rubuta lambar AI a kasuwa.

**Wurin dashboard:** `/dashboard/tools/agent-bridge`
**Rukunin sidebar:** Tools (bayan Cloud Agents)
**Duba kuma:** [`TRAFFIC_INSPECTOR.md`](./TRAFFIC_INSPECTOR.md) — sa ido kan duk zirga-zirgar da aka katse a ainihin lokaci; `docs/security/MITM-TPROXY-DECRYPT.md` (git; ba a haɗa shi cikin `/docs` ba) — yanayin kama zirga-zirga tare da warware ɓoyewa a bayyane na Linux TPROXY wanda hanyar `/api/tools/agent-bridge/tproxy` ke sarrafawa.

## §1 Bayani na Gabaɗaya

### Mene ne AgentBridge?

Lokacin da wakilin IDE (misali, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Claude Code) ya yi kiran API, yana haɗuwa kai tsaye da mai samar da AI na sama (OpenAI, Anthropic, da sauransu). AgentBridge yana katse wannan haɗin a bayyane a matakin TLS — ba tare da buƙatar wani canjin saitin wakili ba — sannan ya sake rubuta buƙatar ta bi ta OmniRoute.

Wannan yana nufin za ka iya:

- **Sake tura kowane wakili zuwa kowane mai samarwa**: Copilot yana magana da OpenAI? Sake tura shi zuwa Anthropic Claude, Gemini, ko ɗaya daga cikin masu samarwa guda 352 na OmniRoute.
- **Aiwatar da taswirar samfura**: `gemini-3-flash` → `claude-sonnet-4.7` a bayyane a matakin handler.
- **Lura da duk zirga-zirgar wakilai**: ana wallafa kowace buƙatar da aka katse zuwa [Traffic Inspector](./TRAFFIC_INSPECTOR.md).
- **Aiwatar da juriyar OmniRoute**: haɗaɗɗen sarrafa hanyoyi, circuit breakers, hanyoyin madadin, da bin diddigin kuɗi suna aiki ga zirga-zirgar wakilan IDE ma.

## §2 Tsarin Gine-gine

### 2.1 Bayanin sassa gaba ɗaya

```
Wakilin IDE (VS Code / Cursor / da sauransu)
    │  HTTPS (tashar 443)
    ▼
/etc/hosts — 127.0.0.1 api.githubcopilot.com   ← karkatar da DNS
    │
    ▼
src/mitm/server.cjs  (tashar 443, tsarin ɗa na CJS)
    │  yana gano manufa ta hanyar SNI na taken Host
    │  yana ƙirƙirar takardar shaidar TLS ga kowane SNI wadda AgentBridge CA ya sanya wa hannu
    ├── Ya dace da jerin kaucewa? → wucewar TCP kai tsaye (ba tare da warware ɓoyewa ba)
    ├── Ya dace da manufa? → fetch → na'ura mai ba da hanya ta OmniRoute (tashar 20128)
    │       └── handler.intercept() — TypeScript
    │               ├── maskSecrets() a jikin buƙata/takenta
    │               ├── TrafficBuffer.push() — yana turawa zuwa Traffic Inspector
    │               └── fetchRouter() → /v1/chat/completions
    └── Babu dacewa? → wucewar TCP kai tsaye (ba tare da warware ɓoyewa ba)
```

### 2.2 Sabar MITM (`src/mitm/server.cjs`)

Babbar sabar MITM tana gudana a matsayin tsarin ɗa na Node.js CJS (don kauce wa sake rubuta rumbun lambar CJS da ake da shi). Tana:

- Sauraro a tashar 443 (yana buƙatar izini ko `authbind`/`setcap`)
- Karɓar ramukan CONNECT daga OS (ta hanyar karkatarwar DNS na `/etc/hosts`)
- Ƙirƙirar takardun shaidar TLS ga kowane SNI waɗanda AgentBridge CA (`DATA_DIR/mitm/ca.crt`) ya sanya wa hannu
- Gano wakilin da aka nufa ta taken Host ta hanyar rajistar `targets/index.ts`
- Tura aiki zuwa matakin mai sarrafa TypeScript ta HTTP zuwa `http://127.0.0.1:20128`

Ana loda `TARGET_HOSTS` daga `DATA_DIR/mitm/targets.json` (wanda `targets/index.ts` ke rubutawa yayin farawa), yana ba da damar sabuntawa kai tsaye ba tare da sake kunna sabar CJS ba.

> **Samfurin tushen CA (#6684).** Bayanin takardar shaidar kowane SNI da CA ya sanya wa hannu da ke sama
> shi ne samfurin tushen CA mai ɗorewa da aka ƙara a #6684 (`src/mitm/cert/rootCa.ts` +
> `src/mitm/_internal/rootCaShim.cjs`, yana sake amfani da ƙirƙirar sirrin CA/takardar reshe da aka riga
> aka tabbatar da ita don TPROXY a `src/mitm/tproxy/dynamicCert.ts`) — yana maye gurbin
> tsohuwar takardar reshe guda ɗaya, tsayayyiya, mai sanya wa kanta hannu (`src/mitm/cert/generate.ts`, har yanzu
> an iyakance ta ga rundunonin antigravity kawai) wadda kasancewar fayilolin `server.crt`/`server.key`
> a faifai ke nunawa. **Halin ƙaura**: sabon shigarwa (ba tare da tsohon
> `server.crt` ba) yana samun samfurin tushen CA ta atomatik; shigarwar da ta riga
> ta amince da tsohuwar takardar reshe mai tsayawa za ta ci gaba da amfani da ita har sai mai gudanarwa ya saita
> `MITM_ROOT_CA_ENABLED=true` kuma ya sake kunna bridge ɗin (`src/mitm/cert/migration.ts`
> ita ce tsantsar aikin yanke shawara — amintacciyar MITM CA da za ta iya sanya hannu kan takardar reshe ga
> **kowace** runduna tana da ƙarfi sosai fiye da tsohuwar takardar reshe mai SAN tsayayye, don haka
> sauyin ba ya taɓa faruwa a ɓoye ga shigarwar da aka riga aka amince da ita). Ana shigar da takardar shaidar CA
> cikin gurbin ma'ajiyar amana na `omniroute-mitm.crt` da tsohuwar takardar reshen ta yi amfani da shi
> (`cert/install.ts::installCaCert`) — ba a buƙatar share amana biyu.

### 2.3 Tushen mai sarrafawa (`src/mitm/handlers/base.ts`)

Duk masu sarrafa wakilai suna faɗaɗa `MitmHandlerBase`:

```ts
export abstract class MitmHandlerBase {
  abstract readonly agentId: AgentId;

  abstract intercept(
    req: IncomingMessage,
    res: ServerResponse,
    body: Buffer,
    mappedModel: string
  ): Promise<void>;

  // Mataimaka masu kariya: fetchRouter, pipeSSE, hookBufferStart, hookBufferUpdate
}
```

Kowane mai sarrafawa yana kiran `hookBufferStart()` kafin aikin wakilci da `hookBufferUpdate()` idan an kammala. Waɗannan suna tura shigarwar `InterceptedRequest` cikin `globalTrafficBuffer` (duba [Mai Binciken Zirga-zirga](./TRAFFIC_INSPECTOR.md) §4).

### 2.4 Rajistar manufofi (`src/mitm/targets/`)

Kowane wakili yana da fayil ɗin manufa mai bayyana ƙa'idoji:

```ts
// src/mitm/targets/copilot.ts
export const COPILOT_TARGET: MitmTarget = {
  id: "copilot",
  name: "GitHub Copilot",
  hosts: ["api.githubcopilot.com", "copilot-proxy.githubusercontent.com"],
  port: 443,
  endpointPatterns: ["/chat/completions", "/v1/chat/completions"],
  defaultModels: [{ id: "gpt-4o", name: "GPT-4o", alias: "gpt-4o" }],
  handler: () => import("../handlers/copilot"),
  riskNoticeKey: "providers.riskNotice.oauth",
};
```

Rajistar (`targets/index.ts`) tana fitar da `ALL_TARGETS` kuma tana samar da `DATA_DIR/mitm/targets.json` yayin farawa.

### 2.5 Wucewa kai tsaye da jerin kaucewa (`src/mitm/passthrough.ts`)

**Jerin kaucewa** (ana fara dubawa, kuma yana da fifiko kan dacewar manufa):

- Tsarukan tsoho: rundunonin bankuna, `.gov.`, masu samar da OAuth/SSO (Okta, Auth0), da sauransu.
- Tsarukan mai amfani: ana adana su a teburin DB `agent_bridge_bypass`
- Rundunonin da aka kauce wa suna samun ramin TCP na kai tsaye — **ba a taɓa warware ɓoyewar TLS**

**Wucewa kai tsaye ta tsohuwa** (babu dacewar manufa kuma ba ya cikin jerin kaucewa):

- Shi ma yana samun ramin TCP — ba a taɓa katse haɗin kai
- Yana hana AgentBridge dagula zirga-zirgar HTTPS na tsarin gaba ɗaya

Jerin fifikon bayar da hanya:

```
jerin kaucewa → dacewar manufa → wucewa kai tsaye
```

### 2.6 Takardar shaidar CA ta sama (`src/mitm/upstreamTrust.ts`)

Don muhallan cibiyar sadarwar kamfani masu CA na musamman:

```bash
AGENTBRIDGE_UPSTREAM_CA_CERT=/path/to/corporate-ca.pem
```

Idan an saita shi, yana daidaita mai rarrabawa na duniya na `undici` tare da ƙarin takardar shaidar CA, yana bai wa AgentBridge damar isa ga masu samarwa na sama ta cikin wakilan ƙarewar TLS na kamfani.

### 2.7 Ɓoye sirrika (`src/mitm/maskSecrets.ts`)

Ana amfani da na'urar bincike mai zaman kanta da aka gina daga tushe kan jikin buƙatu da taken bayanan shiga
**kafin** su shiga ma'ajiyar Traffic Inspector ko kowane rajista. Tana yin zagaye guda na layi:

- Alamu masu gabatarwar `sk-` / `ak-` / `pk-` (irin na OpenAI/Anthropic)
- Bayanan shiga na RFC 6750 `Authorization: Bearer <token>`, tare da fifikon gabaɗayan alamar
- Dogayen alamu marasa bayyana na gama-gari (haruffa ≥40), ciki har da nau'ikan masu ɗigo da masu cikawa

`sanitizeHeaders()` yana mayar da sunayen da aka riƙe zuwa ƙananan haruffa, yana haɗa ƙimomin jeri ta hanya tabbatacciya, yana cire
jerin hana hop-by-hop/framing na gama-gari (ciki har da tantancewar wakili), yana ɓoye `cookie` da
`set-cookie` gaba ɗaya, sannan yana mika ƙimomin bayanan shiga ga na'urar binciken.

## §3 Saiti

### 3.1 Kunna/kashe sabar MITM

Yi amfani da Katin Sabar AgentBridge da ke `/dashboard/tools/agent-bridge`:

| Aiki                          | Bayani                                                                                       |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Kunna Saba                    | Yana ƙaddamar da `src/mitm/server.cjs` a tashar 443                                          |
| Kashe Saba                    | Yana rufe aikin ɗan-tsari cikin tsari mai kyau                                               |
| Sake Kunna Saba               | Kashe + kunna (yana ɗaukar sauye-sauyen manufa)                                              |
| Amince da Takardar Shaida     | Yana shigar da `DATA_DIR/mitm/ca.crt` cikin ma'ajiyar amincewar OS                           |
| Sauke Takardar Shaida         | Yana sauke `ca.crt` domin shigarwa da hannu                                                  |
| Sake Samar da Takardar Shaida | Yana ƙirƙirar sabon maɓallan CA (duk takardun shaidar wakilai da ake da su za su daina aiki) |

### 3.2 Amince da takardar shaida

Dole ne OS ya amince da takardar shaidar CA ta AgentBridge kafin IDE su karɓi haɗin MITM.

**Linux (NSS — Chrome/Firefox):**

```bash
certutil -A -d sql:$HOME/.pki/nssdb -n "OmniRoute AgentBridge" -t CT,, -i ~/.omniroute/mitm/ca.crt
```

**macOS (Keychain):**

```bash
sudo security add-trusted-cert -d -r trustRoot \
  -k /Library/Keychains/System.keychain ~/.omniroute/mitm/ca.crt
```

**Windows (certmgr):**

```powershell
certutil -addstore -f Root $env:USERPROFILE\.omniroute\mitm\ca.crt
```

Ko kuma yi amfani da maɓallin "Amince da Takardar Shaida" a dashboard (yana gudanar da umarnin da ya dace da OS ɗinka, tare da neman izinin sudo idan ana buƙata).

#### IDE masu tushen Electron suna yin watsi da ma'ajiyar amincewar OS (`NODE_EXTRA_CA_CERTS`)

Wasu IDE — musamman **Antigravity IDE**, da sauran manhajojin da aka samo daga Electron / VS Code — suna zuwa
da nasu yanayin gudanar da Node.js wanda **ba ya duba ma'ajiyar amincewar OS** don haɗin
`fetch`/HTTPS masu fita. Amincewa da CA a matakin OS/NSS ya isa ga **backend** na asali na IDE
(misali, sabar harshen Go, wadda ke amfani da tarin CA na OS), amma **frontend na Electron** zai
ci gaba da kasa yin TLS — wannan yana bayyana kamar an _fitar da mai amfani_ daga manhajar ko ana nuna
_"kuskuren haɗi"_ duk da cewa log ɗin MITM yana nuna kiran bootstrap na backend suna dawo da `200`. Ana
buƙatar matakai biyu, kuma dukansu suna da muhimmanci:

1. Nuna wa yanayin gudanarwar inda CA yake kai tsaye:
   ```bash
   export NODE_EXTRA_CA_CERTS=/path/to/omniroute-agentbridge-ca.crt
   ```
2. **Ƙaddamar da IDE daga wannan shell ɗin.** Kunna shi daga alamar desktop / Dock / menu na Start
   **ba ya** gado da exports na shell, kuma `~/.config/environment.d/*.conf` yana aiki ne kawai bayan
   sabon shiga na hoto. Ka rufe IDE gaba ɗaya da farko — makullin singleton na Electron yana nufin
   ƙaddamarwa ta biyu za ta mayar da hankali ne kawai ga tsarin da yake gudana, kuma za a yi watsi da sabon yanayin.

Matakin amincewar OS + NSS da ke sama har yanzu ya zama dole (tarin hanyar sadarwar Chromium da wasu hanyoyin
tantancewa ke amfani da shi yana karanta ma'ajiyar NSS ta kowane mai amfani, kuma yana da nasa tsayayyun pins na
`*.googleapis.com` waɗanda CA da aka amince da shi a gida ke maye gurbinsu). `NODE_EXTRA_CA_CERTS` yana
rufe hanyar `fetch` ta Node a samansa.

### 3.3 Tura DNS

Ga kowane wakili da kake son kutsewa, dole ne host ɗin API ɗinsa ya warware zuwa `127.0.0.1`. AgentBridge yana sarrafa shigarwar `/etc/hosts` kai tsaye lokacin da ka kunna ko kashe DNS na wani wakili a cikin Mayen Saiti.

Misalin shigarwar `/etc/hosts` don GitHub Copilot:

```
127.0.0.1 api.githubcopilot.com
127.0.0.1 copilot-proxy.githubusercontent.com
```

### 3.4 Daidaita samfurori

Yi amfani da Teburin Daidaita Samfuri a cikin kowane katin wakili don ayyana daidaitawar tushe → manufa:

| Samfurin tushe (na asalin wakili) | Samfurin manufa (OmniRoute) |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| `gpt-4o`                          | `claude-sonnet-4.7`         |
| `*` (wildcard)                    | `claude-haiku-4.7`          |

Wildcard `*` yana daidaita duk wani samfurin da ba a gane ba zuwa manufa da aka ayyana. Ana adana shi a teburin `agent_bridge_mappings`.

> **Shawara — gano ainihin ID na samfuran wakilin.** IDE na iya aika sunayen samfura waɗanda suka bambanta da
> alamomin UI ɗinsa kuma suke canzawa tsakanin manyan nau'uka. Misali **Antigravity 2** yana aika
> `gemini-3.1-pro-low`, `gemini-pro-agent`, da `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` ta hanyar sadarwa — ba
> `gemini-2.5-pro` da aka nuna a tsofaffin takardu ba. Aika tattaunawa guda ɗaya ba tare da daidaitawa mai dacewa ba: MITM
> zai rubuta ainihin `model:` mai shigowa a log sannan ya bari buƙatar ta wuce. Daidaita wannan ƙimar kai tsaye, sannan
> za a kutsa buƙata ta gaba kuma a tura ta zuwa manufarka.

### 3.5 Sanarwar haɗari

AgentBridge yana kutse bayanan sirri (tokens na OAuth, maɓallan API) waɗanda IDE ke amfani da su don tabbatar da kansa ga masu samarwa na sama. Ana **ɓoye su kafin rubuta log** (duba §2.7), amma matakin MITM na OmniRoute yana iya ganinsu. Kunna kowane wakili a karon farko yana nuna taga ta sanarwar haɗari da za a iya rufewa.

### 3.6 Kulawa & Binciken Matsala

Dashboard yana nuna katin **Kulawa & Binciken Matsala** (`AgentBridgeMaintenanceCard`, a cikin `src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/tools/agent-bridge/components/`) wanda ke bayyana hanyoyin MITM na aiki waɗanda a baya ba su da UI. /api/tools/agent-bridge/agents/{id}/reset`) — warwarewa ta dannawa ɗaya ga kowane agent, wadda ke cire kwaikwayon hosts na wannan

nasa, domin IDE ya sake sadarwa da ainihin upstream bayan an sake kunna shi gaba ɗaya. Wannan **ba ya** taɓa

| Sunan bincike      | Abin da yake tabbatarwa                                       | Shawara idan an samu gazawa                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `server-running`   | Tsarin aikin MITM server yana aiki                            | "MITM server ba ya aiki. Kunna shi daga shafin AgentBridge."                                                                                               |

Gudanar da Gyara domin tsaftace shi."_ — kuma yana haskaka maɓallin **Gyara**. `Repair` shi ne kwatankwacin tutar `--cleanup` ta ProxyBridge a matakin application (yana miƙa aikin ga `repairMitm()` a cikin `src/mitm/manager.ts`).

> buƙatun sudo (irin halayen mitmproxy/Charles), saboda haka cire shi aiki ne na
> **Cire CA** da ake yi da gangan maimakon wani abu da ke faruwa kai tsaye lokacin dakatarwa.

### 3.7 Shigo da/fitar da config mai ɗaukuwa

AgentBridge na iya mayar da yanayin da **operator zai iya daidaitawa** zuwa blob na JSON mai version domin a iya kwaikwayon setup a kan na'urori daban-daban. Serializer ɗin shi ne `src/lib/inspector/configPortability.ts` (`exportConfig()` / `importConfig()`), wanda `AgentBridgeConfigSchema` ke inganta shi.

Abin da ake fitarwa ya ƙunshi ɓangarori uku kacal (da gangan **BA** a fitar da tsoffin ƙimomin da ke ciki, don haka shigo da su ba zai taɓa maimaita su ko cin karo da su ba):

| Fili             | Tushe                                                             | Bayani                                                               |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `bypassPatterns` | tsarin tsallakewa da mai amfani ya ayyana (`agent_bridge_bypass`) | an cire tsoffin tsarin banki/gov/okta                                |
| `customHosts`    | keɓaɓɓun rundunonin Traffic Inspector (`inspector_custom_hosts`)  | kowanne: `{ host, kind: "llm"\|"app"\|"custom", label? }`            |
| `agentMappings`  | taswirar samfurori na kowane wakili (`agent_bridge_mappings`)     | `{ [agentId]: [{ source, target }] }` ga kowane wakili mai taswirori |

```jsonc
// SAMU /api/tools/agent-bridge/config
{
  "version": 1,
  "bypassPatterns": ["*.internal.example.com"],
  "customHosts": [{ "host": "api.example.com", "kind": "llm", "label": null }],
  "agentMappings": {
    "copilot": [{ "source": "gpt-4o", "target": "claude-sonnet-4.7" }],
  },
}
```

**Halayyar shigo da bayanai** (`POST /api/tools/agent-bridge/config`): tsarin tsallakewa da taswirar kowane wakili suna **maye gurbin komai gaba ɗaya**; ana ƙara keɓaɓɓun rundunoni ta hanya mai **idempotent** (`INSERT OR IGNORE`). Amsar tana ba da rahoton adadin kowanne da aka aiwatar:

```jsonc
{ "ok": true, "bypassPatterns": 1, "customHosts": 1, "agents": 1 }
```

Abubuwan da **BA SA CIKIN** saitin: yanayin aikin sabar, hanyoyin takardar shaida, yanayin DNS na kowane wakili, hanyar CA ta sama, da saitunan TPROXY — waɗannan yanayin masauki/lokacin aiki ne, ba zaɓuɓɓukan da za a iya ɗauka ba.

## §5 Tsaro

### Dokoki Masu Tsauri da aka yi amfani da su

| Doka                              | Aiwatarwa                                                                                     |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **#12** `sanitizeErrorMessage`    | Ana tsabtace duk kurakuran handler kafin amsa ko shigarwa cikin buffer                        |
| **#13** Tura env zuwa shell       | Gyare-gyaren `/etc/hosts` suna amfani da zaɓin `env` — babu saka paths kai tsaye cikin string |
| **#15 + #17** `isLocalOnlyPath()` | `/api/tools/agent-bridge/` na LOCAL_ONLY + SPAWN_CAPABLE ne — ana tilasta loopback kafin auth |

### Jerin bypass na hosts masu muhimmanci

Jerin bypass yana tabbatar da cewa cibiyoyin kuɗi, masu samar da OAuth/SSO, da sauran hosts masu muhimmanci **ba a taɓa decrypt ɗinsu ba**. Zirga-zirgar TLS ɗinsu tana wucewa a matsayin transparent TCP tunnel — OmniRoute ba ya taɓa ganin plaintext ɗin.

Tsarin bypass na asali ya haɗa da:

- `*.bank.*`, `*.gov.*` (kuɗi/gwamnati)
- `*.okta.com`, `*.auth0.com`, `*.microsoft.com` (SSO/shaida)
- `*.apple.com`, `*.icloud.com` (ayyukan tsarin Apple)

Ana adana tsarin bypass da mai amfani ya ƙara a teburin `agent_bridge_bypass`, kuma suna da fifiko a kan komai.

### Ɓoye sirrika

Ana amfani da `maskSecrets()` daga `src/mitm/maskSecrets.ts`:

- A kan kowane request body kafin `TrafficBuffer.push()`
- A kan kowane header kafin logging ko broadcasting

Tsaruka: tokens masu prefix `sk-`/`ak-`/`pk-`, tokens na `Bearer`, da tokens na gama-gari masu haruffa ≥40.

### Cert na CA na upstream

Lokacin da aka saita `AGENTBRIDGE_UPSTREAM_CA_CERT`, ana karanta fayil ɗin lokacin farawa. Idan path ɗin yana nan amma ba za a iya karanta fayil ɗin ba, AgentBridge yana rubuta kuskure mai bayyana matsalar kuma ya ƙi farawa (yana hana gazawar TLS ta ɓoye a muhallin kamfanoni).

### Sanannun iyakoki

- **Port 443 yana buƙatar izini**: A Linux, AgentBridge yana buƙatar `setcap 'cap_net_bind_service=+ep'` a kan Node binary, ko a gudanar da shi ta `authbind`. Setup Wizard yana nuna umarnin da ya dace da OS.
- **Ana buƙatar sake kunna IDE**: Bayan redirect na DNS, dole ne a sake kunna IDE domin sabon fassarar host ta fara aiki.
- **OAuth tokens da aka rubuta kai tsaye**: Wasu agents (Kiro, Antigravity) suna adana OAuth refresh tokens a na'urar. Waɗannan ba sa kawo cikas ga AgentBridge — yana ganin Bearer token a kowace request, wanda ake ɓoyewa kafin logging.
- **Electron frontends suna buƙatar `NODE_EXTRA_CA_CERTS`**: IDEs da frontend ɗinsu ke gudana a kan haɗaɗɗen Node/Electron runtime suna yin watsi da OS/NSS trust store, kuma dole ne a ƙaddamar da su daga shell tare da saita `NODE_EXTRA_CA_CERTS` (duba §3.2). Alamar rashin sa: backend na IDE yana authenticate (MITM yana nuna `200`s) amma UI yana ci gaba da nuna ba a shiga ba.
- **Shigarwa da yawa na IDE ɗaya suna zaman kansu**: shigarwar tsarin (misali `/usr/share/antigravity/antigravity`) da shigarwar "Full" ta mai amfani (misali `~/AntigravityIDE_Full/antigravity-ide`) processes ne daban-daban masu runtimes nasu — dole ne a sake ƙaddamar da kowannensu tare da saka CA. Gano wanda ke gudana ta binary path ɗinsa kafin sake ƙaddamarwa.
- **System prompt na agent ne ke saita identity, ba routed model ba**: idan ka sake daidaita model na agent zuwa wani provider daban, amsar har yanzu tana bayyana identity na asalin agent ɗin (misali Antigravity yana amsa "Gemini ne ke sarrafa ni") saboda IDE yana saka hakan cikin system prompt. Tabbatar da ainihin backend a `call_logs` / `proxy_logs` (`provider`, `model`, `target_format`), ba ta hanyar tambayar model ko shi wanene ba.

## §6 Warware Matsaloli

### Rikicin port 443

Idan wani process ya riga ya fara sauraro a port 443 (web server, VPN, da sauransu):

```bash
lsof -i :443          # nemo process ɗin
sudo fuser -k 443/tcp  # kashe da ƙarfi (a yi amfani da hankali)
```

A madadin haka, saita port marar gata a cikin saitunan AgentBridge sannan ka kafa ƙa’idojin karkatarwa na `iptables` / `pf`.

### Ba a amince da certificate ba

Idan IDE ya nuna kurakuran TLS bayan kunna AgentBridge:

1. Tabbatar an shigar da cert ɗin: `security find-certificate -c "OmniRoute AgentBridge"` (macOS) ko `certutil -L -d sql:$HOME/.pki/nssdb` (Linux/NSS)
2. Wasu apps suna da nasu trust store (Firefox, Chrome a Linux). Sake gudanar da "Trust Cert" sannan ka duba cert store na musamman na NSS/Firefox.
3. Sake kunna IDE bayan amincewa — zaman TLS da ke gudana suna amfani da tsohon yanayin amincewa.

### IDE ya fita daga asusu / "connection error" duk da CA da aka amince da shi

Alama: bayan karkatar da DNS da amincewa da CA, IDE mai tushen Electron (misali Antigravity)
yana buɗewa alhali **an fita daga asusu** ko yana nuna kuskuren tabbatarwa/haɗi, duk da haka log ɗin MITM yana nuna
kiran farawa (`loadCodeAssist`, `fetchAvailableModels`, …) suna dawo da `200`.

Dalili: **bundled Node/Electron runtime na IDE yana watsi da OS trust store**. Native
backend (Go language server) yana amincewa da OS CA kuma yana tabbatar da sahihanci, amma Electron frontend
ba ya yi hakan — saboda haka UI yana ɗauka cewa ba ya kan layi.

Gyara (matakai biyu): export `NODE_EXTRA_CA_CERTS=<ca.crt>` **sannan ka sake ƙaddamar da IDE daga wannan
shell ɗin**, ba daga desktop icon ba. Da farko ka rufe IDE gaba ɗaya — singleton lock na Electron yana nufin
ƙaddamarwa ta biyu kawai za ta mayar da hankali ga process da ke gudana, kuma za a yi watsi da sabon environment ɗin. Duba §3.2.
Wannan ya yi daidai da wani rahoton upstream da har yanzu yake buɗe, inda standalone agent ke aiki ta MITM amma nau’in IDE
ya gaza a ƙarƙashin saitin iri ɗaya.

### DNS bai yaɗu ba

Duba cewa an sabunta `/etc/hosts`:

```bash
grep "omniroute\|127.0.0.1.*github\|127.0.0.1.*cursor" /etc/hosts
```

Share DNS cache:

```bash
# macOS
sudo dscacheutil -flushcache && sudo killall -HUP mDNSResponder
# Linux (systemd-resolved)
sudo systemctl restart systemd-resolved
# Windows
ipconfig /flushdns
```

### Ba a gano IDE ba

Ganowa ta atomatik tana amfani da sanannun wuraren shigarwa. Idan ganowa ta gaza amma an shigar da IDE:

- Duba ko binary na IDE yana wani wuri da ba na yau da kullum ba
- Setup Wizard har yanzu yana aiki — gazawar ganowa kawai tana nufin badge ba zai nuna wurin shigarwar ba

### Kurakuran handler (upstream fetch ya gaza)

Idan AgentBridge yana katsewa amma duk buƙatun suna gaza:

1. Tabbatar an haɗa aƙalla provider guda ɗaya a `/dashboard/providers`
2. Duba logs na server na OmniRoute: `APP_LOG_LEVEL=debug` a cikin `.env`
3. Tabbatar `OMNIROUTE_BASE_URL` yana nuni zuwa madaidaicin router endpoint (na asali: `http://127.0.0.1:20128`)

## §8 Taswirar ci gaba

### Binciken Trae

Trae sabon mataimakin rubuta lamba ne mai amfani da AI. Kafin aiwatar da handler:

1. Gano binary/extension ɗin a kasuwannin VS Code / JetBrains ko a matsayin manhaja mai zaman kanta
2. Kama zirga-zirgar bayanai da mitmproxy don gano masaukin API da siffofin endpoint
3. Tantance tsarin tantancewa
4. Yanke shawarar ci gaba/ƙin ci gaba bisa ga TOS da sauƙin gano API

Har sai an kammala binciken, katin Trae da ke dashboard yana nuna alamar "Ana bincike" tare da mahaɗin "Bayar da rahoton yiwuwar aiwatarwa". Stub ɗin handler da ke `src/mitm/handlers/trae.ts` yana jefa tsararren kuskuren `Not yet implemented`.

### Agents da ke cikin jerin ayyukan gaba (ana buƙatar MITM — babu goyon bayan custom base URL)

Kayan aikin da ke ƙasa ba sa goyon bayan custom base URLs a nau'ikansu na yanzu, wanda hakan ya sa MITM ya zama hanya ɗaya tilo ta katse bayanai. Har yanzu ana jiran tantance yiwuwar aiwatarwa:

- **Windsurf** (Codeium/Cognition)
- **Amp** (Sourcegraph)
- **Amazon Q / Kiro CLI** (AWS Bedrock — dabam da Kiro IDE)
- **Cowork** (Anthropic desktop)

Lura: GitHub Copilot CLI ≥v1.0.19 yana goyon bayan `COPILOT_PROVIDER_BASE_URL` — yi amfani da daidaitawa kai tsaye maimakon MITM don wannan kayan aikin.
