## Bayani

Playground Studio yana sauya `/dashboard/playground` daga editan Monaco mai sauƙi zuwa
cikakken filin aiki na gwaji. Yana maye gurbin tsohon `page.tsx` da tsarin `PlaygroundStudio`
wanda ke nuna shafuka huɗu da kuma ɓangaren daidaitawa na bai ɗaya.

```
┌ Playground ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [💬 Hira] [⚖ Kwatanta] [{} API] [🔧 Gina]      142↑ 38↓ · $0.002 </>│

│                                          │ [✨ Inganta prompt]        │

```

## Shafuka

### Shafin Hira

Yana haɓaka `ChatPlayground.tsx` zuwa filin aiki mai tattaunawa da zagaye da yawa tare da streaming:

- Cikakken nuna markdown ta hanyar `MarkdownMessage.tsx` (tubalan lamba, tebura, jeri, hanyoyin haɗi).
- Ana samo system prompt daga ɓangaren Daidaitawa na bai ɗaya.
- Token/kudin kowane saƙo (prompt + completion tokens).
- Sake samar da amsa ta ƙarshe.
- Yana aikawa zuwa `POST /v1/chat/completions` tare da SSE streaming.

### Shafin Kwatantawa

Babban abin da ke bambanta proxy: gudanar da prompt 1 a kan har zuwa **models 4 a lokaci guda**.

- Har zuwa ginshiƙai 4, kowannensu yana yin streaming da kansa daga `/v1/chat/completions`.
- Maɓallin `+ Ƙara model` (gajeriyar hanya Cmd+K) don ƙara ginshiƙai.
- `Gudanar da duka ▶` yana kunna dukkan streams lokaci guda ta hanyar `Promise.all` + `AbortController` na kowane ginshiƙi.
- **Soke duka** na gama-gari yana katse kowane stream da ke gudana.
- `ProviderMetrics` na kowane ginshiƙi yana nuna TTFT, TPS, tokens, da kiyasin kuɗi a ainihin lokaci.
- An yi wa metrics lakabin **"kimantawar gefen client"** (D12) — ana aunawa daga SSE chunk na farko.

### Shafin API

Yana kiyaye 100% na ainihin editan Monaco don ƙwararrun masu amfani (D14):

- Endpoints 10: chat completions, completions, embeddings, images, audio, speech, transcriptions, moderations, rerank, search.
- Loda fayil mai multimodal.
- SSE streaming tare da fitarwa a ainihin lokaci.
- An naɗe shi a matsayin `ApiTab.tsx` (ana loda shi idan an buƙata, `ssr: false`).

### Shafin Gina

UI na tools/function calling da structured output:

- `ToolsBuilder.tsx` — ƙara/gyara/cire `tools[]` tare da editan JSON schema na kowane tool.
  Yana tabbatar da ingancin parameters ta hanyar `ToolDefinitionSchema` (Zod).
- `StructuredOutputEditor.tsx` — kunna/kashe yanayin JSON + editan JSON schema.
  Yana tabbatar da ingancin response bisa schema ta hanyar `StructuredOutputSchema` (Zod).
- Yana aika request zuwa `/v1/chat/completions` tare da `tools[]` da/ko `response_format`.

## Fannin Saituna (Na Bai-ɗaya)

`StudioConfigPane.tsx` — koyaushe yana bayyane, kuma ana iya naɗewa.

| Fili           | Komfoni               | Bayanan kula                                                                    |
| -------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Endpoint       | `<select>`            | Zaɓuɓɓuka 10 da suka dace da `PlaygroundEndpoint`                               |
| Model          | `<input>`             | rubutu na kyauta, misali `openai/gpt-4o`                                        |
| System prompt  | `<textarea>`          | ana shigar da shi cikin dukkan shafuka                                          |
| Parameters     | `ParamSliders`        | temperature, max_tokens, top_p, presence/frequency penalty, seed, stop          |
| Presets        | `PresetPicker`        | loda/ajiye hotunan saituna masu suna (ana adana su a DB)                        |
| Improve prompt | `ImprovePromptButton` | yana buɗe modal na gargaɗin ƙa'ida, yana kiran `/api/playground/improve-prompt` |

Ana ɗaga state zuwa `PlaygroundStudio.tsx` sannan a tura shi zuwa dukkan shafuka. Sauya shafuka
yana kiyaye state na saituna.

## Mai Inganta Prompt

`ImprovePromptButton.tsx` → `useImprovePrompt.ts` → `POST /api/playground/improve-prompt`:

1. Modal yana yin gargaɗin cewa "zai cinye ƙa'ida".
2. Bayan tabbatarwa, yana aika `{ system, prompt, model, tone }` zuwa route ɗin.
3. Route ɗin yana kiran `/v1/chat/completions` a ciki tare da `promptImprover.META_SYSTEM_PROMPT`.
4. Yana dawo da `{ improvedSystem?, improvedPrompt?, tokensIn, tokensOut }`.
5. UI yana sabunta system prompt na fannin Saituna da user prompt na shafin Chat.

---

## Saitunan da Aka Tanada

`PresetPicker.tsx` → `usePresets.ts` → `/api/playground/presets/*`:

- Ana adana su a teburin SQLite na `playground_presets` (migration `084_playground_presets.sql`).
- Kowanne preset yana adana: `name`, `endpoint`, `model`, `system`, `params_json`, `created_at`.
- CRUD: jerin `GET`, ƙirƙira da `POST`, `GET /:id`, `PUT /:id`, `DELETE /:id`.

---

## Ma'aunin Stream

`useStreamMetrics.ts` + `streamMetrics.ts` (pure function):

- `start()` — yana rubuta lokacin fara request.
- `onFirstChunk()` — yana rubuta TTFT.
- `onChunk(n)` — yana tara adadin completion token.
- `finish(usage?)` — yana lissafa ma'aunan ƙarshe: `ttftMs`, `totalMs`, `tps`, `tokensIn`, `tokensOut`, `costUsd`.
- Farashi daga static table a cikin `src/lib/playground/types.ts` (an yi masa alamar "ƙiyasi" — D13).

## Routes na Backend

| Hanya    | Path                             | Handler                                                                                                     |
| -------- | -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `POST`   | `/api/playground/improve-prompt` | Yana tabbatar da `ImprovePromptRequestSchema` ta Zod; yana kiran `/v1/chat/completions` tare da meta-prompt |
| `GET`    | `/api/playground/presets`        | Yana dawo da `{ presets: PlaygroundPresetListItem[] }`                                                      |
| `POST`   | `/api/playground/presets`        | Yana ƙirƙirar preset; yana tabbatar da `PlaygroundPresetCreateSchema`                                       |
| `GET`    | `/api/playground/presets/:id`    | Yana dawo da preset guda ɗaya ko 404                                                                        |
| `PUT`    | `/api/playground/presets/:id`    | Sabuntawa ta ɓangare                                                                                        |
| `DELETE` | `/api/playground/presets/:id`    | 204                                                                                                         |

Auth: na zaɓi (`REQUIRE_API_KEY`). Kurakurai ta hanyar `buildErrorBody()` (Doka Mai Tsauri #12).

## Magance Matsaloli

| Alama                                        | Dalili                                  | Gyara                                                                                               |
| -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Editan Monaco ba ya bayyana a shafin API     | SSR ya loda Monaco                      | Tabbatar `ApiTab` yana amfani da `dynamic(..., { ssr: false })`                                     |
| Rafukan kwatantawa suna aiki ɗaya bayan ɗaya | Amfani da `Promise.all` ba daidai ba    | Dole ne a fara duk rafukan a cikin kira guda na `Promise.all`                                       |
| Ma'aunai suna nuna `null` TTFT               | Ba a haɗa mai kula da ɓangaren farko ba | Duba cewa ana kiran `useStreamMetrics.onFirstChunk()` a cikin madaukin mai karanta SSE              |
| Saitin da aka riga aka tanada ba ya dawwama  | Ba a gudanar da ƙaura ta DB ba          | Gudanar da `npm run db:migrate` ko sake kunna sabar (ƙaurar tana gudana ta atomatik lokacin farawa) |
| Inganta prompt yana dawo da 502              | Ba a saita model a cikin Config ba      | Dole ne mai amfani ya shigar da sunan model a sashen Config kafin ingantawa                         |
| Lambar fitarwa tana nuna `MISSING_API_KEY`   | Ba a saka alamar maye gurbi ba          | `codeExport.ts` koyaushe yana amfani da `API_KEY_PLACEHOLDER = "$OMNIROUTE_API_KEY"`                |
