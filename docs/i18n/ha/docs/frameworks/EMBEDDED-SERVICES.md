Ayyukan da aka haɗa ciki kayan aikin sidecar ne da ake girka a cikin na'ura, waɗanda OmniRoute ke girkawa, sa ido a kansu, kuma
ke gabatar da su a matsayin cikakkun wuraren da za a iya tura zirga-zirga zuwa gare su. Ba kamar masu samar da sabis na waje ba (waɗanda ake isa gare su ta intanet
ta amfani da maɓallan API), ayyukan da aka haɗa ciki suna gudana a na'ura ɗaya da OmniRoute kuma suna sadarwa ta loopback.

## 1. Bayani

### Me ya sa ake amfani da ayyukan da aka haɗa ciki?

An haɗa ayyuka shida:

| Aiki            | Kunshin npm                         | Tsohuwar tashar | Manufa                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------- | :-------------: | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **9Router**     | `9router`                           |      20130      | Na'urar tura zirga-zirgar AI da OmniRoute zai iya amfani da ita a matsayin ƙaramin mai samar da sabis. Ana gabatar da samfura a matsayin `9router/{sub}/{model}`                                           |
| **CLIProxyAPI** | Binary na sakin GitHub (`cliproxy`) |      8317       | Adaftan proxy na cikin gida don hanyoyin tabbatar da izinin Anthropic CLI. Yana samar da madadin hanyar tura zirga-zirga idan token na OAuth ya ƙare                                                       |
| **Mux**         | `mux` (`mux server` marar GUI)      |      8322       | Daemon na tsara ayyukan wakilai na cikin gida (coder/mux). Ana sarrafa zagayen rayuwarsa kawai — ba wurin tura zirga-zirga ba ne (babu proxy na LLM).                                                      |
| **Bifrost**     | `@maximhq/bifrost`                  |      8080       | Backend na isar da AI-gateway na Go. Lokacin da yake gudana, hanyar relay (`/v1/relay/`) tana zaɓarsa ta atomatik                                                                                          |
| **Dario**       | `@askalf/dario`                     |      3456       | Proxy na rajistar Claude — madadi/mafita idan CLIProxyAPI ya gaza ga zirga-zirga mai tsarin Claude-Code; maɓallin da aka saka ya zama `DARIO_ADMIN_TOKEN` wanda ke kare tsarin sarrafa OAuth na `/admin/*` |
| **open-wa**     | `@open-wa/wa-automate`              |      8323       | Sarrafa WhatsApp Web ta atomatik (Chromium marar GUI ta Puppeteer). Ana sarrafa zagayen rayuwarsa kawai — ba wurin tura zirga-zirga ba ne.                                                                 |

Dukkan ayyukan shida suna bin tsarin sa ido iri ɗaya:

- OmniRoute yana girka su a ƙarƙashin `DATA_DIR/services/{name}/` (a ware daga `package.json` na OmniRoute)
- OmniRoute yana fara su kuma yana sa ido a kansu a matsayin ƙananan processes
- OmniRoute yana saka maɓallin API na wucin gadi a cikin muhallin ƙaramin process ɗin kuma yana sauya shi ba tare da dakatar da aiki ba (inda hakan ya dace)
- Dukkan hanyoyin gudanarwa (`/api/services/*`) **LOCAL_ONLY** ne — ana iya samun damar su daga loopback kawai (ƙa'ida mai tsauri #17)

### Muhimman shawarwari (daga tsarin ƙira)

| Shawara                                           | Ƙima                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Samun damar dashboard zuwa UI na asali na 9Router | Reverse proxy a `/dashboard/providers/services/9router/embed/*`                                     |
| Hanyar girkawa                                    | `npm install {package}` ta `execFile` (babu shell interpolation)                                    |
| Yanayin amfani                                    | An yi rajistar mai samar da sabis a matsayin `9router/{sub}/{model}` a cikin injin tura zirga-zirga |
| Gudanar da maɓallin API                           | OmniRoute yana ƙirƙira, ɓoye shi lokacin ajiya (AES-256-GCM), sannan ya saka shi ta env             |
| Wurin dashboard                                   | `/dashboard/providers/services` (shafuka uku)                                                       |
| Farawa ta atomatik                                | Maɓallin kunnawa/kashewa ga kowane aiki, a kashe ta tsohuwa                                         |

## 3. Injin yanayin zagayen rayuwa

```
                    install()
  ┌─────────────┐ ──────────► ┌─────────────┐
  │ not_installed│             │   stopped   │◄──────────────────┐
  └─────────────┘             └──────┬──────┘                   │
                                     │ start()                   │
                                     ▼                           │ stop()
                               ┌──────────┐                      │
                               │ starting │                      │
                               └────┬─────┘                     │
             binciken lafiya ya yi  │         faɗuwa / SIGTERM   │
                               ┌────▼─────┐  (fita cikin 5s)     │
                               │ running  │──── faɗuwa ─────────►┤
                               └────┬─────┘                   ┌─▼────┐
                             stop() │                          │error │
                                    ▼                          └──────┘
                               ┌──────────┐
                               │ stopping │
                               └──────────┘
```

Ana adana yanayi a cikin teburin DB na `version_manager` (ginshiƙin `status`) kuma ana
kwafarsu a yanayin ƙwaƙwalwar-ciki na `ServiceSupervisor`. Yanayin ƙwaƙwalwar-ciki shi ne
mafi inganci ga tsari mai gudana; yanayin DB shi ne madadin dindindin yayin farawa.

### Sauye-sauyen yanayi

| Daga            | Lamari                                | Zuwa                      |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| `not_installed` | `install()` ya yi nasara              | `stopped`                 |
| `stopped`       | an kira `start()`                     | `starting`                |
| `starting`      | binciken lafiya ya dawo da 200        | `running`                 |
| `starting`      | tsari ya fita kafin ya zama lafiyayye | `error`                   |
| `running`       | an kira `stop()`                      | `stopping` → `stopped`    |
| `running`       | tsari ya fita ba zato (< 5 s)         | `error` (faɗuwa da sauri) |
| `running`       | tsari ya fita ba zato (> 5 s)         | `error`                   |
| `error`         | an kira `start()`                     | `starting`                |
| kowane          | `stop()` yayin `stopping`             | babu aiki                 |

### Makullin aiki

`ServiceSupervisor` yana jera ayyukan zagayen rayuwa ta hanyar makullin aiki na async
(`withLock()`). Kiran `start()` da yawa a lokaci guda a kan supervisor ɗaya yana haifar da
spawn guda ɗaya tak; mai kira na biyu zai jira sannan ya dawo da matsayin da yake akwai.
Wannan yana hana yanayin tseren aiki lokacin da, misali, farawa ta atomatik da maɓallin UI
suka kunna a lokaci guda.

## 4. Bayanin API

Duk hanyoyin da ke ƙarƙashin `/api/services/` **LOCAL_ONLY** ne (loopback kawai, ƙa’ida
mai tsauri #17). Buƙatun da ba na loopback ba suna samun `403 LOCAL_ONLY` ba tare da
la’akari da auth token ba.

### 4.1 Ƙarshen hanyoyin 9Router (hanyoyi 11)

#### `POST /api/services/9router/install`

Shigar da 9Router daga npm. Yana ƙirƙirar `DATA_DIR/services/9router/` tare da nasa
`package.json` da `node_modules/`. Ba ya cin karo da deps na OmniRoute.

**Jikin buƙata** (duk na zaɓi ne):

```json
{ "version": "latest" }
```

| Fili      | Nau'i    | Tsoho      | Bayani                                    |
| --------- | -------- | ---------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| `version` | `string` | `"latest"` | Alamar sigar npm ko semver da za a shigar |

**Amsoshi:**

| Matsayi | Bayani                                                                    |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `200`   | `{ ok: true, installedVersion: "x.y.z", path: "..." }`                    |
| `400`   | Jikin buƙata mara inganci (gazawar tantancewar Zod)                       |
| `409`   | Ana kan shigarwa (an riƙe makulli)                                        |
| `500`   | npm install ya gaza — duba `message` don saƙon kuskure mai sauƙin fahimta |

**Bayanan kula:** Yana amfani da `execFile('npm', [...])` — babu shell, babu interpolation
(ƙa’ida mai tsauri #13). Ana gabatar da kurakuran EACCES a matsayin saƙonni masu sauƙin fahimta.

---

#### `POST /api/services/9router/start`

Fara 9Router. Yana rajistar supervisor idan ba a riga an rajista ba, sannan ya kira
`supervisor.start()`. Ba ya canza sakamako idan yana gudana tuni.

**Jikin buƙata:** babu

**Amsoshi:**

| Matsayi | Bayani                                                |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| `200`   | Abun `ServiceStatus` (duba schema a ƙasa)             |
| `409`   | Ba a shigar da 9Router ba (`status: "not_installed"`) |
| `503`   | Farawa ya gaza (kuskuren tsari — duba `lastError`)    |

**Schema na ServiceStatus:**

```json
{
  "tool": "9router",
  "state": "running",
  "pid": 12345,
  "port": 20130,
  "health": "healthy",
  "startedAt": "2026-05-25T10:00:00.000Z",
  "lastError": null
}
```

---

#### `POST /api/services/9router/stop`

Tsayar da 9Router cikin tsari. Yana aika SIGTERM, ya jira 15 s, sannan ya aika SIGKILL
idan har yanzu yana aiki. Ba ya canza sakamako idan an riga an tsayar da shi.

**Jikin buƙata:** babu

**Amsoshi:**

| Matsayi | Bayani                             |
| ------- | ---------------------------------- |
| `200`   | `ServiceStatus` (state: "stopped") |
| `503`   | Tsayarwa ta gaza ba zato           |

---

#### `POST /api/services/9router/restart`

Daidai yake da `stop()` sannan `start()` a ƙarƙashin makullin aiki.

**Jikin buƙata:** babu

**Amsoshi:** iri ɗaya da `start` (yana dawo da `ServiceStatus` na ƙarshe).

---

#### `POST /api/services/9router/update`

Yana sabunta 9Router zuwa sabuwar sigar npm. Idan sabis ɗin yana gudana, za a fara
tsayar da shi, a gudanar da npm install (ana shigar da sabuwar sigar a wurin da yake),
sannan a sake fara sabis ɗin.

**Jikin buƙata** (duk na zaɓi ne):

```json
{ "version": "latest" }
```

**Amsoshi:**

| Matsayi | Bayani                                                          |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `200`   | `{ ok: true, previousVersion: "...", installedVersion: "..." }` |
| `400`   | Jiki mara inganci                                               |
| `500`   | Sabuntawar npm ta gaza                                          |

#### `POST /api/services/9router/rotate-key`

### 4.7 Reverse proxy (saka dashboard na 9Router)

Dashboard yana saka UI na yanar gizo na 9Router a cikin iframe ta hanyar reverse
proxy na ciki a:

```
GET|POST|... /dashboard/providers/services/9router/embed/[...path]
```

Wannan proxy:

- Yana tura request zuwa `http://127.0.0.1:{port}/{path}` (loopback kawai)
- Yana cire headers masu shigowa na `cookie` da `authorization` (babu fallasar session na OmniRoute)
- Yana saka `Authorization: Bearer {apiKey}` don tantancewar 9Router
- Yana cire `set-cookie`, `content-security-policy`, `x-frame-options`, `cross-origin-*` daga response
- Yana sake rubuta responses na HTML don saka `<base href>` da daidaita cikakkun paths (`/foo` → `/dashboard/.../embed/foo`)

Ana sarrafa upgrades na WebSocket don dashboard ɗin da aka saka ta wani companion server a
keɓaɓɓiyar tashar (duba `src/lib/services/embedWsProxy.ts`).

**Tsaro:** An rarraba hanyoyin embed proxy a ƙarƙashin `LOCAL_ONLY_API_PREFIXES`
kuma loopback ne kawai zai iya isa gare su. Maharin da ya samu JWT ta hanyar
tunnel na Cloudflare/Ngrok ba zai iya yin proxy zuwa sabis ɗin da aka saka ba.

## 5. Tsaro

### Tilasta LOCAL_ONLY (ƙa'ida mai tsauri #17)

Duk hanyoyin da ke ƙarƙashin `/api/services/` da `/dashboard/providers/services/*/embed/` an
rarraba su a matsayin LOCAL_ONLY a cikin `src/server/authz/routeGuard.ts`. Binciken loopback
yana gudana ba tare da wani sharaɗi ba kafin kowane reshen tantancewa:

```
buƙata ta iso
  → isLocalOnlyPath(path)?
      → ba-loopback ba → 403 LOCAL_ONLY (koyaushe, kafin binciken tantancewa)
      → loopback       → ci gaba zuwa tantancewa ta yau da kullum
```

Wannan yana hana JWT da ya fallasa (misali, ta hanyar tunnel) haifar da `npm install` ko
ƙaddamar da process. Duba `docs/security/ROUTE_GUARD_TIERS.md` don cikakken jadawalin
matakai.

### Saka API key

9Router da Mux suna buƙatar API key/bearer token don HTTP endpoints nasu.
OmniRoute:

1. Yana samar da key ta hanyar `crypto.randomBytes(32).toString("base64url")` tare da
   prefix na musamman ga service (`nr_` don 9Router, `mx_` don Mux).
2. Yana ɓoye shi lokacin da yake ajiye ta amfani da AES-256-GCM (cipher ɗin da ake amfani da shi don bayanan sirrin provider).
3. Yana warware ɓoyewar sannan ya saka shi a matsayin environment variable lokacin spawn —
   `NINEROUTER_API_KEY` don 9Router, `MUX_SERVER_AUTH_TOKEN` don Mux (ba a taɓa amfani da CLI
   flag ba, don haka token ɗin ba zai taɓa bayyana a cikin `ps`/jerin process ba).
4. Ba ya taɓa mayar da key ɗin plaintext a cikin kowace HTTP response.

CLIProxyAPI yana karɓar keɓaɓɓen data-plane key da ake sakawa lokacin spawn
(`needsApiKey: true` — ana amfani da shi don daidaita model da adapter).

### Kariyar SSRF

Reverse HTTP proxy (`/dashboard/.../embed/[...path]`) an ƙayyade shi kai tsaye don tura bayanai
kawai zuwa `http://127.0.0.1:{port}`. Ba ya taɓa bin redirects zuwa wuraren da ba loopback ba.
Ana amfani da library ɗin `ssrf-req-filter` don ƙin duk upstream URL da ke resolve zuwa
wajen kewayon loopback.

### Tsaron shell (ƙa'ida mai tsauri #13)

Ana kiran `npm install` ta hanyar `execFile('npm', ['install', pkg, '--prefix', dir])` —
ba template literals, ba shell, kuma ba a saka external paths cikin command
string. Ana miƙa runtime values (ports, API keys) ta hanyar `env` object na child.

### Tsabtace kurakurai (ƙa'ida mai tsauri #12)

Duk error responses daga `/api/services/*` suna bi ta `buildErrorBody()` ko
`sanitizeErrorMessage()`. Ba a taɓa mayar da ɗanyen `err.stack` da `err.message`
kai tsaye ga mai kira.

## 7. Magance Matsaloli

### Sabis bai fara aiki ba

**Alamomi:** Maɓallin farawa yana dawo da 503, matsayin yana ci gaba da kasancewa `"error"` ko `"starting"`.

**Jerin dubawa:**

1. Duba `GET /api/services/{name}/logs` (ko sashen Logs a cikin dashboard). Nemo
   layuka kamar `Error: ENOENT`, `address already in use`, ko `Cannot find module`.
2. Tabbatar cewa `npm` yana cikin PATH: gudanar da `which npm` daga asusun mai amfani ɗaya da ke gudanar da OmniRoute.
3. Tabbatar cewa an girka sabis ɗin: duba `GET /api/services/{name}/status` don
   `installedVersion`. Idan `null` ne, fara gudanar da girkawa.
4. Duba cewa `DATA_DIR/services/{name}/node_modules/` yana nan kuma ba fanko ba ne.
5. Duba filin `lastError` a cikin amsar matsayi don ganin dalilin fita da aka tsabtace.

---

### Farawa daga farko yana tafiyar hawainiya (> s 10 kafin kaiwa `running`)

**Alamomi:** Matsayin yana ci gaba da kasancewa `"starting"` na dogon lokaci kafin ya koma `"running"` ko `"error"`.

**Bayani:** Farawar 9Router daga farko ta haɗa da shigo da manyan bishiyoyin abubuwan dogaro (DNS,
tunnel, MITM modules). Tsohon ƙimar tazarar duba lafiyar aiki ita ce s 2 tare da yunƙuri 3 kafin
supervisor ya ayyana ƙarewar lokaci (amma ya ci gaba da duba lokaci-lokaci).

**Gyara:** Ana iya saita lokacin ƙarewar `healthIntervalMs` da `waitForHealthy`
(`healthIntervalMs * 3`) a cikin `bootstrap.ts`. Don sabis-sabis masu ɗaukar tsawon lokaci
wajen farawa, ƙara `healthIntervalMs` zuwa 5000 da `stopTimeoutMs` zuwa 30 000.

---

### Karo na tashar sadarwa (`EADDRINUSE`)

**Alamomi:** Rajistan ayyuka yana nuna `address already in use :::20130`.

**Dalilai:**

- Wani tsari yana amfani da tashar 20130 tuni.
- Ba a dakatar da wani tsohon tsarin 9Router gaba ɗaya ba (zombie PID).

**Gyara:**

1. Canja tsohuwar tashar ta hanyar environment variable na `NINEROUTER_PORT` a cikin `.env`.
2. Nemo tsarin da ke haddasa karo kuma ka dakatar da shi: `lsof -ti :20130 | xargs kill -9`.
3. Ana iya saita tashar ga kowane sabis a cikin `bootstrap.ts` ta hanyar filin `port`.

**Lura:** A tsohon tsari, 9Router yana amfani da tashar 20130 musamman don kauce wa karo da
tsohuwar tashar OmniRoute ta 20128.

---

### An hana izini (EACCES) yayin girkawa

**Alamomi:** Girkawa tana dawo da 500, rajistan ayyuka yana nuna `EACCES` ko `permission denied`.

**Dalilai:**

- Tsarin OmniRoute ba shi da izinin rubutu a cikin `DATA_DIR` ko babban kundinsa.
- Ana gudanarwa a cikin Docker rootless ba tare da izinin rubutu a kan volume da aka haɗa ba.

**Gyara:**

1. Duba `DATA_DIR` (tsohon ƙima: `~/.omniroute/`): `ls -la ~/.omniroute/`
2. Tabbatar cewa mai amfani da tsarin OmniRoute ne ya mallaki kundin: `chown -R $USER ~/.omniroute/`
3. A cikin Docker, tabbatar cewa volume mount yana da ingantattun izini ga mai amfani da container.

---

### Sabuntawa ta gaza (ƙarewar lokacin `npm install` ko kuskuren hanyar sadarwa)

**Alamomi:** Sabuntawa tana dawo da 500 tare da `InstallError`, rajistan ayyuka yana nuna ƙarewar lokacin hanyar sadarwa.

**Jerin dubawa:**

1. Tabbatar cewa ana iya isa ga npm registry: `npm ping`.
2. Duba corporate proxy: `npm config get proxy`, `npm config get https-proxy`.
3. Gwada girkawar da hannu: `npm install {package}@latest --prefix ~/.omniroute/services/{name}/`.
4. Idan ana bayan air-gap, fara sauke tarball ɗin sannan ka yi amfani da `npm install /path/to/tarball.tgz`.

---

### Sabis yana nuna matsayin `"error"` nan take bayan farawa (rushewa da sauri)

**Alamomi:** Matsayin yana sauyawa daga `"starting"` zuwa `"error"` cikin ƙasa da daƙiƙa 5.
`lastError` yana nuna `"Fast crash (exited with code 1)"`.

**Jerin dubawa:**

1. Karanta cikakken ƙarshen rajistan ayyuka: `GET /api/services/{name}/logs?tail=500`.
2. Dalilin da aka fi samu: environment variables da sabis ɗin ke buƙata sun ɓace.
3. Don 9Router: tabbatar cewa `NINEROUTER_DISABLE_MITM=true` da
   `NINEROUTER_DISABLE_TUNNEL=true` suna cikin env da aka aika lokacin spawn (duba
   `installers/ninerouter.ts` `resolveSpawnArgs`).

---

## 8. Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

**T: Zan iya fallasa endpoints na ayyukan da aka saka ciki ga clients waɗanda ba na loopback ba?**

A'a. Matakin LOCAL_ONLY an tsara shi da gangan (doka mai tsauri #17). Routes waɗanda za su iya kiran
`npm install` ko ƙaddamar da processes na `node` ba dole su kasance masu isa daga
traffic da ba na loopback ba, saboda idan JWT ya zube ta hanyar tunnel (Cloudflare, Ngrok, Tailscale),
hakan zai ba da damar ƙaddamar da kowane irin process. Babu keɓancewar ficewa ga
`/api/services/` — sabanin `/api/mcp/`, an cire shi daga jerin ketarewar manage-scope.
Duba `docs/security/ROUTE_GUARD_TIERS.md`.

---

**T: Shin 9Router da CLIProxyAPI za su kasance samammu a deployments na production/cloud?**

Eh. Duk ayyukan biyu suna bin tsarin local-first iri ɗaya da OmniRoute kansa. Suna aiki
a kan na'ura ɗaya kuma suna sadarwa ta loopback. "Production" a nan yana nufin VPS
ko local server inda aka deploy OmniRoute, ba wani remote cloud provider ba.

---

**T: Ta yaya zan debug supervisor?**

1. Bi SSE log stream kai tsaye: `curl -N http://localhost:20128/api/services/9router/logs`.
2. Duba structured logs a cikin output na pino na OmniRoute waɗanda aka tace ta
   namespace na `service:supervisor`.
3. Bincika row na DB: `sqlite3 ~/.omniroute/omniroute.db "SELECT * FROM version_manager WHERE tool='9router'"`.
4. Yi amfani da `GET /api/services/9router/status` don ganin live state na yanzu, PID, health,
   da `lastError` a kira guda.

---

**T: Supervisor yana nuna `health: "degraded"` ko `health: "unknown"` amma state ɗin shi ne `"running"`. Wannan matsala ce?**

`"degraded"` yana nufin health probe ya dawo da response wanda ba 200 ba. `"unknown"` yana nufin har yanzu babu
probe da ya kammala (gasa da poll na farko). Dukansu na ɗan lokaci ne yayin farawa.
Idan health ya ci gaba da kasancewa `"degraded"` fiye da `healthIntervalMs * 3` ms bayan
`"running"`, embedded service ɗin yana aiki amma HTTP API ɗinsa ba ya amsawa. Duba
ko port ɗin da ke cikin status response daidai ne, da kuma ko service ɗin yana
sauraro a wannan port ɗin da gaske.

---

**T: Zan iya canza API key na 9Router ba tare da cikakken restart ba?**

A'a. Ana aika API key ɗin zuwa 9Router ta hanyar environment variable a lokacin ƙaddamarwa.
Ba za a iya canza environment variables a cikin process da ke gudana ba. `POST .../rotate-key`
yana dakatarwa tare da sake kunna service ɗin ta atomatik don amfani da sabon key. Juyawar key ɗin
za ta fara aiki cikin `stopTimeoutMs` na service ɗin (default 15 s) tare da lokacin
farawarsa.

---

**T: Mene ne iyakar ring buffer kuma me ke faruwa idan ya cika?**

Kowane service yana da keɓaɓɓen ring buffer mai girman 5 MB. Idan buffer ɗin ya cika, ana
cire tsofaffin layukan log don samar wa sababbi sarari. Event na SSE `snapshot` yana dawo da
layuka na baya-bayan nan cikin iyakar `tail`. Ba a adana logs a disk sai dai idan
an saita `logsBufferPath` a cikin row na DB.
