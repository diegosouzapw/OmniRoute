> **Tushen ingantacciyar magana:** `src/lib/localCorpus/index.ts` (fihirisar rubutu mai iyaka),
> `src/lib/localCorpus/configured.ts` (lokacin aiki da aka saita),
> `src/lib/db/localCorpus.ts` (adana hanyar tushe),
> `open-sse/mcp-server/tools/localCorpusTools.ts` (kayan aikin MCP 3 masu karantawa kawai), da
> `src/app/api/settings/local-corpus/route.ts` (API na saituna).

## Mene ne wannan

Local Corpus yana bai wa mai gudanarwa damar fallasa kundin fayilolin rubutu guda ɗaya da aka amince da shi a sarari ga sabar MCP ta OmniRoute. Fayiloli suna nan a kundinsu na asali: OmniRoute yana adana ingantacciyar hanyar tushe kawai a cikin SQLite kuma yana kula da fihirisar bincike a ƙwaƙwalwar kwamfuta. Ba ya kwafe abubuwan corpus zuwa ma'ajiyar lambar ko ma'ajiyar bayanai.

Sabunta fihirisar yana gudana ne a hankali bisa sauye-sauye. Ana sake amfani da fayilolin da ba su canza ba bisa girmansu da lokacin da aka gyara su, ana sake karanta fayilolin da suka canza tare da samar musu da hash ta SHA-256, sannan ana cire fayilolin da aka goge. Bincike yana sabunta fihirisar da ta wuce daƙiƙa 30; masu kira kuma za su iya neman sabuntawa nan take.

## Saita tushen

Hanyar saitunan tana buƙatar irin tantancewar gudanarwa ɗaya da sauran API na saituna. Dole ne hanyar da aka miƙa ta riga ta kasance kuma ta zama cikakkiyar hanyar kundi.

```bash
# Haɗa kundin da aka amince da shi
curl -X POST http://localhost:20128/api/settings/local-corpus \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"rootPath":"/absolute/path/to/approved-text"}'

# Duba saitin da matsayin fihirisar
curl http://localhost:20128/api/settings/local-corpus

# Cire haɗin ba tare da canza fayilolin tushe ba
curl -X DELETE http://localhost:20128/api/settings/local-corpus
```

## Kayan aikin MCP

Dukkan kayan aikin uku suna buƙatar `read:local-corpus`. Amsoshin kayan aikin suna nuna hanyoyi masu dangantaka da sunan ƙarshen kundin tushe, ba cikakkiyar hanyarsa ba.

| Kayan aiki            | Bayani                                                                                                           |
| :-------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `local_corpus_status` | Bayar da rahoton yanayin saiti, girman fihirisa, iyakoki, da lokacin sabuntawa na ƙarshe                         |
| `local_corpus_search` | Bincika rubutun da aka yi wa fihirisa kuma dawo da taƙaitattun sassa masu iyaka bisa layi (har zuwa sakamako 20) |
| `local_corpus_read`   | Karanta zangon layuka mai iyaka daga fayil ɗaya mai izini mai dangantaka da corpus                               |

Misalan bayanan shigar MCP:

```json
{ "query": "Red River monitoring", "limit": 10, "refresh": false }
```

```json
{ "relativePath": "hydrology/stations.md", "startLine": 20, "endLine": 80 }
```

## Iyakokin tsaro

- Jerin izini ya karkata ga rubutu: `.cfg`, `.csv`, `.geojson`, `.htm`, `.html`, `.ini`,
  `.js`, `.json`, `.jsonl`, `.jsx`, `.log`, `.md`, `.mjs`, `.ps1`, `.py`, `.sh`,
  `.sql`, `.toml`, `.ts`, `.tsx`, `.txt`, `.xml`, `.yaml`, da `.yml`.
- Ana tsallake symlinks. Ana daidaita hanyoyin karantawa zuwa ingantaccen tsari kuma dole su kasance a cikin
  tushen da aka saita; ana ƙin cikakkun hanyoyi da yunƙurin ketare kundi.
- Ana ware sunayen kundin da ke da bayanai masu muhimmanci ko waɗanda aka samar ta atomatik: `.build`, `.codex`, `.env`,
  `.git`, `.next`, `.omniroute`, `.ssh`, `coverage`, `dist`, `node_modules`, da
  `secrets`.
- Iyakokin tsoho su ne fayiloli 5,000, 1 MiB ga kowane fayil, 64 MiB na jimillar abubuwan da aka yi wa fihirisa,
  kusan haruffa 4,000 ga kowane ɓangaren bincike, da layuka 400 ga kowane karantawa.
- Ana ɗaukar fayilolin da ke ɗauke da NUL a matsayin marasa rubutu kuma ana tsallake su ko a ƙi su.

Da gangan ba a tallafa wa takardun binary kamar PDF, DOCX, hotuna, da rumbunan fayiloli ba.
Mayar da su zuwa tsarin rubutu da aka amince da shi a cikin kundin da aka saita kafin yin fihirisa.

## Bayanan gudanarwa

- Canzawa ko goge tushen da aka saita yana share fihirisar bai-ɗaya da ke cikin ƙwaƙwalwar kwamfuta.
- Sake kunna tsari yana watsar da fihirisar; bincike na gaba zai sake gina ta daga tushen da aka saita.
- `local_corpus_status` ba ya tilasta yin dubawa. Yi amfani da `local_corpus_search` tare da
  `refresh: true` idan ana buƙatar sake dubawa nan take.
- Ana ƙirga gazawar dubawa da karantawa ko kuma a dawo da su a matsayin kurakurai da aka tsaftace; ba a taɓa
  gyara fayilolin tushe ba.
