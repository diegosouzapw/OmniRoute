## Lokacin zaɓar OmniRoute

- Kana ɗaukar nauyin kansa kuma kana son **mafi girman ɗaukar masu samarwa** (masu samarwa 329, shigarwar kasida kyauta/ba tare da tantancewa ba 155)
- Kana buƙatar **ginannen uwar garken MCP** (kayan aikin LLM, ƙwaƙwalwa, da ƙwarewa da aka fallasa a matsayin kayan aiki)
- Kana buƙatar **yarjejeniyar A2A** don tsarin ayyuka tsakanin wakili da wakili
- Kana son **ɓoye sawun yatsa** (JA3/JA4) don guje wa ganowa daga CAPTCHA na sama
- Kana buƙatar **siffofin kamfanoni** (matakan kariya, evals, tarihin dubawa) ba tare da kuɗin SaaS ba

## Lokacin zaɓar LiteLLM

- Kana **fara da Python** kuma kana buƙatar ƙaƙƙarfan haɗewa da `litellm.completion()`
- Kana buƙatar **ingantattun girke-girken turawa zuwa samarwa** (k8s, Helm charts)
- Tawagarka ta riga ta gudanar da ƙananan ayyukan Python

## Lokacin zaɓar OpenRouter (SaaS)

- Ba ka son ɗaukar nauyin kansa
- Ka yarda da biyan kuɗi bisa kowane token tare da ƙarin farashin SaaS
- Kana buƙatar **hanyar biyan kuɗi guda ɗaya** ga duk masu samarwa

## Lokacin zaɓar Portkey

- Kana buƙatar **SLA na kasuwanci** tare da tabbacin ci gaba da aiki
- Ka fi son **allon gudanarwa da ake sarrafawa** ba tare da nauyin ayyukan gudanarwa ba
- Kana buƙatar **siffofin bin ƙa’idojin kamfanoni** kai tsaye
