> [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md). Karanta shi gaba ɗaya kafin yin kowane canji — yana ɗauke da Dokoki Masu Tsauri guda 23,
> matakan tabbatar da inganci, ƙa’idojin lamba, ƙa’idojin sanya fayiloli / tsaftar tushen ma’ajiyar,
> taswirar ma’ajiyar da bayanan samun damar haɓakawa na gida waɗanda a baya suke cikin wannan fayil.

Bayanan da suka keɓanta da Gemini:

- Ana kunna ƙwarewa ta hanyar kayan aikin `activate_skill` (ana loda metadata na ƙwarewa a farkon zama,
  sannan ana kunna cikakken abun cikin idan an buƙata).
- Babu wasu ƙa’idoji na Gemini kaɗai a yau. Kada a sake ƙara ƙa’idojin aikin a nan — a gyara
  `AGENTS.md` maimakon haka, domin kowane mataimaki ya ga umarni iri ɗaya.
